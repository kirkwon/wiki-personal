# Atomic Habits — James Clear

James Clear's *Atomic Habits* reframes habit change as identity and environment design. Small systems compound into transformative results — a core insight connecting habit formation to strategic thinking. Key principles: make it obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying; focus on systems over goals. Connects to the strategies mental model through its emphasis on designing conditions for success.
