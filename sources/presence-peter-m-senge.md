# Presence - Peter M Senge

Peter M. Senge's [[Presence - Peter M Senge]] explores human purpose, collective awareness, and the field of the future. The book argues that organizations and communities develop a form of intelligence through shared intention, deep listening, and presence to the present moment.

## Key Themes

- Presence as a form of collective intelligence: organizations become more intelligent when members are deeply aware of the present and the larger system.
- Human purpose shapes the field of the future through intention and collective action.
- Systems thinking and organizational learning create emergent intelligence at the group level.

## Connection to Intelligence Model

Senge's perspective connects intelligence to organizational fields and human purpose, showing that intelligence is a property of groups and systems, not just individuals. This complements the broader intelligence mental model and connects to [[planning]] and [[group]].
