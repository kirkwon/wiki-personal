# Superforecasting - Philip E. Tetlock & Dan Gardner

## Summary

*Superforecasting* by Philip E. Tetlock and Dan Gardner explores how some individuals achieve markedly higher prediction accuracy than average forecasters, and argues that these methods are learnable. The book is structured around six chapters:

- **Chapter 1: The Art of Prediction** — Introduces forecasting as a skill that can be improved through scientific analysis and social science methods.
- **Chapter 2: The Forecasting Wars** — Examines cases where forecasting played crucial roles in politics, finance, sports, and weather, establishing why accurate predictions are essential yet challenging.
- **Chapter 3: The Superforecasters** — Introduces "superforecasters" — highly skilled individuals who excel through open-mindedness, intellectual humility, diversity of perspective, and probabilistic thinking.
- **Chapter 4: The Superforecaster Methodology** — Details specific techniques: considering multiple hypotheses, avoiding confirmation bias, seeking diverse perspectives, and incorporating expert input.
- **Chapter 5: The Prediction Market** — Explores prediction markets as an alternative to traditional expert polls, showing how aggregating diverse opinions improves accuracy.
- **Chapter 6: The Future of Prediction** — Discusses future implications including technological advancements, growing complexity of global events, and strategies for improving personal predictive skills.

## Key Concepts

- **Superforecasting** — The learnable ability to make accurate probabilistic predictions through structured methodology.
- **Probabilistic thinking** — Reasoning in terms of likelihoods rather than certainties.
- **Multiple hypotheses** — Considering alternative explanations simultaneously.
- **Confirmation bias avoidance** — Actively seeking disconfirming evidence.
- **Prediction markets** — Aggregating diverse opinions for better forecasts.
- **Bayesian updating** — Continuously revising probability estimates as new information arrives.
