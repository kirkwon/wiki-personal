# New England Seafood

Source document covering New England seafood cuisine — clams, lobsters, cod, scallops, clam chowder, lobster rolls, fried seafood shacks, and regional comparisons.

This source covers the regional American seafood tradition centered on Atlantic Ocean bounty — clams, lobsters, cod, and scallops. Core content includes clam chowder, lobster rolls, fried seafood, fish and chips, and New England boiled dinner. Regional comparison with Pacific Northwest and Gulf Coast seafood traditions is presented, along with staple ingredients, equipment, and preparation guidance.

## Key Dishes
- Clam chowder (creamy white style, distinct from Manhattan tomato-based)
- Lobster roll (Maine butter style vs. Connecticut mayo style, origin legend at Perry's Milford CT 1918)
- Fried clams and fried scallops
- Fish and chips
- New England boiled dinner

## Key Ingredients
- Quahogs
- Maine lobster
- Atlantic cod
- Haddock
- Sea scallops
- Blue mussels
