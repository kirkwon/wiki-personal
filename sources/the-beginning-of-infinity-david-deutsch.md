# The Beginning of Infinity - David Deutsch

David Deutsch's *The Beginning of Infinity* explores the nature of knowledge, progress, and the role of explanatory theories. Deutsch argues that the growth of knowledge is infinite and that ideas—particularly good explanations—are the driving force behind human progress.

## Key Concepts

- Explanatory theories as the basis of knowledge
- The infinity of possible knowledge creation
- The role of error correction in progress
