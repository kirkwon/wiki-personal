# Explanations Source

Mental model treating explanation itself as a universal framework for navigating complexity. Central claim from David Deutsch's *The Beginning of Infinity*: good explanations are hard to vary, and the reach of good explanations is infinite. Bad explanations are easy to vary and are explanatory dead ends. Confidence set to 0.7, suggesting ongoing refinement.
