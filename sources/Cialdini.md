# Cialdini Source Summary

Source referencing Robert B. Cialdini's works on persuasion and influence.

## Sources Referenced
- [[Influence_ The Psychology of Persuasion - Robert B Cialdini]]
- [[Pre-Suasion_ A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade - Robert B Cialdini]]

## Key Concepts
Cialdini is positioned as a universal mental model for navigating complex social situations through understanding persuasion patterns. The six principles of influence—reciprocation, commitment/consistency, social proof, authority, liking, and scarcity—form the core framework.
