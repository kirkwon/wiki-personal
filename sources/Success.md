## Main Arguments & Findings

- **Core claim**: Success is a universal mental model — a framing device that helps organize and make sense of disparate advice and strategies from multiple domains.
- **Support**: References 13 well-known books across success literature, from Dalio's principles to Thiel's startup philosophy to Senge's systems thinking. The breadth of sources provides moderate evidential support, though the model itself remains abstract.
- **Strength**: The cross-referencing is a useful organizational signal. Weakness: the model is defined very broadly ("a conceptual tool or principle"), making it hard to operationalize without drilling into individual sources.
