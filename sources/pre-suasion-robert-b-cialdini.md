# Pre-Suasion — Robert B. Cialdini

Cialdini's *Pre-Suasion* reveals that influence begins before the message — through environmental priming, framing, and contextual setup. The critical window is the moments before attention is captured. This extends strategic thinking into the domain of persuasion, where setting the context determines outcomes. Connects to the strategies mental model through its emphasis on preparation and environmental design.
