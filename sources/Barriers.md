# Barriers Source

This source introduces "Barriers" as a universal mental model for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. The concept is grounded in Edward de Bono's work on lateral thinking, specifically from *Lateral Thinking: Creativity Step by Step*. The model serves as a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world, with medium confidence (0.7) and applicability across general domains.
