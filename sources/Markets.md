# Markets

Markets is a mental model that serves as a conceptual tool for understanding and navigating complex situations. It provides a framework for recognizing patterns and relationships in economic systems, particularly around credit cycles, supply and demand dynamics, and debt burdens.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This mental model is referenced in Ray Dalio's framework "How the Economic Machine Works," which simplifies macroeconomic dynamics into credit cycles, productivity growth, money printing, and deleveraging phases.
