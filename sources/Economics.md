# Economics Source

Economics is framed as a mental model—a conceptual tool for understanding patterns and relationships in complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of the world.

This source is referenced in two well-known behavioral economics works:
- [[Misbehaving - Richard H Thaler]] by Richard H. Thaler
- [[Nudge - Richard H Thaler  Cass R Sunstein]] by Richard H. Thaler and Cass R. Sunstein

The model is applicable universally across general domains with moderate confidence (0.7).
