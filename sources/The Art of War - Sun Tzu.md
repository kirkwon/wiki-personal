# The Art of War - Sun Tzu

Source summary for the book *The Art of War* by Sun Tzu.

## Why it Matters
This book provides actionable frameworks for strategic thinking in competitive situations, emphasizing careful planning, situational assessment, and understanding the factors that determine success or failure.

- Chapter 8: Variation in Tactics – adapting to specific situations
