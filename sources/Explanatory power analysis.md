# Explanatory power analysis

Source document for the skill of Explanatory power analysis — systematic practice of improving efficiency and output by identifying methods that maximize productivity while maintaining sustainability. Rooted in David Deutsch's concept of explanatory power as a criterion for good explanations.

## Key Concepts
- Audit current workflows to identify efficiency losses
- Apply frameworks for high-impact activities
- Iterate based on measurement and results
- Scale effective approaches through documentation and teaching

## Cross-Source Perspectives
David Deutsch, in *The Beginning of Infinity*, emphasizes explanatory power as a measure of the quality of explanations — does a theory explain more phenomena with fewer assumptions than alternatives. This epistemological concept is adapted here as a productivity skill for auditing workflows and maximizing output.
