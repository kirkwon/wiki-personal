# Stress

## Definition

Stress is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This mental model is referenced in three sources:

### Barry Schwartz — The Paradox of Choice
Stress appears in the context of choice overload and decision paralysis, where the abundance of options creates stress as a navigational signal.

### Barry Schwartz — Why We Work
Stress connects to work motivation and the meaning people derive from their labor.

### Mark Manson — Everything Is F_cked: A Book About Hope
Stress is linked to hope and meaning-making, framing it as a lens for understanding how people construct meaning in the face of uncertainty.
