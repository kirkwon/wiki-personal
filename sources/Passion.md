# Passion

Passion is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

This mental model is referenced in Angela Duckworth's work on Grit — The Power of Passion and Perseverance — which integrates passion with perseverance as a framework for sustained achievement across domains.
