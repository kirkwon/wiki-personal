# Data

This source introduces "Data" as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition. The model frames data not as raw information but as a framework for making sense of the world.

Cross-source perspectives include Nassim Nicholas Taleb's *Fooled by Randomness* and Richard H. Thaler's *Misbehaving*, both of which critique naive reliance on data and narrative without accounting for cognitive biases and randomness.

The model has moderate confidence (0.7), acknowledging uncertainty about its completeness and the risk of being too generic without more operational definition.
