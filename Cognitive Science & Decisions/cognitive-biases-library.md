# Cognitive Biases Library

Cognitive biases are systematic patterns of deviation from rationality in judgment, often resulting from heuristics or mental shortcuts. This library compiles key biases such as:

- **Confirmation Bias**: Favoring information that confirms preexisting beliefs
- **Anchoring Effect**: Overreliance on initial information (the "anchor")
- **Availability Heuristic**: Judging probability based on immediate examples
- **Sunk Cost Fallacy**: Continuing an endeavor due to prior investment

These biases, extensively studied by Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky, distort decision-making by altering perception of risk, probability, and value. Understanding these patterns is critical for mitigating their impact in personal and professional contexts.
