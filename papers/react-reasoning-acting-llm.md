# ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models

**arXiv:** [2210.03629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)
**Authors:** Shunyu Yao, Jeffrey Zhao, Dian Yu, Nan Du, Izhak Shafran, Karthik Narasimhan, Yuan Cao
**Year:** 2022

## Abstract

Interleaves reasoning traces (thought) with task-specific actions (act) enabling LLMs to reason about actions and act on reasoning iteratively.

## Relevance

Foundational paradigm for interleaved reasoning traces with task actions. Precursor to virtually all modern agentic loop architectures including Hermes.
