# Expertise OS: Theme Design Specifications

This document outlines the visual philosophy and technical specs for each skin in the Expertise OS.

## 1. Modern Dark (Default)
- **Concept**: High-end SaaS Productivity Tool.
- **Palette**: Deep Navy (#0f172a), Indigo (#6366f1), Violet (#8b5cf6).
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (Headings), Inter (Body).
- **Key Features**: Glassmorphism, backdrop-blur, subtle radial gradients.

## 2. Editorial
- **Concept**: Sophisticated Digital Magazine.
- **Palette**: Charcoal Black (#121212), Gold Accents (#c5a059).
- **Typography**: Playfair Display (Headings), Lora (Body).
- **Key Features**: High-contrast, elegant serifs, minimalist borders.

## 3. Pixel
- **Concept**: Retro 90s Computing / iMac G3.
- **Palette**: Light Gray (#e0e0e0), Bondi Blue (#008080).
- **Typography**: VT323 (Monospace).
- **Key Features**: Hard shadows, pixel-perfect borders, retro terminal vibe.

## 4. Dossier
- **Concept**: Vintage Secret Service Case File.
- **Palette**: Aged Paper (#d4ccc5), Typewriter Ink (#3d3d3d).
- **Typography**: Courier Prime (Monospace).
- **Key Features**: Rigid structure, monochrome earth tones, typewriter aesthetic.

## 5. Etching
- **Concept**: Architect's Sketchbook.
- **Palette**: Stark White (#fafafa), Ink Black (#000000).
- **Typography**: Cormorant Garamond (Headings), Montserrat (Body).
- **Key Features**: Fine-line strokes, minimalist spacing, "hand-inked" feel.

## 6. Whiteboard
- **Concept**: Collaborative Brainstorming.
- **Palette**: Clean White (#ffffff), Marker Blue (#3b82f6), Eraser Red (#ef4444).
- **Typography**: Caveat (Handwriting).
- **Key Features**: High legibility, informal accents, rounded "bubble" corners.

## 7. Sketch
- **Concept**: Charcoal Drawing / Artist's Pad.
- **Palette**: Pure White (#fff), Deep Graphite (#000).
- **Typography**: Caveat (Handwriting).
- **Key Features**: Rough borders, high-contrast, artistic and informal.
