# Tex-Mex

## Overview

Tex-Mex is Texas-Mexican fusion cuisine born from Tejano culture (Texans of Mexican descent). A beloved American comfort food tradition, Tex-Mex blends Mexican techniques with American ingredients, creating dishes like nachos, chili con queso, and the Mission-style burrito.

## Origins

### Tejano Culture

**Texans of Mexican descent**

- Developed in South Texas
- Mexican cooking techniques
- American ingredients (cheddar, flour tortillas)
- Regional pride

### Evolution

| Era | Developments |
|------|-------------|
| **1800s** - Tejano cooking in South Texas |
| **1940s** - Nachos invented (legend) |
| **1970s** - Queso becomes popular |
| **1980s** - Mission burrito (San Francisco) |
| **Present** - Nationwide comfort food |

## Key Characteristics

### Mexican vs. Tex-Mex

| Aspect | Mexican | Tex-Mex |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Cheese** | Oaxacan, queso fresco | Cheddar, American |
| **Tortillas** | Corn | Flour (often) |
| **Cumin** - Used sparingly | Used heavily |
| **Heat** | Regional (variable) | Mild to medium |
| **Beans** - Black, pinto | Refried beans |

### Tex-Mex Elements

| Element | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Flour tortillas** - Softer, larger than corn |
| **Cheddar cheese** - Melting, yellow |
| **Cumin** - Heavily used, earthy |
| **Ground beef** - Standard protein |
| **Refried beans** - Pinto beans, cooked twice |

## Classic Dishes

### Nachos

**The Tex-Mex invention**

**Legend:** Created in 1943 by Ignacio "Nacho" Anaya for military spouses at Victory Club in Piedras Negras, Mexico (border town)

**Components:**
- Tortilla chips (corn)
- Melted cheese (cheddar, American)
- Optional: jalapeños, beans, meat, guacamole, sour cream

**Preparation:**
1. Layer chips on sheet
2. Sprinkle cheese generously
3. Bake 5-10 minutes (375°F/190°C)
4. Add toppings
5. Serve immediately

### Chili con Queso

**Cheese dip**

**Ingredients:**
- Velveeta or processed American cheese
- Rotel tomatoes and green chilies
- Optional: chorizo, ground beef, jalapeños

**Preparation:**
1. Melt cheese in slow cooker or saucepan
2. Add Rotel
3. Add optional meat
4. Keep warm
5. Serve with tortilla chips

### Tacos

**Soft and hard shell**

**Soft tacos:**
- Flour or corn tortillas
- Ground beef, chicken, or pork
- Cheese, lettuce, tomato, sour cream
- Salsa, hot sauce

**Hard shell tacos:**
- Pre-formed corn shells
- Same fillings as soft
- American-style

### Enchiladas

**Rolled, sauced**

**Tex-Mex style:**
- Flour tortillas (sometimes corn)
- Cheese, chicken, or beef filling
- Rolled, topped with cheddar cheese and sauce
- Baked until bubbly
- Served with rice, beans

### Quesadilla

**Cheese quesadilla**

**Components:**
- Flour tortillas
- Cheddar cheese
- Optional: chicken, steak, peppers, onions

**Preparation:**
---
1. Fill tortilla with cheese
2. Fold in half
3. Cook on griddle until cheese melts
4. Cut into wedges
5. Serve with salsa, guacamole

### Mission Burrito

**San Francisco style** (though born in Texas)

**Characteristics:**
- Large flour tortilla
- Rice, beans, meat, cheese, salsa, sour cream, guacamole
- Wrapped tight, ends tucked in
- Hearty, portable meal

## Tex-Mex vs. Mexican

| Dish | Mexican | Tex-Mex |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Tacos** | Corn tortillas, fresh salsa | Flour or corn, cheddar |
| **Cheese** - Queso fresco, Oaxacan | Cheddar, American |
| **Burritos** - Simple, minimal filling | Large, rice, many fillings |
| **Chili** | No beans | Often includes beans |

## Staple Ingredients

| Item | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Flour tortillas** - Soft, pliable | Tacos, burritos |
| **Cheddar cheese** - Melting | Nachos, enchiladas |
| **American cheese** - Velveeta for queso | Queso |
| **Cumin** - Earthy, signature spice | Ground beef seasoning |
| **Chili powder** - Mild, American blend | Seasoning |
| **Rotel** - Canned tomatoes and chilies | Queso |
| **Refried beans** - Pinto beans | Sides, fillings |
| **Jalapeños** - Pickled or fresh | Heat |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Not melting cheese enough | Melt queso thoroughly, keep warm |
| Using wrong cheese | Use melting cheese (cheddar, American) |
| Overfilling burritos | Fill evenly, wrap tight |
| Not warming tortillas | Warm for pliability |
