# Hot Pot

Hot pot (or hot pot meal) is a popular communal Chinese cooking experience where diners cook raw ingredients in a simmering broth at the table. The central feature involves various specialty broths—such as spicy Sichuan, mild bone broth, or miso—which provide the liquid medium for cooking. Participants customize their meals by dipping and cooking items like thinly sliced meats, tofu, vegetables, and noodles into the boiling pot. It is generally considered a social event, emphasizing sharing and conversation while enjoying the meal.
