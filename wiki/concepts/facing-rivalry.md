
# Facing Rivalry

Facing rivalry is the practice of dealing with competition and rivalries — both personal and professional — through a Stoic lens. The key insight is to focus on one's own growth rather than comparing oneself to others.

## Core Principle
Competition is an obstacle that can be reframed as an opportunity for self-improvement. Instead of being consumed by rivalries, channel that energy into developing your own capabilities and character.

## Connection to Strategic Thinking
This concept connects to [[infinite-game-mindset]] — focusing on long-term growth rather than short-term victories — and [[the-game-of-success]] — understanding strengths and weaknesses to achieve goals.
