# Zone Focusing

Zone focusing is a technique where the photographer pre-focuses to a set distance and uses a wide aperture to create a deep depth of field, then shoots without refocusing. This allows for fast, discreet shooting without the delay of autofocus.

## How to Zone Focus

1. Set aperture to f/8 - f/16 for wide depth of field
2. Set distance to 6-10 feet (typical street distance)
3. Switch to manual focus and don't refocus
4. Shoot — subjects in that range will be in focus

## Why Use Zone Focusing

- Faster than autofocus
- No delay between seeing and shooting
- Discreet (no beep or focus light)
- Works well with the "zone focus + wait" technique: pre-focus, find an interesting location, and wait for an interesting person to enter the frame

## Hyperfocal Distance

A related concept: the hyperfocal distance is the closest distance at which a lens can focus while keeping infinity sharp. Setting focus at the hyperfocal distance means everything from half that distance to infinity is in focus, providing maximum depth of field for pre-focusing and shooting freely.
