# High-Yield Savings Accounts

Online savings accounts paying 4-5% interest, compared to 0.01% at traditional brick-and-mortar banks. Sethi recommends opening one as a key step in the six-week program for emergency funds and short-term savings.

## Providers Mentioned
- Marcus (JPMorgan)
- Ally
- Discover
- Capital One 360

## Impact
$10,000 in a high-yield account earns $400-500/year versus $1 at a traditional bank.

## Role in Conscious Spending Plan
Serves as the savings bucket (5-10% of income) for emergency fund and short-term goals. Sub-savings accounts for specific goals (vacation, car repair, wedding) prevent "stealing" from yourself.
