# Blind Trust in Authority

Blind trust in authority describes how individuals act on misinformation or manipulation by influential people despite clear evidence to the contrary, leading to irrational choices and behaviors.

## Core Idea

Ariely uncovers how blind trust in authority figures can lead to irrational choices and behaviors, highlighting instances where individuals have acted on misinformation or manipulation by influential people despite clear evidence to the contrary.^[raw/articles/the-upside-of-irrationality---dan-ariely.md]

## Manifestations

- Compliance with authority figures even when contradictory evidence exists
- Following expert recommendations without critical evaluation
- Social proof from credible sources overriding personal judgment
- Institutional trust overriding individual assessment

## Connection to Existing Concepts

This concept connects to [[radical-transparency]] (the need for openness and truth over comfort) and [[skin-in-the-game]] (the principle that decision-makers must bear consequences). It extends [[ascription-bias]] by showing how authority status amplifies attribution errors.
