# Framing

Framing is using elements within a scene to create a "frame" around the main subject. This technique adds depth, context, and structure to an image.

## Types of Frames

| Type | Example | Effect |
|------|---------|--------|
| Natural | Trees, arches, doorways | Adds depth, context |
| Architectural | Windows, door frames, arches | Adds structure |
| Environmental | Leaves, branches, rocks | Adds organic feel |
| Light | Silhouettes, light beams | Adds drama |

## How to Use

1. Find natural frames (doorways, windows, trees)
2. Position the subject behind or inside the frame
3. The frame doesn't need to be complete — partial framing works
4. Use depth of field: blurred foreground frame with a sharp subject

## Examples

- Portrait through doorway: Subject framed by the door
- Person behind tree branches: Branches frame the person
- Cityscape through tunnel: Tunnel frames the city view
