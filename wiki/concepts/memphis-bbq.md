# Memphis BBQ

Memphis BBQ centers on pork ribs with a heavy dry rub and optional sweet sauce glaze.

## Philosophy

- Pork ribs (spare ribs or baby backs)
- Dry rub (heavy application)
- Optional sweet sauce
- Hickory wood

## The Rub

- Salt (3 parts)
- Brown sugar (2 parts)
- Paprika (1 part)
- Black pepper (1 part)
- Garlic powder (1 part)
- Onion powder (1 part)
- Chili powder (optional)

## Cooking Process

1. Remove membrane from ribs
2. Apply dry rub (12-24 hours before)
3. Smoke at 225°F (107°C)
4. Optionally wrap after 3 hours
5. Cook until tender (4-5 hours)
6. Optional: Glaze with sweet sauce last 30 minutes

## Serving

- Dry (no sauce) — Memphis tradition
- Light sauce (Memphis sweet tomato) — optional
