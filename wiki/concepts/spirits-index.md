## Tasting Framework

### The Whisky Tasting Process
1. **Visual**: Color, clarity, viscosity
2. **Nose**: Initial aroma, complexity, evolution
3. **Palate**: First impression, flavor development, finish
4. **Finish**: Length, warmth, evolution

## Comparison: Bourbon vs Scotch

| Aspect | Bourbon | Scotch |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Grain** | 51%+ corn | Malted barley |
| **Region** | USA (Kentucky/Tennessee) | Scotland |
| **Smoke** | Generally none | Peated in Islay |
| **Flavor** | Vanilla, caramel, sweet | Malt, peat, floral |
| **Price** | More accessible (entry $20-30) | Premium starts higher |
