# Normal Distribution Models

Models based on the bell curve (Gaussian distribution) and its applications. These assume that most observations cluster around a mean with symmetric deviations.

## Key Tension with Power Laws

Normal distribution models assume most events are average with few extremes. Power law models reveal that in many real systems, extremes are far more common than the bell curve predicts. This connects to [[black-swan-events]] and challenges assumptions in [[probability-and-judgment-under-uncertainty]].

## When Appropriate

- Measurement error
- Heights, weights, test scores (when bounded)
- Sums of many independent random variables (Central Limit Theorem)
