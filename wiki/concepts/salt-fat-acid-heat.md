# Salt Fat Acid Heat

Salt Fat Acid Heat is a foundational cookbook by Samin Nosrat that distills the essential principles of cooking into four elements: salt, fat, acid, and heat. The book serves as a practical guide to understanding how these elements interact to create flavorful dishes.

## Overview
The four elements framework provides a simple yet powerful lens for thinking about cooking:
- **Salt** - Enhances and balances flavors
- **Fat** - Carries flavor, adds richness and texture
- **Acid** - Brightens and balances richness
- **Heat** - Drives chemical reactions that develop flavor
