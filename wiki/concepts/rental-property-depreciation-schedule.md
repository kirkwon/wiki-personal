# Rental Property Depreciation Schedule

27.5-year straight-line depreciation schedule for residential rental properties.

## Structure

| Year | Depreciation Amount | Tax Shield | Cumulative Depreciation |
|------|-------------------|------------|-------------------------|
| 1 | $[Amount] | $[Amount] | $[Amount] |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 27.5 | $[Amount] | $[Amount] | $[Total] |

## Calculation

Annual Depreciation = Building Value / 27.5 years
Tax Shield = Annual Depreciation × Tax Rate

Included in the [[single-property-analysis-template]] and [[tax-optimization-templates]].
