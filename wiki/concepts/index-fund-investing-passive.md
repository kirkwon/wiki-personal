# Index Fund Investing (Passive)

A buy-and-hold investment strategy using low-cost index funds that track broad market indices. This approach is central to Sethi's recommendation in *I Will Teach You to Be Rich* and is supported by extensive evidence that index funds outperform the majority of actively managed funds.

## Core Principle
Buy the market, not individual stocks. Index funds own thousands of companies, providing instant diversification and eliminating the risk, time, and stress of stock picking.

## Key Funds Referenced
- **VTSAX**: Vanguard Total Stock Market Index Fund (0.04% expense ratio)
- **VFIAX**: Vanguard S&P 500 Index Fund (0.04% expense ratio)
- **VBTLX**: Vanguard Total Bond Market Index Fund (0.05% expense ratio)
- **VTTHX**: Vanguard Target Retirement 2055 (target-date fund that auto-adjusts allocation)

## Why Index Funds Win
- **Performance**: Index funds outperform 80-90% of active funds over long periods.
- **Low fees**: 0.04-0.05% vs 1%+ for active management.
- **Diversification**: VTSAX alone holds 4,000+ companies.
- **Zero maintenance**: Set up automatic monthly contributions and forget it.

## Asset Allocation: Rule of 110
110 minus your age = percentage to hold in stocks.
- Age 25: 85% stocks, 15% bonds
- Age 35: 75% stocks, 25% bonds
- Age 45: 65% stocks, 35% bonds
- Age 55: 55% stocks, 45% bonds

## Dollar-Cost Averaging
Investing the same amount every month regardless of market conditions. Buy more shares when prices are low, fewer when high. Eliminates market timing risk and removes emotion.

## Automation
Set up automatic monthly investments into Roth IRA, 401(k), and taxable accounts. Spend less than 1 hour per year on maintenance.
