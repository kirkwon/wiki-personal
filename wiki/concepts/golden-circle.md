# Golden Circle

The Golden Circle is Simon Sinek's framework that explains how great leaders and organizations inspire action. It consists of three concentric layers: Why, How, and What.

## The Three Layers
1. **Why** – The core purpose, cause, or belief. Why does your organization exist? Why do you get out of bed in the morning?
2. **How** – The principles, values, or processes that bring the Why to life.
3. **What** – The tangible products, services, or results. What do you do?

## Key Insight
Most organizations communicate from the outside in (What → How → Why), but inspired leaders and organizations communicate from the inside out (Why → How → What). Starting with Why creates loyalty, trust, and action.

## Examples
- **Apple**: Steve Jobs focused on the inner workings (Why: challenge the status quo) rather than just external features.
- **Successful companies**: Those that prioritize employees and customers at the level of Why tend to thrive.

## Connection to Purpose
The Golden Circle is the structural framework through which [[purpose]] is articulated and communicated. Without a clear Why, even good intentions become hollow.
