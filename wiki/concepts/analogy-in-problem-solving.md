# Analogy in Problem-Solving

Analogy is using a similar problem that has already been solved as a template or starting point for the current problem. Polya considers this one of the most powerful heuristics in the "devising a plan" phase.

This strategy directly parallels the [[recognition-primed-decision-model]], where Klein describes experts as recognizing situations that match patterns from past experience. Both approaches leverage prior knowledge to navigate unfamiliar territory.

Polya asks two key questions: "Do you know a related problem?" and "Here is a problem related to yours and solved before"—both pointing to the same principle of drawing on a repertoire of solved problems.
