# The Systems Bible - John Gall

*The Systems Bible: The Beginner's Guide to Systems Large and Small* by John Gall is a wry, accessible introduction to how systems actually behave in practice. Gall emphasizes that systems tend to produce results not intended by their designers and that understanding system behavior requires looking beyond individual components.

## Key Themes

- Systems produce unexpected results
- Complexity is inherent and cannot be fully eliminated
- Human factors and communication gaps are primary sources of system dysfunction
- Simplicity is often the best design principle

## Connection to the System Mental Model

Gall's work is one of the two canonical sources referenced for the [[system-mental-model]], alongside Donella Meadows' *Thinking in Systems*.
