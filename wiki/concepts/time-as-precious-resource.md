# Time as a Precious Resource

Seneca's opening letter in *Letters from a Stoic* argues that time is our most precious and non-renewable resource. This concept parallels Covey's [[time-management-matrix]] and [[quadrant-2-focus]], but predates them by nearly two millennia.

## Key Ideas

- Most people waste time on trivial pursuits while neglecting what is truly important.
- Purposeful living requires constant vigilance against time drift.
- The urgency of time should motivate daily self-examination and intentional action.
- Connects to [[quadrant-2-focus]] by urging investment in important but not urgent activities.

## Practice

Seneca recommends periodic reflection on how time is being spent, asking whether daily actions align with stated values and long-term goals.
