# The Art of Leading Others

The Art of Leading Others explores how to inspire and lead effectively through adversity using Stoic principles such as empathy, humility, and understanding human nature.

## Core Principles
- Lead by example through composure under pressure
- Use empathy to understand the challenges others face
- Maintain humility about what you do not control
- Focus on developing others rather than controlling outcomes
