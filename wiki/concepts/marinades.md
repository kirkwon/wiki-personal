# Marinades

Marinades are liquid mixtures used to infuse flavor and sometimes tenderize ingredients before cooking. They serve four core purposes: flavor infusion, tenderizing, moisture retention, and surface preparation.

## Five Components

### 1. Acid
- **Chinese**: Rice vinegar, black vinegar
- **Japanese**: Mirin, sake, rice vinegar
- **Western**: Lemon juice, lime, wine
- Purpose: Tenderizes via enzymes, adds brightness, balances fat

### 2. Oil
- **Neutral oils**: Vegetable, canola, grapeseed
- **Asian oils**: Toasted sesame (finish), peanut
- Purpose: Coats, retains moisture, carries flavor

### 3. Seasoning
- **Salty**: Soy sauce, fish sauce, salt
- **Umami**: Oyster sauce, hoisin, fermented bean paste
- **Sweet**: Sugar, honey, mirin
- Purpose: Base flavor profile

### 4. Aromatics
- **Fresh**: Ginger, garlic, scallion
- **Dried**: Five-spice, star anise, cinnamon
- Purpose: Complex flavor, aromatic depth

### 5. Cornstarch (Chinese Technique)
- Purpose: Creates velveting effect
- How: Dissolved in marinade, coats protein
- Result: Silky, tender texture when cooked

## Chinese Marinade Template

**Basic Marinade (for 1 lb protein)**:
- Soy sauce: 2 tbsp
- Shaoxing wine (or dry sherry): 1 tbsp
- Cornstarch: 1 tsp
- Sesame oil: 1 tsp
- White pepper: 1/4 tsp
- Ginger (grated): 1 tsp
- Garlic (minced): 1 clove

**Process**:
1. Mix marinade in bowl
2. Add protein, toss to coat
3. Marinate 15–30 minutes (don't over-marinate with soy — gets salty)
4. No need to rinse (unless extremely salty)

## Protein-Specific Timing

| Protein | Minimum | Maximum | Notes |
|---------|----------|----------|--------|
| Chicken | 15 min | 2 hours | Longer = mushy |
| Pork | 30 min | 4 hours | Benefits from longer |
| Beef | 1 hour | 12 hours | Needs time for tenderizing |
| Seafood | 5–10 min | 30 min | Don't overcook! |
| Tofu | 15 min | 1 hour | Absorbs quickly |
| Vegetables | 5–10 min | 30 min | Don't over-marinate |

## Quick Recipes

- **Quick Marinade (15 min)**: Soy sauce + sesame oil + garlic + ginger. Use for stir-fry, pan-sear, grilling.
- **Overnight Marinade (8–12 hours)**: Soy + Shaoxing + five-spice + sugar. Use for roast duck, pork belly, beef.
- **Velveting Marinade**: Soy + cornstarch + egg white (optional). Use for Chinese restaurant-style stir-fry, beef, chicken.

## Common Mistakes

- ❌ Over-marinating seafood — texture becomes mushy (10 min max)
- ❌ Not coating evenly — some parts flavored, some bland
- ❌ Using too much salt — soy sauce is already salty
- ❌ Marinating too long with acid — meat breaks down unpleasantly

## Best Practices

- Use a bowl large enough to toss food easily
- Cover and refrigerate (bacteria grow at room temp)
- Marinate at room temp only if cooking within 30 min
- Save some sauce — don't marinate and cook in same liquid (salty, burned bits)
- Cornstarch trick — add to marinade for silky texture
