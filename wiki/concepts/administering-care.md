# Administering Care

Administering care refers to the practice of actively caring for others—employees, colleagues, and team members—as a leadership discipline. Simon Sinek argues that caring for others contributes to a positive work environment, higher morale, and better overall outcomes.

This concept extends beyond superficial kindness; it involves genuine investment in people's well-being and professional growth. Administering care is both a cultural signal and a practical driver of performance, connecting to [[service-first-leadership]] and [[belonging-in-leadership]].
