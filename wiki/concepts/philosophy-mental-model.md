# Philosophy as Mental Model

Philosophy is a universal mental model that functions as a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations. It provides a framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world, serving as a mental shortcut across all domains.

## Definition
Philosophy helps understand and navigate complex situations by providing a mental shortcut or framework for recognizing patterns and relationships. Unlike domain-specific knowledge, it applies universally to general life and decision-making contexts.

## Connection to Existing Mental Models
This concept extends the wiki's mental model taxonomy alongside other universal conceptual tools such as [[thinking-mental-model]], [[reality-mental-model]], [[planning]], [[stillness]], and [[care]]. Like these models, philosophy operates as a pattern-recognition device for making sense of complexity.

## Ryan Holiday and Stoic Philosophy
The primary source referenced is Ryan Holiday's [[the-obstacle-is-the-way]] and The Daily Stoic, which frames Stoic philosophy as a practical mental model for daily life. Holiday's work emphasizes applying ancient philosophical principles to modern challenges, treating philosophy not as abstract theory but as a living framework for decision-making and resilience.
