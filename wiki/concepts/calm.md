# Calm

Calm is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

As a universal mental model, Calm functions as a navigational principle applicable across all domains — from decision-making and productivity to relationships and personal well-being. It emphasizes the value of approaching complexity with composure and clarity, enabling more effective pattern recognition and relationship mapping.

This model draws from Ryan Holiday's *Stillness Is the Key*, which argues that stillness and calm are foundational capacities for operating well in a noisy, chaotic world. Calm extends that insight into a broader mental model: whenever you encounter a complex situation, adopting a calm posture — both internally and externally — becomes a lens for seeing what matters and responding wisely.

Calm connects to related mental models like [[stillness]], [[settings]], and [[Solutions]], offering a complementary perspective on how we orient ourselves within complexity.
