# Fisherman's Wharf

A prominent tourist area located in San Francisco, California, known for its waterfront location and historic maritime atmosphere. It is famous for the sea lions at Pier 39 and various seafood restaurants. The wharf serves as a major point of interest for both tourists and local residents alike.
