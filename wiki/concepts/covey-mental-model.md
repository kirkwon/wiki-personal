# Covey Mental Model

Covey is a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world, applicable universally across general domains.

## Core Framework

The model draws from two major works by Stephen R. Covey:

- [[the-7-habits-of-highly-effective-people---stephen-r-covey]] — establishes foundational habits including being proactive, beginning with the end in mind, putting first things first, and seeking win-win outcomes.
- [[first-things-first---stephen-r-covey]] — extends the framework with quadrant-based time management, emphasizing priority-driven action and Quadrant 2 focus.

## Key Principles as Mental Models

- **Begin with the end in mind** — A pattern-recognition tool for clarifying desired outcomes before taking action.
- **Put first things first** — A priority framework for distinguishing between urgent and important tasks, connecting to [[quadrant-2-focus]] and [[time-management-matrix]].
- **Be proactive** — Strategic vs reactive thinking, connecting to [[strategic-vs-reactive-thinking]].
- **Seek first to understand, then to be understood** — Effective listening and communication skills.

## Connections

This mental model connects to broader decision-making and personal effectiveness frameworks including [[systematic-decision-making]], [[working-backwards]], and [[planning]]. It reinforces the importance of intentional prioritization over reactive busyness.

The model has moderate confidence (0.7) due to thin elaboration in the source material. The specific principles that qualify as universal mental models remain open for further exploration.
