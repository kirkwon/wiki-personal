# Climate Change and Resource Scarcity

Dalio identifies climate change and resource scarcity as increasingly impactful forces on global economics and political dynamics. Environmental challenges create risks and opportunities that intersect with debt cycles, power transitions, and technological disruption.

## Key Points

- Climate change and resource scarcity are not isolated environmental issues but systemic factors affecting economic stability and geopolitical competition.
- Nations and investors who account for these risks in their strategies are better positioned for long-term success.
- Resource competition can accelerate power transitions and create new sources of conflict.
- This connects to [[scenario-planning]] by adding environmental variables to geopolitical modeling.

## Connections

The intersection of environmental pressures with economic cycles extends the wiki's existing frameworks in [[decision-making-under-uncertainty]] and [[real-options-analysis]] by introducing long-term systemic risk factors that operate on different timescales than financial markets.
