# Multi-Property Portfolio Template

Aggregated portfolio metrics across multiple investment properties.

## Portfolio Overview
- Total properties, total investment, total market value, total equity
- Average LTV, total monthly cash flow, annualized cash flow
- Portfolio cap rate

## Individual Property Performance Table
Compares each property on purchase price, current value, monthly rent, expenses, net cash flow, cash on cash, and cap rate.

## Portfolio-Level Analysis
- Total monthly income, expenses, cash flow
- Annual cash flow, portfolio value, equity
- Portfolio LTV, cash on cash, cap rate, debt coverage ratio
