# Silhouettes

Silhouettes are created by exposing for the bright background (usually the sky) so the subject becomes a dark shape against the light.

## Technique

1. Expose for the bright sky, not the subject
2. The subject becomes a dark silhouette
3. Find recognizable shapes: trees, people, buildings

## When to Use

Silhouettes are effective for dramatic effects, shape recognition, and abstract compositions. They are commonly used during golden hour with backlighting when the sun is behind the subject.
