# Power Law Models

Models where a few events have large impact and many have small impact. The distribution of outcomes follows a power law rather than a normal (bell curve) distribution.

## Key Insight

Power law models reveal that [[black-swan-events]] and [[extreme-outcomes]] are not anomalies but expected features of systems with power law dynamics. This challenges the assumption that averages and standard deviations capture system behavior.

## Examples

- City sizes, earthquake magnitudes, income distributions
- Website popularity, word frequencies
- Financial losses, innovation impact

## Connection to Decision-Making

Understanding power laws changes how we assess risk — focusing on tail events rather than average outcomes, connecting to [[expected-value-ev]] and [[bet-sizing]].
