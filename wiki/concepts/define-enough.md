# The Power of "Enough"

Define what "enough" means to you. Avoid endless wants and focus on contentment.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## Why It Matters

Lifestyle inflation — where spending increases with income — means that without a definition of enough, no amount of wealth will ever feel sufficient. The endless treadmill of wanting more undermines financial progress and well-being.

## Practical Application

- Define your own success metrics rather than comparing to others.
- Focus on contentment over accumulation.
- Minimize regret by focusing on decisions you won't regret.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]
