# Empathy

Empathy is the ability to understand and share the feelings of others. Weinberg includes it in the social and emotional intelligence section of *Super Thinking* as a foundational skill for better decisions and relationships. Covered in Part VI of *Super Thinking* on social and emotional intelligence.

## Core Idea

Empathy enables you to see situations from others' perspectives, predict their reactions, and build stronger relationships. It is distinct from sympathy (feeling for someone) — empathy involves feeling with someone.

## Applications

- **Decision-making**: Consider how stakeholders will be affected by your decisions.
- **Negotiation**: Understand the other party's interests, not just their positions.
- **Leadership**: Build trust and psychological safety through genuine understanding.

## Connection to Other Models

Empathy connects to [[reciprocity]], [[social-proof]], [[authority-bias]], and [[in-group-out-group-bias]] as part of the broader social cognition toolkit. It complements the analytical models in Weinberg's framework with emotional intelligence. Understanding empathy helps overcome tribal thinking and explains why people conform.
