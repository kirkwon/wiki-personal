# Social Influence Models

Models of how opinions, behaviors, and innovations spread through populations. These examine the mechanisms by which individual choices cascade into collective outcomes.

## Mechanisms

- **Threshold models**: Individuals adopt when enough neighbors have adopted
- **Contagion models**: Adoption spreads like an epidemic
- **Social learning**: Individuals imitate successful others
- **Information cascades**: People follow others' actions rather than their own information

## Key Findings

- Network structure determines how quickly and widely influence spreads
- Minority opinions can become majority through social dynamics
- Cascading failures and consensus both depend on network topology
- Herding behavior emerges naturally from social influence
