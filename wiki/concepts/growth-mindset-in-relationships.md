# Growth Mindset in Relationships

How adopting a growth mindset enhances interpersonal relationships through understanding, empathy, and forgiveness. Rather than viewing partners, friends, or colleagues as fixed in their qualities, a growth mindset approach prioritizes growth and adaptation in relationships.

## Key Principles
- **Understanding over judgment** — Viewing others as capable of growth rather than fixed in their flaws or strengths.
- **Empathy and forgiveness** — Recognizing that people can change reduces resentment and increases compassion.
- **Effort in relationships** — Applying the same growth-oriented mindset to investing in and improving relationships over time.
