# Message

Message is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

As a universal mental model, Message is applicable across general domains and serves as a framework for navigating complexity through pattern recognition. With a confidence level of 0.7, it functions similarly to other mental models like [[ideas-mental-model]] and [[thinking-mental-model]], but is distinct in its emphasis on purpose-driven communication and the clarity of messaging as a lens for understanding situations.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This mental model is referenced in Simon Sinek's work on purpose and communication, particularly *Find Your Why*. Sinek's framework emphasizes that effective messaging starts with understanding one's "why" — the core purpose that guides action and connects with others.
