# Side Lighting

Side lighting occurs when the sun is positioned to the side of the subject, creating dramatic shadows and dimension.

## Effect

- Dramatic shadows on one side
- Enhanced dimension and depth
- Textural details emphasized

## When to Use

Side lighting works well for portraits (dimension with shadow on one side), landscapes (shadows create depth), and architecture (emphasize texture). It is a primary golden hour technique alongside [[backlighting]] and [[front-lighting]].
