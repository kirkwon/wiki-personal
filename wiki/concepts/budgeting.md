# Budgeting

Budgeting is the process of creating a detailed financial plan that estimates expected income and expenditures over a specific period (e.g., monthly or annually). It involves tracking anticipated cash inflows against planned outflows to ensure financial stability and achieve savings goals. By categorizing expenses—such as housing, food, and discretionary spending—individuals and organizations can manage resources effectively and identify areas for potential cost reduction or investment.
