# Computer Vision

Computer Vision (CV) is an interdisciplinary field of artificial intelligence that enables computers to interpret and 'understand' visual information from images or videos, mimicking the capabilities of human sight. It involves processing raw pixel data to perform tasks such as object detection, image segmentation, facial recognition, and scene understanding. CV typically combines deep learning techniques, particularly Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), with computer graphics and pattern recognition theory. Its applications span autonomous vehicles, medical imaging analysis, and industrial quality control.
