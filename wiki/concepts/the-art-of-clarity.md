# The Art of Clarity

The foundational concept of Shane Parrish's book, establishing clarity as the central purpose of clear thinking. Clarity is presented as a learnable skill that enhances daily decision-making by reducing confusion, bias, and noise in thought processes.

This concept underpins the broader decision-making framework and connects to [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]] (separating process from outcomes) and [[systematic-decision-making]] (structured processes for better decisions).
