# Temperance

The virtue of self-control, humility, and balance in difficult situations. Discussed in [[the-obstacle-is-the-way]] Chapter 6 as crucial for overcoming obstacles.

## Core Ideas
- Maintaining self-control when facing challenges
- Practicing humility in success and adversity
- Finding balance rather than extremes
- Knowing limits of discipline and optimization
