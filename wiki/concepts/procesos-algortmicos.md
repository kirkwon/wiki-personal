# Procesos Algorítmicos (Algorithmic Processes)

These are structured sets of rules or steps used to solve specific problems or complete a computation. In AI/ML, algorithms dictate how models process data—for instance, gradient descent is an iterative process used to minimize loss functions during training. They form the computational backbone necessary for predictive modeling and automated decision-making systems.
