# Mind Maps

Mind maps are visual tools for organizing and connecting ideas, central to Tiago Forte's framework for building a second brain. They facilitate idea connection, improve comprehension, and enhance decision-making by representing information spatially rather than linearly.

Forte emphasizes mind maps as a way to externalize thinking and reveal relationships between concepts that may not be apparent in traditional note formats. This visual approach connects to [[decomposing-and-recombining]] as a problem-solving technique and supports the broader goal of [[second-brain]] knowledge organization.
