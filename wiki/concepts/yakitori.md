# yakitori

Yakitori is a Japanese skewered chicken dish seasoned with salt or tare (a mixture of sugar, salt, and mirin). It's often grilled over charcoal, resulting in a smoky flavor that complements the meat. Photographically, capturing yakitori can showcase culinary artistry through lighting and composition, while cognitively it represents cultural gastronomy appreciation.


Financially, enjoying yakitori may be considered an affordable yet culturally enriching dining experience that aligns with budget-conscious lifestyles without compromising on taste or authenticity.
