# Skin

Skin is a universal conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It functions as a mental shortcut that helps make sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Connection to Skin in the Game

The model draws directly from [[skin-in-the-game---nassim-nicholas-taleb]], where Taleb argues that actors must bear the consequences of their decisions. This principle of risk alignment is central to how "Skin" operates as a mental model—by identifying situations where risk is misaligned or hidden, one can recognize patterns that others miss.

## Related Concepts

- [[antifragility]] - Closely related; systems that benefit from volatility often require skin in the game to function properly.
- [[moral-hazard]] - The inverse of skin in the game; transferring risk to others without accountability creates hidden asymmetries.
- [[skin-in-the-game---nassim-nicholas-taleb]] - Taleb's specific articulation of this principle.

## Applicability

Skin is applicable across all domains as a universal mental model. It aids in identifying hidden asymmetries in risk, recognizing when accountability structures are missing, and understanding how consequences shape incentives.
