# Social Proof

Social proof is the tendency to look to others to determine appropriate behavior, especially in ambiguous situations. People assume that if others are doing something, it must be correct. Covered in Part VI of *Super Thinking*.

## Core Idea
This bias is exploited in marketing (testimonials, user counts, "bestseller" labels), crowd behavior (panic during emergencies), and group decision-making. It can lead to herding behavior and poor individual decisions.

## Applications
- **Marketing**: Show social proof to increase conversion.
- **Decision-making**: Recognize when you're following the crowd rather than thinking independently.
- **Organizations**: Be aware of groupthink dynamics.
