# Balanced Living

Balanced living, as described by McKeown, involves creating harmony across different areas such as career, relationships, and self-care, leading to an overall sense of fulfillment and effortless living.

## Key Ideas
- Balance across life areas prevents burnout and neglect
- Harmony is not equal time allocation but intentional attention to each area
- Pursuing productivity at the expense of relationships or health undermines the goal
- The "when to stop" criteria flag when balance has been lost

## Connection to Existing Concepts
This relates to [[balancing-ambition-with-well-being]]—the tension between ambition and well-being is central. It also connects to [[minimalism]]—doing less in each area to enable presence in all areas.
