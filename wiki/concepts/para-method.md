# PARA Method

The PARA method is Tiago Forte's organizational framework for structuring digital content within a second brain system. PARA stands for Projects, Areas, Resources, and Archives, providing a hierarchical categorization scheme for personal knowledge.

- **Projects** — Active work with a defined outcome and deadline
- **Areas** — Ongoing responsibilities with no specific endpoint
- **Resources** — Topics of interest for future reference
- **Archives** — Inactive items no longer needing attention

This method provides a practical alternative to more analytical approaches like Sönke Ahrens' Zettelkasten, emphasizing immediate usability and actionability over deep interlinking. It connects to [[categorizing-information]] as a specific implementation of content organization and relates to [[personal-knowledge-vault]] as a broader knowledge management strategy.
