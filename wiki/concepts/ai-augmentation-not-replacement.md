# AI as Augmentation, Not Replacement

The framing that artificial intelligence should be positioned as a tool that enhances human capability rather than substituting for it. This concept connects to broader discussions about human-AI collaboration, the alignment problem, and how technology interacts with cognition and decision-making.

Related to [[emotions-and-ai]] which explores the intersection of affect and artificial intelligence, and [[the-alignment-problem---brian-christian-tom-griffiths]] which examines ensuring AI systems remain beneficial to humans. [[superforecasting]] and [[hyperfocus]] touch on human cognitive strengths that AI can complement rather than replace.
