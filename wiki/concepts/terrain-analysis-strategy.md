# Terrain Analysis Strategy

The systematic assessment of the landscape and environment in which operations take place, as detailed in Chapter 10 of *The Art of War*. Terrain analysis involves understanding physical conditions, navigating different terrain types, and adapting strategy accordingly.

## Key Ideas
- Different types of terrain require different tactical responses
- Understanding the environment is prerequisite to effective action
- Reading the enemy's movements through terrain awareness
- Positioning advantage depends on terrain knowledge

## Application
Applicable to competitive analysis, market positioning, and any domain where understanding the landscape of operations determines strategic success.
