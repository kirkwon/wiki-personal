# Post Corona – From Crisis to Opportunity

Scott Galloway's *Post Corona* frames how major crises can be reframed as opportunities for structural change. It connects to the broader "World" mental model by emphasizing pattern recognition in post-crisis landscapes and identifying where new value is created amid disruption.
