
# Put First Things First

This habit focuses on prioritizing tasks based on importance rather than urgency. Covey introduces the Time Management Matrix, which classifies activities into four quadrants based on their importance and urgency.

## Key Ideas

- **Importance vs. urgency**: Activities are categorized as important/unimportant and urgent/non-urgent, creating four quadrants.
- **Quadrant II focus**: The most effective people invest their time in Quadrant II—important but not urgent activities like planning, prevention, relationship building, and renewal.
- **Discipline of prioritization**: Saying no to less important tasks is essential for protecting time for what truly matters.
