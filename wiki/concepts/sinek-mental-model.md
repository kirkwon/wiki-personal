# Sinek Mental Model

Sinek is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Core Idea

The Sinek mental model draws from Simon Sinek's philosophy, particularly the idea of starting with "why" — understanding the purpose behind actions before addressing how and what. This framework serves as a universal lens for navigating complexity by anchoring decisions and strategies in fundamental purpose.

## Applicability

- **Universal** across domains including leadership, decision-making, personal development, and organizational strategy.
- Functions as a mental shortcut for pattern recognition and relationship understanding.

## Connection to Golden Circle

Sinek's broader framework — often called the Golden Circle — organizes thought in three layers:
1. **Why** — Purpose, cause, belief
2. **How** — Process, values, principles
3. **What** — Actions, results, products

Starting with "why" provides clarity that guides how and what, making this a powerful tool for both individual and team decision-making.
