# Urban Resilience Planning

Urban resilience planning refers to the redesign and rebuilding of cities and urban infrastructure to prioritize public health, social connectivity, and sustainability in the post-pandemic era. Galloway argues that the post-corona world demands reimagined urban environments.

## Key Themes

- Resilient infrastructure that prioritizes public health and social connectivity.
- Redesigned cities accommodating remote work, changing commuting patterns, and reduced density needs.
- Balancing sustainability with economic viability.
- Health-focused urban planning as a response to pandemic lessons.
