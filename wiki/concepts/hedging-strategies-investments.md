# Hedging Strategies for Investments

Techniques to offset or reduce investment risk exposure.

## Financial Hedging
Interest rate hedges (swaps, caps, collars), currency hedges (international properties), commodity hedges (energy-related properties).

## Operational Hedging
Long-term leases (stabilized income stream), rental escalation (inflation protection), expense stop clauses (cost protection), insurance coverage (property and liability protection).
