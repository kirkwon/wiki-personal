# Intentionally Defective Grantor Trust (IDGT)

A trust that is defective for income tax purposes but not for estate tax purposes, allowing the grantor to pay income taxes while removing assets from estate.

## Structure

- Income tax defect: grantor treated as owner for income tax
- Assets removed from grantor's estate for estate tax
- Meets IRS grantor trust rules
- Irrevocable once created
- Assets transferred to beneficiaries at death

## Tax Benefits

- Estate tax savings by removing assets from taxable estate
- Grantor pays income taxes (not the trust)
- Step-up in basis at death
- Tax-free transfer of appreciated assets
- Annual exclusion gifts to fund trust

## Implementation Considerations

- Appreciated assets with growth potential preferred
- Income-producing assets favored
- Grantor must have sufficient resources to pay income taxes
- Proper IDGT documentation required
- Long-term holding strategy
