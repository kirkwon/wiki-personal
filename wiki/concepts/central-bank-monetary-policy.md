# Central Bank Monetary Policy

Central banks manage economies through monetary policy tools, primarily by adjusting interest rates and controlling money supply. In Dalio's economic machine, central banks are key actors that influence inflation, economic growth, and financial stability.

Key functions include:
- Setting interest rates to manage inflation and stimulate or cool economic activity
- Open market operations to control money supply
- Acting as lender of last resort during financial crises
- Communicating policy direction to shape market expectations

Understanding central bank actions is essential for anticipating changes in credit conditions, asset prices, and economic momentum.
