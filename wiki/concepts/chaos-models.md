# Chaos Models

Models of deterministic systems that are highly sensitive to initial conditions — the so-called "butterfly effect." Despite being fully deterministic, chaotic systems are effectively unpredictable beyond short time horizons.

## Key Properties

- **Sensitive dependence on initial conditions**: Tiny differences lead to vastly different outcomes
- **Deterministic but unpredictable**: No randomness in the model, yet prediction fails
- **Strange attractors**: Long-run behavior confined to structured but complex regions
- **Fractal structure**: Self-similarity at different scales

## Implications for Decision-Making

Chaos models illustrate fundamental limits to prediction, connecting to [[decision-making-under-uncertainty]] and the importance of robust strategies that perform well across many scenarios rather than optimizing for a single predicted future.
