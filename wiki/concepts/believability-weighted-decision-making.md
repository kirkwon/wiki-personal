# Believability-Weighted Decision Making

This process involves adjusting the weight given to an opinion based on the source's historical track record and proven expertise. Rather than treating all input equally, decision-makers assign higher credibility scores (or 'believability weights') to those whose past actions reliably predicted positive outcomes. This systematic approach mitigates the risk of being unduly influenced by charismatic but unreliable voices. Effective implementation requires maintaining transparent records of prediction accuracy for all involved parties.
