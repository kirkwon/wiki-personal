# Evolutionary Models

Models of how traits, strategies, and behaviors evolve over time through processes of variation, selection, and retention. These capture adaptation without requiring rational optimization.

## Core Mechanisms

- **Selection**: Successful strategies proliferate
- **Mutation**: New strategies introduced
- **Imitation**: Successful agents copied by others
- **Recombination**: Combining successful elements

## Key Insights

- Rational optimization is not necessary for adaptation
- Simple evolutionary dynamics can produce sophisticated strategies
- Co-evolution: agents adapt to each other creating arms races
- Fitness landscapes shape what strategies are reachable
