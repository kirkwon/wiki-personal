# Judgment

Judgment is the cognitive capacity to evaluate complex situations and form sound conclusions or decisions, particularly when faced with incomplete data or ambiguous information. It involves synthesizing knowledge from disparate sources, weighing potential outcomes, and assessing risk relative to reward. Effective judgment requires critical thinking skills coupled with practical experience, allowing an individual to act rationally despite uncertainty.
