# Persuasion

Persuasion is a universal mental model for navigating complex situations. It provides a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complexity by providing a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition
Persuasion is understood as a conceptual tool that aids in comprehending how influence operates across domains. It serves as a mental shortcut for recognizing patterns of influence and their effects on decision-making and behavior.

## Core Framework
The persuasion mental model is grounded in Robert B. Cialdini's six principles of influence:
- Reciprocity
- Commitment and consistency
- Social proof
- Authority
- Liking
- Scarcity

These principles describe the fundamental levers through which people are persuaded, and they map onto cognitive biases such as social proof, authority bias, and the endowment effect.
