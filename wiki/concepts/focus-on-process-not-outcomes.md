# Focus on Process, Not Outcomes

You can control your decisions but not the results.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## Key Principle

- Good process + bad luck ≠ bad decision.
- Bad process + good luck ≠ good decision.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## Practical Application

This principle connects to decision tracking [[decision-tracking]] and separating decision quality from outcome quality [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]]. By focusing on the process — the reasoning, the considerations, the risk management — rather than the outcome, you build better judgment over time regardless of short-term results.

## Connection to Luck and Risk

The luck and risk duality [[luck-and-risk-duality]] makes it even more important to evaluate decisions on their own merits rather than their results.
