# Limits

Limits is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

The Limits mental model functions as a universal pattern-recognition tool. By identifying boundaries, constraints, and carrying capacities, it enables better navigation of complexity across domains — whether biological, economic, cognitive, or organizational.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This mental model is referenced in Donella H. Meadows' *Limits to Growth: The 30-Year Update*, which examines planetary resource constraints through systems dynamics and feedback loops. Meadows' work demonstrates how understanding limits — on growth, resources, and systems capacity — is essential for making sense of long-term patterns and relationships.
