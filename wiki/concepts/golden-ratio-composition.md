# Golden Ratio Composition

The golden ratio is a mathematical ratio (1:1.618) found in nature and art. It is a more complex version of the rule of thirds and creates natural harmony in compositions.

## How to Apply
- **Spiral (Fibonacci)**: Place the subject on the spiral curve
- **Golden triangle**: Place the subject at the intersection of diagonal lines
- **Golden ratio grid**: Similar to rule of thirds but with different proportions

## When to Use
- Portraits: Place the eye on the golden ratio point
- Landscapes: Horizon at the golden ratio line
- Abstract: Create mathematical harmony

The golden ratio provides a more nuanced framework than the rule of thirds, offering slightly different placement points that align with natural aesthetic proportions.
