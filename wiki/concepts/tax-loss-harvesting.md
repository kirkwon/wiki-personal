# Tax-Loss Harvesting

Selling losing investment positions to offset capital gains, thereby reducing tax liability. This strategy involves tracking unrealized losses and timing sales to maximize the tax benefit while avoiding wash sale rules.

## Key Components
- **Loss Amount**: Current price minus purchase price for losing positions
- **Tax Benefit**: Loss amount multiplied by applicable tax rate
- **Wash Sale Rule**: Replacement securities cannot be purchased within 30 days before or after the sale
- **Tracking**: Maintaining records of harvest dates, replacement securities, and waiting periods

## Wash Sale Considerations
- 30-day period before/after sale must be monitored for replacement securities
- Waiting period: minimum 30 days
- Replacement securities must differ from the sold position
