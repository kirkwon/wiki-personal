# Visionary Leadership

Visionary leadership, as described by Senge, is the practice of cultivating a learning culture through being visionary, communicating effectively, encouraging experimentation, and building trust among employees. Leaders create the conditions for collective learning by articulating a compelling shared vision and modeling the behaviors that sustain a learning organization.

## Key Qualities
- Visionary: articulating a compelling future direction
- Communicative: sharing that vision clearly across the organization
- Experimental: encouraging risk-taking and learning from failure
- Trust-building: creating psychological safety for open dialogue

This form of leadership differs from command-and-control approaches by focusing on developing people and systems rather than directing them. It connects to [[learning-organization]] as the leadership style needed to sustain continuous transformation.
