# karpathy-methodology

The Karpathy methodology refers to a pedagogical approach to machine learning popularized by Andrej Karpathy. It emphasizes bridging the gap between complex theoretical concepts and practical, minimal code implementations. This method often involves building highly simplified, functional examples (like nanoGPT) that allow learners to grasp the core mechanics of large models without being overwhelmed by massive frameworks or boilerplate code. The goal is deep conceptual understanding through direct engineering practice.
