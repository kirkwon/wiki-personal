# intuición

(Intuition is the ability to understand something immediately, without the need for conscious reasoning or explicit study. It involves synthesizing complex information rapidly and arriving at a conclusion that feels instinctively correct. In psychology, it is often described as gut feeling—a non-linear cognitive process that allows people to make quick judgments based on accumulated experience. While sometimes viewed scientifically, it remains an interdisciplinary topic spanning philosophy, cognition, and behavioral science.)
