# Multiplier

A [[mental-model]] for understanding how certain leaders and thinkers amplify the intelligence and capability of those around them, creating environments where people contribute their best thinking. The concept contrasts with "Diminishers" — individuals who suppress talent, create anxiety, and underutilize the people around them.

## Core Framework

The Multiplier model, developed by Greg McKeown, identifies five disciplines that distinguish amplifying leaders from diminishing ones:

1. **Attract and optimize talent** — Multipliers seek out and fully utilize the intelligence of others, rather than hoarding credit or undervaluing contributions.
2. **Create intensity that demands best thinking** — They generate focused pressure that pushes teams to think deeply rather than just execute.
3. **Extend challenges** — Multipliers present challenges that stretch people beyond their comfort zones, assuming capability rather than limiting potential.
4. **Debate decisions** — They foster rigorous debate and diverse perspectives before committing to decisions.
5. **Give credit** — They ensure recognition flows to the team rather than concentrating it with themselves.

## Multiplier vs Diminisher

| Dimension | Multiplier | Diminisher |
|---|---|---|
| Talent utilization | Attracts and optimizes | Hoards and underutilizes |
| Thinking environment | Demands best thinking | Creates anxiety and compliance |
| Challenge scope | Extends challenges | Limits ambition |
| Decision process | Debates openly | Dictates decisions |
| Credit allocation | Gives credit to team | Takes credit for themselves |
