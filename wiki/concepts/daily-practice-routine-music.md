# Daily Practice Routine for Music

Effective music practice follows a structured routine of 30-60 minutes per day, emphasizing consistency over intensity.

## Daily Practice Structure (30-60 minutes)

1. **Warm-up (5-10 min)**: Scales, arpeggios
2. **Technique (10-15 min)**: Exercises, fingerings
3. **Theory Application (10-15 min)**: Chord progressions, ear training
4. **Repertoire (15-20 min)**: Learning songs, improvising

## Key Practice Principles

- **Consistency over intensity**: 30 minutes every day outperforms 2 hours once a week
- **Slow practice**: Practice slowly enough to play without mistakes
- **Focus on weaknesses**: Spend 70% of time on what you can't do yet
- **Use a metronome**: Develop steady rhythm

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Skipping fundamentals
- Playing too fast
- Not actively listening to music
- Only playing what you know
- Ignoring theory

These principles connect to broader learning strategies relevant to [[ear-training]] and skill development.
