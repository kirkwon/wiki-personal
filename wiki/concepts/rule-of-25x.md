# Rule of 25x Expenses

The Rule of 25x states that you need a portfolio worth 25 times your annual retirement expenses, assuming a 4% withdrawal rate.

## Calculation
Portfolio needed = Annual expenses ÷ 0.04

**Example**:
- Annual expenses: $60,000
- Portfolio needed: $60,000 × 25 = $1,500,000

## Adjustments
- Account for Social Security income (reduces portfolio needed)
- Example: $80k expenses, 20% savings rate → $64k needed; minus $25k Social Security → $39k from portfolio → $975k at 4%

## Relationship to 4% Rule
This rule derives directly from the [[4-percent-rule]]. A 4% withdrawal rate implies 25x portfolio coverage.
