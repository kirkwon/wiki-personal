# Overview

Overview is a universal mental model that serves as a conceptual tool for understanding and navigating complex situations. Rather than simple summarization, it provides a framework for recognizing patterns and relationships within complex systems, enabling users to see the bigger picture and make sense of interconnected dynamics.

This model is referenced in Donella H. Meadows' *Limits to Growth*, a foundational text on system dynamics and complex systems modeling. Meadows' work emphasizes how understanding the structure and behavior of systems—rather than just their individual components—allows for better navigation of complexity.

## Core Idea
Overview functions as a meta-framework: using "Overview" helps apply other models more effectively. Where [[recipes-mental-model]] breaks complexity into structured procedures, and [[planning]] uses pattern recognition to navigate situations, Overview provides the overarching lens to see how these frameworks relate to each other and to the situation at hand.

- Universal applicability across domains
- Focuses on pattern and relationship recognition
- Distinguishes itself from simple summarization by emphasizing navigation of complexity
