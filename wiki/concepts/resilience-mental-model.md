# Resilience as a Mental Model

Resilience in Manson's framework is the capacity to sustain meaningful action and maintain well-being in the face of a fundamentally flawed world. It is built through humility, gratitude, honest introspection, and the willingness to embrace failure as part of the process.

## Definition

Resilience as a mental model refers to the ability to maintain direction and purpose despite setbacks, uncertainty, and the inherent brokenness of systems and circumstances.

## Key Principles

- **Built through humility** – Recognizing one's limitations prevents brittle overconfidence.
- **Sustained by gratitude** – Gratitude provides perspective that counters the tendency toward despair.
- **Requires honest introspection** – Confronting self-deception patterns is necessary for genuine resilience.
- **Failure is integrated** – Resilience is not the absence of failure but the ability to learn from and persist through it.
- **Self-care as foundation** – Without structural self-care, resilience degrades over time.
