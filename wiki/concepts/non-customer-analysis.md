# Non-Customer Analysis

**Non-customer analysis** is the systematic process of identifying and targeting people who are not currently customers, arguing that unlocking new demand from non-customers is more valuable than stealing share from existing competitors. Most growth comes from non-customers rather than existing customers. The method examines three tiers of non-customers:

1. **Soon-to-be non-customers** — Those who are on the edge of the market and about to leave due to rising costs or declining offers.
2. **Refused non-customers** — Those who have consciously chosen against the industry's offerings and can articulate why.
3. **Unexplored non-customers** — Those who have never had the chance to buy the industry's offerings (e.g., in distant geographies or different economic segments).

By understanding why these groups refuse or avoid the industry, companies can identify the factors blocking new demand and reconstruct their value proposition accordingly. This approach aligns with the [[six-paths-framework]] and feeds into the [[strategic-sequence]] for validating blue ocean ideas.
