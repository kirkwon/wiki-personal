# Revaluation of All Values

Nietzsche's project to move beyond inherited morality toward life-affirming values. It is the central purpose of *Beyond Good and Evil*. This involves critically examining the psychological origins of moral concepts and creating new values rather than accepting traditional ones as given.

## Core Idea

Moral systems are human creations with psychological origins (resentment, will to power), not divine or rational necessities. Therefore, they can and should be reevaluated.

## What It Involves

- Questioning the assumption that existing moral values are universal, objective, or divinely ordained
- Tracing moral concepts to their historical and psychological origins through genealogical analysis
- Recognizing that master and slave moralities reflect different power positions rather than universal truths
- Creating new values that affirm life rather than merely reacting against existing ones

## Connection to Free Spirit

The revaluation of all values is the task of the free spirit—who questions deeply-held beliefs and creates personal values beyond conventional good and evil.

## Tension with Structured Decision Frameworks

Nietzsche's radical value revaluation contrasts with the wiki's emphasis on structured decision tools. However, the underlying insight — that values have hidden motivations and should be examined critically — connects to [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]] and [[systematic-decision-making]].
