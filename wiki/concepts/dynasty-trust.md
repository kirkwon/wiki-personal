# Dynasty Trust

A long-term irrevocable trust designed to preserve wealth across multiple generations, potentially lasting for centuries.

## Key Features

- Perpetual duration across generations
- Generation-skipping avoids multiple levels of estate tax
- Strong asset protection from creditors and divorces
- Control mechanisms via trustee and protector provisions
- Tax efficiency minimizes estate and income taxes

## Control Mechanisms

- **Trust Protector**: Can remove and replace trustee
- **Directed Trust**: Separates investment and distribution powers
- **Letter of Wishes**: Non-binding guidance to trustees
- **Trust Advisory Board**: Provides expertise and oversight
- **Dynasty Trust Amendment**: Limited amendment powers

## Jurisdiction Considerations

- States with long or perpetual trust duration
- Federal generation-skipping tax with annual exemption limits
- Different rules for perpetual trusts by state
- Trust law evolution over time
