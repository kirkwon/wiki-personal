# Swan Mental Model

Swan is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Swan.md]

The Swan mental model is a shorthand for the black swan concept itself—a rare, extreme, unpredictable event that dominates outcomes. Where "Swans" refers to the broader category and pattern, "Swan" refers to the recognition of any single instance: the moment an unforeseen event reshapes reality.^[The Black Swan - Nassim Nicholas Taleb.md]

## Definition

Swan functions as a universal mental model—applicable across all domains—for recognizing patterns and relationships in complex situations, particularly through the lens of rare or unexpected events.^[raw/articles/Swan.md]

## Distinction from "Swans"

- **Swans**: The general pattern that extreme events dominate outcomes.^[The Black Swan - Nassim Nicholas Taleb.md]
- **Swan**: The specific recognition that any given moment could be a black swan, requiring humility and preparedness.^[The Black Swan - Nassim Nicholas Taleb.md]

## Relationship to Black Swan Events

While the black-swan concept focuses specifically on rare, high-impact, unpredictable events, Swan is presented as a broader framework for pattern and relationship recognition. The distinction is not entirely clear from the source material, raising the question of whether Swan is a distinct mental model or a high-level abstraction of the black-swan concept itself.^[raw/articles/Swan.md]

## Practical Use

When evaluating decisions or systems, ask: what would happen if a black swan hit right now? This question shifts focus from optimization to robustness.^[The Black Swan - Nassim Nicholas Taleb.md]

## Connection to History

History provides the temporal framework for identifying swan-like events—those that appear predictable in retrospect but were unforeseeable in advance. The Black Swan critique warns against over-reliance on historical data when anticipating rare occurrences.^[History.md]
