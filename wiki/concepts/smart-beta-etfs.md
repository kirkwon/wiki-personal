# Smart Beta ETFs

Smart Beta ETFs are exchange-traded funds that implement factor-based investment strategies at low cost. They provide a simple alternative to direct stock picking for investors seeking factor exposure.

## Factor ETF Comparison

| ETF | Factor | Expense Ratio |
|-----|--------|---------------|
| VTV | Value (large cap) | 0.04% |
| MTUM | Momentum | 0.15% |
| VFH | Low volatility | 0.13% |
| VBR | Size + Value | 0.07% |
| QUAL | Quality | 0.15% |

## Implementation Approaches

1. **Direct Stock Picking** – Screen for stocks meeting factor criteria, buy top 20-50, rebalance on schedule. Full control but time-intensive.
2. **Smart Beta ETFs** – Select ETFs for desired factors, buy and hold, rebalance quarterly. Simple and low effort but less control.
3. **Factor-Based Mutual Funds** – Choose factor-based funds (e.g., AQR, Vanguard). Professional management but higher fees and less transparency.
