# Mexican Mole

A Mexican mole is a rich, complex sauce originating from Mexico, typically featuring ingredients like chili peppers, spices, nuts, and sometimes tomatoes or chocolate. It's often used to enhance the flavor of various dishes such as meats and vegetables. The process of creating mole can be seen as a cognitive exercise in layering complex tastes and textures, similar to composing an intricate photograph or managing diverse financial portfolios.
