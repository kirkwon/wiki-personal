# Sichuan Cuisine

Sichuan cuisine is a Chinese regional culinary tradition from Sichuan Province known for bold, complex flavors. It prominently features Sichuan peppercorns (providing a distinctive numbing sensation known as "má"), dried chilies ("là"), garlic, ginger, and fermented bean pastes. Classic dishes include mapo tofu, kung pao chicken, dan dan noodles, and hot pot, combining heat, numbness, and savory depth.
