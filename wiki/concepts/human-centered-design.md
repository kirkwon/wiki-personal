# Human-Centered Design
Human-Centered Design (HCD) is an approach to design that emphasizes understanding human needs and values. In the context of AI systems, HCD is essential for ensuring that AI systems are developed and deployed in a way that promotes wellbeing and minimizes harm.

### Key Principles
1. **Empathy**: Understanding human needs and values.
2. **Co-creation**: Involvement of stakeholders in the design process.
3. **Experimentation**: Testing and iteration of design solutions.
4. **Feedback**: Continuous feedback and evaluation of design solutions.

### Importance
HCD is crucial in AI systems as it helps to ensure that AI systems are developed and deployed in a way that promotes wellbeing and minimizes harm.
