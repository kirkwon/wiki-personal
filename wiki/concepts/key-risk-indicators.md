# Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)

Monitoring metrics used to track investment risk levels and trigger reassessment.

## Financial Metrics
DSCR, LTV, cash flow variance.

## Market Metrics
Vacancy rates, rent growth, absorption rates.

## Operational Metrics
Tenant turnover, maintenance costs.

## Economic Metrics
Employment growth, income trends.

## Monitoring Frequency
Daily (cash flow, rent collection), weekly (occupancy, maintenance), monthly (financial performance, market indicators), quarterly (portfolio review), annually (comprehensive assessment).
