# Analyzing Composition

Analyzing composition is the skill of understanding why a photograph works by examining its structural elements, eye movement, and techniques used.

## When Viewing Photos
1. Identify the main subject: What is the photo about?
2. Trace eye movement: How do you look at the photo?
3. Identify techniques used: Rule of thirds, leading lines, etc.
4. Notice balance: How is the photo balanced or unbalanced?
5. Consider color: How does color contribute?

## When Creating Photos
1. Identify the subject: What's the main subject?
2. Choose technique: Which composition technique works best?
3. Frame carefully: Check edges for distractions
4. Review in camera: Zoom in, check composition
5. Take multiple shots: Vary composition, choose the best

Developing this analytical skill helps photographers make more intentional composition choices.
