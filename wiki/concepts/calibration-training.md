# Calibration training

The practice of assessing whether your confidence levels match your actual accuracy. A well-calibrated forecaster who says they are 70% confident is correct about 70% of the time.

## Key Ideas

- Use calibration plots to visualize the gap between confidence and accuracy
- Track Brier scores or log-loss metrics over time
- Maintain prediction journals to review historical accuracy
- Simple models often calibrate better than complex expert judgments
- Calibration improves through feedback loops and learning from errors

## Tools

- Brier scores measure the accuracy of probability predictions
- Log-loss penalizes overconfident wrong predictions
- Calibration plots compare stated confidence vs actual accuracy

## Connection to Superforecasting

Calibration training is a core component of [[Superforecasting - Philip E Tetlock  Dan Gardner]], where forecasters train to express uncertainty accurately.
