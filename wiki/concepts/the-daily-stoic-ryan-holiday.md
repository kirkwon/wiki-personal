# The Daily Stoic - Ryan Holiday

Ryan Holiday's *The Daily Stoic* is a day-by-day guide to Stoic philosophy, distilling ancient principles into practical, actionable insights for navigating life's challenges. The book emphasizes controlling one's responses, accepting what cannot be changed, and maintaining equanimity through consistent practice.

## Core Ideas

- Stoicism as a universal mental model applicable across all domains
- The dichotomy of control as a foundational practice
- Negative visualization and premeditatio malorum for building resilience
- Virtue as the highest good and primary focus of action
- Present-moment awareness over rumination
- Daily Stoic exercises as a framework for building mental resilience
- Focus on internal responses rather than external events

## Connection to Other Sources

Holiday's *The Daily Stoic* bridges classical Stoic texts (Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius, Seneca) with contemporary mental models documented in the wiki, including [[stillness]], [[the-obstacle-is-the-way]], and [[effort]]. This perspective contributes to the resilience mental model by providing a structured, repeatable approach to developing capacity for handling difficulty.
