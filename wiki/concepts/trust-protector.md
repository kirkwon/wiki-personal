# Trust Protector

A special oversight role in trust administration with powers to ensure the trust is managed according to its purposes.

## Trust Protector Powers

- Remove and replace trustee
- Limited power to amend trust terms
- Add or remove beneficiaries
- Add or remove assets
- Oversight of investment strategy
- Input on distribution timing and amounts

## Protection Considerations

- Clear authorization of protector powers in trust document
- Protector should be independent of trustee and beneficiaries
- Appropriate expertise and experience
- Plan for protector succession
- Address potential conflicts of interest

## Implementation Strategies

- Letter of Wishes for non-binding guidance
- Directed trust splitting investment and distribution powers
- Trust Advisory Board for group expertise
- Family governance through family council
- Professional oversight involvement
