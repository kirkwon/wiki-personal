# Slave Morality

One half of Nietzsche's master vs. slave morality framework. Slave morality originates from resentment (*ressentiment*) of the powerless toward the powerful. Its values include meekness, equality, pity, humility, and submission.

## Characteristics

- Reactive: arises in response to the values of the master
- Life-denying: based on inversion and negation rather than affirmation
- Resentment-based: rooted in the psychological state of *ressentiment*
- Historically dominant: Nietzsche argues slave morality gained dominance through Christianity and modern culture

## Nietzsche's Critique

Nietzsche does not simply advocate for master morality over slave morality. Rather, he uses the distinction to reveal the psychological origins of moral concepts and to challenge the assumption that inherited values are objective truths.
