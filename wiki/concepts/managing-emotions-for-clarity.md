# Managing Emotions for Clarity

Strategies for managing emotions to improve cognitive processes and decision-making ability. Parrish connects emotional intelligence to clarity, arguing that unchecked emotions cloud judgment while deliberate emotional management enhances the quality of thought.

This concept relates to [[emotions-and-ai]] and [[emotion-and-intuition-in-decisions]], both of which explore the role of emotional intelligence in cognitive processes. It also intersects with [[overconfidence-and-certainty]], since emotional regulation helps prevent the overconfidence that impairs judgment under uncertainty.
