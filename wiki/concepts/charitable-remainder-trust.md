# Charitable Remainder Trust (CRT)

An irrevocable trust that pays income to non-charitable beneficiaries for a period, then distributes remaining assets to charitable beneficiaries.

## Types

- **Charitable Remainder Annuity Trust (CRAT)**: Fixed annuity payments
- **Charitable Remainder Unitrust (CRUT)**: Percentage of trust value payments
- **Flip CRT**: Starts as unitrust, converts to annuity trust
- **NIMCRUT**: Income-only with make-up provisions

## Tax Benefits

- Income tax deduction based on present value
- Capital gains avoidance on appreciated asset sales
- Estate tax deduction by removing assets from estate
- Lifetime income stream for beneficiaries
- Philanthropic goals supported

## Advantages

- Tax efficiency by avoiding capital gains taxes
- Lifetime income for beneficiaries
- Significant charitable impact
- Estate tax reduction

## Disadvantages

- Irrevocable once created
- Higher administrative complexity and costs
- Market risk affects payments
- Requires charitable commitment
