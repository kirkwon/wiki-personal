# BBQ Regions

American BBQ (barbecue) is more than cooking—it's culture, community, and regional pride. Each region has its own style: beef or pork, sauce or no sauce, tomato-based or vinegar. From Texas brisket to Carolina pulled pork, BBQ celebrates low-and-slow cooking.

## The Four Main BBQ Styles

- **Texas** — Beef brisket, dry rub, no sauce, oak or mesquite wood
- **Kansas City** — Beef and pork, sweet tomato-based sauce, burnt ends, hickory wood
- **Carolina** — Pork shoulder, vinegar-based sauce (Eastern: vinegar-only; Western: tomato-vinegar hybrid), pulled pork
- **Memphis** — Pork ribs, dry rub, sweet sauce, hickory wood

Each style reflects broader regional identity—Texas emphasizes minimalism and beef; KC embraces sweet, saucy richness; Carolina relies on vinegar acidity; Memphis balances dry rub with optional sweet glaze.
