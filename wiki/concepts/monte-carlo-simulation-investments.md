# Monte Carlo Simulation for Investments

Computational random sampling technique for modeling portfolio outcomes across multiple scenarios.

## Parameters
Variables include rent growth, vacancy, expenses, and appreciation. Distribution types are normal, lognormal, and uniform. Correlations between variables are modeled. 10,000+ iterations are run over 5-30 year horizons.

## Outputs
Probability distribution of possible outcomes, expected returns (mean return of all scenarios), risk measures (standard deviation, VaR, expected shortfall), and worst case scenarios (1%, 5%, 10% worst outcomes).

## Strategic Application
Used for decision-making (evaluate acquisition opportunities), portfolio construction (optimize risk/return profile), and hedging strategies (determine appropriate hedging levels).
