# Tax Optimization Templates

Suite of templates for tax planning and optimization across investment portfolios.

## Tax-Loss Harvesting Tracker
- Position-level loss tracking with harvest dates and tax benefits
- Wash sale considerations (30-day rule, replacement securities)

## Rental Property Tax Analysis
- Depreciation schedule (27.5-year, annual amounts, tax shields, cumulative)
- Section 1031 Exchange analysis (sale price, adjusted basis, depreciation recapture, capital gain, tax savings)

Relates to [[tax-torpedoes-in-retirement]] via RMD tax impact and to [[1031-exchange-strategy]] via deferred tax savings.
