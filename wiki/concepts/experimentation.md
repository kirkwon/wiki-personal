# Experimentation

Experimentation is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Experimentation.md]

## Definition

Experimentation serves as a universal mental model for recognizing patterns and relationships in complex situations. It functions as a mental shortcut for navigating uncertainty by treating engagement with the unknown as a form of inquiry rather than mere trial-and-error.

## Connection to World-Disclosure

Drawing on Sönke Ahrens' framework from *Experiment and Exploration: Forms of World-Disclosure*, experimentation is positioned as a form of world-disclosure—how we open up to understanding through deliberate trial and engagement.^[raw/articles/Experimentation.md] This perspective distinguishes experimentation from exploration: while both involve engaging with the unknown, experimentation emphasizes structured testing and observation, whereas exploration leans toward broader openness and discovery.
