# Living mental model

Living is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Living serves as a universal mental model applicable across all domains. It functions as a framework for pattern and relationship recognition in complex situations, offering a way to reduce cognitive load by identifying underlying structures and connections.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This model is primarily sourced from Greg McKeown's *Effortless*, where it is positioned alongside other universal mental models such as [[thinking-mental-model]], [[solutions-mental-model]], [[effort]], [[planning]], [[perspective]], [[care]], and [[minimalism]].

## Applicability

- **Universal**: Can be applied across all domains and contexts
- **Confidence**: 0.7 (moderate certainty in applicability)
- **Domains**: General

## Relationship to Other Mental Models

Living complements existing mental models in the wiki by framing life itself as a navigational tool. Where [[thinking-mental-model]] focuses on cognition and [[solutions-mental-model]] on structured approaches, Living emphasizes the broader pattern-recognition capacity that underlies effective navigation of complexity.
