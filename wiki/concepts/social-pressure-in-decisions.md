# Social Pressure in Decision-Making

Social pressure in decision-making describes how conformity, groupthink, and peer influence can lead people to behave irrationally in decision-making situations.

## Core Idea

Ariely investigates how social pressure can lead people to behave irrationally, including conformity, groupthink, and peer influence in decision-making situations.^[raw/articles/the-upside-of-irrationality---dan-ariely.md]

## Key Dynamics

- Conformity overrides individual judgment
- Groupthink suppresses dissenting opinions
- Peer influence distorts independent evaluation
- Social approval motivations override objective assessment

## Connection to Existing Concepts

This extends [[group-decision-polarization]] by focusing on individual susceptibility to social pressure, and connects to [[effective-listening]] (the difficulty of maintaining independent thinking under social influence).
