# The 4% Rule

A widely used retirement withdrawal guideline stating that you can safely withdraw 4% of your portfolio annually in retirement without running out of money over a 30-year period. This rule is foundational to the **Freedom Number** concept. It means you need 25x your annual expenses saved to be financially free.

## How It Works
- Multiply annual expenses by 25 to determine the portfolio size needed.
- Example: $60,000 annual expenses × 25 = $1.5 million needed.
- Withdraw 4% ($60,000) in year one, then adjust for inflation each subsequent year.
- At 7% return, saving $1,000/month reaches this in 26 years.

## Freedom Number Calculation

1. Track annual expenses (be honest)
2. Multiply by 25
3. That's your freedom number

## Alternative Approach

80% of current income as retirement target. Example: $100k income → need $80k/year → $2M needed ($80k × 25).

## Connection to Freedom Number
The Freedom Number is directly derived from the 4% Rule. Sethi uses this to reframe "rich" as having enough to live on your terms rather than hitting a specific dollar figure.
