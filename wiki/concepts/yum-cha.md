# Yum Cha (饮茶)

Yum Cha (饮茶) means "drinking tea" and refers to the Cantonese tradition of having dim sum brunch with tea service. It is the social and ceremonial framework surrounding dim sum dining. 饮茶（Yum Cha，饮茶，Yǐn chá）是粤式早茶文化的核心，意为"喝茶"，是 dim sum 体验中不可或缺的仪式。

## Components / 组成

- **Tea service**: Pot, cups, strainer, tea tray (茶壶、茶杯、滤网、茶盘)
- **Dim sum**: Multiple dishes, 3‑6 per person (多道 dim sum 小菜，每人 3‑6 道)
- **Lazy Susan / 圆桌转盘**: Rotating tray for sharing (转盘用于共享)
- **Family‑style**: Dishes shared among the table (家庭式分享用餐)

## Tea Pairing / 茶饮搭配

| 茶 | 特征 |
|----|------|
| 普洱 | 泥土味，助消化，搭配油腻点心 |
| 茉莉 | 花香，清淡，适合各类点心 |
| 菊花 | 花香，清凉解毒 |
| 乌龙 | 半发酵，芳香，百搭 |
| 绿茶 | 搭配清淡食物 |

## Etiquette / 礼仪

- Pour tea for elders first, then others (先为长辈斟茶，再为他人)
- Tap index and middle fingers to thank the pourer (用食指和中指轻敲桌面表示感谢斟茶者)
- Use chopsticks — never touch food with hands (使用筷子夹食，不直接用手接触食物)
- Rotate Lazy Susan clockwise only (转盘顺时针旋转)

## Weekend Brunch Tradition

饮茶是粤菜体验的重要组成部分，周末早茶是家庭聚会的传统形式。
