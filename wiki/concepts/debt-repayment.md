# Debt Repayment

Debt repayment is the process by which a borrower pays back money borrowed from a lender (such as a bank or individual). It typically involves regular payments that cover both the principal amount of the loan and accrued interest. The structure of repayment varies depending on the agreement, often following schedules like amortization to ensure timely clearance of the debt obligation. Effective repayment management is critical for maintaining positive credit standing and financial stability.
