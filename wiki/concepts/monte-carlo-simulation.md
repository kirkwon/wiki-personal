# Monte Carlo Simulation

A computational technique that models investment outcomes by running thousands of simulations with randomized variables to generate probability distributions of possible results.

## Parameters

- **Variables**: Rent growth, vacancy, expenses, appreciation
- **Distribution Types**: Normal, lognormal, uniform
- **Correlations**: Relationships between variables
- **Iterations**: 10,000+ simulations
- **Time Horizon**: 5-30 years

## Outputs

- Probability distribution of possible outcomes
- Expected returns (mean of all scenarios)
- Risk measures: standard deviation, VaR, expected shortfall
- Worst case scenarios (1%, 5%, 10% worst outcomes)

## Strategic Applications

- Decision making for acquisition opportunities
- Portfolio construction to optimize risk/return
- Determining appropriate hedging levels

Monte Carlo simulation extends [[value-at-risk-var]] by providing full outcome distributions rather than single-point estimates.
