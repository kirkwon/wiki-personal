# Wisdom

Wisdom is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Wisdom.md]

This mental model is applicable across all domains and serves as a universal sense-making framework. It functions as a higher-order lens through which specific theories such as the [[wisdom-of-crowds]] can be understood.^[raw/articles/Wisdom.md]

## Connection to The Wisdom of Crowds

James Surowiecki's [[the-wisdom-of-crowds]] is a key manifestation of this broader wisdom framework. The crowd wisdom mechanism—where collective intelligence can outperform individual experts—illustrates how wisdom operates through aggregation of diverse perspectives.^[raw/articles/Wisdom.md]

## Related Concepts

Wisdom connects to [[superforecasting]], which uses probabilistic thinking and diverse perspectives to make accurate predictions, and [[prediction-markets]], which formalize the aggregation of opinions into probability estimates.^[raw/articles/Wisdom.md]
