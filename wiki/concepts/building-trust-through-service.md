# Building Trust Through Service

Building trust through service is the practice of putting employees before customers in order to build trust within an organization. Sinek argues that when leaders serve their teams first, trust grows, teamwork improves, and performance follows.

## Key Ideas
- Employee-first cultures outperform customer-first cultures
- Trust is built through consistent, genuine care for team members
- Service to employees creates reciprocal loyalty and effort
- Examples: Southwest Airlines, Costco, Starbucks
