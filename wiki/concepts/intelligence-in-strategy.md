
# Intelligence in Strategy

The critical importance of information gathering, espionage, and understanding opponents as emphasized in Chapter 13 of *The Art of War*. Without intelligence, strategic success is unlikely.

## Key Ideas
- Spies and information gathering are essential to victory
- Understanding the enemy is prerequisite to effective strategy
- Continuous intelligence collection informs adaptation
- Misinformation and deception are reciprocal tools

## Connections
- Relates to [[attack-by-stratagem]] — stratagem depends on knowing the enemy
- Connects to [[weak-point-exploitation]] — intelligence reveals vulnerabilities
- Bridges to [[decision-making-frameworks]] — information as foundation for decision quality
- Complements [[recognition-primed-decision-model]] — pattern recognition from gathered intelligence

## Application
Applicable to competitive intelligence in business, understanding stakeholder motivations in negotiations, and any domain requiring situational awareness.
