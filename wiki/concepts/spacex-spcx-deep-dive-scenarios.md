## 1. THE VALUATION PROBLEM: Two Businesses, One Ticker

The xAI merger (Feb 2026) means SpaceX is really two completely different businesses trading inside SPCX:

### Sum-of-the-Parts (SOTP) Fair Value: ~$1.25T

| Segment | Fair Value | % of SOTP |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| **Starlink** (consumer, enterprise, direct-to-cell) | $602B | 48% |
| **Starship** (commercial launch, pre-revenue option value) | $170B | 14% |
| **Government/Defense contracts** | $123B | 10% |
| **Falcon 9/Heavy fleet** | $100B | 8% |
| **xAI/Grok** (AI business) | $258B | 21% |
| **Bitcoin treasury** (~$1.2B in BTC) | ~$1.2B | <1% |

**The gap:** Current market cap (~$2.5T) is **2x the SOTP fair value** ($1.25T). The premium exists because:
1. **Scarcity** — only 3-5% float
2. **Forced buying** — Nasdaq-100 + MSCI inclusion
3. **Musk premium** — retail enthusiasm, "first trillionaire" narrative
4. **Correlated upside pricing** — market assumes ALL segments hit bull case simultaneously

### The xAI Revenue Reality

| xAI Revenue Source | Monthly | Annual | Risk |
|--------------------|---------|--------|------|
| **Anthropic** (compute lease, Colossus) | $1.25B/mo | $15B/yr | **90-day termination clause** |
| **Google** (compute lease) | $920M/mo | $11B/yr | **90-day termination clause** |
| **Grok** (product revenue) | ~$42M/mo | ~$500M/yr | 1.6% paid conversion rate |

**Critical insight:** xAI's actual *product* revenue is only ~$500M/yr. The bulk ($26B/yr) is **GPU landlord revenue** from renting Colossus compute to rivals. Both contracts have 90-day termination clauses. If either Anthropic or Google cancels, the xAI valuation narrative collapses.

## 4. THE LOCK-UP CALENDAR — When Supply Hits

This is the most important SPCX-specific dynamic. The float goes from 4% → 58% in 6 months.

### Staggered Unlock Schedule

| Date | Event | Float Impact |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **~Mid-July 2026** | Q2 earnings (first public quarter) | 20% of locked shares unlock |
| **If SPCX >$175.50 for 5/10 days** | Performance trigger | +10% additional release |
| **Aug 21 (Day 70)** | Tranche 1 | +7% |
| **Sep 10 (Day 90)** | Tranche 2 | +7% |
| **Sep 25 (Day 105)** | Tranche 3 | +7% |
| **Oct 10 (Day 120)** | Tranche 4 | +7% |
| **Oct 25 (Day 135)** | Tranche 5 | +7% |
| **~Mid-Oct 2026** | Q3 earnings | +28% release |
| **Dec 8-9, 2026 (Day 180)** | Full release | Float reaches ~58% |
| **June 12-13, 2027 (Day 366)** | Musk's 42% stake unlocks | Final unlock |

### How Lock-Up Interacts with Nasdaq-100

**The stair-step mechanism:**
1. Each lock-up tranche expires → float increases
2. Higher float → higher Nasdaq-100 weight (float-adjusted index)
3. Higher weight → **another wave of forced passive buying**
4. This partially offsets the selling pressure from unlocked shares

**But:** The self-reinforcing loop (price ↑ → weight ↑ → more buying → price ↑) works in BOTH directions. If the initial selling pressure from unlocks pushes price down, it creates a negative feedback loop.

### Is There a Tradeable Short?

**Not a simple directional short.** Each supply flood is met by forced mechanical buying. Instead, the edge is in:
- **Options volatility** — dealers forced to dynamically hedge both directions
- **Gamma exposure (GEX) tracking** — squeeze risk in both directions
- **Calendar spreads** around specific unlock dates
- **Not shorting the stock itself** — the Musk premium + retail floor makes pure shorts dangerous
