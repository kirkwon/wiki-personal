# Pattern Analysis in Digital Life

Pattern analysis is the practice of understanding recurring habits, behaviors, and workflows that shape one's digital experience. Tiago Forte emphasizes this as a critical step in mastering digital life—by identifying consistent patterns, individuals can make targeted improvements rather than pursuing wholesale system overhauls.

This concept relates to [[naturalistic-decision-making]] and the [[recognition-primed-decision-model]], which both focus on how people recognize patterns in their environment to make effective decisions. Understanding digital behavior patterns enables more efficient system design and helps avoid repeating ineffective workflows.

Pattern analysis also connects to [[systematic-decision-making]], as reassessing systems requires identifying which patterns are serving goals and which are creating friction. In the PARA framework, pattern recognition informs how content is categorized and how workflows are streamlined.
