# Experiences

Experiences is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Experiences functions as a universal mental model — a navigational principle applicable across all domains. It positions lived experience as a pattern-recognition mechanism, helping individuals identify recurring relationships and structures in complex environments.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This model is referenced in [[range-david-epstein]] and [[quit-the-power-of-knowing-when-to-walk-away---annie-duke]], suggesting a common thread: experiential learning and the strategic value of accumulated experience. Epstein emphasizes breadth of experience and cross-domain learning, while Duke highlights the importance of knowing when to disengage based on accumulated experience.
