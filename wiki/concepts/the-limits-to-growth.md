# The Limits to Growth

*The Limits to Growth* (1972) is the landmark study co-authored by Meadows and colleagues that used computer modeling to explore how resource constraints and exponential growth interact in global systems. It examined carrying capacity, resource depletion, pollution, and population dynamics to project possible futures for the planet.

While initially controversial, the study's core insight — that exponential growth within finite systems must eventually encounter limits — remains foundational to systems thinking. Meadows' later work in *Thinking in Systems* builds directly on these ideas, connecting resource constraints to feedback loop dynamics and systemic resilience.
