# Mindfulness

Mindfulness is framed here as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. Unlike traditional definitions that focus on meditation or stress reduction, this framing positions mindfulness as a mental shortcut applicable across all domains of life.

## Definition
Mindfulness provides a framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world. It serves as a cognitive lens for understanding how things connect and interact, enabling clearer navigation of ambiguity and complexity.

## Relationship to Existing Concepts
- [[stillness]] shares authorship through Ryan Holiday but is a distinct concept focused on the value of quiet reflection.
- [[attention-budget]] and [[hyperfocus]] / [[scatterfocus]] concern attention management specifically, while mindfulness here is broader—functioning as a pattern-recognition tool rather than a focus technique.
- [[thinking-mental-model]] and [[overview]] are adjacent universal mental models that also deal with pattern recognition in complex situations.

## Confidence and Evidence
The evidence base is minimal—one cross-source reference from Ryan Holiday's *The Daily Stoic*—with confidence rated at 0.7 and priority medium. The abstract framing may overlap with other mental models already in the wiki, and the distinction between mindfulness-as-pattern-tool and traditional mindfulness practice remains an open question.
