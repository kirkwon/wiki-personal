# Prep Techniques

Prep techniques encompass the preparatory steps that occur before cooking begins. These foundational steps include cutting, marinating, brining, portioning, and organizing ingredients for efficient cooking. These methods affect how ingredients respond to heat and seasoning, and how flavors develop during cooking.

Proper prep is essential for achieving consistent results and often determines the success of a dish before any heat is applied. It also enables improvisation during the cooking process.

## Overview
Preparation techniques include:
- Cutting and shaping
- Marinating and brining
- Portioning
- Mise en place (preparation and organization)
- Blanching and parboiling

## Relationship to Other Categories
Prep techniques work alongside [[heat-based-techniques]] and [[seasoning-methods]] to form the complete cooking process. Prep techniques connect to broader concepts like [[ingredient-pairings]] and [[seasoning-methods]], as the way ingredients are prepared affects how flavors develop during cooking. Proper prep enables better heat distribution and flavor penetration during cooking.
