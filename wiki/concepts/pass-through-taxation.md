# Pass-Through Taxation

Pass-through taxation is a tax structure where business income is taxed at the owner level rather than at the entity level, avoiding double taxation.

## How It Works
In pass-through entities (LLC, S Corporation, partnership), the business itself does not pay income tax. Instead, profits and losses "pass through" to the owners' personal tax returns. Owners report their share of income on their individual returns and pay tax at their personal marginal rates.

## Entity Types with Pass-Through Taxation
- **LLC**: Default pass-through treatment; can elect corporate taxation
- **S Corporation**: Pass-through by election (Form 2553)
- **General Partnership**: Automatic pass-through
- **Limited Partnership**: Automatic pass-through
- **LLP**: Automatic pass-through

## Advantages
- No double taxation
- Simpler tax filing for small businesses
- Tax benefits can be allocated to owners with lower tax rates
- Self-employment tax optimization strategies available (especially with S Corps)

## Disadvantages
- Self-employment tax applies to net earnings in LLCs and partnerships
- Less flexibility in retained earnings compared to C Corps
- Income must be reported even if not distributed
