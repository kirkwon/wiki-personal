# Fundamental Attribution Error

The fundamental attribution error is the tendency to overemphasize personality traits and underemphasize situational factors when explaining others' behavior. We attribute actions to character rather than circumstance.

## Examples
- "He's lazy" vs. "He might be exhausted from caregiving"
- Assuming a colleague is uninterested rather than overwhelmed

## Mitigation
- Consider context and circumstances before judging character
- Ask what situational factors might explain the behavior

## Connection to Other Concepts
Related to [[halo-effect]], [[in-group-bias]], and [[communication-skills]]. In relationships, this bias leads to blaming personality rather than circumstances.
