# Biases in Management

Common cognitive biases that affect management and organizational decisions.

## Key Biases
- **Planning Fallacy**: Underestimate project timelines and budgets
- **Sunk Cost**: Continue failing projects due to past investment
- **Halo Effect**: Assume a charismatic leader is competent in all areas
- **Survivorship Bias**: Copy only successful companies while ignoring failures

## Mitigation
- Use reference class forecasting for timelines
- Evaluate projects on future prospects, not past investment
- Blind evaluation of candidates
- Diverse teams to reduce groupthink
