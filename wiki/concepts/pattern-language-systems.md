# Pattern Language Systems

Pattern language refers to reusable solutions—or guidelines for design—for recurring problems encountered in human habitation and architecture. Rather than providing rigid blueprints, a pattern describes a successful response to a specific context or need (e.g., "The Alley" or "Sunspace"). These patterns help designers move beyond conventional approaches by organizing potential solutions into typological categories that can be combined and adapted for unique environments. The goal is to guide the creation of spaces that support human well-being, promoting intimacy, individuality, and community connection.
