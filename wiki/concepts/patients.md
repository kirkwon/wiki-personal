# Patients

Patients is a universal conceptual tool or mental model for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It provides a mental shortcut for making sense of the messiness and imperfection inherent in real-world situations, drawing on the virtue of patience and the perspective of careful, sustained observation.

## Definition

At its core, the Patients model suggests that dealing with imperfect, complex situations requires a patient stance—accepting uncertainty, observing carefully, and resisting premature closure. This applies not only to literal patients in medicine but to any situation or person requiring sustained attention and tolerance for ambiguity.

## Origin

The model is referenced from Atul Gawande's Complications: A Surgeon's Notes on an Imperfect Science, where Gawande explores how medicine and life involve imperfect information, delayed outcomes, and the need to stay with uncertainty rather than rush to resolution.

## Connections to Other Mental Models

- [[minimalism]] – Simplifying engagement with complexity through patient observation

- [[effort]] – Sustained effort sustained through patient engagement
