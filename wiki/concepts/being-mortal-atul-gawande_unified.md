# Being Mortal - Atul Gawande

_by Atul Gawande_

## Chapters

### Chapter 1: The Paradox of Change
Brief description of how our modern healthcare system often conflicts with the natural processes of aging, resulting in a paradoxical situation where we try to extend life at all costs.

### Chapter 2: The End Game
Exploration of various approaches and considerations when planning for end-of-life care and making difficult decisions about quality versus quantity of life.

### Chapter 3: The Good Death
Analysis of the elements that contribute to a meaningful death, including autonomy, comfort, dignity, and family involvement in the dying process.

### Chapter 4: Choices and Consequences
Examination of how our choices about care can shape our lives and affect those who are left behind after we pass away.

### Chapter 5: The Costs of Care
Discussion on the economic burden that an aging population places on society, particularly in healthcare expenditure related to end-of-life services.

### Chapter 6: Rituals and Routines
The importance of rituals, routines, and symbolic gestures as tools for coping with mortality and preserving meaning during the final stages of life.

### Chapter 7: The Gift of Time
A look at how time can be better utilized by focusing on what's truly important rather than trying to extend every minute of our lives artificially through medical intervention.

### Chapter 8: A Culture Change
An overview of the need for systemic changes in healthcare, including shifting from a "cure-centric" mindset to one that values comfort and quality of life at all stages of aging.
