# kaiseki

Kaiseki is a traditional Japanese multi-course haute cuisine that emphasizes seasonal ingredients, artful presentation, and balance of flavors. It originated in the imperial court as a formal dining experience but has evolved into an intricate culinary art form appreciated worldwide. Each kaiseki meal is meticulously composed to reflect both the natural beauty of foods and their seasonality, often accompanied by sake or other traditional beverages.
