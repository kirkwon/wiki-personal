# Financial Reporting Templates

A modular suite of investment tracking and financial analysis tools covering real estate, stocks, bonds, and portfolio management. The templates provide structured frameworks for calculating returns, managing tax optimization, and making better investment decisions.

## Core Templates
- **Single Property Analysis**: Income, expenses, cash flow, depreciation, tax impact for individual holdings
- **Multi-Property Portfolio Tracking**: Aggregated metrics across property portfolios
- **Investment Property Acquisition**: Property evaluation, financial projections, sensitivity analysis
- **Stock and Bond Investment Trackers**: Position tracking, dividend tracking, bond yield analysis
- **Portfolio Performance Template**: Asset allocation, performance by asset class, weighted returns
- **Retirement Savings Calculator**: Projected growth, retirement income planning
- **RMD Calculator**: Life expectancy factor methodology, tax impact of distributions
- **Tax-Loss Harvesting Tracker**: Wash sale tracking, tax benefit calculation
- **Rental Property Tax Analysis**: Depreciation schedule, Section 1031 exchange analysis

## Key Metrics
- Cash on Cash Return = Net Cash Flow / Cash Investment
- Cap Rate = NOI / Property Value
- NOI = Gross Income - Operating Expenses
- Break-Even Ratio = Operating Expenses / Gross Income
- Debt Coverage Ratio = NOI / Annual Debt Service
- IRR via cash flow ranges

## Reporting Cadences
Daily transaction entry, weekly portfolio updates, monthly comprehensive reporting, quarterly rebalancing, annual tax preparation.
