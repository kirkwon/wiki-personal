# Motion Blur and Freezing Motion

Motion blur and freezing motion are controlled by shutter speed.

- **Freezing motion**: Fast shutter speeds (1/1000s–1/2000s) stop action; ideal for sports, wildlife, and street photography
- **Intentional motion blur**: Slow shutter speeds (1/15s–30") create streaks and blur; used for artistic effect, night photography, and light painting

See [[shutter-speed]] and [[exposure-triangle]] for detailed guidance.
