# HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training)

High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT) is an exercise method involving short bursts of maximum-intensity effort alternated with periods of rest or low-intensity activity. Ferriss advocates HIIT as a time-efficient alternative to traditional steady-state cardio, arguing it delivers superior results in fat loss and fitness with significantly less time investment.

## Key Points
- HIIT is efficient, effective, and time-saving compared to conventional cardio.
- Sessions can be completed in a fraction of the time required for traditional workouts.
- The approach fits into Ferriss's broader philosophy of optimizing health with minimal time expenditure.
