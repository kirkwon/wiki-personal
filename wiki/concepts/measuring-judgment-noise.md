# Measuring Judgment Noise

Measuring noise involves quantifying the variability in judgments across decision-makers or across time for the same decision-maker.

## Statistical Approaches

- Having multiple experts evaluate the same set of cases independently
- Computing the variance or standard deviation of their judgments
- Comparing inter-rater reliability across domains

## Domains Where Noise Has Been Measured

- **Medicine**: Variation in diagnosis and treatment recommendations for identical patient presentations
- **Criminal Justice**: Sentencing disparities for similar offenses across judges
- **Finance**: Divergence in investment evaluations for comparable assets

## What Measurement Reveals

Noise measurement often reveals that experts who believe they are consistent are actually highly variable. This invisibility is part of what makes noise such a persistent problem — people are not aware of their own inconsistency.
