# Cryptocurrency Investments

Cryptocurrency includes digital assets using blockchain technology — Bitcoin (store of value), Ethereum (smart contracts), and stablecoins (pegged to USD). It offers diversification from traditional markets, high return potential, and accessibility without accredited investor requirements, but carries extreme volatility, regulatory uncertainty, security risks, and tax complexity.

## Major Cryptocurrencies
- Bitcoin: Digital gold, store of value
- Ethereum: Smart contract platform
- Stablecoins: USDC, DAI (pegged to USD)

## Key Risks
- Extremely volatile price swings
- Regulatory uncertainty and potential bans
- Security risk: hacks, lost keys
- No fundamentals (speculative)
- Tax complexity: FIFO/Specific ID cost basis, detailed reporting required

## Platforms
- Coinbase, Kraken for trading
- Ledger, Trezor for self-custody
- DeFi (Uniswap, Aave) — high risk

## Recommended Allocation
0-10% of alternative investments (speculative portion).
