# Wellbeing

Wellbeing is a broad, multi-dimensional state encompassing physical health, mental stability, emotional satisfaction, and social connection. It goes beyond the mere absence of illness, focusing instead on optimal functioning and overall quality of life. Modern definitions emphasize that wellbeing requires balance across various domains—including professional fulfillment, relational support, and personal growth—and is often studied within psychology and public health.
