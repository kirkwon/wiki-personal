# Difference Equation Models

Models that describe how systems change over discrete time steps. The state at time t+1 is a function of the state at time t.

## Key Features

- Discrete time steps (not continuous)
- Can exhibit complex behavior from simple rules
- Include logistic maps, cobweb models, and iteration schemes
- Bridge between static models and dynamic systems

## Connection to Other Models

Difference equation models are discrete analogs of [[differential-equation-models]]. They connect to [[cellular-automata]] (which are a specific type of difference equation on a grid).
