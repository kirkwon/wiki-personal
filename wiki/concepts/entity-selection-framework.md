# Entity Selection Framework

A systematic decision framework for selecting the appropriate business entity type based on liability exposure, income level, growth plans, and ownership structure.

## Decision Factors

### Liability Assessment
- Business type (high-risk vs. low-risk industries)
- Customer interaction level
- Employee count
- Contractual obligations
- Regulatory environment

### Tax Considerations
- Income level and self-employment tax optimization
- Pass-through vs. double taxation preferences
- Retirement planning options
- State tax differences
- Depreciation benefits by entity type

### Operational Complexity
- Administrative burden assessment
- Formation costs ($50 to $2,000+)
- Annual compliance requirements
- Professional fees (legal, accounting)
- Time commitment for compliance

## Decision Matrix

**Step 1: Liability Assessment**
- High Liability Exposure → Limited Liability Entities (LLC, Corp)
- Low Liability Exposure → Sole Proprietorship or Partnership

**Step 2: Income Level**
- High Income ($100k+) → S Corp or C Corp
- Moderate Income ($50k-$100k) → LLC or S Corp
- Low Income (<$50k) → LLC or Sole Proprietorship

**Step 3: Growth Plans**
- Rapid Growth/Venture Capital → C Corp
- Steady Growth/Small Business → LLC or S Corp
- Lifestyle Business → Sole Proprietorship or Partnership

**Step 4: Ownership Structure**
- Multiple Owners → LLC, Partnership, or S Corp
- Single Owner → Sole Proprietorship, LLC, or S Corp
- Large Number of Owners → C Corp or REIT

## Comparative Analysis

| Entity Type | Liability Protection | Tax Complexity | Formation Cost | Compliance Burden | Growth Potential |
|-------------|---------------------|----------------|--------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Sole Proprietorship | None | Low | Very Low | Low | Low |
| General Partnership | None | Low | Low | Medium | Medium |
| Limited Partnership | Limited for LPs | Medium | Medium | Medium-High | High |
| LLP | Limited for all partners | Medium | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| LLC | Full protection | Medium | Medium | Medium | High |
| S Corp | Full protection | Medium-High | Medium-High | High | Medium-High |
| C Corp | Full protection | High | High | High | Very High |
