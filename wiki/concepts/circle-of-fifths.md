# Circle of Fifths

The Circle of Fifths is the organizing principle for key signatures in Western music. It arranges all major and minor keys in a circle where each adjacent key differs by one sharp or flat.

## Major Keys

|-----|--------------|-------|
| C Major | 0 | C D E F G A B |
| G Major | 1♯ | G A B C D E F♯ |

| D♭ Major | 5♭ | D♭ E♭ F G♭ A♭ B♭ C |
| G♭ Major | 6♭ | G♭ A♭ B♭ C♭ D♭ E♭ F |
| C♭ Major | 7♭ | C♭ D♭ E♭ F♭ G♭ A♭ B♭ |

The Circle of Fifths provides a systematic reference for identifying key signatures, understanding relationships between keys, and practicing scales and chord progressions.
