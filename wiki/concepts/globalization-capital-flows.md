# Globalization and Capital Flows

Globalization refers to the increasing interconnectedness of international markets through trade, capital flows, and exchange rate dynamics. In Dalio's economic machine, globalization is a key force that amplifies the transmission of economic conditions across borders.

Capital flows move between countries seeking higher returns, affecting exchange rates, asset prices, and economic stability. Understanding these relationships is crucial for investors navigating complex international markets.

Key dynamics include trade patterns, cross-border investment, currency movements, and how shocks in one economy propagate globally.
