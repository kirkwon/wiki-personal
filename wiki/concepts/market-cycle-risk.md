# Market Cycle Risk

Risk arising from positioning in different phases of economic and real estate market cycles.

## Cycle Phases
- Expansion: Favorable for acquisitions, growth opportunities
- Peak: Risk of overvaluation, market correction
- Contraction: Increased vacancies, declining values
- Trough: Buying opportunities, distressed properties

## Positioning Strategies
Expansion: aggressive acquisition, leverage, development. Peak: hold, reduce leverage, prepare for downturn. Contraction: preserve capital, maintain liquidity. Trough: selective acquisition, distressed opportunities.
