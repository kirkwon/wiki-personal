# International Business Company (IBC)

An International Business Company is an offshore entity commonly formed in Caribbean jurisdictions for international business operations.

## Overview
- Popular structure in Caribbean jurisdictions
- Designed for international trade and investment
- Available in various offshore jurisdictions

## Considerations
- Tax compliance requirements (FBAR, FATCA)
- Substance requirements in many jurisdictions
- Reporting obligations for international information
- Professional advice essential due to complexity
