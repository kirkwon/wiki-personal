# ciencia-cognitiva

(Cognitive science is an interdisciplinary field that studies the mind and intelligence, aiming to understand how humans acquire knowledge, reason, perceive, and communicate. It synthesizes theories and methods from multiple domains, including psychology, artificial intelligence, linguistics, philosophy, and neuroscience. This approach treats cognition as a fundamental process of computation, exploring everything from memory structures to language processing.)
