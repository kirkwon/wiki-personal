# Cap Rate (Capitalization Rate)

The capitalization rate is a core real estate valuation metric calculated as Net Operating Income (NOI) divided by property value.

## Formula

**Cap Rate = NOI / Property Value**

## Typical Ranges

- Residential properties: 4-8%
- Commercial properties: 6-12%
- High-demand markets: lower cap rates
- Lower-demand markets: higher cap rates

## Role in Analysis

The cap rate is used in the [[single-property-analysis-template]] and [[multi-property-portfolio-template]] to assess investment returns independent of financing structure. It is one of several metrics tracked alongside [[cash-on-cash-return]] and [[net-operating-income]] in real estate investment analysis.

## Limitations

Does not account for financing, appreciation, or tax benefits. Should be used alongside other metrics like debt coverage ratio and break-even ratio for comprehensive evaluation.
