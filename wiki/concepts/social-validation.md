# Social Validation
Social validation is the process of explaining a mental model to someone else and hearing their objections. This process forces articulation, reveals gaps in reasoning, and provides an opportunity to refine the mental model.

## Introduction
Social validation is an essential component of validating mental models. This page provides an overview of social validation, including its definition, importance, and applications.

## Definition
Social validation is the process of explaining a mental model to someone else and hearing their objections. This process involves presenting the mental model to a group of people, receiving their comments and suggestions, and refining the model based on their feedback.

## Importance
Social validation is important because it provides an opportunity to test the mental model against different perspectives, identify areas for improvement, and refine the model to make it more accurate and reliable.

## Applications
Social validation has a wide range of applications, including business, economics, politics, and personal decision-making. It can be used to validate mental models, identify opportunities and risks, and develop effective strategies.
