# Design for Complex Change

Meadows describes strategies for designing effective change initiatives within complex systems. Key considerations include:

- **System boundaries** — Defining what is inside and outside the system being addressed.
- **Leverage points** — Targeting interventions at high-impact locations within the system structure.
- **Feedback loops** — Understanding how the change will be reinforced or counteracted by existing loop structures.

Effective design accounts for nonlinear dynamics, delays, and unintended consequences that arise when intervening in interconnected systems. This approach connects to [[scenario-planning]] and [[systematic-decision-making]] by emphasizing structural understanding over isolated action.
