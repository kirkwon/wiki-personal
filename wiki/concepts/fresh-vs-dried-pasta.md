# Fresh vs. Dried Pasta

The distinction between fresh (pasta fresca) and dried (pasta secca) pasta is fundamental to Italian cooking.

## Fresh Pasta (Pasta Fresca)

- Soft, tender texture
- Cooks quickly: thin pasta 1-2 minutes, thick 2-3 minutes, stuffed 3-5 minutes
- Made with flour, eggs, and salt
- Ideal for stuffed pasta and special occasions
- Regional centers: Bologna for fresh pasta, Genoa for pesto

## Dried Pasta (Pasta Secca)

- Firm texture, cooked al dente
- Shelf-stable, longer cooking times
- Made primarily with durum wheat semolina and water
- Everyday cooking staple
- Regional center: Naples for dried pasta

## Making Fresh Pasta

Basic recipe: flour (all-purpose or semolina), eggs (2 per cup of flour), pinch of salt. Mound flour, make a well, crack eggs in, mix gradually incorporating flour, knead 10 minutes until smooth, rest 30 minutes, roll thin, cut into desired shape.
