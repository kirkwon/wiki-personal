# De Bono's Pointers

De Bono's Pointers are key guidelines for effective thinking, including focusing on the task at hand, challenging assumptions, and considering multiple perspectives. These pointers serve as meta-level reminders that keep thinking productive and open.

They align with the principles behind [[six-thinking-hats]], which structurally enforces multiple perspectives through colored hats. Together, the pointers and the hats framework provide both the philosophical stance (be open, question assumptions) and the operational mechanism (rotate perspectives systematically) for improving creative and decision-making outcomes.
