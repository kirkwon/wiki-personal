# Net Operating Income (NOI)

Net Operating Income is gross rent minus operating expenses, before debt service. It is the foundational metric for real estate investment analysis.

## Formula

**NOI = Gross Rental Income - Vacancy - Credit Loss - Operating Expenses**

Operating expenses include property taxes, insurance, HOA fees, property management, maintenance, repairs, utilities, vacancy allowance, and CapEx reserve.

## Role

NOI serves as the numerator in key metrics:
- [[cap-rate]]: NOI / Property Value
- [[cash-on-cash-return]]: (NOI - Debt Service) / Cash Investment
- [[debt-coverage-ratio]]: NOI / Annual Debt Service
- Break-Even Ratio: Operating Expenses / Gross Income

## Tracking

NOI is tracked in the [[single-property-analysis-template]] and [[multi-property-portfolio-template]] as part of monthly and annual performance reporting.
