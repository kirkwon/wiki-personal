# Keystone Habits

Keystone habits are high-leverage behaviors that, once changed, catalyze positive transformation across other areas of life. Charles Duhigg describes them as the "art of small wins" — modest habit changes that create a cascade of further improvements through a sense of progress and capability.

## How They Work

Keystone habits create a sense of success that reinforces further positive change. They often involve small wins that build momentum, self-discipline, or identity shifts. Not all habits are equal — identifying keystone habits allows you to focus on changes that propagate through your entire system of behaviors.

## Examples

Common examples include exercise routines, which often lead to better eating habits, improved productivity, and reduced stress. Other examples include organizing one's environment, developing a reading habit, daily stand-up meetings, or structured feedback routines that build trust and communication over time.
