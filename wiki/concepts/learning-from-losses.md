# Learning from Losses

Learning from losses is the practice of extracting wisdom and growth experiences from difficult situations or failed projects. Duke argues that losses provide valuable learning opportunities that success alone cannot offer, and that reframing failure as information prevents the emotional paralysis that blocks quitting decisions.

This connects to [[embracing-failure-for-personal-growth]] and [[inattention-to-results]] — when we avoid honest assessment of what went wrong, we lose the ability to recognize patterns that would inform future quitting decisions. The concept also relates to [[decision-tracking]], where recording and reviewing past decisions (including quits) builds a foundation for better future choices.
