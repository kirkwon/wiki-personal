
# Content Curation

Content curation is the practice of filtering, organizing, and maintaining a focused body of information to reduce cognitive load and support effective knowledge work. Tiago Forte emphasizes curation as a core activity in maintaining a second brain, arguing that selectively retaining relevant content is more valuable than indiscriminately collecting information.

Effective curation involves regularly reviewing stored content, removing outdated or irrelevant material, and ensuring that what remains is actionable and connected to current projects and interests. This practice relates to [[categorizing-information]] and [[active-revision-techniques]] as complementary methods for keeping a personal knowledge system effective.
