# Value Factor

The value factor is based on the premise that stocks trading cheap relative to their fundamentals tend to outperform growth stocks over time.

## Metrics

Key valuation ratios used to identify value stocks:
- **P/E Ratio** – Price to earnings (lower = cheaper)
- **P/B Ratio** – Price to book value
- **P/S Ratio** – Price to sales
- **EV/EBITDA** – Enterprise value to EBITDA
- **Free Cash Flow Yield** – FCF / Market cap

## Implementation

1. Screen for top decile by value metrics
2. Equal-weight or market-cap weight the selection
3. Rebalance annually

## Risks

- **Value traps** – Stocks that appear cheap for good reason (fundamental deterioration)
- **Extended underperformance** – Value can lag for 3-10 years during growth-dominated periods
