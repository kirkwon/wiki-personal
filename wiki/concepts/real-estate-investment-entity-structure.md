# Real Estate Investment Entity Structure

Recommended multi-entity structure for real estate investors that provides asset protection, liability isolation, and tax efficiency.

## Three-Level Structure

### Level 1: Holding Company
- **Entity Type**: LLC or C Corp
- **Purpose**: Own and manage multiple properties
- **Benefits**: Centralized management, liability isolation across properties, estate planning advantages
- **Tax Treatment**: Pass-through (LLC) or corporate (C Corp)

### Level 2: Property-Specific Entities
- **Entity Type**: Separate LLCs for each major property
- **Purpose**: Isolate risk for individual properties
- **Benefits**: Bankruptcy-remote entities, creditor isolation, flexible sale terms
- **Liability**: Limited to individual property assets
- **Tax Treatment**: Pass-through taxation

### Level 3: Operating Company
- **Entity Type**: LLC (member-managed)
- **Purpose**: Property management and operations (leasing, maintenance, tenant relations)
- **Relationship**: Contractual relationship with holding companies
- **Benefits**: Professional management, liability protection, scalability

## Why This Structure Works
- If one property faces a lawsuit, only that property's LLC is at risk
- Holding company protects other properties and personal assets
- Operating company isolates day-to-day liability from ownership
- Facilitates 1031 exchanges and estate planning
- Enables strategic financing and sale of individual properties
