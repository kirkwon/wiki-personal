# Basil Storage Methods

Proper storage extends basil's usable life while preserving flavor. Different methods suit different use cases.

## Refrigerator Storage

Place basil stems in a glass of water, cover loosely with a plastic bag, and refrigerate. Change the water daily. This method keeps basil fresh for 3-5 days.

| Timeframe | Quality |
|-----------|---------|
| 1-2 days | Peak flavor |
| 3-5 days | Good, diminishes slowly |
| 6-7 days | Marginal, starting to wilt |

## Freezing

Chop basil and freeze in ice cube trays with olive oil or water. Frozen basil retains flavor significantly better than dried.

- 1 month: Excellent for cooking
- 3 months: Very good
- 6 months: Acceptable, slight flavor loss

Best used in soups, sauces, and stews where texture is less important.

## Drying

Air dry or dehydrate quickly to preserve color. However, dried basil loses significant flavor—acceptable for about 1 week, poor after 2 weeks.

## Key Principle

Freezing retains flavor better than drying. Fresh basil stored in water with daily changes is the best method for short-term use.
