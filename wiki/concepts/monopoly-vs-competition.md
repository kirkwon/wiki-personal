# Monopoly vs competition

A key concept from Peter Thiel's philosophy: monopolies drive progress while competition is for losers. This challenges conventional economic thinking about the benefits of competitive markets.

## Core Claims
- Monopolies create new categories and drive technological progress
- Competition erodes profits and discourages innovation
- The best businesses are so good they have no real competition
- True value comes from being 10x better, not marginally better
