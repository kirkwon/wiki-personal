# World (mental model)

World is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

World functions as a universal mental model for pattern and relationship recognition across domains. It serves as a lens through which to interpret complex, interconnected systems—whether economic cycles, technological progress, ecological feedback loops, or societal shifts.

## Cross-Source Perspectives
