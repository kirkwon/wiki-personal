# Master Morality

One half of Nietzsche's master vs. slave morality framework. Master morality originates from nobility and is characterized by values of strength, creativity, pride, and self-affirmation.

## Characteristics

- Spontaneous: values arise from the noble type's life-affirming expression
- Life-affirming: based on creation and self-overcoming
- Originates from the Will to Power
- Values: strength, nobility, pride, creativity, self-affirmation
