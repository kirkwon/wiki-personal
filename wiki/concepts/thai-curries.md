# Thai Curries

## Overview

Thai curries (แกง, Kaeng) are aromatic, complex dishes made from curry pastes blended with coconut milk or broth. Unlike Indian dry spice blends, Thai curries use fresh ingredients ground into paste, creating vibrant, fragrant sauces.

## Curry Types

### Green Curry (แกงเขียวหวาน, Kaeng Khiao Wan)

**The most popular Thai curry**

**Characteristics:**
- Green chilies (fresh)
- Kaffir lime leaves
- Coconut milk
- Sweet (wan means sweet)
- Spicy but balanced

**Common ingredients:**
- Green chilies
- Galangal
- Lemongrass
- Kaffir lime leaves, zest
- Coriander roots
- Cilantro
- Thai basil

**Typical additions:**
- Chicken or beef
- Thai eggplant
- Bamboo shoots
- Thai basil (holy basil)

### Red Curry (แกงแดง, Kaeng Daeng)

**Rich, aromatic**

**Characteristics:**
- Red chilies (dried)
- Coconut milk
- Rich, slightly sweet
- High heat level

**Common ingredients:**
- Dried red chilies
- Galangal
- Lemongrass
- Coriander seeds
- Cumin seeds
- Shallots
- Garlic
- Shrimp paste

**Typical additions:**
- Chicken, beef, or pork
- Thai basil
- Bamboo shoots
- Green peas

### Yellow Curry (แกงกะหรี่, Kaeng Kari)

**Indian-influenced**

**Characteristics:**
- Yellow from turmeric
- Coconut milk
- Mild spice level
- Sweet potatoes, potatoes

**Common ingredients:**
- Dried red chilies
- Turmeric
- Cumin
- Coriander seeds
- Cardamom
- Cinnamon
- Cloves

**Typical additions:**
- Chicken or beef
- Potatoes
- Onions
- Cilantro

### Panang Curry (แกงพะแนง, Kaeng Phanaeng)

**Thick, rich, peanut**

**Characteristics:**
- Dried red chilies
- Thick coconut milk
- Kaffir lime leaves, zest
- Peanuts (sometimes)
- Rich, concentrated flavor

**Common ingredients:**
- Dried red chilies
- Galangal
- Lemongrass
- Kaffir lime leaves, zest
- Coriander roots
- Peanuts

### Massaman Curry (แกงมัสมั่น, Kaeng Matsaman)

**Muslim-influenced**

**Characteristics:**
- Cardamom, cinnamon
- Potatoes, onions
---
- Mild spice level
- Rich, aromatic

**Common ingredients:**
- Dried red chilies
- Cardamom
- Cinnamon
- Cloves
- Nutmeg
- Coriander seeds

**Typical additions:**
- Beef or chicken
- Potatoes
- Onions

## Making Curry from Scratch

### Green Curry Paste

**Ingredients:**
- 5-10 green chilies (adjust to taste)
- 3 shallots, peeled
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp galangal, sliced
- 2 stalks lemongrass (lower part)
- 5 kaffir lime leaves
- 1 tbsp coriander roots
- 1 tsp shrimp paste
- 1 tsp coriander seeds (toasted)

**Process:**
1. Toast coriander seeds, grind
2. Pound or blend all ingredients
3. Add small amount of water if needed
4. Paste should be smooth

**Cooking:**
1. Fry paste in coconut cream
2. Until oil separates
3. Add protein, cook
4. Add remaining coconut milk
5. Season with fish sauce, sugar
6. Add vegetables
7. Finish with Thai basil

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Not frying paste enough | Fry until oil separates for rich flavor |
| Using dried coconut milk | Fresh or good-quality canned |
| Not adding sugar | Thai curries need sugar to balance heat |
| Overcooking vegetables | Add vegetables late to keep crisp |
