# Domestic Asset Protection Trust (DAPT)

An irrevocable trust established in a state with favorable asset protection laws, allowing the settlor to maintain certain benefits while protecting assets from creditors.

## Key Features

- State-specific: available in Alaska, Delaware, Nevada, South Dakota, etc.
- Self-settled: settlor can be a discretionary beneficiary
- Fraudulent transfer protection (limited if transfers made while insolvent)
- Trustee discretion over distributions
- Assets protected from most creditors by statute

## Advantages

- Domestic formation, no offshore jurisdiction needed
- Familiar laws and privacy
- Potential income and estate tax advantages
- Discretionary distribution powers

## Disadvantages

- No protection from fraudulent transfer claims
- Only available in specific states
- More complex and costly formation
- Limited access to assets during lifetime
