# Feynman Technique (Learning)

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you teach a concept in simple, plain language, as if explaining it to someone with no background in the subject. This process exposes gaps in your understanding and forces you to deepen your grasp of the material.

## Key Principles
- **Explain in simple terms**: If you cannot explain something simply, you do not understand it well enough.
- **Identify gaps**: The act of teaching reveals where your understanding is incomplete.
- **Simplify and refine**: Iteratively simplify your explanation until it is clear and accurate.

## Practical Applications
- Choose a concept and write out an explanation as if teaching a child.
- When you get stuck or use jargon, go back to the source material.
- Revise your explanation until it is clear, concise, and complete.
