# Microsoft vs Tencent Rivalry

The fourth war contrasts Microsoft's expansion into cloud computing and AI with Tencent's dominance in China's digital services ecosystem. Microsoft represents the legacy tech giant reinventing itself around cloud and artificial intelligence, while Tencent embodies the power of integrated digital services in a massive domestic market.^[raw/articles/the-four---scott-galloway.md]

Key dynamics include:
- Cloud and AI investment vs. integrated digital ecosystems
- Western tech expansion vs. Chinese market dominance
- Emerging trends in technology adoption and potential areas for growth or competition

This rivalry illustrates how competition between platform titans increasingly plays out across global markets with different regulatory and technological trajectories.^[raw/articles/the-four---scott-galloway.md]
