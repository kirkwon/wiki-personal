# The 8th Habit - Stephen R. Covey

Stephen R. Covey's The 8th Habit extends his earlier work on effectiveness by introducing the concept of finding one's voice—moving from effectiveness to greatness through purposeful habits aligned with personal mission and contribution.

## Key Themes

- **Find your voice** — Discovering purpose and unique contribution
- **Inspire others to find their voice** — Leadership through empowerment
- **Moving from dependence to independence to interdependence**
- **The sweet spot** — Aligning talent, passion, need, and conscience

## Connection to Habit Mental Model

Covey frames habit as the vehicle for expressing one's deepest purpose, connecting to the habit mental model's emphasis on pattern recognition and navigating complexity through consistent, intentional behavior.
