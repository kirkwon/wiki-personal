# Manager Risk

Manager risk is the dependence on the skill, expertise, and judgment of investment managers, sponsors, or management teams. This is particularly acute in private equity, real estate, and alternative investment vehicles, where information asymmetry exists and past marketing may not reflect actual performance.

## Examples

- **Private equity**: Sponsor performance varies dramatically; selecting a weak sponsor can erase expected returns
- **Real estate**: Manager selection for property acquisition, leasing, and operations directly impacts cash flow and appreciation
- **REITs**: Management team decisions on leverage, property disposition, and acquisitions affect shareholder returns
- **REIGs**: Sponsor track record and deal selection are critical

## Mitigation Strategies

- Conduct thorough due diligence on managers and sponsors
- Evaluate track records beyond marketing materials
- Assess reputation within the industry
- Ensure fee transparency and understand fee structures
- Diversify across multiple managers to reduce single-point-of-failure risk
