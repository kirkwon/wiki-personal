# Relativity in Decision-Making

The principle that we value options relative to comparisons, even when those comparisons are arbitrary. Ariely's foundational insight is that absolute value is rarely what drives choices — relative comparison is.

## Key Insight

People do not evaluate options in isolation. They evaluate options relative to other available options, reference points, and context. This means that adding or removing options — even irrelevant ones — changes the perceived value of remaining options.

## Applications

- Understanding why the structure of choices matters as much as the content
- Recognizing when arbitrary comparisons are driving your decisions
- Designing choice architectures that present options in ways that reveal true preferences
- Identifying when you are comparing obsessively rather than evaluating absolute merit
