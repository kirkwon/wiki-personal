# Predictions

Predictions is a universal mental model that functions as a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world, positioning prediction-making as a general-purpose thinking framework rather than merely a statistical forecasting technique.

## Core Idea

The model draws on two key sources:
- **Philip E. Tetlock & Dan Gardner's superforecasting research**, which demonstrates that probabilistic thinking, intellectual humility, and diverse perspectives lead to highly accurate predictions.
- **Nate Silver's *The Signal and the Noise***, which addresses the fundamental challenge of distinguishing true patterns from randomness in data.

Together, these sources support the view that predictions serve as a pattern-recognition tool applicable across domains—from decision-making under uncertainty to strategic planning.
