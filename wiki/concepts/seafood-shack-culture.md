# Seafood Shack Culture

Seafood shacks are informal, casual dining establishments along the New England coast serving fried seafood — clams, scallops, and fish — as quick, comforting meals rooted in maritime tradition. They are central to New England's coastal food scene, known for serving fried seafood and fresh catch in a no-frills atmosphere.

## Key Locations

- **Massachusetts**: Gloucester, Cape Cod — fresh catch daily
- **Maine**: Portland, Boothbay Harbor — lobster shacks
- **Rhode Island**: Providence — fried seafood

## Signature Offerings

Fried clams, fried scallops, lobster rolls, and clam chowder are the staples. The philosophy is simple: fresh, local, seasonal seafood prepared with minimal fuss.

## Characteristics

- Fried clams and fried scallops as signature items
- Tartar sauce, lemon wedges, melted butter as condiments
- Simple, no-frills preparation emphasizing freshness
