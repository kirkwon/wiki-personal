# Metaphors as Mental Tools

## Definition
Using analogies and metaphors as conceptual tools to comprehend complex ideas by relating them to familiar experiences, thereby shaping thinking patterns.

## Explanation
Parrish identifies metaphors as powerful mental tools that help us understand abstract or complex concepts by mapping them onto familiar domains. This process shapes how we perceive and respond to new situations.
