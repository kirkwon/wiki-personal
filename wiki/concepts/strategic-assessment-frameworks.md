# Strategic Assessment Frameworks

Structured methodologies for evaluating competitive situations before committing to action. From *The Art of War*, these include the five factors (moral influence, weather, terrain, command, doctrine) and seven calculations for comparing opposing sides.

## Key Ideas
- **Five factors**: Moral influence, weather, terrain, command, doctrine—each assessed for both yourself and the opponent.
- **Seven calculations**: Systematic comparison of moral influence, commander ability, terrain advantage, weather advantage, doctrine adherence, troop strength, and reward/discipline systems.
- **Assessment before action**: The framework insists on completing this analysis before any strategic move.

## Connections
Connects to [[planning]] as a mental model, [[situation]] (situation assessment), [[decision-making-frameworks]] (structured decision approaches), and [[superforecasting]] (evaluating probabilities and uncertainties). Also relates to [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]]—good assessment processes lead to better decisions regardless of outcome.

## Application
Before any competitive decision—negotiation, business strategy, project planning—run through the five factors and seven calculations to ensure you understand the full landscape.
