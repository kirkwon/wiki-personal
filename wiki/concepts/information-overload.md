# Information Overload Management

Information overload management, as discussed in Chapter 8 of *Making It All Work*, addresses the challenge of prioritizing what matters when faced with an ever-increasing volume of information.

## Key Ideas

- The increasing amount of available information makes prioritization essential.
- Strategies include filtering inputs, regularly reviewing what is relevant, and eliminating noise.
- Information overload relates to the broader principle of knowing when to stop: not every input deserves attention.
- Managing information is a prerequisite for effective task management—unclear or excessive inputs lead to unclear or excessive action lists.
- The book recommends systematic review routines to continuously reassess what deserves attention.
