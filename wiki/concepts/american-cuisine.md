# American Cuisine

American cuisine encompasses a wide range of regional styles and traditions. This page serves as an entry point for understanding the diversity of American food culture.

## Regional Styles

- [[bbq-regions]] — American BBQ regional styles (Texas, Kansas City, Carolina, Memphis)
- [[southern-soul-food]] — Southern comfort food traditions

## Key Concepts

American BBQ represents one of the clearest examples of regional culinary identity within American cuisine—each style is defined by its approach to meat, sauce, wood, and cooking philosophy rather than a single standardized method.
