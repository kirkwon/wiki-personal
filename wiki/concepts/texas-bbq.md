# Texas BBQ

Texas BBQ is defined by its beef-focused philosophy, dry rub preparation, and minimal sauce approach. The centerpiece is the beef brisket, smoked low and slow for 12-18 hours at 225°F using oak or mesquite wood.

## The Brisket

- **Point** — Fatty, rich section of the brisket
- **Flat** — Lean, uniform section of the brisket

## Dry Rub

- Salt (3 parts)
- Black pepper (2 parts)
- Garlic powder (1 part)
- Cayenne (optional)

## Cooking Process

1. Trim fat cap
2. Apply dry rub (12-24 hours before cooking)
3. Smoke at 225°F (107°C)
4. Wrap in butcher paper after 4-6 hours
5. Continue until internal temp reaches 200°F (93°C)
6. Rest 30-60 minutes
7. Slice against the grain

## Serving

- Plain (no sauce) — the Texas tradition
- With Texas-style BBQ sauce (tomato-based, spicy) — optional

## Key Distinctions

Texas BBQ emphasizes letting the meat speak for itself, with dry rub as the primary flavoring method. This contrasts with Kansas City and Carolina styles that rely heavily on sauce.
