# Heuristic Dictionary

The Heuristic Dictionary is Polya's comprehensive taxonomy of problem-solving tactics and strategies. It includes entries such as:

- **Analogy** - Using similar problems to find solutions
- **Decomposing and Recombining** - Breaking complex problems into simpler parts
- **Working Backwards** - Starting from the goal and working toward the given
- **Specialization** - Examining specific cases to understand general principles
- **Generalization** - Extending solutions to broader cases
- **Taking the Problem As Solved** - Working backward from the solution
- **Look at the Unknown** - Focusing attention on what needs to be found
- **Did You Use All the Data?** - Ensuring all information is used appropriately
- **Signs of Progress** - Indicators that you're moving toward a solution
- **Auxiliary Elements** - Introducing new elements to help solve problems
- **Reducing to a Simpler Problem** - Transforming complex problems into manageable ones
- **Variation of the Problem** - Modifying the problem to gain insight
- **Inventor's Paradox** - Sometimes more ambitious problems are easier to solve
- **Subconscious Work** - How the mind continues working on problems unconsciously
- **Pedantry and Mastery** - The difference between knowing rules and truly understanding

This dictionary serves as a practical reference for selecting and applying strategies during the "Devising a Plan" phase of problem-solving.
