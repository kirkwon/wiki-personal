# IRS Section 7520 Rate

The discount rate published by the IRS used to calculate the present value of annuities and interests retained in trusts for gift and estate tax purposes.

## Applications

- **GRAT**: Determines annuity payment amounts and remainder interest valuation
- **QPRT**: Determines gift tax discount on residence transfer
- Affects the taxable gift value and estate tax benefits
- Changes periodically based on federal mid-term rates

## Planning Considerations

- Lower rates increase the present value of retained interests
- Higher rates decrease the present value, increasing tax benefits
- Current rate environment affects optimal term selection for GRAT/QPRT
- Proper valuation critical for maximizing tax benefits
