
# System Reassessment

System reassessment involves evaluating current workflows, tools, and processes to determine their efficiency and effectiveness in managing digital life. Tiago Forte positions this as a key step in the PARA Method—before optimizing, one must honestly assess what is already in place.

This concept aligns with [[systematic-decision-making]], which advocates for structured evaluation of decisions and processes. It also connects to [[decision-tracking]], the practice of recording and reviewing decisions over time to calibrate judgment, and [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]], which separates the quality of a decision process from its results.

Through reassessment, users can identify bottlenecks, redundant steps, and misaligned tools, then apply PARA's four-category framework to reorganize their digital environment more effectively.
