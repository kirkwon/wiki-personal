# Nonlinear Behavior

Nonlinear behavior refers to system responses where outputs are not proportional to inputs. In complex systems, small changes can produce large effects, and large changes can produce negligible effects, making prediction and control inherently difficult.

## Key Characteristics

- **Emergent properties** — System-level behaviors that cannot be predicted from individual components.
- **Sensitivity to initial conditions** — Minor differences in starting state can lead to vastly different outcomes over time.
- **Feedback-driven dynamics** — Positive and negative feedback loops create exponential growth, oscillation, or collapse.

## Key Insight

Chaos theory and nonlinear dynamics reveal that complex systems are fundamentally unpredictable in the long run, but their structural patterns—feedback loops, leverage points, and archetypes—remain identifiable and actionable.
