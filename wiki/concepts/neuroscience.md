# Neuroscience

Neuroscience is the scientific study of the nervous system, which includes mapping how neurons communicate to create complex behaviors and cognitive functions. It investigates everything from molecular signaling within individual cells to large-scale network interactions across brain regions. This field is crucial for understanding consciousness, memory, and neurological disorders.
