# Leading Lines

Leading lines are lines in an image that guide the viewer's eye toward the main subject. They are one of the most powerful composition tools for directing attention, creating depth, movement, and visual flow.

## Types of Leading Lines

| Type | Example | Effect |
|------|---------|--------|
| Straight | Roads, fences, buildings | Direct, strong |
| Curved | Rivers, paths, coastlines | Soft, flowing |
| Diagonal | Stairs, shadows | Dynamic, energy |
| Converging | Railroad tracks, roads | Perspective, depth |

## How to Use

1. Find lines in your scene (roads, fences, shadows, architecture)
2. Position yourself so lines lead to your subject
3. Frame the shot so lines start at the edge and go to the subject
4. Multiple lines can create depth and interest

## Examples

- Road to sunset: Road edges frame the sun in the distance
- Building shadows: Shadows lead to the subject
- Railroad tracks: Converging lines to a distant subject
