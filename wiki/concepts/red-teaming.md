# Red Teaming

Red teaming is an organizational mitigation strategy where a designated team challenges plans, assumptions, and proposals to identify potential failures, blind spots, and weaknesses before implementation.

## How It Works
- Assign a team to argue against the prevailing plan
- Identify risks, vulnerabilities, and failure modes
- Surface hidden assumptions and biases

## Connection to Other Concepts
Complements [[pre-mortem-analysis]] and [[building-a-decision-culture]]. Red teaming is a structured way to counter [[confirmation-bias]] and [[group-decision-polarization]] at the organizational level.
