# Pre-Suasion

Pre-suasion is a concept introduced by Robert B. Cialdini in his book *Pre-Suasion: A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade*. It refers to the process of shaping someone's attention and mental state before delivering a message, which significantly influences how the message is received.

## Core Idea

The moments before we communicate are as important as the communication itself. By directing attention and priming responses through environmental and contextual cues, we can influence outcomes before the actual persuasion attempt begins.

## Key Principles

- **Attention direction** – Guiding what people focus on before a message shapes their response.
- **Contextual priming** – The physical and psychological environment primes specific reactions.
- **Environmental factors** – Conditions surrounding an interaction are not neutral; they actively shape persuasion.

## Connection to Factors

Pre-suasion is one of the two source inspirations for the [[factors]] mental model. The Factors model generalizes Cialdini's insight that contextual variables matter into a universal framework for navigating complexity.
