# Fresh Pasta Making

Making pasta fresca from scratch involves a simple but precise process of mixing, kneading, resting, and rolling.

## Ingredients
- Flour (all-purpose or semolina)
- Eggs (2 per cup of flour)
- Pinch of salt

## Process
1. Mound the flour on a board or work surface
2. Make a well in the center
3. Crack eggs into the well
4. Mix gradually, incorporating flour from the inner edges
5. Knead for 10 minutes until the dough is smooth
6. Rest the dough for 30 minutes
7. Roll thin using a pasta machine or rolling pin
8. Cut into desired shape

## Cooking Fresh Pasta
Fresh pasta cooks much faster than dried:
- Thin pasta: 1-2 minutes
- Thick pasta: 2-3 minutes
- Stuffed pasta: 3-5 minutes

The texture of fresh pasta is fundamentally different from dried — it is softer and more tender, making it ideal for special occasions and stuffed varieties like [[ravioli]] and [[tortellini]].
