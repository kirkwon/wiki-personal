# Career capital

Career capital refers to the rare and valuable skills, knowledge, connections, and experience that you accumulate over time and that make you indispensable in your field. The concept is central to Cal Newport's framework in *So Good They Can't Ignore You*, where he argues that building career capital should be the primary focus rather than chasing passion directly.

Key characteristics of career capital:
- It must be **rare**: skills that few others possess.
- It must be **valuable**: skills that are in demand.
- It is built through **deliberate practice** rather than passive repetition.

Career capital functions as a form of [[leverage]]—as you accumulate it, you gain increasing control over your work and the ability to pursue meaningful missions.
