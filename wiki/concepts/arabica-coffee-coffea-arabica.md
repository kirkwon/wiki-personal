# Arabica Coffee (Coffea arabica)


## Quick Reference

### Field**: [[Beverages/Coffee]]
### Type**: Coffee Bean Variety
### Species**: Coffea arabica
### Caffeine Content**: 1.2-1.5% (medium)
### Global Production**: 60-70% of world coffee
### Best For**: Specialty coffee, pour-over, espresso
### Flavor Profile**: Complex, aromatic, varied
### Growing Conditions**: High altitude, specific climates



## Summary

Arabica is the premium coffee bean species, accounting for the majority of specialty coffee worldwide. Grown at higher altitudes with more specific climate requirements, Arabica offers complex flavor profiles ranging from fruity and floral to nutty and chocolatey. Its delicate nature results in nuanced flavors but requires careful cultivation and processing.

## Characteristics

### Physical Properties
- **Bean Shape**: Elongated, oval (flat beans)
- **Bean Size**: Larger than Robusta
- **Color Range**: Green to bluish-green
- **Density**: Lower density than Robusta
- **Surface**: More crevices and wrinkles

### Growing Requirements
- **Altitude**: 2,000-6,000 feet (600-1,800 meters)
- **Temperature**: 60-75°F (15-24°C)
- **Rainfall**: 40-60 inches annually
- **Soil**: Well-drained, volcanic soils preferred
- **Shade**: Often grown under shade trees

## Flavor Profile by Origin

### Ethiopian Arabica
- **Flavor Notes**: Blueberry, jasmine, lemon, wine
- **Processing Effects**: Natural = fruitier, Washed = cleaner
- **Varieties**: Heirloom, Yirgacheffe, Sidamo
- **Best Brew Methods**: Pour-over, AeroPress, light roast
- **Acidity**: Bright, wine-like

### Colombian Arabica
- **Flavor Notes**: Caramel, nuts, chocolate, balanced
- **Processing Effects**: Mostly washed, consistent quality
- **Varieties**: Caturra, Castillo, Typica
- **Best Brew Methods**: Drip, French press, medium roast
- **Acidity**: Balanced, smooth

### Guatemalan Antigua
- **Flavor Notes**: Chocolate, spice, smoky, full body
- **Processing Effects**: Washed, volcanic soil influence
- **Varieties**: Bourbon, Typica, Caturra
- **Best Brew Methods**: French press, espresso, dark roast
- **Acidity**: Low to medium, rich

### Costa Rican Tarrazú
- **Flavor Notes**: Citrus, honey, almond, bright
- **Processing Effects**: Honey process common
- **Varieties**: Caturra, Typica, Villa Lobos
- **Best Brew Methods**: Pour-over, light to medium roast
- **Acidity**: Bright, crisp

## Processing Methods

### Washed Processing
```yaml
Process: Remove cherry pulp immediately after picking
Flavor: Cleaner, brighter, more focused
Body: Lighter, higher acidity
Best For: Single-origin specialty coffee
Time: 2-3 weeks total processing
```

### Natural Processing
```yaml
Process: Dry cherry on beans, then remove
Flavor: Fruitier, sweeter, fermented notes
Body: Fuller, lower acidity
Best For: Fruit-forward, sweet profiles
Time: 3-4 weeks total processing
```

### Honey Processing
```yaml
Process: Partial pulp removal, sticky beans
Flavor: Balanced fruit sweetness, clean finish
Body: Medium, balanced acidity
Best For: Sweetness complexity, body
Time: 1-2 weeks drying, sticky beans
```

## Roasting Effects

### Light Roast
- **Color**: Light brown, cinnamon colored
- **Flavor**: Bright acidity, floral, fruity notes preserved
- **Body**: Light to medium
- **Caffeine**: Higher (less mass loss)
- **Best Origins**: Ethiopian, Kenyan, Costa Rican
- **Brew Methods**: Pour-over, AeroPress

### Medium Roast
- **Color**: Medium brown, balanced
- **Flavor**: Balanced sweetness, acidity, body
- **Body**: Medium
- **Caffeine**: Medium
- **Best Origins**: Colombian, Guatemalan
- **Brew Methods**: Drip, French press, espresso

### Medium-Dark Roast
- **Color**: Dark brown, oil sheen beginning
- **Flavor**: Reduced acidity, chocolate, caramel notes
- **Body**: Full
- **Caffeine**: Lower
- **Best Origins**: Brazilian, Sumatran blends
- **Brew Methods**: French press, espresso, Moka pot

## Quality Standards

### Specialty Coffee Grades
- **Grade 1**: 80+ points, defect-free
- **Grade 2**: 80 points, minor defects allowed
- **Premium**: Specialty grade origin
- **Commercial**: Lower quality, defects common

### Defect Categories
- **Primary**: Black, sour, foreign matter
- **Secondary**: Floater, broken, insect damage
- **Quakers**: Underdeveloped beans, no flavor
- **Stinker**: Fermented, off-flavors

## Brewing Recommendations

### Optimal Parameters
- **Grind Size**: Medium (table salt) for most methods
- **Water Temperature**: 195-205°F (90-96°C)
- **Ratio**: 1:15 to 1:17 coffee to water
- **Brew Time**: 2-4 minutes depending on method

### Method-Specific Tips
```yaml
Pour-Over:
- Grind: Medium-fine
- Bloom: 30 seconds
- Pour method: Slow, circular
- Focus: Highlight brightness and complexity

French Press:
- Grind: Coarse (sea salt)
- Steep: 4 minutes
- Press: Slow, even pressure
- Focus: Body and mouthfeel

Espresso:
- Grind: Fine (powder)
- Pressure: 9 bars
- Time: 25-30 seconds
- Focus: Intensity and balance
```

## Storage & Freshness

### Optimal Storage
- **Whole Beans**: Airtight container, cool, dark, dry
- **Grind**: Use immediately within 15-30 minutes
- **Vacuum**: Extend freshness to 2-3 weeks
- **Freezing**: Not recommended (moisture damage)

### Freshness Indicators
- **Aroma**: Fresh, fragrant, not musty
- **Appearance**: Uniform color, no oil sheen (unless dark roast)
- **Sound**: Beans should rattle when shaken
- **Flavor**: Bright, complex, no staleness

## Common Varieties

### Bourbon
- **Origin**: Ethiopia/Burma
- **Character**: High quality, sweet, complex
- **Growth**: Slower but better quality
- **Use**: Premium single-origin coffee

### Typica
- **Origin**: Yemen/Ethiopia
- **Character**: Classic coffee flavor, balanced
- **Growth**: Tall tree, lower yield
- **Use**: Traditional quality coffee

### Caturra
- **Origin**: Bourbon mutation
- **Character**: Bright, acidic, productive
- **Growth**: Compact, high yield
- **Use**: Commercial specialty coffee

### Geisha (Gesha)
- **Origin**: Ethiopia, popular in Panama
- **Character**: Unique floral, tea-like
- **Growth**: Low yield, high quality
- **Use**: Ultra-premium specialty coffee

- **Calories**: 2-5 kcal

- **Antioxidants**: Rich in polyphenols
- **Chlorogenic Acid**: Strong antioxidant content
- **Minerals**: Potassium, magnesium, manganese

### Health Benefits
- **Antioxidant**: High ORAC value
- **Cognitive**: Improved focus and alertness
- **Metabolic**: May aid metabolism
- **Liver**: Associated with liver health benefits
