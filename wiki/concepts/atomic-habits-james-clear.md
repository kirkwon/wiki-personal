# Atomic Habits - James Clear

*Atomic Habits* by James Clear is a framework for building good habits and breaking bad ones through small, incremental changes. Key concepts include:

- **Identity-based habits**: Focus on who you want to become rather than what you want to achieve.
- **Systems over goals**: Build systems and processes rather than relying solely on goals.
- **Environment design**: Shape your surroundings to make good habits easier and bad habits harder.
- **Compounding effects**: Small habits compound over time into significant results.
- **Habit stacking**: Link new habits to existing ones for easier adoption.

The Formation mental model references *Atomic Habits* as a cross-source perspective, suggesting Formation may provide a higher-level framework for understanding how these habit principles interact with broader patterns and relationships in complex situations.
