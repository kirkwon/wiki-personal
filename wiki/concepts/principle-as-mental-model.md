# Principle as Mental Model

Principle is a conceptual tool that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition
A principle serves as a universal mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in complex situations. Rather than offering a rigid rule, it functions as a meta-framework that can be applied across domains to simplify decision-making and sense-making.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

### David Deutsch
From *The Beginning of Infinity*, Deutsch emphasizes the role of explanatory knowledge and principles in enabling progress and understanding. Principles, in his view, are deep explanations that allow humans to create and predict outcomes in a universe that is fundamentally knowable.

### Stephen R. Covey
Covey's *First Things First* contributes a principles-based approach to time management and prioritization. His framework centers on identifying and acting on what is truly important rather than what is urgent, grounding decisions in enduring principles rather than reactive impulses.

### Mark Manson
In *The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck*, Manson reframes principles around values and selective commitment. His perspective argues that effective navigation of life requires choosing which concerns deserve attention, guided by personal principles rather than external expectations.
