# Generalists

Generalists is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Generalists function as a universal mental model applicable across domains. Rather than relying on narrow specialization, generalists leverage broad experience to recognize patterns and relationships that specialists might miss. This breadth-of-perspective approach is particularly valuable when navigating complexity and uncertainty.

## Key Ideas

- **Breadth over depth**: Generalists draw on wide-ranging experience to make connections across domains, enabling more flexible problem-solving.
- **Pattern recognition**: Broad exposure helps identify structural similarities between seemingly unrelated problems.
- **Navigating complexity**: In complex, ambiguous situations, generalist thinking provides a mental shortcut for making sense of relationships and dynamics.

## Connection to Epstein's Range

David Epstein's book *Range* argues that generalists often outperform specialists in complex environments. This connects to the existing [[specialization-and-generalization]] concept, which already documents the tension between deep expertise and broad knowledge. The generalist model provides a counterbalancing perspective to purely specialist-oriented thinking.
