# Cash Flow Stress Testing

A method for evaluating whether a property can sustain payments and maintain positive cash flow under adverse conditions, including rent declines, expense increases, vacancy spikes, and interest rate hikes.

## Key Stress Scenarios

- **20% Rent Decline**: Can the property sustain payments?
- **10% Expense Increase**: Can the property maintain positive cash flow?
- **Vacancy Increase**: What if vacancy doubles?
- **Interest Rate Hike**: Impact on monthly payments?

## Risk Thresholds

- Vacancy rate >10% = High risk
- Operating expenses >50% of gross rent = Medium risk
- Rent growth rate <2% annually = High risk
- Capital expenditures >3% of property value annually = High risk
- Emergency fund <6 months expenses = High risk

Cash flow stress testing is a core component of the [[risk-assessment-framework]] and feeds into [[leverage-risk-analysis]].
