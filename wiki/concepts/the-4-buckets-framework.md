# The 4 Buckets Framework

A spending allocation system that replaces traditional budgeting with four categories, designed to be set up once and automated thereafter.

## The Buckets

1. **Fixed Costs (50-60%)**: Rent, utilities, debt payments, groceries, insurance, phone/internet, car payments. Optimize these first because if they're too high, the plan fails.
2. **Investments (10-20%)**: Retirement contributions (401k, Roth IRA) and long-term investing accounts. Automated monthly.
3. **Savings (5-10%)**: Emergency fund, vacation fund, car repair fund, short-term goals. Sub-savings accounts for specific goals prevent "stealing from yourself."
4. **Guilt-Free Spending (20-35%)**: Dining out, entertainment, shopping, hobbies, random stuff. Spend extravagantly here — no guilt because it's planned.

## Example: $4,000/month take-home

- Fixed Costs (60%): $2,400
- Investments (10%): $400
- Savings (5%): $200
- Guilt-Free Spending (25%): $1,000
- Total: $4,000 (100%)

## Key Insight

The goal is NOT to cut everything — it's to cut the unimportant so you can spend on the important. Big wins (refinancing mortgage, shopping insurance, cutting subscriptions) matter more than small wins (buying generic brands, meal prep).
