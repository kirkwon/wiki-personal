# Make It Stick

"Make It Stick" by Peter C. Brown, Henry Roediger, and Mark McDaniel presents evidence-based learning strategies grounded in cognitive psychology. Key techniques include active recall, spaced repetition, interleaving different topics, and embracing desirable difficulties that make learning harder in the short term but more durable long term. The book debunks popular myths like learning styles and rereading as effective study methods.
