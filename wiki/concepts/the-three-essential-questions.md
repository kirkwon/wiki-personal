# The Three Essential Questions (What, How, Why)

The Three Essential Questions are a framework introduced by Simon Sinek for personal growth, career development, and overall life satisfaction. They are:

1. **What** do you do?
2. **How** do you do it?
3. **Why** do you do it?

These questions are deeply interconnected. Sinek argues that most people can articulate What they do and How they do it, but few can clearly articulate Why—their deeper purpose or cause. Understanding all three, especially Why, creates clarity that guides decisions, inspires others, and prevents good intentions from becoming empty messaging.

This framework is closely tied to [[the-golden-circle]] and [[find-your-why-simon-sinek]]. It also connects to broader decision-making concepts like [[decision-making-frameworks]] and [[systematic-decision-making]], though Sinek emphasizes narrative and intuition over structured analysis.
