# Pasta

Pasta is a staple food product, typically composed of durum wheat semolina and water (and sometimes eggs). While the consumption of grain dough is ancient, modern pasta forms were refined and popularized in various parts of Italy. Due to its versatility, it serves as a fundamental base for countless regional Italian dishes and international cuisines. The preparation method generally involves mixing ingredients into a dough, cutting or shaping it, and then boiling it before serving with sauce.
