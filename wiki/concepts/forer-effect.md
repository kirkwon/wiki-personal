# Forer Effect

The tendency for vague, general personality descriptions to feel specific and accurate. Also known as the Barnum effect, this is why horoscopes and personality tests feel personally meaningful despite being applicable to almost anyone.

## Key Points
- Vague statements are accepted as highly personal and accurate
- Connects to [[confirmation-bias]] — we select confirming evidence
- Connects to [[story-bias]] — we prefer coherent narratives
- The more general the statement, the more people feel it applies to them

## Guardrail
When something feels "spot on" for your personality, ask whether the description is specific enough to be falsifiable or if it could apply to anyone.
