# Insurance Risk Management

Risk transfer through insurance coverage types and management practices.

## Coverage Types
- Property Insurance: Fire, natural disasters, vandalism
- Liability Insurance: Tenant injuries, third-party claims
- Rent Guarantee: Tenant default protection
- Business Interruption: Lost income protection
- Umbrella Coverage: Excess liability protection

## Risk Management Practices
Adequate coverage (full replacement value), deductible management (balance cost vs. risk), claims history monitoring, and selecting insurers with strong financial ratings and claims service.
