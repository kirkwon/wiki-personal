# Beta (Investing)

A measure of a stock's or portfolio's volatility relative to the overall market. A beta of 1.0 means the asset moves in line with the market.

## Usage

Tracked in the [[stock-investment-tracker]] and [[stock-and-bond-tracking]] portfolio performance template alongside P/E ratio, dividend yield, and Sharpe ratio.
