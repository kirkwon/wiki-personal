# Duke

Duke is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

The model is rooted in Annie Duke's work on decision-making, particularly from [[how-to-decide---annie-duke]]. Core ideas include viewing decisions as probabilistic bets rather than certainties, logging decisions to learn from them over time, and focusing on the quality of the decision process rather than the result.

Duke functions as a universal mental model applicable across domains, offering a framework for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition.

This concept connects to [[thinking-in-bets---annie-duke]], [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]], and [[decision-tracking]].
