# Blind Evaluation

Blind evaluation removes identifying information — names, demographics, backgrounds — from assessments to reduce the influence of irrelevant factors. It ensures decisions are based on merit rather than appearance or social group.

## Applications
- Removing names from job applications
- Anonymous code reviews
- Blind tasting panels

## Connection to Other Concepts
Counters [[halo-effect]] and [[in-group-bias]]. Part of organizational mitigation strategies alongside [[red-teaming]] and [[diverse-teams]].
