# Personal History with Money

Your unique experiences shape your financial worldview. Your economic upbringing creates money scars and money scripts.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

No single financial expert has your exact background, which means what seems "obvious" financially is deeply personal. Depression-era grandparents may instill extreme frugality, while tech boom survivors may tolerate speculation.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

Understanding your money story is the first step toward better financial decisions, and recognizing that others' financial decisions reflect their experiences fosters empathy and better judgment.
