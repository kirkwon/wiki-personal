# Anomalies and Exceptions in AI

Anomalies and exceptions refer to out-of-distribution scenarios where machine learning models encounter inputs or situations that differ significantly from their training data. These edge cases are a major source of misalignment between models and real-world requirements.

Key insights:
- Models trained on typical data may behave unpredictably or incorrectly when faced with unusual inputs.
- Anomalies can reveal fundamental gaps in model understanding and reliability.
- Managing anomalies requires strategies beyond standard training — including robust testing, anomaly detection, and human oversight.

This concept connects to [[inattention-to-results]] and [[overconfidence-and-certainty]] in that models may exhibit unwarranted confidence when encountering unfamiliar situations.
