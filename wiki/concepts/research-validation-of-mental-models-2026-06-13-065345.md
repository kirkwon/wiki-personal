# Research Validation of Mental Models (2026)

This entry refers to the methodological process of empirically testing and refining an individual's or group's internal conceptual frameworks—mental models. Rather than simply accepting a model, validation involves designing specific experiments or simulations to determine if the model accurately predicts real-world outcomes. This approach is critical in fields like AI safety and complex system design, ensuring that theoretical understanding aligns with observed reality.
