# Franchise Taxes

Franchise taxes are annual state fees required to maintain corporate status. They vary significantly by state and entity type.

## State Comparison
- **California**: Significant annual franchise tax (minimum $800)
- **Nevada**: Low annual fee ($200–$500)
- **Texas**: No franchise tax for certain entities
- **Florida**: Low annual fees for LLCs and corporations
- **Wyoming**: Low costs with no state income tax

## Impact on Entity Selection
Franchise taxes are a recurring cost that affects the total cost of maintaining a business entity. For small businesses and real estate investors with multiple entities, these fees can add up significantly. Jurisdiction selection (see [[jurisdiction-selection-for-entities]]) is a key factor in minimizing these costs.
