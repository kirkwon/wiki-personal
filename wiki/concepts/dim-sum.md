# Dim Sum

Dim sum is a style of Cantonese cuisine consisting of small plates or snacks traditionally served during brunch gatherings in Guangzhou and Hong Kong. The term translates literally to "touch the heart," referring to items meant for sharing communally among friends and family. It encompasses a wide variety of dishes, including steamed dumplings (like *har gow*), baozi (steamed buns), spring rolls, and savory pastries. While its exact origins are rooted in regional Chinese culinary traditions, it is now recognized as an integral part of Cantonese gastronomy.
