# Substance Requirements

Substance requirements are economic presence requirements that jurisdictions impose on offshore and international business entities. These rules require that an entity conduct actual business operations in the jurisdiction rather than serving as a mere shell.

## Why It Matters
- Many offshore jurisdictions require entities to have real economic activity
- Failure to meet substance requirements can result in loss of tax benefits or entity status
- Includes requirements for local management, employees, office space, and decision-making

## Key Considerations
- FBAR and FATCA reporting requirements apply to foreign accounts
- International information reporting obligations may apply
- Professional advice is essential due to complexity
