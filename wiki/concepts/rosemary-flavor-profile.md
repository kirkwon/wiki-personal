# Rosemary Flavor Profile

Rosemary has a distinctive and robust flavor profile characterized by pine-like, woodsy, and camphoraceous notes. Its strength demands judicious use — overuse can cause a pine-tasting soapiness.

## Primary Flavor Notes
- **Pine**: Coniferous, forest-like aroma — the dominant characteristic
- **Woodsy**: Earthy, tree bark notes
- **Camphor**: Medicinal, cooling undertones
- **Mint**: Subtle mint family characteristics (Lamiaceae)
- **Balsamic**: Sweet, resinous finish

## Varietal Flavor Differences
Different rosemary varieties offer subtle but meaningful flavor variations:
- **Tuscan Blue**: Classic pine, strong — the standard for roasting and grilling
- **Arp**: Milder, sweeter — better suited for fried foods and desserts
- **Prostratus**: Very mild — appropriate for delicate dishes
- **Majorica Pink**: Floral, mild — ideal for garnishes and light applications

## Pairing Principles
Rosemary pairs excellently with hearty, robust ingredients: lamb, chicken, pork, beef, potatoes, garlic, thyme, lemon, and olive oil. It overwhelms delicate flavors, so it should be avoided with very delicate fish or greens.

## Usage Guidelines
- Start with small amounts — less is more
- Toast briefly to enhance aroma
- Crush to release oils before adding to dishes
- Can handle long cooking times (roasting, braising, grilling)
