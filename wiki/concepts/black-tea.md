# black-tea

Black tea is a type of *Camellia sinensis* plant that has been fully oxidized after harvesting its leaves. This oxidation process gives the tea its characteristic rich color and robust flavor profile, differentiating it from green or white teas which undergo minimal processing. It is widely consumed globally and is generally prepared by steeping the dried leaves in hot water. The variety of black teas available—such as Assam, Darjeeling, and Earl Grey—reflects different growing regions and blending methods.
