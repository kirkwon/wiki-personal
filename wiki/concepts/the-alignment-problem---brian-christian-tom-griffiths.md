# The Alignment Problem - Brian Christian & Tom Griffiths

The Alignment Problem is a book by Brian Christian and Tom Griffiths that explores the challenge of ensuring artificial intelligence systems behave in accordance with human values and intentions.

## Summary

The book examines how misalignment between AI systems and human goals can lead to unintended consequences, covering topics such as reward hacking, specification gaming, and the difficulty of defining what "safe" behavior means for complex systems.

## Connection to Safety

The mental model of [[safety]] is referenced in this work as a conceptual tool for making sense of patterns and relationships in complex situations, particularly in the context of AI alignment.
