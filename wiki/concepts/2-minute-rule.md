# 2-Minute Rule

The 2-minute rule is a habit formation technique where any new habit should take less than two minutes to start. The goal is to reduce the barrier to entry so that beginning the behavior is trivially easy.

## How It Works

Instead of committing to a large habit like "exercise for 30 minutes daily," you commit to "put on workout shoes." The emphasis is on showing up and starting, not on completing the full behavior. Once started, momentum often carries you further.

## Application

This rule is used in the PARA Method alongside environment design, time blocking, and focus enhancement techniques. It connects to [[habit-testing]] by providing a low-stakes way to build consistency before scaling up intensity or duration.
