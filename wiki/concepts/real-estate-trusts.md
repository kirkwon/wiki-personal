# Real Estate Trusts

Trust structures for holding real estate assets, providing creditor protection, probate avoidance, tax planning, and succession benefits.

## Trust Structures

- **Property-Specific Trusts**: Separate trusts for individual properties
- **Portfolio Trust**: Single trust holding multiple properties
- **REIT**: Publicly traded or non-traded real estate investment trusts
- **Real Estate Limited Partnership**: Combined with trust structure

## Asset Protection Benefits

- Creditor protection from personal creditors
- Divorce protection for property
- Lawsuit protection from legal judgments
- Probate avoidance for real estate transfers
- Privacy of ownership details

## Tax Planning

- Depreciation benefits for trust-owned real estate
- Step-up in basis planning
- Proposition 13 considerations (California)
- 1031 exchange integration with trusts
- Estate tax planning for real estate

## Vacation Home Planning

- Family usage rights preserved in trust
- Trustee responsible for maintenance
- Smooth succession to next generation
- Estate tax and property tax optimization
- Asset protection for vacation property
