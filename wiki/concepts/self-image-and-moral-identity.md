# Self-Image and Moral Identity

How people's self-perception influences their honest behavior. Individuals maintain a moral identity and adjust their dishonesty to stay within their self-concept as "honest" people. This concept is central to understanding why people don't lie in extreme ways but instead engage in incremental dishonesty.

## Key Ideas
- People are motivated to preserve their self-image as honest individuals
- Dishonesty is calibrated to the minimum needed to benefit while maintaining moral self-image
- Social psychology insights reveal how personal morality interacts with behavior
- Self-image maintenance is a powerful driver that shapes ethical decisions more than abstract principles
