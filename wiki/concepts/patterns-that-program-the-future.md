# Patterns That Program the Future

Patterns That Program the Future refers to the systemic patterns within organizations that lock in poor performance and determine future outcomes. Senge argues that organizations can identify and disrupt these patterns through systemic change rather than merely treating symptoms. Organizations can recognize recurring patterns that constrain their ability to adapt and learn.

## Understanding Systemic Patterns

The concept emphasizes that recurring problems are often symptoms of deeper structural issues. By mapping the feedback loops and systemic structures that generate these patterns, organizations can intervene at the structural level to produce lasting change.

## Practical Application

By understanding the systemic patterns that drive behavior, organizations can intervene at the structural level rather than treating symptoms. This connects to diagnostic tools like [[five-whys]] and [[five-whys-technique]] for root-cause analysis, as well as flowcharting for uncovering root causes.
