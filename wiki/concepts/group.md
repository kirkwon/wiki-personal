# Group (mental model)

Group is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Groups functions as a universal mental model applicable across domains. It aids sense-making by aggregating diverse perspectives or organizing information into groups, enabling pattern recognition in complex situations. The definition is broad and could refer to grouping or categorization as a cognitive strategy, or to group dynamics in decision-making contexts.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

James Surowiecki's *The Wisdom of Crowds* provides the primary cross-source perspective, demonstrating that groups can outperform individuals on certain tasks when diversity of opinion, independence, decentralization, and aggregation are present.
