# Farmland and Timber Investments

Farmland and timber are real asset alternative investments that provide inflation protection, income, and biological growth. Farmland generates rent and crop appreciation; timber generates harvest income and biological growth as trees mature.

## Farmland
- Agricultural land leased to farmers
- Income from rent plus crop appreciation
- Inflation hedge: farmland prices rise with food prices

## Timber
- Forest land for timber harvest
- Biological growth (trees grow over time)
- Commodity exposure as inflation hedge

## Key Risks
- Very illiquid (years to sell)
- Specialized knowledge required
- Location-specific risk (weather, pests)
- Management complexity
