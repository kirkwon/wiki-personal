# Thinking in Bets - Annie Duke

Annie Duke's Thinking in Bets introduces a framework for separating decisions from outcomes using probabilistic language. The core idea is that every decision is a bet, and the quality of a decision should be judged by the process, not just the result.

## Key Ideas

- Frame decisions as bets with associated probabilities.
- Separate decision quality from outcome quality.
- Use probabilistic thinking to evaluate uncertain situations.
- Track decisions over time to improve judgment.

## Connection to Bets Mental Model

This framework is the primary source for the [[bets---mental-model]] abstraction, which generalizes Duke's insights into a universal mental model for navigating complexity.
