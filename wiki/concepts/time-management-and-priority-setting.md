# Time management and priority setting

Time management and priority setting is the practice of systematically improving efficiency and output. It involves identifying and implementing methods, techniques, and frameworks that maximize personal or organizational productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices.

## Core Approach

The skill follows the [[audit-apply-iterate-scale-framework]]: audit your current workflow, apply frameworks to restructure approach, iterate and refine based on results, and scale what works. Effective application focuses on high-impact activities, eliminates or delegates low-value tasks, and builds consistency through habit formation.

## Applications

- **Personal productivity**: Planning weeks more effectively, managing competing priorities, breaking down complex projects into manageable tasks.
- **Professional context**: Team coordination, improving meeting efficiency, enhancing decision-making processes.
- **Learning and growth**: Accelerating skill acquisition, organizing and retaining information, identifying and filling knowledge gaps.
