# Information as a Mental Model

Information is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Information is not merely data or facts — it is an active navigational framework. As a mental model, it functions as a universal conceptual tool for recognizing patterns and relationships in complex situations, similar to how [[thinking-mental-model]] or [[planning]] serve as navigational principles.
