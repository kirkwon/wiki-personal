# stir-frying

Stir-frying is a cooking technique involving quick frying of ingredients in a small amount of oil over high heat while continuously stirring. It originates from East Asian cuisine and relies on the Maillard reaction for flavor development, making it suitable for capturing vibrant colors in photography. From a cognitive perspective, this method requires attentive multitasking to ensure even cooking without overcooking ingredients.


From a personal finance standpoint, stir-frying is cost-effective due to its minimal use of oil and the ability to prepare meals quickly with inexpensive, readily available ingredients.
