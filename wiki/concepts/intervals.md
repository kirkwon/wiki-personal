# Intervals

An interval is the distance between two pitches. Intervals are foundational to understanding harmony, melody, scales, and chord construction.

## Perfect Intervals
- **Unison**: Same note (0 half-steps)
- **Perfect 4th**: 5 half-steps (F to Bb)
- **Perfect 5th**: 7 half-steps (C to G)
- **Octave**: 12 half-steps (C to next C)

## Major Intervals
- **Major 2nd**: 2 half-steps (C to D)
- **Major 3rd**: 4 half-steps (C to E)
- **Major 6th**: 9 half-steps (C to A)
- **Major 7th**: 11 half-steps (C to B)

## Minor Intervals
- **Minor 2nd**: 1 half-step (C to Db)
- **Minor 3rd**: 3 half-steps (C to Eb)
- **Minor 6th**: 8 half-steps (C to Ab)
- **Minor 7th**: 10 half-steps (C to Bb)

Intervals form the building blocks of chords—triads are constructed from a root, a third (major or minor), and a fifth (perfect or diminished/augmented). Intervals also form the building blocks for triads and seventh chords, which are essential for [[jazz-harmony]] and [[chord-voicings]]. Regular practice of interval recognition is a core component of [[ear-training]].
