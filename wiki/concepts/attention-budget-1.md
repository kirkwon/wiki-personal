# Attention Budget

The attention budget framework treats attention as a finite, scarce resource that must be deliberately allocated throughout the day. Like financial budgeting, attention budgeting involves recognizing limits, prioritizing expenditures, and avoiding waste.

## Principles
- Attention capacity is limited and depletes with use
- Not all tasks deserve equal attention investment
- Mindless scrolling and reactive task-switching drain the budget without producing value
- Protecting peak attention hours for highest-value work maximizes return on the budget

## Application
- Identify tasks that require deep [[hyperfocus]] and schedule them during peak energy windows
- Allocate unfocused [[scatterfocus]] time for creativity and restoration
- Minimize attention residue by reducing unnecessary switches
- Use [[strategic-distraction]] to manage budget without total deprivation

## Connection to Existing Frameworks
This complements [[quadrant-2-focus]] by framing attention allocation as a resource management problem rather than just a scheduling one.
