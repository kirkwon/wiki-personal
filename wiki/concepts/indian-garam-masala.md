# Indian Garam Masala

Indian Garam Masala is a blend of ground spices commonly used in Indian cuisine to enhance flavor profiles. Typical ingredients include cumin, coriander, cardamom, and black peppercorns, among others. Its complexity contributes significantly to the depth and warmth characteristic of many traditional dishes.
