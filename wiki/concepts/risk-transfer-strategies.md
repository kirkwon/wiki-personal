# Risk Transfer Strategies

Methods for shifting risk from the investor to third parties through contractual, financial, or structural mechanisms.

## Contractual Transfer

- Lease provisions: expense reimbursement, guarantees, indemnification, insurance requirements, termination rights
- Management agreements: performance clauses, incentive-based fees, liability limitations

## Financial Transfer

- Insurance coverage (property, liability, rent guarantee, umbrella)
- Interest rate hedges (swaps, caps, collars)
- Currency hedges for international exposure

## Structural Transfer

- Entity separation to isolate risk between properties
- Bankruptcy remote entities
- Cross-border entity structuring

Risk transfer is one of three mitigation approaches in [[risk-assessment-framework]] alongside risk reduction and risk acceptance.
