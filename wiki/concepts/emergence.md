
# Emergence

The phenomenon where complex collective behavior arises from simple individual rules. Central to understanding why [[agent-based-modeling]] and [[cellular-automata]] are powerful — they demonstrate that sophisticated system-level patterns require no central designer.

## Key Examples from Page

- Traffic jams emerging from individual driving decisions
- Market prices emerging from individual buy/sell choices
- Cultural norms emerging from individual imitation
- Species diversity emerging from individual reproduction and selection

## Connection to Decision-Making

Emergence implies that [[top-down-control]] is often less effective than designing good rules and environments. This connects to [[incentive-design]] and [[systems-dynamics-models]].
