# Whisky Tasting Notes

Personal collection of 10 whisky bottles spanning bourbon, Scotch, and Tennessee whiskey, with systematic tasting notes covering nose, palate, finish, and overall verdict.

## Collection Overview

The collection ranges from entry-level ($12-18) to premium ($70-80), with total value approximately $250-350. Top-rated selections include Angel's Envy, Macallan 12, and Blanton's, all rated ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐.

## Key Recommendations

- **Best Value**: Buffalo Trace ($26) — sweet, approachable, hint of spice
- **Premium Picks**: Angel's Envy (port wine finish complexity), Macallan 12 (benchmark aged Scotch), Blanton's (collector's bourbon)
- **Daily Sippers**: Woodford Reserve ($35-40) — refined and elegant; Knob Creek 9 ($41) — aged complexity
- **High Proof Bargain**: Wild Turkey 101 ($25) — intense and assertive, great for cocktails

## Comparison Framework

Evaluation across proof, age, sweetness, spice, complexity, and value dimensions provides a structured way to compare whiskies across different tiers.
