
# Delphi Method

The term "Delphi method" is used in two distinct contexts: as a structured, iterative expert forecasting technique designed to aggregate expert judgments while minimizing social influence, and as a personal and organizational productivity optimization framework for systematically improving efficiency and output.

## The Delphi Method (Expert Forecasting Technique)

The Delphi method is an iterative expert forecasting technique that uses anonymous feedback to aggregate expert judgments while minimizing social influence and groupthink.

### Process

1. Experts independently submit forecasts or judgments
2. Results are aggregated and anonymized
3. Experts receive the aggregated results and revise their estimates
4. The process repeats for several rounds
5. Final consensus or median estimate is produced

### Key Features

- **Anonymity**: Experts do not know who else contributed, preserving independence
- **Iteration**: Multiple rounds allow experts to reconsider in light of collective feedback
- **Aggregation**: Statistical combination of individual estimates

### When to Use

The Delphi method is particularly effective when expert knowledge is needed but status effects, anchoring, or groupthink could distort individual judgments. It is less suitable when rapid decision-making is required or when the problem is well-defined enough that simple averaging would suffice.

## The Delphi Method (Productivity Framework)

This productivity framework involves systematically improving efficiency and output by identifying and implementing methods, techniques, and frameworks that maximize personal or organizational productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices. It differs significantly from the established RAND Corporation Delphi method, which is a structured, iterative forecasting technique using anonymous expert panels.

### 4-Step Framework

1. **Audit Your Current Workflow** — Identify where efficiency losses occur, track how you currently spend time and attention, and note patterns that lead to wasted effort.
2. **Apply the Framework** — Restructure your approach to focus on high-impact activities and eliminate or delegate low-value tasks.
3. **Iterate and Refine** — Measure the impact of changes, adjust based on results, and build consistency through habit formation.
4. **Scale What Works** — Standardize effective approaches, document processes for reuse, and teach others to multiply impact.

### Application Areas

- **Personal Productivity**: Weekly planning, managing competing priorities, breaking down complex projects.
- **Professional Context**: Team coordination, meeting efficiency, decision-making processes.
- **Learning and Growth**: Skill acquisition, organizing and retaining information, identifying knowledge gaps.

### Related Concepts

This framework connects to [[scatterfocus]], [[traffic]], [[conditions]], [[Problems]], and is loosely associated with [[the-wisdom-of-crowds]] through James Surowiecki's work on collective intelligence, though the connection is thematic rather than methodological.

## Note on Terminology

This wiki page covers two distinct concepts that share the name "Delphi method." The classic Delphi method, developed by the RAND Corporation, is a structured, iterative forecasting technique using anonymous expert panels to aggregate judgments and minimize groupthink. The productivity framework described here is a separate approach focused on workflow optimization and efficiency, which shares only a thematic connection to the classic method through the concept of iterative refinement and collective intelligence.
