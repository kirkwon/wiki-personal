# Responsibility Awareness

The concept that meaning is found through taking responsibility and committing to something or someone. Frankl argued that self-actualization cannot be pursued directly — it emerges as a side effect of dedication to a cause greater than oneself or of loving another person.

## Key Principles
- Meaning arises through responsibility, not through self-absorption
- Commitment to work, relationships, or a mission provides the structure within which meaning emerges
- Awareness of one's responsibility is the starting point for purposeful living
