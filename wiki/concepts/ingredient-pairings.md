# Ingredient Pairings

## Overview

Ingredient pairings refer to the principles of combining foods and flavors so they complement each other harmoniously. Understanding what works with what is foundational to good cooking, drawing on flavor science and cultural traditions. This concept is closely related to [[flavor-theory]] and [[aromatics-the-holy-trinity]], which provide frameworks for understanding why certain combinations succeed.

## Key Concepts

- **Flavor compatibility** — Certain tastes and aromatics naturally enhance each other
- **Contrast and balance** — Pairing rich with acidic, sweet with salty, or creamy with sharp
- **Cultural pairings** — Traditional combinations rooted in regional cuisines (e.g., soy sauce with ginger and garlic in Chinese cooking)
- **Umami building blocks** — Layering savory ingredients like soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil to create depth
