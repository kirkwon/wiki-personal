# Skin in the Game - Nassim Nicholas Taleb

Nassim Nicholas Taleb's Skin in the Game is a foundational principle asserting that decision-makers must bear the consequences of their actions. This concept is closely tied to the broader mental model of [[accountability]], which frames accountability as a universal tool for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition.

## Core Idea
Agents who make decisions should experience both the upside and downside of those decisions. This symmetry prevents reckless behavior and aligns incentives between decision-makers and those affected by their choices.

## Connection to Accountability
Accountability extends beyond Taleb's skin-in-the-game by positioning itself as a broader conceptual framework for understanding complex situations. While skin-in-the-game focuses on the consequences borne by agents, accountability encompasses the wider principle of being answerable, transparent, and responsive to the outcomes one creates.
