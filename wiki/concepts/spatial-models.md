# Spatial Models

Models of how location and geography affect behavior. These capture the role of physical or conceptual space in determining interactions, outcomes, and dynamics.

## Key Ideas

- Proximity matters: nearby agents interact more
- Spatial segregation emerges from mild preference
- Clustering and agglomeration effects
- Geographic constraints shape economic and social patterns

## Connection to Other Models

Spatial models extend [[agent-based-modeling]] by placing agents in physical or abstract space, and connect to [[network-models]] through spatial networks.
