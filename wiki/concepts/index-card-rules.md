# The Index Card Rules

A financial framework asserting that all essential personal finance advice can be reduced to 10 simple, actionable rules. The philosophy centers on simplicity, automation, low costs, and broad diversification as the path to financial health.

## Core Principles

- **Simplicity beats complexity** — 10 rules instead of 1000 books
- **Automate everything** — removing willpower from the equation through automatic transfers and payments
- **Index funds work** — broad-market low-cost funds outperform most active managers over time
- **Fees matter** — small differences in expense ratios compound dramatically over decades
- **Boring is beautiful** — reliability and consistency beat excitement and speculation

## The 10 Rules

1. **Save 10-20% of income** — Start with 1%, increase by 1% each month, automate transfers. Prioritize emergency fund → employer 401(k) match → Roth IRA → remaining 401(k) → taxable accounts.
2. **Pay off credit cards in full** — Automate full balance payments. Credit card debt carries 18-25%+ interest, destroying wealth.
3. **Max out tax-advantaged accounts** — 401(k), Roth IRA, HSA, 529 plans. Priority: employer match (100% return) → HSA (triple tax advantage) → Roth IRA (tax-free growth).
4. **Never buy individual stocks** — Stock picking is extraordinarily difficult. Diversification through index funds is the reliable alternative.
5. **Buy low-cost index funds** — VTSAX (0.04%), VFIAX (0.04%), FXAIX (0.015%), SWTSX (0.03%). Over 30 years, a 1% fee costs 25%+ of final balance versus 0.04%.
6. **Rebalance annually** — Maintain age-appropriate allocation (20s: 90/10 stocks/bonds, shifting to 50/50 by 60s). Target-date funds handle this automatically.
7. **Avoid too-good-to-be-true products** — Red flags: high returns with low risk, complex fees, pressure to buy now. Skip whole life insurance, private placements, leveraged ETFs.
8. **Buy affordable housing** — Max 2-3x annual income, housing cost < 28-30% of gross income. Rent if moving within 7 years.
9. **Insurance against catastrophe** — Health, disability, auto, home/renters, term life (10-12x income). Skip extended warranties and gimmick policies.
10. **Create a will and advance directives** — Will, power of attorney, healthcare proxy, living will. Include digital assets. Update at major life changes.

## Implementation

The framework provides a 12-month implementation timeline starting with automating savings and paying off credit cards, progressing through tax-advantaged account setup and index fund purchases, and culminating in portfolio optimization.

## Connection to Behavioral Design

The automation emphasis connects to behavior-change principles — removing willpower friction through systems design. This maps to frameworks like the [[fizzgogg-behavior-model]] and [[habit-testing]], where external triggers and reduced friction increase follow-through rates.
