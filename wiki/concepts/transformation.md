# Transformation

Transformation is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world. As a universal mental model, it is applicable across all domains of life and decision-making.

The core idea is that by recognizing recurring patterns and relational structures, one can reduce the complexity of novel situations and apply insights from one domain to another. This aligns with other mental-model entries in the wiki such as [[recipes-mental-model]], [[solutions-mental-model]], and [[planning]], all of which function as navigational tools through pattern recognition.
