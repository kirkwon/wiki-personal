# Regulatory Risk

Regulatory risk is the potential for changes in laws and regulations to negatively impact the value or viability of alternative investments. This affects real estate (rent control), cryptocurrency (bans/regulation), natural resources (environmental rules), and tax treatment across asset classes.

## Examples

- Rent control changes affecting real estate cash flows
- Cryptocurrency bans or restrictive regulation
- Environmental regulations impacting timber and mining
- Tax law changes affecting depreciation, carried interest, 1031 exchanges, or capital gains rates

## Mitigation

- Stay informed on regulatory developments
- Geographic diversification
- Legal compliance
- Plan exit strategies
