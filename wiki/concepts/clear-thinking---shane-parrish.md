# Clear Thinking: Turning Ordinary Moments into Extraordinary Results

*Clear Thinking* by Shane Parrish advocates for disciplined, process-oriented thinking to improve outcomes in everyday situations. The book frames clear thinking as a skill that can be developed through structured approaches.

## Key Ideas
- Ordinary moments can produce extraordinary results through disciplined thinking
- Process-oriented approaches outperform reactive intuition
- This aligns with the Processes mental model's emphasis on structured frameworks
