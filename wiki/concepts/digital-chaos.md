# Digital Chaos

Digital chaos refers to the state of information overload and clutter in digital environments that undermines productivity and creative output. Tiago Forte identifies it as the core problem his second brain framework addresses — the accumulation of unorganized digital content across tools, files, and platforms that makes it difficult to find, use, and build upon stored knowledge.

The antidote, according to Forte, is a structured personal knowledge system that provides consistent capture, organization, and retrieval mechanisms. This concept connects to [[categorizing-information]] as a solution and [[personal-knowledge-vault]] as a broader framework for managing digital knowledge.
