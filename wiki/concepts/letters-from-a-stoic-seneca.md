# Letters from a Stoic - Seneca

Seneca's Letters from a Stoic is a classical philosophical work that addresses themes of time management, priorities, and the wise use of life's finite resources. Seneca's reflections on how we allocate our time and attention resonate with modern productivity frameworks, including the [[audit-apply-iterate-scale-framework]] and concepts like [[attention-budget]].

## Relevance

Seneca's writings emphasize the importance of directing energy toward what truly matters, a principle that underpins modern time management and priority setting practices. The work connects to contemporary concepts such as [[scatterfocus]] and the broader discipline of managing attention as a finite resource.
