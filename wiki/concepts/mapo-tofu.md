# Mapo Tofu

Mapo Tofu is a classic and highly popular dish from Sichuan cuisine of China. It features silken tofu set in a savory and spicy sauce known for its numbing heat, characteristic of the region's chili oil and Sichuan peppercorns (mala flavor). The dish typically incorporates ground meat (such as beef or pork) and aromatic seasonings like fermented black beans and ginger. Due to its intense umami and signature tingling spice, it is a staple comfort food in Chinese cuisine.
