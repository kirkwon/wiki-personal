# Six Paths Framework

The **Six Paths Framework** provides six systematic approaches to reconstruct market boundaries and discover blue ocean opportunities. Each path challenges assumptions about where the market begins and ends, revealing untapped demand and possibilities for value innovation.

1. **Look across alternative industries** — What are the alternative industries to your own, and what factors do they compete on? What alternatives do customers face?
2. **Look across strategic groups** within industries — What are the different tiers of products within your industry, and what trade-offs do they offer? What are the different groupings within the industry?
3. **Look across the buyer chain** — Who are the different buyers in the chain, and what is the total buyer utility? Who are the real purchasers, users, and influencers?
4. **Look across complementary offerings** — What complementary products or services surround your offering? What products and services accompany the industry offering?
5. **Look across functional/emotional appeal** — Where does the industry compete on functionality vs. emotion? What aspects of the offering drive utility?
6. **Look across time** — What trends are shaping the industry, and which are decisive?

This framework extends the [[strategy-canvas]] by providing specific directions for where to look for boundary-reconstruction opportunities. It connects to [[non-customer-analysis]] in that looking across buyer chains and alternative industries often reveals non-customers who would be willing to trade across the industry line.
