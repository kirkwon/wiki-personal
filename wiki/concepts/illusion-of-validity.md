# Illusion of Validity

The overconfidence in predictions and our ability to foresee the future. Even when past predictions have been poor, we maintain faith in our predictive abilities.

## Key Points
- Connects to [[forecast-illusion]] — expert predictions failing
- Connects to [[overconfidence-and-certainty]] — overestimating abilities
- Connects to [[expert-illusion]] — experts overestimating knowledge
- More information and experience often increase (rather than decrease) overconfidence

## Practical Guardrail
Track your predictions over time. If your forecast accuracy doesn't improve with more information, you may be experiencing illusion of validity. Use [[reference-class-forecasting]] to calibrate.
