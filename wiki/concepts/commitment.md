# Commitment

Commitment is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Commitment functions as a universal mental model applicable across all domains. It serves as a mental shortcut for pattern and relationship recognition in complex situations.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

The concept is referenced in [[deep-work---cal-newport]], where commitment to sustained, focused work is identified as a distinguishing trait that separates high performers from others. Cal Newport argues that the ability to commit to deep, distraction-free work is increasingly rare and valuable in an age of constant connectivity.

## Connections

Commitment relates to several existing mental models in the wiki:

- [[effort]] — sustained exertion toward a goal
- [[Perseverance]] — continuing despite difficulty
- [[grit-concept]] — passion and perseverance over time
- [[attention-budget]] — treating attention as a finite resource to be allocated deliberately

The distinction of commitment as a mental model lies in its framing as a navigational principle — a way to make sense of complex situations through pattern recognition — rather than purely a behavioral trait.

## Open Questions

What specifically does "Commitment" add beyond what [[effort]] and [[Perseverance]] already cover? Potential distinctions include social/professional commitment, binding promises, or strategic commitment to a direction.
