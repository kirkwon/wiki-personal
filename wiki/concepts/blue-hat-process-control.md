# Blue Hat - Process Control

The Blue Hat is the metaprocess hat that manages the overall discussion and coordinates all other hats. It serves as the facilitator's role, deciding which hat to use next, setting the sequence of perspectives, and ensuring the group stays on track. As metacognition over the discussion, it represents the mode of thinking focused on managing and coordinating the thinking process itself.

## Key Practices

- Define the goals and agenda of the thinking session
- Decide the order in which hats are used
- Manage the sequence and timing of hat usage
- Manage time and attention during discussion
- Ensure balanced consideration of all viewpoints
- Summarize and synthesize insights from all perspectives
- Identify when the group has explored a perspective sufficiently and should move on

## Role in Decision-Making

The Blue Hat acts as the orchestrator of the thinking process, encapsulating and coordinating the other hats' functionalities to achieve comprehensive problem analysis and resolution. It ensures that the process itself is deliberate rather than chaotic, and that all perspectives receive attention.
