# Covered Calls

A covered call strategy involves selling call options against stock you already own, generating premium income. This is one of the most conservative and widely used options strategies for income generation.

## How It Works

- **Setup**: Own 100 shares of stock, sell a call option against it
- **Income**: Collect premium from the call sale; premium provides income regardless of whether the stock is assigned
- **Theta Benefit**: Earn from time decay while holding the stock
- **Total Return**: Premium income plus any appreciation up to the strike price

## Example

- **Stock**: Own 100 shares at $50
- **Call**: Sell $55 call for $3 premium
- **Income**: $300 per contract from premium
- **Theta Benefit**: $300 from time decay
- **Total Return**: 6% from premium + any appreciation up to $55

## Applications

- **Income Generation**: Regular premium income on existing holdings. Works well in flat or mildly rising markets.
- **Retirement Income**: Generate regular income on retirement holdings
- **Portfolio Enhancement**: Boost returns on existing positions
- **LEAPS Covered Calls**: Income generation on long-term LEAPS positions
- **Conservative Income**: Deep ITM covered calls for higher probability income. Example: Stock at $50, sell $45 call for $6. Generates 12% annualized return but limits upside.

## Risk Considerations

- Limited upside (stock may be assigned if it rises above strike)
- Opportunity cost: capped gains above strike price
- Need to manage assignment risk
- Modest returns: income is limited to premium collected
- Not suitable in IRA/401(k) accounts where naked options are prohibited
- Theta benefit offsets but does not eliminate stock risk
