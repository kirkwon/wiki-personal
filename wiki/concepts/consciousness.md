# Consciousness

The state of being aware of and able to think about one's own existence, sensations, thoughts, and environment. A central problem in philosophy of mind, cognitive science, and AI.

## Key Questions
- **The Hard Problem** (Chalmers): Why is there subjective experience at all? Why not just information processing without qualia?
- **Functionalism**: Consciousness is what the brain's information processing *does* — substrate-independent
- **Integrated Information Theory** (Tononi): Consciousness = Φ, the degree of integrated information in a system
- **Global Workspace Theory** (Baars): Consciousness is what's in the global workspace accessible to multiple cognitive modules

## Relevance to AI
Whether LLMs are conscious is an open philosophical question. Functionalism suggests they could be (information processing), while most neuroscientists argue they lack the requisite biological architecture. For practical purposes, treat them as conscious in the sense of having internal state, but not in the sense of having ethical standing.
