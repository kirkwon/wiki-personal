# Leadership for Complex Change

Meadows argues that effective leadership in complex systems requires understanding system dynamics rather than relying on authority or command-and-control approaches. Leaders must be able to see feedback loops, recognize leverage points, and design interventions that work with — rather than against — the system's own dynamics.

This includes creating conditions for self-organization, respecting the nonlinear nature of change, and being patient with delays between action and effect. The framework connects to [[building-a-cohesive-team]] by suggesting that team and organizational leadership benefits from systems awareness.
