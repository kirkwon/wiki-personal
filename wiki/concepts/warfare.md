# Warfare

The broader concept of strategic conflict—organized, competitive struggle between opposing forces. *The Art of War* treats warfare as an extreme case that reveals universal principles of strategy applicable to any competitive domain.

## Key Ideas
- **War is costly**: Prolonged conflict destroys resources and morale (Chapter 2).
- **Strategy over force**: Superior planning and positioning matter more than raw strength.
- **Avoid unnecessary conflict**: The best victory is one achieved without battle (Chapter 3).
