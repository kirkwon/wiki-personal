# 70-80% Budget Rule

A budgeting heuristic sometimes referenced as allocating 70-80% of income to needs, with the remainder split between savings and discretionary spending. This concept is not well-documented in the source material and requires further sourcing to clarify its specific definition and application.

This rule may relate to broader budgeting frameworks such as the 50/30/20 rule, which allocates 50% to needs, 30% to wants, and 20% to savings. The 70-80% variant could represent a more conservative allocation strategy for individuals prioritizing essential expenses.

Further research is needed to determine the origin, scope, and recommended application of this rule.
