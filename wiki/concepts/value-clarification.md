# Value clarification

Value clarification is the systematic practice of improving efficiency and output by identifying and implementing methods, techniques, and frameworks that maximize personal or organizational productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices.

## Core Process

Value clarification follows a four-step cycle:

1. **Audit** — Identify where efficiency losses occur, track how time and attention are spent, and note patterns that lead to wasted effort.
2. **Apply** — Restructure your approach to focus on high-impact activities and eliminate or delegate low-value tasks.
3. **Iterate** — Measure the impact of changes, adjust based on results, and build consistency through habit formation.
4. **Scale** — Standardize effective approaches, document processes for reuse, and teach others to multiply impact.

## Application Domains

- **Personal productivity** — Planning weeks more effectively, managing competing priorities, breaking down complex projects.
- **Professional context** — Team coordination, meeting efficiency, decision-making processes.
- **Learning and growth** — Accelerating skill acquisition, organizing and retaining information, identifying and filling knowledge gaps.

## Connection to Seneca

The intellectual anchor for this skill is Seneca's *On the Shortness of Life*, which argues that most people fail not from lack of time but from lack of intentionality in how they spend it. Value clarification operationalizes this insight through practical workflow auditing and iterative improvement.
