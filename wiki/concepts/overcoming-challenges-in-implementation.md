# Overcoming Challenges in Implementation

De Bono addresses practical obstacles to adopting lateral thinking approaches, including resistance to unconventional ideas, difficulty sustaining creative habits, and organizational cultures that reward conformity over exploration. The book provides tips for overcoming these obstacles through incremental adoption, habit formation, and creating safe spaces for divergent thinking.

This connects to [[building-a-cohesive-team]], which addresses how team dynamics can either enable or hinder creative collaboration, and to broader [[decision-making-frameworks]] that require organizational buy-in to be effective.
