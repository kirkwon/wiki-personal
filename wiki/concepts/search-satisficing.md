# Search Satisficing

The tendency to stop searching prematurely and settle for adequate rather than optimal solutions. We reach a threshold of "good enough" and stop looking, potentially missing better options.

## Key Points
- Connects to optimization vs satisfaction tradeoffs
- Connects to [[decision-paralysis]] — inability to decide
- Connects to [[opportunity-cost]] — cost of not finding better options
- Sometimes satisficing is rational given search costs

## Guardrail
Distinguish between cases where search costs justify stopping early (satisficing) and cases where you're stopping due to impatience or avoidance.
