# Signs of Progress

Signs of Progress are indicators that you are moving toward a solution while working through a problem. Polya emphasizes the importance of recognizing these signals—small breakthroughs, partial solutions, or patterns that emerge during the process. Being attentive to signs of progress helps maintain momentum and prevents premature abandonment of viable approaches.

Related heuristics include "Did you use all the data?", "Look at the unknown", and "Here is a problem related to yours and solved before."
