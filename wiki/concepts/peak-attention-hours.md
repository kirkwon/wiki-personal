# Peak Attention Hours

Peak attention hours refer to the individual windows during the day when cognitive energy and focus capacity are at their highest. These windows vary by person and are shaped by chronotype, sleep, and habits.

## Key Insight
Working during peak hours on tasks requiring deep [[hyperfocus]] produces disproportionately better results than working during low-energy periods. Protecting these windows from meetings, email, and interruptions is one of the highest-leverage productivity practices.

## Application
- Map your personal energy curve over several days
- Schedule demanding, focused work during peak windows
- Use low-energy periods for administrative tasks, [[scatterfocus]], or breaks
- Align with [[attention-budget]] principles by allocating highest-value work to highest-capacity time

This concept extends [[time-management-matrix]] by adding a biological dimension—when you work matters as much as what you work on.
