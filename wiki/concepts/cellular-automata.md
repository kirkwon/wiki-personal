# Cellular Automata

Models where complex behavior emerges from simple rules applied to cells on a grid. Each cell's next state depends on its own state and its neighbors' states according to a fixed rule set.

## Key Insight

Demonstrates that [[emergent-complexity-from-simple-rules]] is possible — even trivially simple rules can produce patterns of extraordinary sophistication, including computation and self-organization.

## Famous Examples

- Conway's Game of Life
- Rule 110 (Turing complete)
- Models of crystal growth, fire spread, urban development

## Connection to Agent-Based Modeling

Cellular automata are a specific form of agent-based model where agents are cells on a lattice with fixed interaction neighborhoods.
