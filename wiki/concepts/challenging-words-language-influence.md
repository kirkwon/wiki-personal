# Challenging Words / Language Influence

De Bono emphasizes that the words we use to describe problems shape how we think about them. Terminology can create invisible boundaries that constrain creative exploration. By deliberately replacing or challenging words associated with a problem, new perspectives and possibilities emerge.

This concept connects to [[framing-effects-in-decision-making]], which explores how the presentation of options influences choices. Where framing effects typically describe cognitive biases in decision contexts, de Bono's approach is prescriptive: actively change the language to change the thinking. The [[green-hat-new-ideas]] perspective in the Six Thinking Hats framework aligns with this technique by encouraging the deliberate generation of alternative framings.
