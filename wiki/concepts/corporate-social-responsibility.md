# Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

An examination of modern CSR initiatives, analyzing their effectiveness in addressing systemic issues and creating meaningful change within corporations and society.

## Key Discussion Points
- Effectiveness of current CSR initiatives in addressing systemic issues
- The gap between CSR rhetoric and meaningful change
- How corporations can move beyond superficial sustainability efforts
- Integration of social responsibility into core business strategy rather than as an add-on

## Relation to Responsible Capitalism
CSR is one mechanism within the broader [[responsible-capitalism]] framework for rebalancing profit with social and environmental goals.
