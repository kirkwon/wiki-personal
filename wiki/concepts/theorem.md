# Theorem

Theorem is a universal mental model that provides a conceptual shortcut for understanding and navigating complex situations. It helps make sense of patterns and relationships in the world by offering a framework for sense-making in complex systems.

## Definition

A conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This mental model is grounded in John Gall's *The Systems Bible*, which outlines several key principles about how systems behave. Key insights include:

- Complex systems evolve from simple systems that work.
- Systems tend to operate in ways that resist change regardless of need.
- Every system is perfectly designed to get the results it gets.

## Distinction from Related Models

Theorem differs from general theory or abstract principle by grounding itself specifically in systems thinking—focusing on how complex systems behave, evolve, and resist intervention.
