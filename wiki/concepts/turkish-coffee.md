# Turkish Coffee

Turkish coffee is a traditional brewing method originating from Turkey that involves finely ground coffee beans boiled in a pot with water and often sugar, without filtering the grounds. It's known for its strong flavor and thick consistency, typically served in small cups accompanied by a spoon to catch the sediment. The preparation process also includes a ritual of reading fortunes from the coffee grounds left after drinking.
