# Entity Management Software

Entity Management Software (EMS) is a specialized data solution designed to systematically track, structure, and maintain information about real-world objects or concepts—known as entities. These software systems consolidate disparate data sources into unified profiles, ensuring consistent identification and relationships between people, organizations, products, or locations. It is crucial for enterprise resource planning (ERP), Customer Relationship Management (CRM), and knowledge graphs to prevent data silos and accurately map complex business networks.
