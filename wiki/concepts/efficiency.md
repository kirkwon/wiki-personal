# Efficiency

Efficiency is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Efficiency.md]

Efficiency as a mental model emphasizes structured, repeatable processes over improvisation. This aligns with Atul Gawande's checklist thinking in [[the-checklist-manifesto---atul-gawande]], where efficiency in complex tasks such as surgery comes from systematic adherence to proven steps rather than relying on individual brilliance alone.^[raw/articles/the-checklist-manifesto---atul-gawande.md]

The model differs from pure optimization in that it focuses on eliminating waste and reducing cognitive load rather than simply maximizing output. It connects to broader themes of [[systematic-decision-making]] and [[recipes-mental-model]], where structured frameworks serve as navigational tools for complexity.^[raw/articles/Efficiency.md]
