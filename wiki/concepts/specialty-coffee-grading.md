# Specialty Coffee Grading

Specialty coffee grading is a quality classification system used to evaluate coffee beans based on score, defect levels, and processing standards.

## Grade System
- **Grade 1**: 80+ points, defect-free
- **Grade 2**: 80 points, minor defects allowed
- **Premium**: Specialty grade origin
- **Commercial**: Lower quality, defects common

## Defect Categories
- **Primary**: Black, sour, foreign matter
- **Secondary**: Floater, broken, insect damage
- **Quakers**: Underdeveloped beans, no flavor
- **Stinker**: Fermented, off-flavors

The grading system helps consumers and roasters identify quality levels and supports the premium pricing of specialty coffee.
