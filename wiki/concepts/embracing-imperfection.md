# Embracing Imperfection

Embracing imperfection is the practice of accepting uncertainty and limitations as prerequisites for recognizing when a situation may require quitting. Annie Duke argues that perfectionism and the desire for certainty can blind us to signals that a commitment is no longer serving us.

This concept connects to [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]] — a decision can be high quality even when the outcome is poor, and recognizing that distinction requires accepting that we operate under uncertainty. It also relates to [[embracing-failure-for-personal-growth]], where Duke emphasizes that losses provide valuable learning opportunities.

The core insight is that clinging to the illusion of control or perfect information prevents us from making timely quitting decisions. By embracing imperfection, we become more attuned to when persistence has become counterproductive.
