# Everything Is F_cked - Mark Manson

Mark Manson's *Everything Is F_cked: A Book About Hope* introduces the concept of "dark hope" — a philosophical stance that acknowledges the fundamental brokenness and uncertainty of the world while still choosing to engage with it meaningfully. This perspective contributes to the broader resilience mental model by framing hope not as optimism but as a conscious decision to act despite awareness of despair.

## Key Ideas
- The world is inherently flawed and uncertain
- Hope emerges from accepting this reality rather than denying it
- Suffering and difficulty are not obstacles to be eliminated but part of the human condition to be navigated

See also [[will---mark-manson]] for Manson's broader work on will and motivation.
