# Make It Stick

"Make It Stick" by Peter C. Brown, Henry Roediger, and Mark McDaniel is a cognitive science book on evidence-based learning techniques. It advocates for retrieval practice, spaced repetition, and interleaving over common but ineffective methods like re-reading and massed practice. The book synthesizes decades of memory research into practical strategies for durable, flexible learning.
