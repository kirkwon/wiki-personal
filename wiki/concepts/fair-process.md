# Fair Process

Fair Process is an implementation principle that builds trust and commitment for bold strategies. It rests on three pillars:

1. **Engagement** – Involve people in the decisions that affect them
2. **Explanation** – Explain the reasoning behind decisions
3. **Expectation clarity** – Make clear what is expected

Fair process is essential for executing blue ocean strategies because these strategies often require organizational change and may face resistance. When people feel the process is fair, they are more likely to commit to and support even ambitious initiatives.
