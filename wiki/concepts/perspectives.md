# Perspectives

Perspectives is a universal mental model that serves as a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations. It provides a framework for recognizing patterns and relationships in the world, functioning as a mental shortcut that helps make sense of complexity.

## Definition

At its core, Perspectives is a principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations by deliberately adopting different viewpoints or cognitive stances. This framework provides a structured way to make sense of patterns and relationships, counteracting the tendency to get locked into a single way of seeing a problem.

## Connection to Six Thinking Hats

The most concrete implementation of the Perspectives model is Edward de Bono's Six Thinking Hats, which provides six distinct cognitive perspectives:
- **White Hat** – Facts and data
- **Red Hat** – Emotions and intuition
- **Black Hat** – Cautionary, critical perspective
- **Yellow Hat** – Positive, optimistic viewpoints
- **Green Hat** – New ideas and creativity
- **Blue Hat** – Process control and meta-cognition

By deliberately switching between these hats, practitioners can examine a situation from multiple angles, reducing blind spots and improving decision quality.
