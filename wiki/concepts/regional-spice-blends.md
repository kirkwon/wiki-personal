# Regional Spice Blend Systems

Spice blends are organized frameworks of complementary spices that define regional cuisines. Cumin serves as a foundational element across multiple global blend traditions.

## Mexican Blend
- **Base**: Cumin + Chili powder + Oregano
- **Uses**: Taco seasoning, chili, stews
- **Signature**: Smoky, earthy, moderately spicy

## Indian Blend
- **Base**: Cumin + Coriander + Turmeric + Garam masala
- **Uses**: Curry bases, vegetable dishes, lentils
- **Signature**: Complex, warming, aromatic

## Middle Eastern Blend
- **Base**: Cumin + Coriander + Cinnamon + Allspice
- **Uses**: Lamb dishes, rice pilafs, stews
- **Signature**: Warm, aromatic, slightly sweet

## Mediterranean Blend
- **Base**: Cumin + Paprika + Oregano + Garlic
- **Uses**: Spanish stews, vegetable dishes
- **Signature**: Rustic, earthy, savory

The cumin-coriander pairing appears in all four systems, making it the most universal spice combination in global cooking. These blend systems connect to broader [[flavor-theory]] principles and the practice of [[ingredient-pairings]] in global cuisines.
