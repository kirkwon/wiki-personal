# Nonlinearity and Emergent Properties

Nonlinearity means that in complex systems, small inputs can produce disproportionate outputs, and outputs are not proportional to inputs. Emergent properties are system-level behaviors that arise from interactions among components but cannot be predicted from those components alone.

Meadows explores this through case studies and models, showing how chaos theory and system dynamics reveal why complex systems resist simple prediction. This principle undermines linear cause-and-effect reasoning and connects to [[decision-making-under-uncertainty]] by explaining why probabilistic thinking and scenario planning are necessary in complex environments.
