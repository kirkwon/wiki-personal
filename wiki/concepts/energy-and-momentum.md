# Energy and Momentum

The management of force and momentum in strategic action. From *The Art of War* Chapter 5, military forces are compared to water—accumulating energy through flow, seeking low ground, and applying force at the point of greatest advantage.

## Key Ideas
- **Water analogy**: Forces should flow like water—adapting shape and direction to terrain and circumstances.
- **Momentum through continuous action**: Energy builds through sustained, adaptive movement rather than single powerful strikes.
- **Timing of application**: Energy is most effective when applied at the right moment, not held in reserve indefinitely.
- **Conservation of resources**: Prolonged conflict drains energy—rapid resolution preserves strength.
