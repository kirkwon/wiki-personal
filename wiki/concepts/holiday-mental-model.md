# Holiday Mental Model

Holiday is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

This mental model is referenced in relation to Ryan Holiday's Stoic philosophy, drawing from [[the-obstacle-is-the-way-ryan-holiday]] and [[the-daily-stoic-ryan-holiday]]. It functions as a universal navigational principle applicable across domains, though the specific framework beyond Holiday's published works remains underdeveloped in the source material.

The model connects to broader Stoic resilience concepts found in [[the-obstacle-is-the-way]] and the stillness principle from [[stillness-is-the-key]].
