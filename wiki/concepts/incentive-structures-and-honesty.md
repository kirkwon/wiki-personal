# Incentive Structures and Honesty

How different reward systems promote or discourage dishonesty within organizations and social groups. Financial incentives, recognition systems, and performance metrics can either encourage ethical behavior or inadvertently create incentives for dishonesty.

## Key Points
- Financial incentives can influence honesty in market transactions
- Reward systems shape ethical behavior within organizations
- The design of incentive structures is critical for promoting integrity
- Understanding incentive effects connects to [[expected-value-ev]] and [[bet-sizing]] — people weigh perceived benefits against perceived risks

- Relates to [[ethical-design-manipulation-question]] — incentive design is a form of environmental ethics
- Connects to [[decision-making-under-uncertainty]] — people make dishonest decisions under uncertain incentive landscapes
