# Prediction aggregation

The practice of combining multiple forecasts to improve accuracy. Silver shows in Chapter 11 that collective intelligence often outperforms individual experts.

## Key Ideas

- Aggregated forecasts reduce individual bias and error
- Diverse perspectives capture more of the true signal
- Simple averaging or weighted aggregation often beats expert judgment
- Related to ensemble methods and superforecasting

## Examples

Political pundit predictions underperform aggregated polling data. Baseball sabermaticians outperform scout intuition through statistical aggregation.
