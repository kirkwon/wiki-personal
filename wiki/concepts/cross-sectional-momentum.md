# Cross-Sectional Momentum

Cross-sectional momentum compares stocks to each other within the same universe, ranking them by past performance and buying the top performers.

## Example

- Stock A: +20% in past 12 months → Buy
- Stock B: +5% in past 12 months → Neutral
- Stock C: -10% in past 12 months → Avoid

This contrasts with time-series momentum, which compares an asset to its own historical performance rather than to peers.

## Implementation

Rank all stocks in the universe by their 12-1 momentum score, then buy the top 20% (or top decile). Rebalance monthly.
