# Strategic Misrepresentation

The deliberate misrepresentation of information by people and organizations to influence outcomes. This ranges from selective framing to outright deception.

## Key Points
- Connects to [[authority-bias]] — uncritical acceptance of authority
- Connects to [[social-proof]] — looking to others for correct behavior
- Recognizing misrepresentation requires [[devil-s-advocate-perspective]]
- Sources may have incentives to present information favorably

## Guardrail
Consider what incentives the information source has and how they might benefit from you believing a particular narrative.
