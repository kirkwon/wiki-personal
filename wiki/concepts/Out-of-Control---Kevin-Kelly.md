# Out of Control - Kevin Kelly

Kevin Kelly's Out of Control reframes technology as emergent and decentralized, arguing that true control in complex systems is an illusion.^[raw/articles/Technology.md]

## Core Ideas
- Technological systems self-organize and adapt beyond direct management.
- Decentralization and feedback loops drive outcomes more than top-down control.
- Understanding technology requires seeing it as part of larger, dynamic environments.

## Connection to Mental Models
This perspective connects to [[technology-mental-model]] as a systemic complement to Newport's intentional-use approach. It also relates to [[settings]] and [[environment]]—how context and surrounding systems shape technological outcomes beyond individual control.
