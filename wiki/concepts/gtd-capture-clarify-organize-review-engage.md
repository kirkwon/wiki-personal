# GTD Capture, Clarify, Organize, Review, Engage

The five-stage GTD workflow provides a structured approach to managing commitments:

- **Capture** – Keep track of all ideas and tasks by capturing them systematically using tools like note-taking apps and physical notebooks. This externalizes mental clutter and prevents forgotten commitments.
- **Clarify** – Break down captured items, assign actionable goals, and establish context for each task to ensure efficiency in execution. Ambiguous items are converted into concrete next actions.
- **Organize** – Sort all aspects of life including emails, files, digital information, and personal belongings into an effective system that prevents overwhelm and maintains order.
- **Review** – Consistently revisit your organizational systems to ensure effectiveness, making necessary adjustments or improvements. Regular review prevents decay of the system.
- **Engage/Do** – Make informed decisions about what tasks and activities should receive priority based on their value and urgency, considering available time, energy, and context.

This workflow connects to broader decision-making frameworks like [[decision-making-frameworks]] and complements [[time-management-matrix]] and [[quadrant-2-focus]] by providing an operational system for prioritizing important but not urgent activities.
