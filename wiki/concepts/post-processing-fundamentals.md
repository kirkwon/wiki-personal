# Post-Processing Fundamentals

Post-processing is the editing phase after capturing a photograph. Basic adjustments include:

- **Cropping**: Improve composition
- **Exposure adjustment**: Brighten or darken
- **Contrast**: Enhance tonal range
- **White balance**: Correct color cast
- **Clarity/Dehaze**: Enhance details

These fundamentals complement the in-camera techniques of the [[exposure-triangle]] and the compositional principles in [[composition-techniques]]. Effective post-processing can also help recover detail lost in high-contrast situations like [[golden-hour]] shooting.
