# Ingredients (mental model)

Ingredients is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Ingredients.md]

The model is referenced in Timothy Ferriss's *The 4-Hour Chef* as a universal mental model applicable across domains.^[raw/articles/Ingredients.md]

## Key Ideas

- Serves as a mental shortcut for pattern and relationship recognition in complex situations
- Analogous to how ingredients underpin recipes, this model suggests that identifying fundamental components and their relationships can simplify navigation of complexity
- Applicable universally across domains
