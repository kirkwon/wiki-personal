# Step-Up in Basis

Step-up in basis is a tax benefit that occurs when inherited assets receive a new cost basis equal to their fair market value at the time of the original owner's death, rather than carrying over the original purchase price.

## How It Works
When an asset (such as real estate, business interests, or investment holdings) is inherited, the heir's cost basis is "stepped up" to the asset's current fair market value. This can eliminate or dramatically reduce capital gains tax when the heir later sells the asset.

## Example
- Original owner buys property for $200,000
- Property is worth $500,000 at time of death
- Heir inherits property with $500,000 basis
- Heir sells for $500,000 → $0 capital gains tax

## Entity-Level Applications
- FLPs and family holding companies can facilitate controlled transfers with step-up benefits
- Multi-entity structures allow for orderly transfer of property-specific LLC interests
- Dynasty trusts can preserve step-up benefits across generations
