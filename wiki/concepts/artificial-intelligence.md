# Artificial Intelligence

The theory and development of computer systems able to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. A broad field encompassing machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, robotics, and reasoning systems.

## Scope
- **Narrow AI** — specialized systems (LLMs, chess engines, recommendation algorithms)
- **General AI** — hypothetical systems matching human breadth of intelligence
- **Agentic AI** — systems that act autonomously toward goals (Hermes, Claude Code, Gemini CLI)

## Relevance
AI is the infrastructure layer underlying this entire knowledge ecosystem — Hermes, gbrain, NotebookLM, the causal inference pipeline, and the meta-critic calibration loop. Understanding AI's capabilities and limitations is foundational to effective leverage.
