# Commitments

Commitments is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

As a universal mental model, Commitments functions across all domains—personal, professional, and philosophical—offering a lens through which to evaluate the trade-offs and obligations that shape outcomes. It connects to broader decision-making frameworks by emphasizing the importance of being intentional about what one commits to, rather than defaulting to reactive accumulation of obligations.

This model complements existing mental models like [[planning]], [[Solutions]], and [[stillness]] by adding a layer of discipline around how commitments are made, tracked, and honored. Where [[decision-making-frameworks]] focus on evaluating options, Commitments asks: *What should I commit to, and what should I let go?*

The model is grounded in Greg McKeown's philosophy of essentialism, which advocates for the disciplined pursuit of less.^[raw/articles/Commitments.md]
