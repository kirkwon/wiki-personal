# Mirroring neurons

Mirroring neurons are neurological cells that fire both when an individual performs an action and when they observe the same action performed by another. This neurological mechanism enables empathy and connection in conversations, forming a basis for the social motivation system described in *Supercommunicators*.

## Role in communication
- Enable unconscious empathy during interactions
- Help people mirror emotional states of others
- Facilitate nonverbal understanding and rapport
- Underpin the instinct to connect through shared experience
