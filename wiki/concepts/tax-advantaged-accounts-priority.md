# Tax-Advantaged Accounts Priority

The recommended order for utilizing tax-advantaged accounts, based on the Index Card Rules framework. Each account type offers different tax benefits, and the priority order maximizes those benefits.

## Priority Order

1. **Employer 401(k) match** — 100% return on contribution (free money)
2. **HSA** (if eligible) — triple tax advantage (deductible contribution, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawal for medical)
3. **Roth IRA** — tax-free growth forever
4. **Remaining 401(k) contributions** — pre-tax reduction of taxable income
5. **Taxable investment account** — after exhausting tax-advantaged options

## Account Limits (2024)

- 401(k)/403(b): up to $23,000 + employer match
- Roth IRA: up to $7,000
- HSA: up to $4,150
- 529 Plans: for education expenses (state tax benefits)

## Why This Order

- Employer match is an immediate 100% return — unmatched by any other investment
- HSA offers three layers of tax advantage
- Roth IRA provides tax-free growth for life
- Taxable accounts are least tax-efficient but necessary when tax-advantaged space is full
