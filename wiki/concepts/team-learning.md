# Team Learning

Team learning is one of Senge's five disciplines, discussing practices for fostering collective reflection, inquiry, and experimentation to enable teams to learn together effectively.

## Core Ideas

- Collective reflection, inquiry, and experimentation
- Open, honest communication and constructive conflict resolution promote learning
- Teams learn together more effectively than individuals learn in isolation
- Developing shared understanding of complex issues requires deliberate practice

## Key Practices

- Fostering collective reflection on team processes and outcomes
- Encouraging inquiry over defensiveness
- Experimentation that tests assumptions safely
- Constructive conflict resolution that deepens understanding
