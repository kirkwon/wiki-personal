# Investment Allocation by Age

A glidepath approach to asset allocation that shifts from aggressive growth in youth to conservative preservation near retirement.

**20s — Aggressive Growth**:
- Stocks: 80-90% (US large-cap 30-40%, mid/small-cap 20-30%, international 20-30%)
- Bonds: 0-10%
- Cash: 0-10%

**30s — High Growth**:
- Stocks: 70-80%
- Bonds: 15-20%
- Cash/Alternatives: 5-10%

**40s — Balanced Growth**:
- Stocks: 60-70%
- Bonds: 20-30%
- Cash/Alternatives: 5-10%

**50s — Moderate Growth**:
- Stocks: 50-60%
- Bonds: 30-40%
- Cash/Alternatives: 5-10%

**60s — Conservative**:
- Stocks: 30-40%
- Bonds: 40-50%
- Cash: 10-20%

This framework is part of the broader [[retirement-planning]] strategy and supports the sustainable withdrawal rates in the [[4-percent-rule]].
