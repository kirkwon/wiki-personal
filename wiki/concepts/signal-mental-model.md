# Signal (mental model)

Signal is a universal mental model that serves as a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Signal helps understand and navigate complexity by enabling pattern recognition — the ability to identify meaningful structures within noisy, ambiguous, or overwhelming information environments.

## Applicability

The model is positioned as universally applicable across general domains, serving as a foundational lens for analysis, decision-making, and sense-making.
