# In-Group/Out-Group Bias

In-group/out-group bias is the tendency to favor those who are similar to us (in-group) over those who are different (out-group), even when there is no rational basis for the distinction.

## Core Idea
This bias manifests in team dynamics, hiring decisions, political tribalism, and daily social interactions. It can lead to discrimination, groupthink, and missed opportunities for collaboration.

## Applications
- **Team building**: Recognize when you're favoring "people like us" over better candidates.
- **Negotiation**: Be aware of tribal dynamics that may cloud judgment.
- **Leadership**: Actively seek diverse perspectives to counteract this bias.

## Connection to Other Models
In-group/out-group bias connects to [[social-proof]] (conforming to group norms), [[authority-bias]] (trusting perceived insiders), and [[reciprocity]] (favoring those who have helped us). Weinberg includes it alongside empathy in the social and emotional intelligence section of *Super Thinking*.
