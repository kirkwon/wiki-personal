# Things

Things is a universal mental model for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It serves as a conceptual tool that provides a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Things functions as a generic framework for understanding complexity by identifying underlying patterns and relationships. Unlike domain-specific models, it is applicable across all areas of life and decision-making.

## Connection to Prioritization

Things connects closely to [[first-things-first---stephen-r-covey]] and the principle of focusing on what matters most. Stephen R. Covey's work on prioritization—distinguishing between urgent and important tasks—provides a cross-source perspective on how "things" can be organized and addressed effectively.
