# Empathy and Mental Models of Others

## Definition
Understanding other people's perspectives and actions by considering their underlying mental models. Empathy serves as a tool for mapping how others think, leading to better communication and collaboration.

## Explanation
Parrish argues that true empathy goes beyond feeling what others feel — it requires understanding the mental models that shape their decisions. By considering how others construct their reality, we can communicate more effectively and collaborate more productively.
