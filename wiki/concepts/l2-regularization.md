# L2 Regularization

L2 regularization is like adding just the right amount of salt to a dish; it prevents overfitting in machine learning models by penalizing large coefficients, similar to how too much seasoning can ruin a recipe. In photography, it's analogous to balancing exposure and contrast for a well-composed image. For personal finance, think of it as diversifying investments to reduce risk without sacrificing potential returns.
