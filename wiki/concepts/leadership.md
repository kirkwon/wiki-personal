# Leadership

Leadership is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

This mental model is applicable across all domains and serves as a universal principle for pattern and relationship recognition in complex situations. Confidence is moderate (0.7), with the model primarily referenced through Simon Sinek's Find Your Why, which emphasizes purpose-driven leadership.

Leadership connects to [[infinite-game-mindset]] and [[the-infinite-game---simon-sinek]], as Sinek's broader body of work centers on leadership as a function of clear purpose and inspired action. It also relates to broader mental-model frameworks including [[planning]], [[stillness]], and [[perspective]].
