
# Will to Truth

The Will to Truth is Nietzsche's examination of whether the desire for truth is itself a form of the Will to Power. This concept appears in Part One of *Beyond Good and Evil*.

## Core Question

Is truth-seeking a disinterested pursuit of objective reality, or is it a drive to impose order, control, and meaning on chaos? Nietzsche suggests that philosophers and scientists may be motivated by the Will to Power rather than by pure love of truth.

## Connections

This connects to [[overconfidence-and-certainty]] — Nietzsche's critique parallels modern research showing that certainty and truth-seeking can serve psychological needs rather than objective accuracy. It also relates to [[will-to-power]] as the deeper motivational force behind intellectual pursuits.
