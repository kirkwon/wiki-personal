# Single Property Analysis

A comprehensive breakdown of income, expenses, cash flow, depreciation, and tax impact for an individual real estate holding. This is the foundational template for evaluating a single investment property's performance.

## Property Overview
- Property address, type, purchase date, purchase price
- Current market value, property size, units, year built
- Financing details: loan amount, interest rate, loan term, monthly payment, down payment, LTV ratio

## Monthly Cash Flow Analysis
- **Income**: Rent, parking fees, laundry/vending, other income
- **Expenses**: Mortgage payment, property taxes, insurance, HOA, property management, maintenance, repairs, utilities, vacancy allowance, CapEx reserve
- **Net Operating Income (NOI)**: Income minus operating expenses
- **Net Cash Flow**: NOI minus debt service
- **Cash on Cash Return**: Net Cash Flow / Cash Investment

## Annual Performance Metrics
- Gross Rental Income, Operating Income, NOI
- Capitalization Rate: NOI / Property Value
- Cash on Cash Return: Annual Cash Flow / Cash Investment
- Total Return: (Cash Flow + Appreciation) / Cash Investment
- Break-Even Ratio: Operating Expenses / Gross Income
- Debt Coverage Ratio: NOI / Annual Debt Service

## Depreciation Analysis
- Building value vs land value (only building is depreciable)
- Depreciable basis = building value
- Annual depreciation: depreciable basis / 27.5 years
- Tax shield: annual depreciation × tax rate

## Tax Impact
- Annual depreciation, mortgage interest, property taxes, operating expenses
- Total deductible expenses
- Taxable income: NOI minus total deductible expenses
- Federal and state tax savings
