# Grantor Trust Status

An IRS classification where the grantor of a trust is treated as the owner of trust assets for income tax purposes, even though the trust may be irrevocable for estate tax purposes.

## Key Application

- **Intentionally Defective Grantor Trust (IDGT)**: Defective for income tax (grantor pays taxes) but not for estate tax (assets removed from estate)
- Allows tax-free transfer of appreciated assets
- Grantor must have sufficient resources to pay income taxes
- Provides step-up in basis at death

## Tax Implications

- Income earned by the trust is taxed to the grantor
- Trust assets removed from grantor's taxable estate
- Annual exclusion gifts can fund the trust
- Long-term holding strategy for appreciated assets
