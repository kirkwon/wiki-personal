# Diversification Strategies for Investments

Reducing portfolio risk through geographic, asset class, and strategy diversification.

## Geographic Diversification
Multiple cities (reduce local economic risk), multiple states (reduce regulatory risk), international exposure (currency and economic diversification). Target: local 40-50%, regional 20-30%, national 15-25%, international 5-10%.

## Asset Class Diversification
Real estate (different property types), financial assets (stocks, bonds, commodities), alternative investments (private equity, hedge funds).

## Strategy Diversification
Core (stable, cash-flow properties), Value-Add (renovation and optimization), Opportunistic (development and ground-up construction).
