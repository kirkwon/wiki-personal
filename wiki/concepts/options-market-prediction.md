# options-market-prediction

Options market prediction is an advanced field within quantitative finance that utilizes machine learning and statistical models to forecast future movements of option prices or underlying assets. These predictive systems analyze complex data points such as implied volatility surfaces, historical trading volume, and the Greeks ($\Delta, \Gamma, \Theta$). While specific methodologies often involve deep neural networks (DNNs) for time-series forecasting, the goal is generally to predict changes in market sentiment or expected volatility regimes.
