# Outcomes (mental model)

Outcomes is a universal conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. Unlike outcome quality vs decision quality, which focuses on evaluating the quality of decisions and their results separately, Outcomes serves as a broader mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

This model draws from multiple perspectives in judgment and decision literature:
- [[noise-a-flaw-in-human-judgment---daniel-kahneman]] on judgment noise
- [[Pre-Suasion_ A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade - Robert B Cialdini]] on influence and pre-suasion
- [[how-to-decide---annie-duke]] and [[thinking-in-bets---annie-duke]] on decision frameworks under uncertainty
- [[nudge---richard-h-thaler-cass-r-sunstein]] on behavioral nudges
- [[the-checklist-manifesto---atul-gawande]] on systematic approaches via checklists
- [[the-art-of-thinking-clearly---rolf-dobelli]] on cognitive biases
- [[quit-the-power-of-knowing-when-to-walk-away---annie-duke]] on when to disengage

The model provides a lens for understanding how outcomes emerge from complex interplays of judgment, influence, decision-making, and systemic factors. It is distinct from [[solutions-mental-model]] and [[effort]] in that it specifically emphasizes recognizing patterns and relationships rather than prescribing solutions or measuring effort.
