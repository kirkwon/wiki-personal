# Shareholder vs Stakeholder Prioritization

The tension between prioritizing shareholder returns versus serving the broader range of stakeholders—customers, employees, communities, and the environment—is a central theme in critiques of modern capitalism.

## The Shift
Galloway describes how corporate priorities have shifted from serving customers and employees to satisfying shareholder demands, with significant repercussions for businesses and society at large. This shift reflects the triumph of financial engineering over operational excellence and long-term value creation.

## Key Tensions
- Short-term profit pressure versus long-term sustainability
- Employee well-being versus cost reduction
- Customer value versus shareholder returns
- Environmental responsibility versus competitive advantage
