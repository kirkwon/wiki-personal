# Management

Management is a conceptual tool and practice that helps understand and navigate complex situations while systematically improving efficiency and output. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world, involving the identification and implementation of methods, techniques, and frameworks that maximize personal or organizational productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices.^[raw/articles/Management.md]

This mental model is described as having universal applicability across general domains, with a confidence level of 0.7.^[raw/articles/Management.md] It is positioned alongside other mental models in the wiki such as [[planning]], [[stillness]], [[Solutions]], [[settings]], [[environment]], and [[care]].

## Audit-Apply-Iterate-Scale Cycle

The core framework for applying management:

1. **Audit** — Identify where efficiency losses occur, track how time and attention are spent, note patterns leading to wasted effort
2. **Apply** — Restructure approach, focus on high-impact activities, eliminate or delegate low-value tasks
3. **Iterate** — Measure impact of changes, adjust based on results, build consistency through habit formation
4. **Scale** — Standardize effective approaches, document processes for reuse, teach others to multiply impact
