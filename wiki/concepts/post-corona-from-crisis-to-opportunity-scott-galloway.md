# Post Corona: From Crisis to Opportunity

"Post Corona: From Crisis to Opportunity" by Scott Galloway analyzes how the COVID-19 pandemic accelerated structural changes in the economy, particularly in technology, higher education, and retail. Galloway argues the pandemic acted as a great accelerator, benefiting Big Tech and Amazon while devastating traditional retail, hospitality, and small businesses. He explores which changes are temporary and which represent permanent shifts in market power.
