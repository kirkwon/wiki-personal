# Matcha Preparation

Matcha preparation involves sifting the fine green tea powder to remove clumps before whisking it with hot water at an optimal temperature (70-80°C) using a bamboo whisk in a precise, circular motion until a creamy froth forms. This process not only ensures a smooth texture but also maximizes the release of catechins and L-theanine for enhanced flavor and potential cognitive benefits. Proper technique is crucial for achieving the desired quality of matcha in both culinary applications and photographic presentations, where its vibrant color can be a focal point.
