# Insurance Protection for Investments

Risk transfer mechanism through various insurance coverages to protect investment portfolios from financial losses.

## Coverage Types

- **Property Insurance**: Fire, natural disasters, vandalism
- **Liability Insurance**: Tenant injuries, third-party claims
- **Rent Guarantee**: Tenant default protection
- **Business Interruption**: Lost income protection
- **Umbrella Coverage**: Excess liability protection

## Risk Management Considerations

- Adequate coverage at full replacement value
- Deductible management balancing cost vs. risk
- Claims history monitoring
- Insurance company selection based on financial rating and service
