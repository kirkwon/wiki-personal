# External Triggers

External triggers are information or cues from outside the user that prompt action. They tell the user what to do next through explicit signals.

## Types of External Triggers

- **Paid triggers** — advertisements, promoted content
- **Earned triggers** — press mentions, word of mouth, viral sharing
- **Relationship triggers** — notifications from friends, social interactions
- **Owned triggers** — app icons on home screen, bookmarks, email subscriptions

## Role in the Hook Model

External triggers initiate the hook cycle. They are the starting point before internal triggers develop. Effective external triggers are embedded in the user's environment so they are encountered frequently and at relevant moments.

## Transition to Internal Triggers

The goal of habit-forming design is to transition from external to [[internal-triggers]] — when users engage with a product because of emotional association rather than because they were told to. This transition marks the formation of a true habit.
