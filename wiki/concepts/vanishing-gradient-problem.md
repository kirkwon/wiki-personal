# Vanishing Gradient Problem

## Definition
The vanishing gradient problem occurs when gradients become exceedingly small during backpropagation, preventing effective weight updates in early layers of deep neural networks.

## Historical Context
First noted in the 1990s as researchers trained deeper networks. Sepp Hochreiter's 1991 thesis formally analyzed the phenomenon.
