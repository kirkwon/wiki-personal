# Prioritization of Information and Action

Prioritization is a core theme in Tiago Forte's PARA Method, focusing on techniques for identifying what truly matters in one's digital life. The concept involves distinguishing between urgent and important tasks, filtering incoming information, and allocating attention to high-value activities.

This connects to Stephen Covey's [[quadrant-2-focus]], which emphasizes investing time in important but not urgent activities for long-term impact. It also relates to [[categorizing-information]]—the PARA framework itself is a prioritization tool that sorts digital content into Projects, Areas, Resources, and Archives based on current relevance and actionability.

Effective prioritization requires honest assessment of goals and willingness to let lower-value inputs pass by without processing them. Forte's approach integrates this with the broader PARA system, where categorization and prioritization work together to reduce digital overwhelm.
