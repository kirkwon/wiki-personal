# Computation

Computation is a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. Rather than treating computation solely as a technical process, this framing positions it as a lens for understanding how systems, including human cognition and artificial intelligence, process information and make sense of the world.

The model draws from Brian Christian and Tom Griffiths' *The Most Human Human*, which explores the boundaries of computation by examining what makes humans uniquely human in an age of AI. Key themes from the book include the Turing test, language and judgment, creativity, and the limits of algorithmic processing. These concepts are abstracted into a broader mental-model framework applicable across domains.

## Core Idea

Computation provides a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships. It helps identify how information is transformed, how decisions are reached through systematic processing, and where human judgment diverges from purely algorithmic approaches.
