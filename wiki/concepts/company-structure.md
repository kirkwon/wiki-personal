# Company Structure

A company structure refers to the legal and organizational framework by which a business operates and manages its activities. It dictates the relationships between owners, managers, and employees, often determining liability and taxation obligations (e.g., sole proprietorships, partnerships, LLCs, or corporations). Analyzing this structure is crucial for governance, risk management, and defining operational hierarchy within a domain like corporate law.
