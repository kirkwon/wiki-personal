# pacific-northwest

The Pacific Northwest refers to the region encompassing the northwestern United States (specifically Washington and Oregon) and western Canada (British Columbia), known for its diverse culinary scene with an emphasis on locally-sourced ingredients, lush landscapes ideal for photography, a strong focus on sustainable living in personal finance, and rich cultural tapestry that influences cognitive science research.


The term 'Pacific Northwest' denotes the geographical area of the Pacific Ocean coastline extending from California to Alaska, characterized by its unique blend of culinary traditions, breathtaking natural beauty for photography enthusiasts, progressive personal finance approaches prioritizing environmental and social responsibility, and a community that contributes significantly to cognitive science through diverse cultural perspectives.
