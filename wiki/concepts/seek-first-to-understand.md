# Seek First to Understand, Then to Be Understood

This habit places empathetic listening before advocacy. Covey argues that most people listen with the intent to reply rather than to understand, which undermines genuine communication and trust.

## Key Ideas

- **Empathetic listening**: Truly seeking to understand another's perspective, feelings, and concerns before formulating a response.
- **Paraphrasing and reflective questioning**: Techniques for demonstrating understanding by restating what the other person has said and asking clarifying questions.
- **Trust before influence**: When people feel genuinely heard, they become more open to hearing your perspective in return.
