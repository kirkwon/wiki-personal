# Economies as Mental Model

Economies is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Economies.md]

## Definition

Economies functions as a universal mental model applicable across domains. It frames economic systems as a lens for pattern recognition and sense-making in complex situations, connecting to broader frameworks like [[markets]] and [[opportunities]].

## Connections to Ray Dalio's Work

This mental model is directly tied to Ray Dalio's frameworks:

- [[a-template-for-understanding-big-debt-crises---ray-dalio]] — Dalio's debt cycle framework for understanding how credit and debt drive economic behavior.
- [[how-the-economic-machine-works---ray-dalio]] — Dalio's simplified model of how economies function, including the role of credit, money, and deleveraging.

## Key Ideas

- Debt cycles and credit allocation as primary drivers of economic behavior
- Deleveraging phases and their impact on markets
- Pattern recognition in economic systems as a tool for navigating complexity
- Cross-domain applicability: economies as a mental model for any complex system involving resource allocation and feedback loops

## Open Questions

- Should "Economies" remain a standalone mental model or be subsumed under existing models like [[markets]] or [[opportunities]]?
- How can debt cycle principles be applied beyond macroeconomics to personal decision-making?
