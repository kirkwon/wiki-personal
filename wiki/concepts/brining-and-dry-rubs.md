# Brining and Dry Rubs

Brining and dry rubs are advanced flavoring techniques that complement marinades.

## Dry Rubs
- No liquid, just spices
- Great for roasting, grilling
- Example: Five-spice + salt + sugar

## Wet Brining
- Salt water bath (hours to days)
- Juicy, seasoned throughout
- Example: Whole turkey, pork shoulder

## Combination Technique
- Wet brine first (12-24 hours)
- Dry rub before cooking
- Best of both worlds

These techniques represent alternative approaches to flavor infusion when marinades are not the preferred method.
