# Marinations

Marination refers to the process of soaking food items, typically meat or vegetables, in a seasoned liquid mixture before cooking. This technique enhances flavor, tenderizes tougher ingredients, and can also contribute to more vibrant colors when photographically captured. In personal finance, marinations metaphorically describe the initial investment of time and resources into developing skills or assets that yield long-term benefits.


In cognitive science, 'marination' might be used as a conceptual analogy to explain how repeated exposure to information can lead to better retention and understanding, similar to how food absorbs flavors over time.
