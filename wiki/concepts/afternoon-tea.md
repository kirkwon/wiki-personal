# Afternoon Tea

Afternoon tea is a traditional British social event typically occurring between 3 pm and 5 pm, involving the consumption of tea accompanied by various snacks such as scones, sandwiches, and cakes. It serves both as a leisurely break in the day and an opportunity for socializing or networking. From a cognitive perspective, it can be seen as a structured activity that may enhance focus through routine while offering moments of relaxation to rejuvenate mental energy.
