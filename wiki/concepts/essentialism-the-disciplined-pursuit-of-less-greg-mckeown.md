# Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less

Greg McKeown's *Essentialism* presents a framework for identifying what is truly essential and eliminating everything else. The book argues that only by disciplining ourselves to do less can we actually achieve more.

## Core Framework

- **Explore** – Discern what is essential by exploring options and allowing space for play.
- **Eliminate** – Cut non-essential commitments to make space for what matters.
- **Execute** – Focus execution energy on the essential few.

## Key Ideas

- The 90/10 rule: only 10% of activities produce 90% of results.
- "Less but better" as a guiding principle.
- Strategic execution over reactive busyness.
- The role of trade-offs in essentialist thinking.
