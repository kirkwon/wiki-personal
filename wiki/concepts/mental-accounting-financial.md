# Mental Accounting (Financial)

Labeling money for specific purposes creates psychological barriers to spending.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## How It Works

Instead of treating all money as fungible, you create mental categories: "This is for retirement, not vacations." This uses the psychological power of labeling to protect savings from impulse spending.

## Connection to Money Scripts

Mental accounting connects to money scripts — deeply held beliefs about money that shape financial behavior. Both are psychological tools that influence how money is perceived and used.

## Practical Application

- Label money for specific purposes.
- Create psychological barriers to spending.
- Automate transfers to designated accounts.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]
