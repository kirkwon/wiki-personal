# Employee Stock Ownership Trust (ESOP)

A business trust structure where employees own company stock held in trust, facilitating business succession and ownership transition.

## Business Trust Models

- **ESOP**: Employees own company stock
- **Management Buyout Trust**: Facilitates management acquisition
- **Family Trust Holdings**: Family business interests in trust
- **Charitable Business Trust**: Business operations with charitable beneficiaries

## Integration Benefits

- Ownership transfer with business interests held in trust
- Smooth succession planning and transition of control
- Asset protection for business assets
- Tax optimization for business succession
- Business continuity despite ownership changes

## Implementation

- Gradual transfer of business interests
- Professional trustee for business management
- Family governance and oversight
- Buy-sell agreements funded with trust assets
- Integration with employee ownership plans
