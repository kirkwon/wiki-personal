# Atomic Habits - James Clear

James Clear's Atomic Habits presents a framework for habit formation built around a four-step loop: cue, craving, response, and reward. The book emphasizes that small, incremental changes compound into remarkable results over time, treating habits as the "compound interest" of self-improvement.

## Key Concepts

- **The Four Laws of Behavior Change** — Make it obvious, make it attractive, make it easy, make it satisfying
- **Identity-based habits** — Shifting focus from outcomes to identity ("I am the type of person who...")
- **Habit stacking** — Linking new habits to existing routines
- **Environment design** — Shaping surroundings to support desired behaviors

## Connection to Habit Mental Model

Clear's work provides the granular behavioral mechanics that operationalize the broader habit mental model, connecting to the [[hook-model]] of trigger-response-reward loops, [[external-triggers]] in environment design, and [[internal-triggers]] tied to cravings and identity.
