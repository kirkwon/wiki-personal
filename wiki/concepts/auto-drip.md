# Auto Drip

An Auto Drip system is an automated process that consistently delivers small amounts of resources or information over time. In cooking, it refers to a slow-cooking method where ingredients are added gradually for even flavor infusion. For photography, it's the automatic adjustment of camera settings based on predefined conditions. In personal finance, an Auto Drip savings plan involves regular, small contributions to a financial goal. Lastly, in cognitive science, it can describe how information is incrementally presented to enhance learning and retention.
