# Nationalism vs. Globalization

Nationalism vs. globalization examines the tension between rising nationalist sentiments and the forces of international cooperation and global integration in the post-pandemic era. Galloway analyzes how nationalist sentiments have grown during the pandemic, examining their implications for globalization and international cooperation.

## Key Themes

- Rising nationalist sentiments reshape geopolitical dynamics.
- Globalization faces challenges from micro-nations seeking self-determination and autonomy.
- The tension between local identity and global cooperation influences business strategy and policy.
- Micro-nations leverage unique advantages in a shifting world order.
