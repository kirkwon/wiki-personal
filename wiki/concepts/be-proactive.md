# Be Proactive

Being proactive is the first habit in Stephen R. Covey's framework, and it centers on taking responsibility for one's own life and responses rather than reacting automatically to external events. A proactive person recognizes that while they cannot control external circumstances, they can control their reactions to them.

## Key Ideas

- **Stewardship of response**: Rather than being driven by external stimuli, proactive individuals choose how they respond to situations. They operate from a sense of purpose rather than reacting out of habit or emotion.
- **Circle of influence vs. circle of concern**: Proactive people focus their energy on what they can influence rather than wasting effort on things they cannot control.
- **Responsibility mindset**: The habit shifts from blaming external forces to owning one's choices, actions, and outcomes.

## Relation to Other Concepts

Being proactive connects to [[sharpen-the-saw]] through self-awareness and to [[think-win-win]] through the recognition that one's response patterns affect others. It also relates to [[strategic-vs-reactive-thinking]], where proactive orientation means long-term planning rather than short-term reaction.
