# tempura

Tempura is a Japanese dish consisting of seafood or vegetables that have been battered and deep-fried in oil. The light, crispy coating contrasts with the tender interior, making it visually appealing for photography documentation. From a culinary perspective, its preparation requires an understanding of heat transfer principles relevant to cooking and cognitive science's study of sensory perception.
