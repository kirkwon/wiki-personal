# The Honest Truth About Dishonesty

*The Honest Truth About Dishonesty* is a book by behavioral economist Dan Ariely that empirically documents the prevalence of dishonesty and self-deception. The book examines how people cheat in small ways, how they rationalize dishonest behavior, and what structural and psychological conditions enable or discourage cheating. Key concepts include moral disengagement, conflicts of interest, and the gap between how people perceive their own honesty versus their actual behavior.
