# Interactions

Interactions is a universal mental model for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It provides a conceptual tool or framework for making sense of how entities—people, systems, tools, and environments—influence each other.

## Definition

Interactions helps understand and navigate complex situations by identifying the patterns and relationships between different elements. It serves as a mental shortcut for making sense of the world, applicable across domains from digital technology to interpersonal communication.

## Perspectives

### Digital Interaction Design (Cal Newport)

From *Digital Minimalism*, interactions are mediated by technology and shape attention, relationships, and well-being. Newport's framework emphasizes that attention is a scarce resource in digital interactions, and that intentional design of one's interaction environment is key to sustained focus and meaning.

### Communication Interaction Dynamics (Charles Duhigg)

From *Supercommunicators*, interactions between communicators determine understanding or misunderstanding. Duhigg's research highlights that the best communicators adapt their interaction style to the listener, recognizing that effective communication depends on reading and responding to the other party's communication patterns.
