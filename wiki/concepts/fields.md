# Fields

Fields is a conceptual tool or mental model for understanding and navigating complex situations through pattern recognition and relationship mapping. It provides a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Fields functions as a universal framework for making sense of complexity by identifying patterns across domains and contexts. It emphasizes drawing connections between different fields of knowledge to gain insight into complex problems.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

The Fields model draws on two key sources:

- **David Epstein's *Range*** — Epstein argues for the value of cross-domain generalism and drawing from multiple fields to solve problems.
- **Edward de Bono's *Lateral Thinking*** — De Bono's creativity techniques for breaking out of established patterns support the Fields approach to reframing problems.
