# Leaders Eat Last - Simon Sinek

*Leaders Eat Last* is a book by Simon Sinek that explores the relationship between leaders and their teams, arguing that the best leaders create environments of trust and cooperation rather than fear and competition.

## Core Ideas
- **Leadership and sacrifice**: Great leaders prioritize the well-being of their people, even at personal cost. They "eat last" — putting others' needs before their own.
- **Chemistry of trust**: Sinek draws on neurobiology to explain how trust and cooperation are driven by brain chemistry, particularly the release of oxytocin in safe, supportive environments.
- **Circle of Safety**: Organizations that create a "circle of safety" for their employees see higher loyalty, engagement, and performance.
- **Servant leadership**: Effective leadership is fundamentally about service — creating conditions where people feel safe to take risks, be vulnerable, and do their best work.
