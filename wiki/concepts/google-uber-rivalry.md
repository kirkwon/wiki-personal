# Google vs Uber Rivalry

The second battle in Galloway's framework pits Uber's disruption of transportation against Google's dominance in search, advertising, and increasingly cloud computing. Uber represents the challenge of platform-based disruption to established industries, while Google embodies the power of data and infrastructure control.^[raw/articles/the-four---scott-galloway.md]

Key themes include:
- Disruptive platforms vs. entrenched data monopolies
- Transportation innovation vs. information infrastructure
- Strategies for survival when facing tech giants with vastly superior resources

This rivalry illustrates how even well-funded, innovative companies like Uber struggle to compete against platforms with deeper data advantages and ecosystem control.^[raw/articles/the-four---scott-galloway.md]
