# Simple Living Practices

Simple living is a Stoic principle advocating intentional consumption and rejection of luxury and extravagance. Seneca's Letter 10 explicitly urges living simply, focusing resources and attention on what is essential rather than accumulating excess.

## Key Principles

- Avoid luxury and extravagance.
- Evaluate whether possessions and lifestyle choices align with stated values.
- Prioritize depth of experience over breadth of acquisition.
- Recognize that simplicity should not become asceticism that harms health or relationships.

## When to Stop

When simple living becomes harmful asceticism — denying oneself necessities or meaningful pleasures in ways that damage well-being or relationships.
