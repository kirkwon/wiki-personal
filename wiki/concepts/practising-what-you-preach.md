# Practising What You Preach

Seneca's final letter in *Letters from a Stoic* (Chapter 20) emphasizes the imperative to live according to one's philosophical principles. This concept connects to [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]] and [[systematic-decision-making]].

## Key Ideas

- Philosophical knowledge without application is useless.
- Alignment between stated values and daily actions is the measure of genuine commitment.
- Self-examination should reveal gaps between belief and behavior.
- Connects to [[decision-tracking]] by treating the gap between intention and action as a trackable dimension.

## Practice

Regularly assess whether your actions match your principles. When they don't, investigate why and adjust behavior or revise principles — but never use clever reasoning to justify inconsistency.
