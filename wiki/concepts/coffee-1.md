# Coffee

Coffee is a brewed beverage made from roasted coffee beans, the seeds of berries from certain Coffea species. It contains caffeine, which acts as a central nervous system stimulant and can enhance alertness and concentration—factors relevant to cognitive science. In cooking and photography, its rich flavor profile is often highlighted in food pairings and visually appreciated for the coffee's color contrasts.


In personal finance, while not directly impactful, it represents a consumer product with economic implications due to its widespread consumption and market influence.
