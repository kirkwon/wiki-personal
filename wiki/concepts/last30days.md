# last30days

A common metric or time window used in data analysis and reporting to quantify performance, usage, or changes over the preceding 30 calendar days. It is frequently employed by businesses (e.g., SaaS platforms) to provide recent trend analysis, focusing on actionable insights for immediate decision-making. While not a formal algorithm like rolling averages, implementing a "last 30 days" filter helps isolate short-term fluctuations from long-term historical trends.
