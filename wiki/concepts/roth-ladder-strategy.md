# Roth Ladder Strategy

The Roth ladder strategy is a multi-year approach to converting traditional IRA balances to Roth IRA accounts while staying below tax torpedo thresholds.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## How It Works

1. Convert traditional IRA to Roth over multiple years rather than in a single large conversion.
2. Stay below income thresholds for IRMAA, Social Security taxation, and higher tax brackets.
3. Remove the RMD burden from traditional IRAs by shifting balances to Roth.
4. Avoid triggering tax torpedo effects like IRMAA surcharges or Social Security taxation.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## Phased Implementation

- **10+ years before retirement**: Begin Roth conversions, develop investment strategy, build taxable accounts.
- **5-10 years before**: Finalize Roth conversion plan, optimize investment location, plan healthcare costs.
- **1-5 years before**: Execute Roth ladder, finalize withdrawal strategy, plan for RMDs.
- **Retirement**: Implement withdrawal plan, monitor tax torpedo triggers, adjust as needed.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## Key Principles

- **Partial conversions**: Convert only amounts that keep AGI below critical thresholds.
- **Asset location**: Keep growth assets in Roth, income-producing assets in traditional accounts.
- **Tax-bracket management**: Time income sources to avoid provisional income thresholds.
- **Qualified Charitable Distributions (QCDs)**: Offset RMD taxes with charitable giving when needed.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]
