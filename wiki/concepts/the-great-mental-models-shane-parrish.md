# The Great Mental Models (Vol. 1) - Shane Parrish

A 2024 book that introduces mental models as foundational cognitive tools shaping perception, decision-making, and innovation. The book synthesizes existing research rather than presenting new empirical findings, offering a conceptual framework for improving thinking through awareness of mental models.

## Core Concepts Covered
- [[evolutionary-mindset]] — Evolution shaped brains to prioritize immediate concerns
- [[power-of-metaphors]] — Metaphors as cognitive bridging constructs
- [[cognitive-biases-and-heuristics]] — Distortions in judgment despite access to information
- [[precision-vs-power-conflict]] — Tension between accuracy and efficiency in thinking
- [[value-proposition-mental-model]] — Evaluating decisions by impact on life goals and values
- [[understanding-others-mental-models]] — Empathy as a tool for mapping others' perspectives
- [[mental-models-in-innovation]] — How mental models shape incremental and breakthrough innovation

## Key Insights
- Mental models are foundational cognitive tools that shape perception and choices
- Being aware of one's mental models improves decision outcomes
- The precision vs. power conflict is a fundamental tradeoff requiring calibration
- Understanding others' mental models through empathy improves communication and collaboration
- Innovation is constrained or enabled by the mental models in use
