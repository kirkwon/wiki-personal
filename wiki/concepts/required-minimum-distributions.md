# Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs)

Required Minimum Distributions are mandatory withdrawals from traditional retirement accounts starting at age 73 (as of 2024). RMDs are a primary trigger for tax torpedo effects in retirement.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## Tax Torpedo Risk

Large RMDs can:
- Push income into higher tax brackets.
- Increase Medicare IRMAA surcharges.
- Trigger Social Security taxation (via provisional income).
- Create compounding tax effects when multiple torpedo triggers activate simultaneously.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## Mitigation Strategies

- **Roth ladder strategy**: Convert traditional IRA to Roth over multiple years to reduce future RMD burden.
- **Qualified Charitable Distributions (QCDs)**: Remove income from taxable base through charitable giving.
- **Asset location**: Keep growth assets in Roth, income-producing assets in traditional accounts.
- **Partial Roth conversions**: Stay below tax thresholds while gradually shifting balances.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## Interaction with Other Torpedoes

RMDs interact with IRMAA thresholds and Social Security taxation rules. When RMD income + Social Security + investment income converge, effective tax rates can escalate dramatically—illustrated by the $51,000 additional tax from a single $200,000 Roth conversion scenario.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]
