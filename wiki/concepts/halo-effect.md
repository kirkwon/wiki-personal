# Halo Effect

The halo effect is a cognitive bias where a positive impression of one aspect of something influences the overall opinion of that thing.

## Definition

**Halo Effect**: Positive impression of one aspect influences overall opinion. For example, attractive people are perceived as more competent, or charismatic leaders are assumed to be effective in all areas.

## Examples

- Attractive people are perceived as more competent and trustworthy
- A company with a strong brand reputation is assumed to have quality products in all categories
- A charismatic leader is assumed to be competent in strategy, operations, and ethics

## Impact

Hiring mistakes, unfair judgments, and suboptimal decisions in management and relationships. The halo effect causes us to overlook flaws and overestimate capabilities based on a single positive trait.

## Mitigation

Evaluate each trait and dimension independently. Use structured evaluation criteria rather than relying on overall impressions.
