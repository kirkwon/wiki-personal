# Small Batch Production

Small batch production is the principle of doing work in small increments to shorten feedback cycles and reduce waste. In the lean startup methodology, it is the operational counterpart to the Build-Measure-Learn loop: the smaller each batch of work, the faster the team can learn from it and adjust.

The concept draws from lean manufacturing principles, where large batches of work create long feedback delays—problems are only discovered after significant investment. Small batches allow teams to detect errors early, iterate faster, and avoid building features nobody wants.

Key principles:
- Smaller batches mean faster feedback and less waste
- Speed of iteration matters more than size of output
- Organize work around learning goals, not task completion
- Continuous deployment enables small batch production in software

This principle connects to the broader theme of iteration speed and flow optimization. It also relates to the lean philosophy of eliminating waste—any work that does not contribute to validated learning is waste.
