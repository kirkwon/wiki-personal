# Supercommunicators - Charles Duhigg

Charles Duhigg's Supercommunicators examines why some people are exceptionally effective at making themselves understood and understanding others. The book identifies three modes of communication—practical, emotional, and social—and explores how habitual patterns shape how we listen, speak, and connect.

## Key Themes

- **Three modes of communication** — Practical (information), emotional (feelings), social (belonging)
- **Habitual listening patterns** — How we default to certain modes based on context
- **The role of curiosity** — Genuine interest as a foundation for understanding
- **Meta-clarity** — Understanding what type of conversation is needed

## Connection to Habit Mental Model

Duhigg shows how habitual communication patterns determine the quality of interactions, connecting to the habit mental model's framework for understanding automatic behaviors and their downstream effects on relationships and outcomes.
