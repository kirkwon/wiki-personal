# Asset Allocation

Asset allocation matches the distribution of assets to risk tolerance and timeline. Target vs. actual allocation across asset classes including stocks, bonds, real estate, cash, and alternatives.

## Sample Allocation Profiles

| Profile | Stocks | Bonds | Real Estate | Alternatives | Cash |
|---------|--------|-------|-------------|--------------|------|
| Conservative | 30-40% | 50-60% | 5-10% | 5-10% | |
| Moderate | 60-70% | 20-30% | 5-10% | 0-5% | |
| Aggressive | 80-100% | 0-10% | 5-10% | 0-5% | |
