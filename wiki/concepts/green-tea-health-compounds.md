# Green Tea Health Compounds (绿茶健康成分)

Green tea contains several bioactive compounds believed to confer health benefits, though much evidence is associative rather than conclusively causal.

## Key Compounds

### EGCG (Epigallocatechin gallate)
- Powerful antioxidant
- Free radical scavenging
- Associated with reduced cancer risk

### Catechins
- Heart health benefits
- Anti-inflammatory properties
- May support weight management

### L-theanine
- Amino acid promoting relaxation
- Improves focus without drowsiness
- Counterbalances caffeine effects

### Polyphenols
- Anti-inflammatory
- Cardiovascular health support

## Scientific Evidence
- **Antioxidant**: High ORAC value
- **Heart Health**: Associated with reduced cardiovascular risk
- **Brain Health**: L-theanine improves cognitive function
- **Cancer Prevention**: Some studies suggest reduced risk
- **Weight Management**: May aid metabolism and fat burning

## Caveat
Much of the evidence is based on associative studies; causal relationships are not firmly established. Health benefits should not be overstated.
