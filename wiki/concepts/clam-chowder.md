# Clam Chowder

Clam chowder is a creamy, white soup made with clams (quahogs), bacon or salt pork, onions, potatoes, milk or cream, and seasonings. It is the quintessential New England comfort dish, distinct from Manhattan clam chowder which is tomato-based.

## Preparation Basics

1. Render bacon fat
2. Sauté onions in bacon fat
3. Add potatoes, cook 5 minutes
4. Add clams (with liquid if canned)
5. Add milk or cream
6. Simmer 20-30 minutes
7. Season to taste
8. Serve with oyster crackers

## Key Tips

- Don't overcook clams or they become tough
- Adjust milk/cream ratio if chowder becomes too thick
- Yukon Gold potatoes are preferred

## Regional Variants

- **New England style**: creamy, white
- **Manhattan style**: tomato-based
- **Gulf Coast**: gumbo-influenced
