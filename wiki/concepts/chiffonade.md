# Chiffonade

Chiffonade is a knife technique for preparing herbs and leafy greens by rolling leaves into tight cylinders and slicing them into thin ribbons. It is the standard method for preparing basil for pesto, garnishes, and sauces.

## How It Works
1. Stack several leaves on top of each other
2. Roll the leaves tightly from the stem end toward the tip
3. Slice perpendicular to the roll into thin ribbons
4. Unroll to reveal delicate strips

## Why It Matters for Basil
Chiffonade creates maximum surface area for flavor release while maintaining the herb's fresh appearance. It is preferred over mincing for garnishes and over tearing for sauces like pesto where controlled texture matters.
