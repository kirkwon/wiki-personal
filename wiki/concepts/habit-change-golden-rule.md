# Habit Change Golden Rule

The golden rule of habit change is: **keep the cue and reward, substitute the routine**. This principle states that habits are most effectively changed not by eliminating the trigger or the satisfaction, but by replacing the behavior itself.

## Application

1. Identify the cue (what triggers the habit)
2. Identify the reward (what need the habit fulfills)
3. Experiment with alternative routines that satisfy the same reward
4. Repeat until the new routine becomes automatic

## Why It Works

The craving brain has encoded the link between cue and reward. By preserving both, you work with the neurological machinery rather than against it. This is the practical engine behind [[habit-stacking]] and [[keystone-habits]] identification.
