# Stress Testing Investments

Running downside and upside scenarios on investment portfolios and cash flow to evaluate resilience under adverse conditions. Stress testing examines base, down, and up case scenarios to assess resilience.

## Market Stress Scenarios

- **Base Case**: 2.5% economic growth, stable interest rates, 5-7% vacancy, 3-5% rent growth.
- **Down Case (Recession)**: -2% economic growth, 2% interest rate increase, 15-20% vacancy, -5% to 0% rent growth, 10-20% decline in property values.
- **Up Case (Boom)**: 5% economic growth, 1% interest rate decrease, 2-3% vacancy, 8-10% rent growth, 15-25% increase in property values.

## Cash Flow Stress Scenarios

- **Worst Case**: 20% rent decline, 10% expense increase, 5% vacancy increase (e.g., vacancy doubles).
- **Best Case**: 10% rent increase, 5% expense decrease, 2% vacancy decrease.
- **Interest Rate Hikes**: Impact on monthly payments when rates rise.
- **Vacancy Increase**: What if vacancy doubles or rises by 5%?
- **Extended Hold**: 10% cash flow decline sustained for 24 months.
- **Liquidity Crisis**: 30% vacancy for 12 months.

## Financing Stress Scenarios

- Rate increase of 1-2%.
- Inability to refinance at maturity.
- Lender financial distress (lender default).
- Loss of financing availability (credit crisis).
