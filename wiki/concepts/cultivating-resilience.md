# Cultivating Resilience

Resilience as a critical attribute for achieving clear thinking, particularly in overcoming setbacks and pursuing progress amid adversity. Parrish positions resilience not as mere toughness but as a cognitive practice that maintains clarity when circumstances are difficult.

This concept connects to [[embracing-failure-for-personal-growth]] (reframing failure as growth) and [[science-of-joy]] (psychological drivers of happiness), and supports [[managing-emotions-for-clarity]] by providing the emotional stability needed for clear thought.
