# Second Brain

A second brain is a personal knowledge management system that extends cognitive function through organized digital tools and processes. The concept, central to Tiago Forte's *Building a Second Brain*, addresses the problem of digital information chaos by providing a structured framework for capturing, organizing, and retrieving knowledge.

The second brain functions as an externalized extension of memory and thinking, reducing cognitive load by offloading information storage and enabling creative connections across stored content. Key practices include content curation, mind mapping, digital decluttering, and regular system maintenance to keep the knowledge base relevant and actionable.

This concept overlaps with [[personal-knowledge-vault]] and relates to [[categorizing-information]] and [[active-revision-techniques]] as methods for managing personal knowledge effectively.
