# Rental Property Depreciation

Tax shield for rental properties using a 27.5-year straight-line depreciation schedule on the building value (excluding land).

## Calculation

**Annual Depreciation = Depreciable Basis / 27.5 years**

Where depreciable basis = Building Value (Purchase Price - Land Value)

## Tax Shield

**Tax Shield = Annual Depreciation × Tax Rate**

## Tracking

The [[single-property-analysis-template]] includes depreciation analysis in the annual performance metrics section, and the [[tax-optimization-templates]] include a full depreciation schedule table tracking annual amounts, tax shields, and cumulative depreciation over 27.5 years.
