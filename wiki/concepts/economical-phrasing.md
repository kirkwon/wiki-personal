# Economical Phrasing

Economical phrasing is Miles Davis's signature minimalist approach to playing, where every note counts and space and silence are used as musical elements.

## Principles
- Play shorter phrases, leave room for the rhythm section
- Don't overplay
- "It's not the notes you play, it's the notes you don't play"
- "Don't play what's there, play what's not there"

## Techniques
- Lower register playing
- Harmon mute for intimacy
- Vibrato used sparingly, at ends of phrases
- Breath attacks for soft entrances

This approach reflects a philosophy of restraint and intentionality in musical expression.^[raw/articles/miles-davis.md]
