# Collars (Options Strategy)

A collar combines a protective put with a covered call to limit both downside risk and upside potential while reducing the cost of protection.

## How It Works

- **Own stock**
- **Buy protective put** at a lower strike (downside protection)
- **Sell covered call** at a higher strike (offsets put cost)
- **Net cost**: Reduced or eliminated by call premium

## Applications

- **Portfolio Protection**: Protect holdings while generating income
- **Cost Reduction**: Use call premium to offset put cost
- **Retirement Portfolios**: Hedge against downturns while maintaining income
- **Estate Planning**: Protect unrealized gains

## Risk/Reward

- **Downside protected** to the put strike
- **Upside capped** at the call strike
- **Net cost** minimized or zero

Collars are a balanced risk management strategy suitable for conservative portfolios seeking both protection and income.^[raw/articles/Options Trading Guide.md]
