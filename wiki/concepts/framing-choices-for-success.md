# Framing Choices for Success

Framing Choices for Success is the practice of analyzing how framing decisions correctly sets up success or failure scenarios. Duke provides practical techniques for framing decisions that lead to a higher likelihood of successful outcomes, including recognizing when the frame itself is distorting the decision.

This concept connects to [[decision-making-frameworks]] and [[prospect-theory]], where loss aversion causes people to frame potential losses more heavily than equivalent gains. It also relates to [[overconfidence-and-certainty]], where an overly positive frame can prevent someone from seeing the need to quit.

Duke's work here extends themes from [[thinking-in-bets---annie-duke]] about treating decisions as bets and examining the odds honestly. See [[quit-the-power-of-knowing-when-to-walk-away---annie-duke]].
