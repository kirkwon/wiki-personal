# Iron Condors

An iron condor is a defined-risk strategy combining a bull put spread and bear call spread, profitable when the underlying stays within a range. It is one of the most popular strategies for income generation in neutral markets.

## Structure

- Sell OTM put, buy further OTM put
- Sell OTM call, buy further OTM call
- Collect net credit
- Maximum profit at middle strike range
- Maximum loss limited to width of spreads minus credit

## How It Works

Sell an OTM call spread and sell an OTM put spread simultaneously. Collect premium from both sides while defining maximum risk.

## Characteristics

- Limited risk on both sides
- Benefits from time decay when stock stays in the range
- Profits when the underlying stays between the short strikes
- Theta positive: earns from time decay

## Applications

- **Range-Bound Income**: Generate income when stock stays within expected range. Quarterly income generation.
- **Low Volatility Markets**: Best deployed when volatility is expected to stay low
- **Portfolio Enhancement**: Add income with defined risk
- **Portfolio Allocation**: 5% of portfolio for range-bound market strategies according to theta-aware position sizing
- **Retirement Income**: Conservative income generation in IRA/401(k) accounts using defined risk strategies

## Theta Benefit

Iron condors benefit from time decay as the short options lose value faster than the long options gain value, creating a net positive theta position. Theta benefit increases as expiration approaches if stock stays in range.

## Risk Management

- Defined risk makes iron condors suitable for conservative to moderate portfolios
- Maximum loss occurs if stock moves beyond either spread
- Position sizing: typically 5% of portfolio allocation with 65% cash reserves
- Requires active monitoring and adjustment before expiration week
