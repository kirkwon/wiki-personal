# Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

The discount rate that makes the net present value of all cash flows equal to zero. Used in the [[single-property-analysis-template]] and other financial reporting templates as an advanced performance metric.

## Formula Reference

`=IRR(CashFlowRange)` in Excel/Google Sheets

## Usage

IRR accounts for the timing of cash flows and is particularly useful for comparing investments with different cash flow patterns. It is tracked alongside [[cash-on-cash-return]], [[cap-rate]], and total return for comprehensive investment analysis.
