# Dried Herbs

Dried herbs are the dehydrated form of fresh herbs commonly used to enhance flavors in cooking; they also serve as a subject for photographic documentation due to their vibrant colors and textures, can be economically sourced from personal gardens or bulk purchases impacting personal finances, and are studied within cognitive science for their sensory perception effects on human taste experiences.
