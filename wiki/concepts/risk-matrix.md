# Risk Matrix

A visual prioritization tool that plots risks on a grid of probability (Very Low to Very High) against impact (Minimal to Catastrophic) to determine priority level. A probability-impact grid for prioritizing risks and determining response urgency.

## Matrix Structure

| Impact/Probability | Very Low (5%) | Low (15%) | Medium (35%) | High (65%) | Very High (85%) |
|---------------------|---------------|-----------|-------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Catastrophic** (>30%) | Low | Low | Medium | High | Critical |
| **Major** (15-30%) | Low | Medium | Medium | High | High |
| **Moderate** (5-15%) | Low | Medium | Medium | Medium | High |
| **Minor** (1-5%) | Low | Low | Low | Medium | Medium |
| **Minimal** (<1%) | Low | Low | Low | Low | Low |

The risk matrix is a core analytical tool in [[risk-assessment-framework]].

## Construction

The matrix cross-references probability levels (Very Low 5%, Low 15%, Medium 35%, High 65%, Very High 85%) with impact levels (Minimal <1%, Minor 1-5%, Moderate 5-15%, Major 15-30%, Catastrophic >30%) to produce risk ratings from Low to Critical.

## Risk Prioritization

- Critical: Immediate action required
- High: Address within 3-6 months
- Medium: Address within 6-12 months
- Low: Monitor, address as needed
- Lowest: Accept or monitor only
