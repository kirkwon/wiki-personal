# Seasoning Methods

Seasoning methods are the techniques used to add flavor to dishes throughout the cooking process. This includes salting, acid addition, fat incorporation, spice blending, and the application of herbs, spices, and other flavoring agents. Proper seasoning is critical to developing the taste profile and building depth in finished dishes.

## Overview
Seasoning encompasses several approaches:
- **Dry seasoning**: Salting, spicing, seasoning blends
- **Wet seasoning**: Marinating, brining, sauces, acid addition, fat incorporation
- **Layered seasoning**: Seasoning at multiple stages of cooking
- **Wok seasoning**: Building flavor through repeated oil and heat application

Seasoning connects to [[herbs-and-spices]], [[wok-seasoning]], and [[flavor-theory]] as part of the broader understanding of how flavors interact and develop.
