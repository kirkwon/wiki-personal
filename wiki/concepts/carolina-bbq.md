# Carolina BBQ

Carolina BBQ is distinguished by its vinegar-based sauces and pork-centric cooking. Two distinct styles exist within the region.

## Eastern vs. Western Carolina

| Style | Region | Sauce Characteristics |
|-------|--------|-----------------------|
| **Eastern Carolina** | Eastern NC | Thin, vinegar, pepper, no tomato |
| **Western Carolina** (Lexington) | Western NC | Tomato-based, vinegar, sweet |

## Eastern Carolina Sauce

- Apple cider vinegar
- Red pepper flakes
- Salt, pepper
- No tomato

## Western Carolina Sauce

- Ketchup or tomato
- Apple cider vinegar
- Brown sugar
- Red pepper flakes

## Pulled Pork

- Whole hog (Eastern) or pork shoulder (Western)
- Slow-smoked (10-14 hours)
- Pulled (shredded) by hand
- Dressed with vinegar sauce
- Coleslaw served on sandwich (Western style)

## Key Distinction

The Eastern style is purely vinegar-forward with no tomato, while the Western style blends tomato and vinegar sweetness—a direct contrast to the tomato-heavy Kansas City approach.
