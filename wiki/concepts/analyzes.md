# Analyzes

Analyzes is a universal mental model that serves as a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

At its core, analyzes helps users identify and interpret patterns and relationships within complex environments. It functions as a general-purpose framework for structuring thinking about difficult or ambiguous situations.

## Cross-Source Perspective

The model is referenced through Dan Ariely's *Predictably Irrational*, which suggests that human analysis of complex situations is itself subject to systematic irrationality. This implies that effective analysis must account for predictable biases in how people evaluate information.
