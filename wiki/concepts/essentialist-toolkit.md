# Essentialist Toolkit

The Essentialist Toolkit (Chapter 7 of McKeown's book) provides practical tools, frameworks, and habits for cultivating essentialism:

- **Habit tracking and measurement** — monitoring consistency and streaks
- **Time blocking and scheduling** — dedicating specific blocks to essential work
- **Environment design and optimization** — shaping surroundings to support focus
- **The 2-minute rule** — for habit formation and quick wins
- **Task prioritization frameworks** — including the Eisenhower Matrix
- **Focus enhancement techniques** — Deep Work and Flow states
- **Goal setting** — breaking down long-term goals into small steps
- **Elimination of distractions and time-wasters**
- **Systematic review and reflection routines**
- **Energy management throughout the day**

The toolkit also includes clear "when to stop" criteria: when optimization becomes obsession, when productivity systems create more work than they save, when relationships or health are sacrificed, or when joy in work is destroyed.
