# Savings Rate Over Returns

Building wealth has little to do with income or investment returns, and lots to do with savings rate.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## The Insight

Savings = income - expectations, not income - spending.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

This reframes savings as the gap between what you earn and what you expect to have, rather than the gap between income and spending. Managing lifestyle inflation — where spending increases with income — is the primary challenge.

## Practical Implications

- Focus on increasing savings rate rather than chasing higher returns.
- Start saving now, even with small amounts.
- Define "enough" to avoid the endless treadmill of wanting more.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## Connection to Compounding Time

Starting early with even small amounts matters because of compounding. Warren Buffett's wealth: 90% accumulated after age 65.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md] The greatest asset is compounding time — time in market > timing the market.
