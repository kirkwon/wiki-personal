# Ways Mental Model

Ways is a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It functions as a mental shortcut that helps make sense of the world by identifying recurring patterns and how elements relate to one another.

## Definition

Ways provides a framework for understanding complexity by recognizing patterns and relationships. Rather than prescribing a single approach, it serves as a lens for interpreting situations from multiple angles or paths.
