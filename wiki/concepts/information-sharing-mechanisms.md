# Information sharing mechanisms

Information sharing mechanisms is the practice of systematically improving efficiency and output by identifying and implementing methods, techniques, and frameworks that maximize productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices.

## Core Practices

1. **Audit your current workflow** — Identify where efficiency losses occur, track how time and attention are spent, and note patterns that lead to wasted effort.
2. **Apply the framework** — Restructure your approach to focus on high-impact activities and eliminate or delegate low-value tasks.
3. **Iterate and refine** — Measure the impact of changes, adjust based on results, and build consistency through habit formation.
4. **Scale what works** — Standardize effective approaches, document processes for reuse, and teach others to multiply impact.

## Applications

- **Personal productivity**: Planning weeks more effectively, managing competing priorities, breaking down complex projects.
- **Team coordination**: Improving meeting efficiency, enhancing decision-making processes.
- **Learning and growth**: Accelerating skill acquisition, organizing and retaining information, identifying and filling knowledge gaps.
