# The Genie Problem

The Genie Problem is a framing concept from [[the-alignment-problem---brian-christian-tom-griffiths]] describing the challenge of intractable computation — the idea that even well-intentioned AI systems face fundamental computational limits that prevent perfectly solving hard problems. Even a perfectly aligned genie (an AI that wants to help) cannot reliably execute complex, open-ended requests because the underlying optimization problems are computationally intractable.

This concept highlights that alignment is not merely a matter of specifying the right objective function. Some problems are provably hard to solve optimally, and AI systems must navigate this limitation. It connects to broader questions about [[limits-of-computation]] and the practical constraints on AI system design.
