# Vietnamese Cà Phê Sữa Đá

Vietnamese Cà Phê Sữa Đá is a traditional Vietnamese iced coffee made by brewing strong coffee with condensed milk poured over it, served cold over ice. The visual appeal of the contrasting layers and vibrant colors makes it an excellent subject for photography, while its cost-effective nature can be analyzed from a personal finance perspective. From a cognitive science viewpoint, its sensory experience offers insights into taste perception and cultural associations with food.
