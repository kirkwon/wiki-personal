# Negative Space

Negative space is the empty area around the main subject in a photograph. It is a deliberate compositional choice that emphasizes the subject, creates breathing room, and adds impact.

## Why Use It

- Emphasizes the subject by making it stand out
- Creates breathing room for an uncluttered, peaceful feel
- Adds impact through minimalist, powerful compositions
- Can suggest direction or movement

## How to Use

1. Find minimalist scenes: sky, water, walls
2. Place the subject in one part of the frame
3. Leave the rest empty with large areas of negative space
4. Balance the subject against the negative space

## Examples

- Person in vast sky: Subject small, sky dominates
- Small boat in ocean: Boat small, water dominates
- Solitary tree in field: Tree as subject, empty field around it
