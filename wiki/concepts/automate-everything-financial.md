# Automate Everything (Financial)

Remove willpower from financial decisions by setting up automatic transfers and defaulting to saving rather than spending.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

This connects to broader habit-building principles found in [[gtd-method]] and [[habit-testing]] — systems that operate without relying on daily motivation produce more consistent results than goal-oriented approaches.
