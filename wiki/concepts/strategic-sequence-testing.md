# Strategic Sequence Testing

Strategic sequence testing is the final step in validating a blue ocean idea. It tests the business model viability through a sequence of four hurdles:

1. **Buyer utility** - Does the idea offer exceptional utility across the six utility levers?
2. **Price** - Is the target price accessible to the mass of buyers?
3. **Cost** - Can the company achieve the target price while maintaining adequate profit margins?
4. **Adoption** - Can the company overcome the organizational, political, and legal hurdles to bring the idea to market?

This sequence ensures that a blue ocean strategy is not just conceptually attractive but practically viable. Each hurdle must be cleared before moving to the next, providing a disciplined process for turning market insight into executable strategy.
