# Risk Parity

Risk parity is an investment strategy that allocates capital such that each asset class contributes equally to the portfolio's total risk (volatility), rather than allocating based on historical weights or expected returns. Unlike traditional methods, which often overweight highly correlated assets, this approach aims for diversification by managing risk contributions directly. It requires robust estimation of covariance matrices and is a core concept in quantitative finance.

## Relevance to my decisions

Vol contribution weighting is the portfolio-level cousin of vol-aware Kelly ([[vol-volume-aware-kelly-2026-08-23]]): both size by risk (σ) rather than capital. Risk parity equalizes σ-contribution across assets; vol-aware Kelly resizes a single position as its σ² regime shifts.
