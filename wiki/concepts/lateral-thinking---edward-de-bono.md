
# Lateral Thinking: Creativity Step by Step — Edward de Bono

Edward de Bono's *Lateral Thinking: Creativity Step by Step* (2024) argues that creativity is a learnable skill that can be systematized through specific methods and habits. The book distinguishes itself from purely analytical approaches by emphasizing generative, non-linear thinking that challenges conventional patterns.

The book is organized around ten chapters covering the journey to creative thinking, the distinction between problem finding and problem solving, breaking barriers to thinking, the Six Thinking Hats method, de Bono's pointers for effective thinking, lateral movements, metaphors and analogies, challenging words, building better habits, and overcoming implementation challenges.

Central to the book is the idea that lateral thinking is not a mystical gift but a practical discipline. Techniques like "what if," "wonder why," and "undo what you did" serve as deliberate interventions to redirect habitual thought patterns. The Creative Log habit supports sustained creative practice by capturing interesting observations and ideas for later exploration.

This approach complements [[polya-four-steps]] (systematic problem-solving) and [[thinking-in-bets---annie-duke]] (probabilistic decision-making) by offering a generative, pattern-breaking counterpart to analytical and statistical thinking.
