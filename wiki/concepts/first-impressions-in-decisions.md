# First Impressions in Decision-Making

First impressions refer to how initial judgments can significantly impact decisions, with evidence demonstrating that initial judgments can overshadow objective evaluation in various realms such as job interviews, dating, or purchasing experiences.

## Core Idea

Ariely discusses how first impressions can significantly impact decisions, with evidence demonstrating that initial judgments can overshadow objective evaluation in various realms.^[raw/articles/the-upside-of-irrationality---dan-ariely.md]

## Key Dynamics

- Initial evaluations anchor subsequent judgment
- Objective information is filtered through first-impression lens
- Confirmation bias reinforces initial assessments
- Changing first impressions requires disproportionately strong evidence

## Connection to Existing Concepts

This concept extends [[ascription-bias]] (attributing positive qualities based on initial perception) and relates to [[effective-listening]] (the difficulty of truly hearing someone after forming an early judgment).
