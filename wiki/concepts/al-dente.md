# Al Dente

The standard for proper pasta doneness — firm to the bite with slight resistance in the center, never mushy. Cooking time is typically 1-2 minutes less than the package recommendation since pasta continues cooking off heat.

## Key Principles
- Firm to bite, not overcooked
- Slight resistance in the center when bitten
- Reserve 1/2 cup of starchy pasta water before draining — this starchy water is essential for binding sauces, especially in dishes like [[cacio-e-pepe]]
- Never rinse pasta after draining (unless making cold pasta salad) as rinsing removes the starch needed for sauce adhesion

## Process
1. Use a large pot with 4-6 quarts of water per pound of pasta
2. Salt the water liberally (1-2 tbsp) — the water should taste like the sea, as it seasons the pasta from within
3. Bring to a rapid boil before adding pasta
4. Add pasta all at once and stir to prevent sticking
5. Begin taste testing 1-2 minutes before the package-recommended time
6. Reserve starchy cooking water before draining
7. Finish cooking the pasta in the sauce to allow the starch to help bind everything together

Al dente is non-negotiable for proper Italian pasta — overcooking ruins the texture and destroys the sauce-binding capacity of the starchy water.
