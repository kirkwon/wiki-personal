# Storytelling in communication

Storytelling is a core communication skill that enables people to connect, inspire trust, and convey complex ideas clearly and memorably. According to Charles Duhigg's *Supercommunicators*, crafting compelling stories improves one's ability to communicate effectively across personal and professional settings.

## Key principles
- Stories create emotional resonance and facilitate connection
- Effective storytelling requires clarity and memorability
- Stories can convey complex ideas more effectively than abstract arguments
- Trust is built through authentic narrative sharing
