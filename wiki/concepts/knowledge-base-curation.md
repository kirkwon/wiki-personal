# knowledge-base-curation

Knowledge-base curation is the systematic process of collecting, organizing, verifying, and refining information to populate a structured repository (knowledge base). This discipline involves transforming unstructured data from various sources into high-quality, interconnected facts, relationships, and metadata. It ensures data integrity, enhances searchability, and allows AI systems to reason over reliable domain knowledge. The practice is crucial in fields like Semantic Web development and enterprise AI applications.
