# Medicare Overview

Medicare is the federal health insurance program for people age 65 and older, covering hospital, medical, prescription drug, and supplemental coverage.

## Medicare Parts

### Part A (Hospital Insurance)
- **Premium**: $0 if you or spouse paid Medicare taxes 40+ quarters; $278/month (30-39 quarters); $505/month (<30 quarters)
- **Deductible**: $1,632 per benefit period
- **Coinsurance**: Days 1-60: $0; Days 61-90: $408/day; Days 91+: $816/day (lifetime reserve days)

### Part B (Medical Insurance)
- **Standard Premium**: $174.70/month (2024)
- **IRMAA Surcharges**: Based on income
- **Deductible**: $240/year
- **Coinsurance**: 20% of Medicare-approved amount

### Part C (Medicare Advantage)
- Private plans replacing Parts A+B+D
- Often includes dental/vision
- May have lower premiums

### Part D (Prescription Drugs)
- Average premium: ~$50/month
- IRMAA Surcharges apply
- Deductible up to $545 (2024)
- Coverage gap ("Donut Hole"): After $5,030 in drug costs, you pay 25% until catastrophic coverage
- Catastrophic: You pay 5% or minimal copay

### Medigap (Supplemental Insurance)
- Standardized Plans: A, B, C, D, F, G, K, L, M, N
- **Plan F**: Most comprehensive (not available to new enrollees after 2020)
- **Plan G**: Popular alternative (covers everything except Part B deductible)
- Cost varies by age, location, tobacco use, gender

## Enrollment Timeline
- **Initial Enrollment Period (IEP)**: 3 months before to 3 months after 65th birthday (7 months total)
- **General Enrollment Period (GEP)**: January 1 - March 31, coverage starts July 1
- **Special Enrollment Period (SEP)**: 8 months after losing employer coverage
