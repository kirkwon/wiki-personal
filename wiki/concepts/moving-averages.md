# Moving Averages

A moving average (MA) is a calculation used in time series analysis to smooth out short-term fluctuations and highlight longer-term trends. It works by calculating the average of data points over a specific, rolling window period. Common types include Simple Moving Average (SMA) and Exponential Moving Average (EMA), which are fundamental tools for technical analysis in finance and signal processing.
