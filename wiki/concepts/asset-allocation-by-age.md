# Asset Allocation by Age

Guidelines for adjusting stock/bond/cash allocation as you age, balancing growth with preservation.

## 20s: Aggressive Growth
- Stocks: 80-90% (US large-cap 30-40%, mid/small-cap 20-30%, international 20-30%)
- Bonds: 0-10%
- Cash: 0-10%
- Focus: Growth, time to recover from volatility

## 30s: High Growth
- Stocks: 70-80%
- Bonds: 15-20%
- Cash/Alternatives: 5-10%
- Focus: Still aggressive, some stability

## 40s: Balanced Growth
- Stocks: 60-70%
- Bonds: 20-30%
- Cash/Alternatives: 5-10%
- Focus: Balance growth with risk reduction

## 50s: Moderate Growth
- Stocks: 50-60%
- Bonds: 30-40%
- Cash/Alternatives: 5-10%
- Focus: Preservation with some growth

## 60s: Conservative
- Stocks: 30-40%
- Bonds: 40-50%
- Cash: 10-20%
- Focus: Income and preservation

## Key Principle
Inflation erodes purchasing power — bonds alone don't grow enough. Equity exposure is needed throughout retirement to maintain real purchasing power.
