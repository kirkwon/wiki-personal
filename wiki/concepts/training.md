# Training

Training is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

As a universal mental model, Training functions across all domains—athletic, intellectual, professional, and personal—to help identify how deliberate development and practice shape outcomes. It connects to concepts like [[grit-concept]] (sustained effort) and [[active-revision-techniques]] (structured learning methods), offering a lens for understanding how systematic preparation interacts with innate ability.

The model is referenced through [[david-epstein]]'s research in *The Sports Gene*, which explores the science of extraordinary athletic performance and the interplay between genetics and training. Training as a mental model helps distinguish between what can be developed through practice and what is constrained by inherent traits, informing better decisions about investment of time and resources.

Confidence in this model is moderate (0.7) given the thin evidentiary base—currently supported by a single source. Further development with additional research on deliberate practice, skill acquisition, and learning science would strengthen its applicability.
