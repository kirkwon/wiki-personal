# Swans

Swans as a mental model captures Taleb's insight that extreme, rare events (black swans) are the primary drivers of outcomes in complex systems. While we tend to plan around the expected and the average, it is the swans—those unexpected, high-impact occurrences—that actually shape history, markets, and individual lives.

## Core Insight
The model asks: are you prepared for the thing you cannot predict? Rather than trying to forecast rare events, the focus should be on building resilience and robustness against them.

## Practical Implications
- In [[finance]], this means stress-testing portfolios against extreme scenarios rather than optimizing for average returns.
- In [[history]], it means recognizing that narrative explanations of the past are often post-hoc rationalizations.
- In [[life]], it means accepting uncertainty and avoiding the illusion of control.
