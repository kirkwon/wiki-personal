# Llm Workflows

Llm Workflows refer to structured sequences of operations that utilize large language models (LLMs) to perform complex tasks beyond single prompts. These workflows often chain multiple calls—such as retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), prompt engineering, and data parsing—to manage state and achieve a coherent output. They transform raw model capability into reliable, multi-step automated processes, acting as the connective tissue between an LLM and external tools or specialized business logic.
