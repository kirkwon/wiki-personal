# Purpose (Mental Model)

Purpose is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Purpose functions as a universal mental model—applicable across all domains—for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition. It serves as a high-level lens for understanding why things matter and how actions connect to broader outcomes.
