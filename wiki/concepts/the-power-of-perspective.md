# The Power of Perspective

Understanding how personal biases, cultural influences, and emotional states shape thinking. Parrish advocates techniques for broadening perspective to make more informed decisions, recognizing that limited viewpoints lead to distorted judgments.

This concept ties to the broader [[cognitive-biases-library]], particularly [[ascription-bias]] (attributing positive qualities to oneself) and [[confirmation-bias]] (seeking confirming information), both of which narrow perspective.
