# Breaking Composition Rules

After mastering composition rules, intentional rule-breaking becomes a tool for creative expression. Rules should be broken for effect — tension, imbalance, surprise, creative expression, or when the subject demands it.

## When to Break Rules
- For effect: Tension, imbalance, surprise
- Creative expression: Your vision
- Subject demands it: Sometimes the subject is better placed in the center

## Examples of Breaking Rules
- Center composition for symmetry or dramatic effect
- Cluttered background for environmental portraits
- No leading lines for flat, graphic images
- Rule-breaking for storytelling when it serves the narrative

Understanding rules deeply before breaking them is key — this ensures the rule-breaking is intentional rather than accidental.
