# Honesty

Honesty is framed as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It provides a mental shortcut for making sense of how people behave, particularly around self-deception and dishonesty.

Drawing on Dan Ariely's *The Honest Truth About Dishonesty*, honesty as a model highlights how people systematically lie to themselves and others, often unconsciously. Key themes include small-scale cheating, moral disengagement, and the ways conflicts of interest erode truthful behavior. This connects to existing wiki concepts around [[cognitive-biases-library]], [[confirmation-bias]], [[illusion-of-control]], and [[overconfidence-and-certainty]].

The model has medium confidence (0.7) and is still underdeveloped—it needs deeper integration with behavioral economics and ethical design considerations to become a fully useful navigation tool.
