# Effortless by Greg McKeown

*Effortless* builds on McKeown's Essentialism framework by addressing how to make essential activities feel natural and sustainable. The book explores how to perform at your best by aligning with strengths and removing unnecessary friction.

## Core Ideas

- Perform activities that align with natural strengths.
- Eliminate unnecessary friction and steps.
- Design environments and systems that make essential work easier.
- True effortlessness comes from strategic elimination, not from working harder.

## Connection to Wiki

Connects to the [[effort]] mental model by reframing effort as something to be minimized through strategic focus. Complements [[essentialism]] and [[minimalism]] as tools for navigating complexity.
