# roast-duck

A roast duck is a culinary dish where a whole duck is cooked by roasting until the skin becomes crispy while maintaining moist meat underneath. This dish often requires precise temperature control and timing to achieve optimal flavor, making it an interesting subject for both photography (capturing its golden-brown hues) and cognitive science (studying sensory perception of taste). From a personal finance perspective, roast duck can be considered a luxury item due to the cost associated with high-quality ingredients and specialized cooking techniques.
