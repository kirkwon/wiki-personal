# Service Over Profit Leadership

Service over profit leadership is the practice of prioritizing employee well-being and care over short-term profit maximization. Sinek's central claim is that organizations which serve their people first ultimately achieve better financial outcomes.

## Core Argument
The paradox of service-first leadership: by putting employees before customers, organizations build trust, belonging, and performance that lead to superior customer experiences and sustainable profit.

## Examples
- Southwest Airlines
- Costco Wholesale
- Starbucks
