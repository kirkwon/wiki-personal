# June Gloom

June Gloom is a seasonal fog phenomenon that affects the San Francisco Bay Area, particularly in summer. At Ocean Beach, it can create near-zero visibility and last all day, shaping the beach experience from June through August.

## Characteristics

- **Intense summer fog** that can persist all day
- **Visibility near zero** during peak episodes
- **Creates moody, atmospheric scenes** for photography
- **Does not deter locals** — dedicated beachgoers and surfers still go out

## Impact on Ocean Beach

Summer at Ocean Beach is foggy and windy despite warm inland temperatures. The fog affects visibility for surfing, photography, and general beach enjoyment. Fall (September-November) offers the best weather with less fog and more sunshine.^[ocean-beach.md]
