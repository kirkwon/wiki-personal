# Five Whys Technique

A systematic diagnostic tool for identifying root causes of problems within organizations. The technique involves asking "why" repeatedly — typically five times — to move beyond surface-level symptoms to underlying systemic causes.

## Context in Senge's Framework
Part of a suite of tools including flowcharting and paradox management for creative problem-solving within learning organizations. Connects to [[active-revision-techniques]] and [[polya-four-steps]] as approaches to deeper inquiry.
