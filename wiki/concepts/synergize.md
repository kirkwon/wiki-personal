# Synergize

Synergy is the principle that collective effort produces results greater than the sum of individual contributions. Covey describes it as the culmination of the interdependent habits—win-win thinking and empathetic listening create the conditions for creative collaboration.

## Key Ideas

- **1 + 1 = 3 or more**: When people collaborate with genuine openness, they can produce solutions and outcomes that no individual could have conceived alone.
- **Valuing differences**: Synergy requires seeing differences as strengths rather than obstacles. Diverse perspectives, when combined through trust and communication, generate innovation.
- **Creative cooperation**: The habit transforms interpersonal friction from destructive conflict into productive, creative tension.

## Relation to Other Concepts

Synergy connects to [[building-a-cohesive-team]] through its emphasis on collective effectiveness. It also relates to [[think-win-win]], since mutual benefit is a prerequisite for genuine collaboration. The concept of creative combination through diverse inputs parallels [[inventors-paradox]], where more ambitious problems can yield simpler solutions.
