# Velveting

Velveting is a Chinese cooking technique that uses cornstarch (and optionally egg white) in a marinade to create a silky, tender texture in proteins when cooked. It is a key technique in Chinese restaurant-style stir-fry.

## How It Works

Cornstarch is dissolved in the marinade and coats the protein. When cooked, the starch creates a protective layer that seals in moisture and produces a velvety texture.

## Velveting Marinade

- Soy sauce: base seasoning
- Cornstarch: 1 tsp per pound of protein
- Egg white: optional, for extra silkiness
- Other aromatics as desired

## Application

Used in Chinese restaurant-style stir-fry for beef, chicken, and other proteins. The technique is distinct from standard cornstarch coating in that the cornstarch is part of the marinade itself, creating a more integrated silky result.

## Connection to Stir-Fry
Velveting is closely associated with [[stir-fry-technique]] and [[wok-hei]], where the rapid high-heat cooking benefits from proteins that have been pre-treated for tenderness and moisture retention.
