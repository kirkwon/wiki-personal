# Clever Dripper

A Clever Dripper refers to an individual who adeptly manages resources or information flow in various domains such as cooking (efficiency in ingredient usage), photography (optimal light capture), personal finance (strategic investment and saving), and cognitive science (effective learning techniques). This term encapsulates the skill of maximizing utility from limited inputs through intelligent decision-making.
