# Optimism

Optimism is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

Unlike simple positivity, optimism functions as a structured mental model applicable across domains. It is referenced as a universal mental model with moderate confidence (0.7), drawing from David Deutsch's *The Beginning of Infinity*, where optimism is presented as a prerequisite for progress, problem-solving, and infinite knowledge creation.

## Key Characteristics
- Universal applicability across domains
- Functions as a mental shortcut for pattern and relationship recognition
- Distinct from general positivity—it is a framework for navigating complexity
- Connects to infinite-game-mindset, antifragility, and solution-seeking frameworks
