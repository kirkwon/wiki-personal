# Phenomenal Consciousness

Phenomenal consciousness refers to the subjective, qualitative aspect of experience—the "what it is like" feeling that accompanies perception (often called qualia). It denotes the raw content of conscious awareness, such as the redness of red or the ache of pain, distinguishing it from mere functional processing. This concept is central to the Philosophy of Mind and the 'Hard Problem' of consciousness, exploring how physical brain activity gives rise to subjective experience.
