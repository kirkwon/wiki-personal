# Retirement Savings Calculator

Template for projecting retirement savings and planning retirement income.

## Current Retirement Status
- Current age, retirement age, current savings, monthly contribution, annual return

## Projected Growth
- Years to retirement, projected retirement savings
- Monthly contributions total, investment growth

## Retirement Income Planning
- Desired monthly income, expected lifespan
- Required portfolio, current projected, shortfall/surplus

## RMD Calculator
- Account balance, age, life expectancy factor
- RMD amount (Balance ÷ Life Expectancy Factor)
- Federal and state tax impact, effective tax rate
