# Cognitive Science

The interdisciplinary study of the mind and its processes — perception, attention, memory, language, reasoning, decision-making, and problem-solving. Draws on psychology, neuroscience, linguistics, philosophy, and computer science (particularly AI).

## Key Areas

- **Heuristics & Biases** — systematic deviations from rational judgment (Kahneman, Tversky)
- **Dual Process Theory** — System 1 (fast, intuitive) vs System 2 (slow, analytical)
- **Embodied Cognition** — the body and environment shape thought
- **Cognitive Load Theory** — working memory has limited capacity; learning is constrained by it

## Connections
- Informs [[artificial-intelligence]] by modeling human cognition as a target or analogy
- [[critical-thinking]] is applied cognitive science
- The Bayesian Rogue identity draws on cognitive science principles (calibration, debiasing, metacognition)
