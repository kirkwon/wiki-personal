# Algorithmic Processes

Algorithmic processes are defined as finite, ordered sets of instructions or computational steps designed to solve a specific problem or perform a calculation. They provide a systematic method for transforming an input into a desired output by following predictable rules. These procedures underpin modern computing and mathematics, forming the theoretical basis for computer programs and automated systems. Understanding algorithms is crucial for developing efficient software solutions across various fields of science and technology.
