# Sense

Sense is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Overview

Sense functions as a mental shortcut rather than a detailed analytical framework. It emphasizes pattern and relationship recognition as a way to navigate complexity intuitively. This positions it alongside other universal mental models like [[thinking-mental-model]], [[overview]], and [[stillness]], but with a distinct emphasis on sensing patterns rather than structured analysis.

## Connection to Simon Sinek

The concept is attributed to Simon Sinek's work, particularly [[leaders-eat-last---simon-sinek]]. Sinek's broader framework centers on inspirational leadership and the biology of trust, where "sense" likely refers to the ability to intuitively perceive group dynamics, safety signals, and organizational health.

## Relationship to Existing Mental Models

- [[thinking-mental-model]] — Structured pattern recognition through deliberate thinking
- [[overview]] — Broad pattern recognition across complex situations
- [[stillness]] — Navigational stillness as a tool for perceiving patterns
- [[group]] — Pattern recognition within group dynamics
- [[infinite-game-mindset]] — Sinek's infinite game framework connects to how leaders sense long-term dynamics
