# Backlighting

Backlighting is a lighting technique where the subject is placed between the camera and the light source, commonly the sun during golden hour.

## Effect

- Rim light on hair and edges of the subject
- Warm glow around the subject
- Potential for silhouette effects

## When to Use

Backlighting is effective for portraits (creating warmth and glow), landscapes (dramatic and atmospheric), and silhouettes (subject against the sun). It is one of the key techniques for golden hour photography alongside [[side-lighting]] and [[front-lighting]].
