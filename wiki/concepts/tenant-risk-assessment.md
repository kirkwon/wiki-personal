# Tenant Risk Assessment

Evaluating risks associated with tenants including credit quality, income stability, lease terms, and tenant mix.

## Risk Factors
- Credit Quality: Credit score, rental history
- Income Stability: Employment verification, income requirements
- Lease Terms: Length, renewal options, termination clauses
- Guarantor Strength: Individual vs. corporate guarantees
- Tenant Mix: Residential vs. commercial concentration

## Scoring
Excellent: corporate lease, strong credit. Good: stable residential, good credit. Average: mixed tenant base, moderate credit. Poor: high-risk tenants, poor credit. Critical: non-paying tenants, legal issues.
