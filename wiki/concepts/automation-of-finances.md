# Automation of Finances

The principle that financial success comes from building systems that handle money automatically, removing the need for ongoing willpower and decision-making.

## Why Automation Works

Willpower fails — it's a limited resource that depletes. Systems work — they don't get tired, don't require motivation, and execute consistently. Automation is success by default.

## What to Automate

- **Bill payments**: Automatic minimum payments on all credit cards and bills
- **Savings transfers**: Automatic transfer to savings account on payday
- **Investment contributions**: Automatic monthly contributions to Roth IRA, 401(k), taxable accounts
- **Debt payments**: Automatic minimums plus extra payments on target debt
- **Payday workflow**: Income hits checking → automatic transfers to savings/investments → bill payments → remaining balance is guilt-free spending

## Impact

- Setup time: 2-3 hours
- Impact: $100,000+ over 30 years
- Ongoing maintenance: < 1 hour per year
