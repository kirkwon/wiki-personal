# Incremental Validation
Incremental validation is the process of testing a mental model incrementally, starting with small, low-stakes tests and gradually increasing the stakes as the model is refined.

## Introduction
Incremental validation is an essential component of validating mental models. This page provides an overview of incremental validation, including its definition, importance, and applications.

## Definition
Incremental validation is the process of testing a mental model incrementally, starting with small, low-stakes tests and gradually increasing the stakes as the model is refined. This process involves designing a series of tests, conducting the tests, and refining the mental model based on the results.

## Importance
Incremental validation is important because it provides an opportunity to test the mental model in a controlled and systematic way, identify areas for improvement, and refine the model to make it more accurate and reliable.

## Applications
Incremental validation has a wide range of applications, including business, economics, politics, and personal decision-making. It can be used to validate mental models, identify opportunities and risks, and develop effective strategies.
