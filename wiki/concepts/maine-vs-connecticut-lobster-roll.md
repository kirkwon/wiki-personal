# Maine vs Connecticut Lobster Roll

Two distinct regional styles of lobster roll exist in New England.

## Maine (Butter) Style

- Lobster meat chunks with melted, clarified butter
- Toasted split-top bun
- Celery optional

## Connecticut (Mayo) Style

- Lobster meat chunks with mayonnaise, lemon juice, and celery
- Toasted split-top bun

Legend holds that lobster rolls originated in 1918 at Perry's restaurant in Milford, CT.
