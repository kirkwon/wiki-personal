# Principles (Ray Dalio's Framework)

Developed by Ray Dalio of Bridgewater Associates, the Principles is a comprehensive decision-making and management framework centered on root causes and radical transparency. It guides individuals and organizations to define principles that govern behavior, ensuring consistent action regardless of circumstances. Key components include Believability-Weighted Decision Making (BWDM), which evaluates ideas based on the credibility of their proponents, and implementing systematic "radical truth" through open feedback. The framework aims to build high performance by making objective data and systemic improvement the ultimate focus over personal ego or groupthink.
