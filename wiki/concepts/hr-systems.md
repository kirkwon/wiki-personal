# HR Systems

Human Resources (HR) Information Systems are integrated software solutions designed to manage and automate core human capital functions within an organization. These systems track employee data, payroll processing, benefits administration, and performance reviews in a centralized manner. By digitizing these processes, they improve operational efficiency, ensure compliance with labor laws, and provide actionable insights into workforce demographics.
