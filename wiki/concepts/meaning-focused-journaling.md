# Meaning-Focused Journaling

A reflective writing practice derived from Frankl's logotherapy, aimed at identifying and cultivating sources of meaning in one's life. Rather than tracking tasks or emotions, meaning-focused journaling prompts the writer to examine what gives their life purpose and how they can take responsibility for their commitments.

## Practice
- Reflect on what activities, relationships, or causes provide a sense of meaning
- Examine how one's attitude toward unavoidable difficulties can be oriented toward purpose
- Identify areas where responsibility has been avoided and where commitment could be deepened
- Regular practice supports the cultivation of tragic optimism and a defiant stance toward suffering
