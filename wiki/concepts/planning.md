# Planning

Planning is a universal mental model for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world by identifying essential structures and reducing noise. It is the practice of systematically improving efficiency and output by identifying and implementing methods, techniques, and frameworks that maximize productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices.

## Definition

Planning functions as a principle that helps structure how one perceives and engages with complexity by recognizing patterns and relationships. It is positioned as a universal conceptual tool applicable across domains rather than a domain-specific technique.^[raw/Planning.md]

## Core Method

The Planning framework follows a cycle of **Audit → Apply → Iterate → Scale**:

- **Audit** your current workflow to identify efficiency losses, track how time and attention are spent, and note patterns of wasted effort.
- **Apply** frameworks to restructure your approach, focusing on high-impact activities and eliminating or delegating low-value tasks.
- **Iterate** by measuring the impact of changes, adjusting based on results, and building consistency through habit formation.
- **Scale** what works by standardizing effective approaches, documenting processes for reuse, and teaching others to multiply impact.

## Domains of Application

- **Personal Productivity** — Planning weekly schedules, managing competing priorities, breaking down complex projects into manageable tasks.
- **Professional Context** — Team coordination, meeting efficiency, enhancing decision-making processes.
- **Learning and Growth** — Accelerating skill acquisition, organizing and retaining information, identifying and filling knowledge gaps.
