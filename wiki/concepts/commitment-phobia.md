# Commitment Phobia

Commitment phobia refers to the psychological reluctance to fully commit to or quit from certain scenarios, influencing our ability to make clear decisions about when to persevere or walk away. Duke examines how this fear can distort decision-making, leading to either premature quitting or stubborn continuation.

This connects to [[decision-paralysis]], where the inability to decide becomes its own problem. It also relates to [[inattention-to-results]] — when we avoid evaluating outcomes honestly, we cannot accurately assess whether quitting is warranted.

Duke offers constructive approaches to addressing commitment phobia, emphasizing that recognizing the bias is the first step toward making clearer decisions. See [[the-psychology-behind-commitment-phobia]] and [[quit-the-power-of-knowing-when-to-walk-away---annie-duke]].
