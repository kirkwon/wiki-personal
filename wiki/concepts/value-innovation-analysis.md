# Value Innovation Analysis

Value innovation is the cornerstone of Blue Ocean Strategy—the simultaneous pursuit of differentiation and low cost that makes the competition irrelevant. Rather than choosing between creating value for buyers (differentiation) and reducing costs, value innovation breaks the value-cost trade-off by redefining the factors the industry competes on.

Key principles:
- Value innovation occurs only when companies align innovation with utility, price, and cost
- It is not about technological innovation alone, but about providing a leap in buyer value
- The strategy canvas reveals where value innovation opportunities exist by showing the current competitive landscape

Value innovation creates a new market space (blue ocean) where the company can command uncontested demand and achieve both differentiation and low cost.
