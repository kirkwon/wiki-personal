# Roth vs Traditional IRA

Choosing between Roth and Traditional IRA depends on your current versus expected future tax situation.

## Traditional IRA
- Pre-tax contributions reduce current taxable income
- Tax-deferred growth (pay taxes when withdrawing in retirement)
- Taxed as ordinary income upon withdrawal
- Better if: higher income now, expect lower income later, lower tax bracket in retirement

## Roth IRA
- After-tax contributions (no deduction now)
- Tax-free growth and tax-free withdrawals in retirement
- Better if: younger, expect higher income later, expect higher tax rates in retirement

## Decision Framework
| Factor | Favor Roth | Favor Traditional |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Age | Younger | Older |
| Expected future income | Higher | Lower |
| Expected tax rates | Higher | Lower |
| Current tax bracket | Lower | Higher |

This decision intersects with [[tax-torpedoes-in-retirement]] and the broader tax strategy discussed in [[retirement-planning]].

## Contribution Limits (2024)
- $7,000 if under age 50
- $8,000 if age 50 or older
