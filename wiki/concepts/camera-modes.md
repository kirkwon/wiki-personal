# Camera Modes

Camera modes determine how much control you have over exposure settings—specifically aperture, shutter speed, and ISO.

## Modes

### Automatic (Auto/Full Auto)
Camera makes all decisions. Best for beginners or quick shots where creative control is not needed. No creative control.

### Program Mode (P)
Camera selects both aperture and shutter speed. Offers some control for quick shooting when you want some control but less than manual.

### Aperture Priority (Av/A)
**YOU choose aperture**, camera chooses shutter speed. Most commonly used by photographers and is the most commonly used mode for portraits, landscapes, and situations where depth of field control is important.

### Shutter Priority (Tv/S)
**YOU choose shutter speed**, camera chooses aperture. Best for sports, action, and situations requiring intentional motion blur.

### Manual Mode (M)
**YOU choose everything**: aperture, shutter speed, ISO. Provides complete creative control and is essential for difficult lighting situations. Requires understanding of the [[exposure-triangle]].

For [[street-photography]], Aperture Priority or Shutter Priority modes are commonly recommended depending on whether depth of field or motion freezing is the priority.
