# Creativity

Creativity is the ability to generate novel and useful ideas or solutions. In psychology, it is often studied as a function of divergent thinking—the capacity to explore multiple possible answers rather than settling for the first one. While highly valued in human domains like art and problem-solving, defining its mechanisms remains complex, drawing from cognitive science, neuroscience, and philosophy. Modern research explores both innate talent and learned processes that can be fostered through practice and diverse input.
