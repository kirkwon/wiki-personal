# Strategy Canvas

The **strategy canvas** is a central analytical tool in Blue Ocean Strategy for mapping how an industry competes across key factors. It plots the value curves of industry players along the factors the industry competes on, revealing where curves converge (red ocean competition) and where gaps exist (blue ocean opportunity).

By plotting factors on the horizontal axis and performance levels on the vertical axis, the canvas shows the shape of competition — where competitors invest heavily, where they neglect, and where overinvestment or underinvestment occurs. This visualization makes it possible to see that most industry competitors invest in the same factors, leading to strategic convergence and increasingly intense competition.

The canvas reveals where the industry overinvests or underinvests, highlighting opportunities for divergence from the existing competitive landscape. A blue ocean strategy aims to create a value curve that diverges from the industry's consensus by reconstructing which factors matter through the [[four-actions-framework-eliminate-reduce-raise-create]].

The canvas serves as both a diagnostic tool (revealing competitive inertia) and a creative tool (identifying where to break away from industry logic). It is a starting point for applying the Four Actions Framework: after mapping the current industry, a company can decide which factors to eliminate, reduce, raise, or create to reconstruct the value curve.
