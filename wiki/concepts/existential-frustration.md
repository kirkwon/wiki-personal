# Existential Frustration

Existential frustration refers to the modern malaise that arises from a lack of meaning in life. Frankl describes it as a pervasive condition in contemporary society where people feel empty, directionless, or unable to connect their daily activities to a larger purpose.

## Manifestation

- Apathy and depersonalization
- Feeling that traditional success is empty
- Loss of motivation or purpose
- Difficulty engaging with daily life

## Connection to Logotherapy

Existential frustration is the primary condition that logotherapy addresses. By helping individuals identify their sources of meaning—through work, love, or courage in suffering—logotherapy provides a path out of this modern despair.

## Guardrails

Frankl cautions against using meaning-finding as another form of achievement anxiety or as an excuse to avoid addressing genuine mental health issues. The search for meaning should be a genuine exploration, not a performance.
