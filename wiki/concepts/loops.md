# Loops

Loops is a universal mental model that serves as a conceptual tool for understanding and navigating complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world, drawing on systems thinking principles.

## Definition

Loops help identify feedback dynamics—both reinforcing and balancing—that drive behavior in complex systems. By recognizing these circular cause-and-effect patterns, one can better anticipate outcomes and design interventions that account for system dynamics rather than linear cause-and-effect assumptions.

## Key Attributes

- **Applicability**: Universal across domains
- **Confidence**: 0.7 (moderate)
- **Source**: Donella H. Meadows, *Thinking in Systems*
