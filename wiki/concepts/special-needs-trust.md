# Special Needs Trust

Provides for disabled individuals without affecting eligibility for government benefits (SSI, Medicaid, etc.).

## Types

- **First-Party SNT**: Funded with disabled person's own assets
- **Third-Party SNT**: Funded by family members or friends
- **Pooled Trust**: Managed by nonprofit organization
- **Self-Settled SNT**: Funded with disabled person's assets with specific rules

## Permissible Distributions

- Basic needs: food, clothing, shelter, medical care
- Enhanced quality of life: educational, recreational, cultural activities
- Medical expenses not covered by government benefits
- Administrative expenses and supplemental needs

## Prohibited Distributions

- Direct cash that affects benefit eligibility
- Items that would reduce government benefits
- Excessive trustee compensation
- Payments to persons other than disabled beneficiary
