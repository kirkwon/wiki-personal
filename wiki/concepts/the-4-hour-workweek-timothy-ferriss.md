# The 4-Hour Workweek - Timothy Ferriss

Timothy Ferriss's *The 4-Hour Workweek* introduces a lifestyle design framework centered on eliminating unnecessary work, automating income streams, and decoupling earnings from time spent. Key concepts include:

- **Income decoupling** — Designing income that does not require trading hours for dollars.
- **Pareto principle (80/20)** — Focusing effort on the minority of activities that produce the majority of results.
- **Passive income systems** — Building automated or semi-automated revenue streams.
- **Mini-retirements** — Taking extended time off during working years rather than saving it all for retirement.

The book connects to existing productivity themes in the wiki, particularly Cal Newport's *Deep Work* and David Allen's *Getting Things Done*, though Ferriss takes a more aggressive stance on eliminating low-value work entirely.
