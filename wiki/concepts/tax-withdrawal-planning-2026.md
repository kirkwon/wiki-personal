# tax-withdrawal-planning-2026

This planning methodology outlines optimized strategies for managing retirement income and capital drawdowns across multiple taxable accounts (e.g., Roth, Traditional IRA, brokerage). It focuses specifically on minimizing Adjusted Gross Income (AGI) and maximizing the utilization of favorable tax brackets during the 2026 fiscal cycle. Key considerations include Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs), asset location strategies, and anticipating future changes in Medicare premiums or federal tax law.
