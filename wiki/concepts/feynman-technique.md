# The Feynman Technique

Learning by teaching and simplifying concepts — if you can't explain something simply, you don't understand it well enough. The Feynman Technique is a learning method where you learn by teaching — you simplify complex concepts to the point where you could explain them to a child, exposing gaps in your understanding. Covered in Part VII of *Super Thinking*.

## Core Idea
1. Choose a concept to learn.
2. Explain it in simple terms as if teaching someone with no background.
3. Identify gaps in your explanation — where you struggle or resort to jargon.
4. Return to the source material to fill those gaps.
5. Simplify and use analogies.

## Why It Works
Teaching forces you to organize knowledge, identify ambiguities, and distill ideas to their essence. This is more effective than passive re-reading because it requires active engagement.
