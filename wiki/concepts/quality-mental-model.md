# Quality as a Mental Model

Quality is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Quality.md]

## Definition
Quality functions as a universal mental model applicable across domains. It prioritizes what matters—meaningful outcomes, appropriate intervention, and meaningful living—over purely quantitative metrics like survival rates or raw data points.^[raw/articles/Being Mortal - Atul Gawande]

## Cross-Source Perspectives

### Atul Gawande and Being Mortal
Gawande's exploration of end-of-life care in Being Mortal illustrates quality as a decision-making lens. The central tension is between quality of life and length of life—choosing medical interventions that respect patient autonomy and prioritize meaningful living rather than extending existence through aggressive treatment.^[raw/articles/Being Mortal - Atul Gawande]
