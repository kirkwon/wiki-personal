# Commitment Devices

Pre-commitment strategies designed to counteract predictable irrational behaviors, particularly procrastination. By locking in future behavior in advance, individuals can overcome the self-control failures that arise from systematic biases.

## Examples

- Removing distractions before starting work (reducing friction to productive action)
- Setting deadlines with external accountability
- Using financial penalties for missed goals
- Structuring environments so the desired choice is the default option
- Publicly committing to goals to leverage social norms

## Connection to Procrastination

Ariely's work on procrastination shows that self-control failures are systematic rather than random. Commitment devices work precisely because they exploit the predictability of these failures — by designing the choice environment in advance, you bypass the moment of weakness.

## Related to Default Option Design

Commitment devices pair with [[default-option-design]] — both work by restructuring the choice environment so that irrational tendencies are channeled toward better outcomes.
