# Planning Tools & Templates

Planning tools and templates are structured frameworks used across project management, business development, and academic research to organize complex ideas and define actionable steps. They serve as standardized starting points—such as Gantt charts, SWOT analyses, or OKR trackers—helping users visualize scope, manage dependencies, and allocate resources effectively. While highly varied by domain (e.g., Agile methodologies vs. scientific grant writing), they are essential for ensuring systematic progression from conceptualization to execution.
