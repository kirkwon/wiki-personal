# Research as a mental model

Research is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

As a universal mental model, research functions across all domains as a navigational principle—identifying underlying patterns in performance, genetics, training, and any area of inquiry. It connects to existing mental models like [[analysis]], [[examination]], and [[thinking-mental-model]], extending the wiki's framework of pattern-recognition tools for navigating complexity.

The cross-source perspective from David Epstein's *The Sports Gene* illustrates how scientific research reveals patterns in athletic excellence, supporting the broader claim that research functions as a distinct mental model for sense-making.^[raw/articles/Research.md]

Open questions remain about how "research" distinguishes itself from related models like [[analysis]] or [[thinking-mental-model]], but its strength lies in its universal applicability and focus on structured pattern discovery.
