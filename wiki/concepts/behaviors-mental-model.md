# Behaviors (mental model)

Behaviors is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Behaviors.md]

The model is framed as universally applicable, functioning as a lens through which to interpret behavioral patterns, feedback loops, and the dynamics between people and their environments.^[raw/articles/Behaviors.md]
