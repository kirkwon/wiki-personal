# Creamy Clam Chowder vs Manhattan Chowder

New England clam chowder is a creamy, white chowder made with quahogs, potatoes, bacon, onions, milk or cream, and herbs. It is distinctly different from Manhattan clam chowder, which is tomato-based.

## New England Style

- Creamy and white broth
- Clams (quahogs), bacon or salt pork, onions, potatoes
- Milk or cream base
- Thyme, bay leaf
- Served with oyster crackers

## Manhattan Style

- Clear, tomato-based broth
- Includes tomatoes as a key ingredient
- Same clams and potatoes but with a reddish tint

This distinction is central to New England seafood identity, as noted in the comparison with other regional seafood traditions.
