# B Corporations

B Corporations are a business model that prioritizes social impact and well-being over mere financial growth, serving as an alternative way of measuring success within businesses.

## Key Ideas
- Legal structure that balances profit and purpose
- Third-party certification for meeting social and environmental performance standards
- Integration of stakeholder interests into corporate governance
- Example of alternative metrics beyond traditional financial returns

## Connection to Broader Themes
Serves as a concrete model within [[responsible-capitalism]] and [[redefining-success-metrics]] for how businesses can operate differently.
