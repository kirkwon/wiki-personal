# Genealogical Analysis of Morality

Genealogical analysis is Nietzsche's method of tracing moral concepts to their historical and psychological origins rather than accepting them as self-evident truths or divine commands.

## Method

- Instead of asking "Is this moral principle true?", Nietzsche asks "Where did this moral principle come from? What psychological need does it serve? What power dynamics produced it?"
- This reveals that many moral concepts — equality, pity, humility, selflessness — arose from specific historical circumstances (particularly the resentment of the powerless) rather than from universal reason.
- The method exposes the hidden motivations behind moral claims: philosophers are not objective truth-seekers but are driven by personal convictions and the Will to Power.
