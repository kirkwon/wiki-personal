# Contrarianism

Contrarianism is the practice of holding opinions or taking positions that go against prevailing consensus. Peter Thiel emphasizes contrarian thinking as essential for discovering overlooked opportunities and secrets.

## Key Ideas
- Most people agree with the majority, so opportunities lie in what others have overlooked
- Believing in something others don't is the foundation of valuable thinking
- Contrarianism must be combined with critical analysis to avoid being contrarian for its own sake
- Secrets about the world exist but require willingness to look where others won't

## Connection to Thiel
Contrarianism is a core pillar of the [[thiel-mental-model]] and [[zero-to-one-thinking]], connecting to broader themes of [[overconfidence-and-certainty]] and independent judgment.
