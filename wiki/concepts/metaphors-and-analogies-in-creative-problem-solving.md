# Metaphors and Analogies in Creative Problem-Solving

De Bono argues that metaphors and analogies make abstract problems more concrete and accessible. By mapping a familiar domain onto an unfamiliar one, new solution paths become visible that would be invisible through purely analytical reasoning.

This concept extends [[analogical-reasoning-in-problem-solving]], which uses similar solved problems as templates for new ones, and relates to [[generalization-in-problem-solving]], which extends solutions to broader cases. De Bono's emphasis is on the generative power of analogy — not just finding similarities but using the structure of one domain to restructure thinking in another.
