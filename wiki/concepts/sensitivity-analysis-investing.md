# Sensitivity Analysis in Investing

What-if scenario analysis stress-testing key assumptions to evaluate investment risk and opportunity. This approach helps investors understand the range of possible outcomes and identify which assumptions matter most.

## Approach

The [[single-property-analysis-template]] includes sensitivity tables varying purchase price, interest rate, rent level, and expenses:

| Scenario | Purchase Price | Interest Rate | Rent Level | Cash Flow | Cash on Cash | Cap Rate |
|----------|----------------|---------------|------------|-----------|--------------|----------|
| Base Case | $[Amount] | [X]% | $[Amount] | $[Amount] | [X]% | [X]% |
| 10% Lower Price | $[Amount] | [X]% | $[Amount] | $[Amount] | [X]% | [X]% |
| 1% Higher Rate | $[Amount] | [X]% | $[Amount] | $[Amount] | [X]% | [X]% |
| 5% Lower Rent | $[Amount] | [X]% | $[Amount] | $[Amount] | [X]% | [X]% |
| 10% Higher Expenses | $[Amount] | [X]% | $[Amount] | $[Amount] | [X]% | [X]% |

## Variables Tested
- Purchase price variations (e.g., 10% lower price)
- Interest rate changes (e.g., 1% higher rate)
- Rent level assumptions (e.g., 5% lower rent)
- Expense level changes (e.g., 10% higher expenses)

## Output Metrics
For each scenario, the analysis calculates:
- Net cash flow
- Cash on cash return
- Cap rate
- Risk range: best case, worst case, most likely, acceptable range

## Risk Assessment Output

- Best Case: $[Cash Flow], [X]% return
- Worst Case: $[Cash Flow], [X]% return
- Most Likely: $[Cash Flow], [X]% return
- Acceptable Range: $[Min] to $[Max] cash flow

## Connection to Broader Frameworks
This simple what-if approach contrasts with more formal scenario planning frameworks. While the template uses straightforward sensitivity tables, probabilistic methods like [[superforecasting]] or Monte Carlo simulations offer more nuanced risk assessment. The sensitivity analysis serves as a practical starting point for evaluating investment risk.

Sensitivity analysis connects to [[risk-assessment-framework]] and [[decision-making-frameworks]] by providing structured what-if scenarios that inform better investment decisions under uncertainty.
