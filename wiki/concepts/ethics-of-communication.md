# Ethics of communication

The ethics of communication concerns the moral dimensions of using advanced communication techniques. Duhigg's *Supercommunicators* dedicates Chapter 10 to this topic, emphasizing that effectiveness must be balanced with authenticity, transparency, and respect in interpersonal interactions.

## Core principles
- Authenticity: Being genuine rather than manipulative
- Transparency: Being open about intentions and methods
- Respect: Valuing the autonomy and dignity of others
- Balance: Avoiding the temptation to use communication skills for exploitation

## Tension with persuasion
There is an inherent tension between learning persuasion techniques and maintaining ethical communication—the book warns against using influence skills to manipulate others.
