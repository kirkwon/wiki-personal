# Prioritization in Effortless Living

McKeown emphasizes how identifying personal values can guide decision-making and help individuals focus on what truly matters, achieving effortless living.

## Key Ideas
- Personal values should anchor all prioritization decisions
- Identifying what matters prevents spreading effort across too many areas
- Prioritization is the foundation for saying no, delegating, and building habits
- The Eisenhower Matrix and similar frameworks can operationalize priorities
