# Seasonings

Seasonings are the liquid and paste-based flavoring agents used to build depth and complexity in cooking. Unlike dry spices and herbs, seasonings are typically sauces, pastes, or oils that are added during cooking to contribute umami, saltiness, sweetness, or aromatic qualities.

Key seasonings include:
- **Soy sauce** — Salty, umami-rich fermented soy product
- **Oyster sauce** — Briny, savory sauce adding depth to stir-fries
- **Sesame oil** — Nutty, aromatic oil used for finishing and flavoring
- **Fish sauce** — Intensely salty and umami-rich Southeast Asian staple
- **Rice vinegar** — Mild acidity for balancing rich dishes

Seasonings are central to [[umami-building-blocks]] and [[ingredient-pairings]], and work alongside [[aromatics]] like ginger, garlic, and scallion to build flavor layers.
