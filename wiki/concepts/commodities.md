# Commodities

Commodities are physical goods — precious metals, energy, agriculture, and base metals — that serve as real assets and inflation hedges. They provide tangible ownership and crisis protection (gold in market crashes) but generate no cash flow, have storage costs, and experience volatile prices.

## Types
- **Precious metals**: Gold, silver, platinum
- **Energy**: Oil, natural gas
- **Agriculture**: Corn, wheat, cattle
- **Base metals**: Copper, aluminum

## ETFs and Futures
- GLD (Gold ETF)
- USO (Oil ETF)
- DBA (Agriculture ETF)
- Futures contracts offer direct exposure with leverage

## Role in Portfolio
Commodities serve primarily as an inflation hedge and diversifier uncorrelated with stocks and bonds. Recommended allocation within alternatives: 10-20%.

## Key Risks
- No earnings growth or dividends
- Storage and insurance costs
- Price volatility
- Valuation difficulty without daily markets
