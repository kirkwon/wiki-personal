# Organizational Chart

An organizational chart (org chart) is a diagram that depicts an organization's internal structure and reporting relationships. It visually maps out departments, roles, and the chain of command from the highest executive level down to individual employees. These charts are crucial tools used by HR and management teams for planning, understanding power structures, and clarifying lines of authority. They help ensure everyone understands who reports to whom within the corporate hierarchy.
