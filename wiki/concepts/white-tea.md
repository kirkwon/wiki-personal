# White Tea

White tea is a delicate, minimally processed variety derived from the young leaf buds and unopened shoots of *Camellia sinensis*. It represents one of the purest forms of true tea, requiring little oxidation compared to green or black teas. Due to its gentle processing, white tea has a subtle flavor profile that can range from floral and honeyed to slightly sweet, making it an appreciated subject among connoisseurs. Its delicate nature means it is often consumed without milk or sugar to fully experience its nuanced taste.
