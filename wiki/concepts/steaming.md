# Steaming

Steaming is a gentle cooking technique that uses vapor from boiling water to cook food. It preserves nutrients, moisture, and natural flavors, making it ideal for delicate ingredients like vegetables, fish, and dim sum items. Steaming is a cornerstone of [[cantonese-cuisine]] and is central to [[yum-cha]] traditions where bamboo steamers are used to prepare dumplings and buns.
