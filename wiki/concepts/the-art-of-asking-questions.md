# The Art of Asking Questions

A framework for asking insightful questions that uncover deeper meanings and challenge assumptions, central to achieving clearer thinking and better decision-making. Shane Parrish emphasizes that questioning assumptions leads to clearer thinking by forcing examination of hidden premises and unstated beliefs.

This concept connects to [[what-is-the-unknown]], which focuses on clarifying what needs to be found or proven, and to [[decision-making-frameworks]] more broadly. Effective questioning serves as a bridge between recognizing one's biases and acting on clearer understanding.
