# Navigating Information Overload

Methods for effectively managing data intake to avoid confusion and improve clarity. Parrish addresses the challenge that excessive information can hinder rather than help clear thinking, presenting strategies for filtering and prioritizing information.

This concept relates to [[categorizing-information]] (organizing notes for efficiency) and [[personal-knowledge-vault]] (managing personal knowledge), and intersects with [[decision-paralysis]] which can result from overwhelming data.
