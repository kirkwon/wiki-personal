# Optimism Bias

Optimism bias is the cognitive tendency to overestimate the likelihood of positive events happening to oneself and underestimate the probability of negative events (e.g., accidents or illnesses). This psychological phenomenon suggests that individuals are inherently more optimistic about their own future compared to objective statistical probabilities. It can lead to poor risk assessment, as people may fail to prepare adequately for potential dangers.
