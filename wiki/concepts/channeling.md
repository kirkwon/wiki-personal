# Channeling (通道效应)

Channeling occurs when water finds paths through coffee grounds instead of flowing evenly through the entire bed. This causes inconsistent extraction where some areas are over-extracted and others are under-extracted.

## Symptoms

- 萃取时间不均匀
- 斑点状crema
- 不一致的流速
- 风味失衡
- Uneven coffee bed
- Fast draining
- Inconsistent flavor in the cup

## Solutions

- 使用 [[wdt-weiss-distribution-technique]] 改善分布均匀性
- 确保压粉均匀（30-40磅压力，58-62%压缩比）
- 检查研磨度均匀性
- 使用新鲜、干燥的咖啡粉
- 延长预浸泡时间
- 使用适合冲煮器具的研磨度
- 充分闷蒸以释放CO2
- 以圆形手法注水确保均匀浸湿
- 保持水位高于咖啡粉床

## Prevention

Consistent technique and proper equipment (burr grinder, gooseneck kettle) help prevent channeling by ensuring uniform grind size and controlled water distribution.
