# Consistency vs Alignment

Consistency and alignment can be in tension when building AI systems. Consistency — the ability to make reliable, predictable decisions across diverse situations — is a desirable property for AI. However, pursuing consistency too rigidly can conflict with alignment to human intentions, which may be diverse, contextual, and even contradictory.

From [[the-alignment-problem---brian-christian-tom-griffiths]], Chapter 4 explores how consistency pressure can undermine alignment. A system that always behaves the same way may fail to adapt to situations where human values require different responses. This tension mirrors broader questions in [[decision-making-frameworks]] about balancing reliability with contextual judgment.
