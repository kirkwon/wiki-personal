# Trust

Trust, as presented in David Allen's *Making It All Work*, is a foundational element of effective workload management and delegation. The book argues that without trust—both in others and in oneself—productivity systems become bottlenecked by micromanagement and inability to let go.

## Key Ideas

- Trust in delegation allows others to handle responsibilities effectively, freeing capacity for higher-level work.
- Trust is built through clear communication, reliable follow-through, and giving others room to execute.
- Managing workload depends on being able to rely on collaborators, family members, and team members.
- Trust also relates to trusting one's own system: if you trust your GTD capture and review process, you can engage fully in the moment without carrying unresolved tasks mentally.
- The absence of trust leads to control-oriented management that undermines both efficiency and relationships.
