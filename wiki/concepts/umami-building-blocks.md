# Umami Building Blocks

Umami building blocks are the foundational techniques and ingredients used to construct deep, savory flavor in cooking. Umami — the fifth taste — provides richness and satisfaction that rounds out a dish.

Core building blocks include:
- **Glutamate sources** — Soy sauce, Parmesan, mushrooms, tomatoes
- **Nucleotide sources** — Bonito flakes, dried fish, nutritional yeast
- **Synergy** — Combining glutamate and nucleotide sources amplifies umami beyond what either provides alone
- **Maillard reaction** — Browning proteins and sugars during cooking generates additional umami compounds

This concept is central to [[recipe-umami-building-blocks]] and relates to [[flavor-theory]] and [[seasonings]] as practical applications of flavor science.
