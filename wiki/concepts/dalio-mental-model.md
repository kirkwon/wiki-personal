# Dalio Mental Model

The Dalio Mental Model is a comprehensive framework for understanding how complex systems—be they economies, technologies, or societies—operate over time. It emphasizes identifying fundamental cause-and-effect relationships rather than focusing on superficial correlations. Key components include recognizing historical cycles (like the boom-bust cycle) and distilling these observations into universal principles that guide decision-making under uncertainty. By understanding the underlying mechanics of change, practitioners can anticipate systemic shifts and structure their thinking for resilience.
