# PARA

PARA is a personal knowledge management mental model that divides all information into four categories: Projects (current active work), Areas (ongoing responsibilities), Resources (topics of interest), and Archives (inactive items). It serves as a mental shortcut for navigating digital complexity and making sense of patterns and relationships in complex situations.

The model is positioned as a universal conceptual tool applicable across domains, with moderate confidence in its broad applicability. It draws from Tiago Forte's PARA Method framework for organizing digital life.

## Definition

PARA provides a framework for categorizing and relating information through four primary dimensions. This structure helps individuals recognize patterns and relationships in their digital and knowledge workflows.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

Tiago Forte presents PARA as a method to simplify, organize, and master digital life. The framework is treated as a mental model rather than a rigid system, emphasizing its adaptability across different contexts and domains.
