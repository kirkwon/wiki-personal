# Collective Action Models

Models of how groups cooperate or fail to cooperate. These explore the conditions under which individuals contribute to shared goods versus free-riding.

## Core Tension

- Individual rationality can lead to collective irrationality
- Public goods are underprovided when individuals can benefit without contributing
- Coordination failures prevent mutually beneficial outcomes

## Key Frameworks

- **Prisoner's dilemma**: Classic model of cooperation vs. defection
- **Public goods games**: How groups fund shared resources
- **Common pool resource problems**: Tragedy of the commons
- **Mechanism design**: Institutions that incentivize cooperation

## Connection to Existing Concepts

Relates to [[cooperation]] and [[game-theory-models]]. Collective action failures are a primary reason [[absence-of-trust]] and [[lack-of-commitment]] matter in teams.
