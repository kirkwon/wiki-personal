# Green Tea

Green tea refers to *Camellia sinensis* leaves that are processed using methods designed to minimize oxidation, thereby preserving the delicate polyphenols and catechins. Unlike black or oolong teas, which undergo full oxidation, green tea is typically heated (pan-fired or steamed) immediately after harvest to prevent enzymatic browning. This unique processing method gives it its distinct vegetal flavor profile and high concentration of antioxidants like EGCG.
