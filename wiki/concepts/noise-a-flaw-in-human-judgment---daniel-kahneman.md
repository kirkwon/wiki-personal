# Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment

*Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment* by Daniel Kahneman explores the concept of "noise" — unwanted variability in judgments — as a counterpart to the more commonly discussed "bias." The book argues that inconsistent decision-making is a pervasive problem that systematic processes can help mitigate.

## Key Ideas
- Human judgment is subject to random variation (noise) even when biases are controlled
- Systematic processes reduce noise by standardizing evaluation criteria
- This supports the Processes mental model's emphasis on structure over intuition
