# Stock and Bond Investment Templates

Templates for tracking stock and bond portfolio performance across asset classes.

## Stock Investment Tracker
- Company name, ticker, shares owned, purchase details
- Purchase price, current price, total return, annualized return
- Dividend tracking (annual dividend, yield, growth rate, reinvestment)
- Performance metrics (beta, P/E ratio, market cap, 52-week range, volume, volatility)

## Bond Investment Template
- Issuer, coupon rate, face value, purchase/current price
- Maturity date, time to maturity, YTM, duration, credit rating
- Cash flow analysis (annual interest, current yield, capital gain/loss, total return)

## Portfolio Performance Template
- Total portfolio value, cash invested, unrealized gain/loss
- Portfolio return, annualized return, beta, Sharpe ratio
- Asset allocation (target vs actual by asset class)
- Performance by asset class
