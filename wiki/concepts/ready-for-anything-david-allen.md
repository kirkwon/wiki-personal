# Ready for Anything

"Ready for Anything" by David Allen is a companion book to "Getting Things Done," offering 52 insights and practices for maintaining a GTD mindset. Each chapter presents a principle — such as trusting your system before your memory, staying in creative refocus mode, and distinguishing between "controlling" and "engaged." The book focuses less on the mechanics of GTD and more on the psychological shifts needed to sustain stress-free productivity.
