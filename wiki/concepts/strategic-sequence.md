# Strategic Sequence

The **Strategic Sequence** is a four-step validation framework that ensures a blue ocean idea is commercially viable before full commitment. Each step must be satisfied before moving to the next, preventing blue ocean ideas from becoming overambitious bets by grounding them in commercial reality. It ensures that blue ocean initiatives are not just creative ideas but robust business propositions.

1. **Exceptional buyer utility** — Does the idea offer a compelling utility across the entire buyer experience cycle? (Assessed via [[buyer-utility-map]])
2. **Price accessible to the mass** — Is the target price point accessible to a mass of buyers?
3. **Cost that enables target price** — Can the company achieve a cost structure that allows profitability at the target price?
4. **Adoption path** — Can the organization overcome adoption hurdles and reach the target buyers?

The strategic sequence connects to [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]] — the emphasis on testing utility, price, and cost before full execution mirrors the principle of separating decision process quality from outcome quality. It also relates to [[real-options-analysis]] in that it provides a staged validation approach, though Blue Ocean Strategy emphasizes sequential testing rather than flexible optionality.
