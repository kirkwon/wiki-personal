# Scatterfocus

Scatterfocus is the mind-wandering mode of attention, where the mind is unfocused and free to drift. Chris Bailey presents it as the complement to [[hyperfocus]], arguing that intentional mind-wandering is essential for creativity, insight, and mental restoration.

## Key Characteristics
- Unfocused, drifting attention
- Mental wandering without a specific goal
- Associated with creativity and insight generation
- Restorative for preventing burnout

## Benefits
- Leads to creative connections and new ideas
- Restores attention and prevents mental exhaustion
- Allows for subconscious processing of complex problems
- Provides a break from intense concentration

## When to Use
- Before or after a period of deep work
- When stuck on a problem and need fresh perspective
- During low-energy periods
- As a strategic break to maintain long-term productivity
