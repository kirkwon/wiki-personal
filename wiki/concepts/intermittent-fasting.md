# Intermittent Fasting

Intermittent fasting is a dietary strategy that cycles between periods of eating and fasting, used to optimize metabolic health, improve hormone balance, and promote fat loss. Timothy Ferriss presents it in *The 4-Hour Body* as a tool for harnessing [[metabolic-flexibility]] — the body's ability to efficiently switch between using glucose and fat as fuel sources. It contrasts with traditional calorie restriction by leveraging the body's natural fat-burning processes during fasting windows and is positioned as a more effective alternative for fat loss.

## Key Ideas from Ferriss

- Fasting periods train the body to draw energy from stored fat rather than relying solely on glucose.
- Different fasting protocols exist (e.g., 16:8, 20:4, 24-hour fasts, alternate-day fasting) and can be tailored to individual goals, physiology, and genetics.
- Fasting can improve insulin sensitivity, growth hormone levels, and autophagy.
- The approach connects to the [[body-fat-paradox]] — losing fat effectively may require hormonal optimization rather than simple caloric restriction.
