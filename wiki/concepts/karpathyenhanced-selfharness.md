# Karpathy-Enhanced Self-Harness

This refers to an advanced framework for training and evaluating large language models (LLMs), often associated with the work of Andrej Karpathy. It represents a self-contained, highly optimized environment designed to streamline the entire ML lifecycle—from data preparation and model definition to distributed training and performance benchmarking. The goal is to provide researchers with maximum control and efficiency when scaling up complex generative AI systems.
