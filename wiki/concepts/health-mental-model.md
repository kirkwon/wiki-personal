# Health as a Mental Model

Health is defined as a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Health.md]

The model is claimed to be universally applicable across domains, with moderate confidence (0.7). It is positioned as a lens for decision-making, risk assessment, and optimization—drawing on Timothy Ferriss's *The 4-Hour Body* as its primary reference.

This concept is distinct from the existing [[Healthcare]] mental model, which frames healthcare as a universal tool for navigating complexity. Health as a mental model emphasizes pattern recognition and relationship understanding in complex situations, applicable broadly beyond medical contexts.

## Key Characteristics
- Universal applicability
- Focus on pattern and relationship recognition
- Serves as a mental shortcut for complex situations
- Supported by single-source reference (Ferriss)
