# Moka Pot

A Moka Pot is an Italian stovetop espresso maker that brews coffee by forcing pressurized hot water through finely ground coffee beans. Its design has influenced both culinary aesthetics and functional simplicity in kitchenware, making it popular among enthusiasts of home-brewed coffee. The process of using a Moka Pot can also serve as an exercise in patience and precision, reflecting principles observed in cognitive science regarding task focus and sensory experience.
