# Philosophical Journaling

Philosophical journaling is the practice of self-examination through writing, as advocated by Seneca in *Letters from a Stoic*. It involves reflecting on daily conduct, emotions, relationships, and priorities to develop self-knowledge and character.

## Key Practices

- Daily self-examination of actions and motivations.
- Writing letters or reflections modeled on Seneca's epistolary style.
- Tracking alignment between stated values and actual behavior.
- Examining emotional responses through rational analysis.

## When to Stop

When self-examination becomes self-criticism or shame rather than constructive reflection. Journaling should build self-knowledge, not erode self-compassion.
