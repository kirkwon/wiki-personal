# Will to Meaning

The will to meaning is Viktor Frankl's central concept: the primary human drive is not the pursuit of pleasure (as Freud proposed) or power (as Adler argued), but the search for meaning. This drive underlies all human motivation and provides the foundation for logotherapy.

## Definition

The will to meaning is the fundamental need to find purpose, significance, and coherence in one's life. It is distinct from hedonic pleasure-seeking and is instead oriented toward self-transcendence and contribution to something beyond oneself.

## Evidence

Frankl argues this drive is demonstrated by the capacity of people to endure extreme suffering—including his own experiences in concentration camps—when they maintain a sense of meaning. Without meaning, even favorable conditions can feel empty.
