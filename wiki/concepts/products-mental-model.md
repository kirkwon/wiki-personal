# Products as a Mental Model

Products is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

This mental model is referenced in *Zero to One* by Peter Thiel and Blake Masters, and is described as having universal applicability across general domains.

The model functions similarly to other mental models in the wiki such as [[planning]], [[decisions]], and [[problem-solving]] — serving as a lens for pattern and relationship recognition in complex situations.
