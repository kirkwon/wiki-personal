# The PARA Method: Simplify Organize and Master Your Digital Life

Tiago Forte's PARA method is an organizational framework for digital information, structured around four categories: Projects, Areas, Resources, and Archives.

## PARA Framework

- **Projects** — Active efforts with a defined outcome and deadline
- **Areas** — Ongoing responsibilities that require maintenance
- **Resources** — Topics of interest for future reference
- **Archives** — Inactive items no longer needing action

This method serves as the organizational backbone of Forte's broader "Building a Second Brain" system, providing a mental model for navigating digital complexity through pattern recognition and structured categorization.
