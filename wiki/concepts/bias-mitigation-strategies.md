# Bias Mitigation Strategies

Systematic approaches to reduce the impact of cognitive biases at both individual and organizational levels.

## Individual Level

1. **Slow Down** — Take time before important decisions. Sleep on big choices.
2. **Seek Disconfirming Evidence** — Ask "What would prove me wrong?" Play devil's advocate with yourself.
3. **Use Checklists** — Standardize decision-making processes. Prevent skipping important considerations.
4. **Get Outside Perspective** — Ask trusted advisors. Consider others' viewpoints.
5. **Write It Down** — Document predictions and rationale. Compare predictions to outcomes over time.

## Organizational Level

1. **Red Teaming** — Assign a team to challenge plans and identify potential failures.
2. **Pre-Mortem Analysis** — Imagine the project failed, then ask why. Identify risks before they happen.
3. **Blind Evaluation** — Remove names and demographics from applications. Evaluate on merit only.
4. **Diverse Teams** — Different backgrounds reduce groupthink. Broader perspectives improve decisions.
5. **After-Action Reviews** — What went well? What didn't? Document lessons learned.
