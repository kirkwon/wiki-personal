# Jurisdiction Selection for Entities

The choice of state for forming a business entity significantly impacts costs, privacy, liability protection, and operational flexibility.

## Popular Formation States

### Delaware
- Popular for corporations due to flexible law and established case law
- Well-developed corporate governance framework
- Franchise taxes apply

### Wyoming
- Low costs and privacy protections
- Favorable LLC laws with strong charging order protection
- No state income tax
- Anonymous LLCs available

### Nevada
- Asset protection reputation
- No corporate income tax
- Low annual fees ($200–500)
- Anonymous LLCs available

### California
- High costs and significant annual franchise tax (minimum $800)
- Heavy regulatory burden
- Generally unfavorable for entity formation despite large market

### Texas
- No corporate income tax
- Business-friendly environment

## Key Factors
- **Privacy**: Wyoming, Nevada, and New Mexico offer anonymous LLCs
- **Costs**: Nevada and Wyoming have low annual fees; California has high franchise taxes
- **Asset Protection**: Wyoming and Nevada have favorable LLC laws
- **Federal Compliance**: Beneficial ownership reporting requirements now apply regardless of state

## Franchise Taxes
- California: Minimum $800 annually
- Nevada: $200–500 annually
- Texas: No franchise tax for certain entities
- Florida: Low annual fees for LLCs and corporations
