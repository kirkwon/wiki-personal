# Game Theory Models

Models of strategic interaction between rational (or boundedly rational) actors. Each actor's payoff depends on others' choices, creating interdependence and potential conflict or cooperation.

## Core Concepts

- **Nash equilibrium**: Outcomes where no player can improve by changing strategy alone
- **Prisoner's dilemma**: Classic model of cooperation vs. defection
- **Coordination games**: Multiple equilibria where players must coordinate
- ** signaling and commitment**: Strategic communication to alter others' behavior

## Connection to Other Frameworks

Relates to [[strategic-bluffing]] and [[collective-action-models]]. Game theory models provide a formal language for understanding [[collective-action-models]] failures and solutions.
