# Temperance (Virtue)

Temperance is the virtue of self-control, moderation, and balance — particularly in the face of difficulty, temptation, or excess. In Stoic philosophy, it is one of the cardinal virtues alongside courage, justice, and wisdom.

## Definition
The ability to maintain self-control, humility, and balance during difficult situations. It involves knowing when to act and when to restrain, and keeping one's desires and emotions in check.

## Connection to Reframing Adversity
Temperance is crucial for overcoming obstacles because it prevents overreaction, impulsivity, and the pursuit of shortcuts that undermine long-term goals. As discussed in *The Obstacle Is the Way*, temperance helps maintain the steady mindset needed to convert obstacles into progress.
