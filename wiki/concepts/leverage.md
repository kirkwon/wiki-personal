# Leverage

Leverage is a universal mental model—a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Leverage functions as a framework for recognizing how small inputs can produce outsized effects, whether in financial systems, decision-making, or information processing. It captures the idea that understanding the right points of intervention can multiply impact.

## Financial Dimension

Ray Dalio's *A Template for Understanding Big Debt Crises* provides the primary source for this model, emphasizing debt leverage, margin, and the dynamics of credit cycles. In this context, leverage refers to the use of borrowed capital to amplify returns—or amplify losses.

## Broader Applicability

Beyond finance, leverage can be understood as identifying high-leverage activities: actions that disproportionately influence outcomes. This connects to [[effort]] as a mental model and to [[ideas-mental-model]] as a conceptual tool for navigating complexity.
