# Coriander

Coriander (cilantro) is an herb with leaves used widely in cooking to enhance flavors of various cuisines; its distinctive taste appeals to many but not all due to genetic differences affecting perception. In photography, coriander seeds can be a subject for macro shots, showcasing texture and detail. From a cognitive science perspective, the divisive nature of coriander's flavor exemplifies how olfactory receptors influence individual taste experiences.
