# Mental Sandbox

A mental sandbox is a technique for simulating scenarios mentally to explore possibilities, test hypotheses, and anticipate outcomes without real-world risk. Covered in Part V of *Super Thinking*, specifically in the creativity and innovation section.

## Core Idea
Create an internal space where you can mentally play out different scenarios, test assumptions, and observe how systems might behave under various conditions. This is similar to running simulations in your mind.

## Applications
- **Strategic planning**: Mentally simulate how competitors might respond to your moves.
- **Risk assessment**: Explore worst-case scenarios and plan responses.
- **Creative problem-solving**: Imagine unconventional solutions and trace their implications.

## Connection to Other Models
The mental sandbox connects to [[thought-experiments]] (hypothetical scenario testing), [[lateral-thinking]] (creative approaches), and [[working-backwards-in-problem-solving]] (starting from desired outcomes). It functions similarly to [[thought-experiments]] as both are tools for exploring hypotheticals safely.
