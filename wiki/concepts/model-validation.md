# Model Validation

Testing and validating models against reality to ensure they provide useful insights rather than confirming biases or overfitting to noise.

## Key Practices

- Compare model predictions to observed outcomes
- Check for overfitting (models that fit historical data but fail on new data)
- Test sensitivity to assumptions
- Use simpler models as baseline checks
- Seek disconfirming evidence

## Pitfalls to Avoid

- Using models to avoid empirical testing
- Ignoring model uncertainty in decisions
- Confusing mathematical fit with practical usefulness
- Becoming a "model person" rather than solving problems
