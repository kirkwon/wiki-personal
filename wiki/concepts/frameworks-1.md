# Frameworks

A framework is an established set of guidelines or principles that provide structure to complex activities like cooking recipes, photographic composition rules, financial planning strategies, and cognitive models for understanding mental processes. In each context, frameworks help individuals systematically approach tasks, ensuring consistency and efficiency in their pursuits.
