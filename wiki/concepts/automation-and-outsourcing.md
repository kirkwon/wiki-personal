# Automation and Outsourcing

Automation and outsourcing are the final themes in Tiago Forte's PARA Method, focusing on delegating repetitive tasks and streamlining processes to save time and effort. This includes using software tools to automate routine digital workflows, as well as recognizing when manual tasks should be delegated or eliminated.

While this chapter provides general guidance on reducing friction in digital life, its concepts are relatively generic compared to the book's core PARA framework. The idea connects to broader productivity thinking about identifying high-leverage activities and minimizing low-value work.
