# Rice Varieties Index

Comprehensive guide to rice varieties organized by type, region, and culinary use.

## Quick Reference

**Category**: Staple Grain  
**Origin**: Global (Asia, Americas, Europe, Africa)  
**Types**: Long-grain, Medium-grain, Short-grain, Glutinous  
**Preparation**: Steaming, boiling, absorption, pilaf method

## Rice Varieties by Type

### Aromatic Long-Grain
- [[jasmine-rice]] - Thai fragrant rice, floral aroma
- [[basmati-rice]] - Indian/Pakistani, nutty, elongates when cooked

### Short-Grain / Sticky
- [[sushi-rice]] - Japanese short-grain for sushi and onigiri
- [[sticky-rice]] - Glutinous rice for dim sum and desserts
- [[calrose-rice]] - California medium-grain, all-purpose

### European Specialty
- [[arborio-rice]] - Italian risotto rice, creamy texture
- [[valencia-rice]] - Spanish paella rice, absorbs broth well

### Whole Grain / Colored
- [[brown-rice]] - Whole grain with bran layer, nutty flavor
- [[black-rice]] - Forbidden rice, antioxidant-rich, purple when cooked
- [[red-rice]] - Camargue or Kerala varieties, earthy flavor

## By Cuisine

| Cuisine | Primary Rice | Preparation Method |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Thai | [[jasmine-rice]] | Steamed, 1:1.5 ratio |
| Indian | [[basmati-rice]] | Parboiled or biryani method |
| Japanese | [[sushi-rice]] | Rice cooker, vinegar-seasoned |
| Italian | [[arborio-rice]] | Risotto method, gradual liquid |
| Spanish | [[valencia-rice]] | Paella method, socarrat |
| Chinese | [[sticky-rice]], [[jasmine-rice]] | Steamed or glutinous |
| Hawaiian | [[calrose-rice]] | Rice cooker |
