# Dried Herbs

Dried herbs are plant material—leaves, flowers, stems, or roots—that have been harvested and naturally dehydrated to remove moisture content. This preservation method significantly extends shelf life while retaining many volatile aromatic oils and medicinal compounds. They are widely used in culinary arts for flavoring meals (e.g., oregano, thyme) and historically utilized in traditional medicine for various purported health benefits. Proper drying involves controlled dehydration to prevent mold and maintain potency.
