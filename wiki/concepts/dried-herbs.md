# Dried Herbs

Dried herbs are preserved fresh herbs through air-drying or other methods, concentrating flavor but reducing aromatic complexity. Rosemary can be dried by hanging upside down in a dark, dry place for 1-2 weeks, then stored in an airtight container. Dried rosemary is more concentrated than fresh (1 tbsp fresh = 1 tsp dried) but has less aromatic nuance. Properly dried herbs retain excellent flavor for 3 months, good flavor for 6 months, and acceptable flavor for 1+ year, though aroma diminishes over time.

## Key Principles

- **Air drying**: Hang upside down in dark, dry place for 1-2 weeks
- **Storage**: Airtight container, away from light and moisture
- **Fresh vs dried ratio**: 1 tbsp fresh = 1 tsp dried
- **Aroma test**: Should have strong, pleasant aroma; avoid musty or dull-smelling dried herbs
