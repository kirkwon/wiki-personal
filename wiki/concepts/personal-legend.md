# Personal Legend

A Personal Legend is Sinek's term for the deep-seated sense of purpose or calling that drives an individual throughout their lives. Finding one's Personal Legend is the process of identifying the Why that gives meaning and direction to one's actions. Sinek describes it as a calling that often feels intuitive but requires deliberate reflection to articulate clearly.

## Definition

The Personal Legend is the unique Why that each person carries—a motivating force that explains why they do what they do. It is discovered through introspection and lived through consistent action.

## Characteristics
- Deep-seated and enduring, not dependent on external circumstances.
- Often described as a "calling" that persists across career changes and life stages.
- Provides meaning and direction beyond short-term goals or incentives.
- When discovered and lived, it creates lasting fulfillment and influence.

## Finding It

Sinek suggests that the Personal Legend is found by examining patterns in what has consistently motivated you, even when the specific context changed. It is not about what you want to achieve, but about why you want to achieve it.
