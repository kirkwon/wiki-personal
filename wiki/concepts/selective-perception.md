# Selective Perception

Selective perception is a cognitive bias describing the human tendency to interpret information in a way that aligns with pre-existing beliefs, expectations, or needs. Rather than taking in all environmental data equally, the brain filters stimuli, allowing certain inputs (relevant to current focus) while downplaying or ignoring contradictory or irrelevant ones. This phenomenon is central to psychology and cognitive science, demonstrating how our internal frameworks actively shape what we perceive as reality.
