# Opportunities

Opportunities is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

At its core, the Opportunities mental model serves as a lens for recognizing when conditions align to create openings for action, growth, or change. Rather than treating the world as a static set of facts, this model encourages viewing situations as dynamic systems where patterns emerge and relationships shift over time.

## Connection to Ray Dalio

The model is cross-referenced with Ray Dalio's *Principles for Dealing with the Changing World Order*, which frames opportunity recognition as central to navigating large-scale systemic transitions. Dalio describes cycles where old systems decline, creative destruction occurs, and new orders emerge — each phase presenting distinct opportunities for those who can recognize the signals.^[raw/articles/Opportunities.md]
