# Co-Evolutionary Relationships

Co-evolutionary relationships describe a shift from competitive dynamics to cooperative partnerships with suppliers, customers, and employees. Senge argues that organizations can drive innovation and long-term success by moving toward mutually beneficial collaborations rather than zero-sum competition.

This concept reflects a move from zero-sum thinking to systemic collaboration. It connects to the broader theme of learning organizations that prioritize systemic health and long-term adaptability over short-term competitive gains. It complements [[infinite-game-mindset]] by framing business relationships as ongoing adaptive partnerships rather than finite contests.

## Relevance
Connects to [[building-a-cohesive-team]] by extending collaboration beyond internal teams to external stakeholders including suppliers, customers, and the broader ecosystem.
