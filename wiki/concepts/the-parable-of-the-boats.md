# The Parable of the Boats

The Parable of the Boats is a story Simon Sinek uses in *Leaders Eat Last* to illustrate why people work harder when they feel like valued members of their team, rather than mere cogs in a machine. It demonstrates the power of belonging and the difference between serving a purpose and being treated as a tool.

## Key Message
When people feel valued and connected to their team's purpose, they go beyond minimum expectations. When they feel replaceable, they do only what is required.

## Connection to Belonging
This parable supports [[belonging-in-leadership]] by showing the concrete difference belonging makes in individual motivation and collective output.

The parable serves as a memorable metaphor for the importance of belonging, trust, and service-first leadership. It reinforces the central argument that organizations thrive when leaders prioritize people over profit, creating environments where individuals feel connected to something larger than themselves.
