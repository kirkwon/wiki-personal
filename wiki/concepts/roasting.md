# roasting

Roasting is the process of cooking food, typically meats or vegetables, at high temperatures in an oven until it reaches a desired level of caramelization and tenderness. In photography, roasting refers to exposing film to light for development; however, this term isn't directly related to the culinary definition but can be metaphorically applied to enhance image quality by reducing unwanted elements.


In personal finance, 'roasting' is not a standard term and thus does not have an established meaning within that context. However, if one were to draw parallels from cooking, it could humorously imply the act of scrutinizing someone's financial decisions critically or publicly.


In cognitive science, roasting doesn't directly apply but can be used metaphorically to describe a process where information is 'heated up' through critical thinking and analysis, leading to deeper understanding or the uncovering of underlying issues in thought patterns.
