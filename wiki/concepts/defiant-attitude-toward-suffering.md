# Defiant Attitude Toward Suffering

Frankl's observation that while individuals cannot always choose their circumstances, they can always choose their attitude toward those circumstances. This defiant stance — maintaining inner freedom and purpose even under extreme external control — was born from his experience in Nazi concentration camps, where prisoners who preserved a sense of meaning had a greater chance of survival.

## Core Insight
- Freedom of attitude is the last of the human freedoms and cannot be taken away
- Suffering loses its destructive power when it finds meaning
- The defiant attitude is not denial or false positivity but a conscious choice of purpose

## Practical Application
Meaning-focused journaling and reflective practice can help cultivate this attitude in everyday life, transforming ordinary hardship into an opportunity for growth.
