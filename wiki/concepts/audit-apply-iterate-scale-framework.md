# Audit-Apply-Iterate-Scale framework

The Audit-Apply-Iterate-Scale framework is a four-step process for improving productivity. It provides a structured approach to systematically enhancing efficiency and output.

## Steps

1. **Audit** — Identify where efficiency losses occur, track how time and attention are currently spent, and note patterns that lead to wasted effort.
2. **Apply** — Restructure approach using time management and priority setting frameworks, focusing on high-impact activities and eliminating or delegating low-value tasks.
3. **Iterate** — Measure the impact of changes, adjust based on results, and build consistency through habit formation.
4. **Scale** — Standardize effective approaches, document processes for reuse, and teach others to multiply impact.

This framework connects to [[time-management-matrix]] and [[quadrant-2-focus]] for prioritization, [[deep-work---cal-newport]] for sustained focus, and [[scatterfocus]] for managing different modes of attention.
