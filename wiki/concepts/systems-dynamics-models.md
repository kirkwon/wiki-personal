# Systems Dynamics Models

Models that capture how systems behave over time through feedback loops — reinforcing loops that amplify change and balancing loops that stabilize systems.

## Core Ideas

- **Reinforcing feedback loops**: Accelerate change (e.g., compounding growth, viral spread)
- **Balancing feedback loops**: Counteract change (e.g., market saturation, resource limits)
- **Delays**: Time lags between cause and effect create oscillation and overshoot
- **Stocks and flows**: Accumulations and rates of change

## Practical Applications

- Policy analysis and intervention design
- Understanding boom-bust cycles
- Modeling resource depletion
- Predicting system behavior under stress
