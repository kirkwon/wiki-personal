# Sports as a mental model

Sports functions as a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Sports.md]

The model draws on David Epstein's research in *The Sports Gene*, which examines how genetics shape athletic performance alongside training, environment, and deliberate practice.^[raw/articles/Sports.md] This cross-domain perspective reveals that talent and outcomes are rarely the product of a single factor — they emerge from the interaction of predisposition and context.

Sports as a mental model connects to broader themes of pattern recognition, [[biases]], and [[naturalistic-decision-making]], offering a concrete domain for understanding how complex systems produce observable results through the interplay of multiple variables.^[raw/articles/Sports.md]
