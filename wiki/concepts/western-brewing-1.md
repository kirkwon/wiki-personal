# Western Brewing

Western Brewing refers to the traditional methods of beer production originating from Western cultures, emphasizing techniques such as malt fermentation with specific yeast strains and hops for flavoring. It encompasses a diverse range of styles including lagers, ales, stouts, and IPAs, each reflecting regional ingredients and historical brewing practices. Understanding Western Brewing involves appreciating the interplay between science (e.g., fermentation kinetics) and artistry in crafting distinctive beer profiles.
