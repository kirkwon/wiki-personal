# Nudges

Nudges are a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. They provide a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world by recognizing how subtle environmental changes influence decisions.

## Definition

Nudges function as a universal mental model applicable across domains. They describe the mechanism by which choice environments—defaults, presentation formats, social cues—are designed to guide behavior without mandating or prohibiting any particular option. This is sometimes called "libertarian paternalism."

## Choice Architecture

The practical application of nudges is choice architecture—the deliberate design of decision environments. Thaler and Sunstein's *Nudge* established this as a field connecting behavioral science to policy, product design, and institutional strategy.
