# Cognitive Mental Models
Cognitive mental models focus on how people process information, perceive reality, and make decisions. They are crucial for understanding human cognition and behavior.

## Applications
- Artificial intelligence: Cognitive mental models can be used to develop more sophisticated AI systems that mimic human thought processes.
- Psychology: They help psychologists understand human behavior, cognition, and emotion, informing the development of therapies and interventions.

## Limitations
- Simplification: Cognitive mental models can oversimplify complex cognitive phenomena.
- Bias: They can reflect and perpetuate biases, influencing how people perceive and interpret information.
