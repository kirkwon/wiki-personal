# Cash on Cash Return

Measures the annual cash yield on invested capital, calculated as net cash flow divided by cash investment.

## Formula

**Cash on Cash Return = Net Cash Flow / Cash Investment**

Where cash investment includes down payment, closing costs, and initial repairs.

## Usage

Tracked in the [[single-property-analysis-template]] and [[multi-property-portfolio-template]] alongside [[cap-rate]] and [[net-operating-income]]. Helps investors understand the return on actual cash deployed versus total property value.

## Interpretation

A higher cash-on-cash return indicates better annual yield on invested capital. Unlike cap rate, it accounts for the impact of leverage (mortgage financing).
