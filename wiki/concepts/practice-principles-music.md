# Practice Principles for Music

Effective music practice relies on consistency, deliberate slow practice, and focused attention to weaknesses rather than strengths.

## Daily Practice Routine (30-60 minutes)

1. **Warm-up (5-10 min)**: Scales, arpeggios
2. **Technique (10-15 min)**: Exercises, fingerings
3. **Theory Application (10-15 min)**: Chord progressions, ear training
4. **Repertoire (15-20 min)**: Learning songs, improvising

## Key Principles

- **Consistency over intensity**: 30 minutes every day is better than 2 hours once a week
- **Slow practice**: Practice slowly enough to play without mistakes
- **Focus on weaknesses**: Spend 70% of time on what you can't do yet
- **Use a metronome**: Develop steady rhythm

## Common Mistakes

- Skipping fundamentals (scales, intervals, chords)
- Playing too fast before mastering accuracy
- Not actively listening while studying
- Only playing what you know
- Ignoring theory connections to what you hear and play
