# Find Your Why (Simon Sinek)

*Find Your Why* is Simon Sinek's 2024 book on discovering one's deep-seated purpose or calling. The central argument is that knowing your Why is the foundational driver for effective leadership, personal fulfillment, and organizational success. Without a clear Why, good intentions degenerate into marketing.

Key themes include:
- **The Golden Circle**: Starting with Why rather than What creates lasting impact.
- **Apple's journey**: Steve Jobs and Apple exemplify the inside-out approach—focusing on purpose before features.
- **The tragedy of good intentions**: Clear purpose prevents messaging from becoming hollow.
- **Personal legend**: Each person has a deep calling that drives them throughout life.
- **Three essential questions**: What do you do? How do you do it? Why do you do it?

The book also connects to [[personal-branding-and-wellbeing]] by arguing that authentic purpose distinguishes real leadership from performative success, and to [[the-three-essential-questions]] as a practical framework for self-discovery.
