# Tendency

Tendency is a universal mental model that provides a framework for pattern recognition and sense-making in complex situations. It functions as a conceptual tool—a mental shortcut for understanding patterns and relationships in the world.

Dobelli's *The Art of Thinking Clearly* frames tendency within the broader landscape of cognitive biases, suggesting that recognizing tendencies is essential for clearer thinking. This concept connects to existing mental models like [[thinking-mental-model]], [[cognitive-biases-library]], and [[brain-mental-model]].

Tendency is broadly applicable across domains and serves as a lens for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition.
