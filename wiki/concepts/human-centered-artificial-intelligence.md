# Human Centered Artificial Intelligence
Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HCAI) is an emerging discipline that focuses on creating AI systems that prioritize human needs, satisfaction, and trustworthiness. The goal of HCAI is to design AI systems that augment and enhance human capabilities, rather than replacing them.
