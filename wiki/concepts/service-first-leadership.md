
# Service-First Leadership

Service-first leadership is the principle that leaders should prioritize the well-being of their employees before customers, and that this prioritization ultimately produces better business outcomes. Simon Sinek argues in *Leaders Eat Last* that putting people first builds trust, which cascades into improved teamwork, performance, and long-term success.

This approach contrasts with profit-first thinking, where cost-cutting and efficiency optimization come at the expense of employee morale and loyalty. By caring for employees—who in turn care for customers—organizations create a virtuous cycle of trust and performance.

Service-first leadership connects to [[radical-transparency]] in that both approaches emphasize honesty and genuine concern, but service-first leadership frames trust through the lens of care rather than openness alone. It also reinforces the ideas in [[building-a-cohesive-team]] by adding a service and care dimension to team cohesion.
