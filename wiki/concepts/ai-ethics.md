# AI Ethics

AI Ethics is an interdisciplinary field examining the moral implications of artificial intelligence and machine learning systems. It addresses how to ensure that AI technologies are developed and deployed in a way that aligns with human values, promoting fairness, accountability, and transparency (FAT). Key concerns include algorithmic bias, privacy violations, autonomous weapon deployment, and systemic job displacement. The domain involves computer science, philosophy, law, and social sciences working together to establish ethical guidelines for the responsible creation of intelligent systems.
