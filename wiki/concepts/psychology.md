# Psychology

Psychology functions as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for understanding and navigating complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Psychology is a mental model that helps interpret and navigate complexity by recognizing patterns and relationships. Rather than being a single theory, it serves as an overarching lens that connects cognitive biases, decision-making frameworks, emotional dynamics, and behavioral insights into a coherent navigational tool.
