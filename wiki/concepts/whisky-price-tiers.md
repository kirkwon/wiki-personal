# Whisky Price Tiers

A practical taxonomy for categorizing whiskies by price range, helping guide purchasing decisions based on use case and experience level. Whiskies are categorized into three price tiers based on retail price and quality-to-cost ratio.

## Tier Categories

### Entry Tier ($12-30)
Best for beginners and mixing. Examples include Evan Williams 12/18 ($12-18), Buffalo Trace ($26), and Wild Turkey 101 ($25). These whiskies offer approachable flavors and good value for cocktails or casual sipping. These offer accessible entry points into bourbon and blended scotch.

### Mid Tier ($35-45)
Ideal for daily sipping. Examples include Knob Creek 9 ($41) and Woodford Reserve ($35-40). These provide a balance of quality and accessibility. Daily sippers with better complexity.

### Premium Tier ($50-80)
Best for special occasions and gifts. Examples include Angel's Envy ($50-60), Macallan 12 ($70-80), and Blanton's ($60-80). These whiskies offer the highest complexity and refinement. These represent the highest quality-to-price ratio in the collection.

The best value overall is considered Buffalo Trace and Wild Turkey 101, while Angel's Envy and Blanton's represent the premium end.
