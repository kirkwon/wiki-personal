# Digital Divide

The digital divide refers to how technological advancements have influenced lives and deepened social divides, creating unequal access to opportunities and resources.

## Key Points
- Technology has the potential to bridge gaps but often widens them instead
- Responsible innovation practices are needed to address these challenges
- Businesses must consider how their digital products and services affect inequality
- Access to technology is increasingly tied to economic opportunity and social mobility

## Relation to Wider Themes
Connects to [[responsible-capitalism]] on the need for technology to serve broader societal goals rather than purely financial ones. Relates to existing themes in [[decision-culture]] about how organizational choices shape social outcomes.
