# Thinking in Systems - Donella H. Meadows

*Thinking in Systems: A Primer* is Donella H. Meadows' foundational work on systems thinking. The book provides a framework for understanding feedback loops, interconnections, stocks and flows, and leverage points in complex systems.^[raw/articles/World.md]

## Key Concepts

- **Feedback loops** — Reinforcing and balancing loops that drive system behavior
- **Stocks and flows** — Accumulations and rates of change within systems
- **Leverage points** — Places in a system where a small shift can produce large changes in behavior
- **System archetypes** — Common patterns of system behavior (e.g., escalation, tragedy of the commons)

## Connection to Mental Models

Meadows' work contributes to the [[world-mental-model]] by offering tools for recognizing how elements within a system influence one another. It is one of the two canonical sources referenced for the [[system-mental-model]], alongside John Gall's *The Systems Bible*. Together they provide the theoretical foundation for applying systems thinking as a universal navigational tool.
