# What Do You Want?

"What Do You Want?" is the core question Timothy Ferriss poses to readers, challenging them to define success on personal terms rather than conforming to societal expectations. This chapter guides readers through clarifying life goals before attempting to optimize or outsource anything.

The concept connects to [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]]—knowing what you actually want is a prerequisite for making good decisions. It also feeds into the [[lifestyle-design-formula]] and [[lifestyle-design-protocol]] by providing the directional clarity needed for purposeful action.
