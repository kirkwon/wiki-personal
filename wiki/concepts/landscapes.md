# Landscapes

Landscapes is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Landscapes.md]

## Definition
Landscapes functions as a universal mental model for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition. Like other foundational mental models in this wiki such as [[overview]] and [[planning]], it serves as a lens for interpreting the structures and dynamics of complex systems.

## Cross-Source Perspectives
This mental model is referenced in [[the-four---scott-galloway]] by Scott Galloway, who discusses it as a way to conceptualize the broader terrain of competitive dynamics and strategic positioning among major platforms.^[raw/articles/Landscapes.md]

Landscapes shares conceptual ground with several existing mental models:
