# Diversification Strategies

The primary risk reduction technique for investment portfolios, spreading exposure across multiple dimensions to reduce vulnerability to any single adverse event.

## Geographic Diversification

Multiple cities, states, and international exposure reduce local economic, regulatory, and market cycle risks.

## Asset Class Diversification

- Real estate: different property types and sectors
- Financial assets: stocks, bonds, commodities
- Alternative investments: private equity, hedge funds

## Strategy Diversification

- **Core**: Stable, cash-flow properties
- **Value-Add**: Renovation and optimization
- **Opportunistic**: Development and ground-up construction

Diversification directly addresses [[portfolio-concentration-risk]] and is the first line of defense in the [[risk-assessment-framework]].
