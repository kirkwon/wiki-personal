# Cryptocurrency Investing

Cryptocurrency investing involves allocating capital to digital assets built on blockchain technology. Major assets include Bitcoin (digital gold/store of value), Ethereum (smart contract platform), and stablecoins pegged to USD.

## Pros

- Diversified from traditional markets
- High return potential
- Accessible without accredited investor requirement
- Decentralized structure

## Cons

- Extremely volatile
- Regulatory uncertainty
- Security risk (hacks, lost keys)
- No fundamentals (speculative)
- Tax complexity

## Allocation Guidance

Cryptocurrency should represent 0-10% of alternative investments as the speculative portion. This connects to the broader [[alternative-investments]] framework and should be considered alongside [[risk-assessment-framework]] guidelines.
