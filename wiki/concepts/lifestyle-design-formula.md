# Lifestyle Design Formula

The Lifestyle Design Formula is a structured framework presented by Timothy Ferriss for creating one's ideal lifestyle. It centers on four elements: time, money, people, and place. The formula requires self-reflection to define what success means personally, then purposeful action to align life with those definitions.

This concept is distinct from rigid goal-setting—it emphasizes flexibility, adaptability, and continuous adjustment rather than fixed long-term plans. It connects to the [[what-do-you-want]] chapter's guidance on clarifying personal goals and to the [[human-high-frequency-trader]] metaphor of making ongoing small adjustments.
