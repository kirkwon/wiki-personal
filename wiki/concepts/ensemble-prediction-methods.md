# Ensemble prediction methods

Combining multiple forecasts or models to produce a better prediction than any single source. Silver argues that aggregation of diverse predictions often outperforms individual experts.

## Key Ideas

- Group prediction outperforms individual expert judgment
- Diverse models capture different aspects of uncertainty
- Simple aggregated models often beat complex individual models
- Ensemble methods filter out idiosyncratic errors
- Related to [[prediction-aggregation]] and collective intelligence

## Applications

Used in weather forecasting, political polling, and financial modeling. The meta-lesson is that diversity of perspectives improves prediction accuracy.

## Connection to Existing Concepts

This extends [[Superforecasting - Philip E Tetlock  Dan Gardner]] and [[bayesian-thinking]] by emphasizing model diversity as a source of robustness.
