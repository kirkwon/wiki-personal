# Cold-Water Surfing

Cold-water surfing refers to surfing in ocean conditions where water temperatures are low enough to cause hypothermia without proper thermal protection. Ocean Beach is a prime example, with water temperatures consistently between 50-55°F.

## Requirements

- **Wetsuit**: 4/3mm minimum; 5/4mm in winter
- **Booties**: Recommended to protect feet from rocks and cold
- **Hood and gloves**: Essential for winter sessions
- **Towel and change of clothes**: For after surf

## Hazards

Cold water increases the risk of hypothermia, reduces dexterity, and can cause cold-water shock. Surfers must be aware of [[sneaker-waves]] and [[rip-currents]] which are amplified by the powerful conditions at Ocean Beach.

## Local Context

Ocean Beach's cold-water surfing community is dedicated and experienced, with local surf schools and shops like Sunset Surf Shop providing gear and lessons.^[ocean-beach.md]
