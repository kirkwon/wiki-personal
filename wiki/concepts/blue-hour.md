# Blue Hour

Blue hour is the period when the sun is just below the horizon and the sky is deep blue. It complements [[golden-hour]] as an alternative twilight shooting window with distinctly cooler color temperatures, extending shooting opportunities after sunset and before sunrise as the warm golden tones transition to cool blue light and city lights illuminate.

## Characteristics

- **Cool, blue light**: Color temperature is blue
- **Soft, diffused**: No direct sun
- **City lights coming on**: Beautiful blue sky with warm city lights
- **Longer than golden hour**: Can last 30-60 minutes

## Best Uses

Blue hour is ideal for cityscapes with blue sky and warm lights, twilight scenes, urban night photography, and automotive light trails.

## Timing

- 30 minutes after sunset
- 30 minutes before sunrise

## Connection to Golden Hour

Blue hour extends the shooting window after golden hour ends, transitioning from the warm golden tones to cool blue light as city lights illuminate.
