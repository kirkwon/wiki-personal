# Energy and Momentum Strategy

The concept from Chapter 5 of *The Art of War* that military forces should be like flowing water — seeking low ground, exploiting weakness, and maintaining momentum. Energy management involves directing force toward vulnerabilities.

## Key Ideas
- Forces should flow like water to exploit weakness
- Momentum and timing are critical to success
- Energy should be concentrated at decisive points
- Adaptability in movement prevents being forced into disadvantage

- Relates to [[terrain-analysis-strategy]] — water flows according to terrain

## Application
Applicable to project management, competitive execution, and any domain where directing effort toward vulnerabilities determines outcomes.
