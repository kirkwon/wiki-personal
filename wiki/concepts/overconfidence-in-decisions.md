# Overconfidence in Decision-Making

Overconfidence in decision-making describes how excessive confidence in one's own abilities leads to poor decision-making, with real-world examples from finance, healthcare, and other industries illustrating these pitfalls.

## Core Idea

Ariely highlights how overconfidence in one's abilities can lead to poor decision-making, with real-world examples from various industries illustrating these pitfalls.^[raw/articles/the-upside-of-irrationality---dan-ariely.md]

## Key Manifestations

- Overestimating predictive accuracy
- Underestimating risks and uncertainties
- Insufficient preparation due to false confidence
- Resistance to feedback that contradicts self-assessment
