# Red Ocean

A **red ocean** is an existing market space where industries compete head-to-head, the boundaries of the market are defined and accepted, and the competitive rules are known. Companies try to outperform rivals to grab a greater share of existing demand, leading to increasingly bloody competition (hence "red") and shrinking profits.

The strategy canvas reveals red ocean dynamics by showing how competitors' value curves converge on the same factors. Blue Ocean Strategy argues that most companies are trapped in red oceans and that the path to growth lies in creating blue oceans where competition is irrelevant.

This concept connects to [[strategy-canvas]] — red ocean convergence is visible when all competitors invest in the same factors — and contrasts with [[value-innovation]], which seeks to break free from competitive logic entirely.
