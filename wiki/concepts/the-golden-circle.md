# The Golden Circle

The Golden Circle is Simon Sinek's framework that structures how organizations and individuals communicate their value. It consists of three concentric layers:

- **Why** – The purpose, cause, or belief that drives action. This is the deepest and most important layer.
- **How** – The process or values that bring the Why to life.
- **What** – The products, services, or results that are the outward expression.

Sinek argues that most organizations communicate from the outside in (What → How → Why), but the most inspiring leaders and companies start from the inside out (Why → How → What). Apple under Steve Jobs is the primary example: they focused on their purpose (challenging the status quo, thinking differently) rather than listing features.

The Golden Circle connects to [[the-three-essential-questions]] as a practical application of exploring What, How, and Why in personal and professional life. It also relates to [[personal-branding-and-wellbeing]]—authentic purpose distinguishes genuine leadership from marketing. However, Sinek's reliance on intuition and gut feeling for finding one's Why stands in tension with [[thinking-fast-and-slow---daniel-kahneman]]'s warnings about the limitations of intuitive (System 1) reasoning.
