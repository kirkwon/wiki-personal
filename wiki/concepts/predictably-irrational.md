# Predictably Irrational

*Predictably Irrational* by Dan Ariely explores the systematic and predictable ways people deviate from rational decision-making. The book examines how social norms and irrational behaviors shape decision-making, providing evidence that human choices are often predictably influenced by social conventions, contextual factors, and cognitive biases.

## Topics Covered

- Pricing psychology
- The endowment effect
- Social norms influencing behavior
