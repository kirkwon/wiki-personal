# F8 and Be There

"F8 and be there" is a famous philosophy in photography, attributed to [[weegee]], emphasizing that the most important factor in capturing great images is presence and readiness — not technical mastery. The "f/8" refers to using an aperture wide enough for sufficient depth of field, while "be there" emphasizes the need to be out on the street, camera in hand, ready to shoot.

## Meaning

- **f/8**: Aperture wide enough for sufficient depth of field
- **Be there**: Be out on the street, camera in hand, ready to shoot

## Philosophy

The core idea is that technical skills matter less than being present and ready to capture moments. A photographer who is in the right place at the right time, with their camera ready, will capture better images than one who obsesses over settings. The decisive moment cannot be planned or engineered — it must be anticipated through observation and captured through presence. This philosophy aligns with [[decisive-moment]] and prioritizes the photographer's awareness over equipment or settings.

## Connection to Street Photography

This philosophy is foundational to [[street-photography]]. It encourages photographers to focus on being observant and ready rather than obsessing over technical details, reinforcing the ethical principle of respectful presence in public spaces.
