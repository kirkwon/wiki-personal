# Urban Hiking and Stairways

Urban hiking refers to the practice of exploring cities through stairways, hilltop trails, and elevated pathways — a distinctive feature of hilly cities like San Francisco.

## San Francisco Context

San Francisco's steep topography makes stairways both a practical necessity and a recreational asset. Neighborhoods like Golden Gate Heights and the Sunset District feature iconic stairways that serve as informal hiking trails.

## Key Features

- **Steep streets**: Some streets are too steep for cars, creating pedestrian-only paths
- **Stairways**: Community-maintained stairways connecting hillside neighborhoods
- **Hilltop parks**: Elevated parks offering panoramic views
- **Trail networks**: Connections between parks, beaches, and neighborhoods

## Benefits

- Built-in fitness from daily stair climbing
- Unique neighborhood perspectives and views
- Community art projects (e.g., tiled steps)
- Access to elevated viewpoints without driving

## Notable Stairways in SF

- [[16th-avenue-tiled-steps]] - 163 colorful tile mosaic steps
- Grandview Park trail - Steep summit hike
- Moraga St Steps - Residential access stairway
- 14th Ave Steps - Grandview Park access
- 15th Ave Steps - Hillside shortcut

## Practical Considerations

- Wear comfortable shoes
- Fog frequently obscures views
- Fall (September-October) offers clearest conditions
- Biking is challenging on steep terrain
