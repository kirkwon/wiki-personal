# Americano

An Americano is a coffee beverage made by diluting espresso with hot water, typically in a ratio of one part espresso to two parts water. In photography, 'Americano' can metaphorically describe an image that has been heavily edited or manipulated, similar to the way espresso is altered in taste and appearance. In cognitive science, it may refer to a simplified model or concept derived from more complex theories, analogous to how Americano coffee simplifies the robust flavor of traditional espresso.
