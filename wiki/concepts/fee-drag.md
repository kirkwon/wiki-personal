# Fee Drag

The compounding cost of investment fees over time. Small differences in expense ratios between index funds and actively managed funds can erode a significant portion of final portfolio value.

## The Math

- **0.04% expense ratio**: keep 99.96% of returns
- **1% expense ratio**: keep 99% of returns
- **Over 30 years**: a 1% fee costs approximately 25%+ of your final balance

## Implications

This is why the Index Card Rules emphasize buying the lowest-cost index funds available (VTSAX at 0.04%, FXAIX at 0.015%) rather than attempting to pick winning stocks or funds. The fee difference is guaranteed; outperformance is not.
