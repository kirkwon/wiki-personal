# Leaders Eat Last - Simon Sinek

*Leaders Eat Last* by Simon Sinek explores how leaders build cultures of trust and cooperation within organizations. The book argues that the most effective leaders prioritize the well-being of their people, creating environments where trust and collaboration thrive.^[raw/articles/Organization.md]

## Core Ideas

- Leaders who sacrifice personal gain for the group create stronger organizational cultures
- Trust is the foundation of effective teams and organizations
- Chemical mechanisms (like oxytocin) underpin cooperative behavior
- The environment and leadership style shape organizational outcomes

## Connection to Organization as a Mental Model

Sinek's emphasis on how leadership and culture shape organizational patterns and relationships is cited as a cross-source perspective supporting the [[organization-mental-model]].^[raw/articles/Organization.md]
