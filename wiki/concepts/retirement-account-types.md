# Retirement Account Types

Overview of tax-advantaged retirement accounts and when to use each.

## 401(k)/403(b)
- Employer-sponsored retirement plan
- 2024 limits: $23,000 under 50, $30,500 age 50+
- Pre-tax or Roth option
- Employer match = 100% return (never leave it on the table)
- Tax-deferred growth

## IRA (Individual Retirement Account)
- 2024 limits: $7,000 under 50, $8,000 age 50+
- **Traditional IRA**: Pre-tax contributions, tax-deferred growth, taxed in retirement
- **Roth IRA**: After-tax contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals
- Roth is better if: younger, expect higher income later, expect higher tax rates
- Traditional is better if: higher income now, expect lower income later

## HSA (Health Savings Account)
- 2024 limits: $4,150 individual, $8,300 family, +$1,000 age 55+
- **Triple tax advantage**: Pre-tax contribution, tax-free growth, tax-free medical withdrawals
- After 65, can withdraw for any reason (like a 401k)
- One of the most tax-efficient accounts available

## Taxable Accounts
- Use after maxing tax-advantaged accounts
- Provide flexibility and early access
- Good for alternative investments (real estate, private equity)
- Long-term capital gains (hold >1 year): 0%, 15%, 20%
- Short-term capital gains: Ordinary income rates
- Qualified dividends: 0%, 15%, 20%
