# Getting Wealthy vs. Staying Wealthy

These are two distinct skill sets that require different mindsets and behaviors. Financial success requires both, but at different times.

## Getting Wealthy

Requires taking risks, being optimistic, leveraging opportunities, and maintaining high risk tolerance. This is the wealth-accumulation phase where aggressive growth strategies can pay off.

## Staying Wealthy

Requires managing risk, being pessimistic, preserving capital, and maintaining paranoia about losing what you have. This is the wealth-preservation phase.

## Key Insight

"Money is most often made by concentrating, but kept by diversifying."^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

Good financial decisions require both mindsets at different times. The seduction of optimism can lead investors to take excessive risks after accumulating wealth, undoing years of careful compounding.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## Connection to Margin of Safety

Building in a margin of safety — leaving room for error in plans and assuming things will go wrong — is essential for staying wealthy.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md] This connects to the investment concept of margin of safety, which advocates for conservative assumptions and buffers against adverse outcomes.
