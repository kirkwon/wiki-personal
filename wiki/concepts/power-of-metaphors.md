# Power of Metaphors

Metaphors function as cognitive tools that help us comprehend complex ideas by relating them to familiar experiences. They shape thinking patterns by providing structural mappings between known and unknown domains.

## Key Points
- Metaphors are not merely literary devices but fundamental cognitive tools for abstraction
- They allow mapping of complex, unfamiliar problems onto familiar structures
- The choice of metaphor influences which aspects of a problem are highlighted and which are obscured
- This connects to [[analogy-in-problem-solving]] and [[generalization-in-problem-solving]], where drawing parallels to known problems aids solution-finding
- Working backwards from a goal (connected to [[working-backwards-in-problem-solving]]) can be seen as a metaphorical inversion strategy

## Why It Matters
Being conscious of the metaphors we use—and considering alternative metaphors—can reveal blind spots in our thinking. A problem framed as a "battle" invites different solutions than one framed as a "dance" or a "puzzle."
