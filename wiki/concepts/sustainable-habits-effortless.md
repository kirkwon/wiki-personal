# Sustainable Habits for Effortless Living

McKeown highlights how adopting sustainable habits, such as routines and daily rituals, supports an effortless approach to living by minimizing decision fatigue and reducing stress.

## Key Ideas
- Routines and rituals reduce the cognitive load of daily decisions
- Sustainable habits are small, repeatable, and aligned with priorities
- The 2-minute rule can help establish new habits
- Regular review and reflection prevent habit drift

This connects to [[habit-testing]]—testing whether habits actually serve priorities—and [[active-revision-techniques]]—regularly reviewing and refining routines. It also relates to [[mindfulness-presence-productivity]] because presence helps maintain awareness of whether habits are serving or hindering goals.
