# llm.c

(llm.c is a minimalist implementation of transformer models written entirely in C. Created by Andrej Karpathy, it provides a highly educational and dependency-light demonstration of how large language models (LLMs) function at the core computational level. By avoiding high-level frameworks like PyTorch or TensorFlow, it allows users to understand the underlying matrix multiplications and architecture necessary for text generation. It is often used in academic settings to demonstrate foundational AI concepts.)
