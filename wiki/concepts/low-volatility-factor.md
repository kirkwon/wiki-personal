# Low Volatility Factor

The low volatility factor is based on the premise that stocks with lower price volatility provide better risk-adjusted returns than high-volatility stocks.

## Metrics

- **Standard deviation** of daily/weekly/monthly returns
- **Beta** to market (lower = less volatile)
- **Maximum drawdown** – Largest peak-to-trough decline

## Implementation

1. Screen for the lowest volatility decile of stocks
2. Equal-weight or market-cap weight
3. Rebalance quarterly

## Risks

- Can underperform during strong bull markets when risk-taking is rewarded
- May concentrate in defensive sectors (utilities, consumer staples)

## Connection to Factor Investing

Low volatility is one of five primary factors in [[factor-investing]]. VFH ETF implements this factor at 0.13% expense ratio. Low volatility tends to outperform in bear markets.
