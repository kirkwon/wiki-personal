# Carrying Capacity

Carrying capacity is the maximum sustainable load a system can support given its resources and constraints. Meadows examines how resource constraints and carrying capacity affect system stability and sustainability, connecting this to her earlier work on *The Limits to Growth*.

When a system exceeds its carrying capacity, feedback loops that previously stabilized it can reverse — leading to decline, chaos, or collapse. Understanding carrying capacity is essential for assessing long-term system health and designing interventions that respect ecological and social limits.

This concept relates to [[scenario-planning]] by providing a structural constraint within which plausible futures must be evaluated.
