# Composition Basics

Composition basics are the fundamental principles for arranging elements within a frame to create compelling photographs.

## Key Principles

- **Rule of Thirds**: Divide frame into 3x3 grid, place subject at intersections
- **Leading Lines**: Use lines to guide viewer's eye
- **Framing**: Use elements to frame your subject
- **Symmetry and Patterns**: Create balance and interest
- **Backgrounds**: Keep backgrounds simple and uncluttered

These fundamentals connect to the broader [[composition-techniques]] page and are essential across all genres including [[street-photography]] and [[golden-hour]] photography.
