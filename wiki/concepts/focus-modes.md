# Focus Modes

Focus modes determine how the camera acquires and maintains focus on a subject. Selection is closely tied to the subject movement and the [[aperture]] chosen, since depth of field affects how critical focus accuracy becomes.

## Autofocus (AF)

- **Single Shot (AF-S)**: One-time focus, best for stationary subjects like portraits
- **Continuous (AF-C)**: Continually adjusts focus, best for moving subjects
- **Automatic (AF-A)**: Camera chooses between single and continuous

## Manual Focus (MF)

Manual focus gives you direct control over focus distance. It is used for macro photography, low light situations, and creative effects. Requires practice and patience.

## Genre Recommendations

- **Portraits**: AF-S (single shot)
- **Landscape**: Manual or AF-S
- **Street Photography**: Zone focus or AF-C (continuous)
- **Low Light/Night**: Manual focus

See [[street-photography]] for how focus modes apply to genre-specific shooting.
