# Participatory Design (PD)

Participatory Design is an approach to design and development that involves end-users or stakeholders directly in the entire process. Rather than simply testing a finished product, PD treats users as co-designers, ensuring that the resulting system or artifact meets genuine community needs. This methodology shifts power dynamics by valuing local knowledge alongside technical expertise, leading to more contextually relevant and sustainable solutions.
