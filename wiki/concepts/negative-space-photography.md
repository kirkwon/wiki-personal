# Negative Space in Photography

Negative space is the empty space around your main subject. It is a powerful composition tool for emphasizing subjects, creating breathing room, and adding impact.

## Why Use It
- Emphasizes the subject: The subject stands out against empty space
- Creates breathing room: Uncluttered, peaceful compositions
- Adds impact: Minimalist, powerful images
- Directional: Negative space can suggest movement

## How to Use
1. Find minimalist scenes (sky, water, walls)
2. Place the subject in one part of the frame
3. Leave the rest empty: large areas of negative space
4. Balance the subject against negative space

## Examples
- Person in vast sky: Subject small, sky dominates
- Small boat in ocean: Boat small, water dominates
- Solitary tree in field: Tree as subject, empty field
