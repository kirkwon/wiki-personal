# Events

Events as a mental model refers to the recognition that unexpected occurrences—particularly extreme ones—play a dominant role in shaping outcomes across history, finance, and personal life. This model, central to Taleb's framework in *The Black Swan*, emphasizes that rare, high-impact events often matter far more than the routine and predictable.

## Key Idea
Most people and models focus on the typical and the average, but the most consequential moments in any domain are frequently those that could not have been anticipated. Events, in this sense, are not just occurrences but forces that rewrite the rules of the system.

## Application
- In [[history]], events reshape civilizations and power structures in ways that pre-event analysis cannot predict.
- In [[finance]], market crashes and crises dwarf the cumulative effect of normal trading days.
- In [[life]], personal black swan moments—illnesses, accidents, encounters—often define trajectories more than daily routines.
