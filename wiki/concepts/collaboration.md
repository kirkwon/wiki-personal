# Collaboration

Collaboration refers to the process where two or more individuals work together toward a shared goal or objective. It involves combining diverse skills, knowledge, and efforts within a structured environment to produce outcomes that would be difficult to achieve independently. Effective collaboration requires open communication, mutual respect, and defined roles among participants. In academic and professional domains, it is considered critical for complex problem-solving and innovation.
