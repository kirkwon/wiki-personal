# Expert performance vs simple models

The finding that simple statistical models often outperform expert judgment across domains including politics, baseball, and finance. This challenges the assumption that expertise translates to better predictions.

## Key Ideas

- Political pundits underperform simple polling averages
- Baseball scouts underperform sabermetric models
- Expert intuition is subject to biases like overconfidence and confirmation bias
- Simple aggregated models filter out individual errors
- Connects to [[Superforecasting - Philip E Tetlock  Dan Gardner]] and [[thinking-fast-and-slow---daniel-kahneman]]

## Silver's Argument

Silver argues for a hybrid approach (Chapter 9) that leverages both human intuition and computer models, rather than abandoning expertise entirely.
