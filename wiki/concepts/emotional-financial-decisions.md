# Emotional Financial Decisions

Warning signs of poor financial psychology include emotional decision-making driven by fear, greed, or social comparison.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## Types

- **Fear-based selling**: Panic selling during downturns.
- **Greed-based buying**: FOMO-driven purchases of overvalued assets.
- **Revenge trading**: Making impulsive trades to recover losses.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## Prevention

- Automate everything to remove willpower from the equation.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]
- Focus on systems rather than goals.
- Practice tail event thinking.
- Surround yourself with better influences.
