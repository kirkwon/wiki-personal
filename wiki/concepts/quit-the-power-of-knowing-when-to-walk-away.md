# Quit — The Power of Knowing When to Walk Away

Annie Duke's *Quit* is a decision-making framework centered on the strategic value of walking away from losing endeavors. Rather than defaulting to perseverance, Duke argues that recognizing when to quit is one of the most important choices a person can make.

## Core Ideas

- **Quitting is a choice**: Treating quitting as a deliberate decision rather than failure reframes it as a form of strategy.
- **Sunk cost awareness**: Past investment should not dictate future action; rational evaluation of current prospects matters more.
- **Decision quality vs. outcome quality**: A well-reasoned decision to quit can be superior to continuing a doomed course.
- **Bet framing**: Consistent with [[thinking-in-bets---annie-duke]], quitting is evaluated as a bet on future outcomes rather than an emotional reaction.
