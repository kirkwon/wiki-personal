# Humility

Humility is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Humility functions as a universal mental model applicable across all domains. It serves as a mental shortcut for making sense of complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. Where ego-driven thinking leads to overconfidence and poor decisions, humility provides a counterbalance by encouraging openness to being wrong and willingness to revise one's understanding.
