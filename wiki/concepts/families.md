# Families

Families is a mental model or conceptual tool that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Families is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Origin

This mental model is referenced from Atul Gawande's *Complications: A Surgeon's Notes on an Imperfect Science*, where Gawande discusses the idea of clustering cases or situations into "families" of similar problems to better understand patterns in surgical practice and decision-making under uncertainty.

- Particularly useful for recognizing recurring patterns in complex, messy situations
- Connects to broader mental model frameworks for pattern and relationship recognition
