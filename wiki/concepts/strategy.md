# Strategy

The overarching mental model of deliberate, long-term planning to achieve objectives through superior positioning, timing, and adaptation rather than brute force. From *The Art of War*, strategy encompasses planning, terrain analysis, intelligence, deception, and the management of energy and momentum.

## Key Ideas
- **Strategy over tactics**: Tactics are specific actions; strategy is the comprehensive plan that guides all actions.
- **Victory through planning**: The side that plans more carefully and assesses the situation more thoroughly wins before fighting begins.
- **Adaptability as core**: Strategy must flex with changing circumstances—rigid plans fail against adaptive opponents.
- **Economy of force**: Achieve objectives with minimal expenditure of resources.

## Tension
The competitive framing of *The Art of War* contrasts with cooperative frameworks in the wiki. The source acknowledges this tension in its "when to stop" guidance, warning against treating every situation as zero-sum.
