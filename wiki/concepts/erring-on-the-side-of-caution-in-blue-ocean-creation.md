
# Erring on the Side of Caution in Blue Ocean Creation

Blue Ocean Strategy's emphasis on creating new markets raises important cautions. The book itself warns against overestimating ability to create new markets, abandoning profitable segments prematurely, and confusing differentiation with gimmick. These cautions connect to existing concerns in the wiki:

- [[probability-and-judgment-under-uncertainty]] cautions against overconfidence in market creation predictions.
- [[overconfidence-and-certainty]] warns that bold strategic claims can become overconfident.
- [[real-options-analysis]] suggests treating market-creation bets as flexible options rather than irreversible commitments.
- The [[strategic-sequence]] provides a staged validation that mitigates some of this risk by testing buyer utility, price, cost, and adoption before full commitment.

The tension between blue ocean ambition and caution about uncertainty is a productive one — it pushes strategy to be both bold and disciplined.
