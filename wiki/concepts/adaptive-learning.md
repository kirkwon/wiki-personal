# Adaptive Learning

Adaptive learning refers to the process of developing digital competence by adjusting learning strategies to match personal styles, needs, and contexts. Tiago Forte devotes a chapter to this concept in *The PARA Method*, emphasizing that effective digital life management requires ongoing learning and adaptation rather than rigid adherence to a single system.

This connects to [[active-revision-techniques]], which focus on methods for reinforcing understanding through active engagement with material. It also relates to Sönke Ahrens' work in [[experiment-and-exploration-forms-of-world-disclosure---snke-ahrens]], which explores how different forms of engagement with ideas lead to deeper learning.

Adaptive learning in the PARA context means continuously refining one's organizational system, tool choices, and information habits based on what is working and what is not.
