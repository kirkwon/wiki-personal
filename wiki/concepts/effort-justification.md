# Effort Justification

The tendency to rationalize effort by increasing our valuation of the outcomes. The harder something is to achieve, the more we value it — even if the objective value hasn't changed.

## Key Points
- Connects to [[sunk-cost-fallacy]] — continuing due to past investment
- Connects to [[motivation-crowding]] — external rewards undermining intrinsic motivation
- Connects to [[twaddle-tendency]] — preferring complex explanations
- Explains why hazing creates group loyalty

## Guardrail
Evaluate outcomes independently of the effort required to achieve them. Ask: "Would I value this outcome the same if it were easy to obtain?"
