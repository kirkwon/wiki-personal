# Physical Space and Culture

Physical space shapes culture by influencing how people interact, collaborate, and relate to one another. Simon Sinek argues in *Leaders Eat Last* that designing environments that promote collaboration—open layouts, shared spaces, informal gathering areas—positively impacts team culture and trust.

This concept is one of the three elements of leader-led organizations, alongside social structure and shared purpose. While often overlooked, the physical environment sends signals about what an organization values: whether it prioritizes hierarchy and separation or openness and connection.

The design of physical space connects to the broader question of how [[the-three-elements-of-leader-led-organizations]] work together to create thriving organizations.
