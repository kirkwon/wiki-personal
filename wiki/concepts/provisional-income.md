# Provisional Income

Provisional income is the formula used to determine the level at which Social Security benefits become taxable. It is a critical concept in retirement tax planning because crossing its thresholds can trigger a "Social Security tax torpedo."

## The Formula

```
Provisional Income = Adjusted Gross Income + Tax-Free Interest + 50% of Social Security Benefits
```

## Taxation Thresholds

When provisional income crosses thresholds, Social Security benefits become partially taxable:

- **50% torpedo**: Up to 50% of benefits taxable if provisional income exceeds $25,000 (single) / $32,000 (joint).
- **85% torpedo**: Up to 85% of benefits taxable if provisional income exceeds $34,000 (single) / $44,000 (joint).

This creates effective tax rates up to 46.25% on the dollars above the threshold.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## Why It Matters

Provisional income captures the interaction between multiple income sources. A Roth conversion increases AGI, which directly increases provisional income and can push Social Security benefits into taxation. Similarly, Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs) from traditional retirement accounts add to AGI and can trigger the same effect.

## Example from Tax Torpedo Analysis

A couple with $20,000 investment income and $40,000 Social Security:
- No Roth conversion: Provisional income = $20,000 + $0 + $20,000 = $40,000 → no Social Security tax.
- $200,000 Roth conversion: Provisional income = $220,000 + $0 + $20,000 = $240,000 → 85% of $16,000 above threshold becomes taxable, adding $13,600 in tax.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## Strategic Considerations

- Roth conversions should be evaluated against provisional income thresholds, not just tax bracket thresholds.
- Tax-free interest (e.g., municipal bonds) adds to provisional income but is itself tax-free, creating a nuanced planning challenge.
- The 50% and 85% cliffs mean that even small increases in income near thresholds can have outsized tax consequences.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]
- Keep Roth conversions below provisional income thresholds.
- Time capital gains and investment income strategically.
- Use tax-advantaged investments to reduce AGI.
- Manage RMDs to avoid crossing thresholds.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]
