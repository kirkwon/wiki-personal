# Japanese Tea Ceremony

The Japanese Tea Ceremony, also known as Chanoyu or Sado, is a traditional ritual influenced by Zen Buddhism emphasizing harmony, respect, purity, and tranquility. It involves the ceremonial preparation and presentation of matcha (powdered green tea) in a choreographed manner that reflects the host's hospitality and mindfulness to guests.
