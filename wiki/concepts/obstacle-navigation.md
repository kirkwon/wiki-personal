# Obstacle Navigation

The practice of turning obstacles into opportunities for growth through focus, emotional control, and disciplined mindset. Central concept of [[the-obstacle-is-the-way]].

## Key Principles
- Obstacles are raw material, not impediments
- Focus on what is within your control
- Control emotions to maintain clarity
- Embrace failure as learning
- Maintain perspective on time and priorities
