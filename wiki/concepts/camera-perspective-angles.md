# Camera Perspective Angles

Changing your camera position creates different emotional effects and visual perspectives. Each angle offers a unique way to portray a subject.

## Positions and Effects

| Position | Effect | Example |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Eye level | Natural, documentary | Street photography |
| Low angle | Powerful, grand | Architecture, portraits |
| High angle | Diminished, overview | Landscapes, crowds |
| Bird's eye | Abstract, graphic | Cityscapes, patterns |
| Worm's eye | Dramatic, towering | Buildings, trees |

Changing perspective is one of the simplest yet most effective composition tools. Even small shifts in camera height or angle can dramatically alter the mood and narrative of an image.
