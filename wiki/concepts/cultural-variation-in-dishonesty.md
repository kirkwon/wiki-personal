# Cultural Variation in Dishonesty

How different societies view lying and the influence of cultural norms on honest behavior. Dishonesty is not uniform across cultures — what counts as acceptable varies significantly based on social norms, institutional structures, and historical context.

## Key Insights
- Different cultures normalize different degrees of dishonesty
- Attitudes towards dishonesty are shaped by societal values and expectations
- Cross-cultural studies reveal both universal patterns and significant variation
- Cultural norms can either enable or constrain the "fudge factor"
