# Environment

Environment is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Environment.md]

The model draws from two key perspectives:

## Cal Newport — Deep Work
Cal Newport emphasizes how environmental design shapes the capacity for deep, focused work. By deliberately constructing one's surroundings to minimize distractions and support sustained concentration, individuals can achieve higher-quality output. This perspective frames environment as something actively engineered rather than passively experienced.^[raw/articles/Environment.md]

## David Epstein — The Sports Gene
David Epstein's research highlights how genetic and environmental factors interact in athletic performance. Environment shapes outcomes through training conditions, competitive contexts, and developmental opportunities, illustrating how surroundings influence capability and results across domains.^[raw/articles/Environment.md]

## Applicability
The Environment mental model is universal in scope, applicable to knowledge work, athletics, personal habits, and any domain where context and surroundings influence performance. It connects to related mental models such as [[planning]], [[settings]], and [[Solutions]], providing a lens for recognizing how external conditions constrain or enable outcomes.
