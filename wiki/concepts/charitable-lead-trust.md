# Charitable Lead Trust (CLT)

An irrevocable trust that pays income to charitable beneficiaries for a period, then distributes remaining assets to non-charitable beneficiaries.

## Types

- **Charitable Lead Annuity Trust (CLAT)**: Fixed annuity payments to charity
- **Charitable Lead Unitrust (CLUT)**: Percentage of trust value to charity
- **Zeroed-Out CLAT**: No assets passing to non-charitable beneficiaries

## Tax Benefits

- Gift tax deduction for charitable lead interest
- Estate tax exclusion by removing assets from estate
- Potential generation-skipping benefits
- Income tax benefits for charitable deduction

## Advantages

- Transfer wealth to heirs at significant discounts
- Support charitable organizations
- Remove assets from taxable estate
- Small charitable payment enables large transfers to heirs
