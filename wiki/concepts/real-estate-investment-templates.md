# Real Estate Investment Templates

Core set of templates for real estate investment analysis covering single property evaluation, multi-property portfolios, and acquisition due diligence.

## Templates

- [[single-property-analysis-template]] — Cash flow, NOI, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, depreciation, tax analysis
- [[multi-property-portfolio-template]] — Aggregated portfolio metrics, individual property performance comparison
- [[investment-property-acquisition-template]] — Property evaluation, financial projections, sensitivity analysis, risk assessment

## Key Metrics Tracked

- Net Operating Income (NOI)
- Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate)
- Cash on Cash Return
- Break-Even Ratio
- Debt Coverage Ratio
- Depreciation Schedule (27.5-year)
- Sensitivity Analysis / What-If Scenarios
