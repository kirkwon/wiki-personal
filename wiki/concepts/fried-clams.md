# Fried Clams

Fried clams are a seafood shack classic in New England, made by dredging clam bellies and strips in seasoned cornmeal or flour and frying until golden.

## Preparation

1. Clean clams
2. Dredge in seasoned cornmeal or flour (salt, pepper, paprika)
3. Fry at 350°F (175°C) for 2-3 minutes until golden
4. Drain on paper towels
5. Serve with tartar sauce

## Tips

- Serve immediately for best texture
- Common accompaniments: tartar sauce, lemon wedges
