# Philosophy of Mind

The Philosophy of Mind is a branch of philosophy that studies the nature of consciousness and the mind's relationship to the physical body. It explores fundamental problems such as qualia (subjective experience), free will, and identity. Major debates include dualism (mind and body are distinct) versus various forms of physicalism, which posits that mental states are ultimately reducible to physical processes in the brain. Key domains involve cognitive science, artificial intelligence, and functionalism.
