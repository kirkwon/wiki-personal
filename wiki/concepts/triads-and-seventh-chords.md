# Triads and Seventh Chords

## Triads

A triad is three notes played together, built from a root, third, and fifth.

- **Major Triad**: Root - Major 3rd - Perfect 5th (C-E-G)
- **Minor Triad**: Root - Minor 3rd - Perfect 5th (C-Eb-G)
- **Diminished Triad**: Root - Minor 3rd - Diminished 5th (C-Eb-Gb)
- **Augmented Triad**: Root - Major 3rd - Augmented 5th (C-E-G♯)

## Seventh Chords

Seventh chords add a fourth note to a triad, creating richer harmonic colors.

- **Major 7th**: Major triad + Major 7th (C-E-G-B)
- **Dominant 7th**: Major triad + Minor 7th (C-E-G-Bb)
- **Minor 7th**: Minor triad + Minor 7th (C-Eb-G-Bb)
- **Minor Major 7th**: Minor triad + Major 7th (C-Eb-G-B)
- **Diminished 7th**: Diminished triad + Diminished 7th (C-Eb-Gb-Bbb)
- **Half-Diminished 7th**: Diminished triad + Minor 7th (C-Eb-Gb-Bb)

These chord types are fundamental to [[jazz-harmony]] and the practice of [[chord-voicings]].
