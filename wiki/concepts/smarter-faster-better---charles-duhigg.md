# Smarter Faster Better - Charles Duhigg

Charles Duhigg's Smarter Faster Better explores the science of productivity, focusing on how mental models, motivation, and habits drive individual and team performance. The book draws on research from psychology, neuroscience, and organizational behavior to explain why some people and groups consistently outperform others.

## Key Themes

- **Motivation** — The role of autonomy and control in sustaining effort
- **Mental models** — How frameworks for understanding the world improve decision-making
- **Teams** — The importance of psychological safety and diverse thinking
- **Absorption** — Deep engagement and flow states

## Connection to Habit Mental Model

Duhigg treats habits as central to productivity, showing how automatic routines free cognitive resources for higher-level thinking. This connects to the broader habit mental model and informs how habitual patterns shape organizational and personal performance.
