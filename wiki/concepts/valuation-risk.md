# Valuation Risk

Valuation risk refers to the difficulty of determining the fair value of an investment, particularly in opaque or private markets where daily pricing is unavailable. It arises because alternative investments lack daily market pricing, making it difficult to determine fair value. Appraisals are subjective, comparable sales may not exist, and private markets are opaque.

## Sources of Valuation Difficulty

- No daily pricing (unlike publicly traded stocks)
- Appraisals are subjective and can vary widely
- Comparable sales may not exist for unique assets
- Opacity in private markets with limited transparency
- Information asymmetry between managers and investors

## Mitigation Strategies

- Use professional appraisers for real estate and collectibles
- Research comparable sales and market data
- Understand the market thoroughly before investing
- Avoid overpaying by setting strict valuation thresholds
- Conduct thorough due diligence on managers
