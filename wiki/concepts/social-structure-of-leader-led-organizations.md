# Social Structure of Leader-Led Organizations

The social structure of leader-led organizations encompasses communication patterns, decision-making processes, and relational norms that define how teams interact. Simon Sinek emphasizes that these structures must be deliberately designed to support trust and collaboration.

Effective social structures include transparent communication, inclusive decision-making, and norms that encourage vulnerability and honest feedback. This concept connects to [[communication-skills]] and [[building-a-cohesive-team]] by highlighting how relational patterns shape organizational health.
