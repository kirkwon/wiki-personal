# Reciprocity

Reciprocity is the social norm that people tend to return favors, respond to kindness with kindness, and feel obligated to repay what they have received. It is the tendency to return favors and build social bonds.

## Core Idea
This principle is one of the most powerful social forces. It operates at individual, organizational, and societal levels, and can be leveraged (and exploited) in negotiations, marketing, and relationship building.

## Applications
- **Negotiation**: Offering concessions to elicit reciprocal concessions.
- **Marketing**: Free samples, trials, and gifts create a sense of obligation.
- **Relationships**: Genuine generosity builds trust and social capital.

## Connection to Other Models
