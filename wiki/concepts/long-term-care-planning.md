# Long-Term Care Planning

Long-term care is the largest single variable in retirement healthcare costs, with 70% of people 65+ expected to need some form of care.

## Key Statistics
- **Probability**: 70% of people 65+ will need some LTC
- **Average duration**: 3 years
- **Home health aide**: $6,000-$8,000/month
- **Assisted living**: $4,500-$6,000/month
- **Nursing home (semi-private)**: $8,000-$10,000+/month
- **Nursing home (private)**: $9,000-$12,000+/month

## LTC Insurance Options

### Traditional LTC Insurance
- Comprehensive coverage with inflation protection
- Expensive premiums, possible rate increases
- "Use it or lose it" risk
- Best for: Asset protection and peace of mind

### Hybrid LTC/Life Insurance
- Death benefit if LTC not used, level premiums
- Lower LTC benefits than traditional
- Best for: Guaranteed benefit

### Life Insurance with LTC Rider
- Accelerated death benefit for LTC
- Reduces death benefit
- Best for: Those needing life insurance anyway

### Short-Term Care Insurance
- Lower premium, covers 1 year or less
- Best for: Bridging gaps to self-insurance or Medicaid

## Self-Insurance Strategies
1. Dedicated LTC savings account
2. Home equity (reverse mortgage, HELOC, sale)
3. Income-producing assets (rental property, dividends)
4. Family care (with compensation agreements)
5. Medicaid planning (spend-down strategies, trusts)

## Government Programs
- **Medicare**: Limited LTC coverage (100 days skilled nursing after hospital)
- **Medicaid**: Pays for LTC but requires asset spend-down ($2,000 limit for individual)
- **VA Benefits**: Aid and Attendance for veterans and spouses
- **State Programs**: Some states have LTC partnership programs
