# Remote Work as New Norm

Remote work as a new norm refers to the post-pandemic shift in which remote and distributed work arrangements have become permanent features of the modern workplace rather than temporary adaptations. Galloway emphasizes that remote work is already becoming the new norm with lasting effects on how we live, learn, and collaborate.

## Key Points

- Remote work gained massive adoption during COVID-19 and shows no signs of reversing.
- The shift transforms workplace culture, employee expectations, and talent acquisition.
- Companies must prepare for a more diverse, distributed workforce.
- Remote work intersects with productivity optimization, decision culture, and organizational design.
