# Birth of the Cool

Birth of the Cool refers to the 1949-1950 nonet recordings that defined the cool jazz movement. Arranged by Gil Evans, these sessions featured Miles Davis leading a nine-piece ensemble with a cooler, more relaxed sound incorporating French horn and tuba.

## Significance
- Marked a pivotal transition from the intensity of bebop
- Influenced the broader cool jazz movement
- Demonstrated the power of arrangement and orchestration in jazz

## Key Recordings
- "Boplicity"
- "Moon Dreams"
