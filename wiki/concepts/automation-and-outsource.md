# Automation and Outsource

Automation and outsourcing are strategies for streamlining repetitive tasks and delegating responsibilities to save time and effort. This is the final chapter focus of the PARA Method.

## Key Ideas

- Automate repetitive digital tasks where possible
- Delegate responsibilities that others can handle
- Streamline processes to reduce friction
- Focus your energy on high-value, creative work

## Cautions

The PARA Method warns that optimization should not become an obsession. Systems should save more time than they cost to maintain, and the pursuit of productivity should not destroy joy in the work.
