# Five Disciplines Framework

The Five Disciplines Framework, developed by Peter M. Senge, identifies five integrated building blocks for developing a systemic perspective: personal mastery, mental models, shared vision, team learning, and systems thinking.

## The Five Disciplines

1. **Personal Mastery**: Individual journey toward continuous improvement, self-awareness, and learning from experience
2. **Mental Models**: Deeply ingrained assumptions that shape perceptions and decisions in every aspect of life
3. **Shared Vision**: Common understanding among stakeholders about desired futures through dialogue and collaborative visioning
4. **Team Learning**: Collective reflection, inquiry, and experimentation enabling teams to learn together effectively
5. **Systems Thinking**: Holistic approach understanding complex problems through interconnections between variables

## Key Takeaways

- The disciplines work as integrated building blocks rather than isolated practices
- Together they develop a systemic perspective essential for addressing complex challenges
- Applying this framework consistently leads to lasting improvements in decision-making and organizational effectiveness
- Learning organizations continuously adapt, innovate, and thrive through these disciplines
