# Ideas as a Mental Model

An idea is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Ideas.md]

As a universal mental model, Ideas applies across all domains — from decision-making to problem-solving, from creative work to strategic thinking. It functions as a meta-tool for pattern recognition, similar to how [[thinking-mental-model]] and [[planning]] serve as navigational frameworks for complexity.^[raw/articles/Ideas.md]

## Definition

Ideas function as universal conceptual tools that offer frameworks for sense-making in complex situations. They serve as mental shortcuts for recognizing patterns and understanding relationships across domains.
