# Supplementation

Supplementation refers to the strategic use of targeted nutrients — vitamins, minerals, and adaptogenic herbs — to enhance health and performance beyond what diet alone provides. Ferriss's "Power Pill" chapter covers how to identify and use supplements effectively.

## Key Points
- Strategic supplementation fills nutritional gaps and supports specific health goals.
- Essential vitamins, minerals, and adaptogenic herbs are highlighted as particularly valuable.
- The approach emphasizes targeting deficiencies and performance needs rather than blanket supplementation.
