# Savings

Savings refer to the portion of income that is not immediately consumed, representing capital reserved for future use. Financially, it is a fundamental component of personal and national wealth, often held in low-risk instruments like savings accounts or bonds. The act of saving promotes financial stability by providing a buffer against unexpected expenses or funding larger investments over time. Savings rates are key metrics used by economists to gauge a society's overall economic health and future growth potential.
