# Induction

Induction is a conceptual tool that helps understand and navigate complex situations by providing a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world. It is a form of reasoning that moves from specific observations to general conclusions, serving as a foundational epistemological model for identifying recurring patterns across domains.

## Core Idea
Induction functions as a universal mental model applicable to any situation requiring pattern recognition and sense-making. It bridges the gap between raw experience and abstract understanding, enabling individuals to extract general principles from particular instances.

## Connection to Knowledge Creation
In David Deutsch's framework from *The Beginning of Infinity*, induction relates to how knowledge advances through the explicitation of problems and the growth of explanatory reach. Inductive reasoning is not merely a heuristic shortcut but a fundamental mechanism by which humans create new knowledge under fallibilism—the recognition that all knowledge is provisional and open to revision.
