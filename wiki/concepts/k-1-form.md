# K-1 Form

A K-1 form is the tax reporting document used for partnership and S-corporation income. In the context of alternative investments, K-1s are issued for private equity, real estate partnerships, and other pass-through entities.

## Key Points

- Reports investment income, gains, losses, and deductions
- Used for private equity investments where income flows through to investors
- Can complicate tax filing due to multiple forms from various investments
- Connects to carried interest taxation (performance allocations taxed as capital gains)
