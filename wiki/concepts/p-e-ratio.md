# P/E Ratio

Price-to-Earnings ratio, measuring the market price per share relative to earnings per share. Used in the [[stock-investment-tracker]] to assess valuation.

## Usage

Tracked alongside beta, dividend yield, market cap, 52-week range, volume, and volatility in the stock investment template.
