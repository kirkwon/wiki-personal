# Dunning-Kruger Effect

**Origin:** Dunning & Kruger (1999)  
**Category:** Cognitive bias

A metacognitive bias where people with low ability at a task overestimate their ability, while experts tend to underestimate their competence. Novices lack the expertise to recognize their own incompetence (dual burden: you're bad at something AND you don't know you're bad at it).

## The Curve
1. **Peak of "Mount Stupid"** — beginners are confidently wrong
2. **Valley of Despair** — awareness of complexity sets in, confidence drops
3. **Slope of Enlightenment** — genuine competence builds slowly
4. **Plateau of Sustainable Confidence** — experts are calibrated, tentative

## Relevance
Critical for [[critical-thinking]] and self-assessment in any skill domain. In investing, it manifests as overconfidence in recent winners and underappreciation of tail risks.

## Counter
Keep a decision journal. Compare predictions to outcomes relentlessly. Calibration training: estimate confidence intervals, then check how often you're right at each confidence level.
