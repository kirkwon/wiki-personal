# Environment Design

Environment design is a technique for shaping your physical and digital surroundings to make desired behaviors easier and unwanted behaviors harder. It is a key tool in the PARA Method for building sustainable habits and reducing friction in daily activities.

## Core Ideas

Rather than relying on willpower, environment design changes the path of least resistance. This includes arranging your workspace, removing distractions, setting up digital tools for quick access, and creating cues that trigger productive behavior.

## Examples

- Placing your notebook on your desk where you can see it
- Removing social media apps from your home screen
- Setting up templates and automations for recurring tasks
- Creating dedicated spaces for different types of work

## Connection to Habit Formation

Environment design pairs with the [[2-minute-rule]] and [[external-triggers]] to make new habits easier to start. When the environment supports the behavior, less conscious effort is required.
