# Investment Property Acquisition Template

Template for evaluating potential property acquisitions before purchase.

## Property Evaluation
- Address, asking price, comps analysis, target purchase price
- Valuation method (Sales Comparable, Income Approach, Cost Approach)

## Financial Projections
- Purchase costs (price, closing costs 3-5%, initial repairs)
- Financing structure (loan amount, down payment, LTV, interest rate, monthly payment)
- First-year projections (gross annual rent, vacancy rate, effective gross income, operating expenses, NOI, annual cash flow, cash on cash, cap rate)

## Sensitivity Analysis
What-if scenarios varying purchase price, interest rate, rent level, and expenses. Risk assessment with best case, worst case, most likely, and acceptable range.
