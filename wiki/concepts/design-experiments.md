# Design Experiments
Design experiments are the process of designing tests to validate a mental model, conducting the tests, and refining the mental model based on the results.

## Introduction
Design experiments are an essential component of validating mental models. This page provides an overview of design experiments, including their definition, importance, and applications.

## Definition
Design experiments are the process of designing tests to validate a mental model, conducting the tests, and refining the mental model based on the results. This process involves identifying variables, designing tests, conducting tests, and refining the mental model.

## Importance
Design experiments are important because they provide an opportunity to test the mental model in a controlled and systematic way, identify areas for improvement, and refine the model to make it more accurate and reliable.

## Applications
Design experiments have a wide range of applications, including business, economics, politics, and personal decision-making. They can be used to validate mental models, identify opportunities and risks, and develop effective strategies.
