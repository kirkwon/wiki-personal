# Interest Rate Risk

Risk arising from changes in interest rates affecting loan costs, property values, and refinancing ability.

## Assessment
- Fixed Rate Loans: Protection against rate increases
- Variable Rate Loans: Exposure to rate increases
- Short-term Maturities: Refinancing risk
- Long-term Maturities: Lower refinancing risk

## Management Strategies
Fixed vs. variable mix (70/30 to 50/50 depending on outlook), interest rate hedges (swaps, caps, collars), refinancing timing aligned with market cycles, and loan structures avoiding balloon payments.
