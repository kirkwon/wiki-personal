# Mole

Mole (Nahuatl for "sauce") is a complex and highly regarded traditional sauce originating from Mexican cuisine. It is famous for its depth of flavor, which comes from the combination of numerous ingredients including various chiles, spices, seeds, nuts, and often unsweetened chocolate. While recipes vary dramatically by region and family—with some versions being savory while others are sweet—moles typically serve as a centerpiece dish, usually accompanying poultry or pork. Due to its intricate preparation involving dozens of components, mole is considered a culinary staple representing Mexican cultural heritage.
