# Risk Dashboard

A portfolio-level monitoring tool providing a real-time overview of aggregate risk levels, trends, and action items across risk categories. The risk dashboard enables the monitoring phase of the [[risk-assessment-framework]], providing ongoing visibility into portfolio risk.

## Components
- **Overall Risk Score**: Aggregate level, trend (Improving/Stable/Deteriorating), and critical risks count
- **Risk by Category**: Market, Financial, Operational, Regulatory scores and trends
- **Key Risk Indicators**: Portfolio DSCR, Average LTV, Vacancy Rate, Operating Expense Ratio
- **Action Items**: Prioritized as High, Medium, Low with deadlines

## Monitoring Frequency
Daily (cash flow, rent collection), weekly (occupancy, maintenance), monthly (financial performance, market indicators), quarterly (portfolio review, strategic assessment), annually (comprehensive risk assessment, planning).
