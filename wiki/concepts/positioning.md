# Positioning

Positioning is a conceptual tool and strategic principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

As a universal mental model, Positioning is applicable across all domains—from decision-making to strategic thinking to everyday problem-solving. It functions similarly to other pattern-recognition mental models in the wiki, such as [[problem-mental-model]] and [[solutions-mental-model]], by offering a structured way to perceive and interpret complex environments.

The model draws on classical strategic thinking, with Sun Tzu's [[the-art-of-war-sun-tzu]] cited as a cross-source perspective. From *The Art of War* Chapter 4, the ideal is to be invincible in defense while waiting for opportunities to strike—positioning yourself so that victory is assured before battle begins.

## Key Characteristics
- Provides a mental shortcut for complexity
- Focuses on pattern and relationship recognition
- Universal applicability across domains
- Connects to strategic awareness and situational understanding

## Key Ideas
- **Defensive strength through position**: Being in the right place at the right time is more valuable than raw strength.
- **Create advantage before engagement**: Position yourself advantageously before the conflict starts, not during it.
- **Adaptive positioning**: Like water seeking low ground (Chapter 5), position should flow with circumstances rather than resist them.
