# Entity Structure Strategies

Legal entity selection and structuring to isolate risk between properties and protect assets from entity-level failures.

## Entity Options

- **LLC**: Limited liability, pass-through taxation
- **Corporation**: Double taxation but liability protection
- **REIT**: Public market liquidity, regulatory compliance
- **Partnership**: Shared risk and capital

## Risk Management Applications

- Separate legal entities to isolate risk between properties
- Bankruptcy remote entities to protect assets
- Insurance company subsidiaries for specialized coverage
- Cross-border entities for international tax and liability planning

Entity structure is a risk transfer strategy within [[risk-assessment-framework]].
