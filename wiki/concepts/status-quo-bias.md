# Status Quo Bias

Status quo bias is the preference for the current state of affairs. People tend to stick with existing options even when better alternatives are available, due to loss aversion and the discomfort of change.

## Examples
- Not switching banks even when a competitor offers better rates
- Keeping default settings without evaluating alternatives

## Mitigation
- Actively evaluate alternatives regularly
- Frame changes as gains rather than losses
