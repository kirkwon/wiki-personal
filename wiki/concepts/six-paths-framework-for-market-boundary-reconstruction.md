# Six Paths Framework for Market Boundary Reconstruction

The Six Paths Framework provides six analytical paths for discovering uncontested market space and making the competition irrelevant. These paths guide organizations to look beyond their own industry for new demand:

1. **Look across alternative industries** - Examine how alternative industries satisfy the same basic needs
2. **Look across strategic groups** - Within the industry, identify groups that target different buyer groups with different price-performance ratios
3. **Look across buyer groups** - Recognize that the chain of buyers (purchasers, users, influencers) may have different needs
4. **Look across complementary product and service offerings** - Consider what happens before, during, and after the purchase
5. **Look across functional or emotional appeal to buyers** - Determine whether the industry competes on functionality or emotion, and how that could shift
6. **Look across time** - Anticipate the trends that will shape the industry and identify emerging noncustomers

Each path offers a different angle for reconstructing market boundaries and identifying blue ocean opportunities.
