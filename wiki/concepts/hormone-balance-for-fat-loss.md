# Hormone Balance for Fat Loss

Hormone balance is Ferriss's alternative to traditional calorie restriction and cardio for fat loss. The "Body-Fat Paradox" chapter argues that optimizing hormonal environment — through sleep, nutrition, exercise, and stress management — is more effective than conventional dieting approaches.

## Key Points
- Hormone balance (e.g., insulin, cortisol, thyroid, growth hormone) determines fat storage and mobilization more than calorie counting.
- Sleep optimization, strategic nutrition, and HIIT exercise all influence hormonal profiles.
- Traditional approaches like excessive cardio and severe calorie restriction can worsen hormonal imbalances.
