# Strategy as a mental model

Strategy is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Strategy.md]

As a universal mental model, strategy functions across domains as a way to impose structure on ambiguity, identify leverage points, and guide action when outcomes are uncertain. It connects to related mental models like [[planning]], [[thinking-mental-model]], and [[reality-mental-model]], which similarly serve as navigational frameworks for complexity.^[raw/articles/Strategy.md]

The primary historical reference for this model is [[the-art-of-war-sun-tzu]], which articulates strategy as the art of positioning forces and anticipating opponents to achieve victory under conditions of uncertainty.^[raw/articles/Strategy.md]

# Cross-source perspectives

This mental model is referenced in [[the-art-of-war-sun-tzu]] as a foundational text on strategic thinking.^[raw/articles/Strategy.md]
