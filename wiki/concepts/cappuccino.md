# Cappuccino

A cappuccino is a popular coffee beverage made by combining equal parts of espresso, steamed milk, and frothed milk. Its creamy texture and balanced flavor profile make it a favorite among coffee enthusiasts. In cognitive science, the enjoyment of a well-crafted cappuccino can evoke sensory pleasure through taste and olfactory stimuli, contributing to overall mood enhancement.
