# Fairness and Responsibility

Fairness and responsibility are the ethical foundations of Taleb's skin in the game framework. Having a stake in one's actions ensures that decisions are made with genuine accountability rather than abstract principles. Taleb argues that fairness requires that those who benefit from decisions also bear their consequences, and that responsibility is maintained through transparency and personal exposure to outcomes.

## Key Points

- Fairness requires that decision-makers bear consequences of their choices
- Responsibility is maintained through transparency and personal exposure
- Skin in the game ensures ethical integrity in decision-making
- Applies to finance, governance, and community interactions
- Connects to [[asymmetric-information-morality]] and [[accountability]]
