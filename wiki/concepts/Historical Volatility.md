# Historical Volatility

Historical volatility (HV) measures the actual volatility of an asset's price over a past period. It is used alongside implied volatility to assess whether options are fairly priced.

## Σχόλιο
The historical volatility provides a baseline for comparing current implied volatility. When IV is significantly higher than HV, options may be overpriced; when lower, they may be underpriced.
