# Factors

Factors is a universal mental model serving as a conceptual tool for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition. It functions as a meta-framework that helps identify which variables matter in any given situation, providing a mental shortcut for making sense of complex patterns.

## Definition

Factors is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world. Unlike specific techniques, it is a lens for identifying which factors are relevant in any context.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This mental model draws on insights from multiple authoritative sources:

- **Robert B. Cialdini** – In *Pre-Suasion: A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade*, Cialdini emphasizes environmental and contextual factors that shape persuasion before it even begins. Factors as a model aligns with this pre-suasion framework, recognizing that the conditions surrounding communication matter as much as the communication itself.
- **Charles Duhigg** – In *Supercommunicators*, Duhigg explores the factors that determine whether communication succeeds or fails. His research on what makes people connect and understand each other maps onto the Factors model as a tool for parsing the variables that drive effective communication.
