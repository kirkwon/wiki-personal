# Creative Log Habit

A Creative Log is a habit tool for recording interesting thoughts, observations, and ideas as they occur. De Bono recommends maintaining this log to support sustained creative practice by capturing fragments that might otherwise be lost.

The log serves as a reservoir of raw material for later creative exploration. It connects to [[categorizing-information]] (organizing notes for future retrieval) and [[active-revision-techniques]] (reviewing and deepening understanding over time). Unlike structured brainstorming sessions, the Creative Log operates continuously, capturing insights in their original context before they can be evaluated or refined.
