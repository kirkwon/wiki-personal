# Model Selection

The skill of choosing appropriate models for a given situation. Not all models fit all problems, and forcing complex models on simple problems is a common pitfall.

## Principles

- Match model complexity to problem complexity
- Avoid overfitting models to data
- Recognize when a simpler model suffices
- Beware of mathematical elegance obscuring practical reality
- Privilege empirical testing over theoretical preference

## Warning Signs of Poor Model Selection

- Using complex models for simple problems
- Confirming existing biases through model choice
- Ignoring model uncertainty in decision-making
- Privileging quantitative over qualitative insights
- Modeling becomes academic exercise without practical application
