# Momentum + Value

Combining momentum with value is one of the most robust factor combinations for improving risk-adjusted returns. The approach uses momentum to time value purchases, avoiding "value traps" (stocks that are cheap for a reason).

## Strategy Rules

1. Filter for cheap stocks (low P/E, P/B)
2. From value stocks, buy those with positive momentum
3. Hold until momentum turns negative

## Performance

Backtested results (1927-2023):
- Annual return: 15.2%
- Volatility: 19.8%
- Sharpe ratio: 0.77
- Max drawdown: -58% (2008)

This outperforms both pure momentum (Sharpe 0.67) and pure value (Sharpe 0.61) on a risk-adjusted basis, with significantly lower maximum drawdowns.

## Why It Works

Value stocks provide a margin of safety, while momentum ensures the stock is actually moving in the right direction rather than being a "falling knife." The combination smooths returns and reduces crash severity.
