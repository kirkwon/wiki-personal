# Text-to-Speech

Text-to-Speech (TTS) is a technology that converts digital written text into synthesized spoken audio. These systems analyze character strings and utilize complex algorithms to generate human-like voice output, ranging from basic robotic tones to highly natural, emotive speech. Modern TTS approaches employ deep learning models, such as WaveNet or Tacotron, which allow for sophisticated control over prosody, accent, and emotional tone. The resulting technology is critical for accessibility tools, virtual assistants, and automated content delivery systems.
