# Quit: The Power of Knowing When to Walk Away - Annie Duke

## Overview
2024 book by former professional poker player and decision-making expert Annie Duke. Extends themes from her earlier work *Thinking in Bets*, reframing quitting as a strategic skill rather than failure. Central argument: knowing when to walk away is essential for long-term success. The book emphasizes evaluating multiple possible futures and outcomes before committing, connecting to scenario-based thinking and decision-making under uncertainty.

## Key Themes
- Knowing when to quit or walk away
- Evaluating multiple possible futures
- Decision-making under uncertainty
- Scenario-based thinking
- Framing choices for success
- The value of loss aversion and overcoming biases that prevent quitting
- Learning opportunities in losses
- The power of timing in deciding when to quit
- Navigating complex decisions with multiple options
- Quitting as a form of self-care and mental health preservation
- Commitment phobia and its influence on the ability to quit or continue

## Chapters
- **Embracing Imperfection** – Understanding imperfection and uncertainty as prerequisites for recognizing when to quit.
- **The Art of Quitting** – Quitting as a deliberate art form rather than a sign of weakness.
- **Framing Choices for Success** – How decision framing shapes outcomes; practical techniques for framing decisions.
- **The Value of Loss Aversion** – Examining loss aversion and strategies to overcome biases that prevent quitting.
- **Learning Opportunities in Losses** – Extracting wisdom and growth from difficult situations and failed projects.
- **The Power of Timing** – Critical role of timing in deciding when to quit; market trends, personal energy, external factors.
- **Navigating Complex Decisions** – Methods for navigating complex decision-making with multiple options; role of intuition and experience.
- **Quitting as a Form of Self-Care** – Quitting to prevent burnout, maintain mental health, preserve relationships.
- **The Psychology Behind Commitment Phobia** – How commitment phobia influences ability to quit or continue.
- **Written as a Letter to My Younger Self** – Personal reflections guiding when to persist versus quit.
- **The Quitter's Guidebook** – Comprehensive principles for recognizing the right time to walk away.
