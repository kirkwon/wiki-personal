# Hyperfocus - Chris Bailey

Source summary for Chris Bailey's 2024 book *Hyperfocus*, which introduces a two-mode attention framework: hyperfocus (intense single-task concentration) and scatterfocus (deliberate mind-wandering). The book covers attention residue from task-switching, attention budgeting as a finite resource, peak attention hours, mindfulness for attention training, environment design for focus, single-tasking advantages, strategic distraction, breaks, and the relationship between scatterfocus and creativity.

## Key Concepts
- Hyperfocus and scatterfocus as complementary attention modes
- Attention residue from task-switching degrades performance
- Attention budget as finite resource requiring management
- Peak attention hours vary individually
- Mindfulness strengthens attention and reduces mind-wandering
- Single-tasking outperforms multitasking
- Strategic use of distraction vs. elimination
- Scatterfocus drives creativity and prevents burnout
