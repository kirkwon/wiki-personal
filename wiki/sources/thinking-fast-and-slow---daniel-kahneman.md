# Thinking, Fast and Slow - Daniel Kahneman

Daniel Kahneman's seminal work presents a comprehensive framework for understanding how human cognition operates through two distinct systems: System 1 (fast, intuitive) and System 2 (slow, deliberate). The book documents decades of research on heuristics, cognitive biases, loss aversion, framing effects, overconfidence, and group decision dynamics.

## Chapters

- Chapter 1: The Tale of Two Systems — Dual-process theory foundation
- Chapter 2: Heuristics and Biases — Intuitive shortcuts and systematic errors
- Chapter 3: Overconfidence and Certainty — Illusion of control and false certainty
- Chapter 4: Loss Aversion — Losses loom larger than equivalent gains
- Chapter 5: The Endowment Effect and Ascription Bias — Ownership premium and self-attribution
- Chapter 6: Framing — Presentation of choices alters decisions
- Chapter 7: The Role of Money and Dread Aversion — Preferences and anticipatory anxiety
- Chapter 8: The Hindsight Bias — Post-hoc belief that predictions were made
- Chapter 9: Probability and Judgment Under Uncertainty — Challenges in assessing probabilities
- Chapter 10: The Power of Focus Groups and Group Decision-Making — Conformity and polarization
