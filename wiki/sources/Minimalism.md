# Minimalism

## Definition

Minimalism is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This mental model is referenced in Cal Newport's Digital Minimalism as a universal mental model applicable across domains, not limited to digital life.
