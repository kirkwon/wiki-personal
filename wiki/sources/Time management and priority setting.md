# Time management and priority setting

This source covers the skill of systematically improving efficiency and output through identifying and implementing methods, techniques, and frameworks that maximize personal or organizational productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices. It introduces the Audit-Apply-Iterate-Scale framework as a four-step process: audit current workflow, apply frameworks to restructure approach, iterate and refine based on results, and scale what works. The source connects to existing time management concepts including the time management matrix, quadrant-2-focus, and deep work.

The skill is referenced alongside Seneca's Letters from a Stoic, emphasizing the long-standing philosophical tradition of managing time and priorities wisely. Related skills include error-correction mechanisms, peer accountability systems, and scatterfocus.
