# Industries

Industries functions as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition. It serves as a cognitive shortcut for making sense of economic, organizational, and societal patterns across domains.

This source synthesizes four cross-source perspectives:
- Scott Galloway on post-COVID industry reshuffling
- Ray Dalio on long-cycle macro dynamics affecting industries
- Peter Thiel and Blake Masters on innovation requiring new industry creation
- Atul Gawande on systematic checklists for industry execution

Together these perspectives converge on using industry-level analysis as a unifying framework for understanding power dynamics, cycles, and relationships in complex systems.^[raw/articles/Industries.md]
