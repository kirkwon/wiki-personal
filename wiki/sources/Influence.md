# Influence Source

This source documents the "Influence" mental model, a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern recognition. It synthesizes perspectives from Robert B. Cialdini's "Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion" and Daniel Kahneman's "Thinking Fast and Slow," positioning influence as a universal mental model applicable across domains.

The model provides a mental shortcut for recognizing patterns and relationships that drive persuasion and decision-making, with moderate confidence (0.7) based on two established sources.
