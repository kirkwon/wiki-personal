# Predictions Source Summary

This source introduces "Predictions" as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations by recognizing patterns and relationships. It draws on two authoritative references: Philip E. Tetlock and Dan Gardner's superforecasting research, and Nate Silver's work on signal detection in *The Signal and the Noise*. The model positions prediction-making as a general-purpose thinking tool rather than a narrow statistical technique, with moderate confidence (0.7) reflecting conceptual-level evidence.
