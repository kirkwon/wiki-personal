# Game.md Source Summary

This source introduces "Game" as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It draws on two key sources: Nassim Nicholas Taleb's *Skin in the Game* (accountability, consequences) and Simon Sinek's *The Infinite Game* (infinite vs. finite games, long-term play). The model is positioned as a general-purpose framework applicable across all domains.
## Cross-Source Perspectives
- **Nassim Nicholas Taleb** — *Skin in the Game*: Emphasizes accountability and having stakes in outcomes.
- **Simon Sinek** — *The Infinite Game*: Distinguishes infinite games from finite games, focusing on adaptability and long-term purpose.
## Key Claims
- Game is a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships.
- Applicable universally with moderate confidence (0.7).
- Bridges Taleb's accountability lens and Sinek's strategic longevity lens.
