# Management

Management is a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for understanding patterns and relationships in complex situations. It is the practice of systematically improving efficiency and output by identifying and implementing methods, techniques, and frameworks that maximize personal or organizational productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices. This skill is discussed extensively across 30 different sources, indicating its broad applicability and importance across multiple domains. Its definition overlaps with existing mental models like [[planning]], [[Solutions]], and [[stillness]].

## How to Use Management

To apply Management effectively in your productivity system:

1. **Audit Your Current Workflow**
   - Identify where efficiency losses occur
   - Track how you currently spend time and attention
   - Note patterns that lead to wasted effort

2. **Apply the Framework**
   - Use Management to restructure your approach
   - Focus on high-impact activities
   - Eliminate or delegate low-value tasks

3. **Iterate and Refine**
   - Measure the impact of changes
   - Adjust based on results
   - Build consistency through habit formation

4. **Scale What Works**
   - Standardize effective approaches
   - Document processes for reuse
   - Teach others to multiply impact
