# Internal trigger mapping

Internal trigger mapping is a productivity skill focused on systematically improving efficiency and output by identifying internal emotional and thought triggers that drive unproductive behavior, then restructuring workflow to address them. It involves auditing current workflows, applying frameworks to focus on high-impact activities, iterating based on results, and scaling effective approaches. The concept is primarily discussed in Nir Eyal's Hooked framework and connects to behavior design principles from the Fogg behavior model.
