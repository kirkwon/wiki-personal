# Tools of Titans — Timothy Ferriss

Timothy Ferriss's *Tools of Titans* compiles strategies, tactics, and habits from world-class performers across business, health, and creativity. The pattern: effective strategies are often counterintuitive, require systematic experimentation, and involve deliberate questioning of defaults. Connects to the strategies mental model through its emphasis on learning from high performers and distilling actionable principles.
