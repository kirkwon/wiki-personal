# Tendency

Tendency is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

Cross-source perspective from Rolf Dobelli's *The Art of Thinking Clearly* frames tendency as a universal mental model applicable across domains.
