# Delphi method (skill)

This source defines the Delphi method as a personal and organizational productivity optimization framework rather than the established RAND Corporation structured forecasting technique. It presents a 4-step process: audit current workflow, apply restructuring framework, iterate and refine, and scale effective approaches. The source references James Surowiecki's The Wisdom of Crowds as a cross-source but notes the connection is superficial. Confidence is rated low due to single-source sourcing and conceptual conflation with the actual Delphi forecasting method.
