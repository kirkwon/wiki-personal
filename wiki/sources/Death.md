# Death

## Summary

Death is presented as a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world. The model is referenced primarily through Atul Gawande's Being Mortal, which explores how confronting mortality clarifies what matters in life and informs end-of-life care decisions.

## Key Claims

- Death functions as a universal mental shortcut for navigating complexity.
- Awareness of mortality sharpens decision-making about quality of life, legacy, and values.
- Confronting death counters the tendency to avoid difficult end-of-life questions.
