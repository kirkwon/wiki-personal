# The Four - Scott Galloway

Scott Galloway's 2024 book argues that four technology companies—Amazon, Apple, Google (Alphabet), and Facebook (Meta)—have become a de facto oligopoly reshaping entire markets. The book structures its analysis around four rivalries: Amazon vs. Apple, Uber vs. Google, Facebook vs. Baidu, and Microsoft vs. Tencent. A central chapter addresses strategies for businesses competing against these platform titans, emphasizing agility, talent, innovation, and adaptive leadership. The final chapter speculates on future competitive dynamics driven by global economic shifts and technological change.

Key themes include platform dominance and disruption, East vs. West tech rivalry, and the asymmetric nature of competition against winner-take-all companies.^[raw/articles/the-four---scott-galloway.md]
