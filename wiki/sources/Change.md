# Change - Mental Model Source

Change is proposed as a universal conceptual tool for navigating complex situations by recognizing patterns and relationships. This meta-summary references five major frameworks for understanding change across different domains: habit-based change (James Clear), organizational change (Charles Duhigg), systems-based change (Donella Meadows), purpose-driven change (Stephen Covey), and systemic learning (Peter Senge).
