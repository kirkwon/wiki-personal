# Golden Gate Heights Neighborhood Guide

A neighborhood guide for Golden Gate Heights, a residential area on hills south of Golden Gate Park in southwestern San Francisco. Covers geography, housing market data, stairways, parks, community life, and practical tips for visitors and residents.

## Overview

Golden Gate Heights is a residential neighborhood on hills south of Golden Gate Park, known for spectacular views, steep streets, and iconic stairways. Population ~15,000. Median home price $1.5-1.8M (2024). Some homes are stair-only with no car access.

## Location & Geography

- **Boundaries**: 19th Ave (east), Sunset Blvd (west), Lincoln Way (north), Irving St (south)
- **Elevation**: 250-500 feet, with Grandview Park as the highest point (~500ft) offering 360° views
- **Climate**: Foggy and cool (55-65°F), daily fog rolls over hills

## Key Landmarks

- [[16th-avenue-tiled-steps]] - 163 colorful tile mosaic steps installed in 2005
- [[grandview-park]] - Highest point (~500ft), 360° views, coastal scrub habitat restoration
- Lincoln Park / Legion of Honor - Museum and golf course nearby

## Housing

Mid-century homes (1940s-1960s). Median price ~$1.5-1.8M (2024). Some homes are stair-only with no car access.

## Transportation

28-19th Ave line, 38-Geary nearby. Steep streets challenge biking and driving.

## Activities

Stairway exploration, urban hiking, photography, Grandview Park summit views.

## Best Times to Visit

Fall (September-October) offers clearest views. Sunset hours when fog clears provide spectacular vistas.
