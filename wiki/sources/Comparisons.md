# Comparisons

This source documents the mental model of Comparisons as a universal conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It draws on Dan Ariely's work in Predictably Irrational, which demonstrates how people evaluate things relatively rather than absolutely, and how comparisons distort valuation, anchor decisions, and shape preferences.

## Key Concepts
- Comparisons provide cognitive shortcuts for making sense of patterns and relationships
- Relativity in judgment shapes choices and preferences
- Comparison-based reasoning can distort valuation when used without awareness
