# Models

Models is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

This mental model is referenced across four distinct intellectual traditions:
- **Nate Silver** — The Signal and the Noise, focused on prediction and statistical reasoning
- **Richard H. Thaler & Cass R. Sunstein** — Nudge, behavioral economics and choice architecture
- **Peter M. Senge** — The Fifth Discipline Fieldbook, systems thinking and organizational learning
- **Shane Parrish** — The Great Mental Models Vol 1, compendium of mental models as thinking tools

Models function as universal tools applicable across all domains, serving as mental shortcuts rather than precise replicas of reality. Confidence in this meta-concept is moderate (0.7), reflecting some uncertainty about boundaries and completeness.
