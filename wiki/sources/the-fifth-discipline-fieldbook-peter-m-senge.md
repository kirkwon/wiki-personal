# The Fifth Discipline Fieldbook - Peter M Senge

Peter M. Senge's *The Fifth Discipline Fieldbook* builds on the concepts from *The Fifth Discipline*, focusing on practical methods for building learning organizations. It contributes to the [[problems-mental-model]] by emphasizing organizational learning and systemic pattern recognition.

## Key Ideas
- Learning organizations continuously expand their capacity to create desired results
- Systems thinking is the cornerstone discipline for understanding complex organizational dynamics
- Mental models and shared vision enable teams to navigate complexity collectively
