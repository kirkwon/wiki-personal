# Technique Source Summary

Technique is defined as the practice of systematically improving efficiency and output through identifying and implementing methods, techniques, and frameworks that maximize productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices. This skill is referenced across 68 different sources, indicating its broad applicability across productivity, learning, and leadership domains.

The core framework consists of four phases:
1. **Audit** – Identify efficiency losses and track time/attention usage
2. **Apply** – Restructure approach, focus on high-impact activities
3. **Iterate** – Measure impact, adjust based on results, build consistency
4. **Scale** – Standardize effective approaches, document processes, teach others

Related skills include error-correction mechanisms, peer accountability systems, and scatterfocus. The concept connects to numerous existing works including David Allen's Getting Things Done, Sönke Ahrens' How to Take Smart Notes, Cal Newport's Slow Productivity, and Ray Dalio's Principles.
