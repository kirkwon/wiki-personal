# Getting

## Summary

"Getting" is introduced as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It serves as a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world. The model is referenced in David Allen's Ready for Anything.

## Key Claims

- Getting is a universal mental model applicable across domains.
- It provides a framework for understanding patterns and relationships.
- Referenced in David Allen's Ready for Anything.
