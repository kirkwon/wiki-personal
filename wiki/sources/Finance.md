# Finance Source

Finance is positioned as a conceptual tool or universal mental model for navigating complex situations by recognizing patterns and relationships. The sole cross-source reference is Nassim Nicholas Taleb's *The Black Swan*, linking Finance to the concept of low-probability, high-impact events and tail risk.
## Cross-Source Perspectives
- **Nassim Nicholas Taleb** — *The Black Swan* frames Finance as a lens for understanding rare, high-impact events.
