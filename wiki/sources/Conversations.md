# Source: Conversations

## Summary
Conversations is introduced as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern recognition in dialogue. The primary source is Charles Duhigg's *Supercommunicators*, which examines how conversation dynamics shape understanding, identity signaling, and outcomes. The model treats conversation not merely as an activity but as a framework for making sense of patterns and relationships.
