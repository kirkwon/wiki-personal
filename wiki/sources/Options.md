# Options Source

This source document introduces "Options" as a mental model — a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations by providing a mental shortcut for understanding patterns and relationships. The model is cross-referenced with Barry Schwartz's work on the Paradox of Choice.

## Key References
- [[The Paradox of Choice - Barry Schwartz]] — Barry Schwartz's Paradox of Choice, which discusses how having more options can lead to decision paralysis and reduced satisfaction.
- [[choice-overload]] — Concept related to the cognitive burden of too many options.
- [[decision-making-frameworks]] — Broader decision-making frameworks.
- [[decision-paralysis]] — Related concept of being unable to decide due to overwhelming choices.
