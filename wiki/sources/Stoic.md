# Stoic Mental Model Source

This source documents the Stoic mental model as a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern recognition and relationship awareness. It is supported by two Ryan Holiday sources: The Obstacle Is the Way and The Daily Stoic.

## Key Claims
- Stoic functions as a universal mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in complex situations.
- Applicability is universal across domains, with moderate confidence (0.7).
- Cross-referenced with The Obstacle Is the Way and The Daily Stoic by Ryan Holiday.
