# Pre-Suasion: A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade

## Overview

Robert B. Cialdini's Pre-Suasion introduces the concept that the moment before a message is delivered is as important as the message itself. By strategically priming attention, framing contexts, and managing environmental cues, practitioners can dramatically increase receptivity to subsequent persuasion attempts. This extends Cialdini's earlier work on the Six Principles of Influence — reciprocation, commitment and consistency, social proof, authority, liking, and scarcity — by adding a preparatory layer of influence.

## Key Concepts

- **Pre-Suasion**: The practice of influencing what happens before a request is made, using subtle environmental and cognitive cues to shift receptivity.
- **Six Principles of Influence**: Reciprocation, commitment/consistency, social proof, authority, liking, scarcity — foundational persuasion levers.
- **Cognitive Economy**: Minimizing cognitive load to optimize persuasion, avoiding overwhelm while maintaining attention.
- **Trust and Rapport**: Building connection before persuasion to create a favorable foundation for influence.
- **Ethics of Persuasion**: Distinguishing legitimate pre-suasion from manipulative exploitation.
