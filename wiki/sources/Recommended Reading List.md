# Source: Recommended Reading List.md

### Analysis of the Recommended Reading List Document

---

#### **1. Core Structure & Purpose**
This document is a **curated reading list** migrated from Apple Notes, organized into **8 categories** with **26 books**. The primary purpose is to:
- **Systematize learning** across behavioral economics, systems thinking, philosophy, productivity, and business strategy.
- **Provide a structured learning path** (4 phases) for progressive knowledge acquisition.
- **Enable data-driven analysis** via Dataview queries for tracking progress and categorization.

---

#### **2. Key Components**
**A. Categories & Book Selection**  
Books are grouped into thematic categories, reflecting a **holistic approach** to learning:
- **Behavioral Economics & Decision-Making**: Focuses on cognitive biases and decision science (e.g., *Predictably Irrational*, *The Art of Thinking Clearly*).
- **Systems & Complexity**: Explores emergent systems and dynamics (e.g., *Out of Control*, *The Systems Bible*).
- **Antifragility & Uncertainty**: Emphasizes resilience and philosophy (e.g., *Meditations*, *Man's Search for Meaning*).
- **Productivity & Focus**: Practical tools for time management and habit formation (e.g., *Indistractable*, *Hyperfocus*).
- **Leadership & Organizations**: Team dynamics and organizational behavior (e.g., *The Five Dysfunctions of a Team*).
- **Business Strategy & Innovation**: Entrepreneurship and competitive advantage (e.g., *The Lean Startup*, *Blue Ocean Strategy*).
- **Mental Models & Learning**: Cognitive frameworks for problem-solving (e.g., *Super Thinking*, *How to Solve It*).
- **Philosophy & Wisdom**: Classical texts on ethics, resilience, and meaning (e.g., *Beyond Good and Evil*).

**B. Learning Path Phases**  
The document outlines a **4-phase learning journey**:
1. **Foundations (Decision-Making)**: Start with behavioral economics and cognitive biases.
2. **Systems (Complexity Thinking)**: Build understanding of dynamic systems and multi-model thinking.
3. **Wisdom (Philosophy & Resilience)**: Explore stoicism, existential psychology, and strategic thinking.
4. **Application (Productivity & Business)**: Apply knowledge to real-world productivity and leadership challenges.

**C. Individual Book Entries**  
Each book is expected to have:
- **YAML frontmatter** (title, author, category).
- **1500-word summary** with structured content (e.g., key themes, frameworks, practical applications).
- **Frameworks/skills extraction** into separate files (e.g., "Hyperfocus" → focus management techniques).
- **Cross-references** to related concepts (e.g., linking *The Art of War* to antifragility).

---

#### **3. Dataview Queries & Data Utilization**
The document includes **three Dataview queries** for analysis:
1. **By Category**: Sorts books alphabetically by category and title.
2. **By Status**: Filters books marked as "to-read" for progress tracking.
3. **Reading Progress**: Aggregates books by category to visualize reading habits.
