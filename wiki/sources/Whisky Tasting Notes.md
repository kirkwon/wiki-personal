# Whisky Tasting Notes

Personal collection of 10 whisky bottles spanning bourbon, Scotch, and Tennessee whiskey, focused on entry-to-mid-range. Tasting methodology follows nose, palate, finish evaluation. Price tiers range from entry ($12-30) to premium ($50-80). Key recommendations include Buffalo Trace and Wild Turkey 101 as best value, Angel's Envy and Blanton's as premium picks, and Macallan 12 as aged Scotch benchmark.

## Price Tiers

- **Entry** ($12-30): Evan Williams, Buffalo Trace, Wild Turkey — best for beginners and mixing
- **Mid** ($35-45): Knob Creek, Woodford Reserve — daily sippers
- **Premium** ($50-80): Angel's Envy, Macallan 12, Blanton's — special occasions and gifts
