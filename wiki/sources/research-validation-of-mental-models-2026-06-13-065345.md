# Research Validation of Mental Models
This page summarizes the research on validating mental models, including peer review, incremental validation, social validation, and design experiments.

## Introduction
Validating mental models is crucial for ensuring their accuracy and reliability. This page provides an overview of the methods for validating mental models, including peer review, incremental validation, social validation, and design experiments.

## Methods for Validation
### Peer Review
Peer review involves sharing the mental model with others and receiving feedback. This process helps identify areas for improvement and refine the mental model.

### Incremental Validation
Incremental validation involves testing the mental model incrementally, starting with small, low-stakes tests and gradually increasing the stakes as the model is refined.

### Social Validation
Social validation involves explaining the mental model to someone else and hearing their objections. This process forces articulation, reveals gaps in reasoning, and provides an opportunity to refine the mental model.

### Design Experiments
Design experiments involve designing tests to validate the mental model, conducting the tests, and refining the mental model based on the results.
