# Events Source

This source documents the "Events" mental model — a universal conceptual tool for understanding patterns and relationships in complex situations. It draws on Nassim Nicholas Taleb's works (Antifragile, Fooled by Randomness, The Black Swan) and Rolf Dobelli's The Art of Thinking Clearly.
## Key Perspectives
- **Taleb's framework**: Events as they relate to randomness, black swans, and antifragility
- **Dobelli's lens**: Cognitive biases that distort perception of events
- **Universal applicability**: A shortcut for pattern recognition across domains
