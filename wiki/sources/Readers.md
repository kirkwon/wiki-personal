# Readers

Readers is a mental model that serves as a conceptual tool for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition derived from reading. It functions as a mental shortcut for making sense of complex situations.

This source references three key authors:
- [[how-to-take-smart-notes---snke-ahrens]] – Sönke Ahrens on smart note-taking
- [[the-4-hour-workweek-timothy-ferriss]] – Timothy Ferriss on time optimization
- [[the-para-method-tiago-forte]] – Tiago Forte on digital life organization (PARA Method)

The model is applicable universally across general domains.
