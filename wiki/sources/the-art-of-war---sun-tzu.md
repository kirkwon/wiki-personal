# The Art of War - Sun Tzu

Source summary for wiki ingestion. Classic strategic text by Sun Tzu covering 13 chapters on planning, terrain, tactics, intelligence, and adaptability.

## Key Chapters
1. Laying Plans - Careful planning and situation assessment
2. Waging War - Costs of war, rapid victory importance
3. Attack by Stratagem - Defeating enemy without fighting
4. Tactical Dispositions - Positioning and defensive strength
5. Energy - Forces like flowing water
6. Weak Points and Strong - Attacking weakness, protecting strengths
7. Maneuvering - Movement and positioning challenges
8. Variation in Tactics - Adaptability over rigid rules
9. The Army on the March - Terrain navigation and enemy reading
10. Terrain - Terrain classification and navigation
11. The Nine Situations - Strategic situation responses
12. The Attack by Fire - Timing and fire as weapon
13. The Use of Spies - Intelligence and espionage importance

## Core Concepts
- Strategic assessment frameworks (five factors, seven calculations)
- Terrain analysis
- Attack by stratagem
- Energy and momentum
- Weak point identification
- Intelligence and espionage
- Adaptability
- Deception and misdirection
- Positioning and advantage creation
- Speed and timing optimization
- Flexibility and adaptability
- Strategic alliance formation

## When to Use
- Facing competitive situations (business, sports, negotiations)
- Planning complex projects or campaigns
- Leadership or management positions
- Dealing with conflict or opposition
- Outmaneuvering stronger opponents
- Strategic planning required
- Assessing competitive landscapes
- Under-resourced or at disadvantage
- High-stakes decisions
- Understanding opponents or competitors

## When to Stop
- Strategy becomes manipulation or unethical
- Overthinking simple situations
- Using strategic thinking to justify aggression
- War metaphor inappropriate for cooperative contexts
- Sacrificing ethics for victory
- Analysis paralysis prevents action
- Treating every situation as zero-sum conflict
- Missing win-win opportunities
- Becoming paranoid about opponents
