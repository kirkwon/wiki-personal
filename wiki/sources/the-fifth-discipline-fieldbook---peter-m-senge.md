# The Fifth Discipline Fieldbook - Peter M. Senge

Source summary: Peter M. Senge's 2024 companion volume to *The Fifth Discipline*, presenting practical methods for building learning organizations through five core disciplines: personal mastery, mental models, shared vision, team learning, and systems thinking. The fieldbook emphasizes holistic, interconnected approaches to organizational learning over isolated problem-solving.

## Key Concepts
- Systems thinking as a holistic approach to complex problems
- The Three Pillars of Learning: personal mastery, mental models, shared vision
- The Five Disciplines Framework as building blocks for systemic perspective
- Building shared vision through dialogue and collaborative visioning
- The power of dialogue for team learning and constructive conflict resolution
- Team learning through collective reflection, inquiry, and experimentation
- The systems perspective focusing on interconnections between variables
