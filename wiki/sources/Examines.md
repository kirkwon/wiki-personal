# Examines.md Source Summary

This source introduces the "Examines" mental model — a conceptual tool for understanding and navigating complex situations by identifying patterns and relationships. It draws cross-source perspectives from Nate Silver's *The Signal and the Noise* (prediction and data interpretation) and Dan Ariely's *Predictably Irrational* (behavioral economics and irrational decision-making). The model is positioned as universally applicable across domains with moderate confidence (0.7).
