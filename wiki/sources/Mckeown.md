# Mckeown Source

## Summary

This source introduces Greg McKeown's mental model framework, drawing from his two major books: *Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less* and *Effortless*. The core idea is a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition—essentially a mental shortcut for discerning what is essential and eliminating the non-essential.

## Key Concepts

- **Essentialism** – Disciplined pursuit of less; identifying what truly matters and eliminating the rest.
- **Effortlessness** – Performing activities aligned with strengths and eliminating unnecessary friction.
- **Mckeown mental model** – Universal tool for making sense of complex situations through disciplined prioritization.
