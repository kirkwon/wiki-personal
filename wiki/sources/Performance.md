# Performance Source Summary

This source introduces "Performance" as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations by recognizing patterns and relationships. It draws on David Epstein's *The Sports Gene* to support the idea that performance outcomes emerge from multifactorial interactions (genetics, environment, training, opportunity) rather than single explanations like innate talent or pure effort. Confidence level is moderate (0.7).
