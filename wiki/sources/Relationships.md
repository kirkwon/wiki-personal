# Relationships Source

This source introduces "Relationships" as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It cross-references perspectives from Ray Dalio's Principles, Stephen R. Covey's The 8th Habit, Ryan Holiday's Ego Is the Enemy, Scott Galloway's The Algebra of Happiness, Mark Manson's Everything Is F*cked and The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck, and David Allen's Making It All Work.

The model is positioned as a meta-framework connecting these disparate works on effectiveness, ego, happiness, and principles, suggesting that recognizing patterns and relationships—whether interpersonal, systemic, or conceptual—is a fundamental navigational shortcut.
