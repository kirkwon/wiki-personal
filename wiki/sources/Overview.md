# Overview (Mental Model Source)

This source introduces the "Overview" mental model, a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations by recognizing patterns and relationships. It is referenced in Donella H. Meadows' *Limits to Growth*, a seminal work on system dynamics and complex systems modeling. The model functions as a meta-framework applicable across all domains.

## Key Points
- Overview provides a mental shortcut for making sense of complexity
- It focuses on seeing patterns and relationships rather than just summarizing
- Confidence level: 0.7, indicating it is a general heuristic
- Applicability: universal
- Cross-referenced with Limits to Growth by Donella H. Meadows
