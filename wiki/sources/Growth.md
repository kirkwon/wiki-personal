# Growth - Source Summary

Growth is presented as a universal mental model — a conceptual tool for pattern recognition and navigating complexity across all domains. The source synthesizes perspectives from 14 diverse sources including Donella Meadows' Limits to Growth, Taleb's Antifragile, Ryan Holiday's The Obstacle Is the Way, Carol Dweck's Mindset and Self-Theories, Ray Dalio's economic frameworks, Simon Sinek's Infinite Game, Mark Manson's Will and The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck, Greg McKeown's Multipliers, and Charles Duhigg's Smarter Faster Better.

The model aggregates distinct concepts — growth mindset, antifragility, limits to growth, infinite play, and overcoming adversity — into a single umbrella framework with moderate confidence (0.7). Key tension exists between unlimited growth (Sinek, Dalio) and constrained growth (Meadows, Taleb).
