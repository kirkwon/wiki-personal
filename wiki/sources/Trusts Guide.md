### Key Concepts
- **Asset Protection** — Shielding assets from creditors and lawsuits; central throughout
- **Generation-Skipping Tax (GST)** — Tax planning across generations; discussed in dynasty and wealth transfer sections
- **Grantor Trust Status** — Income tax treatment where grantor is treated as owner; key for IDGT and GRAT
- **Spendthrift Provisions** — Creditor protection via trustee discretion over distributions
- **Fraudulent Transfer Rules** — Legal constraints on transfers made while insolvent; relevant to APT planning
- **Look-Back Period (Medicaid)** — 5-year window for transfer analysis; central to Medicaid planning
- **IRS Section 7520 Rate** — Applicable federal rate for GRAT/QPRT calculations
- **Perpetual/Revocable Trust Duration** — Legal ability of trusts to last indefinitely; dynasty trust concept
- **Crummey Powers** — Annual exclusion gift mechanism for trusts
- **Cy Pres Doctrine** — Modification of charitable trusts when original purpose becomes impossible
- **Directed Trust** — Separating investment and distribution trustee roles
- **Letter of Wishes** — Non-binding guidance to trustees
