# Adrift - Scott Galloway

Scott Galloway's *Adrift* examines how social and economic systems drift without clear direction, creating structural challenges that affect individuals, communities, and nations. The book analyzes the forces shaping modern society and the lack of intentional steering in key institutions.^[raw/articles/Society.md]

## Main Arguments & Findings
- Modern capitalism has drifted: consumerism, shareholder primacy, and profit obsession have caused environmental harm, widened inequality, and eroded well-being.
- Companies must redefine success beyond shareholder returns — embracing employee well-being, sustainability, and meaningful CSR.
- Technology has created a digital divide that compounds social stratification.
- Galloway proposes rethinking corporate metrics and promoting sustainable business practices as solutions.
- Economic and social drift creates structural challenges that compound over time, and individuals must navigate these forces to make better decisions.^[raw/articles/Society.md]
