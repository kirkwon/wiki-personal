# Cross-training in different skills or disciplines

Source summary for the skill of cross-training across disciplines, drawing primarily on David Epstein's work on Range.

This source introduces the practice of systematically improving efficiency by identifying and applying methods, techniques, and frameworks across different skills and disciplines. The skill involves auditing workflows, restructuring approaches, iterating based on measurement, and scaling what works.

## Key Claims
- Cross-training improves productivity by transferring methods from one domain to another.
- Broad cross-disciplinary experience leads to better problem-solving than narrow specialization.
- The skill applies to personal productivity, team coordination, and accelerated learning.
