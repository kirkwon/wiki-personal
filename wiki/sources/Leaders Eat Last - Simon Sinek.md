# Leaders Eat Last - Simon Sinek

## Summary
Simon Sinek's *Leaders Eat Last* argues that organizations thrive when leaders prioritize people over profit. The book introduces the **Triple Bottom Line** framework—balancing people, profit, and purpose—and emphasizes that creating a sense of belonging, building trust through service, and designing environments that foster collaboration lead to superior organizational outcomes. Sinek uses examples from Southwest Airlines, Costco, and Starbucks to illustrate how service-first cultures outperform profit-first ones.

## Core Thesis
Leaders who serve their teams first—prioritizing employees over customers and competitors—create cultures of trust, belonging, and high performance. This service-first approach paradoxically leads to better customer outcomes and sustainable profit.
