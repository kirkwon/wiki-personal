# Find Your Why - Simon Sinek

Simon Sinek's *Find Your Why* (2024) argues that discovering one's deep purpose—the "Why"—is the foundation for effective leadership, personal fulfillment, and organizational success. The book introduces the Golden Circle framework (Why → How → What) and uses Apple's journey under Steve Jobs as a primary case study to illustrate how starting with purpose rather than features creates lasting impact. Sinek emphasizes that good intentions without a clear Why degenerate into mere marketing, and that true leadership prioritizes people and purpose over profits and features. The book also shares Sinek's personal journey toward understanding his own purpose and offers a framework of three essential questions (What, How, Why) for personal growth and career development.

This work is referenced in the [[purpose]] mental model as a key perspective on purpose as a navigational framework, exploring the foundational role of "why" in motivation and organizational direction.

Key chapters cover the power of purpose, Apple's inside-out story, the tragedy of good intentions, finding one's personal legend, and why leadership matters now more than ever. The book connects to broader themes of purpose-driven decision-making, team dynamics, and authentic personal branding.
