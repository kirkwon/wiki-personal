# Grit

Grit is framed as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for understanding and navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It serves as a mental shortcut for making sense of the world.

This source connects to Angela Duckworth's research on passion and perseverance as sustained effort toward long-term goals. The model is applicable across domains with moderate confidence (0.7), reflecting some uncertainty about its universal scope.
