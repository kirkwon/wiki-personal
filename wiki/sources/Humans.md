This source introduces the "Humans" mental model, a conceptual tool for understanding and navigating complex situations through human-centered pattern recognition. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

Cross-referenced with Daniel Kahneman's Thinking Fast and Slow, which describes dual-process theory (System 1/System 2) as a way humans naturally process information.
