# Post Corona — Scott Galloway

Scott Galloway's *Post Corona* analyzes how the pandemic reshaped markets, platforms, and power structures. Key themes include platform dominance, the accelerating role of technology, and strategic positioning during disruption. Connects to strategies mental model through recognition of structural shifts and opportunity mapping.
