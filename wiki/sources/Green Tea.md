# Green Tea (绿茶 - Lǚ Chá)

Source summary for the Green Tea reference page covering processing methods, major varieties, brewing styles, health benefits, and cultural significance.

## Key Topics

- **Processing**: Fixation (杀青) methods differ between China (stir-frying at 100-150°C) and Japan (steaming at 80-100°C), creating distinct flavor profiles.
- **Major varieties**: Dragon Well (龙井), Biluochun (碧螺春), Gunpowder (珠茶), Pi Lo Chun (白毫银针), Sencha, Gyokuro, Matcha, Hojicha, Vietnamese and Indian green teas.
- **Brewing styles**: Western (160-180°F, 2-3 min), Japanese (175-185°F for sencha, 140-160°F for gyokuro, matcha whisked), Gongfu (multiple short infusions).
- **Health compounds**: EGCG, catechins, L-theanine — antioxidant, heart health, cognitive benefits, though evidence is largely associative.
- **Storage**: Airtight, cool (50-70°F), dark, dry; loose leaf shelf life 6-12 months.

## Defining Characteristics

- Minimal oxidation (0-10%) — defining characteristic of green tea
- Fixation (杀青) — heat-treatment process to prevent oxidation; core technique
- Flavor spectrum: vegetal, marine, nutty, umami

## Key Entities

- **Dragon Well (龙井)** — Chinese green tea variety, Hangzhou origin
- **Sencha, Gyokuro, Matcha, Hojicha** — Japanese green tea varieties
- **Biluochun, Gunpowder, Pi Lo Chun** — Chinese green tea varieties
- **Catechins, EGCG, L-theanine** — Bioactive compounds supporting health claims

## Health Claims & Evidence

- EGCG, catechins, and L-theanine associated with antioxidant activity, cardiovascular health, and cognitive benefits
- ORAC, free radical scavenging, and cancer prevention claims referenced, though evidence for cancer prevention is mixed and preliminary
- Overall health evidence is largely associative rather than deeply sourced
