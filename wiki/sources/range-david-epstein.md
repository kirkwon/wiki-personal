# Range - David Epstein

*Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World* by David Epstein explores how broad experience and interdisciplinary thinking outperform narrow specialization in many domains. The book argues that generalists are better equipped to navigate complexity, make novel connections, and adapt to uncertain environments.

## Key Themes

- The power of breadth over depth in complex, unpredictable situations
- How diverse experience enhances problem-solving and creativity
- The limits of early specialization in rapidly changing fields
