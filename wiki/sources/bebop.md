# Bebop

## Quick Reference

**Field**: Music Theory & Jazz
**Type**: Jazz Style/Movement
**Era**: 1940s-1950s
**Key Innovation**: Fast tempos, complex harmonies, virtuosic improvisation
**Pioneers**: Charlie Parker, Dizzy Gillespie, Thelonious Monk, Bud Powell
**Classic Lineup**: Trumpet, saxophone, piano, bass, drums

## Summary

Bebop revolutionized jazz in the 1940s, shifting from dance-oriented big bands to virtuosic small ensembles focused on complex improvisation. It introduced sophisticated harmonic concepts that became the foundation of modern jazz.

## Definition

**Bebop** (or "bop") is a style of jazz characterized by:
- Fast tempos
- Complex chord progressions
- Virtuosic instrumental technique
- Improvisation based on chord changes rather than melodies
- Small ensembles (quintet/sextet vs big band)

## The Classic Bebop Quintet

The definitive bebop ensemble configuration:
- Trumpet
- Saxophone
- Piano
- Bass
- Drums
