# Indistractable - Nir Eyal

Nir Eyal's 2024 book *Indistractable* reframes distraction as a psychological problem rooted in internal discomfort rather than an external technology problem. The core framework introduces the Indistractable Model — a four-phase cycle of internal triggers, actions, rewards, and the distinction between traction (productive time) and distraction. Eyal argues that blocking notifications is insufficient; the real work is learning to tolerate discomfort, reimagining internal triggers, and designing commitment devices (effort pacts, price pacts, identity pacts) to protect attention. The book also addresses stakeholder synchronization, environment design, and warns against productivity perfectionism becoming its own form of distraction.
