# Lateral Thinking - Edward de Bono

Edward de Bono's *Lateral Thinking: Creativity Step by Step* introduces techniques for breaking out of established thinking patterns. The book provides practical methods for reframing problems and generating novel solutions, complementing the Fields model's emphasis on cross-domain pattern recognition.

## Key Themes
- Breaking established thinking patterns
- Reframing problems to reveal new solutions
- Creativity as a learnable skill
