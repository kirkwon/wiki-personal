# Perseverance

Perseverance is defined as a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

This mental model is referenced in Angela Duckworth's *Grit: The Power of Passion and Perseverance*, where perseverance is presented as a universal mental shortcut applicable across all domains with moderate confidence (0.7).
