# The Power of Habit - Charles Duhigg

## Overview

*The Power of Habit* by Charles Duhigg is a book summarizing habit science across individuals, organizations, and societies. The core framework is the habit loop: cue, routine, reward. Habits can be deliberately rewired by targeting the routine while preserving the cue and reward. Keystone habits create cascading positive change across life domains.

## Key Concepts

- **Habit Loop**: cue → routine → reward — the automatic cycle that drives habitual behavior
- **Craving Brain**: neuroscience of how cravings encode habits in the brain
- **Habit Change Golden Rule**: keep cue and reward, substitute routine
- **Keystone Habits**: habits that catalyze change in other areas of life
- **Habit Stacking**: linking new habits to existing ones
- **Implementation Intentions**: pre-commitment strategies for behavior change

## Practical Tools

- Habit loop analysis (cue, routine, reward)
- Cue identification techniques
- Routine experimentation and substitution
- Reward mapping and craving analysis
- Habit stacking
- Keystone habit identification
- Implementation intentions
- Habit tracking and monitoring
- Environmental design for habit support
- Community and social proof for habit reinforcement

## When to Stop

- When habit tracking becomes obsession
- When judging yourself for every missed day
- When habits become rigid and prevent flexibility
- When changing too many habits at once
- When using habits to avoid underlying issues
- When focusing on habits to the detriment of values
- When forcing habits that don't align with personality
- When using guilt and shame rather than support
- When becoming rigid about optimal habit timing
