# Theory Source

Theory is defined as a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

This source is cross-referenced with three key works:
- Richard H. Thaler's *Misbehaving*
- Carol S. Dweck's *Self-Theories: Their Role in Motivation, Personality, and Development*
- Daniel Kahneman's *Thinking, Fast and Slow*

Each of these works exemplifies how theories serve as frameworks for simplifying complexity, though they can also mislead when treated as absolute truths rather than tools.
