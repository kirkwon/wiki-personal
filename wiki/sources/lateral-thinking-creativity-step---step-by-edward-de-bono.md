# Lateral Thinking: Creativity Step by Step by Edward de Bono

Edward de Bono's *Lateral Thinking: Creativity Step by Step* (2024) is a prescriptive guide to creative thinking that treats creativity as a trainable skill rather than an innate trait. The book presents a systematized approach to breaking out of conventional thought patterns through specific techniques and habits.

Key chapters cover the distinction between problem finding and problem solving, breaking barriers to thinking through techniques like "zooming out," the Six Thinking Hats method for structured group deliberation, lateral movement techniques ("what if," "wonder why," "undo what you did"), the power of metaphors and analogies, the influence of language on thinking, and the cultivation of habits like maintaining a Creative Log. The book emphasizes that effective creativity requires both identifying the right question and executing answers.
