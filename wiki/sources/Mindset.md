# Mindset Source Summary

Mindset is defined as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It serves as a mental shortcut for making sense of the world.

The source draws on two key references: Carol S. Dweck's psychological research on fixed versus growth mindsets, and Greg McKeown's leadership framework from *Multipliers*, which distinguishes between leaders who multiply intelligence and those who diminish it.

## Key Concepts
- Mindset as a universal mental model applicable across all domains
- Fixed vs. growth mindset (Dweck)
- Multiplier vs. diminisher leadership (McKeown)
