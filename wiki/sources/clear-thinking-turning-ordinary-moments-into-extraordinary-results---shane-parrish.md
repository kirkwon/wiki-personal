# Clear Thinking: Turning Ordinary Moments into Extraordinary Results

Shane Parrish's 2024 book presents a practical framework for developing clear thinking as a learnable skill. The book is organized around ten chapters covering clarity, perspective, uncertainty, habits, focus, information overload, questioning, listening, emotional management, and resilience.

Core claim: clear thinking transforms ordinary moments into extraordinary results through deliberate practices. The book synthesizes established principles from cognitive science, decision-making, and mindfulness without offering novel research or distinctive frameworks beyond what exists in the broader literature on biases, habits, and emotional regulation.
