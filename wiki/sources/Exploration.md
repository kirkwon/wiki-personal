# Exploration

Exploration is a mental model positioned as a universal conceptual tool for navigating complex situations by recognizing patterns and relationships. The model draws on cross-disciplinary perspectives from five authors, suggesting exploration as a cross-domain insight.

The page identifies five source references converging on exploration as a theme:
- Sönke Ahrens — Experiment and Exploration: Forms of World-Disclosure
- David Epstein — The Sports Gene
- Dan Ariely — The Honest Truth About Dishonesty
- Brian Christian & Tom Griffiths — The Most Human Human
- Barry Schwartz — Why We Work

Key angles include world-disclosure as a form of revealing reality, generalist exploration versus specialization, dishonesty as cognitive boundary exploration, authenticity in the face of AI, and motivation beyond compensation. The model functions as a mental shortcut for making sense of complexity.
## Cross-Source Perspectives

The model synthesizes perspectives from cognitive science, behavioral economics, and decision-making research, positioning exploration as both a personal strategy and a framework for understanding how people interact with uncertainty.
