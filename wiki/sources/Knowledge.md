# Knowledge

Knowledge is positioned as a universal conceptual tool for navigating complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

Cross-source perspectives include Sönke Ahrens on world disclosure through note-taking, David Epstein on the value of diverse knowledge and breadth of experience, and David Deutsch on knowledge as a fundamental creative capacity that can be created and grows without limit.
