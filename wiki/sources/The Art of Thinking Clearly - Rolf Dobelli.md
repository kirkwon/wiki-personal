# The Art of Thinking Clearly - Rolf Dobelli

## Overview
A comprehensive catalog of roughly 100 cognitive biases and mental errors that distort human thinking. The book organizes biases into categories such as Tendency, Decisions, Events, Others, Information, Outcomes, Judgments, and Success. Each bias is presented as a named chapter with illustrative examples and historical anecdotes.

## When to Stop
- When analysis paralysis prevents necessary action
- When debiasing becomes more costly than the bias itself
- When fast, intuitive decisions are required and time is critical
- When relationships and social contexts require going with the flow
- When over-correcting and missing opportunities through excessive caution
- When acknowledging uncertainty is more valuable than false precision
- When cognitive load of debiasing exceeds decision stakes
- When developing analysis anxiety and fear of making any decision

## Key Insights
Human cognition is riddled with systematic biases that distort perception, judgment, and decision-making. Awareness and structured countermeasures can improve thinking. The book emphasizes that debiasing should not override situations requiring quick action, and that analysis paralysis is itself a risk.
