# Problems Source

This source documents "Problems" as a universal mental model for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition. It draws on three intellectual traditions: John Gall's systems thinking from *The Systems Bible*, James Surowiecki's collective intelligence from *The Wisdom of Crowds*, and Peter M. Senge's organizational learning from *The Fifth Discipline Fieldbook*.

## Cross-Source Perspectives
- **John Gall** – Systems awareness and the nature of complex systems
- **James Surowiecki** – Crowd wisdom and collective intelligence
- **Peter M. Senge** – Learning organizations and systems thinking

## Key Takeaway
Problems functions as a conceptual lens rather than a problem-solving process, providing a mental shortcut for recognizing patterns and relationships across domains.
