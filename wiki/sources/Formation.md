# Formation Source

Formation is presented as a universal mental model for navigating complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world. The concept is cross-referenced to James Clear's *Atomic Habits*, though the specific mapping between Formation and Clear's concepts (identity-based habits, systems over goals, environment design, compounding effects) is not detailed in the source material.

Confidence in this model is set to 0.7, suggesting moderate uncertainty about its distinctiveness from other universal mental models already in the wiki.
