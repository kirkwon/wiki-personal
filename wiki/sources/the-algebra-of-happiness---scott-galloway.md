# The Algebra of Happiness - Scott Galloway

Scott Galloway's 2024 book reframes the pursuit of happiness through strategic and psychological lenses. Rather than relying on traditional metrics like wealth or status, Galloway argues that fulfillment emerges from purpose, authentic relationships, personal branding, and holistic self-investment. The book's central analogy—**CV = XCV**—treats life decisions as a competitive strategy, where choices (CV) produce outcomes (XCV) analogous to a company's market position.

Key chapters include:
- **The Science of Joy**: Psychological research on biological and social drivers of happiness.
- **The Game of Success**: Understanding strengths and weaknesses to achieve goals.
- **Leveraging Brand Power for Happiness**: Personal branding for identity, self-esteem, and social connection.
- **Investment Strategies for Life Satisfaction**: Long-term financial investments aligned with personal values.
- **Embracing Failure for Personal Growth**: Failure as a catalyst for growth rather than a setback.
- **CV = XCV**: Life choices mapped to competitive strategy outcomes.
- **Balancing Ambition with Well-being**: Integrating wellness into ambitious goals for true happiness.
