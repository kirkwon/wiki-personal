# Post Corona: From Crisis to Opportunity - Scott Galloway

Scott Galloway's *Post Corona* analyzes the post-pandemic landscape, arguing that the crisis accelerated trends already underway: platform dominance, remote work, digital transformation, and the consolidation of power among a small number of technology titans.

## Key Themes

- The pandemic permanently shifted consumer and business behavior toward digital platforms.
- Companies that controlled essential infrastructure (Amazon, Apple, Google, Meta) emerged even stronger.
- The "Four" framework from *The Four* remains the dominant lens for understanding market power.
- New opportunities exist for those who understand bundling, brand power, and distribution control.
