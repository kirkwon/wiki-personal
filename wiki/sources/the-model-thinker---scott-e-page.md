
# The Model Thinker - Scott E. Page

A 2024 book by Scott E. Page covering 27 chapters across 6 parts, introducing a wide range of modeling frameworks for understanding complex phenomena. The central thesis is that using multiple models in concert outperforms relying on any single framework.

## Core Themes

- **The Many Model Thinker**: Using diverse models provides complementary perspectives and reduces blind spots.
- **Model Selection**: Choosing appropriate models for a given situation; not all models fit all problems.
- **Model Combination**: Integrating insights from multiple models for richer understanding.
- **Simple models are powerful**: Linear models, normal distributions, and basic game theory deliver disproportionate insight when applied appropriately.
- **Complexity emerges from simplicity**: Agent-based models and cellular automata show how complex collective behavior arises from simple individual rules.
- **Model Validation**: Testing models against reality to avoid overfitting or bias confirmation.

## Key Model Types Covered

Linear Models, Power Law Models, Normal Distribution Models, Network Models, Game Theory Models, Agent-Based Models, Markov Models, Cellular Automata Models, Systems Dynamics Models, Evolutionary Models, Chaos Models, Catastrophe Models, Difference/Differential Equation Models.
