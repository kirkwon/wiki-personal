# Ready for Anything - David Allen

Source document for David Allen's 2024 book *Ready for Anything*, which extends and reinforces his Getting Things Done (GTD) methodology. The book emphasizes present-moment focus, systematic task management, and building resilient habits through practical frameworks including habit tracking, time blocking, delegation, and mindfulness practices.

Key themes include the GTD workflow (Capture → Clarify → Organize → Reflect → Engage), the 2-minute rule for habit formation, time batching, delegation frameworks, and crucially, a "when to stop" section warning against productivity obsession, relationship sacrifice, and loss of joy.

Hermes skill integration covers triggers for productivity struggles, tools like habit tracking and environment design, timeline stages from immediate assessment to continuous refinement, and metrics including focus sessions, task completion rates, and stress levels.
