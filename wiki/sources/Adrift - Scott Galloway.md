# Adrift - Scott Galloway

Summary of Scott Galloway's book *Adrift*, which examines capitalism, consumerism, and their effects on society while proposing practical solutions.

## Core Themes
- Historical development of consumerism and its shaping of modern society
- The ideology behind capitalism, its role in driving innovation but also unsustainable practices
- Shift from serving customers and employees toward prioritizing shareholder interests
- Environmental degradation and widened social inequalities from relentless profit pursuit
- Redefining corporate success metrics beyond GDP and financial growth
- Digital divide and technology's deepening of social inequality
- Responsible capitalism and sustainable business practices
