# Algorithms to Live By - Brian Christian & Tom Griffiths

## Summary

*Algorithms to Live By* by Brian Christian and Tom Griffiths applies computer science algorithms to everyday human decision-making, exploring cognitive biases, heuristics, and decision-making limits. The book spans 14 chapters covering problem-solving strategies, chess as a decision metaphor, the minimax algorithm, game theory applications (Nash equilibria, zero-sum situations), search space optimization, branch and bound techniques, heuristics and instincts, evolutionary algorithms and personal growth, the algorithmic nature of intuition, Deep Blue and AI's influence on strategic thinking, algorithms in emotional life, AI as a decision-enhancement tool, the intersection of algorithms with spirituality and free will, and the relationship between effective decision-making and wellbeing.

## Key Concepts

- **The Art of Problem Solving** – Historical development of problem-solving strategies and cognitive biases in decision-making
- **Chess and Choices** – Chess as a metaphor for life's complex decisions, emphasizing pattern recognition, forward planning, and anticipating consequences
- **The Minimax Algorithm** – Strategy of minimizing maximum loss applied to competitive scenarios and trade-offs
- **Game Theory's Influence on Life** – Nash equilibria, zero-sum situations, cooperation, and conflict resolution applied to real life
- **The Search Space of Options** – Expanding options improves outcomes but risks cognitive overload
- **Branch and Bound** – Managing complex problems with many possible solutions through systematic pruning
- **The Heuristic of Instincts** – Heuristics in decision-making, instinctual responses, and potential pitfalls like cognitive biases
- **Evolutionary Algorithms and Personal Growth** – Genetic programming parallels to personal development, adaptation, and learning from experience
- **The Algorithmic Nature of Intuition** – Gut feelings as pattern-matching processes, sometimes leading closer to optimal solutions
- **Deep Blue and AI** – Artificial intelligence's influence on strategic thinking and decision-making implications
- **The Algorithmic Heart** – Algorithms shaping emotional lives, love, social media interactions, and interpersonal connections
- **AI as Decision-Enhancement Tool** – Leveraging artificial intelligence to improve decision-making in education, healthcare, and workplace
- **The Algorithmic Soul** – Intersection of algorithms with spirituality, predestination, free will, and self-improvement
- **Finding Joy in Algorithms** – Happiness and wellbeing as outcomes of effective decision-making

## Authors

Brian Christian and Tom Griffiths are the same authors behind *The Most Human Human*, which explores the limits of computation and what defines human intelligence.
