# Dishonesty

Source documenting the Dishonesty mental model, referencing Dan Ariely's *The Honest Truth About Dishonesty*. The model frames dishonesty as a universal mental shortcut for understanding how people self-deceive, rationalize dishonest behavior, and maintain a positive self-concept while cheating in small ways.
