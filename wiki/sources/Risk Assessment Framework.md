# Risk Assessment Framework

A systematic investment risk management framework covering real estate, stocks, bonds, and portfolio-level risks. Combines quantitative metrics (VaR, Monte Carlo simulation, DSCR, LTV) with qualitative analysis (risk matrices, stress testing, scenario analysis) to identify, measure, and mitigate investment risks.

## Key Components

### Risk Identification
- Risk scoping, brainstorming, and documentation via risk registers
- Probability and impact assessment scales
- Risk matrix prioritization (Critical, High, Medium, Low, Lowest)
- Property-level risk assessment: location scoring, condition indicators, tenant risk scoring
- Financial risk assessment: leverage risk, cash flow stress testing

### Real Estate-Specific Analysis
- Location scoring, property condition, market cycles
- Leverage metrics: LTV, DSCR
- Cash flow stress testing
- **Specific thresholds**:
  - LTV < 60% = low risk
  - DSCR > 1.5 = low risk
  - Vacancy > 10% = high risk
  - Operating expenses > 50% of gross rent = medium risk

### Portfolio-Level Concerns
- Geographic concentration
- Property type allocation and size distribution
- Market cycle positioning
- Concentration risk across asset classes

### Mitigation Strategies
- Diversification strategies
- Hedging strategies (interest rate swaps, caps, collars, currency, commodity)
- Insurance coverage (property, liability, umbrella, rent guarantee, business interruption)
- Contractual risk transfer
- Entity structure (LLC, Corporation, REIT, Partnership)

### Advanced Techniques
- Stress testing (base/down/up cases)
- Value at Risk (VaR) analysis
- Monte Carlo simulation
- Scenario analysis

## Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
Monitoring metrics including DSCR, LTV, cash flow variance, vacancy rates, rent growth, tenant turnover, and economic indicators. Assessment frequency ranges from daily (cash flow) to annual (comprehensive review). Regular assessment schedules and trigger-based reviews are used.

## Stress Testing
Running downside/upside scenarios on portfolios and cash flow — including 20% rent decline, 10% expense increase, vacancy spikes, interest rate hikes, and extended cash flow declines. Related to [[scenario-planning]] and [[monte-carlo-simulations]].

## Risk Register and Risk Dashboard
Documented inventory of identified risks with mitigation strategies and portfolio risk dashboards tracking aggregate risk levels, trends, and action items by category.
