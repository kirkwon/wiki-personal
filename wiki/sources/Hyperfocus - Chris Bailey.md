# Hyperfocus - Chris Bailey

Source summary for Chris Bailey's *Hyperfocus*, a 2024 book on attention management. The book introduces two complementary attention modes: hyperfocus (deep concentration) and scatterfocus (mind-wandering). Key concepts include attention residue from task-switching, attention budgeting as a finite resource, peak attention hours, and single-tasking advantages. The book provides frameworks for cultivating both focus and unfocused time, with practical tools like energy mapping, environment design, and mindfulness practices. It also warns against hyperfocus becoming an obsession or rigidity.
