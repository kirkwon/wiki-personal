# Find Your Why - Simon Sinek

## Overview
Find Your Why is a book by Simon Sinek that guides readers through discovering their deep, motivating purpose—what he calls their "Why." The central claim is that starting with a clear sense of purpose is what separates inspiring leaders and organizations from those that merely execute. Understanding your Why influences others to take action and is essential for personal fulfillment and effective leadership.

## Key Concepts
- **Golden Circle**: The What/How/Why framework where Why is the innermost, core driver of behavior and influence.
- **Purpose (Why)**: The deep, motivating reason behind actions—the innermost layer of the Golden Circle.
- **Personal Legend / Calling**: A deep-seated sense of purpose that drives lifelong action.
- **Leadership through purpose**: Leading by articulating and living one's Why rather than by authority or features.

## Key Takeaways
- Sinek emphasizes the importance of purpose in every individual's life and how it can be harnessed for personal and professional development.
- Understanding your Why forms the core message that can influence others to take action.
- A clear purpose prevents good intentions from becoming just another marketing message.
- Sharing your purpose with others creates a more connected world.
