# The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F_ck - Mark Manson

Mark Manson's core book on values, suffering, and prioritization. The central thesis is that choosing what to care about—selective concern—is essential to reducing unnecessary suffering and living a meaningful life.

Key themes include the importance of discomfort, rejecting the positivity culture of endless optimism, and developing self-awareness about one's values and motivations.
