# Role

Role is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This mental model is referenced across five source works:

- **Edward de Bono** — [[Six Thinking Hats - Edward de Bono]]: Role-playing perspective-taking through wearing different hats to see problems from multiple angles.
- **Ryan Holiday** — [[ego-is-the-enemy---ryan-holiday]]: Ego and identity attachment to roles distorts judgment.
- **Scott Galloway** — [[the-algebra-of-happiness---scott-galloway]]: Life-role optimization — how the roles you inhabit (parent, professional, partner) drive happiness.
- **Robert B. Cialdini** — [[influence-the-psychology-of-persuasion-robert-b-cialdini]]: Social influence through roles — authority, reciprocity, and social proof amplified by role-based expectations.
- **Annie Duke** — [[quit-the-power-of-knowing-when-to-walk-away---annie-duke]]: Quitting as role redefinition — recognizing when a role no longer serves you and having the courage to exit.
