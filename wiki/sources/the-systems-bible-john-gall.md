# The Systems Bible - John Gall

Source referenced in the Implications mental model. John Gall's *The Systems Bible* is a foundational text on systems thinking that explores how complex systems behave, fail, and interact, and examines what can be inferred about their inner workings from observable outcomes. It contributes to the [[problems-mental-model]] by providing a framework for systems awareness and pattern recognition.

## Key Ideas
- Systems exhibit behaviors that are often counterintuitive
- Understanding patterns of interaction is more important than understanding individual components
- Problems in complex systems are best navigated through awareness of systemic relationships
