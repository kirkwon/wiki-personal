# The Index Card Rules

A financial framework arguing that all essential personal finance advice can be distilled into 10 simple, actionable rules that fit on an index card. The philosophy emphasizes simplicity, automation, low costs, and broad diversification over complexity and stock picking.

## The 10 Rules

1. **Save 10-20% of your income** — Automate savings, start small (1%), increase gradually. Prioritize emergency fund, then employer 401(k) match, Roth IRA, remaining 401(k), taxable accounts.
2. **Pay off credit cards in full every month** — Credit card debt carries 18-25%+ interest. Automate full balance payments. Use 0% APR transfer cards if carrying debt.
3. **Max out tax-advantaged accounts** — 401(k)/403(b) up to $23,000 (2024) + match, Roth IRA up to $7,000, HSA up to $4,150, 529 plans. Priority: employer match → HSA → Roth IRA → remaining 401(k).
4. **Never buy individual stocks** — Picking winners is extremely difficult. Buy low-cost index funds instead for instant diversification.
5. **Buy low-cost index funds** — VTSAX (0.04%), VFIAX (0.04%), FXAIX (0.015%), SWTSX (0.03%). Over 30 years, a 1% fee costs 25%+ of final balance vs 0.04%.
6. **Rebalance portfolio annually** — Maintain age-appropriate allocation: 20s (90/10 stocks/bonds), 30s (80/20), 40s (70/30), 50s (60/40), 60s (50/50). Target-date funds auto-rebalance.
7. **Avoid financial products that sound too good to be true** — Red flags: high returns with low risk, complex fees, pressure to buy now. Avoid whole life insurance, private placements, leveraged ETFs, most hedge funds.
8. **Buy a home you can afford** — Max 2-3x annual income, housing cost < 28-30% of gross income. Include mortgage, taxes, insurance, maintenance. Rent if moving within 7 years.
9. **Insurance: protect against catastrophe** — Essential: health, disability, auto, home/renters, term life (10-12x income, until kids grown or retirement). Skip extended warranties, credit card insurance, flight insurance.
10. **Create a will and advance directives** — Will, power of attorney, healthcare proxy, living will. Update at marriage/divorce, having children, major asset changes, every 5 years. Include digital assets.

## Key Principles

- Simplicity beats complexity — 10 rules > 1000 books
- Automate everything — removes willpower from the equation
- Index funds work — stop trying to beat the market
- Fees matter — low costs = higher returns; fee drag (1% vs 0.04% over 30 years costs 25%+ of final balance)
- Boring is beautiful — reliability beats excitement
- Time is your ally — start early, stay consistent
- Prioritization hierarchy guides account selection
- Month-by-month implementation strategy for adoption

## Implementation Timeline

- **Month 1**: Automate savings (1%), pay off credit cards, open high-yield savings, track spending
- **Month 2**: Get 401(k) match, review insurance, create will, build $1,000 emergency fund
- **Month 3-6**: Increase savings to 10%, open Roth IRA, buy first index fund, build 3-month emergency fund
- **Month 7-12**: Increase to 15-20%, max HSA, rebalance portfolio
