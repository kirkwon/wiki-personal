# Patients Mental Model Source

This source introduces "Patients" as a universal conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. The model is derived from Atul Gawande's book Complications: A Surgeon's Notes on an Imperfect Science, drawing on themes of patience, tolerance for uncertainty, and the need to stay with imperfect, evolving information rather than rushing to resolution.
