# Habits Source Summary

This source documents Habits as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It synthesizes insights from eight key sources spanning productivity, communication, and effectiveness literature.

## Key Sources Referenced
- **Atomic Habits** by James Clear — atomic habit stacking, identity-based change
- **Slow Productivity** by Cal Newport — deliberate, sustainable pace over hustle culture
- **Smarter Faster Better** by Charles Duhigg — mental models for productivity and motivation
- **Supercommunicators** by Charles Duhigg — habit loops in communication contexts
- **The 4-Hour Workweek** by Timothy Ferriss — lifestyle design and automation
- **Tools of Titans** by Timothy Ferriss — habits of high performers
- **The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People** by Stephen R. Covey — effectiveness habits
- **The 8th Habit** by Stephen R. Covey — moving from effectiveness to greatness

## Core Concept
Habits function as a mental shortcut for pattern recognition in complex situations, applicable across all domains. The model draws on cue-routine-reward loops, atomic habit stacking, slow productivity principles, and effectiveness frameworks.
