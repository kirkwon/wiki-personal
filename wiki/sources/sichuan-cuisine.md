## Core Argument
Sichuan cuisine is not simply "spicy" but a complex flavor system composed of **麻 (má)** — the numbing sensation from Sichuan peppercorns — **辣 (là)** — the heat from dried chilies — **香 (xiāng)** — the aromatic depth of fermented bean paste — and **咸 (xián)** — balanced seasoning. This má là profile defines the cuisine's sensory identity, distinct from other Chinese regional styles.

## Seven Flavors (七味)
川菜七种风味轮廓：麻辣、鱼香、红烧、姜毒/姜督、咸酥/咸鲜、清淡、酸辣。

## Key Ingredients
- **花椒 (Sichuan Peppercorns)** — [[sichuan-peppercorns-huajiao]]: delivers the characteristic numbing má sensation along with a citrus-like aroma.
- **豆瓣酱 (Doubanjiang / Fermented Broad Bean Paste)** — [[doubanjiang]]: regarded as the soul of Sichuan cooking, a mixture of fermented beans, chilies, and salt.
- **干辣椒 (Dried Chilies)**: provide heat and color to many dishes.
