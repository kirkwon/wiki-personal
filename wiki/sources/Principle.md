# Principle Source

This source documents "Principle" as a mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern recognition and relationship analysis. It cross-references David Deutsch's *The Beginning of Infinity*, Stephen R. Covey's *First Things First*, and Mark Manson's *The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck*.

## Summary
Principle is framed as a universal mental shortcut applicable across all domains. It provides a framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in complex situations. The model draws on three distinct perspectives: Deutsch's exploration of explanatory knowledge, Covey's prioritization and time management framework, and Manson's approach to values and personal philosophy.
