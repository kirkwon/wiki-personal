# Making It All Work - David Allen

David Allen's follow-up to Getting Things Done integrates purpose and workflow into productivity systems. This source is referenced in the Purpose mental model as a key perspective on connecting purpose to practical action and organization.

## Key Entities
- **David Allen** — Author, productivity and workflow management expert, creator of the GTD (Getting Things Done) methodology. Central role. Likely not yet in wiki (no Allen page exists in index).
- **GTD (Getting Things Done)** — Allen's productivity system. Mentioned as the foundational philosophy this book expands upon. Partially represented in wiki via [[time-management-matrix]] (Covey) and [[personal-knowledge-vault]] but no dedicated GTD page exists.

## Key Concepts
- **Reconciling inner life with outer responsibilities** — Balancing personal values, inner state, and external demands. Connects to [[balancing-ambition-with-well-being]] and [[personal-branding-and-wellbeing]].
- **Trust in managing workload** — Delegation and collaboration require trust. Relates to [[trust-fundamentals]] and [[absence-of-trust]] (Lencioni's team dysfunction).
- **Embracing uncertainty** — Letting go of control over details to enable creativity and better decisions. Connects to [[decision-making-under-uncertainty]], [[real-options-analysis]], and [[embracing-failure-for-personal-growth]].
- **Information overload management** — Prioritizing what matters amid abundance. Relates to [[categorizing-information]] and [[cognitive-biases-library]] (filtering relevance).
- **Work-life integration** — Using technology to merge rather than separate work and personal life. Relates to [[quadrant-2-focus]] and [[time-management-matrix]].
- **Finding purpose beyond work** — Personal passions, relationships, community. Connects to [[science-of-joy]] and [[balancing-ambition-with-well-being]].
- **Navigating change and transition** — Building resilience and redefining priorities. Relates to [[embracing-failure-for-personal-growth]] and [[growth-mindset]].

## Main Arguments & Findings
Allen's core claim: effective productivity requires integrating inner clarity (values, purpose, psychological state) with outer action (tasks, responsibilities, relationships), rather than treating them as separate domains. Key themes:
1. Trust (in self and others) is the foundation for delegation and workload management.
2. Embracing uncertainty enables creativity and prevents over-control.
3. Technology is a double-edged sword — useful for integration but also a source of distraction.
4. Purpose and meaning must extend beyond professional achievement.
5. Change requires active reframing of priorities rather than passive resistance.

Evidence is primarily anecdotal and prescriptive — consistent with Allen's broader GTD methodology. Strength is moderate: philosophical framework rather than empirical research.
