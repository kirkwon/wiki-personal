# Effortless - Greg McKeown

Source summary page for the book *Effortless* by Greg McKeown (2024).

## Core Message
Striving to do everything causes overwhelm, stress, reduced creativity, and strained relationships. A better approach is to identify priorities, say no, delegate, build sustainable habits, practice mindfulness and emotional intelligence (EQ), and seek balance across career, relationships, and self-care.

## Key Concepts
- **Effortless living** – Prioritizing what truly matters to reduce stress and overwhelm
- **Priorities** – Identifying personal values to guide decision-making
- **Saying no / boundaries** – Declining to protect energy and focus
- **Delegation** – Sharing tasks to focus on strengths
- **Sustainable habits** – Routines and rituals to minimize decision fatigue
- **Mindfulness** – Enhancing awareness to reduce distractions
- **Emotional intelligence (EQ)** – Managing emotions and empathizing for smoother interactions
- **Balanced living** – Harmony across career, relationships, and self-care

## When to Stop Optimizing
McKeown flags warning signs that optimization has gone too far: when it becomes obsession, sacrifices relationships, neglects health, destroys joy, measures the wrong things, becomes rigid, or is used to avoid deeper issues.
