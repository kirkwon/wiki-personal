# Memorization Source

This source introduces Memorization as a universal mental model applicable across all domains. It functions as a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. The source references Peter C. Brown et al.'s *Make It Stick* as a cross-source perspective on learning science principles including retrieval practice, spacing, interleaving, and elaboration.

The model positions memorization not as mere rote learning but as a framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.
