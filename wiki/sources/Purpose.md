# Purpose

Purpose is a mental model defined as a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

This model is referenced in two sources:
- [[find-your-why-simon-sinek]]
- [[making-it-all-work---david-allen]]

Purpose is positioned as a universal mental model applicable across all domains, with medium priority and moderate confidence (0.7). It connects to existing mental models including [[planning]], [[care]], [[obstacles]], [[Solutions]], [[stillness]], and [[Reality]].
