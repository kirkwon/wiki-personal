# Resilience.md Source Summary

This source introduces "Resilience" as a universal mental model for navigating complex situations through pattern recognition. It synthesizes three philosophical perspectives: Nassim Nicholas Taleb's antifragility, Ryan Holiday's Stoic daily practice from The Daily Stoic, and Mark Manson's dark hope from Everything Is F_cked. The model is positioned as a broader, more general framework distinct from antifragility.
