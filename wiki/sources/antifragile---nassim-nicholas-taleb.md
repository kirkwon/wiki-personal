# Antifragile - Nassim Nicholas Taleb

Taleb's concept of antifragility describes systems, individuals, and ideas that gain from disorder, volatility, and uncertainty rather than being merely resilient. This contrasts with fragile systems that break under stress.

## Key Themes
- Embracing uncertainty rather than eliminating it
- Antifragile systems benefit from volatility
- The difference between risk (measurable) and uncertainty (unmeasurable)
- Skin in the game and accountability
