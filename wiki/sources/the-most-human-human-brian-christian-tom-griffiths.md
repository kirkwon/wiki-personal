# The Most Human Human - Brian Christian Tom Griffiths

Brian Christian and Tom Griffiths' [[the-most-human-human---brian-christian-tom-griffiths]] explores what makes humans uniquely intelligent, particularly in the age of artificial intelligence. The book examines human qualities like empathy, creativity, and nuanced communication that distinguish human cognition from machine processing.

## Key Themes

- Human intelligence is characterized by qualities that go beyond computation: empathy, meaning-making, and emotional depth.
- The "most human human" demonstrates the full range of human cognitive and emotional capabilities.
- Understanding human uniqueness clarifies what intelligence means beyond mere pattern matching.

## Connection to Intelligence Model

This source provides the individual-human perspective on intelligence within the broader mental model, emphasizing what makes human pattern recognition and cognition distinctive. This connects to [[intelligence-as-a-mental-model]] and [[superforecasting]].
