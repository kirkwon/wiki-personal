# Street Photography Source

This source covers the practice of street photography — candid photography of public life capturing unplanned, unposed moments in public spaces. Key topics include the philosophy of street photography, equipment recommendations (35mm focal length preferred), zone focusing technique, the decisive moment concept, composition methods (leading lines, framing, reflections, patterns), ethical guidelines, San Francisco location recommendations, and practice exercises. The source emphasizes presence and anticipation over technical perfection, with minimal post-processing and frequent B&W conversion.
