# Interactions

Interactions is presented as a universal mental model for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It functions as a conceptual tool for making sense of how entities, systems, and people influence each other.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

This mental model draws from two key sources:

- **Cal Newport** — *Digital Minimalism*, which explores how digital interactions shape attention, relationships, and well-being.
- **Charles Duhigg** — *Supercommunicators*, which examines how conversational and interpersonal interactions determine understanding.
