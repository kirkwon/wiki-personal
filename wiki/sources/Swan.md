# Swan (Mental Model Source)

## Summary

Swan is a conceptual tool or principle for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It is referenced in Taleb's *The Black Swan* as a universal mental model applicable across domains.

## Cross-Source Perspectives

- **Nassim Nicholas Taleb** – The Black Swan introduces Swan as a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in complex, unpredictable situations.
