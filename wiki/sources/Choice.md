# Choice.md Source Summary

This source introduces "Choice" as a universal mental model—a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations through pattern and relationship recognition. It synthesizes two cross-source perspectives: Barry Schwartz's warning about choice overload and decision paralysis from *The Paradox of Choice*, and Cal Newport's argument for deliberate choice of rare, valuable skills from *So Good They Can't Ignore You*. The source frames choice as a top-level mental model applicable across all domains, with confidence 0.7 indicating ongoing development. No original analysis is provided; the value lies in cross-referencing these divergent perspectives on choice as a navigational principle.
