# Blue Ocean Strategy - W Chan Kim & Renee Mauborgne

**Author**: W. Chan Kim & Renee Mauborgne
**Year**: 2024
**Difficulty**: intermediate

## Quick Reference

**Why it Matters**: This book provides frameworks for identifying uncontested market spaces and creating new demand through value innovation.

**The Big Picture**: Move beyond competitive "red ocean" markets into uncontested "blue ocean" spaces by reconstructing market boundaries using systematic tools.

**The Bottom Line**: Apply the strategy canvas, four actions framework, and six paths to consistently identify and capture new market opportunities.
