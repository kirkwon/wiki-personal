# The Four - Scott Galloway

Galloway's *The Four* identifies four companies — Apple, Amazon, Google, and Facebook (Meta) — as the dominant forces reshaping the global economy through platform capitalism.

## Core Framework

- **Apple**: Master of branding, pricing power, and ecosystem lock-in.
- **Amazon**: Dominates distribution and logistics, effectively controlling how goods reach consumers.
- **Google**: Monopolizes information access and advertising revenue.
- **Facebook/Meta**: Controls social connection and identity data.

## Key Concepts

- **Bundling**: Combining products to increase perceived value and switching costs.
- **Distribution over manufacturing**: In the modern economy, controlling the channel matters more than making the product.
- **Winner-take-most**: Platform businesses tend toward extreme concentration.
