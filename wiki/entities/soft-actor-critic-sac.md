# Soft Actor‑Critic (SAC)

The Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) is an advanced reinforcement learning algorithm that optimizes decision-making by balancing reward maximization with entropy to encourage exploration; it's akin to experimenting in cooking or adjusting camera settings for diverse photography shots. In personal finance, SAC could be likened to diversifying investments while seeking optimal returns, and from a cognitive science perspective, it mirrors the brain's ability to weigh various outcomes when making choices under uncertainty.
