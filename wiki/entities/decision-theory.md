# Decision Theory

Decision theory is the study of how individuals make choices among various alternatives based on preferences, potential outcomes, and probabilities. In cooking, it helps in selecting recipes; for photography, it aids in choosing camera settings; in personal finance, it guides investment decisions; and within cognitive science, it explores the processes behind decision-making.
