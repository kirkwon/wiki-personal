# probabilistic-illusion

A probabilistic illusion is the cognitive bias where individuals misinterpret random events or data as having meaningful patterns or trends when in fact they are independent occurrences. In cooking, this may lead to overconfidence in recipe outcomes; in photography, it might result in expecting specific compositions without considering chance; in personal finance, it could cause misjudgment of investment returns; and within cognitive science, understanding these illusions is crucial for studying human decision-making processes.
