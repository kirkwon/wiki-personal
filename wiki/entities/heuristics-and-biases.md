# Heuristics and Biases

Heuristics are mental shortcuts that simplify decision-making processes, often used when facing complex tasks like cooking recipes or photographic composition. Biases result from these heuristics leading to systematic errors in judgment and can affect personal finance decisions by skewing risk assessment or investment choices. In cognitive science, understanding heuristics and biases helps explain human behavior and the limitations of our thinking patterns.
