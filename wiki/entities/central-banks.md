# central-banks

Central banks are institutions that manage a country's currency, money supply, and interest rates to ensure economic stability and growth. They act as regulators of the financial system, overseeing monetary policy and often playing roles in bank supervision. In personal finance, understanding central banks can help individuals make informed decisions on savings, investments, and inflation impacts.
