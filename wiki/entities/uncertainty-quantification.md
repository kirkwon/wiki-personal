# Uncertainty Quantification

Uncertainty quantification is the process of systematically determining how uncertainness in input parameters affects the output or results within various fields such as cooking (recipe outcomes), photography (image quality), personal finance (investment risks), and cognitive science (decision-making accuracy). It involves statistical methods to assess, represent, and reduce uncertainties.
