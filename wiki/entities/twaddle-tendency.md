# Twaddle tendency

The Twaddle tendency refers to the inclination towards trivial or inconsequential topics that distract from meaningful discussions in areas such as cooking (recipe overhauls), photography (focusing on gear rather than composition), personal finance (chasing fads instead of sound investment strategies), and cognitive science (overlooking foundational research for sensationalized theories).
