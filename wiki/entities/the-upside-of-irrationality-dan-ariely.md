# the-upside-of-irrationality - dan-ariely

The Upside of Irrationality by Dan Ariely explores the counterintuitive ways in which human irrationality can positively influence creativity and decision-making in cooking (by inspiring unique recipes), photography (through artistic expression), personal finance (via unconventional investment strategies), and cognitive science (by challenging traditional rational models).
