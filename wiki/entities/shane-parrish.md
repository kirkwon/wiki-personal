# Shane Parrish

Shane Parrish is the founder of Farnam Street and author of *The Great Mental Models (Vol. 1)* (2024) and *Clear Thinking: Turning Ordinary Moments into Extraordinary Results* (2024). He is known for popularizing mental models as practical tools for decision-making and navigating complexity. His work emphasizes model-based thinking as a universal approach to making sense of patterns and relationships. He advocates that clear thinking is a learnable skill achievable through deliberate practices across perspective, uncertainty, habits, focus, information management, questioning, listening, emotional management, and resilience. *Clear Thinking* introduces clarity as a mental model for pattern recognition and sense-making in complex situations, focusing on developing mental clarity and making better decisions in everyday life. His framework synthesizes established principles from cognitive science and decision-making rather than introducing novel frameworks, connecting to broader literature on biases, habits, and emotional regulation.

Parrish's work is referenced as a source supporting the [[techniques]] mental model, emphasizing that clear thinking itself is a technique for navigating complexity. His framework aligns with process-oriented thinking by emphasizing that structured thinking processes, rather than reactive intuition, lead to better results, and with the [[clarity-mental-model]] he discusses.

His work synthesizes and extends ideas from [[thinking-fast-and-slow---daniel-kahneman]], [[cognitive-biases-library]], [[the-art-of-asking-questions]], [[managing-emotions-for-clarity]], and various decision-making frameworks already in the wiki, including [[decision-making-frameworks]] and [[techniques]]. He also connects to Kahneman's dual process theory, Taleb's work on uncertainty, and Polya's problem-solving frameworks.

## Key Contributions
- Framed mental models as a practical toolkit for improving cognition
- Introduced the [[precision-vs-power]] conflict as a key tension in thinking
- Developed the [[value-proposition-mental-model]] for aligning decisions with life priorities and evaluating decisions against life goals and values
- Examined how [[metaphors-as-mental-tools]] help comprehend complex ideas
- Analyzed the [[evolutionary-mindset]] and its impact on short-term thinking bias
- Investigated the role of mental models in [[innovation-through-mental-models]]
- Advocated for deliberate practices in perspective, uncertainty, habits, focus, information management, questioning, listening, emotional management, and resilience
- Emphasized empathy and mental model mapping for understanding others
- Explored the importance of recognizing, analyzing, and consciously managing mental models for better outcomes across problem-solving, creativity, collaboration, empathy, and personal growth
