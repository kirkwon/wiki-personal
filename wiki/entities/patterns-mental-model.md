# patterns-mental-model

A patterns-mental model is an integrated framework that combines observed regularities (patterns) with cognitive understanding to predict outcomes in various domains such as cooking recipes, photographic composition rules, financial trends analysis, and decision-making processes within cognitive science research. It serves as a mental blueprint for recognizing and applying systematic approaches to complex tasks or phenomena.
