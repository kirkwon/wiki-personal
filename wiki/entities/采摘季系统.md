# 采摘季系统

'采摘季系统' refers to the structured approach of selecting specific items or subjects during particular seasons for thematic consistency in projects such as cooking with seasonal ingredients, photography capturing seasonal landscapes, personal finance strategies tailored to fiscal quarters, and cognitive science studies observing behavioral patterns across different times of the year.
