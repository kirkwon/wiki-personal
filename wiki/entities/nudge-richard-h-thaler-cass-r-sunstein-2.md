# nudge - richard-h-thaler-cass-r-sunstein

A 'nudge,' as conceptualized by Richard H. Thaler and Cass R. Sunstein, is an aspect of choice architecture that subtly influences decision-making without restricting options or significantly altering economic incentives. In the contexts of cooking, photography, personal finance, and cognitive science, nudges can guide behavior towards healthier eating habits, better financial planning, more creative photo composition, and enhanced memory retention by leveraging psychological insights into human behavior.
