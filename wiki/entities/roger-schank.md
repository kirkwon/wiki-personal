# Roger Schank

Roger Schank was an influential cognitive scientist known for his work on conceptualizing the way humans process information, which has implications in various fields including cooking (recipe understanding), photography (image interpretation), personal finance (decision-making processes), and beyond. He developed the earliest form of artificial intelligence that focused on natural language understanding through case-based reasoning, aiding advancements in AI applications across diverse domains.
