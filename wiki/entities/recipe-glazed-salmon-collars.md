# Recipe: Glazed Salmon Collars

A premium preparation method for salmon collars featuring a glazed finish that caramelizes during cooking. The recipe emphasizes the importance of [[resting-meat]] after cooking to maximize juiciness and flavor.

Key elements:
- Salmon collar cuts prepared with a glaze of soy sauce, mirin, and sugar
- High-heat searing to develop [[maillard-reaction]] browning
- Glaze application and reduction during cooking
- Resting before serving to allow juices to redistribute

This recipe is a signature dish in the gastronomy portal and connects to [[salmon-collars]] and [[seasonings]].
