# Principles-driven decision making

Principles-driven decision making is the process of basing choices on fundamental guidelines or core values rather than relying solely on outcomes or external influences. In cooking, this might mean adhering to dietary principles; in photography, it could involve a commitment to artistic integrity; for personal finance, it entails following ethical investment strategies; and within cognitive science, it refers to the application of established theories on human thought processes.
