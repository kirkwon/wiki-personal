# real-assets

Real assets refer to physical or tangible items that have intrinsic value due to their substance, such as land, buildings, equipment, or artwork. In cooking, real-assets might include kitchen appliances and ingredients; in photography, it's the cameras and lenses; for personal finance, they encompass property holdings like homes or investment properties; while in cognitive science, real assets could be considered as physical tools that aid learning or research.
