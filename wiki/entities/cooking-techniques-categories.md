# cooking-techniques-categories

Cooking techniques categories refer to the various methods used to prepare food, such as baking, grilling, sautéing, steaming, and fermenting. These categories are essential for understanding different cooking processes, flavor development, and texture outcomes in culinary practices. They serve as a foundational framework similar to how photography techniques categorize light manipulation or cognitive science classifies learning strategies.


**Cooking Techniques Categories:**

- **Heat Application Methods:** Includes baking, grilling, sautéing, and frying which determine the texture and flavor through direct heat control.

- **Moisture Control Techniques:** Encompasses steaming and braising that utilize water or other liquids to cook food evenly without excessive browning.

- **Preservation Methods:** Such as fermenting, pickling, and curing which extend the shelf life of ingredients through chemical changes induced by specific conditions.
