# paradox-of-change

The paradox-of-change refers to the conflicting nature of change within various domains; in cooking, it's between innovation and tradition; in photography, balancing technological advancement with artistic expression; in personal finance, reconciling immediate gratification with long-term goals; and in cognitive science, understanding how adaptability can lead to both growth and stagnation.
