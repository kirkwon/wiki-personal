# data-visualization

Data visualization is the graphical representation of information and data to enable easier understanding and decision making. In cooking, it helps identify ingredient proportions; in photography, it assists in editing color balance; for personal finance, it aids in tracking expenses and investments; and within cognitive science, it visualizes complex brain activity patterns.
