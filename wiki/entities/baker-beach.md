# Baker Beach

Baker Beach is a San Francisco location noted for golden hour photography at sunset, offering views of the bridge.

## Golden Hour at Baker Beach

Sunset at Baker Beach provides beach scenes with bridge views, long shadows on sand, and warm golden light reflecting off the water. It is one of the great golden hour locations in San Francisco alongside [[ocean-beach]].
