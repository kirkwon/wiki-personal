# Deep Ensembles

Deep ensembles are an advanced machine learning technique where multiple deep neural networks (DNNs) with different initializations or architectures are trained together to improve predictive performance and robustness by aggregating their predictions. This approach can be likened to a group of chefs each preparing the same dish but using slightly varied recipes, resulting in a more reliable final outcome. In photography, it's similar to combining multiple exposures or filters for enhanced image quality; while in cognitive science, it reflects how diverse neural pathways contribute to stronger decision-making processes.
