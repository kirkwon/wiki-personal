# Health Savings Account

A Health Savings Account (HSA) is a tax-advantaged financial account available to individuals enrolled in high-deductible health plans, allowing them to save for medical expenses on a pre-tax basis. Contributions lower taxable income, and funds can be withdrawn tax-free when used for qualified medical costs. It's an effective tool for managing personal finances related to healthcare needs.
