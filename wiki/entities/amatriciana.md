# Amatriciana

A classic Roman pasta dish with guanciale, tomato, onion, and Pecorino Romano.

## Ingredients
- **Guanciale**: Cured pork cheek, rendered until crispy
- **Tomato**: Typically a simple tomato sauce or passata
- **Onion**: Finely diced, optional in some versions
- **Pecorino Romano**: Grated over the finished dish

## Distinction from Carbonara
Amatriciana includes tomato, distinguishing it from carbonara which has no tomato. Both use guanciale and Pecorino Romano, making them part of the Roman trio of classic pasta dishes alongside [[cacio-e-pepe]].
