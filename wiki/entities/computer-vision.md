# Computer Vision

Computer vision is an area of artificial intelligence that enables computers to interpret and understand visual information from the world, much like human sight. In cooking, it can assist in identifying ingredients or analyzing food textures; for photography, it aids in image enhancement and object recognition; in personal finance, it helps detect fraudulent activities through pattern analysis of transactions; while in cognitive science, it provides insights into human visual perception by simulating how humans process images.
