# Thinking mental model

A thinking mental model is an individual's internal representation of how something works or operates within their domain of interest, such as cooking techniques, photographic composition principles, investment strategies in personal finance, or cognitive processes studied in science. These models guide decision-making and problem-solving by providing a framework for understanding complex concepts and predicting outcomes based on past experiences and knowledge acquisition.
