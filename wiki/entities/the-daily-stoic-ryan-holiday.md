# The Daily Stoic - Ryan Holiday

The Daily Stoic is a daily email newsletter by Ryan Holiday that distills ancient Stoic philosophy into practical advice for modern life challenges. It offers insights on resilience, emotional intelligence, and personal growth through the lens of Stoicism, applicable to various aspects including cooking (e.g., patience in learning recipes), photography (e.g., focus amidst distractions), personal finance (e.g., managing expectations), and cognitive science (e.g., understanding biases).
