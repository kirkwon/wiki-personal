# The Honest Truth About Dishonesty

*The Honest Truth About Dishonesty* is Dan Ariely's book examining why people cheat, lie, and deceive—including themselves. Through a series of experiments, Ariely demonstrates that dishonesty is not binary but exists on a spectrum, and that people are highly motivated to maintain a positive self-image while still engaging in dishonest behavior.

Key findings include:
- The rubber band experiment showing people cheat more when they can rationalize the behavior
- Distance from money reduces ethical friction
- Self-deception and motivated reasoning play central roles in dishonesty

## Connection to Deception Model

This book provides the empirical foundation for the [[deception]] mental model, which uses deception as a lens for understanding patterns and relationships across complex situations.
