# Outcome bias

Outcome bias is the tendency to judge a decision by its eventual result rather than by the quality of the decision at the time it was made. In cooking, this might mean praising an experimental recipe solely if it turns out well; in photography, favoring photos that capture perfect moments over those with artistic value but less-than-perfect results; in personal finance, valuing investments based on their returns rather than the soundness of the initial choice. In cognitive science, outcome bias reflects a fundamental error in evaluating decision processes and can skew learning and future judgment.
