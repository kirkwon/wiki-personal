# Attention residue

Attention residue refers to the lingering focus on an initial task or thought after shifting attention to another activity. In cooking, it may cause a chef's mind to wander back to previous recipes while preparing a new dish. For photography, this could result in overthinking composition techniques instead of capturing spontaneous moments. In personal finance, one might continuously compare investments rather than focusing on current financial goals. Cognitively, it's the difficulty to disengage from an original idea or task, which can hinder productivity and decision-making across various domains.
