# Searing

Searing is a high-heat cooking technique that uses the [[maillard-reaction]] to develop deep, complex flavors on the surface of proteins and other ingredients. By quickly browning the exterior at high temperatures, searing creates a flavorful crust while sealing in moisture.

## Key Facts
- Relies on the [[maillard-reaction]] for flavor development
- Typically done at high heat with oil in a pan or skillet
- Essential technique for proteins like meat, fish, and poultry
- Often used as a first step before other cooking methods like braising or roasting

## Relationship to Other Techniques
Searing is closely related to [[wok-hei]] and [[wok-cooking]] in that all involve rapid, high-heat application. It differs from [[steaming]] in that it relies on dry heat and browning rather than moisture retention.
