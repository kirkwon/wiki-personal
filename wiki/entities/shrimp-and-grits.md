# Shrimp and Grits

Shrimp and grits is a Southern comfort breakfast featuring creamy stone-ground grits topped with sautéed shrimp in bacon grease.

## Key Details
- **Grits**: Stone-ground, cooked with milk, cream, butter, salt
- **Shrimp**: Peeled, sautéed in bacon grease with garlic and Cajun seasoning
- **Assembly**: Grits topped with shrimp and bacon

## Technique
- Cook grits until creamy (30-40 minutes)
- Sauté shrimp in bacon grease with garlic and Cajun seasoning
- Top grits with shrimp and bacon
