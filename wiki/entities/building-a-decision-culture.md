# Building-a-decision-culture

Building a decision culture involves creating an environment where informed choices are valued and made consistently across various domains such as cooking (choosing ingredients), photography (selecting angles/lighting), personal finance (investment decisions), and cognitive science (experimental design). It emphasizes the importance of data-driven decision-making, critical thinking, and adaptability to change.
