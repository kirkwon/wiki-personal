# controlled-extraction

Controlled extraction refers to the deliberate process of removing or isolating specific elements from a source with precision. In cooking, it involves carefully extracting flavors; in photography, capturing light selectively; in personal finance, strategically withdrawing funds; and in cognitive science, studying controlled stimuli effects on mental processes.
