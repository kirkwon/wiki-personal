# Cognitive Science & Decisions

Cognitive Science & Decisions explores the psychological processes behind decision making, integrating insights from neuroscience, philosophy, and economics to understand how we make choices in areas like cooking recipes selection, photographic composition, financial planning, and more. It emphasizes cognitive biases, heuristics, and rationality that influence our daily decisions.


'Cognitive Science & Decisions' is an interdisciplinary field examining the mental mechanisms that drive decision-making in everyday life. This includes how we choose ingredients for recipes, frame shots in photography, manage personal finances, and more—all through a lens of cognitive psychology, computational models, and behavioral economics.
