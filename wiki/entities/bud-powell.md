# Bud Powell

Bud Powell (1924–1966) was a pianist renowned for his virtuosic bebop piano style. He is considered one of the most technically accomplished pianists in jazz history.

## Role in Bebop

Powell adapted the fast tempos and harmonic complexity of bebop to the piano, translating the linear improvisational approach of horn players like Charlie Parker and Dizzy Gillespie into the keyboard medium. His single-note, horn-like lines and relentless rhythmic drive became a model for bebop piano.

## Legacy

Powell's influence on jazz piano is immense. His approach bridged the gap between the melodic freedom of bebop improvisation and the harmonic richness of the piano as a solo instrument.
