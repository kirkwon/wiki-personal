# Reality mental model

A reality mental model is an individual's internal representation of the world that guides their understanding and actions in various domains such as cooking (e.g., recipe interpretation), photography (e.g., light exposure effects on images), personal finance (e.g., investment risk assessment), and cognitive science (e.g., decision-making processes). It influences how one perceives, interacts with, and makes decisions about their environment and experiences.
