# chipotle

A chipotle is a smoked, dried jalapeño pepper that originates from Mexico, commonly used in various cuisines to add depth of flavor through its rich, smoky taste and mild heat. In photography, 'chipotle' refers to the color tone resembling this spice's hue, often employed for a warm, earthy feel. From a cognitive science perspective, it could metaphorically describe an intense but controlled sensory experience or emotional response.
