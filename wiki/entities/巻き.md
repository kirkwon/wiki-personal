# 巻き

In cooking, '巻き' refers to the technique of rolling or wrapping ingredients in dough, such as making sushi rolls with rice and fish. In photography, it denotes a panoramic image created by stitching together multiple photos taken sequentially. Regarding personal finance, '巻き' can metaphorically describe the process of compiling various financial documents or transactions into an organized format for review. In cognitive science, it may relate to the conceptualization of information as a continuous flow or stream in memory processing.
