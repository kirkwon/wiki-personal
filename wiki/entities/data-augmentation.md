# Data Augmentation

Data augmentation is the process of artificially expanding a dataset by creating modified versions of existing data points to improve model robustness and generalization. In cooking, it could mean experimenting with ingredient substitutions; in photography, applying filters or cropping images; for personal finance, diversifying investment strategies; and in cognitive science, simulating varied scenarios through computational models.
