# Trend Following

Trend following is an investment strategy that involves buying or selling assets based on their price trends to capitalize on market movements. In cooking, it refers to adapting recipes according to popular food trends. In photography, it means capturing images that reflect current visual styles. For personal finance, it's about adjusting spending and saving habits in line with economic indicators. In cognitive science, it pertains to studying patterns of thought or behavior over time.
