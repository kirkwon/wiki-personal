# On the Shortness of Life - Seneca

"On the Shortness of Life," authored by Seneca, is an ancient Stoic text that explores the concept of time's brevity and advocates for a life lived with purpose, emphasizing quality over quantity. It suggests focusing on what truly matters—personal growth, relationships, and meaningful experiences—rather than accumulating material wealth or pursuing superficial pleasures. This philosophy aligns with cognitive science by highlighting the importance of intentional living for psychological well-being.
