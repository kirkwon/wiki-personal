# Problem with averages

A problem with averages arises when the mean value does not accurately represent individual data points or trends within a set, leading to misguided conclusions in cooking recipes, photographic exposure settings, financial budgeting, and cognitive research analysis. In each context, relying solely on average values can overlook outliers or skewed distributions that significantly impact the outcome or interpretation of results.
