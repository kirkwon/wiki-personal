# Self-Theories

Self-theories are cognitive frameworks that individuals use to understand their abilities and control over outcomes in various domains such as cooking (culinary skills), photography (visual talent), personal finance (financial acumen), and broader aspects of Cognitive Science. These theories influence motivation, goal setting, and perseverance by shaping how people perceive their competence and the likelihood of success in these areas.
