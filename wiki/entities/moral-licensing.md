# moral-licensing

Moral licensing is the psychological phenomenon where an individual justifies unethical behavior by previously engaging in good deeds or adhering to moral standards. In cooking, it might manifest as feeling entitled to indulge after preparing a healthy meal; in photography, taking credit for capturing beautiful moments while disregarding copyright laws; in personal finance, justifying overspending by earlier charitable donations; and within cognitive science, using prior moral actions to excuse biased decision-making.
