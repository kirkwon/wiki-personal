# Tom Griffiths

Tom Griffiths is a cognitive scientist and professor who collaborates with Brian Christian on applying computational thinking to human life. Co-author of *The Most Human Human* and *Algorithms to Live By*, he brings expertise in psychology, decision-making, and artificial intelligence to the intersection of algorithms and everyday choices. Griffiths delivers the scientific foundation for the philosophical considerations of *The Most Human Human*.

## Key Ideas

- Human cognition involves distinctive capabilities that differentiate it from machine intelligence.
- Pattern recognition and meaning-making are central to human intelligence.
- Studying human uniqueness informs our understanding of intelligence broadly.
