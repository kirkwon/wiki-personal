# stir-fry

Stir-fry is a cooking technique originating from Chinese cuisine where ingredients are quickly fried in a small amount of very hot oil while being stirred continuously over high heat. This method preserves the texture and nutrients, allowing for vibrant colors and flavors to meld together harmoniously. It's an efficient way to prepare meals that align with cognitive science principles by reducing decision fatigue through simplicity in recipe steps.
