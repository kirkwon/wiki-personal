# Social Security

Social Security is a federal retirement income program. Its taxation rules interact with other income sources to create significant tax planning considerations in retirement.

## Taxation Basics

- Up to 50% of benefits taxable if income exceeds $25,000 (single) / $32,000 (joint).
- Up to 85% of benefits taxable if income exceeds $34,000 (single) / $44,000 (joint).^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## Provisional Income

Social Security taxation is based on provisional income (AGI + tax-free interest + 50% of benefits), not just AGI. This makes Roth conversions and RMDs potential triggers for increased taxation.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]

## Optimization Strategies

- Delay claiming for maximum benefits.
- Coordinate with spouse's benefits.
- Manage other income sources to avoid triggering Social Security taxation.
- Consider restricted applications for optimal benefit timing.^[Tax Torpedoes.md]
