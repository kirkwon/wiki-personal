# grain-preparation

Grain preparation refers to the process of cleaning, sorting, and conditioning grains before cooking or consumption. In photography, it may metaphorically describe refining raw data for clarity and focus. For personal finance, it involves organizing financial information, while in cognitive science, it can relate to processing sensory input into coherent thoughts.
