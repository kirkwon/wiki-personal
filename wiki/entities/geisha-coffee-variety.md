# Geisha Coffee Variety

Geisha (also spelled Gesha) is an ultra-premium Arabica coffee variety originating from Ethiopia, now especially celebrated in Panama. It is known for its unique floral, tea-like character.

## Key Characteristics
- **Origin**: Ethiopia, popular in Panama
- **Character**: Unique floral, tea-like
- **Growth**: Low yield, high quality
- **Use**: Ultra-premium specialty coffee

Geisha coffees regularly command some of the highest prices in the specialty coffee market due to their distinctive and complex flavor profiles.
