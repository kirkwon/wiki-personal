# Demis Hassabis

Demis Hassabis is the co-founder of DeepMind Technologies, an AI research company known for developing AlphaGo, a computer program that defeated world champion Go players. He has a background in cognitive science and holds a PhD from University College London. Although not directly related to cooking or photography, his work on artificial intelligence intersects with these fields through applications like automated recipe generation and image recognition technologies. Hassabis also contributes insights into personal finance by advocating for the use of AI in financial decision-making processes.
