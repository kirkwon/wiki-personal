# Loss aversion

Loss aversion is a cognitive bias where the emotional impact of losing something is stronger than the joy of gaining something of equal value. In personal finance, this can lead to overly cautious investment strategies; in cooking and photography, it may result in hesitancy to experiment with new recipes or techniques due to fear of failure. Understanding loss aversion is crucial for cognitive science as it sheds light on decision-making processes across various domains.
