# Principles for Dealing with the Changing World Order - Ray Dalio

Ray Dalio's *Principles for Dealing with the Changing World Order* analyzes long-cycle macro dynamics that reshape industries and nations. The book examines how rising and emerging powers follow predictable patterns that affect industry structures over decades.^[raw/articles/Industries.md]

## Key Themes

- Long-cycle patterns in economic and geopolitical power affect which industries thrive or decline
- Rising powers reshape global industry landscapes through investment, trade, and competition
- Understanding these cycles provides foresight for navigating industry changes

## Connection to Industries Mental Model

Dalio's perspective contributes to [[industries]] by providing the macro-cyclical dimension—showing that industry structures follow long-term patterns tied to broader economic and geopolitical forces.^[raw/articles/Industries.md]
