# Net Worth Monitoring

Net worth monitoring is the systematic process of tracking an individual's assets (e.g., cash, investments) and liabilities (e.g., debts) to calculate their net worth over time. This financial metric provides a snapshot of one’s economic status and helps in making informed decisions regarding spending, saving, and investing. It is particularly relevant for those with interests in personal finance, as it aligns with the analytical mindset found in cognitive science and offers practical insights similar to evaluating recipes or photography projects' outcomes.
