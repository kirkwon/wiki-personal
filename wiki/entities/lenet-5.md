# LeNet-5

The LeNet-5 is an early convolutional neural network designed primarily for digit recognition tasks, similar to identifying ingredients in cooking or focusing on specific elements within a photograph. Its architecture consists of layers that progressively extract features and patterns, analogous to building up complexity from basic financial concepts to advanced cognitive science theories.
