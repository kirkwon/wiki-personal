# mental model

A mental model is an individual's internal representation of the external world that guides their understanding and decision-making processes in various domains such as cooking (e.g., recipe interpretation), photography (e.g., light exposure principles), personal finance (e.g., budget planning strategies), and cognitive science (e.g., memory recall techniques). These models are shaped by experience, education, and cultural influences, affecting how one perceives, interacts with, and solves problems in these areas.
