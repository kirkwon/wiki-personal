# Out of Control

In cooking, "out of control" refers to a situation where the dish's flavor profile deviates significantly from intended recipe specifications due to factors like incorrect ingredient ratios or improper cooking techniques. In photography, it describes images that lack focus or composition balance, failing to convey the desired visual message. Within personal finance, being out of control means having unmanaged expenses and debts leading to financial instability. In cognitive science, it pertains to thought processes or behaviors that are erratic and not aligned with rational decision-making principles.
