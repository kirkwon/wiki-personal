# Atomic Habits

*Atomic Habits* by James Clear is a foundational text on habit formation. It introduces the four laws of behavior change: make it obvious, make it attractive, make it easy, make it satisfying. The book emphasizes identity-based change—shifting who you believe you are rather than just what you do.

## Key Concepts
- Atomic habit stacking — building small habits that compound
- Identity-based change — "Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish to become"
- The aggregation of marginal gains

## Relevance
Central source for the Habits mental model, providing the most widely adopted modern framework for understanding habit formation.
