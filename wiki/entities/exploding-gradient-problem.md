# Exploding Gradient Problem

The Exploding Gradient Problem occurs when the gradients during backpropagation become excessively large, causing numerical instability in neural networks akin to over-seasoning a dish or underexposing a photo. This can lead to erratic learning behavior and financial model predictions that are unreliable due to disproportionate weight adjustments. In cognitive science terms, it's like the brain struggling with too much information at once, leading to impaired decision-making processes.
