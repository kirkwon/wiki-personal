# Endowment effect

The endowment effect is a cognitive bias where individuals ascribe higher value to items they own compared to identical or similar objects not owned by them. This psychological phenomenon can influence personal finance decisions, such as overvaluing kitchen utensils in cooking, cherishing photographic equipment, and reluctance to part with possessions that have sentimental attachment.
