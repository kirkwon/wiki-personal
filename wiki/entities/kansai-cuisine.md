# kansai-cuisine

Kansai cuisine refers to the traditional culinary style originating from the Kansai region of Japan, encompassing dishes like sushi, tempura, and kaiseki-ryori. It is characterized by its use of local ingredients such as seafood and seasonal vegetables, with a focus on balance and presentation. This regional cuisine has influenced modern Japanese cooking techniques and photography through the emphasis on visual appeal in food plating.
