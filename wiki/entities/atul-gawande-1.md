# Atul Gawande

Atul Gawande is an accomplished surgeon, writer, and public health researcher known for his work on the complexities of medical practice and patient care. With a keen interest in cognitive science, he explores decision-making processes within medicine. Beyond his professional pursuits, Gawande has dabbled in cooking and photography as hobbies that reflect his appreciation for detail and precision.
