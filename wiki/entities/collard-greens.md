# Collard Greens

Collard greens are a slow-cooked staple of Southern soul food, traditionally simmered with smoked meat for deep flavor.

## Key Details
- **Greens**: Collard, mustard, turnip
- **Smoked meat**: Turkey wing or ham hock
- **Seasonings**: Onion, garlic, apple cider vinegar, hot sauce
- **Cook time**: 1-2 hours

## Technique
1. Sauté onion, garlic
2. Add smoked meat
3. Add collards and broth
4. Simmer 1-2 hours
5. Season with vinegar and hot sauce

The slow cooking process is a form of braising that develops deep, complex flavors over time. [[braising]]
