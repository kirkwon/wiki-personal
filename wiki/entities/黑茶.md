# 黑茶

黑茶 (Hēichá) refers to black tea, which is fully oxidized tea leaves that have been dried and heated. It's known for its robust flavor profile with a range of possible tastes from malty to floral, depending on the specific blend or origin. In cognitive science, consuming black tea has been associated with increased alertness due to caffeine content, which can be beneficial during activities requiring sustained attention like cooking and photography.
