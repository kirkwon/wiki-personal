# Guanciale

Guanciale is cured pork cheek, the preferred fat for Roman pasta dishes including carbonara and amatriciana. It has a rich, unctuous flavor and renders cleanly, providing the fat base for sauces.

## Key Facts

- Cured pork cheek (not pancetta or bacon)
- Rich, fatty, and intensely flavored
- Renders easily into a silky fat
- Essential in carbonara and amatriciana
- Available in Italian specialty stores or can be made at home

## Usage

Diced and rendered slowly, guanciale fat becomes the cooking medium for Roman classics. The rendered fat is then tossed with pasta and sauce ingredients.
