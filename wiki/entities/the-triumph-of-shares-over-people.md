# the-triumph-of-shares-over-people

The phrase 'the triumph of shares over people' refers to the phenomenon where investment returns from stock markets are prioritized or valued more highly than human well-being or societal needs. In personal finance contexts like cooking and photography, this could manifest as individuals focusing on profitable food trends or marketable content at the expense of culinary diversity or authentic storytelling. Cognitive science might explore how such prioritization affects decision-making processes and societal values.
