# winners-curse-analysis

Winner's curse analysis refers to the phenomenon where individuals who win a competition or bid on an item may overestimate its value due to their success bias. In cooking, this could mean paying too much for rare ingredients; in photography, overspending on equipment based on winning awards; in personal finance, inflated expectations of returns from high-performing investments; and in cognitive science, it relates to the overconfidence effect where winners attribute their success solely to skill rather than chance.
