# self-serving-bias

Self-serving bias is the cognitive tendency to attribute positive outcomes to one's own actions while attributing negative outcomes to external factors. In cooking, this might manifest as a chef taking credit for a successful dish but blaming ingredient quality or timing issues when it fails. In photography, an individual may praise their skill in capturing great shots yet dismiss poor results due to camera limitations. Regarding personal finance, one could attribute financial successes to savvy investments while attributing losses to market volatility. This bias is a common phenomenon studied within cognitive science as it influences self-perception and decision-making processes.
