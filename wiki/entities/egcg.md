# EGCG

EGCG stands for Epigallocatechin gallate, a potent antioxidant found predominantly in green tea leaves. It is known to support cognitive function by potentially improving memory and focus, which can be beneficial both personally and professionally. Additionally, EGCG has been studied for its role in photography as it may protect sensitive equipment from oxidative damage when used in photo development processes.
