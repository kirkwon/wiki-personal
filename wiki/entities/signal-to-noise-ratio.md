# Signal-to-noise ratio

The Signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) is the measure of the desired signal's strength relative to background noise within a given context. In cooking, it represents the clarity of flavor profiles amidst competing ingredients; in photography, it denotes image quality by comparing detail against graininess or blurriness; for personal finance, SNR can metaphorically assess investment returns over market volatility; and in cognitive science, it may relate to the clarity of thought processes amidst distracting stimuli.
