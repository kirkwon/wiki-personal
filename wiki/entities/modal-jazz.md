# Modal Jazz

Modal jazz is a style of jazz music that emphasizes the use of musical modes rather than chord progressions as the basis for improvisation and composition. It often features fewer chords per measure, allowing musicians to explore extended melodies over longer periods, similar to how an artist might focus on lighting or subject matter in photography. This approach can be likened to a minimalist cooking technique that relies on the intrinsic flavors of ingredients rather than complex sauces.
