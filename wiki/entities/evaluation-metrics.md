# Evaluation Metrics

Evaluation metrics are quantifiable standards used to assess the quality or performance of various activities such as cooking recipes (taste test scores), photography images (sharpness, composition grades), personal finance management (investment growth rates), and cognitive tasks (accuracy in memory recall). These metrics provide objective measures for improvement and comparison.
