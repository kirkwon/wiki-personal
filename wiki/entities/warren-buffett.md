# Warren Buffett
Investor and billionaire whose wealth accumulation illustrates the power of [[compounding-time]]. 90% of his wealth was accumulated after age 65.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md] This example demonstrates that time in market matters more than timing the market, and that consistent compounding over decades is the primary driver of wealth creation, highlighting the importance of long-term investment strategies in achieving financial goals, such as [[getting-wealthy-vs-staying-wealthy]].
