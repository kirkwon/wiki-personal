# The Beginning of Infinity

*The Beginning of Infinity* is a book by David Deutsch that explores the nature of knowledge, progress, and the human capacity for infinite improvement. It presents optimism as a foundational principle—arguing that optimism is a prerequisite for problem-solving, scientific progress, and the creation of infinite knowledge.

## Key Ideas
- Optimism as a mental model for navigating complex situations
- Knowledge creation as the driver of progress
- Problem-solving as central to human advancement
