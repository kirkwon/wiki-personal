# bias

Bias refers to an inclination or prejudice towards certain outcomes or perspectives that can skew judgment and decision-making processes. In cooking, it may manifest as favoring specific ingredients; in photography, through selective framing; in personal finance, by preferring particular investments; and in cognitive science, as a systematic error in thinking or data interpretation.
