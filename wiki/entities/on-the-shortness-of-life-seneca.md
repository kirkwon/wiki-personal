# On the Shortness of Life by Seneca

Seneca's *On the Shortness of Life* is a philosophical essay arguing that life is not too short but that we waste much of it on trivial pursuits. The work is foundational to the modern concept of life auditing—systematically reviewing how time is spent to eliminate waste and focus on what truly matters.

## Key Themes

- Most people squander their time on busyness rather than meaningful activity.
- Reflective examination of daily habits reveals large amounts of wasted effort.
- Intentional allocation of time leads to greater fulfillment and productivity.

## Relevance to Life Audit

Seneca's essay provides the philosophical basis for the audit step in the life audit cycle: before optimizing, one must first honestly assess where time is going. This connects to [[stillness]] as a practice of pausing to observe, and to [[quadrant-2-focus]] in prioritizing what is truly important.
