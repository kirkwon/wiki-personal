# Tools of Titans - Timothy Ferriss

"Tools of Titans by Timothy Ferriss is a compilation of strategies from successful individuals across various fields including cooking, photography, personal finance, and cognitive science. It emphasizes the use of unconventional tools and techniques to maximize efficiency and productivity in these domains."
