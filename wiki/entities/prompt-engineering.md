# Prompt Engineering

Prompt Engineering is the strategic crafting of questions or instructions to elicit specific responses from AI systems in various domains like cooking (recipe generation), photography (image editing tips), personal finance (budget planning advice), and cognitive science (simulating human decision-making). It involves optimizing prompts for clarity, relevance, and effectiveness to achieve desired outcomes.
