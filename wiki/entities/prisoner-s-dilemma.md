# prisoner-s-dilemma

The prisoner-dilemma is a fundamental concept in game theory where two individuals must choose between cooperation or betrayal without knowing the other's choice, often leading to suboptimal outcomes for both parties. In cooking, it can represent recipe sharing versus keeping secrets; in photography, choosing collaborative projects over solo work; in personal finance, deciding whether to invest jointly with a partner or independently; and in cognitive science, studying how individuals make choices when their actions affect others' outcomes.
