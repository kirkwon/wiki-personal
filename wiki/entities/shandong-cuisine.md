# shandong-cuisine

Shandong cuisine refers to the traditional culinary practices originating from Shandong Province in China, characterized by its diverse flavors, emphasis on seafood due to coastal proximity, and notable dishes like sweet and sour carp and braised pork belly. This regional gastronomy is rich in history and has influenced modern Chinese cooking techniques and ingredient usage.


Photographically, capturing Shandong cuisine can involve showcasing its vibrant colors, textures, and the cultural context of food preparation and presentation. It's an opportunity to document a culinary tradition through visual storytelling that highlights both the dishes themselves and their significance in Chinese culture.
