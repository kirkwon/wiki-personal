# Salmon

A Salmon is a pinkish-orange fish belonging to the family Salmonidae, renowned in cooking for its rich flavor and versatility in various cuisines; it's also popular subject matter in photography due to its vibrant colors and graceful movements. From a personal finance perspective, investing in salmon stock or fisheries can be lucrative, while cognitively, consuming omega-3 fatty acids found in salmon is linked to improved brain function.
