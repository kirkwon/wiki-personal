# portfolio-allocation

Portfolio allocation refers to the strategic distribution of investments across various assets or projects to optimize returns while managing risk. In cooking, it's akin to balancing ingredients for flavor; in photography, diversifying equipment and techniques enhances creativity; in personal finance, it involves spreading savings and expenses to secure financial health; and in cognitive science, it relates to the allocation of mental resources across tasks.
