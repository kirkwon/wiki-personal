# The Obstacle Is the Way

Book by Ryan Holiday that presents a framework for turning obstacles into advantages. The core philosophy divides the response to adversity into three disciplines: perception, action, and will. This work builds on Stoic philosophy and provides a practical mental model for navigating complexity and setbacks.
