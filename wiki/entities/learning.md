# learning

Learning is the process of acquiring new knowledge or skills through experience, study, or teaching. In cooking, it involves mastering recipes and techniques; in photography, it's about understanding light and composition; for personal finance, learning entails grasping budgeting and investment strategies; within cognitive science, it encompasses exploring how the brain processes information.
