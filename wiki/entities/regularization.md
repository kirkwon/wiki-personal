# Regularization

Regularization is a technique used to prevent overfitting by adding a penalty term to the loss function in machine learning models, ensuring generalizability of predictions. In cooking or photography, it's like balancing flavors or light exposure for optimal results; in personal finance, regularizing spending habits can lead to more sustainable financial health. Cognitive science applies the concept metaphorically to cognitive biases and decision-making processes.
