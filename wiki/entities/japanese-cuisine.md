# Japanese Cuisine

Japanese cuisine is characterized by its emphasis on seasonality, quality of ingredients, and presentation. It includes a variety of dishes such as sushi, ramen, tempura, and bento boxes that showcase the balance between taste and visual appeal. The culinary tradition also reflects principles from Zen Buddhism, focusing on simplicity and mindfulness in both cooking and consumption.
