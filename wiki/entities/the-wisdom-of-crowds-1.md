# The Wisdom of Crowds

"The Wisdom of Crowds" refers to the phenomenon where collective opinion or decision-making by a diverse group often leads to accurate conclusions or optimal solutions, even without centralized control. In cooking and photography, this can manifest as trends emerging from community feedback; in personal finance, it may influence investment strategies through market sentiment; within cognitive science, it's studied as the aggregation of individual judgments leading to insightful outcomes.
