# DBpedia

DBpedia is an open knowledge graph that extracts structured information from Wikipedia to create interconnected data across various domains including cooking recipes, photographic metadata, financial concepts, and cognitive science principles. It enables users to query and analyze relationships between entities in these fields for enhanced research and personal knowledge expansion.
