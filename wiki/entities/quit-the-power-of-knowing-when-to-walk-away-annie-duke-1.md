# quit-the-power-of-knowing-when-to-walk-away - annie-duke

Quitting the power of knowing when to walk away encapsulates Annie Duke's philosophy on decision making across various domains such as cooking (choosing recipes wisely), photography (selecting moments for capturing images), personal finance (timing investments or withdrawals), and cognitive science (understanding the limits of knowledge). It emphasizes recognizing when to disengage from a pursuit in order to avoid overcommitment and potential losses.
