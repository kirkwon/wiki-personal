# Fried Chicken

Fried chicken is the iconic dish of Southern cuisine, characterized by a buttermilk-marinated coating fried to golden crispiness. It is a cornerstone of both Southern and soul food traditions.

## Key Details
- **Proteins**: Chicken pieces (legs, thighs)
- **Marinade**: Buttermilk (24-hour soak)
- **Coating**: Seasoned flour (salt, pepper, paprika)
- **Oil**: Canola or peanut oil
- **Temperature**: 350°F (175°C)
- **Cook time**: 12-15 minutes until golden

## Technique
1. Marinate in buttermilk overnight
2. Dredge in seasoned flour
3. Fry at 350°F
4. Cook 12-15 minutes until golden
5. Rest on wire rack

The Maillard reaction is central to achieving the crispy, golden crust that defines great fried chicken. [[maillard-reaction]]
