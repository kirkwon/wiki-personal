# Residual Connections (ResNet)

Residual Connections (ResNet) are neural network layers that allow the training of deeper networks by adding shortcut connections which skip one or more layers. In a culinary context, think of them as ingredients that enhance flavor without overpowering others; in photography, they're like filters that improve image quality while preserving original details; for personal finance, they're strategies to boost investment growth without compromising stability; and in cognitive science, they represent neural pathways that facilitate learning complex concepts by reinforcing foundational knowledge.
