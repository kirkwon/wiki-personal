# Scott E. Page

Professor of complex systems and author of *The Model Thinker* (2024). Page advocates for using multiple models in concert to understand complex phenomena, arguing that no single model captures all relevant dynamics. His work bridges mathematics, social science, and practical decision-making, emphasizing that modeling is a creative, practical skill rather than purely theoretical.

## Key Contributions

- **The Many Model Thinker**: Central thesis that diverse models outperform single models.
- **27 modeling frameworks**: From linear models to chaos and catastrophe theory.
- **Model selection and combination**: Practical skills for choosing and integrating models.
- **Agent-based and network models**: Frameworks for understanding emergent complexity.
