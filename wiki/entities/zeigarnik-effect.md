# Zeigarnik effect

The Zeigarnik effect is a psychological phenomenon where individuals remember uncompleted tasks better than completed ones. In cooking or photography projects, this means you're likely to recall details of recipes or shots that are still pending completion. For personal finance, it implies focusing on incomplete financial goals can enhance memory retention and motivation for those objectives.
