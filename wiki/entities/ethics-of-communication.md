# Ethics of communication

The ethics of communication refers to the moral principles guiding how information is shared responsibly and honestly across various domains such as cooking (recipe sharing), photography (crediting original work), personal finance (transparent disclosure of financial matters), and cognitive science (accurate representation of research findings). It emphasizes respect for intellectual property, truthfulness, and the potential impacts on stakeholders.
