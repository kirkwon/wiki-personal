# Asset-Diversifikation

Asset diversification is the strategic allocation of investments across various asset classes (e.g., stocks, bonds, real estate) to reduce risk exposure while aiming for potential returns. In personal finance, it mirrors cooking's ingredient variety or photography's diverse subjects, ensuring no single element dominates the portfolio. Cognitively, diversification aligns with spreading knowledge and skills broadly rather than deep specialization.
