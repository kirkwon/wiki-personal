# Expense Ratios

Expense ratios refer to the annual fees expressed as a percentage of total assets that investors pay to fund managers for operating a mutual fund or an ETF. In personal finance, understanding expense ratios is crucial when comparing different investment options to minimize costs and maximize potential returns on your investments.


In the context of photography, while 'expense ratio' isn't directly applicable, a similar concept could be the cost-to-benefit analysis of camera equipment expenses versus the quality of images produced.


For cognitive science research, an analogous term might not exist; however, one could consider the efficiency ratios in terms of resource allocation for experiments or studies relative to outcomes achieved.
