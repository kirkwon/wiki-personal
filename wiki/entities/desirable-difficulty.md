# desirable-difficulty

Desirable difficulty refers to challenges intentionally integrated into activities that promote learning and skill development by requiring effortful engagement. In cooking, it might mean mastering complex recipes; in photography, tackling advanced techniques like HDR or long exposure; for personal finance, navigating investment strategies with moderate risk; and within cognitive science, solving intricate puzzles to enhance problem-solving abilities.
