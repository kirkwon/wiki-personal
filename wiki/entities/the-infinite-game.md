# The Infinite Game

"The Infinite Game" refers to an ongoing process without a predetermined endpoint, where strategies evolve over time in response to changing circumstances. It's applicable across various domains like cooking (recipe experimentation), photography (continuous learning of techniques), personal finance (long-term investment planning), and cognitive science (lifelong learning).
