# Supercommunicators - Charles Duhigg

*Supercommunicators* is a book by Charles Duhigg that explores what makes people connect and communicate effectively. Duhigg identifies specific factors that determine whether communication succeeds or fails, drawing on research across multiple fields.

## Key Concepts

- **Factors of communication** – Variables that determine whether people truly understand each other.
- **Connection vs. understanding** – The distinction between feeling heard and actually comprehending a message.
- **Communication dynamics** – How context, audience, and message structure shape outcomes.

## Connection to Factors

Duhigg's work is one of the two source inspirations for the [[factors]] mental model. The Factors model extends Duhigg's communication-specific insights into a universal framework for identifying relevant variables in any complex situation.
