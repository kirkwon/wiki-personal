# trust-administration

Trust-administration refers to the management and oversight of assets or resources entrusted to an individual's care. In cooking, it might involve recipe secrets; in photography, managing exclusive rights to images; in personal finance, handling investments for beneficiaries; and in cognitive science, studying trust dynamics within social interactions.
