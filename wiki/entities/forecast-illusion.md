# forecast-illusion

Forecast-illusion is the cognitive bias where individuals overestimate their ability to predict future events accurately in fields like cooking (recipe outcomes), photography (image quality), personal finance (investment returns), and beyond, often leading to disappointment or misguided decisions.


In photography, it manifests as an unrealistic expectation of capturing the perfect shot despite varying conditions; in cooking, forecast-illusion may cause a chef to overlook potential recipe failures; for personal finance, investors might believe they can predict market movements with precision. Cognitive science studies this phenomenon to understand how our brains process information and make predictions about the future.
