# 功夫茶

功夫茶, also known as Pu-erh tea, is an aged black tea originating from Yunnan province in China. Renowned for its complex flavor profile that evolves over time, it's often stored and fermented to enhance depth. Its preparation requires careful attention to steeping temperature and duration, reflecting a blend of artistry and precision akin to photography composition or cognitive science experiment design.
