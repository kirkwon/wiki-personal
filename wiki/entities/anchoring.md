# Anchoring

Anchoring is a cognitive bias where an individual relies too heavily on the first piece of information (the "anchor") when making decisions. In cooking, it might manifest as over-relying on a recipe's initial measurements; in photography, anchoring could influence composition choices based on initial framing; in personal finance, it can affect investment strategies by fixating on an initial stock price; and within cognitive science, understanding anchoring helps explain how judgments are influenced by irrelevant starting points.
