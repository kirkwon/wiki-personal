# Fuzzy Logic

Fuzzy logic is an approach to reasoning that deals with approximate rather than fixed or exact values, allowing for degrees of truth. In cooking, it helps adjust recipes based on taste preferences; in photography, it aids in automatic exposure settings; in personal finance, it assists in making investment decisions under uncertainty; and in cognitive science, it models how humans process vague information.
