# compound-growth-math

Compound growth math refers to the mathematical formula used to calculate the exponential increase of an investment or quantity over time, factoring in periodic interest or growth rates. In cooking, it can describe how ingredient quantities scale up for larger servings; in photography, it may relate to image resolution scaling; in personal finance, it's crucial for understanding compound interest on savings and loans; while in cognitive science, it could model the exponential growth of knowledge acquisition.
