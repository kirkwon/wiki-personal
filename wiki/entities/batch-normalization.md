# Batch Normalization

Batch Normalization is a technique used to standardize the inputs of each mini-batch, improving training stability by reducing internal covariate shift in deep learning models. It's like ensuring all ingredients are at optimal temperatures before mixing them for consistent results. In photography, it's analogous to adjusting exposure levels across multiple shots to achieve uniform brightness and contrast.


In personal finance, Batch Normalization can be likened to diversifying investments in different asset classes to balance risk and return consistently over time. From a cognitive science perspective, it's similar to calibrating sensory inputs (like sight or touch) for consistent perception across various stimuli.
