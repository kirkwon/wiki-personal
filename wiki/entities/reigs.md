# REIGs

Registered Education Investment Groups (REIGs) are investment vehicles that pool resources from multiple individuals to fund educational programs or courses with a focus on skill development in areas like cooking, photography, personal finance management, and cognitive science enhancement. They provide structured learning experiences often led by experts in the respective fields while offering potential tax benefits for participants' contributions.
