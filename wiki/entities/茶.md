# 茶

茶 (Cha) refers to the traditional Chinese beverage made from Camellia sinensis leaves and buds steeped in hot water. In cooking contexts, it is often used as a base ingredient or flavor enhancer. From a photography perspective, capturing tea ceremonies can offer insights into cultural practices. In personal finance, the cost of high-quality teas may reflect on spending habits and luxury consumption trends.
