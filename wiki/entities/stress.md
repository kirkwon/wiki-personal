# stress

Stress is the body's response to any demand or challenge, often manifesting as physical tension, emotional strain, or cognitive overload. In cooking, stress can arise from time constraints or complex recipes; in photography, it may stem from creative pressure or technical challenges; personal finance stress typically involves managing debt and budgeting under uncertainty; while in cognitive science, stress is linked to the brain's processing of information during high-stakes decision-making.
