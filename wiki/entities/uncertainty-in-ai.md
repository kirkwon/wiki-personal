# Uncertainty in AI

In artificial intelligence (AI), uncertainty refers to the inherent unpredictability of AI systems' decisions due to incomplete data or complex algorithms. This is crucial in cooking for recipe scaling, photography for dynamic scene analysis, personal finance for risk assessment, and cognitive science for modeling human decision-making under ambiguity.
