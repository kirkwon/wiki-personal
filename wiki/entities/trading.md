# trading

Trading is the exchange of goods or services between parties to mutually benefit from each other's offerings. In cooking, it refers to swapping ingredients; in photography, trading equipment knowledge; within personal finance, it involves buying and selling assets for profit; and cognitively, it represents the mental process of evaluating options before making a decision.
