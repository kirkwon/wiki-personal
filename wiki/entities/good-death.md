# good-death

A good death refers to the peaceful passing of an individual that aligns with their values, desires, and cultural beliefs. In the context of cooking, it may involve a meal prepared in accordance with one's dietary preferences; for photography, it could mean capturing memories or final images reflective of personal identity; within personal finance, ensuring financial security to avoid stress at life's end; and from a cognitive science perspective, it encompasses mental well-being leading up to the end of life.
