# Information bias

Information bias refers to the distortion of decision making due to an excess or imbalanced focus on information that is not relevant or necessary for a particular context. In cooking, this could mean overemphasizing recipe details while ignoring taste; in photography, it might involve fixating on technical specs rather than composition; within personal finance, it may lead to obsessing over minute investment fluctuations instead of long-term goals; and in cognitive science, information bias can skew research outcomes by giving undue weight to certain data points.
