# Base-rate neglect

Base-rate neglect is the cognitive bias where individuals ignore statistical base rates (general prevalence) in favor of specific information or anecdotal evidence when making decisions. This can lead to misjudgments in areas like cooking recipe success, selecting photography techniques without considering their general effectiveness, choosing financial investments without understanding market trends, and interpreting cognitive science research findings.
