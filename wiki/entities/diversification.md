# Diversification

Diversification is the strategic approach of spreading investments across various assets to reduce risk exposure. In cooking, it involves using a range of ingredients for balanced flavors; in photography, employing different techniques and subjects; in personal finance, diversifying portfolios with varied stocks and bonds; and in cognitive science, studying multiple domains to enhance understanding of complex mental processes.
