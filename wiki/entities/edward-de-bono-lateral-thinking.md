# Edward de Bono

Edward de Bono is a psychologist and author known for developing lateral thinking as a creative problem-solving technique. His book [[lateral-thinking-creativity-step-by-step]] is referenced as a source supporting the [[techniques]] mental model. De Bono's work emphasizes breaking conventional patterns to generate novel solutions, connecting to the existing wiki coverage of [[six-thinking-hats]] and [[lateral-thinking]].

His framework provides structured methods for navigating complex, ambiguous situations through deliberate pattern disruption.
