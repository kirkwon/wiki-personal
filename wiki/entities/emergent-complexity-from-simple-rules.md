# emergent complexity from simple rules

Emergent complexity arises when basic principles or actions lead to intricate outcomes not immediately apparent from the initial simplicity of those rules. In cooking, a few ingredients combine chemically; in photography, simple light adjustments create complex images; personal finance strategies can yield unpredictable wealth growth; and cognitive science shows how rudimentary neural connections form sophisticated thought patterns.
