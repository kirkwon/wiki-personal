# Find Your Why - Simon Sinek

*Find Your Why* is Simon Sinek's book on discovering purpose and communicating it effectively. It builds on his earlier work in *Start With Why* and *The Infinite Game*, providing practical guidance for individuals and organizations to clarify their core purpose and translate it into compelling messaging.

## Key Ideas

- Purpose-driven communication begins with understanding your fundamental "why."
- Clear messaging connects with others by articulating core values and intent.
- The framework supports both personal and organizational clarity.
