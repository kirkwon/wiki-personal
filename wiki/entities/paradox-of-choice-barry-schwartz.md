# paradox-of-choice---barry-schwartz

The paradox of choice, as described by Barry Schwartz, suggests that an overabundance of options in areas like cooking recipes, photography techniques, personal finance investments, or cognitive science theories can lead to decision paralysis and decreased satisfaction. This phenomenon occurs because the effort required to make a choice increases with more alternatives, potentially reducing overall happiness due to regret over missed opportunities.
