# The OODA Loop
The OODA Loop is a mental model that describes how people observe, orient, decide, and act in response to changing situations. It is particularly useful in dynamic and competitive environments.

## Applications
- Military strategy: The OODA Loop was originally developed for military applications, emphasizing quick decision-making.
- Business: It can be applied to improve strategic decision-making and adaptability in competitive markets.
