# 4 Percent Rule

The "4 Percent Rule" is a retirement savings guideline suggesting that one should aim to save enough so their portfolio can generate approximately 4% annually in retirement without depleting the principal. This rule assumes moderate annual withdrawals and adjusts for inflation, serving as a benchmark for financial planning.


In cooking, it might refer to a recipe yield where ingredients are scaled up or down by 4%. In photography, it could denote an exposure setting that yields optimal results in 4% of tested conditions. Cognitive science may use the term metaphorically for processes that achieve desired outcomes within a specific range (e.g., memory recall efficiency).
