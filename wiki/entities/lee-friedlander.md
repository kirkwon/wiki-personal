# lee-friedlander

Lee-Friedlander is an innovative fusion technique combining leftover ingredients with Friedlander's method of layered presentation to create visually appealing and cost-effective dishes. In photography, it refers to a style that captures the essence of subjects through minimalistic composition and lighting, often used in personal finance blogging for clear visual data representation. The term also relates to cognitive science as an analogy for layered information processing strategies.
