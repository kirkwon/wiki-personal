# Category:Music Theory

In music theory, the category encompasses principles that govern musical structure, including scales, chords, rhythm, and harmony. It provides a framework for understanding how compositions are constructed and analyzed, similar to how ingredients combine in cooking or elements align in photography composition. Music theory is essential for both performers and composers, much like foundational knowledge of personal finance principles.
