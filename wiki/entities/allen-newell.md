# Allen Newell

Allen Newell was an American computer scientist and cognitive psychologist who co-founded the field of artificial intelligence with Herbert A. Simon. His work on human problem solving has influenced areas like cooking recipes development, photography composition techniques, personal finance decision-making models, and understanding how people process information in these domains.
