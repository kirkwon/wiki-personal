# Lateral Thinking

Lateral thinking is an approach to problem solving that involves looking at situations from fresh perspectives or unconventional angles, often leading to innovative solutions. In cooking, it might mean using unexpected ingredients in a dish; in photography, experimenting with unusual compositions; for personal finance, devising creative budget strategies; and within cognitive science, exploring novel ways of understanding thought processes.
