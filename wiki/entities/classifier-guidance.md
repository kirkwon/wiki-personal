# Classifier Guidance

Classifier guidance refers to the process of using algorithms or expert systems to categorize items into predefined groups based on their attributes. In cooking, it aids in recipe selection; in photography, it helps organize images by style or theme; for personal finance, it assists in budget allocation and investment categorization; while in cognitive science, it models decision-making processes.
