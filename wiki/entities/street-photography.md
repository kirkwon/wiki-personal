# Street Photography

Street photography is the art of capturing candid moments within public spaces to document everyday life. It focuses on people in motion, often unposed, creating a raw and authentic narrative through visual storytelling. This genre requires an understanding of composition and timing, similar to how one might carefully balance flavors or manage finances for optimal results.
