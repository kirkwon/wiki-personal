# Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)

A Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) is an artificial intelligence framework where two neural networks contest with each other to generate new, synthetic instances of data that mimic the original dataset. In cooking terms, it's like having a recipe improver and taste tester working together; in photography, it's as if you have a camera lens adjusting itself for perfect shots based on previous images; for personal finance, imagine an algorithm predicting future financial scenarios to help with investment decisions; and from a cognitive science perspective, GANs can be seen as simulating the brain's ability to learn and replicate patterns.
