# Real Estate

Real estate refers to property consisting of land or buildings on it, with an inherent value based on location, utility, and market demand. It encompasses residential homes, commercial spaces, and undeveloped land that can be bought, sold, or leased. In the context of personal finance, real estate is often considered a long-term investment due to its potential for appreciation and income generation through renting.
