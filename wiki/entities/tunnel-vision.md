# tunnel-vision

Tunnel vision refers to the focused attention on a specific task or goal while ignoring other relevant information, which can lead to overlooking important details in cooking recipes, missing broader composition elements in photography, neglecting diversified investments in personal finance, and potentially disregarding alternative cognitive processes in problem-solving.
