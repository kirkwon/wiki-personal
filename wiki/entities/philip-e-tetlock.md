# Philip E. Tetlock

Psychologist and researcher known for his work on forecasting accuracy and the Good Judgment Project. He is the central figure behind *Superforecasting*, co-authored with Dan Gardner, which presents evidence that some individuals ("superforecasters") can achieve markedly higher prediction accuracy than average forecasters through learnable methods.

His research identified specific traits and techniques that distinguish superforecasters, including open-mindedness, intellectual humility, probabilistic thinking, and a disciplined approach to updating beliefs. This work has implications for how organizations and individuals handle uncertainty and make decisions under conditions of limited information.
