# identity-based-habits

Identity-based habits are routines or behaviors that individuals develop as extensions of their self-concept across various domains such as cooking (e.g., signature dishes), photography (e.g., preferred subjects), personal finance (e.g., budgeting strategies), and cognitive science (e.g., memory techniques). These habits reflect a person's values, interests, and identity, influencing their choices and actions within these areas.
