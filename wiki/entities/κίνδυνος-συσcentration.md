# Κίνδυνος Συσcentration

'Κίνδυνος Συσcentration' refers to the inherent risk of overconcentration in a single area or skill set, which can lead to vulnerability in personal endeavors like cooking (recipe reliance), photography (style fixation), personal finance (investment narrowness), and cognitive science (thought monopoly). It emphasizes the importance of diversification across disciplines for resilience and adaptability.
