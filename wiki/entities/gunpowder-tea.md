# Gunpowder Tea (珠茶)

Gunpowder is a Chinese rolled green tea from Zhejiang Province, named for its small pellet appearance that unfurls when brewed. It has a toasted, nutty, and strong flavor.

## Key Facts
- **Origin**: Zhejiang Province
- **Processing**: Rolled into small pellets
- **Flavor**: Toasted, nutty, strong
- **Appearance**: Small pellets, unfurl when brewed
- **Best Brew**: 180-190°F (82-88°C), 3-4 minutes

## Processing
Leaves are rolled tightly into small pellets during processing, which helps preserve freshness and creates the characteristic appearance.
