# Recognition-Primed Decision Model

The Recognition-Primed Decision (RPD) model is a framework that describes how individuals make quick decisions by recognizing patterns based on experience without comparing all possible alternatives. It's particularly useful in fast-paced environments like cooking, where chefs rely on intuition to adjust recipes, or photography, where photographers swiftly decide composition changes. In personal finance and cognitive science, it explains how people can make sound financial choices or understand decision processes without exhaustive analysis.
