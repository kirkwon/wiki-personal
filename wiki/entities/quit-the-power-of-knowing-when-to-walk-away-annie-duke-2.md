# Quit: The Power of Knowing When to Walk Away

2024 book by Annie Duke, former professional poker player and decision-making expert. Extends the themes of her earlier work *Thinking in Bets* by reframing quitting as a strategic skill essential for long-term success.

## Core Arguments
- Quitting is a skill, not a sign of weakness
- Embracing imperfection and uncertainty is prerequisite for recognizing when to quit
- Loss aversion is a primary obstacle to timely quitting
- Timing is critical in deciding when to walk away
- Quitting can be an act of self-care preventing burnout
- Losses provide valuable learning opportunities

## Key Chapters
