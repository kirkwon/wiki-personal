# Salt Fat Acid Heat

Salt Fat Acid Heat is a foundational cooking resource that breaks down the four elements of seasoning and flavor building. It provides a framework for understanding how salt, fat, acid, and heat interact to create balanced, delicious dishes. The book is widely regarded as an essential guide for developing intuitive cooking skills.
