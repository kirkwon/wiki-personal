# signal-to-noise-control

Signal-to-noise control refers to the process of enhancing relevant information (signal) while minimizing irrelevant or distracting data (noise). In cooking, this could mean focusing on flavor profiles; in photography, it's about capturing clear images amidst visual clutter; for personal finance, distinguishing between essential expenses and frivolous spending; and in cognitive science, improving the clarity of thought by reducing mental distractions.
