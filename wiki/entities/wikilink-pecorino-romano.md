# wikilink: pecorino-romano

Pecorino-Romano is an Italian hard cheese made from sheep's milk, primarily produced in the Lazio region near Rome. In cooking, it adds a rich, salty flavor to dishes and can be grated over pasta or used as a filling for savory tarts. Its distinct taste also makes it a subject of interest in photography when capturing its textures and colors, while from a cognitive science perspective, studying the sensory perception of foods like Pecorino-Romano can provide insights into flavor recognition and preference mechanisms.
