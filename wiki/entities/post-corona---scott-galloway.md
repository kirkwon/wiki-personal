# Post Corona - Scott Galloway

Scott Galloway's *Post Corona: From Crisis to Opportunity* examines how the COVID-19 pandemic fundamentally reshuffled industry power. The book argues that crises are not just disruptions but catalysts for lasting structural changes in how industries are organized, who holds power, and what business models survive.^[raw/articles/Industries.md]

## Key Themes

- Industry power is not static; external shocks like pandemics restructure competitive landscapes
- Companies and industries must be understood as ecosystems affected by systemic forces
- Post-crisis opportunities emerge from recognizing where power has shifted

## Connection to Industries Mental Model

Galloway's perspective contributes to the [[industries]] mental model by showing that industry analysis must account for crisis-driven disruption as a recurring pattern in how industries evolve.^[raw/articles/Industries.md]
