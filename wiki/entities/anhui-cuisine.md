# anhui-cuisine

Anhui cuisine refers to the regional food style originating from Anhui Province in China, characterized by its use of fresh local ingredients, diverse vegetables, and aromatic herbs. It often features simple yet flavorful dishes with bold tastes, such as smoked meats and pickled vegetables. The cuisine's emphasis on natural simplicity aligns with minimalist photography principles and can be appreciated for its cost-effectiveness in personal finance due to the use of readily available local produce.
