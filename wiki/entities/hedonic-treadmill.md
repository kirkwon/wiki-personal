# Hedonic treadmill

The hedonic treadmill is the observed phenomenon where individuals quickly return to a relatively stable level of happiness despite major positive or negative events or life changes. In personal finance, this implies that increased wealth may only temporarily boost satisfaction until new financial goals are reached. Similarly, in cooking and photography, initial excitement from learning new skills fades as one becomes accustomed to the routine.
