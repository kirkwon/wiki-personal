# Commitment and Consistency

Commitment and consistency is the psychological principle where once an individual commits to something or makes a decision, they are more likely to follow through with it due to their desire for self-consistency. In cooking, this manifests as sticking to recipes; in photography, adherence to stylistic choices; in personal finance, maintaining budget plans; and in cognitive science, understanding the influence on decision-making processes.
