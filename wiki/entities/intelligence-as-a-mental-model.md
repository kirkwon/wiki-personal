# Intelligence as a Mental Model

Intelligence as a mental model refers to the ability to apply knowledge from various domains—such as cooking techniques, photographic composition principles, personal finance strategies, and cognitive science theories—to solve problems, innovate, and make informed decisions effectively in real-world situations. It involves understanding underlying patterns, adapting to new information, and integrating diverse concepts into a cohesive framework for practical application.
