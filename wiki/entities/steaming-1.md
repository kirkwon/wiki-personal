# Steaming

In cooking, steaming is the process of heating food by exposing it to steam, which gently cooks the ingredients while preserving nutrients and flavors. In photography, 'steaming' refers to a visual effect where objects appear misty or hazy due to light diffusion through moisture in the air. Within personal finance, steaming could metaphorically describe the gradual accumulation of wealth over time with minimal effort, similar to how steam slowly builds up pressure. In cognitive science, 'steaming' may not have a direct application but could be used as an analogy for the slow and steady processing of information in the brain.
