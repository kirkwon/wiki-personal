# Statistics

Statistics is the science of collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data to uncover patterns, trends, and relationships that inform decision-making in various domains such as cooking (recipe optimization), photography (image analysis), personal finance (budget planning), and cognitive science (behavioral research).

Statistics involves using numerical methods and probability theory to summarize data through measures like mean, median, and mode; it also employs statistical tests for hypothesis testing and confidence intervals to make informed predictions or inferences.
