# scattered-focus

Scattered-focus refers to an approach where attention is divided across multiple tasks or areas of interest simultaneously rather than concentrating on one task at a time. In cooking, this might involve multitasking recipes; in photography, it could mean juggling different camera settings; for personal finance, it involves managing various investments and expenses concurrently; and within cognitive science, it pertains to processing diverse information streams at once.
