# direct-ownership

Direct-ownership refers to the complete control an individual has over assets or investments without intermediaries. In cooking, it means owning all ingredients; in photography, it's having full rights to images taken; for personal finance, direct-ownership implies holding stocks or bonds directly rather than through funds; and in cognitive science, it could relate to understanding one's own mental processes without external influence.
