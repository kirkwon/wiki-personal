# probability-distributions

Probability distributions describe the likelihood of various outcomes within an event or experiment; they are essential in predicting ingredient proportions for recipes (Cooking), optimizing exposure settings for photographs (Photography), assessing financial risks (Personal Finance), and modeling decision-making processes (Cognitive Science).


Probability distributions quantify the chance of different results, guiding cooking measurements, photographic adjustments, investment strategies, and understanding thought patterns.
