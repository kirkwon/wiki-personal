# Eisenhower Matrix

The Eisenhower Matrix is a time management tool that categorizes tasks into four quadrants based on urgency and importance, helping prioritize activities in areas like cooking (meal planning), photography (project selection), personal finance (investment choices), and cognitive science research (study topics).


The Eisenhower Matrix divides tasks into 'Do First', 'Schedule', 'Delegate', or 'Eliminate' categories, facilitating efficient task management in diverse fields such as cooking (recipe execution), photography (shooting schedules), personal finance (budget planning), and cognitive science (research focus).
