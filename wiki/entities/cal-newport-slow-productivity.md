# Cal Newport - Slow Productivity

Cal Newport is the author of *Slow Productivity*, a book that challenges hustle culture and advocates for a deliberate, sustainable pace of work. Newport connects productive habits to long-term rhythms rather than short-term optimization.

## Key Ideas
- Reject the "new work" mindset that equates busyness with productivity
- Focus on fewer, higher-quality outputs
- Align work habits with deep, meaningful engagement rather than constant activity

## Relevance
Newport's perspective enriches the Habits mental model by emphasizing sustainability and intentionality in habit formation, contrasting with rapid optimization approaches.
