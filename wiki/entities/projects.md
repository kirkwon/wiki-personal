# Projects

A project is an organized effort with specific objectives aimed at achieving a particular outcome within defined constraints such as time, budget, or resources. In the context of cooking, it could be developing a new recipe; for photography, creating a photo series; in personal finance, planning and executing a savings strategy; and in cognitive science, designing an experiment to test a hypothesis about human behavior.
