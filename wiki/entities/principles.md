# principles

Principles are fundamental guidelines or core beliefs that shape decision-making processes across various domains. In cooking, principles dictate the balance of flavors and techniques; in photography, they govern composition and light exposure; in personal finance, principles guide budgeting and investment strategies; while in cognitive science, they underpin theories about human thought and behavior.
