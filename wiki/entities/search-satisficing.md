# Search satisficing

Search satisficing is the process of selecting an adequate option from available choices without exhaustively searching for the optimal one. In cooking, it means using a recipe that tastes good enough rather than finding the perfect dish; in photography, choosing filters or settings quickly to capture a scene effectively; in personal finance, picking investments based on sufficient criteria instead of seeking the best possible returns; and within cognitive science, understanding how individuals often settle for satisfactory solutions due to limited time and resources.
