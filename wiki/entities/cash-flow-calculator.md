# Cash Flow Calculator

A Cash Flow Calculator is an analytical tool used to track the movement of money into and out of your finances over time. It helps you understand how much cash you have available after accounting for expenses, income sources, and investments. This financial instrument aids in budget planning and identifying potential areas for cost savings or increased revenue generation.
