# Bias checklist

A Bias Checklist is a structured tool used to identify and mitigate unconscious prejudices or assumptions in various areas such as cooking (recipe selection), photography (composition choices), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (research methodologies). It helps ensure decisions are made based on objective criteria rather than subjective bias.
