# Explainable AI

Explainable AI (XAI) refers to artificial intelligence systems designed with transparency in mind, allowing users to understand the decision-making process of algorithms. In cooking, XAI could help explain why a recipe suggests certain ingredients; in photography, it might clarify image enhancement choices; for personal finance, XAI can elucidate investment recommendations; and within cognitive science, it assists in interpreting AI-driven research findings.
