# Flavor Theory

Flavor Theory posits that the perception of taste is not solely based on chemical composition but also influenced by psychological factors, cultural background, and individual experiences, which can alter one's flavor profile appreciation in cooking or photography themes depicting food. In personal finance, it suggests that financial decisions are similarly subjective, shaped by emotional biases and societal influences. Cognitive science explores this through the study of how sensory information is processed to form flavor perceptions and decision-making patterns.
