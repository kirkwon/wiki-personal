# Decision-making-frameworks

A decision-making framework is a structured approach used to evaluate options and make choices based on specific criteria relevant to the context, such as cooking recipes selection, photographic composition techniques, financial investment strategies, or cognitive behavioral analysis in psychology. It typically involves identifying goals, gathering information, assessing alternatives, weighing potential outcomes, and making a final decision that aligns with personal values and desired results.
