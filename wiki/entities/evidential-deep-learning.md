# Evidential Deep Learning

Evidential Deep Learning is an AI approach that combines deep learning with probabilistic reasoning to make inferences based on evidence from data. In cooking, it could predict recipe outcomes; in photography, it might enhance image quality by understanding contextual elements; for personal finance, it can assess investment risks more accurately; and within cognitive science, it aids in modeling complex decision-making processes.
