# Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)

A financial leverage metric measuring a property's ability to cover its debt obligations from operating income.

## Risk Thresholds
- DSCR > 1.5: Low leverage risk
- DSCR 1.2-1.5: Medium leverage risk
- DSCR < 1.2: High leverage risk

DSCR is tracked as a Key Risk Indicator alongside LTV, cash flow variance, and vacancy rates.
