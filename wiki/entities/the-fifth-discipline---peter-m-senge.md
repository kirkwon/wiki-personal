# The Fifth Discipline - Peter M. Senge

Peter M. Senge's *The Fifth Discipline* introduces the concept of learning organizations and systems thinking as a core discipline. The book emphasizes feedback loops and argues that organizations must develop shared vision, mental models, and team learning to adapt to complexity and drive systemic change.

## Key Ideas

- Five learning disciplines: personal mastery, mental models, shared vision, team learning, systems thinking
- Systems thinking as the integrative discipline that connects multiple disciplines into a coherent approach to organizational learning
- Organizations are learning systems that can improve through systemic feedback
- Mental models and assumptions drive behavior and must be surfaced
- The role of dialogue and reflective conversation in organizational change

## Connection to Change

Senge's framework represents systemic change through organizational learning — understanding that change in complex environments requires shifting how teams think and interact, not just implementing new processes.

## Connection to Systems Mental Model

Senge's work is one of the seven source perspectives informing the [[systems]] mental model.
