# Seasoning Methods

Seasoning methods refer to the techniques used to enhance flavor profiles in cooking through the application of various spices, herbs, and condiments. These methods can vary widely across cultures and cuisines, reflecting both traditional practices and individual creativity. In photography, seasoning might metaphorically describe adjustments made to images (e.g., contrast or saturation) to achieve a desired visual impact; however, this usage is less conventional than in cooking.


**Note: The second definition provided attempts to draw an analogy with the context of photography but clarifies that it's not standard terminology within the field.
