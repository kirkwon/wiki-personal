# Gambler's fallacy

The Gambler's Fallacy is the erroneous belief that past independent events affect future outcomes in random processes; for instance, thinking a coin flip will be heads after several tails, despite each toss being independent and unaffected by previous results. This cognitive bias can lead to misguided decisions in various domains such as gambling, investing, or even choosing camera settings based on past outcomes rather than current conditions.
