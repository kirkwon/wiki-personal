# The Beginning of Infinity - David Deutsch

David Deutsch's *The Beginning of Infinity* explores the nature of knowledge, progress, and explanatory depth. The book argues that the universe is comprehensible and that human progress depends on the creation and application of good explanations—principles that provide deep understanding rather than superficial rules.

## Key Ideas
- **Explanatory knowledge** is the foundation of progress.
- The universe is fundamentally knowable through principles and explanations.
- Human creativity and problem-solving depend on adopting correct principles.
- Deutsch connects principles to the broader capacity for error-correction and learning.

## Relevance to Principle Mental Model
Deutsch's perspective provides the philosophical foundation for understanding principles as deep explanatory frameworks rather than mere heuristics. His work emphasizes that principles enable humans to navigate complexity by providing actionable knowledge.
