# Jazz Improvisation

Jazz improvisation is the spontaneous creation of music within the framework of jazz standards or original compositions during live performances. It involves real-time composition, where musicians express themselves through melody, harmony, and rhythm, often interacting with other band members in a dynamic exchange. This concept parallels creative cooking techniques that rely on intuition and adaptation, as well as the unpredictable nature of capturing moments in photography or managing financial decisions based on real-time market changes.
