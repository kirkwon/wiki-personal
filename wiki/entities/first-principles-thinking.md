# First Principles Thinking

First Principles Thinking is an analytical approach that breaks down complex problems into their most fundamental components to derive solutions from basic truths or principles. In cooking, it involves understanding the core ingredients' properties; in photography, it means mastering light and composition basics; for personal finance, it entails recognizing essential financial concepts like budgeting and interest rates; and in cognitive science, it refers to studying basic mechanisms of thought processes.
