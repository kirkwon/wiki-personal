# Motivation crowding

Motivation crowding occurs when external rewards diminish intrinsic motivation to engage in an activity, such as cooking or photography, leading to a decrease in personal satisfaction and creativity. In personal finance, it can manifest as reduced enthusiasm for saving due to overemphasis on financial gains rather than the value of financial security. Cognitive science research suggests that this phenomenon may result from an individual's perception that their actions are being controlled by external incentives, undermining internal motivation.
