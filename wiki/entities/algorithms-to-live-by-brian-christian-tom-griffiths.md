# Algorithms to Live By - Brian Christian  Tom Griffiths

"Algorithms to Live By" by Brian Christian and Tom Griffiths explores the application of computational algorithms in everyday decision-making processes across various domains such as cooking (recipe optimization), photography (image processing techniques), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (understanding human thought patterns). It provides a framework for using data-driven approaches to enhance efficiency, creativity, and financial wellbeing.
