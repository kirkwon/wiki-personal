# Dialogue Agents

A Dialogue Agent is an AI system designed to simulate human conversation, providing interactive responses based on predefined rules or learned patterns. In the context of cooking, it can offer recipe guidance; for photography, suggest composition techniques; in personal finance, assist with budget planning and investment advice; and within cognitive science, facilitate discussions about mental processes and learning theories.
