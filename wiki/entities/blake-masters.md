# Blake Masters

Blake Masters is a former student of Peter Thiel, an author, and an entrepreneur who co-authored *Zero to One: Notes on Startups, or How to Build the Future*. He worked closely with Thiel to translate Thiel's venture capital philosophy and startup philosophy into the book's structured chapters, contributing to articulating the book's framework around innovation, competition, contrarian thinking, monopoly, and the creation of new value.
