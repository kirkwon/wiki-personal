# the-para-method---tiago-forte

The Para-Method is a structured approach to problem-solving that involves breaking down complex issues into smaller, manageable parts (the 'para' aspect) while considering the broader context or implications ('method'). Originating from Tiago Forte's principles of productivity and efficiency, it can be applied across various domains like Cooking (recipe breakdown), Photography (composition analysis), Personal Finance (budget planning), and Cognitive Science (studying decision-making processes).
