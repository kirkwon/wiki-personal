# The Stoic mental model

The Stoic mental model emphasizes rational control over emotions by focusing on what is within one's power to change while accepting external events as they are. In cooking, it translates to maintaining composure amidst kitchen mishaps; in photography, it means capturing moments without attachment to outcomes; for personal finance, it involves prudent decision-making irrespective of market fluctuations; and within cognitive science, it underscores the importance of understanding thought processes over emotional reactions.
