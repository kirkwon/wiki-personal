# crema

Crema refers to the creamy layer that forms on top of espresso coffee when it is freshly brewed. In photography, 'crema' denotes a rich, dark foam atop champagne or other effervescent beverages, often used as an artistic element in food and drink imagery. The term can also metaphorically describe the initial surge of financial gain after investing, though this usage is less common.
