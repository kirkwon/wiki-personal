# Zero to One - Peter Thiel & Blake Masters

"Zero to One is a book by Peter Thiel and Blake Masters that explores the concept of creating unique products or services in an oversaturated market. It emphasizes innovation through disruption and focuses on achieving dominance with one successful idea, drawing parallels between entrepreneurial ventures and personal endeavors such as cooking (unique recipes), photography (distinctive style), personal finance (original investment strategies), and cognitive science (groundbreaking research)."
