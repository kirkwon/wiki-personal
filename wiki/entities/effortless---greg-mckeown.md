# Effortless - Greg McKeown

Greg McKeown's 2024 book *Effortless* argues that striving to do everything leads to overwhelm, stress, reduced creativity, and strained relationships. Instead, he advocates identifying priorities, setting boundaries, delegating, building sustainable habits, practicing mindfulness, leveraging emotional intelligence, and balancing life areas.

## Key Chapters
- **Introduction: The Illusion of Doing It All** – Challenges societal obsession with productivity
- **Why We Over-Do It** – Societal expectations and personal pressures for achievement
- **The Hidden Costs** – Stress, reduced creativity, strained relationships
- **A Better Way** – Defining effortless living through prioritization
- **Priorities** – Identifying personal values to guide decisions
- **The Art of Saying No** – Boundaries free time and energy
- **Delegating** – Sharing burdens to focus on strengths
- **Sustainable Habits** – Routines minimize decision fatigue
- **Mindfulness** – Presence reduces distractions
- **Emotional Intelligence** – EQ improves interactions
- **Balanced Living** – Harmony across career, relationships, self-care

## When to Stop Optimizing
McKeown identifies warning signs: optimization becoming obsession, sacrificing relationships for efficiency, neglecting health, losing joy, measuring wrong things, rigid systems, using productivity to avoid deeper issues, and no longer being present.
