# Nir Eyal

Nir Eyal is a product design expert and author of *Hooked: How to Build Habit-Forming Products*. He developed the Hook Model, a four-phase framework for creating products that users return to habitually.

His work bridges behavioral psychology and product strategy, focusing on how variable rewards, user investment, and internal triggers drive engagement. He also writes on the ethical responsibilities of designers and users in habit-forming systems.

Key contributions:
- The Hook Model (Trigger → Action → Variable Reward → Investment)
- Integration of the Fogg Behavior Model into product design
- Ethical frameworks for evaluating habit-forming design
- Habit testing methodology for validating product engagement
