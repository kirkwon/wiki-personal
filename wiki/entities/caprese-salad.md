# caprese-salad

A caprese salad is an Italian dish consisting of sliced fresh mozzarella cheese, tomatoes, and basil leaves, often seasoned with salt, pepper, and olive oil. Its vibrant colors make it visually appealing for photography enthusiasts, while its simplicity in preparation aligns with principles of personal finance by minimizing costly ingredients.
