# So Good They Can't Ignore You - Cal Newport

"Cal Newport's 'So Good They Can't Ignore You' is a book that emphasizes the importance of developing unique skills in your chosen field to achieve professional success. It advocates for deliberate practice and deep work over generalist approaches, which can be applied across various domains such as cooking (mastering specific cuisines), photography (specializing in techniques or subjects), personal finance (developing expertise in investment strategies), and cognitive science (focusing on niche research areas)."
