# IRA Taxation

An Individual Retirement Account (IRA) is subject to tax treatment based on its type; traditional IRAs may offer immediate tax deductions but require mandatory withdrawals starting at age 72 with penalties for non-compliance, while Roth IRAs provide tax-free growth and distributions. Taxation of IRA contributions and earnings can vary depending on the individual's income level and whether they are covered by a workplace retirement plan.
