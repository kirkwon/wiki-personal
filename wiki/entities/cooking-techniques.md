# cooking-techniques

Cooking techniques refer to the methods and processes used to prepare food, including but not limited to chopping, baking, grilling, sautéing, steaming, and sous-vide. These techniques can significantly affect flavor development, texture, and nutritional value of dishes. Mastery of various cooking techniques is essential for both home chefs and professional culinary experts in creating diverse and high-quality meals.
