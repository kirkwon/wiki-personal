# analogical-reasoning-in-problem-solving

Analogical reasoning in problem solving involves drawing parallels between unrelated domains to generate insights or solutions. In cooking, it might mean applying a technique from one cuisine to another; in photography, using light principles across different scenes; for personal finance, comparing investment strategies; and within cognitive science, understanding how learning processes can be similar despite varied contexts.
