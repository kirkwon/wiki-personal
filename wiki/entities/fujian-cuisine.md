# fujian-cuisine

Fujian cuisine is an umbrella term encompassing the diverse culinary traditions of Fujian province in China, characterized by its use of fresh seafood, fragrant spices, and intricate cooking techniques such as steaming and braising. Known for dishes like oyster shell soup and fish balls, it reflects regional ingredients and cultural influences from neighboring countries.
