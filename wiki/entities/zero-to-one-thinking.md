# Zero to One thinking

Zero to One thinking is the process of generating ideas that do not currently exist or are unique, focusing on innovation rather than improvement. In cooking, it involves creating novel recipes; in photography, capturing unprecedented visuals; for personal finance, devising original investment strategies; and within cognitive science, exploring groundbreaking theories about the mind.
