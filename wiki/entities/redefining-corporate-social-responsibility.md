# redefining-corporate-social-responsibility

Redefining Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) involves integrating ethical practices into business operations beyond traditional philanthropy, focusing on sustainable development goals that align with societal values and environmental stewardship. It's about companies actively participating in social change by addressing issues like inequality, climate action, and community engagement through transparent and accountable measures.
