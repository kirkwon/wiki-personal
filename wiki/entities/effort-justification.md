# Effort justification

Effort justification is the psychological phenomenon where individuals assign greater value to an outcome that required significant effort to achieve. In cooking, this might mean valuing a complex dish more highly due to the time and skill invested; in photography, it could relate to appreciating a challenging shot after extensive practice; in personal finance, one may justify expensive investments with high returns; while in cognitive science, effort justification can explain why people persist in solving difficult problems.
