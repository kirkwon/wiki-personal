# roast-duck

A roast duck is a culinary dish featuring a whole duck that has been cooked by roasting in an oven or over an open flame until the skin becomes crispy while maintaining moist meat underneath. Photographically, capturing its golden-brown hue and succulent texture can be challenging yet rewarding. From a financial perspective, it's considered a luxury item due to its higher cost compared to other poultry options, reflecting socioeconomic status in consumption patterns. Cognitively, the preparation process involves both procedural memory and decision-making skills related to cooking techniques and seasoning choices.
