# Pre-Suasion: A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade

Pre-Suasion is the strategic application of psychological principles before an argument or decision to subtly influence perceptions and attitudes, enhancing persuasiveness in areas like cooking recipe adoption, photography style preferences, financial investment choices, and cognitive bias mitigation.


Pre-Suasion leverages the power of suggestion prior to presenting a case or argument, effectively setting the stage for more receptive audiences across diverse fields such as culinary arts, visual storytelling through photography, prudent financial planning, and critical thinking in cognitive science.
