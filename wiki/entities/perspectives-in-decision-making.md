# perspectives-in-decision-making

Perspectives-in-decision-making refers to the various viewpoints individuals adopt when making choices across different domains such as cooking (e.g., health vs. taste), photography (e.g., artistic expression vs. technical precision), personal finance (e.g., risk tolerance vs. long-term growth), and cognitive science (e.g., rational analysis vs. emotional intuition). These perspectives influence decision criteria, prioritization of options, and the overall approach to problem-solving within each field.
