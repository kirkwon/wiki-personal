# Εναλλακτικά Επενδύματα

'Εναλλακτικά Επενδύματα' refers to alternative investments that include a diverse range of assets such as real estate, private equity, hedge funds, and collectibles like art or vintage cameras. These are typically less liquid than traditional stocks and bonds but can offer higher returns and diversification benefits for personal portfolios in the context of Cooking (investing in culinary businesses), Photography (collectible photographic equipment), Personal Finance, and Cognitive Science (research funding).
