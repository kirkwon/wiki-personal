# Pecorino Romano

Pecorino Romano is a sharp, salty sheep's milk cheese from Rome and Lazio. It is the defining cheese in Roman pasta dishes and the key ingredient in cacio e pepe.

## Key Facts

- Made from sheep's milk (pecora = sheep in Italian)
- Intensely salty and sharp
- Finely grated for carbonara and cacio e pepe
- Distinct from Parmesan — different milk, different flavor profile
- Aged at least 8 months

## Usage in Pasta

In carbonara, Pecorino is combined with egg yolks to create a rich, creamy coating. In cacio e pepe, it is the sole cheese, emulsified with starchy pasta water and black pepper to form a silky sauce.
