# The Alignment Problem - Brian Christian Tom Griffiths

The Alignment Problem refers to the challenge of ensuring that artificial intelligence (AI) systems act in accordance with human values and ethics, particularly when their decision-making processes are complex and not fully transparent. In contexts like cooking or photography, this means developing AI tools that enhance creativity without compromising personal expression; for personal finance, it involves creating algorithms that prioritize fairness and individual benefit; in cognitive science, the problem lies in aligning machine learning with human cognition to improve understanding of both.
