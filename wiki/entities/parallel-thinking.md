# parallel-thinking

Parallel-thinking is an approach where multiple ideas or strategies are considered simultaneously to solve problems in cooking (recipe innovation), photography (composition techniques), personal finance (investment diversification), and cognitive science (neural network processing). It emphasizes the exploration of diverse perspectives to enhance creativity and efficiency.
