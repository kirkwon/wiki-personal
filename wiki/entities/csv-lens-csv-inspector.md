# csv-lens---csv-inspector

CSV-Lens is a tool designed to filter and analyze data from CSV files, which can be particularly useful in cooking for ingredient tracking or photography for organizing image metadata; it allows users to focus on specific datasets quickly. CSV-Inspector complements this by providing an in-depth analysis of the filtered data, offering insights that could aid personal finance management through expense categorization and trend identification, as well as cognitive science research by enabling detailed examination of experimental results stored in CSV format.
