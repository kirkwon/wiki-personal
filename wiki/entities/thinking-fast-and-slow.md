# thinking-fast-and-slow

The "Thinking Fast and Slow" concept refers to two distinct cognitive systems identified by psychologist Daniel Kahneman. The fast thinking system is intuitive, automatic, and emotional, often used in everyday decision-making like choosing recipes or composing photos. In contrast, the slow thinking system is deliberate, logical, and analytical, crucial for complex tasks such as financial planning and understanding cognitive biases.
