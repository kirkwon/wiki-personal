# Winner's curse

The Winner's Curse refers to the phenomenon where an individual who wins a bid or contest ends up overpaying due to incomplete information about other participants' valuations. In cooking, it might manifest as paying too much for rare ingredients; in photography, winning a prestigious award but at exorbitant costs; in personal finance, overspending on assets during auctions; and in cognitive science, the overestimation of one's value or skills leading to costly decisions.
