# Regime shifts in prediction

A regime shift in prediction refers to an unexpected change in the underlying patterns or behaviors that models use to forecast outcomes. In cooking, this might mean a new recipe suddenly alters taste profiles; in photography, it could be a sudden shift in lighting conditions affecting exposure; in personal finance, unforeseen market trends can disrupt investment predictions; and in cognitive science, it may involve an abrupt change in decision-making patterns.
