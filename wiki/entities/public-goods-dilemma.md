# Public goods dilemma

The Public Goods Dilemma refers to the situation where individuals face choices that affect collective resources without being able to exclude others from using them. In cooking, it's like sharing a kitchen space; in photography, it involves open-source image editing tools; in personal finance, it relates to public infrastructure funded by taxes; and in cognitive science, it pertains to the study of how people make decisions about shared resources.
