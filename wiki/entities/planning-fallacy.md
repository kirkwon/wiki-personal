# Planning fallacy

The planning fallacy is the tendency to underestimate the time required to complete future tasks, despite knowing historical evidence suggesting otherwise. This cognitive bias can lead to overly optimistic timelines in cooking recipes, photography projects, financial goals, and other personal endeavors, often resulting in delays or unmet expectations.
