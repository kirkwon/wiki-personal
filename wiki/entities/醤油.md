# 醤油

醤油 (jiāoyóu) is a staple condiment in Chinese cuisine, characterized by its rich, savory flavor derived from fermented soybeans. In cooking, it's used to enhance the taste of various dishes and can be found in both traditional recipes and modern fusion foods. Photographically, capturing the vibrant hues of 醤油 can showcase its depth and texture, while from a cognitive science perspective, studying cultural preferences for such flavors may reveal insights into taste perception and dietary habits.
