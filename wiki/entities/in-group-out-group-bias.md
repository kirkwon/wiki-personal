# In-group out-group bias

In-group out-group bias is the tendency to favor individuals within one's own group (e.g., fellow chefs or photographers) over those outside it, potentially influencing cooking recipes shared and photography styles adopted. This cognitive bias can extend into personal finance by affecting investment decisions towards familiar entities and in Cognitive Science as a subject of study for understanding social dynamics.
