# spices

Spices are natural substances derived from plants, seeds, fruits, roots, or bark used to flavor food. In cooking, they enhance taste profiles; in photography, spice-related imagery can add cultural depth; financially, the spice trade historically influenced economies; cognitively, studying spices' effects on sensory perception contributes to understanding human experience and memory formation.
