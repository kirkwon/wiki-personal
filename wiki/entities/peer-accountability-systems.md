# peer accountability systems

Peer accountability systems are structured frameworks where individuals within a group hold each other responsible for adherence to agreed-upon standards or goals. In contexts like cooking, this may involve recipe sharing and critique; in photography, it could mean peer reviews of work; in personal finance, mutual support in budget tracking; and in cognitive science, collaborative experiment design and data analysis verification.
