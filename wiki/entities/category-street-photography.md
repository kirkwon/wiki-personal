# Category:Street Photography

Category:Street Photography refers to the genre of photography that captures candid moments of everyday life in public spaces. It focuses on documenting human interactions, urban landscapes, and spontaneous events without posed subjects or staged settings. This category is distinct from other forms due to its emphasis on authenticity and immediacy.
