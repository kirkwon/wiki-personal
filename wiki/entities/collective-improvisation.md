# collective improvisation

Collective improvisation refers to the spontaneous collaboration among individuals with shared goals or interests, where each person contributes unique ideas or actions in real-time without a predefined plan. In cooking, it manifests as chefs adapting recipes on the fly; in photography, photographers coordinating shots during events; in personal finance, families making joint budget decisions dynamically; and in cognitive science, researchers developing hypotheses through group brainstorming sessions.
