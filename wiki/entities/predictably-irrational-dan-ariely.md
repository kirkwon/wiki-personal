# Predictably Irrational - Dan Ariely

Predictably Irrational is Dan Ariely's influential book on behavioral economics, documenting systematic patterns of irrationality in human decision-making. The book explores how price cues, social norms, and contextual factors distort judgment in predictable ways.

## Key Topics Covered
- Price anchoring and its effects on perceived value
- Zero-price effects and the irrational power of "free"
- Decoy pricing strategies and how they manipulate choices
- Arbitrary coherence—how arbitrary initial prices shape future valuations
- The gap between perceived value and actual cost

## Relevance to Mental Models
This work provides the empirical foundation for the [[price-mental-model]], showing how price functions as a conceptual shortcut for navigating complex situations involving value, cost, and perception.
