# emotional-decision

An emotional-decision is a choice made primarily influenced by one's feelings rather than objective analysis or logical reasoning. In cooking, it might involve selecting ingredients based on mood; in photography, choosing subjects that evoke personal sentiment; in personal finance, making impulsive purchases driven by emotional satisfaction; and within cognitive science, studying how emotions impact decision-making processes.
