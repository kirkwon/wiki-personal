# Προσαρμογή Αποχίρησης

Προσαρμογή Αποχίρησης refers to the strategic allocation of resources or assets to maximize returns while minimizing risks. In cooking, it's about balancing ingredients for optimal flavor; in photography, it involves choosing equipment that offers the best quality within budget constraints; in personal finance, it means diversifying investments to protect against market volatility; and in cognitive science, it relates to optimizing neural resource distribution during learning tasks.
