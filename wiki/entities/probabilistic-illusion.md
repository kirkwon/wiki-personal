# Probabilistic illusion

A probabilistic illusion refers to the cognitive bias where individuals misinterpret random data or events as having meaningful patterns or trends that do not actually exist. In cooking, this might manifest in overestimating the likelihood of a dish's success based on unrelated past experiences; in photography, it could involve perceiving intentional composition elements where none are present; within personal finance, one may erroneously predict market trends from random fluctuations; and in cognitive science, this illusion is studied to understand how the brain processes probabilities.
