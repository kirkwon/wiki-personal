# Sun Tzu

Ancient Chinese military strategist and philosopher, traditionally credited as the author of *The Art of War*, a foundational text on strategy, leadership, and competitive thinking. His work spans 13 chapters covering planning, terrain, tactics, intelligence, and adaptability, and has been applied broadly beyond military contexts to business, sports, negotiations, and leadership. His core ideas emphasize victory through superior planning, intelligence, adaptability, and positioning rather than direct confrontation.

## Key Contributions
- Five factors framework for strategic assessment (morale, weather, terrain, leadership, discipline)
- Seven calculations for evaluating competitive situations
- Water analogy for energy and momentum management — forces should be like water, flowing to exploit weakness
- Intelligence and espionage as force multipliers
- Terrain analysis as strategic advantage

## Key Facts
- Author of *The Art of War*, a classic strategic text
- His teachings emphasize victory without fighting as the highest form of generalship
- Emphasized intelligence gathering, terrain analysis, and adaptability
- Prolonged conflict is costly; rapid victory is preferable
