# Social Security Breakeven Analysis

Social Security Breakeven Analysis is the evaluation of the point at which an individual's lifetime earnings from Social Security benefits equal their contributions to the system. It assesses whether one will receive more in benefits than they have paid into over their working years, considering factors like retirement age and life expectancy.
