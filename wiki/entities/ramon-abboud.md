# Ramon Abboud

Author of *I Will Teach You to Be Rich*, a six-week personal finance program focused on automating finances, conscious spending, and index fund investing. The book's core philosophy is that consistency through systems beats perfection through willpower.

## Key Contributions

- **Conscious Spending**: Replaces traditional budgeting with a guilt-free framework of spending extravagantly on what you love and cutting mercilessly on what you don't.
- **The 4 Buckets Framework**: Fixed costs (50-60%), investments (10-20%), savings (5-10%), guilt-free spending (20-35%).
- **Automation Philosophy**: Financial success comes from building systems that handle money automatically, removing the need for ongoing willpower.
- **Employer Match Priority**: Contributing enough to get the full 401(k) match is the single highest-priority financial action.
