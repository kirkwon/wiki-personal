# cognitive-biases-and-heuristics

Cognitive biases and heuristics are mental shortcuts that influence decision-making processes across various domains like cooking (e.g., recipe improvisation), photography (e.g., composition choices), personal finance (e.g., investment decisions), and cognitive science research, often leading to systematic errors in judgment or perception.


In the context of cooking, these biases might manifest as overconfidence in improvising recipes without considering nutritional balance. In photography, they could lead to a preference for familiar compositions despite new techniques that may enhance images. Personal finance decisions are often swayed by biases like the endowment effect or loss aversion, affecting investment choices and savings behavior. Cognitive science studies these phenomena to understand how our brains process information and make judgments under uncertainty.
