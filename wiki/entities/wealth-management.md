# wealth-management

Wealth-management refers to the strategic planning and execution of financial activities aimed at preserving, growing, and optimizing an individual's assets over time. It encompasses investment advice, tax planning, estate planning, risk management, and retirement preparation, ensuring a holistic approach to personal finance that aligns with one's long-term goals and lifestyle aspirations.


In the context of cooking, wealth-management could involve budgeting for high-quality ingredients or investing in culinary education; within photography, it might mean managing funds to purchase equipment or fund a portfolio project; personal finance directly relates as it deals with financial planning and resource allocation; cognitive science intersects by applying decision-making theories and understanding biases that can affect wealth management choices.
