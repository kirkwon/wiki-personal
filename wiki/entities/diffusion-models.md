# Diffusion Models

A diffusion model is an approach used to simulate the spread of substances or ideas over time and space, often represented mathematically by partial differential equations. In cooking, it can describe how flavors blend in a dish; in photography, it models light distribution within scenes; in personal finance, it might illustrate market trends' impact on investments; while in cognitive science, it explains the dissemination of information through neural networks.
