# Simple logic

Simple logic refers to the fundamental principles of reasoning that guide decision-making processes across various domains such as cooking (choosing ingredients), photography (composition rules), personal finance (budget planning), and cognitive science (understanding thought patterns). It involves clear, straightforward thinking based on premises leading to a logical conclusion.
