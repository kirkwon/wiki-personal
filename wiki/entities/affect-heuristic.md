# Affect heuristic

The affect heuristic is a mental shortcut where our emotions (affect) influence decision-making processes, often leading to judgments based on positive or negative feelings rather than objective analysis. In cooking, this might mean choosing ingredients we love over healthier options; in photography, selecting images that evoke strong reactions; in personal finance, making impulsive purchases driven by emotional satisfaction; and within cognitive science, studying how affect shapes perceptions and choices.
