# Markov Decision Process (MDP)

A Markov Decision Process is a mathematical framework used to model decision making where outcomes are partly random and partly under the control of a decision maker. In contexts like cooking or photography, it can help in optimizing recipes or shots by considering probabilities of success for different actions; in personal finance, MDPs assist in maximizing long-term financial gains through sequential investment decisions; and in cognitive science, they model how humans make choices under uncertainty.
