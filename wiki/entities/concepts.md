# concepts

In cooking, concepts refer to fundamental ideas such as flavor profiles, cooking techniques (e.g., sautéing, baking), and ingredient combinations that form the basis of recipes and culinary practices. In photography, concepts encompass composition rules like the rule of thirds, lighting principles, and camera settings which are essential for capturing high-quality images. Personal finance concepts include budgeting, investment strategies, and financial planning methods crucial for managing one's economic resources effectively. Within cognitive science, concepts pertain to mental representations that categorize our understanding of the world, including schemas and heuristics used in learning and decision-making processes.
