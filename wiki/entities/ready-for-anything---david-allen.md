# Ready for Anything - David Allen

*Ready for Anything* is David Allen's follow-up to *Getting Things Done*, focusing on strategies for remaining flexible and responsive in the face of unpredictable tasks and changing circumstances.

## Key Themes

- Building resilience to unexpected demands and interruptions
- Creating buffers and slack in task systems
- Shifting from reactive to proactive task management
- Maintaining clarity and calm amid complexity

## Connection to Tasks Mental Model

Allen's emphasis on flexibility and pattern recognition in task management aligns with the [[tasks]] mental model's definition as a framework for navigating complex situations.
