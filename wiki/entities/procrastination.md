# procrastination

Procrastination is the intentional delay of tasks despite knowing that it may lead to negative consequences. In cooking, this could mean postponing meal preparation, resulting in unhealthy eating habits; in photography, missing crucial moments for capturing images; within personal finance, procrastinating on budgeting or investments can hinder financial growth; and from a cognitive science perspective, it reflects the struggle against self-regulatory processes that prioritize immediate gratification over long-term goals.
