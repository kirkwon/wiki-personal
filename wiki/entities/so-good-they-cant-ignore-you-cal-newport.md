# So Good They Cant Ignore You - Cal Newport

"Cal Newport's 'So Good They Can't Ignore You' is a book that applies principles of cognitive science to career development, emphasizing the importance of finding work that genuinely engages and challenges you. It suggests focusing on skills that are in high demand rather than following trends like cooking or photography fads, aligning with personal finance strategies for long-term success."

"The book also touches upon how mastery of a craft can lead to recognition and opportunities, which is relevant when considering the dedication required in both culinary arts and professional photography. However, it primarily advocates for career paths that offer deep engagement over those with immediate but fleeting appeal."
