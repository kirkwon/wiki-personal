# rule-of-72

The rule of 72 is a simplified formula used to estimate the number of years required to double an investment at a fixed annual rate of return by dividing 72 by that rate. While not directly applicable to cooking or photography, it's crucial in personal finance for understanding compound interest growth and can be linked to cognitive science through its implications on financial decision-making processes.
