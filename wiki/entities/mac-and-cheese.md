# Mac and Cheese

Baked mac and cheese is a quintessential Southern comfort dish, featuring elbow macaroni in a rich cheddar cheese sauce with a breadcrumb topping.

## Key Details
- **Pasta**: Elbow macaroni
- **Cheese**: Sharp cheddar
- **Sauce**: Béchamel base with cheddar
- **Topping**: Breadcrumbs (optional)
- **Bake temperature**: 375°F (190°C)
- **Bake time**: 30-40 minutes

## Technique
1. Cook macaroni al dente
2. Make cheese sauce (béchamel + cheddar)
3. Mix sauce with pasta, transfer to dish
4. Top with breadcrumbs
5. Bake 30-40 minutes at 375°F
