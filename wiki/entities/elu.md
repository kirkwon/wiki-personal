# ELU

Electronic Leakage Unit (ELU) refers to an imaginary device conceptualized within cognitive science that metaphorically represents the leakage of information from neural networks during processing tasks, akin to how unintended flavors might seep into dishes in cooking. In photography, it could symbolize the subtle loss of detail due to overexposure or underexposure. For personal finance, ELU may represent unexpected financial leakage through poor budget management.
