# わさび

わさび (wasabi) is a Japanese horseradish plant whose grated root forms the green paste used as a condiment in sushi dishes. Its pungent flavor enhances other tastes and can be appreciated for its complexity, similar to how focusing on details enriches photography composition or financial analysis. In cognitive science, it symbolizes the sharpness of sensory perceptions that contribute to taste discrimination.
