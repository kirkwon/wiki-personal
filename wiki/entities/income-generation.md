# income-generation

Income generation refers to the process of creating revenue streams through various activities or services. In cooking, this could involve selling homemade meals or catering; in photography, it's earning from portfolio sales and commissioned work; within personal finance, income-generation strategies might encompass investments and side hustles; and in cognitive science, researchers may generate income through consultancy or patent licensing.
