# scientific-method

The scientific method is an empirical approach involving systematic observation, measurement, experimentation, formulation of hypotheses, testing, analysis, and conclusion to understand phenomena or solve problems in various fields such as cooking (recipe optimization), photography (light exposure effects), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (memory retention techniques).


In the context of cooking, it entails experimenting with ingredient combinations to determine optimal flavor profiles. For photography, it means testing different camera settings under various light conditions to achieve desired exposures. In personal finance, applying this method involves tracking expenses and investments over time to identify patterns that lead to financial growth or loss. Lastly, in cognitive science, the scientific method is used to design experiments that test theories about how memory works, such as recall accuracy under different conditions.
