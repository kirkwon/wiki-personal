# Investment Strategy

An investment strategy is a systematic plan designed to allocate assets across various financial instruments with the aim of achieving specific financial goals while managing risk levels. It involves diversification, asset allocation, and periodic rebalancing based on market conditions and personal objectives. Investment strategies can be tailored to individual preferences and life stages, much like selecting ingredients for a recipe or choosing camera settings for photography.
