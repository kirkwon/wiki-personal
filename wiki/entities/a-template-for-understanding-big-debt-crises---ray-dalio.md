# A Template for Understanding Big Debt Crises - Ray Dalio

Ray Dalio's *A Template for Understanding Big Debt Crises* is a framework for analyzing how debt cycles interact with policy decisions across economies. The work provides structured templates for understanding the dynamics of debt buildup, deleveraging, and policy responses.^[raw/articles/Policies.md] The framework identifies recurring patterns in how excessive debt accumulates, leads to crises, and eventually resolves through deleveraging, restructuring, or other mechanisms.^[raw/articles/Crises.md]

This source underpins the [[policies]] mental model, framing policies as universal tools for navigating complex macroeconomic patterns and relationships.^[raw/articles/Policies.md]

## Core Framework
Dalio's template frames crises as patterned events with identifiable stages:
- **Debt accumulation** — Periods of rising leverage and credit expansion
- **Deleveraging** — When debt levels become unsustainable and begin to contract
- **Restructuring** — How debts are reorganized or written down
- **Recovery** — The path back to sustainable growth

This framework serves as a mental model applicable beyond pure economics, helping recognize crisis patterns in personal, organizational, and systemic contexts.^[raw/articles/Crises.md]
