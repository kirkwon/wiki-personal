# Tax Torpedoes in Retirement

A 'Tax Torpedo' is a retirement strategy involving the deferral of income taxes on certain assets until after withdrawal from retirement accounts, potentially reducing overall tax liability during one's working years and in retirement. This tactic requires careful planning to navigate complex tax laws and can significantly impact long-term financial outcomes for individuals nearing or in retirement.
