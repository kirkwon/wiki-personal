# problem-solving

Problem-solving is the process of identifying issues within cooking recipes, photography techniques, financial planning, or cognitive patterns to devise effective strategies for resolution. It involves critical thinking, analysis, and application of knowledge from various domains such as culinary arts, visual aesthetics, economic principles, and psychological theories to overcome challenges and achieve desired outcomes.
