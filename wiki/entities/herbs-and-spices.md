# Herbs and Spices

Herbs and spices are natural ingredients derived from plants used to enhance the flavor, color, and aroma of food. In cooking, they play an essential role in creating diverse culinary experiences, while their visual appeal is often captured through photography. From a cognitive perspective, their sensory impact can influence mood and memory recall, and understanding their economic value contributes to personal finance knowledge.
