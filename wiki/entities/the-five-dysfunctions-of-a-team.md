# The Five Dysfunctions of a Team

"The Five Dysfunctions of a Team" refers to the five key barriers that can hinder team effectiveness: lack of trust, fear of conflict, absence of commitment, avoidance of accountability, and inattention to results. In contexts like cooking or photography, these dysfunctions manifest as poor collaboration, missed deadlines, subpar outcomes, unresolved disagreements, and disregard for the final product's quality.

In personal finance, they translate into misaligned financial goals, avoidance of necessary discussions about money matters, lack of dedication to saving or investing plans, failure to hold each other accountable in spending habits, and neglect of long-term wealth accumulation. In cognitive science research, these dysfunctions can lead to flawed experiments, non-collaborative studies, inconsistent data interpretation, evasion of peer review responsibilities, and overlooking the broader implications of findings.
