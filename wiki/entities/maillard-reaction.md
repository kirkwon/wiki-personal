# Maillard reaction

The Maillard reaction is a chemical process between amino acids and reducing sugars that occurs when cooking food at high temperatures, resulting in the browning of food and development of complex flavors. This non-enzymatic browning contributes significantly to the taste and color of baked goods, roasted coffee, and seared meats. Understanding this reaction can enhance culinary techniques and photography by anticipating visual changes during cooking.
