# David Epstein

David Epstein is an author and journalist known for *The Sports Gene: Inside the Science of Extraordinary Athletic Performance* and *Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World*. His work examines how genetic and environmental factors interact to shape athletic performance, and how breadth of knowledge and diverse experience improve problem‑solving and decision‑making across domains.^[raw/articles/Environment.md]

Epstein argues that broad experience and interdisciplinary thinking often outperform narrow specialization in complex environments. His work connects to decision-making, learning strategies, and the value of diverse experience.^[raw/articles/Generalists.md]

His work challenges simplistic narratives of pure talent or pure effort, providing empirical grounding for the [[performance]] mental model.^[raw/articles/Performance.md]

His work connects to the [[experiences]] mental model and themes of [[specialization-and-generalization]].

## Key Works

- *The Sports Gene: Inside the Science of Extraordinary Athletic Performance* — Exploring the science of athletic performance. Demonstrates that extraordinary athletic performance emerges from multifactorial interactions rather than single explanations. Explores how genetics, environment, training, and opportunity interact to produce exceptional athletic outcomes.
- *Range: Why Generalists Triumph in a Specialized World* — Arguing for the power of generalists over specialists. Shows how broad experience and diverse knowledge enable pattern recognition and analogical reasoning across domains.^[raw/articles/Experiences.md]
