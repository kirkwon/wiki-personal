# Wok Cooking

Wok cooking is a high-heat stir-fry technique central to Asian cuisines, particularly Chinese, Thai, and other Southeast Asian cooking traditions. The wok's unique shape allows for rapid heat distribution and the development of [[wok-hei]], the characteristic "breath of the wok" char that results from extreme temperatures and constant tossing.

Key principles include:
- **High heat** — Woks are heated to very high temperatures before food is added
- **Constant motion** — Food is tossed frequently to ensure even cooking
- **Layered cooking** — Ingredients are added in sequence based on cook time
- **Seasoning in stages** — Aromatics, sauces, and seasonings are introduced at different points

This technique connects to [[flavor-theory]] through the importance of understanding how tastes combine under high-heat conditions, and to [[maillard-reaction]] as the browning process that generates deep flavor compounds during stir-frying.
