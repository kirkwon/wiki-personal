# Hoppin' John

Hoppin' John is a Southern dish of black-eyed peas and rice, traditionally eaten on New Year's Day for good luck.

## Key Details
- **Protein**: Black-eyed peas or field peas
- **Grain**: Rice
- **Smoked meat**: Pork or bacon
- **Seasonings**: Onion, bell pepper, Cajun seasoning
- **Cook time**: 45 minutes simmer + 20 minutes rice

## Technique
1. Sauté pork and vegetables
2. Add peas and broth
3. Simmer 45 minutes
4. Add rice
5. Cook 20 minutes until rice is done

The New Year's tradition connects to superstitions about luck and prosperity. [[comfort-food]]
