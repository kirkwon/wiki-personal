# working-backwards

Working backwards is the process of starting with the desired outcome or goal and then determining the steps needed to achieve it in reverse order. In cooking, this means planning a meal by first deciding on the dish you want to prepare before selecting ingredients. For photography, it involves envisioning the final image composition prior to taking shots. In personal finance, working backwards helps set savings goals based on future financial objectives. Within cognitive science, this approach can be used in problem-solving and hypothesis testing by analyzing results first and deducing possible causes or methods that led there.
