# Why We Work - Barry Schwartz

*Why We Work* is a book by psychologist Barry Schwartz that examines the motivations behind work and what gives work meaning. Schwartz argues that intrinsic factors — such as purpose, autonomy, and mastery — play a far greater role in satisfaction than commonly assumed, challenging purely economic models of labor motivation.^[raw/articles/Satisfaction.md]

This work is referenced in the [[satisfaction]] mental model as a cross-source perspective on what drives human engagement and fulfillment.
