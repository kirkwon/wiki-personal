# Ingredient Pairings

Ingredient pairings refer to the combination of different food ingredients that complement each other in taste, texture, or color, enhancing a dish's overall flavor profile. In photography, it relates to selecting elements that harmonize visually, while in personal finance, it denotes combining assets for balanced portfolios. From a cognitive science perspective, ingredient pairings can study the psychological effects of food combinations on perception and decision-making.
