# arabica

Arabica refers to the species of coffee plant (Coffea arabica) known for its higher acidity and complex flavor profile compared to Robusta beans. In cooking, Arabica is often preferred as a base ingredient due to its subtle taste that complements various dishes without overpowering them. From a cognitive science perspective, the consumption of Arabica coffee may influence mood and alertness levels due to its caffeine content and unique phytochemicals.
