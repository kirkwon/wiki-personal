# Survivorship bias

Survivorship bias is the logical error of focusing on individuals or entities that have passed through a selection process while overlooking those that did not due to their absence from current records. In cooking, it might lead to recipes based only on successful dishes; in photography, highlighting techniques used by popular photographers; in personal finance, analyzing investment strategies of the wealthy without considering failed cases; and in cognitive science, studying behaviors or outcomes from surviving subjects while ignoring those that did not.
