# Generalization

Generalization is the process of applying broad concepts or principles learned from specific instances to new, related situations. In cooking, it means using a recipe's basic techniques for different dishes; in photography, adapting composition rules across various subjects; in personal finance, transferring investment strategies to diverse financial goals; and in cognitive science, applying learning theories to understand behavior patterns beyond initial observations.
