# Velveting (cooking technique)

Velveting is a cooking technique used to tenderize meat by coating it with flour, egg white, or cornstarch before deep-frying or pan-frying. The process creates a protective barrier that prevents the surface from becoming tough and ensures even cooking. This method enhances texture and flavor, making it particularly beneficial for meats like pork and chicken.
