# BabyAGI

BabyAGI (Artificial General Intelligence) refers to an emerging class of AI systems with the ability to understand, learn from, and interact in a wide range of domains autonomously. In cooking, it could optimize recipes; in photography, enhance image processing; for personal finance, manage investments adaptively; and within cognitive science, model human-like reasoning processes.
