# Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM)

Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) is an advanced type of Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) that excels in learning from sequences with long-term dependencies, making it ideal for tasks where context over time matters significantly. In cooking, LSTM can predict recipe outcomes based on ingredient combinations; in photography, it aids in enhancing image quality by recognizing patterns across frames; and in personal finance, it assists with forecasting market trends or individual spending habits over time. Cognitively, LSTMs mimic the human brain's ability to remember past events, which is crucial for understanding complex sequences and making informed decisions based on historical data.
