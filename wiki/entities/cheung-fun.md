# Cheung Fun (肠粉)

Cheung Fun (肠粉, Chángfěn) — Rice Noodle Rolls — are silky, smooth rolls served hot with various fillings. It is a classic dim sum dish.

## Varieties

- **Shrimp**: Plump shrimp inside
- **Beef**: Thinly sliced beef, bean sprouts
- **BBQ pork**: Char siu
- **Plain**: Sweet soy sauce, sesame seeds

## Texture

Silky, smooth, slightly translucent, served hot.

## Hosting Tip

Best made fresh on the day — does not freeze well.
