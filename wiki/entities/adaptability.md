# adaptability

Adaptability refers to the ability to adjust one's strategies or behaviors effectively when faced with new challenges or changing circumstances in various domains such as cooking (modifying recipes), photography (changing techniques for different subjects), personal finance (altering budget plans), and cognitive science (applying learning theories). It is the skill of being flexible, responsive, and resourceful to optimize outcomes across diverse activities.
