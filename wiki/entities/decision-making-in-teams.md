# decision-making-in-teams

Decision-making in teams refers to the collaborative process where group members contribute diverse perspectives and expertise to reach consensus on choices that impact collective goals or outcomes. This approach leverages cognitive diversity to enhance problem-solving, particularly useful in fields like cooking (recipe development), photography (creative direction), personal finance (investment planning), and cognitive science (research design).
