# Evan Williams 12/18

Kentucky Bourbon with an age statement, serving as the budget sipper in the collection.

## Key Facts
- **Type**: Kentucky Bourbon (Age Statement)
- **Price**: $12-18
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐

## Tasting Notes
- **Nose**: Simple, corn-forward
- **Palate**: Sweet, straightforward
- **Finish**: Short, uncomplicated
- **Verdict**: Budget sipper, good for mixing

## Comparison
Moderate sweetness (★★★) with low complexity (★★) and short finish. Top-tier value (★★★★★) for the entry price tier.
