In cooking, post-processing refers to the steps taken after meal preparation, such as plating or garnishing, which enhances presentation and appeal. In photography, it involves editing images using software tools to improve quality or achieve a desired artistic effect. Within personal finance, post-processing could mean reviewing financial statements for insights into spending habits or investment performance.
