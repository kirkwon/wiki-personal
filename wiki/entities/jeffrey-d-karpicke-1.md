# Jeffrey D. Karpicke

Jeffrey D. Karpicke is an esteemed cognitive scientist known primarily for his research on the science of learning and memory retention. With a focus on metacognition and effective study strategies, he has contributed significantly to educational psychology through studies like the "Forgetting Illusion." Although not directly involved in cooking or photography, his work indirectly supports these fields by improving how individuals learn and remember recipes or techniques.
