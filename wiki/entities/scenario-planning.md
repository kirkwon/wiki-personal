# Scenario-planning

Scenario planning is the strategic process of envisioning and analyzing possible future events or conditions to inform decision-making. In cooking, it involves considering various culinary outcomes based on ingredient availability; in photography, it entails anticipating different lighting scenarios for optimal shots; within personal finance, it's about preparing for diverse economic situations; and in cognitive science, it pertains to predicting potential thought patterns or behaviors.
