# Storytelling in communication

Storytelling in communication is the art of conveying information through narrative techniques to engage audiences effectively. It involves structuring content with a clear beginning, middle, and end, often using personal anecdotes or relatable scenarios that resonate across various contexts like cooking recipes, captivating photography captions, financial advice stories, and cognitive science concepts.
