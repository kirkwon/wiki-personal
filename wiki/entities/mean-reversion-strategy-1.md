# Mean Reversion Strategy

A Mean Reversion Strategy is an investment approach that assumes asset prices will revert to their historical average over time. In cooking, it's akin to balancing flavors; in photography, adjusting exposure for even light; in personal finance, diversifying assets to mitigate risks; and in cognitive science, the belief that perceptions of reality will stabilize after fluctuations.
