# Dex‑NVG

Dex-NVG refers to Dexcom's Continuous Glucose Monitoring system designed with Night Vision (NVG) compatibility. It allows users, including those interested in cognitive science and photography, to monitor glucose levels during low-light conditions without compromising accuracy or visual clarity. This technology can be particularly useful for individuals managing diabetes while engaging in activities like cooking or nighttime photography sessions.
