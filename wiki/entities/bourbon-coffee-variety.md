# Bourbon Coffee Variety

Bourbon is a premium Arabica coffee variety originating from Ethiopia and Burma. It is characterized by high quality, sweet, complex flavors and is widely used in premium single-origin coffee.

## Key Characteristics
- **Origin**: Ethiopia/Burma
- **Character**: High quality, sweet, complex
- **Growth**: Slower but better quality
- **Use**: Premium single-origin coffee

Bourbon is one of the most prized Arabica cultivars, valued for its refined flavor profile and consistency. It performs well in high-altitude growing regions and is a cornerstone of specialty coffee production.
