# Neoophobia

Neoophobia is the fear of new things or experiences, which can manifest as anxiety when faced with unfamiliar cooking techniques, photographic concepts, financial products, or cognitive theories. In personal development and learning contexts like cooking or photography, it may hinder experimentation; in finance, it could prevent adaptation to innovative investment strategies; while in cognitive science, it might limit the exploration of novel research ideas.
