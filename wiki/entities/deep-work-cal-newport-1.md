# deep-work - Cal Newport

Deep work is a concept introduced by Cal Newport where individuals focus intensively on a single task without distractions to achieve high levels of productivity and skill mastery. In the contexts of cooking, this could mean perfecting a recipe; for photography, it's about honing one's technique; in personal finance, it involves concentrated learning on investment strategies; while in cognitive science, deep work relates to conducting rigorous research or experiments.
