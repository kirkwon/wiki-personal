# Peter Thiel

Peter Thiel is a technology investor, venture capitalist, entrepreneur, and author, best known for co-authoring *Zero to One* with Blake Masters. He co-founded PayPal and Palantir Technologies, founded the venture capital firm Founders Fund, and is a central figure in the [[thiel-mental-model]] framework. His philosophy emphasizes contrarian thinking, the creation of entirely new things (going from 0 to 1) rather than copying existing models, and the importance of monopolies in driving technological progress.

## Key Ideas
- Competition is for losers; monopolies drive progress
- Contrarian thinking is essential for discovering overlooked opportunities
- Focus on secrets and undiscovered truths about the world
- Technology and globalization reshape how value is created
- First mover advantage, differentiation, secrecy, and deep customer understanding are critical to building transformative companies
- Distinguish between horizontal progress (copying) and vertical progress (creating something entirely new)

## Investment Philosophy
His investment philosophy emphasizes identifying companies that represent "zero to one" breakthroughs — entirely new products or markets — rather than incremental improvements. He argues that first mover advantage, differentiation, secrecy, and deep customer understanding are critical to building transformative companies.
