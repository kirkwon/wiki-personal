# Sigmoid

In cooking, the sigmoid function can represent flavor intensity over time as ingredients meld together. In photography, it models exposure curves where there's a gradual increase to optimal brightness before saturation. In personal finance, it could describe investment growth with diminishing returns at higher values, and in cognitive science, the sigmoid curve illustrates learning progressions from initial confusion to eventual mastery.
