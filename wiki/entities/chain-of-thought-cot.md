# Chain‑of‑Thought (CoT)

A Chain-of-Thought (CoT) is an analytical process where one logically connects ideas or steps sequentially to reach a conclusion or solve a problem. In the context of cooking, it involves linking ingredients and techniques for recipe creation; in photography, it's about connecting camera settings with desired outcomes; in personal finance, CoT is used to link financial decisions to long-term goals; and within cognitive science, it refers to understanding how thoughts progress from premise to conclusion.
