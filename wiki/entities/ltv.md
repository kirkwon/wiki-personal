# Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)

A financial leverage metric expressing the loan amount as a percentage of the property's value.

## Risk Thresholds
- LTV < 60%: Low leverage risk
- LTV 60-75%: Medium leverage risk
- LTV > 75%: High leverage risk

Higher LTV increases risk exposure. LTV is tracked as a Key Risk Indicator alongside DSCR, cash flow variance, and vacancy rates.
