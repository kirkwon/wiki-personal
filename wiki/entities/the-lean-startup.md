# The Lean Startup

The Lean Startup is an approach to building businesses that focuses on short product development cycles to test market assumptions, adapt quickly, and avoid the waste of resources on unviable products. It emphasizes customer feedback over intuition and encourages iterative design to refine offerings based on real-world usage data.


In cooking terms, it's like experimenting with recipes using minimal ingredients before scaling up; in photography, it's akin to shooting multiple shots to find the best composition quickly; for personal finance, it translates to making small investments and adjusting strategies based on performance; and from a cognitive science perspective, it mirrors hypothesis testing with minimal assumptions.
