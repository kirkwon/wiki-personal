# Presence_ Human Purpose and the Field of the Future - Peter M Senge

Peter M. Senge's concept of 'Presence' emphasizes the importance of being fully engaged in activities like cooking or photography to foster personal growth and innovation. In terms of human purpose, it suggests that our future lies in cultivating mindfulness and deep connection with our passions, which can lead to breakthroughs in fields such as cognitive science and beyond.
