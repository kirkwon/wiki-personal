# Gary Klein

Gary Klein is an American research psychologist known for pioneering the field of [[naturalistic-decision-making]] (NDM). Unlike laboratory-based decision researchers who study novices making hypothetical choices, Klein spent decades studying experts in real-world, high-stakes environments — fireground commanders, emergency room physicians, military officers, nuclear power plant operators, nurses, chess players, and business decision-makers. His field studies, documented in *Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions*, revealed that expert decisions rely on rapid pattern recognition and mental simulation rather than systematic option comparison.

Klein's key insight was that experts rarely compare options analytically. Instead, they recognize patterns from past experience, evaluate them through mental simulation, and commit to a course of action — often within seconds. He formalized this as the [[recognition-primed-decision-model]] (RPD), which argues that intuition is not mysterious gut feeling but rapid pattern matching honed by thousands of hours of practice.

His power sources framework identifies intuition, mental simulation, metaphor, storytelling, conscious thinking, and experience as the foundations of expert judgment. His work challenges classical decision theory and contrasts with [[daniel-kahneman]]'s emphasis on cognitive biases — Kahneman focuses on how intuition fails, Klein on how it succeeds. He argues that intuition, when built on extensive experience, is a reliable and powerful decision-making tool.

Although their conclusions seem to contrast, Klein and Kahneman engaged in a productive adversarial collaboration in the 2000s. Their joint work, culminating in a co-authored article and mutual respect, demonstrated that both perspectives are valid for different contexts and levels of expertise. Their dialogue is explored in [[two-views-of-decision-kahneman-vs-klein]].

## Key Works

- *Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions* (1998) — The foundational text of [[naturalistic-decision-making]], studying expert decision-making in the wild
- *Streetlights and Shadows: Searching for the Keys to Adaptive Decision Making* (2009) — A more accessible treatment of NDM principles for general audiences
- *The Power of Intuition: How to Use Your Gut Feelings to Make Better Decisions at Work* (2003) — Practical applications of the RPD model for professionals
- *Working Minds: A Practitioner's Guide to Cognitive Task Analysis* (2000, with others) — A methodological guide for studying expert cognition

## Influence

- Founded the [[naturalistic-decision-making]] movement, which now has annual conferences, a professional society, and applications in healthcare, aviation, firefighting, and military command
- His [[recognition-primed-decision-model]] is used in training programs for firefighters, emergency responders, and military commanders worldwide
- His work on [[mental-simulation]] as a cognitive tool for decision-making influenced training methods and user interface design
- The adversarial collaboration with [[daniel-kahneman]] helped bridge the gap between the heuristics-and-biases and naturalistic decision-making traditions
- His power sources framework informs recommendations for training, technology design, and organizational culture that align with how people actually decide
