# Deep Work

Deep work refers to the practice of focusing intently on complex tasks without distraction, leading to higher levels of expertise in areas like cooking or photography. In personal finance, it involves concentrated effort on understanding and improving financial strategies. From a cognitive science perspective, deep work is essential for strengthening neural connections related to skill mastery and knowledge retention.
