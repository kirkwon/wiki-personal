# identity

Identity refers to the unique combination of characteristics, values, experiences, and skills that define an individual's sense of self across various domains such as cooking (culinary preferences), photography (photographic style), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (thought processes). It encompasses the distinctive traits that differentiate one person from another in these areas.
