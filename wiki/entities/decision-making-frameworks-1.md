# Decision Making Frameworks

Decision Making Frameworks are structured approaches used to analyze choices systematically across various domains such as cooking (recipe selection), photography (composition techniques), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (perception of stimuli). They provide a step-by-step methodology for evaluating options, considering consequences, and making informed decisions.
