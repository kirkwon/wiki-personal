# LLC vs S Corporation

Side-by-side comparison of the two most common pass-through entity structures for small businesses and real estate investors.

## Tax Treatment
- **LLC**: Default pass-through taxation. Can elect corporate taxation via Form 8832. Self-employment tax on all net earnings.
- **S Corp**: Pass-through taxation by default (Form 2553 election). Self-employment tax only on reasonable compensation (salary); distributions not subject to SE tax.

## Liability Protection
- Both provide full limited liability protection for members/shareholders from business debts and obligations.

## Compliance Burden
- **LLC**: Moderate. Requires operating agreement, annual filings, state fees. No requirement for board meetings or corporate formalities.
- **S Corp**: Higher. Must maintain corporate formalities (board meetings, shareholder meetings, minutes). Must pay reasonable compensation to shareholder-employees. More complex record-keeping.

## Profit Allocation
- **LLC**: Flexible — income/losses can be allocated according to ownership percentage or custom agreement.
- **S Corp**: Less flexible — distributions must correspond to stock ownership. Only one class of stock allowed.

## Growth Potential
- **LLC**: High — can scale and add members easily.
- **S Corp**: Medium-high — capped at 100 shareholders, one class of stock limits equity fundraising.

## Best For
- **LLC**: Small businesses, real estate investors, family businesses, startups testing concepts.
- **S Corp**: High-earning sole proprietors seeking self-employment tax optimization, small businesses with growth potential and multiple employees.

## Key Considerations
- S Corps require paying yourself a "reasonable compensation" salary, which can be complex to determine.
- LLCs offer more flexibility in profit distribution and ownership structure.
- For real estate investors, LLCs are often preferred due to simplicity and multi-entity structuring flexibility.
- Conversion between entity types is possible but involves tax implications and compliance requirements.
