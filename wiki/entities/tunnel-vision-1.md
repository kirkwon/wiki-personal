# Tunnel vision

Tunnel vision refers to the cognitive phenomenon where an individual focuses intensively on one specific area or task while neglecting others. In cooking, it might mean concentrating solely on a recipe's steps; in photography, focusing only on framing and composition; in personal finance, prioritizing investments without considering broader financial health; and in cognitive science, studying one aspect of perception or decision-making at the expense of understanding the whole picture.
