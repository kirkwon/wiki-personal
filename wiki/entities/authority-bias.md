# Authority bias

Authority bias is the tendency to attribute greater accuracy or validity to the opinions of an authority figure, regardless of the content's actual merit. In cooking, this may lead one to follow a celebrity chef's recipe without question; in photography, it could cause adherence to a renowned photographer's techniques; within personal finance, individuals might unquestioningly accept advice from financial experts; and in cognitive science, researchers might give undue weight to findings presented by established scientists.
