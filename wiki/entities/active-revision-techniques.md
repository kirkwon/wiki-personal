# Active revision techniques

Active revision techniques are strategies that involve engaging with the material to enhance memory retention and understanding. These methods include self-quizzing, teaching concepts to others, and applying knowledge in practical scenarios, such as experimenting recipes or adjusting camera settings based on learned principles.
