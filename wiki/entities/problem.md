# problem

A problem is an obstacle or challenge that hinders progress towards achieving desired outcomes in various domains. In cooking, it could be a recipe gone wrong; in photography, it might be capturing the perfect shot under difficult lighting conditions; within personal finance, it's managing debt or saving for goals; and in cognitive science, it refers to understanding complex mental processes.
