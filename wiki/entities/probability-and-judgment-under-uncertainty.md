# Probability and judgment under uncertainty

Probability under uncertainty refers to the likelihood of various outcomes when making decisions without complete information. In cooking, it guides ingredient adjustments; in photography, it informs exposure settings for desired results; in personal finance, it aids in risk assessment and investment choices; and in cognitive science, it explains how individuals estimate chances to make rational judgments.
