# Compounding

In cooking, compounding refers to the combination of ingredients to create complex flavors or textures in dishes. In photography, it's a technique where multiple exposures are combined into a single image for enhanced depth and detail. Financially, compounding involves reinvesting interest rather than taking it out, leading to exponential growth over time. Cognitively, compounding can describe the process of building upon existing knowledge or memories to form new insights or understandings.
