# the-sports-gene

The "sports gene" refers to the theory that certain genetic factors may predispose individuals to excel in athletic performance. It encompasses a range of inherited traits linked to physical abilities such as muscle composition, oxygen utilization, and injury recovery. However, this concept is nuanced by environmental influences and training, emphasizing the complex interplay between genetics and lifestyle choices in sports success.
