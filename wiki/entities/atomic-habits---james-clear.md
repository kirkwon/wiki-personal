# Atomic Habits - James Clear

James Clear's *Atomic Habits* presents a framework for change through small, incremental habit formation. The book emphasizes identity-based behavior change, environment design, and the compounding power of tiny improvements over time.

## Key Ideas
- 1% improvements compound into remarkable results
- Identity-based habit change: shifting who you believe you are
- Environment design as the foundation of behavior change
- The four laws of behavior change: make it obvious, attractive, easy, satisfying

## Connection to Change

Clear's framework represents habit-based change — the principle that sustainable change comes not from dramatic overhauls but from small, consistent improvements in daily behavior.
