# jiangsu-cuisine

Jiangsu cuisine refers to the traditional food culture of Jiangsu province in China, characterized by its diverse flavors, emphasis on fresh ingredients, and regional specialties such as Suzhou's dim sum and Nanjing's salted duck. As a cooking enthusiast, exploring Jiangsu cuisine offers insights into the cultural heritage and culinary techniques that can be captured through photography. Understanding its historical context also provides an interesting perspective for cognitive science studies on food perception and memory.
