# Multipliers - Greg McKeown

Greg McKeown's concept of Multipliers refers to individuals who amplify the impact of their work through influence, inspiration, and collaboration, much like how a catalyst accelerates a chemical reaction. In contexts such as cooking or photography, multipliers can elevate culinary experiences or visual storytelling by sharing innovative techniques. Within personal finance and cognitive science, they drive change by disseminating knowledge that leads to greater collective financial well-being and enhanced understanding of the mind.
