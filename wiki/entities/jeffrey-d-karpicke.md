# Jeffrey D. Karpicke

Research psychologist and co-author of *Make It Stick*. Karpicke is known for his experimental work on testing effects and spaced learning, providing the empirical foundation for many of the book's recommendations.

## Key Contributions
- Pioneered research on retrieval practice and its effects on long-term retention.
- Conducted influential experiments demonstrating the testing effect—how the act of retrieving information strengthens memory.
