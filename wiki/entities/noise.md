# Noise

In cooking, noise refers to unwanted sounds that can disrupt the process or concentration during food preparation. In photography, noise denotes visual disturbances in an image, often due to high ISO settings leading to grainy textures. Within personal finance and cognitive science, 'noise' signifies extraneous information or variables that may obscure clear decision-making or data interpretation.
