# Smoky Flavor

A 'Smoky Flavor' refers to the distinct taste imparted by smoke during cooking processes such as grilling or smoking meats, which is characterized by a rich, savory profile with hints of char and woodiness. In photography, it describes an atmospheric effect where images have a hazy, ethereal quality reminiscent of being in a dimly lit environment. From a cognitive science perspective, the term can metaphorically represent the lingering impact or memory associated with experiences that leave a lasting impression on one's mind.
