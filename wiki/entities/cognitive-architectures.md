# Cognitive Architectures

Cognitive architectures are computational models designed to simulate human thought processes and behaviors. These frameworks integrate various cognitive functions like memory, learning, reasoning, and problem-solving, providing a structured approach to understanding complex mental activities in fields such as cooking recipes, photographic composition techniques, personal finance management, and broader cognitive science research.
