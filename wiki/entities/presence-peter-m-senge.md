# Presence - Peter M Senge

Presence, as defined by Peter M. Senge, is the state of being fully engaged and attentive in an activity or interaction, fostering deep learning through mindfulness and reflective observation. In cooking, it translates to a chef's focused attention on their craft; in photography, it means capturing moments with intentionality; for personal finance, it involves conscious decision-making; and within cognitive science, it pertains to the heightened awareness of one's thought processes.
