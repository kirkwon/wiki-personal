# Paradox of choice

The Paradox of Choice refers to the phenomenon where an overabundance of options leads to decision paralysis, increased anxiety, and potential dissatisfaction with choices made due to perceived missed opportunities. In cooking, it can result in longer meal prep times; in photography, it may cause indecision on equipment or style; for personal finance, an overwhelming number of investment options might hinder effective financial planning; and within cognitive science, this paradox illustrates how too many choices can strain mental resources.
