# Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) is an AI training methodology where human input guides the learning process to improve decision-making algorithms. In contexts like cooking or photography, it enables systems to adapt recipes or camera settings based on user preferences; in personal finance, it helps tailor investment strategies to individual goals and risk tolerances; while in cognitive science, RLHF can model human learning patterns more accurately.
