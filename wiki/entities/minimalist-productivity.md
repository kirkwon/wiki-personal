# minimalist-productivity

Minimalist productivity is the practice of achieving maximum efficiency by simplifying tasks and eliminating unnecessary elements in various domains such as cooking (by using fewer ingredients), photography (through minimal composition), personal finance (with streamlined budgeting), and cognitive science (by reducing mental clutter). It focuses on essential actions to enhance productivity without excess.
