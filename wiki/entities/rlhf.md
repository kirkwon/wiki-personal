# RLHF

Real-time Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF) is an approach where human input guides the learning process in real time to improve outcomes or performance. In cooking, it could mean adjusting recipes based on taste tests; in photography, fine-tuning camera settings through viewer reactions; for personal finance, tailoring investment strategies with expert advice; and in cognitive science, refining models of human thought patterns using immediate feedback from experiments.
