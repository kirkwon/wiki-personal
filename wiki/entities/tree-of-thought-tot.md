# Tree‑of‑Thought (ToT)

A Tree-of-Thought (ToT) is a structured mental model that maps out the logical connections between ideas or steps in problem-solving across various domains such as cooking recipes, photography techniques, financial planning strategies, and cognitive processes. It visually represents decision paths and potential outcomes to enhance clarity and efficiency in complex tasks.


In personal finance, a ToT can help organize investment options by branching into different risk levels, expected returns, and time horizons, guiding more informed financial decisions. In photography, it assists in understanding the relationship between lighting conditions, composition rules, and camera settings to achieve desired outcomes. For cooking, a ToT can outline recipe steps from ingredient selection to final plating, while in cognitive science, it illustrates how different theories interconnect to explain human thought processes.
