# not-invented-here-syndrome

Not Invented Here Syndrome (NIH) is the tendency to reject or undervalue ideas, products, or innovations that originate outside one's own group or personal experience. In cooking, it manifests as dismissal of foreign cuisines; in photography, ignoring techniques from other cultures; in personal finance, overlooking global investment strategies; and in cognitive science, disregarding research methodologies developed elsewhere.
