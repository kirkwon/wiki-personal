# Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment

"Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment," by Philip E. Tetlock, explores the impact of random variations (noise) on decision-making processes across various domains including cooking, where it affects recipe outcomes; photography, influencing shot selection and composition; personal finance, leading to inconsistent investment choices; and cognitive science, shedding light on how our brains process information amidst uncertainty. The book emphasizes the importance of recognizing and mitigating noise for better judgment in everyday life.
