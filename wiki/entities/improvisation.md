# improvisation

Improvisation is the spontaneous creation or modification of content without prior planning. In cooking, it involves adapting recipes on-the-fly based on available ingredients; in photography, it means capturing moments as they unfold unexpectedly; within personal finance, improvisation refers to making quick decisions with limited information; and in cognitive science, it pertains to the brain's ability to generate novel solutions or ideas without premeditation.
