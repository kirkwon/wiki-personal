# OpenAI Five

OpenAI Five refers to the AI system developed by OpenAI that played real-time, competitive video games against human opponents in Dota 2, a complex strategy game requiring strategic thinking and adaptability. This demonstration showcases advanced machine learning capabilities for decision making under uncertainty. While not directly related to cooking or photography, it exemplifies the potential of AI in cognitive tasks that involve pattern recognition, planning, and real-time adaptation—skills valuable across various domains including personal finance management.
