# Mirroring neurons

Mirroring neurons are brain cells that fire both when an individual performs an action and when they observe someone else performing the same action. This discovery in cognitive science suggests a neural basis for empathy and understanding others' intentions, which can be relevant to cooking (understanding recipes), photography (interpreting subjects' expressions), personal finance (reading market trends), and social interactions.
