# Deterministic Policy Gradient (DPG)

Deterministic Policy Gradient is an algorithm used to optimize decision-making processes by learning the best actions to take in given states, similar to how a recipe guides cooking steps or camera settings adjustments for photography. In personal finance, it helps in making optimal investment choices over time, and in cognitive science, it models how humans might learn from experience to improve decision-making strategies.
