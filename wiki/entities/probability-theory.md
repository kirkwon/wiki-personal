# Probability Theory

Probability theory is the branch of mathematics concerned with analyzing random phenomena and quantifying uncertainty through mathematical models. In cooking, it helps predict ingredient substitution outcomes; in photography, it aids in assessing light exposure probabilities; for personal finance, it underpins investment risk evaluation; and within cognitive science, it contributes to understanding decision-making processes amidst uncertainty.
