# Category:Prep Techniques

Prep techniques refer to the systematic methods used prior to engaging in an activity to enhance efficiency and effectiveness. In cooking, this includes mise en place; in photography, it involves pre-shoot preparations like lighting setup; for personal finance, it entails budget planning and goal setting; while in cognitive science, prep techniques might involve priming or mental rehearsal to optimize performance.
