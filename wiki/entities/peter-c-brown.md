# Peter C. Brown

Co-author of [[make-it-stick-the-science-of-successful-learning]], Peter C. Brown worked with researchers [[jeffrey-d-karpicke]] and [[henry-l-roediger-iii]] to translate decades of cognitive psychology research into practical advice for learners and educators. His book synthesizes evidence-based learning techniques such as retrieval practice, spaced repetition, interleaving, and elaborative interrogation, challenging common study habits in favor of strategies grounded in cognitive science.

## Key Contributions
- Co-authored *Make It Stick*, which promotes evidence-based techniques like retrieval practice, spaced repetition, interleaving, and elaborative interrogation.
- Collaborated with leading memory researchers to make cognitive science accessible to general audiences.
- His work is referenced as a source supporting the [[techniques]] mental model, emphasizing that effective learning strategies are a form of pattern recognition for navigating complexity.
