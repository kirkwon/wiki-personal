# Tax-Loss Harvesting

Tax-Loss Harvesting is the strategic selling of underperforming investments at a loss to offset capital gains tax liabilities on other profitable assets. This financial maneuver, much like adjusting ingredients for optimal flavor in cooking or framing a shot with negative space in photography, seeks to minimize the overall tax burden and enhance portfolio efficiency over time.


In cognitive science terms, it's an example of loss aversion where individuals aim to reduce losses (tax liabilities) rather than just seeking gains.
