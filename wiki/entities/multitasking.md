# multitasking

Multitasking refers to the ability to perform multiple tasks simultaneously or switch between them efficiently. In cooking, it involves managing various recipes at once; in photography, it's about capturing different shots concurrently; personal finance multitasking entails balancing investments and expenses; cognitive science studies how the brain handles these simultaneous tasks without significant loss of performance.
