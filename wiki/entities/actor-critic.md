# Actor‑Critic

The Actor-Critic method is an approach that combines value function estimation (the Critic) with policy improvement (the Actor), akin to balancing recipe adjustments for cooking perfection and evaluating photo composition in photography, while also managing personal finance investment strategies. In cognitive science, it parallels the process of learning from feedback to refine decision-making skills.
