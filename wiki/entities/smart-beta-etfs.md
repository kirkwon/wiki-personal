# Smart Beta ETFs

Smart Beta ETFs are investment funds that aim to outperform market benchmarks by using alternative index construction rules based on factors like size, value, momentum, or quality, rather than solely following traditional market capitalization-weighted indices. Similar to adjusting ingredients for a recipe in cooking or choosing the right camera settings for photography, Smart Beta ETFs offer tailored investment strategies that can potentially enhance returns and reduce risk compared to conventional index funds.


In cognitive science terms, they represent an approach to decision-making under uncertainty by leveraging systematic patterns in market data, akin to using heuristics for efficient problem-solving. For personal finance enthusiasts, Smart Beta ETFs provide a structured way to diversify investment portfolios and pursue specific financial goals with an emphasis on performance over the long term.
