# Man's Search for Meaning

"Man's Search for Meaning," authored by Viktor Frankl, is a profound exploration of human resilience through the lens of existential psychology and logotherapy, emphasizing that our primary drive in life is to find meaning. It intersects with cooking as it discusses how creating nourishing meals can be an act of purposeful living; with photography by highlighting capturing moments as a quest for significance; in personal finance through the lens of value-driven spending and investment, and within cognitive science by examining the psychological underpinnings that guide our search for meaning.
