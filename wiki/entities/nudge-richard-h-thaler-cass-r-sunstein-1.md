# Nudge - Richard H Thaler  Cass R Sunstein

A nudge is an aspect of choice architecture that alters people's behavior in a predictable way without forbidding any options or significantly changing their economic incentives. Richard Thaler and Cass Sunstein popularized the concept, emphasizing its application in personal finance to encourage better decision-making through subtle prompts rather than direct control.


In cooking and photography, nudges could manifest as recipe recommendations or photo editing tips that guide users towards more satisfying results without restricting creative freedom. In cognitive science, understanding how nudges influence behavior can help in designing interventions to improve decision-making processes.
