# Expert performance vs simple models

Expert performance refers to the advanced skills and knowledge that individuals possess, allowing them to excel in areas like cooking with complex recipes or photography using sophisticated techniques. Simple models, on the other hand, are basic approaches or tools used for fundamental tasks such as budgeting personal finances or understanding cognitive processes at a rudimentary level.
