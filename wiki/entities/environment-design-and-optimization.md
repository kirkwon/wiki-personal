# environment-design-and-optimization

Environment design and optimization refer to the strategic arrangement of elements within cooking (e.g., kitchen layout), photography (e.g., lighting setup), personal finance (e.g., budget allocation), and cognitive science (e.g., study environments) to enhance efficiency, effectiveness, and overall experience. It involves analyzing the current state, identifying areas for improvement, and implementing changes that lead to optimal outcomes in each respective field.
