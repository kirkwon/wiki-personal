# Limits to Growth

The "Limits to Growth" is an analysis of the potential consequences of population growth, resource depletion, environmental damage, and other factors on global sustainability. It emphasizes that unchecked expansion in various domains—be it culinary trends, photographic techniques, financial markets, or cognitive capacities—may lead to diminishing returns or even collapse if not managed within ecological boundaries.
