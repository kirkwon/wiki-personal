# Complications: A Surgeon's Notes on an Imperfect Science

"Complications: A Surgeon's Notes on an Imperfect Science" is a memoir by Atul Gawande that explores the inherent uncertainties in surgical practice, emphasizing the complexities and challenges surgeons face despite their expertise. It draws parallels to other fields like cooking (precision under pressure), photography (attention to detail), personal finance (risk management), and cognitive science (decision-making processes).
