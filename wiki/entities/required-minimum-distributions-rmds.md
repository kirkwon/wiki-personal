# Required Minimum Distributions RMDs

Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs) are the annual withdrawals that individuals must take from their retirement accounts, such as IRAs or employer-sponsored plans, starting at age 72 to avoid penalties and ensure tax benefits. These distributions are calculated based on life expectancy factors provided by IRS tables and can impact long-term financial planning, similar to how precise measurements affect recipes in cooking or exposure settings in photography. Understanding RMDs is crucial for managing retirement funds effectively, akin to using cognitive strategies for decision making.
