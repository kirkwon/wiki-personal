# Adversarial Examples

Adversarial examples are inputs to machine learning models that have been intentionally modified with small, often imperceptible changes to deceive the model into making incorrect predictions or classifications. In cooking, this could mean altering recipe ingredients; in photography, it might involve subtly manipulating an image's pixels; within personal finance, adversarial examples could trick fraud detection systems; and in cognitive science, they can challenge our understanding of human perception and decision-making.
