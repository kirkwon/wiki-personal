# tax-loss-harvesting-tracker

A tax-loss-harvesting tracker is a tool designed to monitor investment portfolios for opportunities to sell underperforming assets at a loss, which can be used to offset capital gains tax liabilities. It helps individuals in personal finance optimize their tax burden by aligning with principles of cognitive science that emphasize systematic decision-making and reducing mental load through automation. This concept is analogous to selecting the right ingredients or lighting for a dish, ensuring each element contributes optimally to the final outcome.
