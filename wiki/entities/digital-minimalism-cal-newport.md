# Digital Minimalism - Cal Newport

Digital Minimalism is an approach advocated by Cal Newport that emphasizes intentional use of digital technology to enhance life quality while minimizing distractions. It aligns with the principles of personal finance and cognitive science by promoting focused attention, reducing unnecessary consumption, and fostering meaningful engagement in activities like cooking and photography.
