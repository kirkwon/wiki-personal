# Daniel Kahneman

Daniel Kahneman is an influential psychologist known for his work on the psychology of judgment and decision-making, as well as behavioral economics, which challenges traditional economic assumptions about human rationality. His collaboration with Amos Tversky led to the development of prospect theory, a cornerstone in understanding how people make choices under uncertainty—a concept that can be applied to personal finance strategies and risk assessment in cooking experiments or photography investments.
