# Lasting effect

A lasting effect refers to the enduring impact or result of an action that persists over time in various domains. In cooking, it's a recipe's influence on taste preferences; in photography, it's how images shape visual perception; in personal finance, it's investment strategies affecting long-term wealth; and in cognitive science, lasting effects are the enduring changes in brain function or behavior due to learning.
