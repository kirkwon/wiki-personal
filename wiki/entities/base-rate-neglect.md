# Base rate neglect

Base rate neglect is the cognitive bias where individuals ignore or undervalue statistical base rates (general prevalence) in favor of specific information, such as anecdotal evidence from a small sample size. In cooking, this might lead to overestimating rare ingredients' importance; in photography, it could cause focusing on unusual techniques at the expense of fundamental skills; in personal finance, neglecting overall market trends for individual investment performance; and in cognitive science, disregarding general learning patterns when examining unique cases.
