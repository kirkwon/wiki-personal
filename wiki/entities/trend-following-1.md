# trend-following

Trend-following is an approach that involves tracking the direction of market trends to make investment decisions based on anticipated future movements rather than historical data alone. In cooking, it refers to adapting recipes and techniques in response to evolving food trends. For photography, it means capturing images that reflect current visual styles or themes. Within cognitive science, it could relate to studying patterns of thought or behavior as they emerge over time.
