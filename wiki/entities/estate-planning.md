# estate-planning

Estate planning is the process of organizing an individual's assets to ensure their distribution according to their wishes after death or incapacitation. It involves creating legal documents such as wills, trusts, and power of attorney, which are crucial for managing personal finances effectively. Estate planning also considers tax implications and the minimization thereof, ensuring financial stability for beneficiaries.
