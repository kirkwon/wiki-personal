# Multipliers

In cooking, multipliers refer to ingredient ratios that scale recipes up or down while maintaining flavor balance. In photography, they are values used in exposure calculations (aperture-time relationship). For personal finance, multipliers denote the factor by which investments grow over time due to compound interest. In cognitive science, they can describe how small changes in one area of study may amplify effects across related domains.
