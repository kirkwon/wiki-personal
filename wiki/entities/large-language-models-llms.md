# Large Language Models (LLMs)

Large Language Models (LLMs) are advanced AI systems that process and generate human-like text by analyzing vast amounts of written data. In the context of cooking, they can suggest recipes or explain culinary techniques; in photography, LLMs might offer tips on composition or editing styles; for personal finance, they could assist with budget planning or investment strategies; and within cognitive science, these models help understand language patterns and human thought processes.
