# Personification

Personification is attributing human characteristics or behaviors to non-human entities or abstract concepts. In cooking, it might describe ingredients as having personalities that interact during preparation; in photography, personifying light and shadows can evoke emotions through imagery; within personal finance, one could personify money's influence on life choices; while in cognitive science, personification aids in conceptualizing mental processes by likening them to human actions.
