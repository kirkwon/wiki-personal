# reality

Reality is the objective state of existence that encompasses all phenomena as they are independent of perception or interpretation. In cooking, it refers to the actual ingredients and processes; in photography, it's the captured moment that exists beyond subjective viewpoint; within personal finance, reality involves tangible assets and liabilities; and in cognitive science, it is the empirical truth about human thought and behavior.
