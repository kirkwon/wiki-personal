# Early Stopping

Early stopping is a strategy used to halt the training of machine learning models at the point when performance on a validation set starts to deteriorate, thus preventing overfitting and saving computational resources. In cooking or photography, it could metaphorically refer to ceasing an experimental process once optimal results are achieved to avoid unnecessary effort.


In personal finance, early stopping might imply cutting back on investments before they reach a point of diminishing returns to maximize overall gains. Within cognitive science, this concept can relate to discontinuing a learning task when it no longer contributes to knowledge acquisition or skill development.
