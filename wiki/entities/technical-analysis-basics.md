# Technical Analysis Basics

Technical analysis is the study of past market data, primarily price and volume, to forecast future stock trends. It involves analyzing charts for patterns that can indicate potential investment opportunities or risks, similar to how a chef might analyze ingredient combinations for optimal flavor balance.
