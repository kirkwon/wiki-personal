# influence-six-principles

The Influence Six Principles are fundamental guidelines that shape effective persuasion techniques across various domains including cooking (recipe adaptation), photography (composition rules), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (decision-making processes). These principles—Reciprocity, Scarcity, Authority, Consistency, Liking, and Consensus—help individuals understand how to influence others' choices or behaviors in a principled manner.
