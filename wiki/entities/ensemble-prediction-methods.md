# Ensemble prediction methods

Ensemble prediction methods combine multiple models or algorithms to improve forecast accuracy by leveraging their diverse strengths and mitigating individual weaknesses. In cooking, this approach could optimize recipe outcomes; in photography, it enhances image quality through varied techniques; for personal finance, it refines investment strategies; and in cognitive science, it aids in more robust data analysis by integrating different cognitive theories.
