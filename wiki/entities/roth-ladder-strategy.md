# Roth Ladder Strategy

The Roth IRA contribution strategy involves systematically investing in traditional IRAs (pre-tax contributions) while converting to Roth IRAs (post-tax contributions). By doing this over time, it allows for tax diversification and can provide tax-free income during retirement. This approach is akin to layering ingredients in cooking or sequencing shots in photography for optimal results.


Roth Ladder Strategy refers to the method of holding multiple Roth IRA accounts with different maturity dates, similar to how one might use various lenses and light settings when capturing a series of photographs. It's designed to manage tax liability and provide income streams at different points in retirement, reflecting an understanding of cognitive load management by spreading financial tasks over time for better mental processing.
