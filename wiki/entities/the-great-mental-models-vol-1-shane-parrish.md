# The Great Mental Models Vol 1 - Shane Parrish

Shane Parrish's *The Great Mental Models Vol 1* is a compendium of essential mental models for clear thinking. It organizes mental models into categories and provides frameworks for applying them to complex situations.

The book treats innovation as one of many mental models—a conceptual shortcut for pattern and relationship recognition. It supports the view that mental models are universal tools applicable across domains.
