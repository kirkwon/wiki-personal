# Blanton's

Single Barrel Kentucky Bourbon. Rated as a collector's bourbon and one of the highest-rated selections in the collection.

## Key Details

- **Type**: Single Barrel Kentucky Bourbon
- **Price**: $60-80
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose**: Complex, caramel, dried fruit
- **Palate**: Rich, layered, sophisticated
- **Finish**: Exceptionally long, evolving
- **Verdict**: Collector's bourbon, worth the hunt
