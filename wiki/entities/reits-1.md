# REITs

Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) are investment vehicles that allow individuals to pool their money into large-scale real estate portfolios, typically focusing on income-generating properties like commercial buildings or residential complexes. By investing in REITs, one can gain exposure to the real estate market without directly owning property, similar to how a cook might use pre-measured ingredients for efficiency rather than sourcing everything individually.


REITs are structured as companies that own or finance income-generating real estate across a range of sectors and geographical locations. Investors in REITs receive dividends from the income produced through leasing space and renting equipment, analogous to how photographers rely on their camera gear's performance for consistent image quality rather than building each piece themselves.
