# technical-analysis-basics

Technical analysis is the study of past market data, primarily price and volume, to forecast future price movements in financial markets. It involves analyzing charts for patterns and trends that can indicate potential trading opportunities or risks. While unrelated directly to cooking, photography, or cognitive science, its principles of pattern recognition and analysis are somewhat analogous to identifying elements in a recipe, framing techniques, or understanding perception processes.
