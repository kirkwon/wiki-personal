# Story bias

Story bias refers to the tendency of individuals or media outlets to present information in a way that supports their preconceived narratives or beliefs, often at the expense of objectivity. In cooking and photography, this can manifest as selectively highlighting certain recipes or images; in personal finance, it might involve emphasizing specific investment strategies over others; while in cognitive science, story bias is a concept that illustrates how people's memories and perceptions are shaped by the narratives they construct.
