# generalization-in-problem-solving

Generalization-in-problem-solving is the ability to apply learned principles or techniques from one domain (e.g., cooking) to another unrelated area (e.g., photography), such as using timing and composition skills in both fields. In personal finance, it involves transferring budgeting strategies across different financial goals. Within cognitive science, this concept refers to the brain's capacity to use similar mental processes or heuristics when tackling diverse problems.
