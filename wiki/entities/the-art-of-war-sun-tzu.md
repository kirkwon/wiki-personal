# The Art of War - Sun Tzu

*The Art of War* is an ancient Chinese military treatise attributed to Sun Tzu, a high-ranking military general and strategist. It is one of the most influential works on strategy, leadership, and conflict resolution, and its principles have been applied far beyond military contexts to business, politics, sports, and personal development.

## Key Themes

- Strategic positioning and competitive advantage
- Knowledge of oneself and one's opponent
- The importance of adaptability and flexibility
- Achieving victory through preparation and positioning rather than direct confrontation
- The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting
