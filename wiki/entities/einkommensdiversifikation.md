# Einkommensdiversifikation

Einkommensdiversifikation refers to the strategy of diversifying one's income streams by engaging in multiple sources of revenue. In personal finance, this can mean combining passive income from investments with active earnings from hobbies like cooking and photography. By applying principles from cognitive science, such as understanding decision-making biases, individuals can better manage their diverse portfolio to optimize financial stability.
