# Twin Peaks

In the context of cooking, 'Twin Peaks' refers to two dishes with similar ingredients or flavor profiles that can be prepared simultaneously for efficiency in meal prep. In photography, it denotes capturing images from dual perspectives to create a balanced composition. Regarding personal finance and cognitive science, the term metaphorically represents parallel decision-making processes or thought patterns influencing behavior.
