# Wild Turkey 101

Kentucky Straight Bourbon at 101 proof. Rated as a high proof, cocktail‑friendly bargain, great for cocktails.

## Key Details

- **Type**: Kentucky Straight Bourbon
- **Price**: $25
- **Proof**: 101
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose**: Bold, spicy, high proof evident
- **Palate**: Intense vanilla, cinnamon, caramel
- **Finish**: Long, warming, assertive
- **Verdict**: High proof bargain, great for cocktails

High sweetness (★★★★) and spice (★★★★) with top‑tier value (★★★★★). Comparable to Buffalo Trace as best value.
