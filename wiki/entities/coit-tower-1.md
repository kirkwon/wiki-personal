# Coit Tower

Coit Tower is a historic landmark located at Fort Mason in San Francisco, offering panoramic views that are highly sought after by photographers for capturing the city's skyline. It also serves as an educational site with exhibits on civic history and architecture, which can be of interest to those studying urban development or cognitive science related to spatial awareness. Additionally, its free admission makes it a cost-effective destination for personal finance enthusiasts looking to enjoy cultural experiences without significant expense.
