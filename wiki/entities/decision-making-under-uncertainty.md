# Decision-making-under-uncertainty

Decision-making under uncertainty involves choosing actions when outcomes are unpredictable, often relying on probability assessments or heuristics. In cooking, it's about improvising with ingredients; in photography, selecting the right moment for a shot amidst changing conditions; in personal finance, managing investments without knowing future market trends; and in cognitive science, understanding how humans make choices when information is incomplete or ambiguous.
