# Robust Optimization

Robust Optimization is an approach to decision-making that seeks solutions resilient to uncertainty in model parameters or external conditions. In cooking, it ensures a recipe's success despite ingredient variations; in photography, it helps achieve desired exposure regardless of light changes; in personal finance, it guards against market volatility affecting investment returns; and in cognitive science, it models how individuals make choices under ambiguous or incomplete information.
