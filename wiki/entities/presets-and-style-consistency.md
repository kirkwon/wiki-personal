# Presets and Style Consistency

Presets refer to predefined settings or configurations that streamline processes across various domains such as cooking recipes, photographic filters, financial budget templates, and cognitive behavioral therapy protocols. Style consistency ensures uniformity in these presets' application, enhancing efficiency, reliability, and aesthetic coherence within each respective field.
