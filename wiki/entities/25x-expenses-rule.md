# 25x Expenses Rule

The "25x Expenses Rule" is a guideline suggesting that individuals should limit non-essential spending to not more than 25% of their monthly income. This rule helps in budgeting for hobbies like cooking and photography, ensuring personal finance stability while allowing room for discretionary expenditures without compromising financial goals.


In the context of cognitive science, this rule can be linked to decision-making processes where individuals must balance their desires (e.g., investing in a new camera) with rational budget constraints to avoid impulsive spending and maintain cognitive control over finances.
