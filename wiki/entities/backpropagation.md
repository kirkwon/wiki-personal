# Backpropagation

Backpropagation is an algorithm used to train neural networks by adjusting weights through error correction across layers, similar to refining recipes or editing photos for optimal results. In personal finance, it's akin to iterative learning from investment outcomes, and in cognitive science, it mirrors the brain's method of strengthening connections based on feedback.
