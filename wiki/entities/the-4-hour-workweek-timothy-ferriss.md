# The 4-Hour Workweek - Timothy Ferriss

"The 4-Hour Workweek by Timothy Ferriss is a book that challenges traditional work norms, proposing strategies for minimizing daily labor to free up time for personal passions like cooking and photography. It delves into unconventional methods of achieving financial independence and leverages cognitive science principles to optimize productivity."
