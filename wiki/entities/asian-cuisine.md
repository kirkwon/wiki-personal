# asian-cuisine

Asian cuisine encompasses the diverse culinary traditions of Asia, characterized by its rich flavors, varied ingredients, and regional specialties such as sushi from Japan, curry from India, or dim sum from China. It often involves techniques like stir-frying, steaming, and fermentation, which contribute to the unique taste profiles found in dishes across this vast continent. Understanding Asian cuisine can enhance cooking skills, influence photography composition through food presentation, inform personal finance decisions related to ingredient sourcing, and provide insights into cultural cognitive patterns regarding meal preparation and consumption.
