# Sutro Baths

The Sutro Baths were historic outdoor swimming pools located in San Francisco, California, built by Adolph Sutro in the late 19th century. Designed as a luxurious public attraction, they featured large natural rock formations and stunning views of the bay. Today, remnants of the Baths serve as an intriguing subject for photography enthusiasts and are studied by cognitive scientists interested in historical landmarks' impact on community identity and memory.
