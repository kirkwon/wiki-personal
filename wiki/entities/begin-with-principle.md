# Begin With Principle

The "Begin With Principle" suggests starting with the foundational elements of an activity to build competence and understanding effectively. In cooking, this means mastering basic techniques; in photography, it involves learning fundamental composition rules; for personal finance, it entails grasping core concepts like budgeting and saving; and in cognitive science, it refers to comprehending the basics of human thought processes before advancing further.
