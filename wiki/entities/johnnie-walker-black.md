# Johnnie Walker Black

Blended Scotch rated as reliable for newcomers to Scotch.

## Key Details

- **Type**: Blended Scotch
- **Price**: $26
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose**: Smoky, accessible, fruit
- **Palate**: Balanced, slightly peaty, approachable
- **Finish**: Medium, consistent
- **Verdict**: Reliable blended, great for newcomers to Scotch

## Comparison

Moderate sweetness (★★★) with low spice (★) and complexity (★★). Despite similar price to Wild Turkey 101, rates lower due to personal palate preference for bolder flavors.
