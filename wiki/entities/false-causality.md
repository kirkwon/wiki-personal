# False causality

False causality, or non causa pro causa, is the logical fallacy where an incorrect cause-and-effect relationship is assumed between two events. In cooking, it might be mistakenly attributing a dish's flavor to an unrelated ingredient; in photography, blaming poor image quality on camera brand alone without considering lighting or composition; in personal finance, falsely correlating investment success with one specific stock choice. In cognitive science, it refers to misattributing the cause of a behavior based on superficial observations rather than empirical evidence.
