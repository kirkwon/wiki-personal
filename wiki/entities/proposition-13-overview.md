# Proposition 13 Overview

Proposition 13 refers to California's landmark property tax reform law passed in 1978, which significantly reduced property tax rates and capped annual increases for most homeowners. This proposition has had a profound impact on personal finance by altering the cost of owning real estate and influencing investment decisions within the state's economy. Its long-term effects are also studied in cognitive science, as it affects individual financial planning behavior.
