# Out of Control - Kevin Kelly

*Out of Control* by Kevin Kelly explores emergence, decentralization, and the nature of complex adaptive systems. The book's themes of unintended consequences, emergent order, and the limits of control inform the Implications mental model by illustrating how the most significant patterns in complex systems often arise from interactions that no single agent designed or anticipated.
