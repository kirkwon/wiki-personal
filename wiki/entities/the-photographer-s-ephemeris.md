# The Photographer's Ephemeris

A Photographer's Ephemeris is an app or tool that provides real-time sunrise, sunset, moon phases, and other astronomical data to assist photographers in planning their shots by considering the optimal lighting conditions for photography. It integrates with personal finance apps to help budget for potential equipment purchases based on celestial events of interest, while cognitive science principles can be applied to understand how users interact with this information and make decisions accordingly.
