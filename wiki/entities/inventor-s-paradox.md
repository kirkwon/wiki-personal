# inventor-s-paradox

The inventor's paradox refers to the phenomenon where individuals who excel at creating new ideas or techniques in fields like cooking (culinary innovation) or photography (creative composition), often struggle with applying those same skills to personal finance management, due to a lack of practical experience and discipline. In cognitive science, this paradox highlights the discrepancy between high-level creativity and the ability to execute consistent, effective problem-solving strategies in everyday life.
