# World mental model

A world mental model is an individual's internal representation of how various aspects of the world interconnect, including cooking techniques, photographic composition principles, personal financial strategies, and cognitive processes involved in learning and problem-solving. It serves as a framework for understanding complex information across diverse domains by drawing parallels between seemingly unrelated concepts.


In the context of cooking, it might involve knowing how ingredients interact to create flavors or textures; photography could relate different lighting techniques and angles to achieve desired effects in images; personal finance would encompass understanding investment principles and budget management as interconnected elements influencing financial well-bee. Cognitive science contributes by explaining the mental processes that underpin these understandings, such as memory retention of recipes or cognitive biases affecting financial decisions.
