# Imprecise Probabilities

Imprecise probabilities refer to the representation of uncertainty where exact likelihoods are not available; instead, intervals or sets describe the range within which an event's probability lies. This concept is useful in areas like cooking when ingredient proportions aren't precisely known, photography for uncertain light conditions, personal finance with fluctuating market risks, and cognitive science to model human decision-making under uncertainty.
