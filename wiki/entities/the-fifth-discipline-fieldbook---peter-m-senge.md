# The Fifth Discipline Fieldbook - Peter M Senge

*The Fifth Discipline Fieldbook* is a companion volume to *The Fifth Discipline*, offering practical tools, case studies, and exercises for applying systems thinking in organizations.

## Key Ideas

- Practical application of systems thinking principles
- Case studies of organizational learning in action
- Tools for building shared mental models and feedback loops

## Connection to Systems Mental Model

This fieldbook is one of the seven source perspectives cited in the [[systems]] mental model.
