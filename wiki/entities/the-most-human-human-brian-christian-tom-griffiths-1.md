# The Most Human Human - Brian Christian  Tom Griffiths

"The Most Human Human - Brian Christian & Tom Griffiths is an exploration of what it means to be human through the lens of artificial intelligence (AI) research. It delves into cognitive science by examining how AI algorithms mimic human thought processes, and relates this to everyday activities like cooking and photography, while also touching on personal finance decisions influenced by our inherent biases."
