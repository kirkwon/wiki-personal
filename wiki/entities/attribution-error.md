# Attribution error

Attribution error refers to the cognitive bias where individuals incorrectly infer the causes of others' behaviors, often over-attributing actions to dispositional factors rather than situational ones. In cooking, this might lead one to assume a chef is incompetent without considering kitchen constraints; in photography, it could result in blaming an artist for poor lighting instead of technical limitations; in personal finance, attributing financial missteps solely to laziness rather than market conditions; and within cognitive science, this error highlights the challenges in understanding human behavior.
