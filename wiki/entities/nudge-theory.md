# Nudge theory

Nudge theory is a concept within behavioral economics suggesting that positive reinforcement and indirect suggestions can influence people's decisions and behaviors in predictable ways without restricting their freedom of choice. In personal finance, it could mean subtly guiding choices towards better savings habits; in cooking or photography, nudges might encourage healthier eating or creative experimentation.


In cognitive science, nudge theory explores how environmental cues can shape thought processes and decision-making patterns.
