# The Power of Habit - Charles Duhigg

## Overview

*The Power of Habit* is a book by Charles Duhigg that synthesizes habit science across individuals, organizations, and societies. The central framework is the habit loop (cue, routine, reward), and the key insight is that habits can be deliberately rewired by targeting the routine while preserving the cue and reward.

## Key Frameworks

- **Habit Loop**: cue → routine → reward
- **Craving Brain**: neuroscience of habit encoding
- **Golden Rule of Habit Change**: keep cue and reward, substitute routine
- **Keystone Habits**: habits that catalyze change in other areas
- **Habit Stacking**: linking new habits to existing ones
- **Implementation Intentions**: pre-commitment strategies for behavior change

## Structure

The book is organized into three parts:
1. Habits of Individuals — personal habit loops, craving science, keystone habits, organizational examples (Starbucks, Target)
2. Habits of Successful Organizations — listening, empathy, trust as organizational habits
3. Habits of Societies — social networks, moral courage, faith, family habits

## Evidence Base

The book relies heavily on anecdotal case studies (Starbucks training, Target data analytics, social movements). While well-established in popular science, the evidence strength is moderate — case-study based rather than primary research.
