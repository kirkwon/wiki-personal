# Retirement Planning Tools

Retirement planning tools are software or applications designed to assist individuals in preparing financially for their post-working years. These tools typically offer features such as investment tracking, savings goal setting, and retirement income projection, enabling users to make informed decisions about their future financial security. They serve a similar purpose to recipes in cooking or camera settings in photography by providing structured guidance for achieving desired outcomes.
