# ガリ

'ガリ' refers to the Japanese term for fat or grease, often encountered in cooking contexts as it can affect flavor and texture of dishes. In photography, 'ガリ' metaphorically represents unwanted visual noise that detracts from image clarity. From a personal finance perspective, 'ガリ' symbolizes unnecessary expenditures or excessive spending that impedes financial goals. In cognitive science, it can be used to describe the mental clutter or distractions that hinder focus and decision-making processes.
