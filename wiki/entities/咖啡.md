# 咖啡

咖啡 (kafee) refers to the beverage made by brewing roasted coffee beans in hot water, typically served either black or with added milk and sweeteners. In cognitive science, it's often studied for its effects on alertness and mood enhancement. From a personal finance perspective, regular consumption can impact budgeting due to the cost of purchase and potential health-related expenses if overconsumed.
