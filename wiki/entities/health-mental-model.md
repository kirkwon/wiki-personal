# Health mental model

A health mental model is an individual's cognitive framework that guides their understanding of wellness, including emotional balance, stress management, and self-care practices. It influences how one approaches daily tasks like cooking nutritious meals or managing personal finances with a focus on long-term benefits rather than short-term gains. In cognitive science, it reflects the underlying beliefs about mental health that shape decision-making processes and coping strategies in various life domains.
