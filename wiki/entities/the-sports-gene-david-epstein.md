# The Sports Gene - David Epstein

"The Sports Gene" by David Epstein is an exploration of the genetic factors that contribute to athletic performance, suggesting that certain individuals have innate predispositions towards excelling in sports due to their DNA. It delves into how these genetic traits interact with training and environment to shape elite athletes' abilities. This concept parallels personal finance by examining the role of inherent skills versus learned strategies in achieving success, much like understanding one's financial aptitude is crucial for wealth accumulation.
