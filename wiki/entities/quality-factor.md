# Quality Factor

The Quality Factor represents the intrinsic value or excellence of an item or process within its respective domain. In cooking, it denotes the superior taste and presentation of a dish; in photography, it signifies the sharpness and clarity captured by a camera lens; in personal finance, it reflects the long-term benefits versus costs of an investment; and in cognitive science, it measures the efficiency of information processing.
