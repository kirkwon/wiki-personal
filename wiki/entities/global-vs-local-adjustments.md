# Global vs Local Adjustments

In cooking, global adjustments refer to broad changes affecting the entire dish's flavor profile or presentation, while local adjustments are specific tweaks made to individual ingredients or components for optimal taste and appearance. In photography, global adjustments alter overall image properties like exposure and contrast, whereas local adjustments focus on selective areas for enhanced detail or color correction. Personal finance's global adjustments involve large-scale budget changes affecting all spending categories, while local adjustments target specific expenses to optimize savings. In cognitive science, global adjustments pertain to widespread alterations in thought processes or behavior patterns, and local adjustments are precise modifications within particular cognitive tasks or learning activities.
