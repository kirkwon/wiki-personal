# Not-invented-here syndrome

Not Invented Here (NIH) Syndrome is the tendency to reject or undervalue ideas, products, or solutions originating outside one's own group or area of expertise. This can lead to a lack of innovation and missed opportunities for improvement in domains such as cooking recipes, photography techniques, personal finance strategies, and cognitive science research methodologies.
