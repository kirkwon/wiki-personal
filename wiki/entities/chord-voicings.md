# Chord Voicings

A chord voicing is the arrangement of notes within a chord that determines its harmonic structure and sound quality. In cooking, it's like choosing ingredients to balance flavors; in photography, it's selecting elements for composition; in personal finance, it's diversifying investments; and in cognitive science, it relates to how information is structured in the brain.
