# Fusion

In cooking, fusion refers to the blending of culinary traditions or ingredients from different cultures to create innovative dishes. In photography, it denotes a style that combines various genres and techniques for unique visual effects. Personal finance fusion involves integrating diverse financial strategies to optimize wealth management. Within cognitive science, fusion pertains to the integration of multiple sensory inputs or concepts to enhance understanding and problem-solving abilities.
