# brewing

Brewing is the controlled process of fermenting ingredients to produce beverages like beer or tea. It involves precise temperature management and timing to ensure optimal flavor development. In cognitive science, brewing can metaphorically describe the careful crafting of ideas through iterative experimentation and refinement.
