# Momentum Factor

The Momentum Factor refers to the principle that an initial positive trend or momentum can continue in a given domain due to compounding effects. In cooking, it's about building on successful recipes; in photography, capturing images with good composition leading to better results; in personal finance, consistent investment growth over time; and in cognitive science, the reinforcement of neural pathways strengthening memory retention.
