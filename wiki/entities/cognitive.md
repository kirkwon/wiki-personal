# Cognitive

In the context of cooking, cognition refers to the mental processes involved in understanding recipes, ingredient combinations, and techniques for preparation. In photography, it involves perception, memory, and decision-making when capturing images or editing photos. Regarding personal finance, cognitive aspects encompass budget planning, risk assessment, and investment strategies based on information processing and judgment skills. Cognitive science broadly studies these mental processes across various activities to understand how knowledge is acquired, processed, and applied in everyday tasks like cooking or managing finances.
