# elimination-of-distractions

Elimination-of-distractions refers to the deliberate practice of removing or minimizing external interruptions that can impede focus during activities such as cooking, photography, personal finance management, and cognitive tasks. This process enhances concentration, improves performance quality, and fosters a more efficient workflow by allowing individuals to fully engage with their chosen activity without unnecessary disruptions.
