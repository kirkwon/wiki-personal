# Scarcity

Scarcity refers to the fundamental economic problem of having limited resources while human wants are unlimited. In cooking, it manifests as ingredient availability; in photography, through rare equipment or light conditions; in personal finance, via restricted income and expenses; and cognitively, as our mental bandwidth is finite when processing information.
