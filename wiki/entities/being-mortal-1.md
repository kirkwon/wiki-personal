# Being Mortal

"Being Mortal" refers to the inherent human condition of finite existence, influencing our perspectives on life's value, urgency in pursuits like cooking (time-sensitive recipes), photography (capturing moments before they fade), personal finance (planning for long-term security), and cognitive science (understanding the biological limits of human consciousness).
