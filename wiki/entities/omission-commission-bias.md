# Omission commission bias

Omission commission bias is the tendency to overlook or undervalue negative outcomes (commissions) while focusing more on positive results in various activities such as cooking recipes' successes, photographs' highlights, financial gains, and cognitive experiments' findings. It can lead to a skewed perception of reality by emphasizing what is present or successful over what might be absent or negative.
