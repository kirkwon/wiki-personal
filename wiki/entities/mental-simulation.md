# Mental Simulation

Mental simulation is the cognitive process of imagining or visualizing scenarios, actions, or outcomes in one's mind, without immediate external stimuli. In cooking, it involves envisioning recipes and techniques; in photography, anticipating compositions; for personal finance, projecting investment growth; and within cognitive science, exploring problem-solving strategies.
