# Getting Things Done - David Allen

"Getting Things Done (GTD) is a productivity methodology developed by David Allen that focuses on capturing all tasks and ideas externally to free the mind for creative work. By organizing these items into actionable categories, GTD helps individuals manage their responsibilities efficiently, which can be particularly beneficial in managing cooking schedules, photography projects, personal finance planning, and cognitive task management."
