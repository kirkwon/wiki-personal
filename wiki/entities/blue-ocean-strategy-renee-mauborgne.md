# blue-ocean-strategy-renee-mauborgne

Blue Ocean Strategy, as conceptualized by Rene Mauborgne and Kimberly Wilder, is an approach to business that focuses on creating new market spaces (blue oceans) rather than competing in overcrowded industries (red oceans). It involves innovating products or services to make the competition irrelevant. In personal contexts like cooking, it could mean inventing unique recipes; in photography, developing novel techniques; for finance, devising unconventional investment strategies; and within cognitive science, exploring new ways of understanding human thought processes.
