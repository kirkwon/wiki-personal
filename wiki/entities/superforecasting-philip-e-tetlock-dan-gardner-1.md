# superforecasting - Philip E Tetlock Dan Gardner

Superforecasting is the practice of making highly accurate predictions about future events by combining diverse perspectives, rigorous analysis, and continuous learning—a skill that can be applied to various domains including cooking (anticipating food trends), photography (predicting popular themes), personal finance (investment outcomes), and cognitive science (understanding behavioral patterns).


Philip E Tetlock's research on superforecasting emphasizes the importance of open-mindedness, intellectual humility, and systematic evaluation in improving forecast accuracy. Dan Gardner expands on this by exploring how individuals can harness these principles to enhance their predictive capabilities across different life aspects.
