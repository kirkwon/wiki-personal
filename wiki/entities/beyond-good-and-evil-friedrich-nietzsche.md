# Beyond Good and Evil - Friedrich Nietzsche

*Beyond Good and Evil* (*Jenseits von Gut und Böse*) is a philosophical book by Friedrich Nietzsche, published in 1886. It extends his critique of traditional morality and challenges fundamental assumptions about knowledge, morality, and the nature of philosophical inquiry.

Nietzsche's genealogical method, developed across his later works including *On the Genealogy of Morality* and *Beyond Good and Evil*, involves tracing the historical origins of moral concepts to reveal the power relations and social conditions that produced them. This approach critiques unexamined moral assumptions and questions the foundations of values we treat as self-evident.

The source document referencing this work applies the phrase "genealogical analysis of moral concepts" to a productivity framework, which represents a significant departure from Nietzsche's philosophical method.
