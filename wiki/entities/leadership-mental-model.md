# leadership-mental-model

A leadership-mental model is an individual's cognitive framework that guides their decision-making process in various domains such as cooking (e.g., kitchen management), photography (e.g., composition and lighting choices), personal finance (e.g., investment strategies), and cognitive science (e.g., understanding human thought processes). It encompasses beliefs, values, and experiences that shape how one leads in these areas, influencing their approach to challenges and opportunities for innovation.
