# Latent Space

Latent space refers to an abstract multi-dimensional representation where hidden or unobserved variables are mapped out. In cooking, it could represent the underlying flavor profiles of ingredients; in photography, latent spaces might capture image features not immediately visible to the naked eye; for personal finance, it could denote undisclosed financial patterns affecting one's economic status; and within cognitive science, latent space can be used to model unseen mental constructs influencing behavior.
