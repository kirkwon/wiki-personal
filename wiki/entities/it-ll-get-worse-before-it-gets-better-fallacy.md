# It'll-get-worse-before-it-gets-better fallacy

The 'It'll-get-worse-before-it-gets-better fallacy' is the flawed reasoning that a situation or problem will improve only after worsening first. In cooking, this might mean insisting on perfecting a recipe by repeatedly failing before success; in photography, it could be overcoming initial technical challenges to achieve better images; in personal finance, enduring short-term financial setbacks for long-term gains; and in cognitive science, the belief that complex concepts are only understood after struggling with them.
