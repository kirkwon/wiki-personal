# Leaky ReLU

A Leaky ReLU is an activation function used in neural networks that allows small negative values when the input is less than zero, unlike the traditional Rectified Linear Unit (ReLU) which outputs zero for any negative input. This can be likened to a cooking technique where a pinch of salt (small leakage) enhances flavor even if it's not strictly necessary, or in photography, adjusting exposure slightly below the threshold to capture more detail in shadows without overexposing highlights.


In personal finance, think of Leaky ReLU as a cautious investment strategy that allows for minimal losses (negative leakage) even when market conditions are unfavorable, ensuring some growth rather than complete stagnation or loss. In cognitive science, it's like the brain's ability to retain weak memories that don't fully activate but still contribute to learning and pattern recognition.
