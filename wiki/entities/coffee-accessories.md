# Coffee Accessories

Coffee accessories are tools or equipment designed to enhance the preparation, serving, and enjoyment of coffee. They range from simple items like spoons and stirrers to more specialized gear such as grinders, filters, and brewing machines. These accessories can influence both the taste and presentation of coffee, catering to personal preferences and culinary practices.
