# Seneca

Lucius Annaeus Seneca (c. 4 BCE – 65 CE), known as Seneca the Younger, was a Roman Stoic philosopher, statesman, and playwright. He served as advisor to Emperor Nero and is one of the most influential figures in Stoic philosophy. His *Letters from a Stoic* (also known as *Epistulae Morales ad Lucilium* or *Moral Epistles*) is one of the most influential works of Stoic philosophy, offering practical wisdom for daily living through 124 letters addressed to his protégé Lucilius. He also wrote *On the Shortness of Life*, *On Anger*, and *On the Happy Life*.

## Key Works
- *On the Shortness of Life* — Argues life is wasted through distraction and postponement
- *Letters from a Stoic* (also known as *Epistulae Morales ad Lucilium*, *Moral Epistles*, or *Letters to Lucilius*) — Practical Stoic advice on daily living, addressing diverse situations including grief, anger, friendship, death, aging, and wealth, covering time management, moderation, simple living, and practical philosophy.
- *On Anger* — Addressed emotional regulation and anger management
- *On the Happy Life* — Explores the nature of happiness and virtue

## Core Ideas
Seneca's philosophy emphasizes present-moment awareness, the uselessness of excess wealth and ambition, philosophy as a practical tool for freeing oneself from fear and desire, and the urgency of living rather than merely existing. Central to his teachings is the idea that emotional regulation comes through rational analysis rather than suppression.

- **Memento mori**: Encouraged reflection on mortality as a means to prioritize what matters.
- **Practical philosophy**: Argued that philosophy must be lived, not merely studied.
- **Stoic emphasis on virtue and reason**: Central to his teachings is the idea that emotional regulation comes through rational analysis rather than suppression.
- **Philosophical journaling**: Provided frameworks for self-examination and letter-writing as tools for self-development.
- **Time management and emotional regulation**: Advocated for eliminating distractions, managing time wisely, and regulating emotions through rational analysis.
