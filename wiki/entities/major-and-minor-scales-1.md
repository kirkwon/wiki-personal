# major-and-minor-scales

Major scales are musical sequences of seven notes with specific intervals between them, creating a harmonious sound often associated with positive emotions; minor scales follow the same structure but result in a melancholic tone. In photography, major-and-minor tones can influence mood through color grading and lighting choices. Understanding these concepts is crucial for cognitive science research on emotion perception and personal finance strategies that consider emotional responses to market trends.
