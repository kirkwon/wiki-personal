# five-dysfunctions-of-a-team

The Five Dysfunctions of a Team are: lack of trust (crucial for collaboration in cooking or photography), fear of conflict (hindering financial decision-making), absence of commitment (affects project completion and photo editing quality), avoidance of accountability (impairs budget management and recipe adherence), and inattention to results (detrimental to achieving desired outcomes in cognitive science research).
