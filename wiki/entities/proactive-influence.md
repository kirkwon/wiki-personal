# Proactive Influence

Proactive influence refers to the intentional actions taken by an individual to shape outcomes or behaviors in areas such as cooking (by experimenting with recipes), photography (through composition techniques), personal finance (via budget planning and investment decisions), and cognitive science (by applying learning strategies). It is a deliberate effort to exert control over one's environment, rather than reacting passively.
