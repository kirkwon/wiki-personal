# Score-Based Models

Score-Based Models are computational frameworks that assign numerical values to elements based on predefined criteria or performance metrics. In cooking, they could predict recipe success; in photography, they might evaluate image composition; for personal finance, these models assess investment options; and in cognitive science, they quantify decision-making processes.
