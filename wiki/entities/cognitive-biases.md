# cognitive-biases

Cognitive biases are systematic patterns of deviation from norm or rationality in judgment, often resulting in illogical inferences. In cooking, they can lead to overestimating the ease of recipes; in photography, affecting composition choices; in personal finance, influencing investment decisions and spending habits; while in cognitive science, they are studied as fundamental aspects of human thought processes.
