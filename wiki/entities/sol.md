# Sol

A Sol is an individual's unique cognitive processing speed that influences their ability to multitask in the kitchen or manage complex recipes efficiently. In photography, a Sol reflects one's aptitude for quickly adjusting camera settings and compositions. Financially, it denotes how swiftly someone can make informed decisions with money matters.
