# The Systems Bible

The Systems Bible is an integrated framework that applies systems thinking to understand complex interrelationships across various domains such as cooking (recipe optimization), photography (composition and light balance), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (decision-making processes). It offers a holistic approach for analyzing, designing, and managing systems with an emphasis on interconnectivity and feedback loops.
