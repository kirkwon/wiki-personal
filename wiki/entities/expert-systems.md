# Expert Systems

An Expert System is an AI software that emulates the decision-making abilities of a human expert in specific domains such as cooking recipes, photographic techniques, financial advice, or cognitive processes. It uses knowledge bases and inference rules to provide solutions or recommendations based on input data.
