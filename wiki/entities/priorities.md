# priorities

Priorities are the essential tasks or goals that take precedence over others based on their importance to my well-being and long-term objectives. In cooking, I prioritize meal planning for health; in photography, capturing unique moments is key; personal finance focuses on budgeting and saving; cognitive science drives the pursuit of understanding human thought processes.
