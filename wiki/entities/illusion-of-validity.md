# Illusion of validity

The Illusion of Validity is the cognitive bias where people overestimate the accuracy of their judgments or predictions based on limited evidence. In cooking, it might lead to assuming a dish tastes good without sufficient feedback; in photography, believing an image captures reality perfectly; in personal finance, trusting investment choices with incomplete data; and in cognitive science, overconfidence in theories without rigorous testing.
