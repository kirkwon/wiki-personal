# beta

Beta refers to the sensitivity of an investment or system to market movements (in finance) or changes in light intensity (in photography). In cooking, it can describe a recipe's adjustability for different ingredient quantities. In cognitive science, beta brain waves are low-frequency neural oscillations associated with active, alert states of consciousness.
