# Scarcity error

A scarcity error occurs when an individual overvalues items or opportunities perceived as limited, leading to irrational decision-making in cooking (e.g., overspending on rare ingredients), photography (e.g., chasing exclusive equipment), personal finance (e.g., hoarding cash instead of investing), and cognitive science (e.g., overestimating the importance of scarce information).
