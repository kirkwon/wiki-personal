# Nudge - Richard H. Thaler & Cass R. Sunstein

Richard H. Thaler and Cass R. Sunstein are the authors of *Nudge: Improving Decisions About Health, Wealth, and Happiness*, a foundational work in behavioral economics and public policy design. Their collaboration introduced the concept of "nudge" as a tool for influencing behavior through environmental design rather than mandates or restrictions.

## Key Contributions

- **Nudge theory** — The framework for understanding how subtle changes in choice architecture can significantly affect decision outcomes
- **Libertarian paternalism** — The philosophical position that it is acceptable to steer people's choices without limiting options
- **Choice architecture** — The idea that the way options are presented and defaults are set profoundly shapes decisions

## Relevance to Wiki

Their work connects to [[thinking-fast-and-slow---daniel-kahneman]] through shared roots in cognitive science, and to [[framing-effects]] and [[decision-making-frameworks]] as a prescriptive approach to leveraging cognitive biases rather than merely describing them.
