# Strategic sequence testing

Strategic sequence testing is the process of evaluating the effectiveness of different steps or actions within a larger plan to achieve a specific goal. In cooking, it involves experimenting with ingredient order for optimal flavor; in photography, it's adjusting camera settings sequentially for best exposure; in personal finance, testing various investment sequences can maximize returns; and in cognitive science, studying the sequence of mental processes to understand decision-making.
