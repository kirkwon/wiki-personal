# Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte

"Building a Second Brain by Tiago Forte refers to the concept of creating an externalized knowledge management system that complements one's cognitive abilities, enhancing productivity in diverse areas such as cooking (recipe organization), photography (image metadata tracking), personal finance (budget and expense monitoring), and cognitive science (research data curation)."
