# Meta‑Learning

Meta-learning is the process of learning how to learn efficiently across various domains such as cooking techniques, photographic styles, financial strategies, or cognitive tasks by identifying patterns in one's own learning experiences and applying those insights to accelerate future skill acquisition. It involves self-reflection on past successes and failures to optimize personal growth trajectories.


In the context of cooking, meta-learning might involve recognizing which ingredients or techniques work best for different cuisines, allowing a chef to adapt recipes more effectively. For photography, it could mean understanding how light and composition impact images across genres, leading to quicker mastery in new styles. In personal finance, this approach helps individuals identify the most effective saving habits or investment strategies that suit their unique financial goals. Cognitive science benefits from meta-learning by enabling researchers to develop more sophisticated models of learning and memory based on empirical observations of cognitive processes in diverse tasks.
