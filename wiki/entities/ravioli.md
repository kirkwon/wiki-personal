# ravioli

Ravioli is an Italian pasta dish consisting of small pockets of cheese or filling enclosed by thin layers of pasta dough. In photography, capturing ravioli can showcase texture and color contrasts; in cooking, it's a versatile ingredient for various recipes; financially, purchasing fresh ravioli may be considered an indulgence but also a culinary investment; cognitively, the process of making or eating ravioli involves sensory integration and memory recall.
