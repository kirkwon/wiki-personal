# 绿茶-lǚ-chá

绿茶-lǚ-chá refers to green tea, which is unfermented tea leaves from Camellia sinensis plant. Known for its antioxidant properties, it's a staple in cooking and photography due to its vibrant color and health benefits. In personal finance, investing in high-quality green tea can be seen as both a hobby and an asset, while cognitive science studies suggest that regular consumption may improve focus and mental clarity.
