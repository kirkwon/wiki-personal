# Herbs & Spices

Herbs and spices are natural plant products used to enhance the flavor, color, and aroma of food. They play a crucial role in culinary arts by adding depth and complexity to dishes, while also having applications in photography for creating visual interest and cognitive science as they can influence mood and memory recall through sensory stimulation.
