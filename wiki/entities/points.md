# points

In cooking, points refer to the quantifiable aspects of recipes such as ingredient measurements or steps in preparation. In photography, points denote specific elements within an image that contribute to composition and focus. Within personal finance, 'points' often represent credit score increments affecting interest rates on loans. Lastly, in cognitive science, points can symbolize discrete units of information processed by the brain during learning or memory tasks.
