# Bayesian thinking

Bayesian thinking is an approach that uses probability to update beliefs based on new evidence or information. In cooking, it can help predict ingredient interactions; in photography, adjust exposure settings for desired outcomes; in personal finance, assess investment risks and returns; and in cognitive science, model how people revise their understanding of the world.
