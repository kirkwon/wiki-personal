# Yield Trap

A yield trap is the financial phenomenon where investors are lured into buying assets that show initial high returns but fail to sustain them over time, often leading to significant losses. In cooking, it's akin to following trendy recipes without understanding their long-term impact on taste and health. Similarly, in photography, chasing popular techniques can result in uninspired work if not grounded in fundamental skills. Cognitively, falling for yield traps reflects our susceptibility to confirmation bias, where we prioritize information that confirms pre-existing beliefs over more rational long-term considerations.
