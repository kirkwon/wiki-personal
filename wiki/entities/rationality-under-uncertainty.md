# Rationality under Uncertainty

'Rationality under uncertainty' refers to the principle of making decisions that maximize expected utility when outcomes are unpredictable. In cooking, this means choosing ingredients based on their likelihood to enhance flavor; in photography, it involves selecting camera settings for optimal image quality despite variable lighting conditions; in personal finance, it entails investment choices balancing potential returns against risks; and in cognitive science, it pertains to how individuals process ambiguous information to make informed judgments.
