# lasting-effect

A 'lasting-effect' refers to the enduring impact or outcome of an activity or decision that persists over time. In cooking, it might mean a recipe's influence on one's culinary skills; in photography, it could be the lasting impression of captured moments; for personal finance, it entails long-term financial habits formed from budgeting practices; and within cognitive science, it signifies enduring changes to neural pathways due to learning or experience.
