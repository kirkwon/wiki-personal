# Belief Propagation

Belief Propagation is an algorithm used to compute the marginal probabilities of nodes within a graphical model by passing messages between neighboring nodes iteratively until convergence. In cooking terms, it's like adjusting ingredient quantities based on feedback from taste tests; in photography, it's optimizing exposure settings through trial and error; for personal finance, it involves updating investment strategies with new market information; and in cognitive science, it models how beliefs are updated as we receive new evidence.
