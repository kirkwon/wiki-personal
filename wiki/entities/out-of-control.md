# out-of-control

In cooking, 'out-of-control' refers to a dish that has deviated from the intended recipe or technique, resulting in an undesirable taste or texture. In photography, it denotes images with unintended motion blur or overexposure, compromising visual quality. Within personal finance, out-of-control signifies spending habits exceeding budget limits, leading to financial instability. Lastly, in cognitive science, an 'out-of-control' state may describe erratic thought patterns that disrupt normal decision-making processes.
