# Temperance

Temperance is the practice of moderation or self-restraint concerning consumption and actions across various domains. In cooking, it refers to balanced ingredient use; in photography, it's about controlled exposure settings; within personal finance, temperance involves prudent spending habits; and in cognitive science, it pertains to managing impulses for better decision-making.
