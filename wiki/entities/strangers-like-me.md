# Strangers like me

'Strangers Like Me' is an exploration of the commonalities that unite us despite our diverse backgrounds in cooking, photography, personal finance, and cognitive science. It delves into how shared experiences can bridge gaps between individuals from different walks of life, fostering a sense of community and understanding through the lens of these varied interests.
