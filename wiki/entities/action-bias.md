# Action bias

Action bias is the tendency to favor action over inaction, often leading individuals to make decisions or take steps even when evidence suggests that maintaining status quo could be more beneficial. In cooking, this might manifest as adding ingredients without considering if they're necessary; in photography, it may involve taking additional shots instead of reviewing the best one already taken; in personal finance, investors might trade stocks frequently rather than holding onto a well-performing portfolio. Cognitive science studies this bias as part of our natural inclination to act and avoid regret, despite potential negative outcomes from unnecessary actions.
