# habits-mental-model

A habit-mental model is an individual's cognitive framework that shapes their approach to daily tasks such as cooking, photography, personal finance management, and understanding cognitive processes. It encompasses the mental schemas, routines, and patterns of thought that guide behavior in these areas, influencing efficiency, creativity, and decision-making.
