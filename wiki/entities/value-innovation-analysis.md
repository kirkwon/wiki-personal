# Value innovation analysis

Value innovation analysis is the process of identifying and creating new value propositions that differentiate products or services from competitors by simultaneously pursuing high levels of value creation while reducing costs. In cooking, it could mean inventing a unique fusion cuisine; in photography, developing novel techniques for capturing images; in personal finance, devising unconventional investment strategies; and in cognitive science, proposing groundbreaking research methods that challenge existing paradigms.
