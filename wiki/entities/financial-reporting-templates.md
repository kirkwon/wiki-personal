# Financial Reporting Templates

A comprehensive suite of investment tracking templates covering real estate, stocks, bonds, retirement, and tax optimization. The templates are designed for real estate investors but can be adapted for various investment types.

## Core Templates

- [[single-property-analysis-template]] — Single property cash flow, NOI, cap rate, cash-on-cash return
- [[multi-property-portfolio-template]] — Aggregated portfolio metrics across properties
- [[stock-investment-tracker]] — Ticker-level performance tracking
- [[bond-investment-template]] — Coupon, YTM, duration tracking
- [[retirement-savings-calculator]] — Retirement projections and RMD calculations
- [[tax-loss-harvesting-tracker]] — Tax optimization tools
- [[section-1031-exchange-analysis]] — Tax-deferred exchange analysis

## Implementation Guidelines

- Tools: Excel, Google Sheets
- Reporting frequency: Daily transaction entries, weekly portfolio updates, monthly comprehensive reporting, quarterly rebalancing, annual tax preparation
- Best practices: Consistent data entry, documentation of assumptions, regular updates, monthly accuracy reviews
- Security: Password protection, access control, encryption, secure backups

## Design Philosophy

The modular design allows customization based on individual investment strategies. Regular use enables better decision-making, tax optimization, and long-term wealth building through systematic financial reporting.
