# Multi‑Agent Systems

A Multi-Agent System (MAS) consists of multiple interacting intelligent agents within an environment to solve problems that are beyond the capabilities of a single agent or system. In cooking, MAS can optimize recipe preparation by coordinating various kitchen appliances and ingredients; in photography, it could manage different camera settings for optimal image capture; in personal finance, multiple agents might handle budget allocation, investment decisions, and risk assessment to improve financial outcomes. In cognitive science, MAS models how individual entities with distinct roles contribute to complex behaviors or decision-making processes.
