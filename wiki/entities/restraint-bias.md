# Restraint bias

Restraint bias is the tendency to underestimate one's ability to exercise self-control when faced with temptations or impulsive choices. In cooking, this may lead to overindulgence in unhealthy food; in photography, it could result in excessive editing; in personal finance, it might manifest as overspending, and within cognitive science, restraint bias is a key concept studied for understanding self-regulation.
