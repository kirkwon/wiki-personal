# Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday

"Ego Is the Enemy," authored by Ryan Holiday, is a book that explores how ego can sabotage personal growth and success across various domains including cooking (by hindering culinary innovation), photography (through overconfidence in one's skills), personal finance (by influencing poor investment decisions), and cognitive science (as it affects learning and decision-making processes). It advocates for self-awareness and humility as tools to overcome ego-driven obstacles.
