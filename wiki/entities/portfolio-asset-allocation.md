# portfolio-asset-allocation

Portfolio-Asset Allocation is the strategic distribution of investments across various asset classes (e.g., stocks, bonds, real estate) to balance risk and return, akin to selecting diverse ingredients for a well-rounded recipe or choosing complementary elements in photography composition. In personal finance, it's the method by which an individual tailors their investment portfolio according to their financial goals, much like adjusting camera settings based on desired photo outcomes. Cognitive science parallels this concept with how individuals allocate attention and memory resources for optimal information processing.
