# withdrawal-rate

The withdrawal rate refers to the percentage of investment portfolio value an individual takes out annually as income during retirement. It is crucial for maintaining financial stability without depleting resources too quickly. In cognitive science, it can also describe a gradual reduction in stimuli that leads to decreased responsiveness or activity levels.
