# Jazz Scales

A Jazz scale is a set of pitches used as the foundation for improvisation and composition within jazz music, characterized by specific intervals that create its distinctive sound. These scales often include chromatic notes to add tension and color, enabling musicians to explore complex harmonies and melodic lines. Understanding Jazz scales is crucial for anyone looking to delve into the intricacies of jazz performance or composition.
