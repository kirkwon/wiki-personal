# altered-chords

Altered chords are musical alterations where one or more notes of a chord are changed from their original pitch to create tension or color in music composition. In cooking, this concept can be likened to substituting an ingredient for another to achieve a unique flavor profile. Similarly, in photography and cognitive science, altered elements (like lighting or perception) are used to evoke different responses or highlight particular aspects of the subject matter.
