# Birth of the Cool

Birth of the Cool refers to the 1949-1950 nonet recordings led by Miles Davis, arranged by Gil Evans. These sessions defined the cool jazz movement with a cooler sound featuring French horn and tuba.

## Key Tracks
- "Boplicity"
- "Moon Dreams"

The recordings marked a pivotal transition from bebop to a more relaxed, lyrical approach to jazz.^[raw/articles/miles-davis.md]
