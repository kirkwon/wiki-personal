# Adobe Lightroom Classic

Industry-standard photo editing and organizing software. Central to post-processing workflows.

## Key Facts
- Platform: Desktop and Mobile
- Best for: Organizing and editing photos
- Cost: Subscription-based
- Widely regarded as the industry standard with a good learning curve

## Role in Post-Processing
Lightroom Classic serves as the primary tool for the systematic workflow: import, cull, global adjustments, white balance, HSL, tone curve, lens corrections, effects, crop/straighten, local adjustments, and export.
