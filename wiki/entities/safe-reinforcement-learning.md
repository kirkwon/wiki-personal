# Safe Reinforcement Learning

Safe Reinforcement Learning (SRL) is an approach to reinforcement learning that prioritizes safety during the training process by incorporating constraints or reward shaping to prevent undesirable behaviors. In contexts like cooking and photography, SRL can guide algorithms to avoid hazardous actions; in personal finance, it ensures risk-averse strategies are learned; while in cognitive science, it models how humans learn from consequences without harmful outcomes.
