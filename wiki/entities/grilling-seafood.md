# Grilling Seafood

Grilling seafood involves cooking various aquatic foods over an open flame or hot surface to achieve desired flavors and textures. This technique requires precise temperature control to prevent overcooking delicate proteins found in fish and shellfish. Properly executed, grilled seafood can offer a smoky taste while retaining moisture, making it visually appealing for photography documentation and cost-effective compared to other cooking methods.
