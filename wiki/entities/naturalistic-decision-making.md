# Naturalistic Decision Making

Naturalistic Decision Making (NDM) refers to the process of making decisions based on real-world observations and experiences rather than formal models or theories. In contexts like cooking, it involves using sensory feedback and intuition; in photography, it's about capturing moments instinctively; for personal finance, it means adapting strategies to changing economic conditions; while in cognitive science, NDM examines how people make judgments under uncertainty.
