# Overfitting

In cooking, overfitting refers to an excessively complex recipe that perfectly caters to one's taste but fails to appeal broadly due to its specificity. In photography, it describes a photo with such high detail that it captures noise instead of the intended subject. Overfitting in personal finance means tailoring investments too closely to past performance, risking poor future returns. Cognitively, overfitting is when learning models excessively adapt to training data, impairing their ability to generalize new information effectively.
