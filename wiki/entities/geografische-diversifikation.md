# Geografische-Diversifikation

Geografische-Diversifikation refers to the strategic diversification of interests or investments across various geographical regions. In cooking, it might involve exploring cuisines from different countries; in photography, capturing landscapes and cultures worldwide; for personal finance, spreading assets internationally to mitigate risks; and within cognitive science, studying how diverse environments influence human behavior.
