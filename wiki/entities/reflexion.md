# Reflexion

Reflection refers to the process of introspection where one critically examines their own actions, decisions, or experiences in various domains such as cooking (evaluating recipes), photography (assessing composition and technique), personal finance (reviewing spending habits), and cognitive science (studying thought processes). It is a deliberate practice aimed at gaining deeper insights and improving future performance.
