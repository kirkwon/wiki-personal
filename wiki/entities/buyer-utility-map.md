# Buyer utility map

A Buyer Utility Map is a visual representation that outlines the perceived value of various cooking techniques, photographic styles, financial products, or cognitive strategies to an individual based on their personal preferences and priorities. It helps in assessing which options maximize satisfaction by plotting utility values against different attributes for informed decision-making.
