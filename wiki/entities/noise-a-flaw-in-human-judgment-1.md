# Noise_ A Flaw in Human Judgment

"Noise_A_Flaw_in_Human_Judgment" refers to the inherent biases and errors in human decision-making that can distort perceptions of reality, leading to suboptimal choices. In cooking, it might manifest as overestimating one's culinary skills; in photography, misjudging lighting conditions; in personal finance, poor investment decisions due to emotional biases; and within cognitive science, challenges in accurately processing information or making logical inferences.
