# Autoregressive Models

An autoregressive model is a statistical tool that predicts future values based on past observations within the same time series. In cooking, it can forecast ingredient usage; in photography, it might analyze light patterns over shots; for personal finance, it could estimate expenses or savings trends; and in cognitive science, it may model sequential behavioral data.
