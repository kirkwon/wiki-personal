# Searing

In cooking, searing refers to the process of browning the surface of food at high temperatures, creating flavorful crusts through Maillard reactions. In photography, a 'sear' captures an image with intense contrast and sharp focus on details. Within personal finance, it metaphorically describes a significant financial loss or setback. Cognitive science uses the term to describe the acute awareness of one's thoughts or surroundings after a moment of heightened perception or realization.
