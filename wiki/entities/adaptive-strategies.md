# adaptive-strategies

Adaptive strategies are tailored approaches to efficiently manage tasks or challenges across various domains such as cooking (e.g., improvising recipes with available ingredients), photography (e.g., adjusting camera settings based on light conditions), personal finance (e.g., budgeting for unexpected expenses), and cognitive science (e.g., employing mnemonic devices to enhance memory recall). These strategies are flexible, context-sensitive, and aimed at optimizing performance or outcomes in each respective field.
