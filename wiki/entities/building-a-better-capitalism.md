# building-a-better-capitalism

Building better capitalism involves reforming economic systems to promote sustainability, equity, and innovation while aligning with individual values and societal needs. It entails creating financial structures that support personal growth, encourage responsible consumption, and foster creative industries like cooking and photography. By integrating cognitive science principles, it aims to enhance decision-making processes for improved economic outcomes.
