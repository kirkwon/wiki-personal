# The Wisdom of Crowds

*The Wisdom of Crowds* is a book by James Surowiecki that argues groups can be remarkably intelligent and effective decision-makers—often more so than individuals—under the right conditions. The book examines how diverse, independent opinions aggregated through proper mechanisms can lead to superior collective judgments. This connects to the [[conditions]] mental model and to concepts like [[superforecasting]] and [[prediction-markets]].
