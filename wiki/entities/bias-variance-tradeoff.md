# Bias-Variance Tradeoff

The Bias-Variance Tradeoff is the balancing act between underfitting (high bias) and overfitting (high variance). In cooking, it's like finding the right spice mix; in photography, adjusting exposure for optimal image quality; in personal finance, diversifying investments to avoid too much risk or reward; and in cognitive science, developing models that generalize well without being overly simplistic.
