# Portfolio Tracking Templates

Portfolio Tracking Templates are structured documents designed to systematically record and analyze an individual's collection of work or investments across various domains such as cooking recipes, photography portfolios, financial assets, and cognitive science experiments. These templates facilitate consistent data entry, comparison over time, and informed decision-making based on performance metrics within each field.
