# Scripts (AI)

A Script (AI) is a predefined sequence of instructions designed to automate tasks within various domains like cooking, photography, or personal finance management. In cognitive science, it represents the underlying mechanisms that guide behavior and decision-making processes. These scripts enable efficient execution of complex activities by leveraging artificial intelligence algorithms for pattern recognition, prediction, and optimization.
