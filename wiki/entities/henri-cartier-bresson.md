# Henri Cartier-Bresson

Henri Cartier-Bresson (1908-2004) was a French photographer and one of the pioneers of street photography and photojournalism. He is best known for coining the concept of the [[decisive-moment]] and for his humanist approach to photography.

## Key Contributions

- **The Decisive Moment**: Cartier-Bresson developed the theory that photography is about capturing the exact instant when all visual elements align — a concept that became foundational to street photography
- **Humanist Photography**: His work focused on people and their interactions with environment, capturing candid moments across Europe from the 1930s through the 1950s
- **Documentary Style**: His images tell stories about culture, era, and human experience

## Notable Work

- Europe 1930s-1950s: His iconic street and documentary photographs from across the continent

## Legacy

Cartier-Bresson's philosophy of presence, anticipation, and capturing fleeting moments remains central to [[street-photography]] practice today.
