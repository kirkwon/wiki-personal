# Real Estate Investment

A Real Estate Investment involves purchasing property with the intent to generate income through rental or capital appreciation over time. It's a tangible asset class that can diversify an investment portfolio and provide steady cash flow, similar to how ingredients contribute to a complex dish in cooking or foundational elements do in photography composition.
