# Multipliers - Greg McKeown

Greg McKeown is the author of *Multipliers: How the Best Leaders Make Everyone Smarter*, a leadership framework that identifies how exceptional leaders amplify the intelligence and capability of their teams rather than diminishing it.

## Key Ideas

McKeown's research and executive interviews led to the identification of the **Multiplier** archetype — leaders who create environments where people think deeply and contribute their best work. The counterpart is the **Diminisher** — leaders who create anxiety, suppress talent, and underutilize the people around them.

The five disciplines of Multipliers:
1. Attract and optimize talent
2. Create intensity that demands best thinking
3. Extend challenges
4. Debate decisions
5. Give credit
