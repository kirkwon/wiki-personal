# management-mental-model

A management-mental model is an individual's internal representation of how to organize tasks, make decisions, and manage resources effectively in various domains such as cooking (recipe planning), photography (composition techniques), personal finance (budgeting strategies), and cognitive science (information processing). It guides behavior by providing a structured approach for problem-solving and goal achievement.
