# Smarter Faster Better

*Smarter Faster Better* by Charles Duhigg explores the science of productivity through mental models. The book covers motivation, teams, focus, and decision-making, showing how understanding cognitive patterns enables better habitual systems.

## Key Concepts
- Motivation as a skill that can be developed
- Mental models for managing information and making decisions
- The role of autonomy and control in sustaining productive habits

## Relevance
Duhigg's work bridges cognitive science and habit formation, providing scientific grounding for the Habits mental model.
