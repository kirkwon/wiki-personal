# the-power-of-habits---charles-duhigg

Charles Duhigg's concept of 'the power of habits' refers to the psychological framework where routines become automatic behaviors through repetition, impacting various life aspects such as cooking (meal prep), photography (creative process), personal finance (budget adherence), and cognitive science (decision-making processes). This theory emphasizes understanding habit loops—cue, routine, reward—to effectively change or enhance behaviors.
