# Ray Dalio
Ray Dalio is an investor, author, and founder of Bridgewater Associates, one of the world's largest and most influential hedge funds. He is best known for developing a systematic, principles-driven approach to decision-making, investing, and organizational management, which he detailed in his 2024 book *Principles: Life and Work*. He is also known for extending these frameworks into macro geopolitical analysis. His simplified model of how economies function, focusing on credit cycles, productivity growth, and the relationship between spending and income, is documented in [[how-the-economic-machine-works---ray-dalio]].

## Key Contributions
- **Bridgewater Associates**: Founded in 1975, it became one of the world's largest and most influential hedge funds, known for its systematic and principled approach to investing.
- **Principles-driven framework**: A comprehensive system for making decisions by identifying and consistently applying personal and institutional principles, outlined in his book *Principles: Life and Work*. Key concepts include radical transparency, believability-weighted decision making, and treating organizations as machines.
- **Systems thinking in decisions**: Modeling complex decisions as systems with multiple variables, incorporating risk management and diversification.
- **Radical transparency**: Advocating for honesty and truth-seeking in relationships and organizations as a foundation for effectiveness.
- **Portfolio diversification**: Principles for diversifying across asset classes and geographies to mitigate risk and maximize long-term growth.
- **Organizational culture**: Emphasizing culture, communication, and employee engagement as critical to organizational success.
- **Big Cycle Theory**: In his 2024 book on the changing world order, Dalio argues that empires follow predictable patterns of rise and decline driven by debt accumulation, monetary policy, and shifting power balances. This framework extends his investment-oriented principles into the domain of global geopolitics and historical pattern recognition.
- **Economic Machine**: His simplified model of how economies function, focusing on credit cycles, productivity growth, and the relationship between spending and income.
