# Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion

*Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion* is a seminal book by psychologist Robert B. Cialdini, first published in 1984 and updated in subsequent editions. The book presents six universal principles that govern how people are persuaded and influenced in everyday interactions.

Robert B. Cialdini is a psychologist best known for his work on influence, compliance, and persuasion. His book *Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion* is a foundational text in behavioral psychology and marketing, identifying key principles that drive human decision-making in social contexts.

## The Six Principles of Influence
1. **Reciprocity** — People feel obligated to return favors, creating a powerful lever for influence.
2. **Commitment and Consistency** — Once people commit to a position, they are more likely to act in ways consistent with that commitment.
3. **Social Proof** — People look to the behavior of others to determine their own actions, especially in uncertain situations.
4. **Authority** — People defer to figures perceived as experts or authority figures.
5. **Liking** — People are more easily persuaded by those they like or find attractive.
6. **Scarcity** — Perceived scarcity or limited availability increases the desirability of something.

## Key Contributions
- Identified six key principles of persuasion: reciprocity, commitment/consistency, social proof, authority, liking, and scarcity.
- Developed frameworks for understanding how situational cues trigger compliance behaviors.
- His work on "situations" as a mental model emphasizes pattern recognition in complex social environments.

## Significance
Cialdini's framework is foundational to understanding human behavior in contexts ranging from marketing and sales to negotiation and leadership. The principles align with and complement concepts from Kahneman's dual process theory, where many of these influence tactics exploit fast, heuristic-based thinking.

## Relationship to Situations Model
Cialdini's work provides the source material for the Situations mental model, which uses situational analysis as a framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[Situations.md]
