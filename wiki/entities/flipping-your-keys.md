# flipping-your-keys

Flipping keys refers to the deliberate practice of switching between different perspectives or approaches within various domains such as cooking (recipe adaptation), photography (composition techniques), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (problem-solving methods). It involves actively seeking out alternative viewpoints to enhance creativity, efficiency, and decision-making.
