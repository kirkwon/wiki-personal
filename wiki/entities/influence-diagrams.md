# Influence Diagrams

An Influence Diagram is a graphical representation that illustrates the relationships among variables (factors), decisions, and uncertainties within a system. It helps in identifying key elements and their interconnections to optimize outcomes or understand complex interactions, applicable across diverse fields like recipe adjustments for cooking, composition rules for photography, investment strategies in personal finance, and decision-making processes in cognitive science.
