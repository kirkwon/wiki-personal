# stock-investment-tracker

A stock-investment-tracker is a digital tool designed to monitor the performance of various investments in real time, including stocks, bonds, and other securities. It provides analytical insights such as trends, risk assessments, and portfolio diversification recommendations, helping users make informed decisions about their financial assets. This functionality is particularly useful for individuals with an interest in personal finance who wish to track the progress of their investment portfolios alongside other hobbies like cooking or photography.
