# seeing-the-unseen

"Seeing-the-unseen" refers to the ability to perceive underlying patterns or information that is not immediately obvious in various contexts such as cooking (identifying subtle flavor combinations), photography (recognizing compositional elements beyond the frame), personal finance (spotting hidden expenses and investment opportunities), and cognitive science (understanding implicit biases). It involves honed intuition or analytical skills to uncover insights that enhance decision-making.
