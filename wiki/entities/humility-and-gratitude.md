# humility-and-gratitude

Humility-and-gratitude refers to the acknowledgment of one's limitations and appreciation for others' contributions in various domains such as cooking (recipe sharing), photography (crediting photographers), personal finance (recognizing financial advice from peers), and cognitive science (acknowledging research collaborations). It embodies a mindset that values learning, collaboration, and the recognition of interdependence in achieving goals.
