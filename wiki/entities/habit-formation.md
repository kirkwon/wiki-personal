# habit-formation

Habit-formation is the process by which new behaviors become automatic through repetition over time. In cooking, it involves regularly practicing recipes to master techniques; in photography, it means consistently applying skills for better image capture; in personal finance, habitual budgeting leads to improved financial health; and within cognitive science, studying habits helps understand brain function and behavior modification.
