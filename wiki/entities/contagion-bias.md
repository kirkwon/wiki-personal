# Contagion bias

Contagion bias is the cognitive tendency to overestimate the influence of one's own actions on external events or outcomes. In cooking, this may lead to attributing a dish's success solely to personal skill rather than recipe quality. In photography, it could cause an individual to believe their camera settings alone determine image excellence, ignoring light and composition factors. Within personal finance, contagion bias might result in overemphasizing one's investment decisions on market performance, disregarding broader economic trends. Lastly, from a cognitive science perspective, it reflects the flawed reasoning where individuals perceive their actions as more impactful than they truly are in shaping others' behaviors or outcomes.
