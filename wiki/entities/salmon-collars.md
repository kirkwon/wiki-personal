# Salmon Collars

[[salmon-collars]] are a cut from the head and collar area of the salmon, prized for their rich fat content and flavor. When grilled at high heat (400°F+), the [[maillard-reaction]] creates a crispy skin while rendered fat adds intensity and moisture to the flesh.

## Cooking Notes

- High heat promotes Maillard browning for crispy skin
- Rendered fat from the collar adds flavor complexity
- Pat dry before cooking for optimal browning
