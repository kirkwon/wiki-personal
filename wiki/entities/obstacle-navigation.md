# Obstacle navigation

Obstacle navigation refers to the process of identifying, assessing, and maneuvering around barriers or challenges that impede progress towards a goal. In cooking, it involves adapting recipes when faced with ingredient shortages; in photography, it's about adjusting techniques for unforeseen environmental factors; in personal finance, navigating through unexpected expenses to maintain financial stability; and within cognitive science, it pertains to overcoming mental blocks or biases that hinder decision-making.
