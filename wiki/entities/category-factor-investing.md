# Category:Factor Investing

In finance, Factor Investing is an approach that involves selecting stocks or other securities based on specific, quantifiable characteristics (factors) such as value, size, momentum, and quality, aiming to enhance returns relative to the market by exploiting these underlying traits. This strategy diverges from traditional investment methods which often focus on broad market exposure without differentiating between assets' intrinsic attributes.


In a personal context, adopting Factor Investing principles can be likened to choosing ingredients (factors) for recipes or selecting camera settings that highlight particular elements in photography, with the goal of achieving better outcomes through deliberate and informed choices rather than relying on general techniques.
