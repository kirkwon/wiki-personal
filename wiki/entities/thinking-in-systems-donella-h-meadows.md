# Thinking in Systems - Donella H Meadows

"Thinking in Systems is a framework developed by Donella H Meadows that emphasizes understanding complex systems through their interconnected components and feedback loops. It's applicable to cooking (recipe balancing), photography (composition harmony), personal finance (budget planning), and cognitive science (thought processes)."
