# Variational Inference

Variational inference is an approach to approximate probability distributions through optimization of simpler functions, similar to how one might estimate the perfect balance of ingredients in a recipe or adjust camera settings for optimal exposure. In personal finance, it's like finding the best investment strategy by approximating market trends; and in cognitive science, it helps model complex mental processes with more manageable computations.
