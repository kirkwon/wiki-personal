# mental-model

A mental model is an individual's internal representation of the external world that guides their understanding and decision-making processes in various domains such as cooking (recipe structure), photography (composition rules), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (perception and memory). It shapes how one interprets information, solves problems, and predicts outcomes.


Mental models are the frameworks that help us simplify complex systems by creating a simplified version of reality in our minds. They influence how we approach tasks like recipe creation, framing shots, budgeting, or analyzing cognitive phenomena.
