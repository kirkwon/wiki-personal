# Evaluation of Generative Models

Evaluation of generative models involves assessing their ability to create new data instances that are indistinguishable from real ones in specific contexts like cooking recipes or photographic compositions. In personal finance, it's about predicting future financial states based on past patterns. Within cognitive science, it aids in understanding and simulating human thought processes for more accurate models of intelligence.
