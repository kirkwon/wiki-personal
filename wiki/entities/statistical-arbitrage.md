# statistical-arbitrage

Statistical arbitrage is an investment strategy that exploits price discrepancies between related financial instruments using statistical models to predict short-term mispricings for profit. It involves complex mathematical analysis akin to adjusting camera settings or recipe proportions, but applied to market data instead of photos or ingredients.


In the context of personal finance and cognitive science, it's like identifying patterns in spending habits (cooking) or photographic trends (photography), then applying that knowledge to make informed financial decisions for potential gains.
