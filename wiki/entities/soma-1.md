# SoMa

SoMa stands for "Science of Money," which is an interdisciplinary approach that combines principles from cognitive science to understand financial decision-making processes. In the context of cooking and photography, SoMa can be applied by analyzing how individuals budget for ingredients or equipment based on their cognitive biases and heuristics. For personal finance, it involves studying patterns in spending behavior and investment strategies to optimize financial well-being.
