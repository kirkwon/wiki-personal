# Platform dominance

Platform dominance refers to the situation where one digital platform holds significant market power over others within its industry, often leading to reduced competition and potential monopolistic behavior. In contexts like cooking (e.g., recipe sharing platforms), photography (e.g., image editing software), personal finance (e.g., budget tracking apps), and cognitive science (e.g., research databases), a dominant platform can shape user habits, influence market trends, and impact the availability of diverse tools or services for consumers.
