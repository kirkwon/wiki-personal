# superforecasting - Philip E Tetlock  Dan Gardner

Superforecasting is the practice of making highly accurate predictions about future events by analyzing patterns, trends, and data through cognitive science principles. Philip E Tetlock's research emphasizes that expertise in forecasting involves a blend of open-mindedness, intellectual humility, and rigorous analytical skills. Dan Gardner explores the psychological aspects behind successful prediction, highlighting how personal biases can be managed for better decision-making.


In relation to Cooking, Superforecasting could involve predicting food trends or ingredient availability; in Photography, it might mean anticipating technological advancements and their impact on the art form; Personal Finance would benefit from forecasting economic shifts affecting investments; and within Cognitive Science, superforecasting can help understand how people make future-oriented decisions.
