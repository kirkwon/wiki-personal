# Annie Duke

Former professional poker player, author, and decision-making expert. Known for applying principles from poker and cognitive science to everyday decision-making.

## Works
- *Thinking in Bets* (2018) – Framework for treating decisions as bets under uncertainty
- *How to Decide* (2024) – Practical decision-making strategies
- *Quit: The Power of Knowing When to Walk Away* (2024) – Reframing quitting as a strategic skill

## Key Ideas
- Decision quality should be evaluated independently of outcome quality
- Embracing uncertainty and imperfection enables better quitting decisions
- Loss aversion and framing effects distort when we choose to persist or quit
- Quitting can be an act of self-care and strategic optimization
