# expectation

Expectation refers to the anticipated outcome or result based on prior knowledge or experience. In cooking, it's the predicted taste and presentation of a dish; in photography, it involves foreseeing the captured image quality; in personal finance, it pertains to projected savings or investment growth; while in cognitive science, expectation shapes our predictions about sensory stimuli.
