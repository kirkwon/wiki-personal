# Active Learning

Active learning is an educational approach where individuals actively engage with the material through hands-on experiences or problem-solving rather than passive reception of information. In cooking, it involves experimenting with recipes; in photography, it's practicing techniques and editing photos; for personal finance, it means applying budgeting strategies to real expenses; and within cognitive science, it refers to conducting experiments or simulations to understand learning processes.
