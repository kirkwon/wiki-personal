# storm-watching

Storm-watching is the act of observing weather phenomena, specifically storms, often with the intent to capture their beauty or intensity through photography. It requires an understanding of atmospheric conditions similar to how one studies ingredients and processes in cooking, involves patience akin to personal finance investment strategies, and engages cognitive skills for anticipating changes and planning shots like predicting market trends.
