# Kalita Wave Brewer

The Kalita Wave is a flat-bottom pour-over brewer with three small holes. It is designed to be more forgiving and consistent, making it better for beginners.

## Characteristics
- Flat bottom, three small holes
- More forgiving, consistent
- Better for beginners
- Emphasizes body and sweetness

## Technique
- Can be more aggressive
- Less precise pouring needed
- More forgiving of timing
- Total time: 3-5 minutes

## Best For
Those seeking consistent results with emphasis on body and sweetness rather than acidity and clarity.
