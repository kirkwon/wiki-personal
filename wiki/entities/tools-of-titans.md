# Tools of Titans

*Tools of Titans* by Timothy Ferriss compiles the habits, rituals, and strategies of world-class performers across business, health, and creative domains. The book functions as a survey of high-performer routines.

## Key Concepts
- Morning routines and energy management habits
- Learning and skill acquisition habits
- Decision-making frameworks used by top performers

## Relevance
Provides empirical examples of habit patterns across domains, supporting the universal applicability of the Habits mental model.
