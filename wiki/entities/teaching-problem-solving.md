# teaching-problem-solving

Teaching-problem-solving is the educational approach that equips individuals with strategies to identify issues in various domains such as cooking (recipe adaptation), photography (creative composition), personal finance (budget management), and cognitive science (memory enhancement). It emphasizes critical thinking, practical application of knowledge, and adaptability to overcome challenges.
