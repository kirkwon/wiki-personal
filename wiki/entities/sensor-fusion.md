# Sensor Fusion

Sensor fusion is the process of integrating data from multiple sensors to produce more accurate, reliable, and comprehensive information than what could be obtained by using any individual sensor alone. In cooking, it can mean combining temperature readings from different sources for precise heat control; in photography, it involves merging inputs from cameras or light detectors for better image quality; in personal finance, it might refer to aggregating data from various financial tracking tools to gain a holistic view of one's economic status; and in cognitive science, sensor fusion can describe the brain's ability to combine sensory information for enhanced perception.
