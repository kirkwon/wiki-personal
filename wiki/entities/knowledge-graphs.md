# Knowledge Graphs

A Knowledge Graph is a structured representation of information where entities (such as recipes or photographic techniques) are interconnected through relationships, enabling efficient data retrieval and analysis across various domains like cooking, photography, personal finance management, and cognitive science research. It facilitates the understanding of complex networks by mapping out how different concepts relate to one another.
