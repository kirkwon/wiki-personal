# Stable Baselines3

Stable Baselines3 is an advanced machine learning framework designed to provide robust, efficient, and scalable algorithms for reinforcement learning tasks. Its structure facilitates the development of stable models that can be easily adapted across various domains such as cooking (recipe optimization), photography (image enhancement), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (behavior prediction).
