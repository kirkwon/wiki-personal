# Big Debt Crises

A Big Debt Crisis refers to an economic situation where the collective debt of individuals or nations reaches unsustainable levels, leading to financial instability, reduced spending power, and potential defaults on loans. In personal finance terms, it can mean a point where one's accumulated debts overwhelmingly exceed their income capacity, risking credit score damage and difficulty in securing future loans.


In the context of cognitive science, understanding Big Debt Crises involves analyzing how financial stress impacts decision-making processes, potentially leading to suboptimal choices due to heightened anxiety or short-term thinking over long-term planning.
