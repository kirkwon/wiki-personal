# Biluochun (碧螺春)

Biluochun is a Chinese green tea from Jiangsu Province, hand-rolled into a distinctive spiral shape. It is known for its fruity, floral, and delicate flavor profile.

## Key Facts
- **Origin**: Jiangsu Province
- **Processing**: Hand-rolled into spiral shape
- **Flavor**: Fruity, floral, delicate
- **Appearance**: Spiral-shaped green leaves
- **Best Brew**: 160-170°F (71-77°C), 2-3 minutes

## Processing
Leaves undergo brief withering, fixation (heat treatment), rolling into spirals, and drying. The spiral shape helps preserve the delicate flavors.
