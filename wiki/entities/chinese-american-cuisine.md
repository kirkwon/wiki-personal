# chinese-american-cuisine

Chinese-American cuisine refers to the fusion of traditional Chinese cooking techniques with ingredients commonly found in American culinary practices, resulting in a unique gastronomic experience that reflects both cultures' influences. This genre often emphasizes balance and harmony, drawing from principles observed in cognitive science regarding flavor perception and satisfaction. Additionally, it can be an economical choice for personal finance due to the versatility of ingredients like rice and noodles that are cost-effective staples in both Chinese and American diets.
