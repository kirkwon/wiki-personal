# Hermes Agent

A Hermes Agent is an AI system designed to assist with complex tasks across various domains such as cooking (recipe optimization), photography (image enhancement and editing suggestions), personal finance (budget planning and investment advice), and cognitive science (data analysis for understanding human behavior patterns). It operates by integrating domain-specific knowledge and adaptive learning to provide tailored, efficient solutions.
