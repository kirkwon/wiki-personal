# Photography

Photography is the art and science of capturing light on a photosensitive surface to create images that represent a moment in time or convey an idea. It involves understanding composition, exposure, and lighting techniques to produce visually compelling photographs. In relation to cooking, photography can be used to document recipes, present dishes attractively, and share culinary experiences through visual storytelling.
