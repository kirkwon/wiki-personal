# historical-volatility

Historical volatility refers to the degree of variation in the price or value of an asset over a specified period. In cooking terms, it's like measuring how much ingredient quantities fluctuate during recipe adjustments; in photography, it's analogous to variations in exposure settings affecting image brightness; for personal finance, it mirrors the unpredictability of investment returns over time; and in cognitive science, it parallels the variability in reaction times or decision-making processes.
