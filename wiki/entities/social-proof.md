# Social proof

Social proof is the psychological phenomenon where people assume the actions of others reflect correct behavior, often used in marketing to influence consumer decisions. In cooking, it refers to following popular recipes or techniques; in photography, adopting widely-used editing styles; for personal finance, mimicking successful investment strategies; and in cognitive science, studying behaviors that are prevalent within groups as indicators of normative actions.
