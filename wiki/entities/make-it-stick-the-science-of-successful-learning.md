# Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning

"Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning" is a book that explores effective learning strategies grounded in cognitive science principles. It emphasizes the importance of active engagement, spaced repetition, and retrieval practice to enhance long-term retention of information—key for mastering cooking recipes, photography techniques, personal finance concepts, and more.
