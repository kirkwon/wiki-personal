# Transfer Learning

Transfer learning is the process of applying knowledge gained from solving one problem to a different but related problem. In cooking, it's like using recipes for similar dishes; in photography, adapting camera settings for various light conditions; in personal finance, leveraging investment strategies across diverse portfolios; and in cognitive science, applying learning techniques from one domain to enhance understanding in another.
