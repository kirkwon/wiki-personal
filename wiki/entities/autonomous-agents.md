# Autonomous Agents

An autonomous agent is an entity capable of making independent decisions based on its own perceptions and objectives without external control. In cooking, this could refer to a smart kitchen appliance that adjusts recipes; in photography, it might be an AI-driven camera system selecting optimal settings; for personal finance, autonomous agents are software programs managing investments or budgeting tasks; and within cognitive science, they represent models simulating human decision-making processes.
