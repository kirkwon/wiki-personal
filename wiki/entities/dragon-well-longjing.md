# Dragon Well (龙井茶)

Dragon Well (Longjing) is one of China's finest green teas, originating from Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province. It is pan-fired with flattened leaves, producing a chestnut, vegetal, smooth, and complex flavor profile.

## Key Facts
- **Origin**: Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province
- **Processing**: Pan-fired, flattened leaves
- **Flavor**: Chestnut, vegetal, smooth, complex
- **Appearance**: Flat, green leaves, pale liquor
- **Best Brew**: 160-175°F (71-80°C), 2-3 minutes
- **Special Note**: Famous for its "four wonders": flat leaves, emerald color, fresh aroma, mellow taste

## Processing
The leaves are stir-fried at high temperature (100-150°C) for 5-10 minutes, a Chinese fixation method that creates more complex, slightly roasted flavors compared to Japanese steaming.

## Pairings
Pairs well with light meals, seafood, salads, and simple desserts. Avoid heavy or strongly spiced dishes.
