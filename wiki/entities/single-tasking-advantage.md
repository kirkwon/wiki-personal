# single-tasking-advantage

Single-tasking-advantage refers to the enhanced focus and quality outcomes achieved when concentrating on one activity at a time. In cooking, it allows for precise flavor control; in photography, it leads to sharper images; in personal finance, it improves decision accuracy; while in cognitive science, it correlates with better memory retention and learning efficiency.
