# Woodford Reserve

Kentucky Straight Bourbon described as a refined daily sipper in the mid price tier.

## Key Details

- **Type**: Kentucky Straight Bourbon
- **Price**: $35-40
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Tasting Notes

- **Nose**: Refined vanilla, oak, mint
- **Palate**: Balanced, complex, approachable
- **Finish**: Medium-long, elegant
- **Verdict**: Refined daily sipper

## Comparison

Moderate sweetness (★★★) and spice (★★) with good complexity (★★★★) and value (★★★★) for daily drinking.
