# Description Logics

Description Logics (DL) are formal frameworks used to represent knowledge about concepts within domains, similar to defining ingredients in recipes for cooking or camera settings for photography. They enable the structuring of complex information through hierarchies and relationships, akin to categorizing expenses in personal finance or understanding cognitive processes in science. DLs facilitate reasoning over these structures, allowing for inferences that can be as precise as adjusting light exposure based on subject matter in photography.
