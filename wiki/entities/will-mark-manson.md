# Will - Mark Manson

"Will by Mark Manson is an influential book that challenges conventional wisdom with a focus on simplicity and authenticity in life's pursuits. It advocates for prioritizing meaningful goals over material success, which resonates with personal finance strategies centered around purpose-driven spending. The principles of 'Will' can also be applied to cooking by emphasizing quality ingredients and mindful preparation, as well as in photography through the lens of capturing authentic moments."
