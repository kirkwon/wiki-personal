# enemy-mental-model

An enemy-mental model is a cognitive framework that individuals use to anticipate and strategize against perceived threats or challenges within various domains such as cooking (e.g., recipe failures), photography (e.g., capturing elusive moments), personal finance (e.g., unexpected expenses), and broader cognitive processes (e.g., overcoming biases). It involves understanding the adversary's tactics, weaknesses, and potential counter-strategies to effectively navigate or mitigate negative outcomes.
