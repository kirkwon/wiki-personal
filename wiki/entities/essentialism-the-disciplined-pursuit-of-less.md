# Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less

*Essentialism: The Disciplined Pursuit of Less* is a book by Greg McKeown that advocates for a disciplined approach to identifying and focusing on what is truly essential. The core thesis is that by carefully selecting commitments and eliminating the non-essential, individuals can achieve greater effectiveness and fulfillment.

The book provides the intellectual foundation for the [[commitments]] mental model, emphasizing that commitments should be chosen deliberately rather than accumulated passively.
