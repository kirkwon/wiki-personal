# Policy Gradient Methods

Policy gradient methods are optimization techniques used to directly adjust the parameters of a policy function that dictates decision-making in uncertain environments, similar to how one might tweak recipes or camera settings for desired outcomes. In personal finance, they can be likened to dynamically adapting investment strategies based on market feedback. From a cognitive science perspective, these methods model the learning process of agents that improve their behavior over time through trial and error.
