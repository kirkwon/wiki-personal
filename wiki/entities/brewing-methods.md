# brewing-methods

Brewing methods refer to the various techniques used to prepare beverages through fermentation or steeping processes. These include traditional styles such as lagering, ale-making, and sour brewing, each with unique steps affecting flavor, aroma, and texture. Understanding these methods is crucial for both crafting high-quality drinks at home and analyzing the cognitive aspects of taste preference in personal finance decisions related to beverage consumption.
