# Six paths framework for market boundary reconstruction

The Six Paths Framework is an analytical tool used to delineate market boundaries by examining six key dimensions: product attributes, customer segments, distribution channels, competitor strategies, regulatory environment, and technological advancements. It aids in understanding the complex interplay between these factors across various industries such as Cooking (culinary markets), Photography (photo equipment market), Personal Finance (financial services market), and Cognitive Science (educational technology market).


In essence, it provides a structured approach to dissect and reconstruct the boundaries of different markets by assessing how each dimension influences market dynamics.
