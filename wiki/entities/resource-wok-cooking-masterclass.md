# Resource: Wok Cooking Masterclass

A masterclass resource focused on developing proficiency in wok cooking techniques, including proper heat management, wok preparation, and the development of [[wok-hei]]. This resource covers the skills needed to execute high-heat stir-fry methods effectively.

Key topics:
- Wok selection and seasoning (seasoning the wok)
- Achieving and maintaining proper wok temperature
- Tossing and stirring techniques for even cooking
- Layering ingredients based on cook time
- Post-cook techniques like [[resting-meat]] for proteins

This resource complements [[wok-cooking]] and connects to [[recipe-perfect-stir-fry]] as a practical learning path.
