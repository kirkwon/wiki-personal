# Distributional RL

Distributional Reinforcement Learning (DRL) is an approach that focuses on learning value functions based on the distribution of experiences rather than just individual rewards. It's akin to understanding diverse ingredients in cooking for optimal flavor combinations, or capturing varied lighting conditions in photography for richer images; DRL seeks patterns across different scenarios to make more informed decisions. In personal finance and cognitive science, it helps model complex decision-making processes by considering the full spectrum of possible outcomes rather than isolated events.
