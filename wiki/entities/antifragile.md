# Antifragile

Antifragility refers to the capacity of systems or entities to not only withstand stressors but also to improve as a result of them. In cooking, this could mean recipes that enhance in flavor when exposed to high heat; in photography, techniques that produce better images under challenging light conditions; within personal finance, investment strategies that grow stronger through market volatility; and in cognitive science, the concept of neural plasticity where brain connections strengthen with diverse learning experiences.
