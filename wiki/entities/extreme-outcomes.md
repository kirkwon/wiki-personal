# extreme-outcomes

Extreme-outcomes refer to the most significant possible results or consequences within specific domains such as cooking (e.g., overcooked food leading to inedible meals), photography (e.g., extreme lighting causing glare on photos), personal finance (e.g., catastrophic financial loss due to poor investment choices), and cognitive science (e.g., severe memory impairment affecting daily functioning). These outcomes represent the upper bounds of risk or impact in each field, often requiring careful management or mitigation strategies.
