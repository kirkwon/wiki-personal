# Monetary illusion

Monetary illusion refers to the misconception that changes in nominal money supply directly affect real economic variables like purchasing power or employment when they actually influence only price levels. This concept is analogous to mistaking a blurred photograph for a clear one, where the true image (economic reality) remains obscured by the distortion (nominal changes).


In personal finance, it's like confusing an increase in cash holdings with increased wealth if not accounting for inflation. Cognitively, this illusion can lead to flawed decision-making similar to misjudging a recipe by ignoring the impact of ingredient proportions on taste and texture.
