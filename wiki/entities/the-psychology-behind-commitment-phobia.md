# the-psychology-behind-commitment-phobia

Commitment phobia is an anxiety disorder characterized by the persistent avoidance of emotional attachment or long-term commitments due to fear of loss, confinement, or responsibility. This psychological phenomenon can manifest in various aspects of life, including relationships and career choices, often leading individuals to prioritize short-term pleasures over long-term goals. Understanding its roots requires an interdisciplinary approach, drawing insights from cognitive science's study of decision-making processes, as well as considering the impact on personal endeavors like cooking, photography, and financial planning.
