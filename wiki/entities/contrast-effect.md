# Contrast effect

The contrast effect is the phenomenon where perception or judgment of something is influenced by comparison with an adjacent or associated stimulus that differs in intensity or quality. In cooking, this might mean a spicy dish tastes less intense after eating a sweet dessert. In photography, it can affect how we perceive the brightness and color balance between different elements of an image. For personal finance, contrast effect could alter our view on savings or expenses when compared to others' financial situations. In cognitive science, this concept explains variations in decision-making based on comparative contexts rather than absolute values.
