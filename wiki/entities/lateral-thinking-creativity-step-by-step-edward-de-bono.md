# Lateral Thinking_ Creativity Step by Step - Edward de Bono

Lateral Thinking is an approach to problem solving that emphasizes the value of ideas or solutions that are not immediately obvious through traditional step-by-step logic. Edward de Bono's method involves challenging assumptions, exploring unorthodox connections between seemingly unrelated phenomena, and employing creative techniques like 'Six Thinking Hats' to enhance cognitive flexibility in areas such as cooking innovation, photography composition, personal finance strategies, and understanding complex mental processes.
