# James Clear

James Clear is the author of *Atomic Habits* (2024), one of the most influential books on habit formation and behavior change. His work distills research from psychology and behavioral science into practical frameworks for building good habits and breaking bad ones. Clear emphasizes identity-based habit change, systems thinking, environment design, and the power of marginal gains. He argues that small behavioral changes compound into transformative results and provides practical frameworks including habit stacking, the habit loop, and accountability systems.

## Key Works
- *Atomic Habits* — A framework built around the four-step habit loop (cue, craving, response, reward) and the four laws of behavior change: make it obvious, make it attractive, make it easy, make it satisfying.

## Core Concepts
- Systems over willpower
- Exponential power of compounding habits
- Identity-based change
- Atomic habit stacking
- Behavioral science bridging self-imimprovement
- Environment design
- Power of marginal gains

## Relevance
Clear's framework is central to the Habits mental model, providing the most widely cited modern framework for understanding how habits form and persist. His work bridges behavioral science and self-improvement, emphasizing the exponential power of compounding habits. The Formation mental model references his work as a cross-source perspective. Clear's model operationalizes the broader habit mental model, connecting to the [[hook-model]], [[habit-testing]], and related behavior design concepts already documented in the wiki. His framework complements Nir Eyal's [[hook-model]] by applying habit design principles to personal rather than product contexts.
