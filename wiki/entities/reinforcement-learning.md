# Reinforcement Learning

Reinforcement Learning (RL) is an area of machine learning where algorithms learn optimal behaviors through rewards and penalties, akin to how one might experiment with recipes or adjust camera settings for perfect shots. In personal finance, it can optimize investment strategies by maximizing returns over time, while in cognitive science, RL models mimic the way humans learn from consequences of actions.
