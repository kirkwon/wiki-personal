# time-delay-decisions

Time-delay decisions are choices made with the understanding that their outcomes will not be immediately realized or known; they require patience and foresight in areas such as cooking (e.g., slow fermentation), photography (e.g., long exposure shots), personal finance (e.g., retirement planning), and cognitive science (e.g., delayed gratification experiments). These decisions often involve weighing immediate benefits against potential future gains or consequences, necessitating a strategic approach to timing and execution.
