# rational-decision-making

Rational decision-making is the process of making choices based on logical analysis and objective criteria to achieve desired outcomes efficiently. In cooking, it involves selecting recipes that optimize taste and nutrition; in photography, it's about choosing settings for optimal image quality; in personal finance, it entails budgeting and investment strategies aimed at maximizing returns while minimizing risks; and within cognitive science, it refers to understanding how individuals make decisions under uncertainty.
