# Belief‑Desire‑Intention (BDI)

The Belief-Desire-Intention (BDI) model is an approach that explains human decision-making by considering three components: beliefs about the world, desires representing personal goals, and intentions as plans to achieve those goals. In cooking, it helps understand recipe choices based on ingredient availability; in photography, it aids in selecting camera settings aligning with desired outcomes; for personal finance, it guides investment decisions reflecting risk tolerance and financial objectives; and within cognitive science, it provides insight into how individuals form strategies to navigate complex tasks.
