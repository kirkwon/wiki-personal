# Areas

An 'Area of Interest' refers to a specific domain or subject that an individual is passionate about and seeks knowledge in. For example, cooking could involve exploring various cuisines and culinary techniques; photography might encompass learning different styles and equipment use; personal finance would focus on budgeting, investment strategies, and financial planning; while cognitive science pertains to understanding human thought processes and brain function.
