# Just-world hypothesis

The Just-world hypothesis is the belief that people get what they deserve; it's an assumption that actions lead to fair consequences. In personal finance, this might manifest as a conviction that hard work leads to wealth. However, in cognitive science, researchers study how this bias can affect perception and decision-making processes.


**Note:** While the Just-world hypothesis is not directly related to cooking or photography, it's relevant when considering personal biases that might influence these activities, such as assuming a good dish will always turn out well (cooking) or that skillful photographers consistently capture perfect images.
