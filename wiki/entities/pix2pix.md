# Pix2Pix

Pix2Pix is an artificial intelligence framework designed to perform image-to-image translation tasks by learning from paired examples; it can be applied in cooking for recipe visualization, photography for enhancing images, personal finance for generating financial charts or reports, and cognitive science for simulating experimental data.
