# when-to-stop

In cooking, the 'when-to-stop' rule refers to determining the precise moment when ingredients are perfectly prepared or a dish is fully cooked without overdoing it. In photography, it denotes stopping at the optimal exposure time before underexposure or overexposure occurs. For personal finance, 'when-to-stop' means ceasing investment contributions when one has reached their financial goals or risk tolerance level. Lastly, in cognitive science, it signifies halting a learning process once mastery is achieved to prevent stagnation and encourage further growth.
