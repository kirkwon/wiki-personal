# Self-serving bias

Self-serving bias is the cognitive tendency to attribute successes to internal factors while blaming failures on external circumstances. This psychological phenomenon can influence cooking by leading chefs to credit their skills for a dish's praise, but not its flaws; in photography, it might cause photographers to take credit for good shots and dismiss poor ones as technical issues; in personal finance, individuals may overestimate their investment choices during gains but deflect responsibility when losses occur. Understanding this bias is crucial across various domains including cognitive science, where its implications on decision-making processes are studied.
