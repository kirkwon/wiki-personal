# Tools of Titans

*Tools of Titans* by Timothy Ferriss compiles the habits, routines, and tactics of world-class performers across domains. It treats the strategies of elite individuals as transferable tools that can be adopted and adapted for personal optimization.

## Key Concepts
- Compilation of elite habits and routines
- Transferable tools across domains
- Systematic optimization through adopted practices

## Relevance to Tools Mental Model
The book exemplifies the Tools mental model by cataloging and curating actionable strategies from high performers as tools anyone can deploy.
