# 16th Avenue Tiled Steps

The most famous stairway in Golden Gate Heights, the 16th Avenue Tiled Steps is a 163-step mosaic stairway installed in 2005 as a community art project. Each tile is unique, creating a colorful mosaic that has become an iconic landmark of the neighborhood.

## Description

- **Steps**: 163 steps covered in colorful tiles
- **Origin**: Community art project by local volunteers
- **Installation**: 2005
- **Design**: Each tile is different, creating a mosaic effect
- **Popularity**: Popular for photography and as a must-see attraction

## Access

- Location: 16th Avenue and Moraga Street
- Street parking on surrounding streets
- Walkable from Golden Gate Park (15-20 minutes)

## What to See

- Beautiful tile mosaic up the stairway
- View from the top: Ocean Beach, Golden Gate Park, and the city
- Golden Gate Heights park at the top
- Best visited early morning to avoid crowds

## Other Notable Stairways

- **14th Ave Steps**: 14th Ave & Moraga, Grandview Park access, steep
- **Moraga St Steps**: Moraga & 15th Ave, residential access, garden views
- **15th Ave Steps**: 15th Ave & Irving, shortcut through hillside
