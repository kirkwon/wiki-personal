# Herbert A. Simon

Herbert A. Simon was an American economist, political scientist, and cognitive psychologist renowned for his work in decision-making processes and bounded rationality. His theories have influenced various fields including cooking by emphasizing the importance of heuristics in recipe creation, photography through understanding visual perception, personal finance via behavioral economic insights, and cognitive science as a foundational figure in artificial intelligence research.
