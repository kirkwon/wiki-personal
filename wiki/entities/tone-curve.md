# Tone Curve

A Tone Curve is a graphical representation of the tonal range within an image or dataset, used to adjust contrast and exposure by mapping input levels (measured in luminance) to output levels (perceived brightness). In cooking, it might metaphorically describe balancing flavors; in photography, it's crucial for image editing; in personal finance, it could symbolize the distribution of investment risk; and in cognitive science, it may relate to perception thresholds.
