# incentive-design

Incentive-design refers to the strategic creation of motivations or rewards within systems to influence behavior positively. In cooking, it might involve crafting recipes that are both delicious and healthy; in photography, designing challenges with prizes for capturing unique images; in personal finance, structuring savings plans with clear benefits; and in cognitive science, developing experiments where participants gain insights or recognition.
