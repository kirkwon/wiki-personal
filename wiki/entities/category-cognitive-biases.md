# Category:Cognitive Biases

A cognitive bias is an inherent pattern of deviation in judgment that occurs within the human thought process, often leading to perceptual distortion, inaccurate judgment, or illogical interpretation. In cooking and photography, these biases can influence creativity but also result in flawed decision-making. For personal finance, understanding cognitive biases is crucial for making rational investment choices and avoiding common pitfalls like overconfidence or loss aversion.
