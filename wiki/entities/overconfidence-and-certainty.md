# Overconfidence and certainty

In cooking, overconfidence may lead to disregarding recipe instructions, resulting in culinary mishaps. In photography, certainty can cause photographers to miss creative opportunities by sticking rigidly to technical rules. Within personal finance, overconfidence often leads individuals to take on excessive risks with investments. Cognitive science explores how overconfidence and certainty biases affect decision-making processes across various domains.
