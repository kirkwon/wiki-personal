# tax-planning

Tax planning is the strategic approach to arranging financial affairs with the aim of minimizing tax liability within legal boundaries. It involves analyzing one's income, expenses, and investments to optimize fiscal efficiency over time. Like adjusting recipes for cost-effective cooking or choosing camera settings for optimal photos, tax planning tailors personal finance strategies to individual circumstances.
