# team-decisions

Team decisions refer to collaborative choices made by a group of individuals with diverse expertise or interests in areas such as cooking (recipe selection), photography (shooting angles), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (experimental design). These decisions are reached through consensus, leveraging collective knowledge to optimize outcomes.
