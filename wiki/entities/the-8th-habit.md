# The 8th Habit

*The 8th Habit* by Stephen R. Covey extends his earlier framework by addressing the need to find one's voice and purpose. The book argues that effectiveness alone is insufficient—greatness requires alignment with deeper meaning.

## Key Concepts
- Moving from effectiveness to greatness
- Finding your voice through disciplined habits
- The importance of contribution and service

## Relevance
Extends the Habits mental model by connecting disciplined habit formation to purpose and meaning.
