# Expert illusion

An expert illusion is the false perception of possessing superior knowledge or skill in an area such as cooking, photography, personal finance, or cognitive science, often due to limited experience or understanding. It can lead individuals to overestimate their abilities and make uninformed decisions based on this misplaced confidence.


In the context of cooking, it refers to someone who assumes they are a gourmet chef without having extensive culinary training. In photography, it's when an amateur believes they can capture professional-level images without formal education in the field. For personal finance, it involves individuals thinking they have mastered investment strategies or financial planning without proper knowledge. Lastly, within cognitive science, expert illusion might manifest as someone who claims to understand complex mental processes without a background in psychology or neuroscience.
