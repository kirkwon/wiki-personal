# Barry Schwartz

Psychologist and author best known for *The Paradox of Choice*, which argues that an abundance of options in modern society leads to decision paralysis, stress, anxiety, regret, and lower satisfaction rather than higher happiness. His work draws on psychological research and behavioral economics to demonstrate that more choices reduce rather than enhance well-being.

## Key Work
- **The Paradox of Choice** (2004, 2024 edition) — argues that more choices do not increase satisfaction and can cause psychological harm.

## Core Ideas
- Humans evolved for scarcity of options, not abundance.
- Loss aversion intensifies when many options are available.
- Satisfaction is higher under limitations than under endless abundance.
- Popularized the concept of choice overload as a significant psychological phenomenon.
- Challenged the assumption that more freedom of choice always leads to greater happiness.
- Social norms and context shape how difficult decisions feel.
- Relationships can help mitigate the stress of overchoice.
- Emphasized the role of social norms, context, and interpersonal relationships in mitigating the stress of choice.

## Practical Strategies
- Prioritizing values, setting limits on options, and embracing imperfection
- Coping with decision paralysis through deliberate constraint
