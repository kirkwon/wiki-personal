# Markov Decision Processes

A Markov Decision Process (MDP) is a mathematical framework used to model decision making in situations where outcomes are partly random and partly under the control of a decision maker. In cooking, it could represent choosing ingredients based on their probabilities of enhancing flavor; in photography, selecting camera settings for desired exposure; in personal finance, deciding investment strategies considering market volatility; and in cognitive science, understanding how humans make choices under uncertainty.
