# Flavor Building

Flavor building is the process of enhancing taste profiles through careful selection and combination of ingredients in cooking or photographic composition to create a harmonious final product. In personal finance, it refers to strategically accumulating assets that complement each other for diversified investment growth. Within cognitive science, flavor building can describe the mental construction of complex ideas by integrating various concepts and knowledge points.
