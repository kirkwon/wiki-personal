# PhotoPills

PhotoPills is an app designed to help users understand how light affects their bodies and photographs in various situations, offering insights into exposure settings for photography. It also provides information on sun protection and skin health, which can be valuable knowledge when spending time outdoors during activities like cooking or hiking. Additionally, understanding the app's features contributes to cognitive science by enhancing visual literacy and decision-making skills in image capture scenarios.
