# Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)

Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) is an advanced reinforcement learning algorithm that iteratively improves decision-making policies by balancing exploration with exploitation to maximize cumulative rewards over time. In the context of cooking, it could optimize recipe adjustments for taste and efficiency; in photography, it might refine camera settings for desired image outcomes; in personal finance, PPO can be used to develop strategies that enhance investment returns while managing risk; and within cognitive science, it aids in modeling how humans learn from consequences of actions.
