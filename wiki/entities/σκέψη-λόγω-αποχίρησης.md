# Σκέψη Λόγω Αποχίρησης

Σκέψη Λόγω Αποχής (SLA) refers to the cognitive process of deliberating over financial decisions, weighing potential risks against rewards. In personal finance contexts like investments or budgeting, it involves analyzing data and probabilities similar to how one might evaluate a recipe's success or photograph composition for optimal results.
