# Visual Design

Visual design refers to the art of creating visual content that is both functional and appealing. In cooking, it involves plating techniques; in photography, it encompasses composition and lighting; for personal finance, it's about presenting data clearly through charts or infographics; and within cognitive science, it relates to how visual elements influence perception and decision-making.
