# Mise en Place

'Mise en Place' is the practice of organizing ingredients and tools before cooking to streamline the process. In photography, it refers to preparing equipment and setting up a scene for optimal workflow efficiency. Similarly, in personal finance, it involves arranging financial documents and planning ahead, while in cognitive science, it pertains to structuring information for effective processing and memory retention.
