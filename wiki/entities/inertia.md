# inertia

Inertia refers to the resistance of any physical object to a change in its state of motion or rest. In cooking, it describes how ingredients maintain their temperature until acted upon by heat. In photography, it's the camera's tendency to continue exposure after shutter release. Financially, it represents the reluctance to alter investment strategies without compelling reasons. Cognitively, inertia can describe mental habits that persist despite new information or evidence.
