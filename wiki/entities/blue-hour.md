# Blue Hour

The Blue Hour refers to the period of twilight that occurs just after sunset or before sunrise when the remaining daylight is predominantly blue in color. This timeframe is particularly significant for photography enthusiasts due to its unique lighting conditions, which can enhance the aesthetic appeal of images. In cognitive science, it's an interesting subject as it relates to human perception and the psychological impact of natural light on mood and circadian rhythms.
