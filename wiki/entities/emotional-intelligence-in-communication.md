# Emotional intelligence in communication

Emotional intelligence (EI) in communication refers to the ability to perceive, understand, and manage one's own emotions as well as those of others during interactions. It involves empathy, active listening, and adaptability, which are crucial for effective collaboration and conflict resolution across various domains such as cooking teams, photography crews, financial advisors, and cognitive science researchers.
