# Thinking in Systems - Donella H Meadows

*Thinking in Systems* by Donella H. Meadows provides a foundational framework for understanding system dynamics, including stocks, flows, feedback loops, delays, and leverage points. Meadows draws on decades of work in environmental systems and organizational dynamics, offering a primer on systems thinking that explains how to identify high-leverage interventions capable of shifting system behavior at fundamental levels.

## Key Ideas

- Systems are defined by their structure, behavior, and interconnections—made of elements, interconnections, and purposes
- Feedback loops drive system behavior—reinforcing loops amplify, balancing loops stabilize
- Leverage points are places within a system where a small shift can produce large changes
- Stock and flow as fundamental system structures
- Understanding mental models and assumptions is critical to system analysis

## Connection to Systems Mental Model

Meadows' work is one of the seven source perspectives informing the [[systems]] mental model, and connects to the existing [[limits-to-growth]] concept in this wiki.

## Connection to Change

Meadows' framework represents systems-based change — the understanding that in complex systems, the most effective change comes from identifying and intervening at high-leverage points rather than applying surface-level fixes.
