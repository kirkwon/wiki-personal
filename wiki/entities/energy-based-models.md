# Energy-Based Models

Energy-based models (EBMs) are computational frameworks that represent complex systems through energy landscapes, where the system's state corresponds to a low-energy configuration. In cooking, EBMs can optimize recipe outcomes by evaluating ingredient interactions; in photography, they assist in image processing for enhanced quality; within personal finance, they model investment risks and returns; and in cognitive science, they offer insights into decision-making processes.
