# decision-making frameworks

Decision-making frameworks are structured approaches used to evaluate options and make choices systematically across various domains such as cooking (e.g., recipe selection), photography (e.g., composition techniques), personal finance (e.g., budget planning), and cognitive science (e.g., problem-solving strategies). They provide a consistent method to assess the pros and cons, predict outcomes, and align decisions with individual goals or scientific principles.
