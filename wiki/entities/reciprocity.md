# Reciprocity

Reciprocity is the mutual exchange of benefits or actions between individuals or entities, often leading to an ongoing relationship where each party provides value in return for what they receive from the other. In cooking, it's seen when sharing ingredients or recipes; in photography, through collaborative projects and feedback; in personal finance, via barter systems or shared investments; and in cognitive science, as a principle underlying social interactions and cooperative behaviors.
