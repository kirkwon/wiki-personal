# I Will Teach You to Be Rich

A six-week personal finance action program by Ramit Sethi that focuses on automating savings, investing, and debt payoff. The book's central premise is that traditional budgeting fails because it relies on willpower, which is unreliable. Instead, Sethi prescribes setting up systems—automatic transfers, investments, and payments—that handle financial decisions without daily effort.

## Core Framework
- **Conscious Spending Plan**: Four-bucket allocation (fixed costs 50-60%, investments 10-20%, savings 5-10%, guilt-free spending 20-35%) replacing traditional budgeting.
- **Automation**: Automatic transfers, bill payments, and investment contributions as the highest-leverage action.
- **Six-Week Program**: Credit cards → bank accounts → retirement accounts → conscious spending → debt payoff → investing.

## Key Ideas
- Employer 401(k) match is "free money" and should be claimed first.
- Index funds (VTSAX, VFIAX, VBTLX) outperform 80-90% of active funds.
- The **Freedom Number** is 25x annual expenses (the 4% rule).
- **Debt avalanche** (highest rate first) saves the most money; **debt snowball** (smallest balance first) builds psychological momentum.
- **Rule of 110**: 110 minus your age = percentage in stocks.
- Big wins (refinancing, negotiating, automating) matter more than small daily frugality.
