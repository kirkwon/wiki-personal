# ちらし

'Chirashi' refers to the Japanese art of scattering sushi rice over vinegared rice with various ingredients on top, creating a visually appealing dish that balances flavors and textures. In photography, it denotes an unconventional composition technique where elements are randomly placed for an abstract effect. Within personal finance, 'chirashi' symbolizes the scattering of resources or investments to diversify risk. In cognitive science, it can metaphorically describe how information is distributed across neural networks during processing.
