# Reward Shaping

Reward shaping is the process of modifying the reward function to guide an agent's learning behavior towards desired outcomes by providing additional incentives or penalties. In cooking, it could mean adjusting recipe steps for better taste; in photography, tweaking camera settings for optimal exposure; in personal finance, structuring savings plans with rewards; and in cognitive science, designing experiments to shape behavioral responses.
