# default-options

Default options refer to pre-set choices or settings that are automatically applied when creating recipes, organizing photographic workflows, managing personal finances, or conducting cognitive science experiments unless explicitly overridden by the user's preferences. In cooking, this might mean standard measurements; in photography, default camera settings; in personal finance, baseline investment allocations; and in cognitive science, initial experimental conditions.
