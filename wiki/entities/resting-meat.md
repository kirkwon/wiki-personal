# Resting Meat

Resting meat refers to the practice of allowing cooked meat to sit undisturbed at room temperature after being removed from heat sources. This period allows for the redistribution of juices within the meat, resulting in a more tender and flavorful dish upon serving. In photography, capturing resting meat can showcase its natural state before plating, while in cognitive science, it may relate to understanding patience and anticipation in personal experiences.
