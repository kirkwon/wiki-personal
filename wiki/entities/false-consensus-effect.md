# False consensus effect

The False Consensus Effect is the cognitive bias where individuals overestimate how much others share their beliefs, attitudes, or behaviors. In cooking, this might lead to assuming everyone prefers a certain cuisine; in photography, it could result in presuming widespread agreement on composition styles; in personal finance, one may erroneously believe that most people manage money similarly; and within cognitive science, researchers might incorrectly assume uniformity of thought processes across populations.
