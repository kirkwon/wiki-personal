# Resource: Salt Fat Acid Heat

*Salt, Fat, Acid, Heat* is a foundational cooking reference by Samin Nosrat that breaks down the four elements of good cooking. The book teaches cooks to Season (salt), Breathe (fat), Brighten (acid), and Transform (heat) as interconnected principles for building flavor in any dish.

Key takeaways:
- **Salt** — Enhances all other flavors and is the most critical seasoning
- **Fat** — Carries flavor, adds richness, and creates texture
- **Acid** — Balances richness and brightness, preventing dishes from feeling flat
- **Heat** — Drives chemical reactions (like [[maillard-reaction]]) that create new flavors

This resource connects to [[flavor-theory]] and [[ingredient-pairings]] as a practical framework for understanding how flavors are built.
