# Getting Things Done

"Getting Things Done (GTD) is a productivity methodology that emphasizes the importance of capturing all tasks externally to free up mental space for creative thinking and problem-solving. It involves categorizing tasks by context, prioritizing them based on urgency and importance, and committing to actionable steps in order to efficiently manage personal projects across various domains such as cooking recipes, photography assignments, financial planning, and cognitive research."
