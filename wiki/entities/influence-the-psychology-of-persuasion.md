# Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion

"Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion" is a landmark book by Robert B. Cialdini that identifies six universal principles of persuasion: reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social proof, authority, liking, and scarcity. These principles describe the psychological triggers that lead people to say "yes" to requests and influence their decisions and behaviors.

The book is a key source for the [[influence-mental-model]], providing the external perspective on how social dynamics and environmental cues shape decision-making.
