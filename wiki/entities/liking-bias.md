# Liking bias

Liking bias is the tendency to favor or prefer things that we like personally, which can skew our objectivity in areas such as cooking (favoring recipes similar to our own), photography (preferring styles we enjoy), personal finance (choosing investments aligned with our interests), and cognitive science (having a bias towards theories or research that resonate with us).
