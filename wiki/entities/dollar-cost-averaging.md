# Dollar-Cost Averaging

Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) is an investment strategy where an individual invests a fixed amount of money into a particular asset at regular intervals, regardless of the asset's price. This approach can potentially reduce the average cost per share over time and mitigate the impact of volatility in the market. In personal finance terms, it's like buying ingredients for cooking or photography equipment in small batches to spread out expenses while maintaining a consistent investment level.


In cognitive science, DCA can be likened to habit formation where regular, scheduled actions lead to more stable and predictable behavior patterns over time.
