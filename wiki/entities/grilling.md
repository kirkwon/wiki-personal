# Grilling

Grilling refers to the cooking method where food is prepared by exposing it directly to heat sources such as charcoal or gas flames on a grate. This technique imparts distinctive flavors through caramelization and smoke infusion while also creating visually appealing patterns, which can be captured in photography. From a cognitive science perspective, the sensory experience of grilling—tactile, olfactory, and visual stimuli—can influence taste perceptions and memory encoding related to food enjoyment.


Financially, investing in quality grilling equipment may represent an initial expense but can lead to long-term savings by reducing the need for professional catering services or dining out frequently.
