# emotions-and-intuition-in-decisions

Emotions-and-Intuition-in-Decisions refers to the role of affective responses and subconscious cues in shaping decision-making processes across various domains such as cooking (taste preferences), photography (creative instincts), personal finance (risk assessment), and cognitive science (heuristic judgments). This concept underscores the interplay between rational analysis and emotional intelligence, where intuition often guides choices in complex or ambiguous situations.
