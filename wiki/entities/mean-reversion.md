# Mean Reversion

Mean reversion is the financial principle that asset prices and returns eventually return to their historical average or trend over time. In cooking, it refers to a dish's flavor profile returning to its original taste after adjustments are made. In photography, mean reversion can describe an image's exposure level reverting back to the standard setting for optimal results. Within cognitive science, this concept is used metaphorically to explain how memory recall tends to revert to a person's baseline knowledge or beliefs when confronted with new information.
