# false-positive-detection

False-positive detection refers to the identification of an incorrect result or error within various contexts such as cooking (misidentifying ingredients), photography (incorrectly flagging a photo's quality), personal finance (erroneously categorizing transactions), and cognitive science (falsely recognizing patterns in data). It is crucial to minimize false positives to ensure accuracy and reliability in decision-making processes.
