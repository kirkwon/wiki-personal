# adrift - Scott Galloway

Adrift refers to the state of being without direction or purpose, which Scott Galloway applies metaphorically in various contexts such as cooking (lacking a recipe), photography (taking aimless shots), personal finance (managing money with no clear strategy), and cognitive science (studying thought patterns without specific goals).
