# Deep Learning

Deep learning is an advanced subset of machine learning inspired by the neural networks found in human brains. It involves training algorithms to recognize patterns and make decisions with minimal human intervention, which can be applied to cooking recipes optimization, enhancing photographic image processing, automating personal finance analysis, and understanding cognitive processes through data-driven models.
