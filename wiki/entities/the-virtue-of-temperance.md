# the-virtue-of-temperance

The virtue of temperance is the ethical principle that encourages moderation and self-control across various activities. In cooking, it promotes balanced dietary choices; in photography, it guides restraint to capture impactful images without overindulgence; within personal finance, it advises prudent spending habits; and in cognitive science, temperance is linked to the regulation of impulses for better decision-making.
