# Groupthink

Groupthink is the psychological phenomenon where the desire for group harmony or conformity results in an irrational decision-making outcome. In contexts like cooking, it may lead to a reluctance to question traditional recipes; in photography, it could suppress individual creativity; within personal finance, it might cause adherence to popular but unwise investment trends; and in cognitive science, groupthink can skew research findings due to peer pressure.
