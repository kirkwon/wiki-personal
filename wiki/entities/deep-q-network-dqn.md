# Deep Q-Network (DQN)

A Deep Q-Network (DQN) is an advanced reinforcement learning algorithm that uses deep neural networks to approximate the optimal action-value function in complex decision-making scenarios. In personal finance, it could be applied for automated investment strategies; similarly, in cooking or photography, DQNs might optimize recipe selection or camera settings dynamically based on desired outcomes.


In cognitive science research, a DQN can model how humans learn from sequential decisions and rewards, potentially offering insights into the neural mechanisms of learning and decision-making processes.
