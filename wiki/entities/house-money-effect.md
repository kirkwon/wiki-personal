# House money effect

The House Money Effect refers to the psychological phenomenon where individuals are more likely to take risks with money they perceive as a windfall or 'house' money, rather than their own hard-earned income. In personal finance, this can lead to increased spending and riskier investments when one feels wealthy due to unexpected gains.


In the context of cooking and photography, an individual might splurge on expensive ingredients or equipment under the belief that their recent success justifies the expense, despite not having initially planned for such costs. This effect is rooted in cognitive biases where past wins influence future financial decisions.
