# Risk Sensitivity

Risk sensitivity refers to an individual's inclination towards considering potential negative outcomes when making decisions across various domains such as cooking (e.g., choosing ingredients), photography (e.g., exposure settings), personal finance (e.g., investment choices), and cognitive science (e.g., experimental design). It reflects the degree to which one weighs risks against rewards before taking action, influencing both strategy and creativity in these fields.
