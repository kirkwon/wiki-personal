# Hindsight bias

Hindsight bias is the cognitive distortion where one believes an event was predictable or inevitable after it has occurred, often leading to overestimating one's own foresight. In cooking, this might manifest as thinking a recipe will always turn out perfectly; in photography, assuming every shot can be salvaged; in personal finance, believing past investment decisions were clear-cut winners; and within cognitive science, it reflects the challenge of studying subjective perceptions post-event.
