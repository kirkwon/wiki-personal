# Out of Control - Kevin Kelly

"Out of Control," as described by Kevin Kelly, refers to systems or processes that have deviated from their intended behavior due to unforeseen variables or complex interactions within the system. In cooking, this could mean a recipe not turning out as expected; in photography, it might be an unexpected lighting issue; for personal finance, it could signify erratic market fluctuations affecting investments; and in cognitive science, it may relate to unpredictable thought patterns or decision-making processes.
