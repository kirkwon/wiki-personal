# second-hand-first

The 'second-hand-first' approach refers to prioritizing learning from existing resources or experiences before attempting new tasks in various domains. In cooking, it means using tried recipes as a foundation; in photography, studying classic shots first; for personal finance, understanding established investment strategies; and within cognitive science, building on foundational theories to inform novel research.
