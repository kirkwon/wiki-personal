# trust-in-communication

Trust-in-communication refers to the confidence one places in exchanging information with others, particularly regarding sensitive topics like cooking recipes, photographic techniques, financial advice, or cognitive theories. It encompasses beliefs about honesty, accuracy, and reliability of shared knowledge, which is essential for effective collaboration and learning within these fields.
