# Symbolic AI

Symbolic AI refers to artificial intelligence systems that manipulate symbols to perform tasks by following predefined rules or logic, similar to how recipes guide cooking processes or camera settings dictate photography outcomes. In personal finance, it could analyze numerical data and make decisions based on financial models, while in cognitive science, it mimics human reasoning patterns for understanding complex concepts.
