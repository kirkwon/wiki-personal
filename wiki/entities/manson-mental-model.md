# Manson (Mental Model)

The Manson model refers to the conceptual framework of mental models used to understand complex systems by simplifying them into more manageable representations. In cooking, it helps in predicting ingredient interactions; in photography, it aids in anticipating light and composition effects; for personal finance, it assists in forecasting market trends; and within cognitive science, it explains how individuals form beliefs about the world based on limited information.
