# top-down-control

Top-down control refers to the influence of higher cognitive processes or overarching goals on more immediate actions in various domains. In cooking, it's about how recipe planning shapes ingredient selection; in photography, it involves composition decisions guiding shot framing; within personal finance, strategic financial objectives dictate spending habits; and in cognitive science, top-down control describes the brain's use of prior knowledge to interpret sensory information.
