# jazz-scales

Jazz scales are melodic frameworks used predominantly in jazz music that consist of specific sequences of notes with characteristic intervals, often including chromatic or whole-tone steps to create a distinctive sound. In the context of cooking, they can be likened to unique recipe ingredients; for photography, they resemble creative compositions; in personal finance, they represent tailored investment strategies; and within cognitive science, they symbolize individualized learning patterns or thought processes.
