# Ethos Pathos Logos

- Ethos refers to establishing credibility or ethical appeal by demonstrating expertise in cooking techniques, photographic composition principles, financial acumen, or cognitive theories.

- Pathos involves evoking emotions through the presentation of a well-cooked meal, captivating images, sensible budgeting advice, or relatable cognitive experiences.

- Logos is about using logical arguments and clear reasoning in recipes, photo editing rationale, financial planning strategies, or scientific explanations to persuade an audience effectively.
