# ethics-and-decision-making

Ethics-and-decision-making refers to the process of evaluating moral principles and values when making choices in various contexts. In cooking, it involves selecting ingredients with consideration for health and sustainability; in photography, respecting subjects' privacy and authenticity; in personal finance, balancing self-interest against societal impact; and in cognitive science, ensuring research integrity while interpreting data ethically.
