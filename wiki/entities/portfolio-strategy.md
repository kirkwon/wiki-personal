# portfolio-strategy

Portfolio strategy refers to the deliberate selection and management of diverse assets or projects across different domains such as cooking recipes, photographic techniques, investment portfolios, and cognitive exercises aimed at enhancing mental agility. The goal is to optimize returns (in terms of personal growth, financial gain, or culinary success) while balancing risk exposure in each area.
