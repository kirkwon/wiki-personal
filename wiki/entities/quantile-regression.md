# Quantile Regression

Quantile regression is an econometric technique that estimates the conditional median or other quantiles of the response variable, providing insights into the distributional effects across different points in the data spectrum. In personal finance, it helps understand how investment returns vary at different levels of risk; similarly, in cooking and photography, it can analyze how ingredient proportions affect taste outcomes or image quality at various intensities. Cognitive science may employ quantile regression to study decision-making processes under uncertainty across diverse scenarios.
