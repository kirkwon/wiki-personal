# Probabilistic reasoning

Probabilistic reasoning is the process of making inferences or decisions based on uncertain information by applying mathematical principles to estimate likelihoods. In cooking, it helps predict recipe outcomes; in photography, it aids in assessing exposure risks; in personal finance, it guides investment choices under market volatility; and in cognitive science, it models how humans intuitively handle uncertain scenarios.
