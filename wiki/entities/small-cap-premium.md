# Small Cap Premium

The Small Cap Premium is the additional cost investors are willing to pay over the market average price per share for small-cap stocks due to their perceived higher risk and potential for growth. In personal finance terms, it's akin to valuing rare ingredients or unique photography equipment that may offer greater returns on investment despite higher initial costs. From a cognitive science perspective, this premium reflects the human tendency to overvalue uncommon assets based on their scarcity and potential for high reward.
