# Steaming

Steaming is a gentle cooking technique that uses vapor from boiling water to cook ingredients. It preserves moisture, nutrients, and delicate flavors, making it ideal for vegetables, fish, and dumplings.

## Key Facts
- Uses indirect heat from steam rather than direct contact with water or oil
- Retains more nutrients compared to boiling
- Common in [[cantonese-cuisine]] and [[yum-cha]] traditions
- Essential for preparing dim sum items like dumplings and buns

## Relationship to Other Techniques
Steaming contrasts with [[searing]] and [[wok-hei]] in that it uses low, moist heat rather than high dry heat. It is a cornerstone of [[cantonese-cuisine]] cooking philosophy, which emphasizes freshness and gentle preparation.
