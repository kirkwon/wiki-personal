# Alcatraz Island

Alcatraz Island is a former federal prison located on an island in San Francisco Bay, California. Known for its high-security measures and notorious inmates, it has become a popular tourist destination with historical significance. Its stark landscapes offer unique photographic opportunities that contrast the harshness of its past with natural beauty.
