# The Algebra of Happiness

"The Algebra of Happiness" refers to the mathematical model that quantifies happiness through variables such as cooking experiences (quality meals), photographic memories (capturing joyful moments), personal finance stability (savings and expenses balance), and cognitive well-being (mental health status). It aims to provide a structured approach to understanding how these factors interrelate and contribute to overall life satisfaction.
