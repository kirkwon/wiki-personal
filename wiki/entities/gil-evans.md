# Gil Evans

Gil Evans was an American jazz composer, arranger, and pianist known for his innovative orchestral arrangements that blended classical music techniques with jazz elements. His work has significantly influenced the genre of big band jazz, particularly through collaborations with Miles Davis on albums like "Birth of the Cool."
