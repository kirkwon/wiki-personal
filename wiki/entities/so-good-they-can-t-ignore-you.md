# So Good They Can't Ignore You

"The phrase 'So Good They Can't Ignore You' encapsulates the idea that exceptional skills or work in any field—be it cooking, photography, personal finance, or cognitive science—attracts significant attention and recognition due to their superior quality. It implies a self-reinforcing cycle where high visibility leads to more opportunities for growth and improvement. In essence, excellence becomes its own best advertisement."
