# First Things First - Stephen R Covey

"First Things First," coined by Stephen R. Covey, emphasizes prioritizing tasks based on their urgency and importance to achieve long-term goals effectively. In cooking, this translates to planning meals around nutritional value; in photography, it means capturing key moments that define a story; for personal finance, focusing on high-impact investments first; and within cognitive science, prioritizing foundational research over less critical studies.
