# Meta‑Reinforcement Learning

Meta-reinforcement learning (meta-RL) is an advanced machine learning approach that enables algorithms to quickly adapt to new tasks by leveraging past experiences. In the context of cooking, it could optimize recipe adjustments for different dietary needs; in photography, it might automate camera settings for diverse lighting conditions; within personal finance, it can tailor investment strategies to market changes; and in cognitive science, it simulates how humans learn from varied experiences.
