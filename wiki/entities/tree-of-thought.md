# Tree‑of‑Thought

A Tree-of-Thought is a structured framework representing the logical progression of ideas or decision paths in various domains such as cooking recipes, photographic composition techniques, financial planning strategies, and cognitive processes studied in science. It visually maps out interconnected concepts and steps, facilitating clearer understanding and problem-solving by highlighting relationships and dependencies among different elements within each field.
