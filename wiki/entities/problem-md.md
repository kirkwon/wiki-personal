Problem-MD is an integrated knowledge base that catalogs challenges encountered across various domains such as cooking (recipe optimization), photography (image processing techniques), personal finance (budgeting strategies), and cognitive science (memory enhancement methods). It serves as a reference tool for identifying, analyzing, and proposing solutions to specific problems within these areas.
