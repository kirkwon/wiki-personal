# GAN Applications

Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) are AI frameworks used to generate new data samples that mimic the distribution of a given dataset. In cooking, they can create recipes; in photography, GANs enhance image quality and produce realistic images; for personal finance, they predict market trends; and in cognitive science, they model complex brain functions.
