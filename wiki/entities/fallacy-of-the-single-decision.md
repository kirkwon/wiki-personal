# Fallacy of the single decision

The Fallacy of the Single Decision refers to the erroneous belief that one choice or action will determine all subsequent outcomes in a complex process, such as cooking a recipe perfectly with a single ingredient change, capturing an image flawlessly by altering only one camera setting, achieving financial success through a singular investment decision, or solving cognitive problems solely based on one thought pattern. This oversimplification ignores the multifaceted nature of these activities and outcomes.
