# Kalman Filter

A Kalman Filter is an algorithm that uses mathematical models to estimate the state of a dynamic system over time, accounting for noise in measurements. In cooking, it could predict ingredient depletion; in photography, adjust exposure based on light changes; in personal finance, forecast cash flow; and in cognitive science, model decision-making processes under uncertainty.
