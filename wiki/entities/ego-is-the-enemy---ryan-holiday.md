# Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday

Ego Is the Enemy is a book by Ryan Holiday that explores how ego undermines success, decision-making, and personal growth. The book argues that ego — the belief in one's own importance — is the primary obstacle to achieving one's goals and living well.

## Key Themes
- Ego leads to poor decisions and failure
- Humility is the antidote to ego-driven thinking
- Self-awareness and self-discipline are essential counterweights
- The three stages of ego: aspiration, success, and failure

## Connection to Humility

The book serves as the primary source for the Humility mental model in this wiki, positioning humility as a universal tool for navigating complexity through pattern and relationship recognition.
