# Clustering illusion

The clustering illusion is the cognitive bias where individuals perceive patterns or clusters in random data when there are none. In cooking, this might lead to overestimating ingredient compatibility; in photography, it could result in misinterpreting image composition; in personal finance, one may falsely identify trends in investment returns; and within cognitive science, researchers study how the brain erroneously infers structure from randomness.
