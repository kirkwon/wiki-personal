# self-theories---carol-s-dweck

Self-Theories (Carol Dweck) refer to the beliefs individuals hold about their own abilities as fixed or malleable traits that can be developed through effort. In contexts like cooking and photography, these theories influence one's approach to learning new skills; a growth mindset fosters resilience in overcoming challenges, while a fixed mindset may lead to avoidance of difficult tasks. Understanding self-theories is crucial for personal finance management as it affects financial decision-making and risk tolerance, aligning with cognitive science principles that explore the impact of belief systems on behavior and learning outcomes.
