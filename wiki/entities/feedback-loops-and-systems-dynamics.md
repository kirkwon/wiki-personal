# feedback-loops-and-systems-dynamics

Feedback loops are mechanisms within systems that can amplify or dampen effects, influencing the system's behavior over time. In cooking, this might relate to ingredient ratios affecting taste; in photography, it pertains to exposure settings impacting image brightness; in personal finance, investment returns influence future savings strategies; and in cognitive science, neural feedback shapes learning processes. Systems dynamics is the study of how these loops interact within a system to shape its overall behavior and evolution.
