# Coincidence

A coincidence is an event that occurs by chance without apparent causal connection to other events. In cooking, it might be stumbling upon a unique flavor combination; in photography, capturing the perfect moment unexpectedly; within personal finance, encountering similar investment opportunities at different times; and in cognitive science, observing unrelated thoughts converging simultaneously.
