# gall-s-law

The Gall-Pearson law (often referred to as 'gall's law') is an empirical relationship in photography that states the reciprocal of exposure time multiplied by the square root of aperture area equals a constant, which helps determine correct exposure. In cognitive science, it refers to a heuristic suggesting people tend to overestimate their abilities or performance under pressure. While not directly related to cooking and personal finance, understanding this law can be metaphorically applied to the precision needed in recipe measurements (cooking) and investment strategies (personal finance).
