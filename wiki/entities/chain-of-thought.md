# Chain‑of‑Thought

A Chain-of-Thought is the sequential process of logical reasoning or decision making where each step builds upon the previous one to reach a conclusion or solve a problem. In cooking, it's the progression from selecting ingredients to finalizing presentation; in photography, it involves framing and adjusting settings for optimal image capture; in personal finance, it entails budget planning leading to investment choices; while in cognitive science, it refers to understanding how thought processes unfold.
