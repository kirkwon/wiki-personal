# Building a Second Brain - Tiago Forte

Tiago Forte's *Building a Second Brain* is a methodology and framework for capturing, organizing, and leveraging digital information to augment one's thinking and knowledge work. The framework centers on the PARA method (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives) for organizing personal knowledge. The book emphasizes capturing information, organizing it systematically, and deploying knowledge for creative output.

## Key Concepts

- **PARA Method** — A system for organizing information into Projects (active efforts with deadlines), Areas (ongoing responsibilities), Resources (topics of interest), and Archives (inactive items)
- **Progressive Summarization** — A technique for distilling and building on captured notes over time
- **Creative Knowledge Work** — The practice of using a curated knowledge base to generate original output
- **Personal Knowledge Management as a Tool** — Treating knowledge management systems as cognitive tools for navigating information complexity

Forte's framework is a direct application of the Tools mental model—treating knowledge management systems as cognitive tools for navigating information complexity.
