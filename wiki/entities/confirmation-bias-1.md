# confirmation-bias

Confirmation bias is the tendency to search for, interpret, favor, and recall information that confirms pre-existing beliefs or hypotheses in areas like cooking (favoring recipes we believe work best), photography (selective editing of photos), personal finance (choosing investments aligning with our financial views), and cognitive science (interpreting research to support one's theories). It can lead to overlooking contradictory evidence, thus skewing decision-making processes.
