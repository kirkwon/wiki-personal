# Haight-Ashbury

Haight-Ashbury refers to the neighborhood in San Francisco known as the epicenter of the 1960s counterculture movement, including the Summer of Love. It's historically significant for its influence on music, art, and social change, which may inspire creative expression in cooking, photography, and cognitive exploration. However, it holds little direct relevance to personal finance or culinary techniques.
