# inflation-protection

Inflation-protection refers to financial strategies or instruments designed to safeguard the purchasing power of money against the eroding effects of inflation over time. In personal finance, this can include investing in assets like stocks, real estate, or Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), which adjust their payout based on inflation rates to maintain value. Understanding these concepts is crucial for long-term financial planning and ensuring that savings retain their intended worth despite rising prices.
