# Capital Gains

Capital gains refer to the profit from the sale of an asset, such as stocks or real estate, where the selling price exceeds the purchase price. In personal finance, this concept is crucial for understanding investment growth and tax implications. For photographers or chefs who sell their work or equipment, capital gains can affect net income from these endeavors.


Capital gains are essentially the increase in value of an asset over time that's realized when it's sold at a higher price than its original purchase cost. This concept is relevant to personal finance as it impacts one’s investment portfolio and tax liabilities, while for photographers or chefs selling their creations or tools, capital gains represent the financial benefit from these sales.
