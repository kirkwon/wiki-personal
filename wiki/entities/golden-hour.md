# Golden Hour

The Golden Hour refers to the period shortly after sunrise or before sunset when daylight is redder and softer than at other times of the day. In cooking, it's a metaphor for the optimal time frame for preparing dishes that require gentle heat. In photography, it denotes the ideal lighting conditions to capture high-quality images with warm tones. While not directly related, in personal finance, one might use this term to describe an opportune moment to make significant investments or financial decisions due to favorable market conditions.


In cognitive science, 'Golden Hour' could be a colloquial reference to the critical window following traumatic events when interventions are most effective in mitigating long-term psychological impacts.
