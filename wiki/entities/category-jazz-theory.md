# Category:Jazz Theory

A Jazz Theory category encompasses the principles and practices of improvisation, chord progressions, scales, and rhythmic patterns unique to jazz music composition and performance. It involves understanding harmonic structures like ii-V-I progressions and incorporating elements such as syncopation and swing timing into creative expression. This knowledge is essential for musicians aiming to master the art of jazz improvisation and arrangement.
