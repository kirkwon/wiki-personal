# Model‑Based RL

Model-based Reinforcement Learning (RL) is an approach where the agent builds an internal model of the environment's dynamics to predict future states and rewards from current actions. This allows for planning ahead by simulating outcomes before taking real decisions, akin to testing recipes in cooking or adjusting camera settings before capturing photos. In personal finance, it could optimize investment strategies by forecasting market trends, while cognitive science applications might involve understanding decision-making processes through simulated scenarios.
