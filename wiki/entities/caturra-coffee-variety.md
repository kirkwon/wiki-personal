# Caturra Coffee Variety

Caturra is a mutation of the Bourbon Arabica variety, known for being bright, acidic, and highly productive. It is widely used in commercial specialty coffee.

## Key Characteristics
- **Origin**: Bourbon mutation
- **Character**: Bright, acidic, productive
- **Growth**: Compact, high yield
- **Use**: Commercial specialty coffee

Caturra is favored by producers seeking both quality and volume, making it one of the most common Arabica cultivars worldwide.
