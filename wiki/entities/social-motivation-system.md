# Social motivation system

The social motivation system refers to the psychological framework that drives individuals to engage with others through activities like cooking or photography for social approval, connection, and shared experiences. In personal finance, it influences decisions made in communal contexts, such as group investments or financial planning collaborations. Within cognitive science, this system is studied to understand how social interactions affect motivation and behavior patterns across various domains.
