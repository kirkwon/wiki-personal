# Group polarization

Group polarization is the phenomenon where group discussions lead to more extreme positions than initially held by individual members. In contexts like cooking or photography workshops, this can result in bolder flavor combinations or daring compositions. Within personal finance groups and cognitive science circles, it might manifest as riskier investment strategies or stronger convictions about theories.
