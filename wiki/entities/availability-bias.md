# Availability bias

Availability bias is the cognitive tendency to overestimate the likelihood of events based on their recent occurrence or memorable impact, which can influence cooking choices (e.g., favoring familiar recipes), photography composition (e.g., focusing on common subjects), financial decisions (e.g., investing in trending stocks), and understanding cognitive processes (e.g., misjudging probabilities).
