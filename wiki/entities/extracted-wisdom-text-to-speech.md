# extracted-wisdom-text-to-speech

Extracted Wisdom Text to Speech (EWTTS) is an automated system that converts curated knowledge from various domains such as cooking recipes, photographic techniques, personal finance tips, and cognitive science principles into audible speech for easy consumption and learning. It serves as a convenient tool for individuals seeking quick access to expert advice without the need for manual reading or searching.
