# Health Savings Account Strategy

A Health Savings Account (HSA) Strategy is a financial planning approach that involves contributing pre-tax dollars to an HSA for medical expenses, offering tax advantages while promoting cost-effective healthcare management. It aligns with cognitive science principles by encouraging proactive decision-making and long-term investment in one's wellbeing. In the context of personal finance, it complements other savings methods like retirement funds or emergency reserves.
