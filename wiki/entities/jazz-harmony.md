# Jazz Harmony

Jazz harmony refers to the complex chord progressions and improvisations that form the backbone of jazz music, characterized by extended chords, altered dominants, and modal interchange. It's akin to blending diverse ingredients in cooking for unique flavors or balancing light and shadow in photography; it requires an understanding of underlying structures while allowing creative expression. In cognitive science, it parallels the brain's ability to process multiple stimuli simultaneously, leading to innovative problem-solving approaches.


In personal finance, jazz harmony can be likened to diversifying investments; just as a variety of chords create rich musical textures, a mix of asset classes can lead to more resilient financial portfolios.
