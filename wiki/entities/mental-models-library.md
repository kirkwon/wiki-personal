# mental-models-library

A mental-models library is an organized collection of conceptual frameworks that represent how individuals perceive and interpret various domains such as cooking techniques, photographic composition rules, personal financial strategies, and cognitive processes in learning and decision making. It serves as a reference tool to understand underlying principles and improve expertise across these diverse fields by providing structured knowledge.
