# Salience effect

The salience effect refers to the phenomenon where certain stimuli stand out more prominently in our perception due to their distinctiveness or emotional relevance. In cooking, this can mean a unique ingredient catches your eye; in photography, it's an element that draws attention; in personal finance, salient information might be highlighted on statements; and within cognitive science, it relates to how our brains prioritize important or novel data.
