# lifestyle-inflation

Lifestyle inflation refers to the increase in spending that occurs as one's income grows without corresponding adjustments in savings or investment strategies. In personal finance terms, it is often seen when individuals start treating their higher earnings as a permanent state rather than an interim phase, leading to increased expenditures on non-essential items and experiences that may not align with long-term financial goals.
