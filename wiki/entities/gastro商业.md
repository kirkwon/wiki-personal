# gastro商业

'Gastro商业' refers to the commercial aspect of food preparation and presentation, where culinary skills intersect with business acumen. It involves not only creating delectable dishes but also managing costs, marketing, and customer relations in the food industry. This concept is relevant for those interested in leveraging their cooking expertise into a profitable venture while considering personal finance management to ensure sustainability.
