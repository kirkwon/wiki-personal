# Confirmation bias

Confirmation bias is the tendency to search for, interpret, or recall information in a way that confirms one's preexisting beliefs or hypotheses. In cooking, it may lead to favoring recipes that align with personal taste preferences; in photography, selecting images that support an artistic vision; in personal finance, choosing investments based on prior successes rather than objective analysis; and within cognitive science, studying phenomena that validate existing theories.
