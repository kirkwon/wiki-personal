# Cognitive Biases

Cognitive biases are systematic patterns of deviation from norm or rationality in judgment, often resulting in illogical inferences about other people or situations. In cooking, they may lead to overestimating the impact of a single ingredient; in photography, they can cause an individual's preferences to skew their editing choices; within personal finance, biases might result in poor investment decisions due to overconfidence or herd mentality. In cognitive science, understanding these biases is crucial for studying human thought processes and decision-making.
