# Wok Seasoning

Wok seasoning is the process of coating a carbon steel or cast iron wok with oil to create a non-stick surface and prevent rust. This technique enhances cooking performance by improving heat distribution and adding flavor complexity through polymerization during high-heat stir-frying, which can be visually documented in culinary photography. Understanding the seasoning process also offers insights into material properties relevant to cognitive science and personal finance, as it involves cost-effective maintenance of kitchen equipment over time.
