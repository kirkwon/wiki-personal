# cooperation

Cooperation refers to the act of working together towards a common goal or benefit. In cooking, it involves collaborative meal preparation; in photography, it's about jointly capturing images; in personal finance, cooperating could mean pooling resources for investments; and in cognitive science, it pertains to interdisciplinary teamwork to solve complex problems.
