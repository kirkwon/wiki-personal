# Memory (AI)

Memory in AI refers to the system's ability to store, retrieve, and utilize information over time, similar to human memory processes. In cooking, it enables recipe recall; in photography, it aids in tagging images with metadata; for personal finance, it assists in tracking expenses and investments; while in cognitive science, it's studied as an analogy to understand natural intelligence.
