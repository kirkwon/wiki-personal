# MCMC

Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) is a computational technique used to approximate complex probability distributions by simulating random samples from these distributions. In the context of cooking, it can be likened to experimenting with ingredient combinations; in photography, adjusting camera settings for desired outcomes; in personal finance, modeling investment risks and returns; and in cognitive science, understanding decision-making processes under uncertainty.
