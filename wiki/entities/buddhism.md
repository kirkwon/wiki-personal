# Buddhism

Buddhism is a spiritual tradition that emphasizes personal enlightenment through meditation, ethical living, and understanding the nature of reality. It offers insights into mindfulness which can enhance focus in cooking and photography, while its teachings on impermanence align with cognitive science concepts and aid in managing expectations for financial stability.
