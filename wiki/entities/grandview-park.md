# Grandview Park

The namesake hill of the Golden Gate Heights neighborhood, Grandview Park is the highest point in the neighborhood at approximately 500 feet elevation, offering 360-degree panoramic views of Ocean Beach, Golden Gate Park, the city, and the bridges on clear days.

## Features

- **Elevation**: ~500 feet, the highest point in Golden Gate Heights
- **Views**: 360-degree panoramic views including Ocean Beach, Golden Gate Park, San Francisco skyline, and bridges (on clear days)
- **Coastal scrub habitat**: Native plant restoration area on the hillside
- **Hiking**: Short but steep trail from base to summit

## Access

- Stairs from 14th Avenue and Moraga Street
- Street parking nearby
- Walkable from Golden Gate Park (15-20 minutes)
- Short but steep hike to the summit

## Best Times to Visit

- Clear days (fall season best, September-October)
- Sunset when visible
- Winter storm watching

## Nearby

- Connected to the 16th Avenue Tiled Steps via Moraga Street
- Hilltop Park: Small neighborhood park with Ocean Beach and Golden Gate Park views, additional views and benches
- 14th Ave Steps: Access to Grandview Park, steep
- Moraga St Steps: Residential stairway with garden views
- Lincoln Park and the Legion of Honor accessible from the area
