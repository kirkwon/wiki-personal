# Indian Green Tea

Indian green tea comes from regions like Darjeeling and Assam, with minimal processing and light oxidation. It has a malty, slightly floral, and light flavor profile.

## Key Facts
- **Origin**: Darjeeling, Assam
- **Processing**: Light oxidation, minimal processing
- **Flavor**: Malty, slightly floral, light
- **Appearance**: Dark green, tippy
- **Best Brew**: 175-185°F (79-85°C), 2-3 minutes
