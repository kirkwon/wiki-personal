# algorithmic-trading

Algorithmic trading is the use of computer programs to execute trades at high speeds based on predefined criteria, much like following a recipe in cooking or using automated settings for photography. It involves complex algorithms that analyze market data and make decisions without human intervention, similar to cognitive processes but with an emphasis on efficiency and speed over personal judgment. In personal finance, it's a tool for managing investments by leveraging technology to optimize trade execution and minimize costs.
