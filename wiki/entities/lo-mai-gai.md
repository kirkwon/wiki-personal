# Lo Mai Gai (糯米鸡)

Lo Mai Gai (糯米鸡, Nuòmǐ jī) — Sticky Rice in Lotus Leaves — are aromatic packets of glutinous rice steamed in lotus leaves.

## Components
- Glutinous rice
- Chicken, Chinese sausage, shiitake
- Wrapped in lotus leaf
- Steamed until aromatic

## Hosting Tip
Freeze before steaming. Rest dough 30 minutes for better elasticity.
