# care-mental-model

A care-mental model refers to an individual's cognitive framework that integrates empathy with mental processes when engaging in activities like cooking or photography. It involves understanding the emotional impact of actions and decisions on oneself and others, which is crucial for personal finance management as it affects spending habits and investment choices. In cognitive science, this model aids in studying how empathy influences human behavior and decision-making processes.
