# Constrained MDPs

A Constrained Markov Decision Process (MDP) is an extension of the standard MDP framework that incorporates additional limitations or restrictions on state transitions and actions, reflecting real-world scenarios where choices are not entirely free but subject to certain constraints. In cooking, this could represent ingredient availability; in photography, camera settings limits; for personal finance, budgetary constraints; and in cognitive science, neurobiological limitations on decision-making processes.
