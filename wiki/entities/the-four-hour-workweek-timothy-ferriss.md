# the-four-hour-workweek---timothy-ferriss

The Four Hour Workweek by Tim Ferriss is an influential book that advocates for extreme productivity techniques to free up time for personal passions like cooking, photography, and financial independence. It introduces the concept of "minimum viable work" in various life domains, including cognitive science principles applied to optimize efficiency and leisure.
