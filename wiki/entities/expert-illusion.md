# expert-illusion

Expert-illusion refers to the phenomenon where individuals perceive themselves as more knowledgeable or skilled in an area than they actually are, often due to limited experience or exposure. In cooking, this might manifest as someone confidently experimenting with complex recipes without foundational culinary skills; in photography, it's assuming one can capture professional-quality images without understanding technical aspects; in personal finance, believing one can manage investments effectively without formal education; and within cognitive science, overestimating one's grasp of psychological theories.
