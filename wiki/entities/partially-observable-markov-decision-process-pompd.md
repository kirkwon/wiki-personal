# Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMPD)

A Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP) is a framework used to model decision-making in environments where the agent cannot fully observe the state of the system. In contexts like cooking or photography, it helps plan actions when outcomes are uncertain due to hidden variables; for personal finance, POMDP can guide investment strategies amid market volatility; and in cognitive science, it models how humans make decisions with incomplete information about their surroundings.
