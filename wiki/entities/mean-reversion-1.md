# mean-reversion

Mean reversion is the financial theory suggesting that asset prices and returns eventually move back towards their historical average over time. In cooking, it refers to a dish's flavor profile returning to its original taste after adjustments are made. Photographically, mean reversion implies an image's exposure compensating for previous underexposure or overexposure. Cognitively, it describes how human memory tends to recall information closer to the average of related memories rather than extremes.
