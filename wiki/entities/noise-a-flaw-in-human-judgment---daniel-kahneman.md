# Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment

A 2024 book by Daniel Kahneman (with Olivier Sibony and Cass R. Sunstein) that argues judgment noise — unwanted variability in human decisions — is a pervasive, costly problem distinct from bias.

## Core Thesis

Human judgment is far more variable than people realize. When different experts evaluate the same case, they often reach very different conclusions. This noise is costly in medicine, finance, criminal justice, and other domains, and it can be measured and reduced.

## Key Distinctions

- Noise (random variability) vs. Bias (systematic error)
- Decision quality vs. Outcome quality
- Individual judgment vs. Structured processes

## Domains Covered

- Medicine: diagnostic and treatment inconsistency
- Finance: investor judgment variability
- Criminal Justice: sentencing disparities

## Reduction Strategies

- Structured decision processes
- Calibration exercises
- Collaborative organizational culture
- Decision audits
