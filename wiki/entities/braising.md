# Braising

Braising is a cooking technique that involves searing meat at high heat and then slowly simmering it in a covered pot with a small amount of liquid to tenderize the tough fibers. This method enhances flavors through caramelization while ensuring moisture retention, making it ideal for complex dishes where depth of taste is desired.
