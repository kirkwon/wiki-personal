# speed

In cooking, speed refers to the efficiency of preparation and execution of recipes, impacting overall kitchen workflow. In photography, it denotes the rate at which a camera captures images or adjusts settings for optimal results. Within personal finance, speed signifies the pace of investment growth or debt repayment. Cognitive science interprets speed as the time taken to process information and make decisions.
