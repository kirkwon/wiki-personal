# Zero to One - Peter Thiel & Blake Masters

*Zero to One: Notes on Startups, or How to Build the Future* by Peter Thiel and Blake Masters argues that truly valuable startups must create new industries rather than compete within existing ones.^[raw/articles/Industries.md]

## Key Ideas

- Competition is for losers; the most successful ventures do something nobody else is doing
- Creating new industries (going from zero to one) is more valuable than incremental improvement within established markets
- Monopoly, not competition, drives lasting value creation

## Connection to Industries Mental Model

Thiel's framework contributes to [[industries]] by reframing "industry" as something that can be invented rather than merely analyzed. This adds an entrepreneurial dimension to the mental model—industries are not just structures to observe but opportunities to create.^[raw/articles/Industries.md]
