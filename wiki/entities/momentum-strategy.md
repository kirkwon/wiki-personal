# Momentum Strategy

A Momentum Strategy involves capitalizing on trends by investing in assets that are gaining popularity or value rapidly. In cooking, it means using ingredients that are currently in high demand; in photography, selecting subjects with current visual appeal; in personal finance, choosing stocks rising in the market; and in cognitive science, focusing on research areas experiencing a surge of interest.
