# gongfu brewing

Gongfu brewing is an intricate Chinese tea preparation method that emphasizes skill, precision, and patience to extract the full flavor profile of the tea leaves. It involves multiple steeping cycles with specific water temperatures and infusion times, akin to a craftsman's dedication in their art form. This practice can be likened to cognitive science principles where attention to detail and iterative processes enhance sensory experience and appreciation of subtle nuances.
