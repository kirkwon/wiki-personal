# Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG)

Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG) is an algorithm used to optimize decision-making in complex environments by learning a deterministic policy that maps states to actions. In the context of cooking or photography, it could be applied to automate recipe selection or camera settings adjustments for desired outcomes; similarly, for personal finance, it might guide investment strategies, and in cognitive science, model how humans learn from sequential decisions.
