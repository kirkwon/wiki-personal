# Προσαρμογή Αποχώρησης

Προσαρμογή Αποχώρησης (Space Allocation) refers to the strategic division of resources such as kitchen space for efficient cooking, camera settings for optimal photography results, budget distribution in personal finance management, and cognitive load balancing in memory tasks.
