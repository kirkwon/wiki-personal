# ResNet

A ResNet (Residual Network) is an advanced deep learning architecture that simplifies the training of very deep neural networks by introducing skip connections or shortcuts to jump over some layers. This design helps in mitigating the vanishing gradient problem and allows for more effective feature extraction, akin to how focusing on key ingredients can enhance both cooking recipes and photographic composition while maintaining financial models' robustness against complex data patterns.


In cognitive science, ResNet serves as an analogy for understanding neural processing pathways where information bypasses certain stages of analysis, potentially leading to more efficient problem-solving strategies or creative thinking processes.
