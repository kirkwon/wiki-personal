# career capital

Career capital refers to the accumulation of skills, experiences, and networks that enhance one's employability and career advancement in fields like cooking (culinary expertise), photography (visual storytelling proficiency), personal finance (financial literacy), and cognitive science (understanding human thought processes). It encompasses both tangible assets, such as certifications or portfolios, and intangible ones like reputation and connections within these industries.
