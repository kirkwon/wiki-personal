# Dual Process Theory

The Dual Process Theory posits that human cognition operates via two distinct systems: System 1 (intuitive) handles automatic, quick judgments often based on heuristics; System 2 (analytical) is slower and more deliberate, involving logical reasoning. In cooking, this theory explains why some recipes are instinctively followed while others require careful calculation. For photography, it accounts for both the immediate artistic decisions and methodical editing processes. In personal finance, intuition might guide spending habits, but System 2 is crucial for budget planning and investment strategies. Cognitive science studies these systems to understand how they influence decision-making across various domains.
