# Factor Investing

Factor investing is an approach to building an investment portfolio by targeting specific drivers of return across asset classes. It's akin to using precise ingredients or light settings in cooking and photography to achieve desired outcomes; similarly, it involves identifying factors like value, size, momentum, quality, and volatility that can influence the performance of investments over time. This method is grounded in cognitive science principles, as it requires understanding human biases and market psychology to optimize portfolio construction for long-term financial growth.
