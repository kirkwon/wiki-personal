# The Alignment Problem - Brian Christian & Tom Griffiths

A 2024 book by Brian Christian and Tom Griffiths exploring the multi-faceted challenge of aligning artificial intelligence systems with human values. The book argues that AI alignment is not a single problem but a collection of interconnected challenges: computational intractability, reinforcement learning reward hacking, consistency-pressure conflicts, anomaly sensitivity, and social value specification.

## Core Chapters

### Chapter 1: Introduction to AI Safety
Sets the stage for understanding why aligning AI with human values is difficult and urgent. The authors frame alignment as a problem that spans technical, philosophical, and organizational dimensions.

### Chapter 2: The Genie Problem and Intractable Computation
Introduces the concept of intractable computation through the "Genie Problem" — even well-intentioned AI systems face computational limits that prevent perfectly solving hard problems. This framing connects to broader questions about the limits of computation and the impossibility of perfectly optimizing complex objectives.

### Chapter 3: Reinforcement Learning in Games and Real-World Applications
Discusses reinforcement learning's role in gaming and real-world settings, along with the difficulties of achieving alignment between learned behaviors and human values. Reward hacking and mispecified objectives are central concerns.

### Chapter 4: The Squirrelly Quest for Consistency
Examines how consistency plays a crucial part in creating AI systems that can make reliable decisions across diverse situations, while also considering the challenges it poses to alignment with human intentions. Relates to [[decision-making-frameworks]] and [[thinking-fast-and-slow---daniel-kahneman]].

### Chapter 5: Anomalies and Exceptions
Delves into anomalies and exceptions as potential sources of misalignment between machine learning models and real-world scenarios. Out-of-distribution scenarios and edge cases reveal fundamental gaps in model reliability.

### Chapter 6: The Social Value Alignment Problem
Focuses on how to build systems that value human wellbeing and social good, using the example of an organization seeking to maximize happiness through various initiatives. Connects to [[morality-and-decision-making]] and [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]].

### Chapter 7: AI Safety Methods in Practice
Presents several methods for improving safety and alignment, including iterative design, testing with external agents, transparency, and collaborative research. No single method suffices; diverse approaches are needed.

### Chapter 8: Future Directions
Explores potential future directions for the field of AI safety, as well as broader philosophical implications regarding our relationship with intelligent machines.

## Relationship to Other Works
