# Superforecasting

Superforecasting is the practice of making highly accurate predictions by aggregating diverse perspectives and expertise, often using structured methods like prediction markets or Delphi techniques. It leverages collective intelligence to outperform individual forecasts in areas such as market trends, culinary innovations, photographic developments, and financial projections. In cognitive science, it explores how group dynamics enhance predictive accuracy through shared knowledge and collaborative analysis.
