# Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)

A Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) is an advanced machine learning model inspired by the human visual cortex that excels in image recognition tasks such as identifying ingredients or food quality in cooking and photography. In personal finance, CNNs can analyze patterns to detect fraudulent transactions, while in cognitive science, they help simulate aspects of human perception for research purposes.
