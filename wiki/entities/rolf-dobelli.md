# Rolf Dobelli

Swiss author and thinker best known for *The Art of Thinking Clearly*, a comprehensive catalog of roughly 100 cognitive biases and mental errors. Dobelli organizes biases into categories: **Tendency**, **Decisions**, **Events**, **Others**, **Information**, **Outcomes**, **Judgments**, and **Success**. He also provides practical debiasing tools including bias checklists, pre‑mortem analysis, devil’s advocate exercises, and reference class forecasting.

His work explores how tendencies and heuristics shape perception and decision‑making. Dobelli emphasizes that while bias awareness is valuable, debiasing should not override situations requiring fast, intuitive action, and that analysis paralysis is itself a risk.
