# Major and Minor Scales

A Major Scale is a sequence of seven pitches with specific intervals (whole and half steps) that creates a harmonious sound often associated with Western music; it's the foundation for many melodies in cooking recipes or photography themes, symbolizing balance. A Minor Scale has a similar structure but starts from a different note, giving rise to a more somber tone, which can influence mood-driven content creation and financial decision-making strategies.


**Major Scales:** Harmonious sequences of pitches used in music composition; analogous to balanced recipes or thematic photography sets.


**Minor Scales:** Somber sequences with a distinctive starting note, influencing emotional content and strategic financial planning.
