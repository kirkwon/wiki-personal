# Thelonious Monk

Thelonious Monk (1917–1982) was a pianist and one of the pioneers of bebop. He is celebrated for his unconventional approach to harmony, rhythmic innovation, and his unique compositional voice.

## Role in Bebop

While Monk's harmonic language was often more dissonant and angular than that of other bebop pioneers, his contributions were foundational. He expanded the harmonic palette of jazz, introducing intervals and voicings that challenged conventional expectations.

## Legacy

Monk is recognized as one of the most original composers in jazz history, with standards like "Round Midnight" and "Straight, No Chaser" remaining central to the repertoire.
