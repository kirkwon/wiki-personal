# Strategy canvas analysis

A Strategy Canvas Analysis is a visual tool used to map out competitors' strategies across various dimensions relevant to an industry or market segment. It helps identify opportunities for differentiation by plotting key factors that influence consumer choices and highlighting gaps in the current offerings, which can guide decision-making in cooking recipes, photography techniques, personal finance planning, and cognitive science research approaches.
