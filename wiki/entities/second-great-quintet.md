# Second Great Quintet

The Second Great Quintet was Miles Davis's innovative small group active from 1963 to 1968. It featured Herbie Hancock on piano, Wayne Shorter on saxophone, Ron Carter on bass, and Tony Williams on drums.

## Characteristics
- More complex harmonies and free rhythmic interplay
- Telepathic communication and collective improvisation
- Pushed boundaries of small-group jazz

## Key Albums
- *E.S.P.* (1965)
- *Miles Smiles* (1966)
- *Nefertiti* (1967)
- *Miles in the Sky* (1968)

The ensemble is considered to represent the peak of harmonic complexity in jazz.^[raw/articles/miles-davis.md]
