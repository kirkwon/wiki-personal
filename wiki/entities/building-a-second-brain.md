# building-a-second-brain

The concept of building a second brain refers to creating an external digital repository that mirrors one's cognitive processes, such as recipes for cooking or photographic metadata, enabling quick access and recall. In personal finance, it involves structuring information in a way that supports efficient decision-making. From a cognitive science perspective, this approach leverages the brain's ability to offload routine tasks, potentially improving overall mental efficiency.
