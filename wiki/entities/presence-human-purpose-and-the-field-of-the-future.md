# Presence: Human Purpose and the Field of the Future

"Presence: Human Purpose and the Field of the Future" is a book by Peter M. Senge that explores human purpose, collective awareness, and the future of systemic change. It is referenced as a cross-source for the Senge mental model framework.

## Context
The book extends Senge's earlier work on systems thinking and learning organizations into questions of individual and collective purpose within complex systems.
