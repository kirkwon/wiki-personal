# DBA

DBA stands for Database Administrator, an individual responsible for managing and maintaining computer databases to ensure data integrity, security, and availability. In the context of cooking, a DBA might oversee recipe inventory systems; in photography, they could manage image archives; within personal finance, they would handle expense tracking databases; and in cognitive science research, they'd be tasked with managing data sets for experiments.
