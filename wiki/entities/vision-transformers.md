# Vision Transformers

A Vision Transformer (ViT) is an AI model that processes visual data by breaking it down into fixed-size patches, similar to how a neural network might analyze individual ingredients in cooking or elements of a photograph. It then applies self-attention mechanisms to understand the relationships between these patches, which can be likened to understanding flavor profiles in recipes or composition in photography. ViTs have shown promise in cognitive science for modeling how humans process visual information and could potentially enhance personal finance applications by analyzing complex data patterns visually.
