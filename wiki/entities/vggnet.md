# VGGNet

VGGNet is an advanced deep learning convolutional neural network architecture designed primarily for visual recognition tasks, such as image classification in photography or facial recognition in cooking videos. Its uniform structure of successive convolutional layers makes it efficient and effective at extracting hierarchical features from images, which can be applied to enhance personal finance security through biometric verification or contribute to cognitive science research by analyzing visual patterns.
