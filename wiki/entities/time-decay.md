# time-decay

Time-decay refers to the gradual decrease of importance or effectiveness over time. In cooking, it describes how flavors meld together as they sit; in photography, it relates to exposure settings that change with prolonged shutter speeds; in personal finance, it's a strategy for reducing debt interest by paying off older balances first; and in cognitive science, it pertains to the fading of memories over time.
