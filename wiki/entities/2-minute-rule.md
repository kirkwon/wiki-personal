# 2-minute rule

The "2-minute rule" is a decision-making guideline suggesting that if an action or task can be completed within two minutes, it should be done immediately without delay. This principle encourages efficiency by minimizing procrastination and promoting quick resolution of small issues in various domains such as cooking (e.g., chopping ingredients), photography (e.g., adjusting camera settings), personal finance (e.g., updating budget spreadsheets), and cognitive science (e.g., conducting brief experiments).
