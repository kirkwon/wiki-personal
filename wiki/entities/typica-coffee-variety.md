# Typica Coffee Variety

Typica is a classic Arabica coffee variety with origins in Yemen and Ethiopia. It is known for its balanced, traditional coffee flavor.

## Key Characteristics
- **Origin**: Yemen/Ethiopia
- **Character**: Classic coffee flavor, balanced
- **Growth**: Tall tree, lower yield
- **Use**: Traditional quality coffee

Typica was one of the first coffee varieties spread globally and remains a benchmark for evaluating coffee quality.
