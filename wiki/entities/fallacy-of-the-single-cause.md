# Fallacy of the single cause

The Fallacy of the Single Cause is an erroneous reasoning that attributes an outcome to one single factor while ignoring other potential contributing factors. In cooking, it might lead to blaming a dish's failure on just one ingredient; in photography, overlooking multiple elements like light and composition; in personal finance, oversimplifying complex economic outcomes; and in cognitive science, disregarding the multifaceted nature of human thought processes.
