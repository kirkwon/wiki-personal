# Αγοραστικός Κίνδυνος

Αγοραστικός Κίνδυνος (market risk) refers to the potential for loss in investments due to fluctuations in market conditions such as changes in interest rates, currency exchange rates, and stock prices. In personal finance, it signifies the inherent uncertainty of achieving desired financial outcomes when engaging with various markets like real estate or commodities. Understanding this risk is crucial for making informed decisions that align with one's investment goals and tolerance for potential losses in cooking-related business ventures, photography sales, or cognitive science research funding.
