# Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE)

Generalized Advantage Estimation (GAE) is an algorithmic approach used to optimize reinforcement learning policies by efficiently estimating the advantages of actions taken in a given state, balancing bias and variance for improved policy performance. In personal finance terms, it's like finding the best investment strategy that maximizes returns while minimizing risks over time.


In photography, GAE could be likened to adjusting camera settings (exposure, focus) iteratively to capture the most visually appealing images by evaluating different combinations of shots and their outcomes.
