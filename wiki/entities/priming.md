# Priming

Priming is the psychological phenomenon where exposure to one stimulus influences the response to a subsequent stimulus, without conscious guidance or intention. In cooking, priming can affect flavor perception; in photography, it might influence composition choices; for personal finance, it could shape spending habits; and in cognitive science, it's studied as a mechanism of memory and decision-making processes.
