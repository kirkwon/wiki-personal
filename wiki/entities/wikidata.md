# Wikidata

Wikidata is a free, open knowledge base that stores structured data across various domains including cooking recipes, photographic techniques, personal finance tips, and cognitive science concepts. It allows for easy cross-referencing of information and can be integrated into personal wikis to enhance research and learning efficiency in these areas.
