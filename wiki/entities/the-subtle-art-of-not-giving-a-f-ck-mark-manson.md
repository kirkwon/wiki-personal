# The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F_ck - Mark Manson

"The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F_ck by Mark Manson is a self-help book that emphasizes the importance of personal values over societal expectations, advocating for authenticity in all aspects of life including cooking (creative expression), photography (perception and composition), personal finance (value-driven decision making), and cognitive science (understanding one's thought patterns). It encourages readers to confront their own limitations and embrace the imperfect journey towards self-improvement."
