# ROI Calculator

A ROI (Return on Investment) Calculator is a tool used to assess the profitability of an investment by comparing gains or losses relative to its cost. It calculates the percentage increase or decrease in value from the initial outlay, providing insight into financial efficiency and decision-making effectiveness across various domains like cooking (e.g., ingredient costs vs. dish popularity), photography (equipment expenses versus image sale revenue), personal finance (investments yield), and cognitive science research funding outcomes.
