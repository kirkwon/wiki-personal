# The Wisdom of Crowds - James Surowiecki

"James Surowiecki's 'The Wisdom of Crowds' posits that collective judgment can often surpass individual expertise when it comes to problem-solving, decision making, and innovation due to diverse perspectives and decentralized information. In cooking, this translates to crowd-sourced recipes; in photography, popular editing trends; in personal finance, investment strategies; and in cognitive science, understanding group dynamics."
