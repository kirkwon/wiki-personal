# Strategic misrepresentation

Strategic misrepresentation is the intentional distortion of information to influence decisions or perceptions in areas such as cooking recipes (altering ingredient proportions), photography techniques (manipulating image editing tools), personal finance advice (overstating investment returns), and cognitive science research findings (selectively reporting data).
