# Policy Gradient

A Policy Gradient is an approach in reinforcement learning that directly optimizes the policy—the strategy of choosing actions—by adjusting it based on the gradient of expected rewards. It's akin to refining recipes for better outcomes or tweaking camera settings for perfect shots, iteratively improving decision-making processes over time.
