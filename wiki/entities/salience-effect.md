# salience-effect

The salience effect refers to the phenomenon where certain stimuli or information stand out more prominently in our perception due to their distinctiveness or relevance, influencing decision-making processes. In cooking and photography, it can affect taste preferences and visual focus; in personal finance, it may sway investment choices, while in cognitive science, understanding this effect is crucial for studying attention and memory.
