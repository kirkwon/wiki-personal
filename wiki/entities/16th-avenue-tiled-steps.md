# 16th Avenue Tiled Steps

The 16th Avenue Tiled Steps are the signature landmark of Golden Gate Heights — 163 steps covered in colorful tiles creating a community mosaic art project.

## Description

- **Steps**: 163 colorful tile steps
- **Installation**: 2005
- **Origin**: Community art project by local volunteers
- **Each tile**: Different design, creating a unique mosaic
- **Popular for**: Photography and sightseeing

## Access

- Location: 16th Ave and Moraga St
- Street parking on surrounding streets
- Walkable from Golden Gate Park (15-20 minutes)

## What to See

- Beautiful tile mosaic up the stairway
- View from top: Ocean Beach, Golden Gate Park, city skyline
- Golden Gate Heights park at the top

## Photography

One of the best photography spots in the neighborhood — colorful, iconic, and popular for photos.
