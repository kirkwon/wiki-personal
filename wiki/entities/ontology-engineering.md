# Ontology Engineering

Ontology engineering is the systematic approach to creating structured frameworks that define concepts, relationships, and axioms within specific domains such as cooking recipes, photographic elements, personal finance management systems, or cognitive science theories. It involves designing ontologies that facilitate data interoperability, knowledge representation, and reasoning across diverse applications in these fields.
