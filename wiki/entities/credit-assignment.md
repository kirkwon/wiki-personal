# Credit Assignment

Credit assignment is the process of determining which actions or decisions are responsible for an outcome, such as identifying which ingredient made a dish taste better in cooking, attributing success to specific camera settings in photography, recognizing effective financial strategies in personal finance, and understanding neural mechanisms behind learning in cognitive science.
