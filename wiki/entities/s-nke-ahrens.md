# Sönke Ahrens

Sönke Ahrens is an author known primarily for his work on personal finance with the bestselling book "Your Money or Your Life." He has also contributed to cognitive science through his writing that explores decision-making processes and behavioral economics, often intersecting with financial topics. As a hobbyist photographer, Ahrens captures moments reflective of his diverse interests in life.
