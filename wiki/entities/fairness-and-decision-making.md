# fairness-and-decision-making

Fairness-and-decision-making refers to the ethical principles guiding equitable choices in various contexts, such as recipe ingredient proportions (Cooking), balanced exposure settings (Photography), prudent budget allocation (Personal Finance), and unbiased cognitive assessments (Cognitive Science). It involves evaluating options impartially to achieve optimal outcomes.
