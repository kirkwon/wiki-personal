# Decision fatigue

Decision fatigue refers to the deteriorating quality of decisions made by an individual after a long session of decision-making. In contexts like cooking or personal finance, it can lead to poor choices such as opting for unhealthy food options or impulsive spending. Within cognitive science and photography, understanding this concept helps in designing user interfaces that minimize unnecessary decisions, thereby maintaining the photographer's focus and improving culinary experiences through streamlined recipe execution.
