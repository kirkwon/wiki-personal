# joel-meyerowitz

Joel Meyerowitz is an American photographer renowned for his color photography of landscapes, architecture, people, and street scenes. His work emphasizes the importance of light in capturing images and has significantly influenced modern photographic practices. Outside of photography, Joel's interest in cognitive science pertains to understanding human perception through visual stimuli, which complements his artistic endeavors.
