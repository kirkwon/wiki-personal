# motivation-crowding

Motivation-crowding occurs when external rewards or pressures undermine intrinsic motivation in an activity. In cooking, this might manifest as a loss of joy in preparing meals once monetary compensation is introduced. Similarly, in photography and personal finance, the pursuit of perfection can be stifled by overemphasis on financial gain or social approval. Cognitive science research suggests that intrinsic motivation is crucial for creativity and learning, which can be compromised when extrinsic rewards dominate.
