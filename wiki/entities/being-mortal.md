# Being Mortal - Atul Gawande

*Being Mortal* is a book by Atul Gawande that examines how modern medicine deals with aging, death, and end-of-life care. It is cited as a source for the mental model of [[care]], emphasizing the importance of recognizing patterns and relationships when navigating complex, sensitive situations.

## Key Themes
- End-of-life decision making
- The role of care in navigating difficult realities
- Pattern recognition in complex human situations
