# The PARA Method

The PARA Method is an acronym that stands for Planning, Analysis, Recording, and Assessment. It's a structured approach used across various disciplines: in cooking to plan recipes and analyze ingredients; in photography for planning shots and assessing compositions; in personal finance for budgeting and tracking expenses; and in cognitive science as a framework for designing experiments and evaluating results.
