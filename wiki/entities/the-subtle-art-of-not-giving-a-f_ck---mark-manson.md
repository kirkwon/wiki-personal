# The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck - Mark Manson

Mark Manson's *The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck* argues that effective living requires selective commitment to what truly matters. The book challenges the pursuit of unlimited positivity and instead advocates for principles grounded in honesty, responsibility, and values-based choice.

## Key Ideas
- **Selective concern**: Not all issues deserve equal attention; principles help identify what matters.
- **Honest self-confrontation**: Effective principles require facing uncomfortable truths about values and priorities.
- **Values over feelings**: Acting on principles rather than emotional reactions leads to better outcomes.
- **Impermanence and commitment**: Accepting limitations and committing to meaningful concerns.

## Relevance to Principle Mental Model
Manson's contribution to the principle framework is the idea that principles must be filtered through personal values. His perspective emphasizes that the most useful principle is one that clarifies what to care about and what to let go.
