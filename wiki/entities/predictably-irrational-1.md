# Predictably Irrational

"Predictably Irrational" refers to the concept that human decision-making often deviates from rationality due to cognitive biases, emotions, and social influences, even in areas like cooking (recipe choices), photography (composition decisions), personal finance (spending habits), and understanding of visual perception. It highlights the predictable patterns in our irrational behaviors across various disciplines.
