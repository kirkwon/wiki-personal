# Underfitting

In cooking, underfitting occurs when a recipe lacks complexity or seasoning, resulting in bland dishes. In photography, it's like using overly simplistic settings that fail to capture the subject's depth and nuances. Financially, it means not diversifying investments enough to optimize returns. Cognitively, underfitting refers to a model or theory that is too simple to explain observed phenomena adequately.
