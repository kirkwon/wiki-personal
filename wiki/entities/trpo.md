# TRPO

Thompson Recurrent Policy Optimization (TRPO) is an advanced reinforcement learning algorithm that maximizes the expected return by taking step-sized updates on policy parameters to ensure stable improvement without large deviations from previous policies. It's akin to fine-tuning recipes or camera settings incrementally for optimal results, ensuring each adjustment contributes positively to the overall outcome while maintaining consistency. In personal finance and cognitive science, TRPO can be metaphorically related to iterative investment strategies that adapt over time with minimal risk of significant setbacks.
