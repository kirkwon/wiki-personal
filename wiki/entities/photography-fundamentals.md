# Photography Fundamentals

Photography fundamentals encompass the essential principles of capturing images through a camera lens, including understanding exposure (light sensitivity), composition (framing elements within the shot), and focus (the sharpness of the subject). Mastery of these basics is crucial for creating visually appealing photographs that effectively communicate a message or emotion.
