# stochastic-processes

Stochastic processes are mathematical models that describe systems or phenomena subject to randomness over time, such as fluctuating ingredient quantities in cooking recipes, unpredictable light patterns in photography, variable interest rates in personal finance, and probabilistic decision-making in cognitive science.


Stochastic processes involve sequences of random variables representing the evolution of a system where future states depend on both deterministic factors and inherent uncertainty, applicable to recipe adjustments based on taste preferences, capturing spontaneous photographic moments, managing financial risks with uncertain returns, and simulating cognitive behavior under ambiguous conditions.
