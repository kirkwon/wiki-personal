# Knob Creek 9

Kentucky Straight Bourbon aged 9 years. Noted as an aged value pick in the mid price tier.

## Key Details
- **Type**: Kentucky Straight Bourbon
- **Age**: 9 years
- **Price**: $41
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Mid‑tier note**: Aged value pick in the mid price tier

## Tasting Notes
- **Nose**: Nutty, oak, vanilla
- **Palate**: Aged complexity, caramel, toasted nuts
- **Finish**: Long, satisfying
- **Verdict**: Aged bourbon value

Moderate sweetness (★★★) and spice (★★), with notable complexity (★★★★) and good value (★★★★) for the mid tier.
