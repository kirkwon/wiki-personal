# timing

Timing refers to the precise moment or interval at which an action should occur to achieve optimal results. In cooking, it's crucial for flavor development; in photography, it affects exposure and composition; in personal finance, timing can influence investment returns; while in cognitive science, it pertains to the speed of mental processes.
