# Why We Work - Barry Schwartz

"Why We Work" by Barry Schwartz explores the psychological impact of work on individuals' well-being, questioning modern society's emphasis on job satisfaction over meaningful engagement. It relates to personal finance and cognitive science through its analysis of how economic pressures shape our career choices and affect mental health. For those interested in cooking and photography as hobbies or professions, it offers insights into balancing passion with financial stability.
