# Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP)

A Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP) is a framework used to model decision making in situations where the outcomes are uncertain due to incomplete information, much like estimating ingredient proportions without precise measurements or predicting photo exposure times under variable light conditions. In personal finance, it helps assess investment risks with imperfect market data, and in cognitive science, it can model how humans make choices based on limited sensory input.
