# Peter M. Senge

Peter M. Senge is an influential thinker known for his work on systems thinking and learning organizations. His seminal book "The Fifth Discipline" has shaped modern approaches to personal growth and organizational development, intersecting with areas like cooking (systemic processes), photography (capturing complex scenes), personal finance (interconnected economic factors), and cognitive science (learning and adaptation).
