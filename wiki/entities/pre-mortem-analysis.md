# Pre-mortem analysis

A pre-mortem analysis is a proactive risk assessment technique where you envision the failure of a project or endeavor before it occurs. In cooking, this could mean anticipating potential recipe disasters; in photography, predicting equipment malfunctions; for personal finance, forecasting investment pitfalls; and within cognitive science, understanding possible errors in experimental design.
