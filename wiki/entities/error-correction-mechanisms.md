# error-correction mechanisms

Error-correction mechanisms are processes or techniques used to identify and rectify mistakes within various domains. In cooking, they ensure recipe accuracy; in photography, they adjust for exposure errors; in personal finance, they correct budget discrepancies; and in cognitive science, they relate to the brain's ability to recognize and amend misinformation or memory lapses.
