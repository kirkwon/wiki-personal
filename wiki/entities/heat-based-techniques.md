# Heat-Based Techniques

In cooking, heat-based techniques involve methods such as sautéing, grilling, or baking to transform ingredients through thermal energy application. In photography, heat sources like infrared cameras can reveal details not visible in standard light spectrums. Personal finance may use 'heat' metaphorically for high-risk investments that could yield significant returns but also potential losses, while cognitive science explores how thermal stimuli affect brain function and decision-making processes.
