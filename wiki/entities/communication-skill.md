# Communication skill

A communication skill is the ability to convey information effectively and efficiently through various mediums such as verbal language, non-verbal cues, or written text. It involves active listening, clear articulation of ideas, and adaptability in different contexts like cooking instructions, photography critiques, financial advice, and scientific discussions. Mastery of communication skills enhances collaboration, understanding, and knowledge sharing across diverse domains.
