# Coffee Water Quality

Coffee water quality refers to the purity and mineral content of water used to brew coffee, significantly impacting its flavor profile and extraction process. Optimal water quality for coffee involves balanced mineral levels (e.g., calcium, magnesium) without excessive impurities that can cause bitterness or off-flavors. Ensuring high-quality water is akin to selecting the right lens in photography; it's foundational to achieving the desired outcome.
