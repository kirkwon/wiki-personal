# Dizzy Gillespie

Dizzy Gillespie (1917–1993) was a trumpeter and key architect of the bebop movement. He is renowned for his virtuosic trumpet playing, his role in developing bebop's harmonic language, and his charismatic performance style.

## Role in Bebop

Gillespie was one of the primary figures who formalized the harmonic innovations of bebop. Alongside Charlie Parker, he helped establish the small-group quintet format and the emphasis on complex chord progressions and fast tempos that define the style.

## Legacy

Gillespie's contributions to jazz harmony and trumpet technique remain foundational. He was also known for his showmanship, including his iconic bent trumpet and puffed cheeks.
