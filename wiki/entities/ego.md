# ego

The ego is the part of one's self-concept that includes one's sense of identity and personal achievements. In cooking, it manifests as pride in culinary skills; in photography, through creative expression and image curation; in personal finance, by managing wealth with confidence; and in cognitive science, as the mediator between self-awareness and external validation.
