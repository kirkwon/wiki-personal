# bias-checklist

A bias-checklist is a structured tool used to identify and mitigate unconscious prejudices or assumptions in decision-making processes across various domains such as cooking (recipe selection), photography (subject framing), personal finance (investment choices), and cognitive science (research design). It helps ensure objectivity by prompting critical reflection on potential biases that could skew outcomes.
