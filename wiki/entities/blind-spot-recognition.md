# Blind spot recognition

Blind spot recognition refers to the ability to identify overlooked or unnoticed elements within various contexts. In cooking, it's about recognizing ingredients that may affect a dish without being obvious; in photography, it involves noticing details missed by standard viewpoints; in personal finance, it entails identifying hidden costs or risks; and in cognitive science, it pertains to the awareness of biases or errors in thinking.
