# Blind spot bias

Blind spot bias is the cognitive tendency to overlook one's own biases or errors in judgment while easily identifying them in others. In cooking, it might lead a chef to dismiss their recipe tweaks as unnecessary; in photography, an artist could ignore flaws in composition they see clearly in peers' work; and in personal finance, individuals may not recognize their own spending habits that differ from recommended financial advice.
