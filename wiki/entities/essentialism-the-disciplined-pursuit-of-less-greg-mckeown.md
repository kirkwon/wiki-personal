# Essentialism_The_Disciplined_Pursuit_of_Less-Greg_McKeown

Essentialism is the philosophy of focusing on what is truly important and eliminating non-essential elements to achieve greater efficiency in various life aspects such as cooking (by simplifying recipes), photography (through minimalistic composition), personal finance (by prioritizing key financial goals), and cognitive science (by streamlining research focus).


'The Disciplined Pursuit of Less-Greg McKeown' is a book that emphasizes the value of decluttering one's life by identifying and cutting out unnecessary commitments, thereby enhancing productivity and well-being across diverse fields including culinary arts, visual arts, financial management, and cognitive research.
