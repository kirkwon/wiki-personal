# hedging

Hedging is a strategy used to minimize potential losses or risks by taking offsetting positions in related assets or markets. In cooking, it might involve using complementary ingredients; in photography, adjusting exposure settings for uncertain light conditions; in personal finance, diversifying investments across different asset classes; and in cognitive science, employing probabilistic reasoning to account for uncertainty in decision-making processes.
