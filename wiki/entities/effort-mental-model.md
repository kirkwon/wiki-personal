# effort-mental-model

An effort-mental-model is an individual's cognitive framework that maps the mental processes involved in executing tasks requiring both physical action (effort) and decision-making or creativity (mental). In cooking, it guides recipe execution; in photography, it influences composition choices; for personal finance, it aids in budget planning; and within cognitive science, it's studied to understand human behavior patterns.
