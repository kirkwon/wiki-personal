# Dropout (Neural Networks)

In neural networks, dropout is a regularization technique where randomly selected neurons are ignored during training to prevent overfitting, similar to how varying ingredients can affect the outcome of a recipe or adjusting camera settings influences photography quality. It's like diversifying investments in personal finance for risk management and mirrors cognitive science principles by simulating brain activity patterns where not all neurons fire simultaneously.
