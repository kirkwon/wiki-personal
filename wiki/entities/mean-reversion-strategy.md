# mean-reversion-strategy

A mean-reversion strategy is an investment approach that assumes asset prices will eventually return to their historical average or long-term trend after deviating from it. In personal finance, this can guide decisions on buying undervalued assets and selling overpriced ones, akin to selecting affordable ingredients for cost-effective cooking or adjusting camera settings for optimal exposure in photography.


In cognitive science, mean-reversion reflects the tendency of individuals' beliefs to align with average opinions over time, which can influence social learning and decision-making processes.
