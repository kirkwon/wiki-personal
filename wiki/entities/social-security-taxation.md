# Social Security Taxation

Social Security taxation refers to the mandatory federal income tax that funds the Social Security program, which provides retirement benefits, disability insurance, and survivor benefits. The current rate is a fixed percentage of an individual's gross wages up to an annual limit, with self-employed individuals paying both employee and employer portions.
