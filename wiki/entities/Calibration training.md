# Calibration training

Calibration training is the practice of systematically improving efficiency and output through productivity methods. It involves identifying and implementing techniques and frameworks that maximize personal or organizational productivity while maintaining sustainable work practices.

## How to apply it

1. **Audit your current workflow** — Identify where efficiency losses occur, track how you spend time and attention, and note patterns that lead to wasted effort.
2. **Apply the framework** — Restructure around high-impact activities, eliminate or delegate low-value tasks.
3. **Iterate and refine** — Measure the impact of changes, adjust based on results, build consistency through habit formation.
4. **Scale what works** — Standardize effective approaches, document processes for reuse, teach others to multiply impact.

## Examples

- Planning your week more effectively using calibration techniques.
- Managing competing priorities and breaking down complex projects.
- Improving team coordination, meeting efficiency, and decision-making.
- Accelerating skill acquisition and organizing information for retention.
