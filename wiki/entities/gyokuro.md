# Gyokuro (玉露)

Gyokuro is a premium Japanese green tea shade-grown in Uji, Kyoto Prefecture. The shade-growing technique increases umami content, resulting in a deep, sweet, and complex flavor.

## Key Facts
- **Origin**: Uji, Kyoto Prefecture
- **Processing**: Shade-grown, steamed
- **Flavor**: Deep umami, sweet, complex
- **Appearance**: Dark green, needle-like
- **Best Brew**: 140-160°F (60-71°C), 2-3 minutes

## Shade-growing
Leaves are covered for 20-30 days before harvest, reducing sunlight exposure and increasing L-theanine and chlorophyll content, which enhances umami flavor.
