# Value Investing

Value investing is an approach to personal finance where one seeks out assets that appear underpriced relative to their intrinsic value, akin to selecting high-quality ingredients for cost-effective cooking or capturing compelling images with limited resources. It involves thorough analysis and patience, much like developing photographic skills over time or understanding cognitive processes through study.
