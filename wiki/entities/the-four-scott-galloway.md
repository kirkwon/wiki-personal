# The Four - Scott Galloway

The Four by Scott Galloway is an analysis framework that categorizes major companies into four distinct groups based on their business strategies, market influence, and competitive dynamics: the Disruptors (innovative startups), the Superstars (dominant industry leaders), the Digitals (technology-focused firms), and the Laggards (traditional or struggling companies). This model can be applied to various fields including Cooking (e.g., comparing restaurant chains), Photography (analyzing market players), Personal Finance (evaluating investment opportunities), and Cognitive Science (understanding research institutions' impacts).
