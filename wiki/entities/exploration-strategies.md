# Exploration Strategies

Exploration strategies are systematic approaches to discover new cooking recipes, photographic techniques, investment opportunities, or cognitive patterns by experimenting with different combinations of ingredients, settings, financial instruments, and mental exercises respectively. These methods aim to expand one's skill set and knowledge base through trial and error while minimizing risk.
