# intuition

Intuition is an immediate understanding or perception without the need for conscious reasoning, often based on experience and subconscious cues. In cooking, it guides instinctive flavor combinations; in photography, it informs spontaneous composition choices; in personal finance, it aids in recognizing investment opportunities; while in cognitive science, it is studied as the brain's ability to make quick judgments and decisions.
