# categorizing-information

Categorizing information involves organizing data into distinct groups based on shared characteristics or themes. In cooking, this could mean sorting recipes by cuisine type; in photography, it's arranging images by subject matter or composition techniques; for personal finance, categorization might entail grouping expenses and income sources; within cognitive science, information is often sorted into frameworks like memory types or decision-making processes.
