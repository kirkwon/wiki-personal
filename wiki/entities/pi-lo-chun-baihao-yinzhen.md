# Pi Lo Chun / Baihao Yinzhen (白毫银针)

Pi Lo Chun, also known as Baihao Yinzhen, is a Chinese green tea made from downy buds with minimal processing. It is one of the most delicate green teas, featuring floral and sweet flavors.

## Key Facts
- **Origin**: Jiangsu Province
- **Processing**: Minimal processing, downy buds
- **Flavor**: Delicate, floral, sweet
- **Appearance**: Silvery down, white-tipped
- **Best Brew**: 160-165°F (71-74°C), 1-2 minutes

## Processing
Only the youngest buds with silvery down are harvested, then heat-treated to prevent oxidation. The minimal processing preserves the delicate flavors.
