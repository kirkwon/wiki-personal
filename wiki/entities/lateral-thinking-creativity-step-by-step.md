# Lateral Thinking: Creativity Step by Step

Lateral thinking is an approach to problem solving that involves looking at situations from fresh perspectives, often unrelated to conventional methods. In cooking, it means experimenting with unusual ingredient combinations; in photography, it's about capturing images through unconventional angles or lighting; for personal finance, it entails creative budgeting strategies; and within cognitive science, it refers to innovative ways of understanding mental processes.
