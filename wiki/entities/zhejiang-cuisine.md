# zhejiang-cuisine

Zhejiang cuisine refers to the traditional food culture of Zhejiang Province in China, known for its diverse flavors, fresh ingredients, and unique cooking techniques such as steaming and braising. It features regional specialties like West Lake Fish, Shengjian mantou (pan-fried dumplings), and Suzhou Almond Cake, which are celebrated both locally and internationally for their culinary artistry.
