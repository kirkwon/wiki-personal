# Expense Tracking Systems

An Expense Tracking System is a digital tool designed to monitor and categorize financial outflows related to daily activities such as cooking (e.g., grocery expenses) or hobbies like photography (equipment costs). It also assists in personal finance management by recording transactions, facilitating budget planning, and aiding cognitive science research through data analysis of spending patterns.
