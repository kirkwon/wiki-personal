# chat-hygiene

Chat-hygiene refers to the practices that maintain clarity, respect, and productivity in online communication. It involves using appropriate language, being mindful of tone, and ensuring information is accurately conveyed, akin to how precision and cleanliness are vital in cooking recipes or photographic techniques. In personal finance discussions and cognitive science debates, it helps prevent misunderstandings and fosters constructive dialogue.
