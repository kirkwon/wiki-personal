# standard-deviation

Standard deviation measures the amount of variation or dispersion from the average (mean) value within a set of data points. In cooking, it quantifies ingredient consistency; in photography, it assesses image sharpness variability; for personal finance, it reflects investment risk fluctuation; and in cognitive science, it evaluates performance score spread among subjects.
