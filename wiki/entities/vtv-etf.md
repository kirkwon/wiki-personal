# VTV ETF

VTV is a Smart Beta ETF that implements the value factor for large cap stocks. It has the lowest expense ratio among common factor ETFs at 0.04%.

## Details

- **Factor**: Value (large cap)
- **Expense Ratio**: 0.04%
- **Provider**: Vanguard

## Role in Factor Investing

VTV provides a low-cost way to gain value factor exposure without direct stock picking. See [[factor-investing]] and [[value-factor]] for context.
