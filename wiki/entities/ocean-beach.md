# Ocean Beach

Ocean Beach is San Francisco's main beach, stretching ~3.5 miles from the [[cliff-house]] in the north to the [[san-francisco-zoo]] in the south. It is a sandy, wide, open-ocean beach known for powerful waves, cold water (50-55°F), and iconic fog. It is best suited for surfing, beach walking, storm watching, and bonfires rather than sunbathing due to its cold and foggy climate. The location is also prized for golden hour photography, particularly at sunset.

Ocean Beach est la principale plage de San Francisco, située sur la côte ouest de la ville, bordée par l'océan Pacifique. Elle se trouve dans le [[sunset-district]] and is bordered by the [[presidio]].

## Key Facts

- **Location**: Western edge of San Francisco, accessed from Sunset District, Parkside, and Presidio
- **Length**: ~3.5 miles
- **Water temperature**: 50-55°F year-round
- **Best for**: Surfing, beach walking, bonfires, storm watching, golden hour photography
- **Weather**: Foggy, windy, cool year-round; "June Gloom" in summer — do not expect a sunny beach in summer due to fog
- **Café**: Café near the beach

## Beach Sections

### Kelly's Cove (North End)

Near the [[cliff-house]] and [[sutro-baths]]. More protected from wind with smaller waves, popular with beginner surfers. Closest to public transit. More crowded with beach volleyball. Accessible via parking at Ocean Beach near Great Highway or walking from Sutro Baths and Land's End.

### Noriega Street (Center)

Consistent surf break, popular with intermediate surfers. Good surf spot year-round. Quieter than the north end, with morning surf sessions. Access via Noriega Street parking lot or walking from the Sunset District.

### Sloat Boulevard (South End)

Near the San Francisco Zoo. Larger waves, more exposed to wind, stronger currents. More solitude and less crowded, suited for advanced surfers. Access via Sloat Blvd parking or walking from Outer Sunset.

## Surfing

- **Waves**: 3-6 feet typical, up to 10+ feet in winter
- **Gear**: 4/3mm wetsuit minimum (5/4mm in winter), booties, hood and gloves for winter
- **Skill mapping**: Beginners at Kelly's Cove, intermediate at Noriega, advanced at Sloat
- **Safety**: Always surf with a buddy, check conditions via resources like Surfline, be aware of rip currents and sneaker waves

## Seasonal Guide

| Season | Air Temp | Water Temp | Conditions |
|--------|----------|------------|------------|
| Spring | 55-65°F | 52-55°F | Variable, windy |
| Summer | 55-60°F | 52-55°F | Foggy, windy ("June Gloom") |
| Fall | 60-70°F | 54-56°F | Best weather, sunnier |
| Winter | 50-60°F | 50-54°F | Storms, large waves |

Fall (September-November) offers the best weather; winter offers the most dramatic conditions for storm watching and photography.

## Golden Hour at Ocean Beach

Ocean Beach is a great golden hour location in San Francisco. Sunset golden hour produces beautiful beach scenes with waves, sun over water, and golden light on the sand. Sunset golden hour at Ocean Beach features warm light on the water with golden reflections, long shadows on the sand, and dramatic colors in orange, pink, and red. The ocean provides a reflective surface that amplifies the golden hour glow.

### Golden Hour Shooting

- **Best time**: Sunset
- **Subjects**: Beach, waves, sun over water
- **Tips**: Arrive early, use tripod for long exposures, watch for reflections in water

See [[golden-hour]] for detailed golden hour photography guidance.

## Safety Hazards

- **Sneaker waves**: Large, unexpected waves — stay back from the water's edge
- **Rip currents**: Strong shore currents that can pull swimmers out to sea
- **Cold water**: Hypothermia risk; wetsuit required for any water activity
- **Fog**: Reduced visibility, especially in summer
- **Wind and debris**: Can knock people down; hidden rocks and debris
- **Swimming**: Ocean Beach is not recommended for swimming

## Access

- **Parking**: Great Highway (free), Noriega Street lot, Fulton Street, Sloat Boulevard
- **Transit**: N-Judah, 28-19th Ave, 5-Fulton to Great Highway (30-40 min from downtown)
- **Biking**: Bike lanes on Great Highway, flat terrain
- **Neighborhoods**: Access from Sunset District, Parkside, and Presidio
- **Walking**: Accessible on foot from [[sunset-district]]; the [[lands-end]] trail nearby offers spectacular coastal views.
