# Mental Models

A mental model is an individual's internal representation of how something works in the real world, shaping their understanding and decision-making processes across various domains like cooking (recipe interpretation), photography (composition rules), personal finance (investment strategies), and cognitive science (perception & memory).


In cooking, mental models guide how one perceives ingredient interactions; in photography, they influence composition choices; in personal finance, they affect investment decisions; while in cognitive science, they underpin the study of human thought processes.
