# Skin in the Game

"Skin in the Game" refers to an individual's financial stake or investment in an endeavor, which creates a sense of accountability and motivation for success. In cooking, it means owning ingredients; in photography, it involves funding equipment; in personal finance, it signifies risk-taking with capital; and in cognitive science, it represents the commitment to research outcomes.
