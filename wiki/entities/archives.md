# Archives

An archive is a collection of historical documents or records providing information about a place, institution, or group of people. In the contexts of cooking, it could refer to recipe collections; in photography, it might mean an organized database of images; for personal finance, it's a structured repository of financial documents and transactions; and within cognitive science, archives can be databases storing experimental data or research findings.
