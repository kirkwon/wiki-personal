# Markov Logic Networks

A Markov Logic Network (MLN) is an extension of Markov networks that incorporates first-order logic to handle structured data with uncertainty. In cooking or photography, MLNs can model complex recipes or camera settings; in personal finance, they help predict market trends considering various economic indicators; and within cognitive science, they aid in understanding decision processes under uncertain conditions.
