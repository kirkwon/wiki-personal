# Sepp Hochreiter

Sepp Hochreiter is an influential computer scientist known for his pioneering work on recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and long short-term memory (LSTM) architectures which have applications in machine learning models used for pattern recognition, including image processing relevant to photography. His contributions also extend to cognitive science through the development of algorithms that mimic human decision-making processes, aiding advancements in personal finance and cooking automation technologies.
