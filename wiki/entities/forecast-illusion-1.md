# Forecast illusion

The forecast illusion is the cognitive bias where individuals overestimate their ability to predict future outcomes accurately, leading to unrealistic expectations in various domains such as cooking recipes' success rates, anticipating photography lighting conditions, financial market predictions, and understanding complex scientific phenomena.


In personal finance, the forecast illusion manifests when individuals overly rely on their intuition or past experiences to predict future investment returns, often disregarding statistical probabilities and risk factors.
