# Illusion of control

The illusion of control is a cognitive bias where individuals overestimate their ability to influence outcomes that are largely determined by chance or external factors. In cooking, this might manifest as insisting on precise measurements despite recipe flexibility; in photography, it could be the belief that one's skill alone can overcome poor lighting conditions; in personal finance, an overreliance on market predictions for investment success; and within cognitive science, a misunderstanding of randomness affecting decision-making processes.
