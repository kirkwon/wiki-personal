# noise---daniel-kahneman

Noise, as defined by Daniel Kahneman in the context of decision making, refers to irrelevant or extraneous information that can distort judgment and lead to suboptimal choices. In cooking, it could mean misleading recipe advice; in photography, unnecessary editing tips; in personal finance, spurious investment advice; and within cognitive science, the study of how such noise affects human reasoning processes.
