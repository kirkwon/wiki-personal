# Thinking in Bets - Annie Duke

"Thinking in Bets," authored by Annie Duke, is a book that applies the principles of betting to decision-making processes, emphasizing probabilistic thinking over certainty. It offers strategies for managing risk and uncertainty across various domains including cooking (recipe experimentation), photography (creative composition choices), personal finance (investment decisions), and cognitive science (understanding biases in human judgment).
