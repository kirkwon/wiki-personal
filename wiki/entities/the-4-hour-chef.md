# The 4-Hour Chef

*The 4-Hour Chef* by Timothy Ferriss uses cooking as a teaching and learning metaphor. The book extracts principles from the culinary world—such as pattern recognition, decomposition, and understanding relationships between components—and applies them universally to skill acquisition and problem-solving. It introduces the DSSS method (Deconstruct, Select, Sequence, Stakes) and the "tiger cooking method," using meal preparation as a meta-learning framework that breaks complex skills into digestible portions to accelerate mastery across domains.

## Key Ideas
- Cooking as a framework for learning
- Pattern recognition through analogy
- Transferring culinary principles to other domains
- DSSS method: Deconstruct, Select, Sequence, Stakes
- Tiger cooking method

## Connection to Mental Models
The book aligns with the wiki's broader framework of using structured, deconstructable frameworks ([[recipes-mental-model]]) to navigate complexity.
