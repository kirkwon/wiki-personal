# Statistical Arbitrage

Statistical arbitrage is an investment strategy that involves simultaneously buying and selling financial instruments based on statistical models to predict price movements and profit from temporary discrepancies in their prices. It's akin to using recipes (cooking) with precise measurements (statistics) to ensure the best outcome, similar to how one might use camera settings (photography) optimized for capturing perfect shots or adjusting financial portfolios (personal finance) based on data analysis for optimal returns.


In cognitive science terms, it's like applying a learned heuristic that leverages statistical patterns observed in human behavior to make informed decisions.
