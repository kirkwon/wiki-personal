# bruce-gilden

Bruce Gilden is an acclaimed American photographer known for his high-contrast black-and-white street photography that captures raw human emotions and interactions. His work often reflects a candid approach to subjects, emphasizing the authenticity of moments over staged compositions. While not directly related to cooking or personal finance, Gilden's techniques can inspire attention to detail and patience in both culinary arts and financial planning.
