# Knowledge Graph

A Knowledge Graph is an interconnected data structure that represents relationships between various entities (e.g., ingredients in cooking, camera settings in photography) and concepts within personal finance or cognitive science topics. It visually maps out the connections to facilitate understanding of complex information by showing how different pieces relate to one another.
