# Persuasive entities

A persuasive entity is an influential factor or concept that can sway opinions, decisions, or behaviors in various domains such as cooking (e.g., a signature recipe), photography (e.g., a renowned photographer's style), personal finance (e.g., investment strategies), and cognitive science (e.g., persuasive communication techniques). These entities leverage their authority, credibility, or emotional appeal to effectively influence individuals or groups within these fields.
