# Principles---ray-dalio

The Principles-Ray Dalio framework is an investment philosophy based on the author's extensive experience in finance. It emphasizes understanding economic history through a multi-layered approach to decision making, which can be analogous to carefully balancing ingredients for cooking or composing elements for photography. In cognitive science and personal finance, it relates to recognizing patterns and systemic biases in human behavior and market trends.
