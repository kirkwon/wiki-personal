# 押し

In cooking, '押し' refers to the technique of pressing ingredients together, often used in making dough or combining flavors. In photography, it denotes a method where objects are pressed against each other for image capture, emphasizing texture and depth. Within personal finance, it symbolizes investment strategies that involve leveraging assets, while in cognitive science, '押し' relates to the concept of reinforcement learning, where behaviors are strengthened through repeated positive feedback.
