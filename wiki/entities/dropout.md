# Dropout

In cooking, Dropout refers to intentionally leaving out ingredients from a recipe or dish as an experimental technique to discover unique flavors. In photography, it's the practice of randomly disabling parts of an image during editing to create abstract effects. Financially, Dropout can describe individuals who temporarily leave their jobs for personal growth or travel. Within cognitive science, Dropout is a neural network training method that helps prevent overfitting by randomly omitting units from layers during the learning process.
