# dashi

Dashi is a Japanese stock or broth used as the foundation of many traditional dishes such as miso soup. It's made primarily from kombu (dried kelp) and sometimes with shiitake mushrooms or bonito flakes, contributing umami flavor to recipes. Understanding dashi is fundamental in Japanese cuisine for creating authentic tastes.
