# Systematic decision making

Systematic decision making is an organized approach to problem-solving that involves identifying objectives, gathering relevant information, evaluating alternatives based on predefined criteria, and selecting the most suitable option. It ensures consistency and efficiency in choices across various domains such as cooking recipes selection, camera settings for photography, investment strategies in personal finance, or experimental design in cognitive science research.
