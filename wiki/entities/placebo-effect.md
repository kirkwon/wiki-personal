# Placebo effect

The placebo effect is the phenomenon where an individual experiences a perceived improvement in their condition due to belief in the treatment rather than the treatment itself. In cooking, it might manifest as tasting food better when expecting a gourmet dish; in photography, seeing images more appealing if convinced they're high-quality prints; in personal finance, feeling wealthier after reading positive investment advice without actual financial gain; and in cognitive science, enhanced memory recall from believing one has taken an effective supplement.
