# 1031 Exchange Calculator

A 1031 Exchange Calculator is a financial tool used to determine the tax implications of exchanging one investment property for another under Section 1031 of the U.S. Internal Revenue Code. It helps identify potential deferred capital gains and assists in maximizing after-tax returns, which can be particularly useful when managing personal finances or considering significant real estate investments related to one's hobbies like cooking (e.g., restaurant property) or photography studios.
