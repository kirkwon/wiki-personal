# Medicare Overview

Medicare is the federal health insurance program primarily for individuals aged 65 or older, as well as some younger people with disabilities. It consists of different parts covering hospital care (Part A), medical services and outpatient care (Part B), prescription drugs (Part D), and Medicare Advantage plans (Part C). Understanding its structure is crucial for managing healthcare costs, especially when planning long-term financial strategies.
