# fusilli

Fusilli is an Italian pasta shape characterized by its corkscrew design, which allows it to hold sauces effectively due to increased surface area. In photography, the term 'fusilli' metaphorically describes intricate patterns or spirals that capture attention in compositions. From a cognitive perspective, fusilli can symbolize complex thought processes involving intertwined ideas and concepts.
