# Neglect of probability

Neglect of probability is the tendency to underestimate or ignore the likelihood of events when making decisions. In cooking, this may lead to overlooking risks in recipe outcomes; in photography, it could result in disregarding chances of capturing a perfect shot; within personal finance, ignoring probability can cause poor investment choices due to an underestimation of market fluctuations; and in cognitive science, this neglect may affect the understanding of risk perception.
