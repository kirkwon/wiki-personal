# Modern Portfolio Theory

Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT) is an investment framework that emphasizes the benefits of diversification to optimize returns for a given level of risk through carefully selecting a mix of assets. It's analogous to using various ingredients in cooking or different lenses in photography to achieve desired outcomes, but applied to managing financial portfolios efficiently.


In cognitive science terms, MPT can be seen as the process of balancing risk and reward based on probabilistic models, akin to how we might balance sensory inputs for optimal perception or decision-making in complex environments.
