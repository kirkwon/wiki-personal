# Sencha (煎茶)

Sencha is Japan's most common green tea, steamed to preserve freshness and then rolled. It features marine, seaweed, and grassy flavors with a brisk, lively acidity.

## Key Facts
- **Origin**: All major Japanese tea-growing regions
- **Processing**: Steamed, then rolled
- **Flavor**: Marine, seaweed, grassy
- **Appearance**: Long, needle-shaped leaves
- **Best Brew**: 175-185°F (79-85°C), 1-2 minutes

## Processing
Japanese steaming method (80-100°C for 30-90 seconds) halts oxidation at lower temperatures than Chinese stir-frying, resulting in more vegetal, fresh, and bright flavors.
