# The Algebra of Happiness - Scott Galloway

"The Algebra of Happiness by Scott Galloway is a book that explores the mathematical principles behind achieving happiness through success in business and life. It draws parallels between personal finance strategies and cognitive science insights to optimize well-being, offering practical advice for those interested in balancing professional ambition with personal contentment."
