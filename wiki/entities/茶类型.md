# 茶类型

茶类型 refers to the various categories of tea based on processing methods, such as green, black, oolong, white, and pu-erh. Each type exhibits distinct flavor profiles, aromas, and health benefits that appeal to different consumer preferences within the culinary world. Understanding these classifications is essential for both enthusiasts and professionals in tea appreciation and marketing.
