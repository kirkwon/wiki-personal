# Stir-Fry Technique

The stir-fry technique is a quick cooking method that involves rapidly frying small pieces of food in a hot pan with minimal oil while continuously stirring to ensure even heat distribution and prevent burning. This technique preserves texture and nutrients, making it ideal for photographically vibrant dishes and efficient meal preparation, aligning with time-management principles found in personal finance strategies. Additionally, the cognitive science aspect of this method requires attentive multitasking skills to maintain consistent stirring while monitoring food doneness.
