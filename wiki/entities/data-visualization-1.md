# Data Visualization

Data visualization is the graphical representation of information and data using statistical, mathematical or computational techniques to provide an accessible way to see and understand trends, outliers, and patterns in complex datasets. In cooking, it helps analyze recipe ingredients; in photography, it assists in editing color balance; for personal finance, it aids in budget tracking and investment analysis; and within cognitive science, it visualizes neural activity or behavioral data to facilitate comprehension.
