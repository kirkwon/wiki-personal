## Overview

Mapo tofu (麻婆豆腐, Mápó dòufu) is the iconic Sichuan dish of silken tofu in a spicy, numbing, aromatic sauce. Named after "Old Lady Ma" (the elderly widow who supposedly created it), this dish exemplifies the má là (麻辣) flavor profile - numbing Sichuan peppercorns and spicy chilies.

## The Story

**Legend:**
- Created by "Old Lady Ma" (Pockmarked Ma)
- Operated small restaurant in Chengdu
- Pockmarked face from smallpox
- Famous for comforting, affordable dish

**Evolution:**
- Originally simple peasant food
- Became regional specialty
- Now world-renowned
- Many variations exist

## Authentic Ingredients

### The Essentials

| Ingredient | Amount | Purpose |
|------------|--------|---------|
| **Silken tofu** | 1 lb (450g) | Main ingredient |
| **Ground pork** (optional) | 2 oz (60g) | Umami, richness |
| **Sichuan peppercorns** | 1 tbsp | Numbing (ma) |
| **Dried chilies** | 8-10 | Spicy (la) |
| **Fermented broad bean paste** | 2 tbsp | Umami depth |
| **Ginger** | 1 tbsp, minced | Aromatic |
| **Garlic** | 2 cloves, minced | Aromatic |
| **Scallions** | 2 stalks, chopped | Garnish |
| **Soy sauce** | 1 tbsp | Salty, umami |
| **Cooking wine** - Shaoxing | 1 tbsp | Aromatic |
| **Chicken stock** | 1/2 cup | Liquid |
| **Cornstarch slurry** | 1 tsp | Thickener |
| **Sesame oil** | 1 tsp | Finish |
| **Chili oil** | 1 tbsp | Extra spice |

### The "Magic" Ingredients

**Sichuan peppercorns (花椒, Huājiāo)**
- Toast whole in dry wok until fragrant
- Grind fresh (powder loses potency quickly)
- This is the "ma" (numbing) component

**Fermented broad bean paste (豆瓣酱, Dòubànjiàng)**
- Pixian brand preferred
- Sauté until red oil separates
- This is the umami foundation

**Dried chilies (干辣椒, Gān làjiāo)**
- Erjingtiao or similar
- Adds "la" (spiciness)
- Left in dish for continuous heat

## Preparation

### Preparing the Tofu

**Traditional method:**
1. Cut tofu into 1-inch cubes
2. Blanch in boiling water (2-3 minutes)
3. Remove and set aside
4. Keeps tofu from breaking

**Alternative:**
- Use extra-firm tofu
- No blanching needed
- Less authentic texture

### Preparing Aromatics

- Mince ginger
- Mince garlic
- Chop scallions (separate white and green parts)
- Toast and grind Sichuan peppercorns

## Cooking Method

### Step 1: Infuse Oil

1. Heat wok over medium-high heat
2. Add 2 tbsp oil
3. Add Sichuan peppercorns
4. Cook until aromatic (30 seconds)
5. Remove peppercorns (save for later)
6. Keep oil in wok

### Step 2: Bloom Aromatics

1. Add dried chilies to hot oil
2. Cook until slightly darkened (30 seconds)
3. Add fermented broad bean paste
4. Sauté until red oil separates (1-2 minutes)
5. Add ginger and garlic
6. Cook 30 seconds until aromatic

### Step 3: Cook Meat (Optional)

1. Add ground pork
2. Break up and brown (2-3 minutes)
3. Cook until no longer pink

### Step 4: Add Liquid

1. Add cooking wine
2. Add soy sauce
3. Add chicken stock
4. Bring to gentle simmer

### Step 5: Add Tofu

1. Gently add tofu cubes
2. Fold in (don't stir vigorously)
3. Simmer gently (3-5 minutes)
4. Tofu absorbs flavors

### Step 6: Thicken

1. Create cornstarch slurry (cornstarch + water)
2. Stir into simmering sauce
3. Cook until slightly thickened (1 minute)
4. Sauce should coat tofu lightly

### Step 7: Finish

1. Add toasted Sichuan peppercorn powder
2. Add chili oil
3. Add sesame oil
4. Add scallion greens
5. Gently fold in
6. Remove from heat

## Serving

**Traditional:**
- Serve in shallow bowl
- Serve with steamed rice
- Garnish with extra scallions
---

**Modern:**
- Serve in individual bowls
- Add more chili oil on top
- Serve with plain congee

## Variations

### Vegetarian Mapo Tofu

**Omit pork**
- Increase fermented bean paste slightly
- Use vegetable stock
- Same technique

### Seafood Mapo Tofu

**Replace pork with:**
- Minced shrimp
- Dried scallops
- Clams (shell-on)

### Mapo Eggplant

**Replace tofu with:**
- Japanese eggplant
- Cut into thick rounds
- Same sauce

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Tofu breaking | Use silken tofu, blanch first, gentle handling |
| Not numbing enough | Add more Sichuan peppercorn powder |
| Not spicy enough | Add more dried chilies or chili oil |
| Sauce too thin | Add more cornstarch slurry |
| Sauce too thick | Add more stock |
| Dish not aromatic | Sauté bean paste until oil separates |

## Wok Hei Technique

**For authentic mapo tofu:**
- Preheat wok until smoking
- Use high heat throughout
- Work quickly
- Stir-fry briefly, let flavors develop
- See [[Gastronomy/Concepts/Wok Hei]] for detailed technique
