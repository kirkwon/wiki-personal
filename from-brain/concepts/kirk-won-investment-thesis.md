# Kirk Won Investment Thesis

## Core Premise

Bonds/debt are NOT a reliable equity counterweight. In the post-2022 monetary regime, bonds and equities have become too correlated to serve as diversifiers. The traditional 60/40 portfolio is structurally flawed for the current environment.

## Rationale

The 2022 selloff demonstrated that both equities and bonds can decline simultaneously when inflation is sticky and central banks are forced into aggressive tightening. This breaks the fundamental assumption of 60/40 — that bonds zig when equities zag.

With interest rates near a floor and inflation persistent, duration is now a risk factor, not a hedge. The 40-year bond bull market (1982-2022) that made 60/40 work is over.

## Preferred Hedges

1. **Sector rotation** — Rotate into defensive/value sectors during equity drawdowns rather than buying bonds for duration exposure
2. **Commodities** — Real assets that benefit from the same inflation forces that hurt financial assets
3. **Cash** — Capital preservation and optionality (dry powder to deploy at dislocation)

## Risk Awareness

- Cash is not risk-free: currency debasement and inflation erode purchasing power over time
- Cash is tactical, not permanent — held for optionality, not long-term store of value
- Commodities carry volatility and roll-cost dynamics

## Portfolio Implications

- Dashboard IPS targets should reflect this thesis, not generic 35/30/15/10 splits
- Drift alerts based on bond-heavy targets are noise, not signal
