### Contention: Can LLMs self-correct at inference time?

**Position A:** Spectral Origins paper argues autoregressive architectures have spectral blind spots that make self-correction unreliable at inference time.

**Position B:** Self-Improving is Often Sudden paper shows that enlightenment-style finetuning can achieve rapid improvement, suggesting training-time interventions work where inference-time doesn't.

**Assessment:** Both may be correct — inference-time self-correction has architectural limits, but training-time approaches can overcome them. The evidence suggests the answer depends on whether you update weights.
