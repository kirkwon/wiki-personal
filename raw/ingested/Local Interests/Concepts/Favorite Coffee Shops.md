## Criteria for Good Coffee Shop

### What Matters Most
1. **Quality of coffee** - Beans, roasting, preparation
2. **Consistency** - Same experience every visit
3. **Atmosphere** - Vibe, comfort, noise level
4. **Service** - Friendly, efficient, knowledgeable
5. **Convenience** - Location, hours, parking

### Deal-Breakers
- Burnt coffee
- Dirty bathrooms
- Rude service
- Poor WiFi (for work spots)
- Uncomfortable seating

## Discovery Process

### Finding New Spots
- **Local recommendations** - Ask friends, check neighborhood groups
- **Coffee apps** - Yelp, Google Maps, coffee-specific apps
- **Roaster lists** - Check local roasters' café maps
- **Walking/biking** - Explore neighborhoods, spot cafés
- **Social media** - Instagram recommendations, coffee hashtags

### Evaluation Checklist
When trying a new café:
- [ ] **Coffee quality** - Try pour-over and espresso
- [ ] **Atmosphere** - Is it a place I'd return?
- [ ] **Service** - Were they friendly/knowledgeable?
- [ ] **Prices** - Reasonable for quality?
- [ ] **Amenities** - WiFi, outlets, seating?
- [ ] **Uniqueness** - What makes it special?
