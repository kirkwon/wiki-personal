# The Algebra of Wealth


## Quick Reference

### Field**: [[personal-finance]]
### Type**: Wealth Framework
### Purpose**: Mathematical approach to building wealth
### Key Principles**: Focus, stoicism, time, diversification
### Related Concepts**: [[the-psychology-of-money]], [[the-index-card-rules]], [[career-strategy]]



## Summary
The Algebra of Wealth proposes that wealth building follows a formula: **Wealth = (Focus + Stoicism) × Time × Diversification**. This framework emphasizes that building wealth requires both psychological discipline (focus + stoicism) and mathematical principles (time × diversification).

## The Wealth Formula

### Wealth = (Focus + Stoicism) × Time × Diversification

#### The Multipliers: Time × Diversification
These are mathematical multipliers that compound your efforts.

#### The Additive Components: Focus + Stoicism
These are psychological foundations that enable everything else.

## Component 1: Focus

### Definition
Concentrating your energy on high-leverage activities that actually move the needle on wealth building.

### The Focus Trifecta

#### 1. Career Focus
**Why:** Your career is your primary wealth engine

**Strategy:**
- Choose high-paying industries (tech, finance, healthcare)
- Develop rare and valuable skills
- Maximize income in your 20s and 30s
- Switch jobs every 2-3 years for 15-20% raises

**High-Income Skills:**
- Technical skills (coding, data science, AI)
- Sales and business development
- Leadership and management
- Specialized expertise

**Career Optimization:**
- Geographic arbitrage (move to high-paying cities)
- Industry arbitrage (choose lucrative fields)
- Negotiate everything (salary, bonus, equity)

#### 2. Financial Focus
**Why:** Attention to financial details saves and makes money

**Key Areas:**
- **Tax optimization:** Maximize deductions, use tax-advantaged accounts
- **Fee minimization:** Low-cost index funds, negotiate recurring bills
- **Asset allocation:** Right risk for your stage of life
- **Automated systems:** Remove willpower from equation

**Financial Focus Checklist:**
- Review all subscriptions monthly
- Negotiate insurance annually
- Check credit reports quarterly
- Rebalance portfolio yearly
- Track spending weekly

#### 3. Relationship Focus
**Why:** Your network determines your net worth

**Strategy:**
- Build relationships with people ahead of you
- Help others without expecting immediate return
- Attend industry events consistently
- Maintain weak ties (acquaintances often provide best opportunities)

**Network Quality > Quantity:**
- 5 close mentors
- 20 active professional relationships
- 100+ LinkedIn connections (keep them warm)

### The Distraction Economy
**Problem:** Modern life is designed to steal your focus

**Focus Killers:**
- Social media (designed for addiction)
- News (designed for anxiety, not information)
- Consumerism (designed for dissatisfaction)
- Busy work (feels productive, isn't)

**Focus Builders:**
- Time blocking (deep work hours)
- Digital minimalism (limit phone/apps)
- Say "no" more often
- Focus on 3 big goals per year

### Action Items for Focus
- [ ] Identify your 3 highest-impact activities
- [ ] Eliminate/reduce bottom 20% of activities
- [ ] Create "deep work" blocks (2-4 hours daily)
- [ ] Build 5 relationships with people ahead of you
- [ ] Learn one high-income skill this year

## Component 2: Stoicism

### Definition
Emotional discipline that prevents bad financial decisions and keeps you focused on long-term goals.

### The Stoic Trifecta

#### 1. Emotional Detachment from Money
**Why:** Money decisions driven by emotion are usually wrong

**Practice:**
- View money as tool, not status symbol
- Don't compare yourself to others
- Separate net worth from self-worth
- Focus on what you control, not market returns

**Stoic Money Mindset:**
- Enough is enough (define it, live it)
- Voluntary discomfort (practice living with less)
- Negative visualization (imagine losing everything, appreciate what you have)

#### 2. Resilience in Setbacks
**Why:** Markets and careers have downturns; stoicism prevents panic

**Market Crashes:**
- Expect them (they happen every 5-10 years)
- Don't sell in panic (buy more if anything)
- Remember: time in market > timing market

**Career Setbacks:**
- Layoffs happen (have 6-month emergency fund)
- Industries change (develop transferable skills)
- Failure is data (learn and move on)

**Stoic Response:**
- Control what you can (savings rate, skill development)
- Accept what you can't (market returns, economy)
- Focus on process, not outcomes

#### 3. Delayed Gratification
**Why:** Wealth building requires sacrificing now for later

**Practice:**
- Save first, spend later (reverse typical behavior)
- Wait 72 hours before purchases over \$100
- Practice "no spend days" regularly
- Calculate hours worked vs. cost of items

**The Time Value of Money:**
- \$1 saved at 25 = \$11.58 at 65 (7% return)
- \$1 saved at 45 = \$3.38 at 65 (same return)
- Early savings = exponentially more impact

### Stoicism Exercises

#### Daily
- Morning reflection: What must I accept today?
- Evening review: Where did I let emotions rule?
- Gratitude practice: What do I already have?

#### Weekly
- Negative visualization: What if I lost everything?
- Memento mori: Remember I'll die, how do I want to live?
- Voluntary discomfort: Go without something you want

#### Monthly
- Review spending: What was driven by emotion?
- Audit goals: Am I focused on what matters?
- Relationship check: Am I comparing myself to others?

### Action Items for Stoicism
- [ ] Define "enough" for your life
- [ ] Practice one voluntary discomfort weekly
- [ ] Calculate cost of items in "hours worked"
- [ ] Review 3 worst-case scenarios (and plan for them)
- [ ] Express gratitude for 5 things daily

## Component 3: Time

### Definition
The mathematical multiplier that turns modest savings into substantial wealth through compounding.

### The Power of Compounding

#### The Math
- \$1,000/year starting at 25 = \$228K at 65 (7% return)
- \$1,000/year starting at 35 = \$114K at 65 (same return)
- Starting 10 years earlier = 2x the final result

#### The Rule of 72
Money doubles at: 72 ÷ interest rate = years to double
- 7% return: doubles every 10.3 years
- 10% return: doubles every 7.2 years

**Example:**
- \$10K at 25, 7% return
- Age 35: \$20K
- Age 45: \$40K
- Age 55: \$80K
- Age 65: \$160K

### Maximizing Time Advantage

#### Start Early (Even Small Amounts)
- \$50/month at 25 = \$121K at 65
- \$500/month at 35 = \$204K at 65
- Early start > larger amount later

#### Never Interrupt Compounding
- Don't cash out investments for lifestyle
- Borrow against assets, don't sell
- Use tax-advantaged accounts (no annual taxes = more compounding)

#### Extend Your Time Horizon
- Work longer (builds more wealth, shorter retirement)
- Delay Social Security (8% increase per year after 67)
- Invest for growth, not income (until retirement)

### The Time Value of Money Decisions

#### Career vs. Leisure
- **Year 10-20:** Income maximization phase (focus on career)
- **Year 20-30:** Balance career with life
- **Year 30+:** Enjoy wealth, work less

#### Spending vs. Investing
- \$5 latte daily = \$1,825/year = \$72K over 20 years (if invested)
- Not about deprivation, but awareness

#### Debt vs. Investment
- High-interest debt: Pay off immediately (guaranteed return)
- Low-interest debt: May invest instead (math says yes, psychology says maybe)

### Action Items for Time
- [ ] Start investing TODAY (even \$50)
- [ ] Calculate your retirement number (25x annual expenses)
- [ ] Extend time horizon (work to 67, delay Social Security)
- [ ] Never cash out for lifestyle (borrow against assets instead)
- [ ] Use tax-advantaged accounts (maximize compounding)

## Component 4: Diversification

---
### Definition
Spreading risk across multiple assets, income sources, and skills to protect against downturns in any one area.

### The Four Types of Diversification

#### 1. Asset Diversification
**Why:** Different assets perform differently at different times

**Asset Classes:**
- **Stocks:** Growth, high volatility
- **Bonds:** Stability, income
- **Real Estate:** Inflation hedge, tax advantages
- **Cash:** Safety, opportunity fund

**Target Allocations by Age:**
- **20s:** 90% stocks, 10% bonds (maximize growth)
- **30s:** 80% stocks, 20% bonds
- **40s:** 70% stocks, 30% bonds
- **50s:** 60% stocks, 40% bonds
- **60s:** 50% stocks, 50% bonds

**Simple Approach:**
- Target-date fund for your retirement year
- Automatically adjusts allocation as you age

#### 2. Geographic Diversification
**Why:** Different countries perform differently

**Strategy:**
- 70-80% US (largest, most stable market)
- 20-30% International (developed + emerging)
- Real estate: Local + REITs (national)

**Example:**
- Total Stock Market Index (US)
- Total International Stock Index
- Real Estate (primary residence + REITs)

#### 3. Income Diversification
**Why:** Job loss, industry decline, economic shifts happen

**Income Sources:**
- **Primary career:** Main income source
- **Side hustle:** 10-20% of primary income
- **Investments:** Dividends, interest, rent
- **Skills:** Freelancing, consulting

**Target Structure:**
- 70% primary career
- 15% investments
- 10% side hustle
- 5% other

#### 4. Skill Diversification
**Why:** AI, automation, economic shifts can make skills obsolete

**Strategy:**
- **Technical skills:** Coding, data analysis, AI tools
- **Soft skills:** Leadership, communication, sales
- **Industry knowledge:** Your specific domain expertise
- **Financial skills:** Investing, taxes, negotiation

**Learning Plan:**
- One new technical skill every 2 years
- Read 20+ books per year
- Attend 2-3 industry conferences annually
- Build network across industries

### Diversification Mistakes

#### ❌ Over-Diversification
- Too many accounts to manage
- Diworsification (buying everything = market return with fees)
- **Fix:** 3-5 fund portfolio is plenty

#### ❌ False Diversification
- 10 different tech stocks (not diversified)
- All S&P 500 funds (all same underlying assets)
- **Fix:** Different asset classes, not just different funds

#### ❌ Home Bias
- All investments in home country/region
- Misses global opportunities
- **Fix:** 20-30% international allocation

### Action Items for Diversification
- [ ] Audit current portfolio (calculate actual allocation)
- [ ] Develop side income (freelancing, consulting, content)
- [ ] Learn skills outside your primary field
- [ ] Own 3+ asset classes (stocks, bonds, real estate)
- [ ] Build network across multiple industries

## Putting It All Together

### The Wealth-Building Timeline

#### Age 20-30: Foundation
**Focus:** Career + Stoicism
- Maximize income through career growth
- Live below means (save 20-50%)
- Build emergency fund (6 months expenses)
- Learn investing basics
- Start investing in index funds

#### Age 30-40: Acceleration
**Focus:** All components, emphasis on income
- Career peak earning years
- Maximize tax-advantaged accounts
- Build side income
- Increase investment rate to 30-50%
- Learn advanced strategies (real estate, etc.)

#### Age 40-50: Diversification
**Focus:** Asset + Income diversification
- Multiple income sources
- Real estate investments
- Business ownership or equity
- Protect wealth (insurance, legal planning)
- Teach kids about money

#### Age 50-60: Optimization
**Focus:** Time + Diversification
- Maximize all retirement accounts
- Catch-up contributions
- Pay off mortgage
- Delay Social Security to 70
- Plan retirement timeline

#### Age 60+: Decumulation
**Focus:** Stoicism + Diversification
- Convert portfolio to income
- Required minimum distributions (RMDs)
- Tax-efficient withdrawal strategy
- Enjoy wealth, leave legacy

### Common Pitfalls

#### ❌ Lifestyle Inflation
- Spending increases with income
- Solution: Save 50% of every raise

#### ❌ Comparison Trap
- Comparing to outliers (not the norm)
- Solution: Compare to your past self

#### ❌ Timing the Market
- Trying to buy low, sell high
- Solution: Dollar-cost average, stay invested

#### ❌ Ignoring Fees
- High fees destroy returns
- Solution: Index funds, negotiate everything

#### ❌ Single Point of Failure
- One job, one skill, one investment
- Solution: Diversify everything

## Key Takeaways

1. **Focus determines your income** - Career and attention to details matter most

2. **Stoicism protects your wealth** - Emotional discipline prevents bad decisions

3. **Time multiplies everything** - Start early, never interrupt compounding

4. **Diversification reduces risk** - Spread bets across assets, income, skills

5. **All components are necessary** - Weak in any area reduces total wealth

6. **Wealth is a marathon** - Consistency over decades beats intensity for months

7. **You're the formula** - Not a victim of circumstances, but product of daily choices
