# Alternative Investments


## Quick Reference

### Field**: [[personal-clients]]
### Type**: Asset Class
### Purpose**: Diversification beyond stocks/bonds, inflation protection
### Key Asset Classes**: Real estate, private equity, commodities, collectibles
### Related Concepts**: [[risk-management]], [[retirement-planning]], [[options-trading]]



## Summary
Alternative investments are asset classes beyond traditional stocks, bonds, and cash. Used for diversification, inflation protection, and return enhancement. Typically less liquid, higher fees, more complex, but potentially uncorrelated with traditional markets. Allocate 5-20% of portfolio depending on risk tolerance.

## Types of Alternative Investments

### 1. Real Estate

#### Direct Ownership
**Residential:**
- Rental properties
- Vacation rentals (Airbnb)
- House hacking (live + rent units)

**Commercial:**
- Office buildings
- Retail spaces
- Warehouses

**Pros:**
- Tangible asset
- Leverage (mortgage)
- Tax benefits (depreciation, deductions)
- Potential for cash flow

**Cons:**
- Illiquid (months to sell)
- Active management required
- Concentrated risk (single property)
- Transaction costs (6%+)

#### REITs (Real Estate Investment Trusts)
**Definition:** Companies that own/finance income-producing real estate

**Types:**
- **Equity REITs:** Own properties (office, retail, residential)
- **Mortgage REITs:** Finance mortgages (riskier)
- **Hybrid REITs:** Both

**Pros:**
- Liquid (trade like stocks)
- Diversified (many properties)
- Regular dividends (required to distribute 90%)
- Professional management

**Cons:**
- Interest rate sensitivity
- Leverage on balance sheet
- Management fees
- Limited control over properties

#### REIGs (Real Estate Investment Groups)
**Definition:** Group of investors pooling money for deals

**Pros:**
- Larger deals than individual
- Professional sponsor
- Passive investment

**Cons:**
- Illiquid (locked up 3-7 years)
- Limited liquidity
- Sponsor risk
- Fees

### 2. Private Equity

#### Definition
Investments in private companies (not publicly traded). Includes venture capital (early-stage), growth equity (expanding), and buyouts (mature).

#### Pros
- Illiquidity premium (potentially higher returns)
- Direct ownership
- Active value creation
- Not subject to daily market volatility

#### Cons
- Highly illiquid (5-10 year lockup)
- Information asymmetry (less transparent)
- Manager risk
- High fees (2% management + 20% performance)

#### Access
- **Minimum investment:** $100k-$1M+
- **Accredited investors only:** $200k income OR $1M net worth
- **Platforms:** AngelList, Fundrise, RealtyMogul, crowdfunding sites

### 3. Commodities

#### Physical Commodities
**Types:**
- **Precious metals:** Gold, silver, platinum
- **Energy:** Oil, natural gas
- **Agriculture:** Corn, wheat, cattle
- **Base metals:** Copper, aluminum

**Pros:**
- Real assets (inflation hedge)
- Tangible ownership
- Crisis protection (gold in crashes)

**Cons:**
- No cash flow/dividend
- Storage costs
- No earnings growth
- Volatile prices

#### Commodity ETFs/Futures
**ETFs:**
- **GLD** (Gold ETF)
- **USO** (Oil ETF)
- **DBA** (Agriculture ETF)

**Futures:**
- Direct commodity exposure
- Leverage (small margin)
- Professional tool (high risk)

### 4. Collectibles

#### Types
- **Art & antiques:** Paintings, sculptures, furniture
- **Watches & jewelry:** Luxury brands, precious metals
- **Coins & stamps:** Numismatics, philately
- **Wine & whiskey:** Rare vintages, aged spirits
- **Sports memorabilia:** Cards, autographs, equipment

#### Pros
- Enjoyment value (can display/use)
- Potential for appreciation
- Niche markets (less competition)
- Tangible assets

#### Cons
- Highly illiquid
- No intrinsic value (subjective)
- Storage/insurance costs
- Authenticity/fraud risk
- No income/cash flow

### 5. Cryptocurrency
**Definition:** Digital assets using blockchain technology

**Major cryptocurrencies:**
- **Bitcoin:** Digital gold, store of value
- **Ethereum:** Smart contract platform
- **Stablecoins:** Pegged to USD (USDC, DAI)

**Pros:**
- Diversified from traditional markets
- High return potential
- Accessible (no accredited investor requirement)
- Decentralized

**Cons:**
- Extremely volatile
- Regulatory uncertainty
- Security risk (hacks, lost keys)
- No fundamentals (speculative)
- Tax complexity

### 6. Farmland and Timber
**Farmland:**
- Agricultural land leased to farmers
- Income from rent + crop appreciation
- Inflation hedge (farmland prices rise with food prices)

**Timber:**
- Forest land
- Income from timber harvest
- Biological growth (trees grow)
- Inflation hedge (commodity exposure)

**Pros:**
- Real, tangible assets
- Inflation protection
- Income + appreciation
- Non-correlated with stocks

**Cons:**
- Very illiquid (years to sell)
- Specialized knowledge required
- Location-specific risk (weather, pests)
- Management complexity

---
### 7. Infrastructure
**Definition:** Investments in essential public services

**Types:**
- **Toll roads:** Bridge/road tolls
- **Utilities:** Pipelines, power grids
- **Ports:** Shipping terminals
- **Communication:** Cell towers, fiber

**Pros:**
- Stable cash flows (monopolies/oligopolies)
- Inflation-protected (regulated rates)
- Long-term contracts
- Non-cyclical

**Cons:**
- Political/regulatory risk
- Long investment horizon
- Capital intensive
- Limited growth

## Allocation Guidelines

### Traditional Allocation
| Risk Profile | Traditional | Alternatives | Total |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|--------|
| **Conservative** | 90-95% | 5-10% | 100% |
| **Moderate** | 80-90% | 10-20% | 100% |
| **Aggressive** | 75-85% | 15-25% | 100% |

### Alternative Allocation
**Diversify within alternatives:**
- **Real estate:** 40-60% of alternatives
- **Private equity:** 20-40%
- **Commodities:** 10-20%
- **Collectibles/crypto:** 0-10% (speculative portion)

## Risk Considerations

### 1. Illiquidity Risk
**Problem:** Cannot sell quickly or at fair price

**Examples:**
- Real estate: 6-12 months to sell
- Private equity: 5-10 year lockup
- Collectibles: Find buyer, negotiate, verify

**Mitigation:**
- Don't need the money soon
- Keep in long-term portfolio
- Maintain adequate emergency fund

### 2. Valuation Risk
**Problem:** Hard to determine fair value

**Examples:**
- No daily pricing (unlike stocks)
- Appraisals are subjective
- Comparable sales may not exist
- Opacity in private markets

**Mitigation:**
- Use professional appraisers
- Research comparable sales
- Understand market
- Don't overpay

### 3. Concentration Risk
**Problem:** Alternatives are concentrated bets

**Examples:**
- Single property = 100% of real estate exposure
- Single private equity fund = 100% of private equity
- Gold = 100% of commodity exposure

**Mitigation:**
- Diversify within asset class
- Multiple properties/funds
- Multiple commodities
- Multiple collectibles

### 4. Manager Risk
**Problem:** Rely on skill/expertise of managers

**Examples:**
- Private equity sponsor performance
- Real estate manager selection
- REIT management team

**Mitigation:**
- Due diligence on manager
- Track record (not just marketing)
- Reputation in industry
- Fee transparency

### 5. Regulatory Risk
**Problem:** Laws/regulations change value

**Examples:**
- Rent control (real estate)
- Crypto bans/regulation
- Environmental regulations (timber/mining)
- Tax law changes

**Mitigation:**
- Stay informed on regulation
- Geographic diversification
- Legal compliance
- Exit strategies

## Tax Treatment

### Real Estate
- **Depreciation:** Annual deduction (27.5 years residential)
- **1031 Exchange:** Defer gains when selling property
- **Rental income:** Ordinary tax rate
- **Capital gains:** Depends on holding period

### Collectibles
- **Long-term gains:** 28% maximum (not favorable 20%)
- **Short-term gains:** Ordinary rate
- **No 1031 exchanges** (no like-kind deferral)

### Private Equity
- **K-1 Form:** Reporting investment income
- **Carried interest:** Performance allocation taxed as capital gains
- **Qualifying dividends:** Some distributions taxed favorably

### Cryptocurrency
- **Property:** Taxed as property (capital gains)
- **FIFO/Specific ID:** Choose cost basis method
- **Tax loss harvesting:** Offset other gains
- **Reporting:** Require detailed records

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Overallocating to Alternatives
- Alternatives should be 5-20%, not 50%+
- Higher fees and illiquidity hurt returns
- Rule: Core in traditional, satellite in alternatives

### ❌ Understanding the Illiquidity
- Buying alternative investments with money needed soon
- Forced to sell at bad price when liquidity needed
- Rule: Money you don't need for 5+ years

### ❌ Chasing Returns
- Past performance doesn't guarantee future returns
- Private equity past 10 years ≠ next 10 years
- Research manager thoroughly

### ❌ Underestimating Fees
- 2% management fee matters over 10 years
- Performance fees (20%) eat into returns
- Calculate net returns after fees

### ❌ Concentrated Bets
- All in one property/fund/crypto
- Single point of failure
- Diversify within asset class

### ❌ Not Understanding Valuation
- Paying too much for real estate/collectibles
- No comparable pricing in private markets
- Get professional appraisals

## Best Practices

1. **Start small** - 5-10% of portfolio maximum
2. **Diversify** - Multiple alternatives, not concentrated
3. **Due diligence** - Research manager, platform, track record
4. **Understand liquidity** - Can you afford to be locked up?
5. **Watch fees** - High fees compound against you
6. **Tax planning** - Understand tax consequences
7. **Long-term horizon** - Alternatives are illiquid
8. **Don't chase** - Past performance ≠ future
9. **Stay informed** - Regulation, market changes
10. **Emergency fund first** - Don't lock up money you need

## When to Use Alternatives

### **Appropriate for:**
- Investors with adequate capital ($100k+)
- Long time horizon (5+ years)
- Comfortable with illiquidity
- Seeking diversification from stocks/bonds
- Inflation protection needed

### **Inappropriate for:**
- Short-term money (needed <5 years)
- Small portfolios (<$25k)
- Risk-averse individuals
- Need liquidity
- Inadequate emergency fund

## Alternative to Alternatives

### **Traditional Diversification**
- **International stocks:** Low correlation with US
- **Small-cap stocks:** Different risk profile than large-cap
- **Sector rotation:** Different cycle timing
- **Factor investing:** Value, momentum, quality

### **Low-Cost Index Funds**
- **Total Market:** VT, VTI, SWTSX
- **International:** VXUS, IXUS
- **Real Estate (liquid):** VNQ, SCHH, RWO
- **Commodities (liquid):** DBC, GCC, PDBC

**Advantages:**
- Liquid (trade daily)
- Low fees (0.03-0.50%)
- Transparent
- No manager risk
- Easier to understand
