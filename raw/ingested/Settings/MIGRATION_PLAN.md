## 🔐 Sensitivity Classification for Personal Vault

### **Personal Vault Acceptable Content:**
- ✅ Personal client information
- ✅ Gastronomy knowledge (even if technical)
- ✅ Local business information
- ✅ Personal preferences and workflows
- ✅ Red-teaming techniques (if too sensitive for public)
- ✅ Internal tools and processes
- ✅ Personal correspondence notes

### **Content to Keep Private (Not Even in Personal Vault):**
- ❌ API keys, tokens, credentials
- ❌ Private financial information
- ❌ Legal documents
- ❌ Highly sensitive personal information
