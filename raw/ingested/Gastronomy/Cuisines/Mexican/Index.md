# Mexican Cuisine

## Overview

Mexican cuisine (Cocina mexicana) is one of the world's great culinary traditions, known for its complex flavors, vibrant colors, and deep cultural roots. A UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, Mexican food ranges from simple street tacos to elaborate moles, blending indigenous and Spanish influences.

## Core Philosophy

### Maíz (Corn) - The Sacred Grain

**Foundation of Mexican cuisine:**

- **Tortillas** - Fresh, handmade
- **Tamales** - Steamed corn dough parcels
- **Atole** - Corn-based drink
- **Nixtamalization** - Corn treated with alkali (cal) for nutrition and flavor

### Chili Peppers (Chiles) - The Soul

**Heat + flavor:**

| Chili | Heat Level | Use |
|-------|------------|-----|
| **Jalapeño** - Medium | Salsas, pickled |
| **Serrano** - Hot | Fresh salsas |
| **Poblano** - Mild | Rellenos, moles |
| **Ancho** (dried poblano) | Mild | Moles, sauces |
| **Guajillo** - Mild-medium | Moles, adobo |
| **Arbol** - Hot | Salsas, hot sauces |
| **Chipotle** (smoked jalapeño) | Medium | Adobo, sauces |

### Ancient Techniques

- **Nixtamalization** - Treating corn with cal (slaked lime)
- **Molcajete** - Stone mortar and pestle
- **Comal** - Griddle for tortillas
- **Barbacoa** - Pit-roasted meat

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Using pre-ground spices | Toast and grind whole spices |
| Not making fresh tortillas | Fresh masa makes all the difference |
| Under-toasting chiles | Toast until fragrant, not burned |
| Not developing flavors | Layer chiles, spices, let meld |

## Practice

1. **Make fresh tortillas** - Masa, press, cook on comal
2. **Try a mole** - Start with mole poblano or verde
3. **Master carnitas** - Slow-roasted pork
4. **Host taco night** - Various fillings, fresh tortillas

**Remember:** Mexican cuisine is ancient and complex. Respect the ingredients, master the techniques, and embrace the heat.
