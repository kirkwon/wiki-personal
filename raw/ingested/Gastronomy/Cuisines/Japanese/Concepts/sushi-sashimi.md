## Overview

Sushi (寿司) is vinegar-seasoned rice combined with various toppings, while sashimi (刺身) is fresh raw fish without rice. Both celebrate the purity and freshness of ingredients, requiring exceptional quality seafood and precise technique.

## Types of Sushi

### Nigiri (握り)

**Hand-pressed sushi**

**Structure:**
- **Shari** - Vinegared rice, hand-pressed
- **Neta** - Topping (fish, egg, vegetable)

**Common toppings:**
- **Maguro** - Tuna (lean, medium, fatty)
- **Salmon** - Salmon (farmed or wild)
- **Hamachi** - Yellowtail
- **Ebi** - Shrimp (boiled)
- **Unagi** - Eel (grilled with sauce)
- **Tamago** - Sweet egg omelet
- **Ikura** - Salmon roe
- **Uni** - Sea urchin

### Maki (巻き)

**Rolled sushi**

**Types:**

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Hosomaki** - Thin roll, 1 ingredient |
| **Futomaki** - Thick roll, multiple ingredients |
| **Uramaki** - Inside-out roll (rice outside, nori inside) |
| **Temaki** - Hand roll (cone shape) |

**Common fillings:**
- Cucumber (kyūri)
- Avocado (abokado)
- Tuna (maguro)
- Salmon (samon)
- Crab stick (kanikama)
- Egg (tamago)

### Temaki (手巻き)

**Hand rolls**

**Characteristics:**
- Cone-shaped
- Diners make their own
- Casual, fun

### Chirashi (ちらし)

**Scattered sushi**

**Components:**
- Bowl of sushi rice
- Various sashimi toppings
- Garnishes
- Festive, celebratory

### Oshi (押し)

**Pressed sushi**

**Characteristics:**
- Rice and toppings pressed in box
- Sliced into rectangles
- Kansai-style

## Sashimi (刺身)

**Raw fish without rice**

**Types of fish:**

| Fish | Season | Characteristics |
|------|--------|----------------|
| **Tuna (Maguro)** - Year-round | Firm, various fat levels |
| **Salmon (Sake)** - Year-round | Rich, fatty |
| **Yellowtail (Hamachi)** - Spring, Fall | Buttery, mild |
| **Squid (Ika)** - Year-round | Chewy, sweet |
| **Octopus (Tako)** - Year-round | Chewy, nutty |
| **Mackerel (Saba)** - Fall | Rich, oily |
| **Sea bream (Tai)** - Spring, Fall | Delicate, sweet |
| **Flounder (Hirame)** - Winter | Delicate, subtle |
| **Horse mackerel (Aji)** - Summer | Rich, oily |
| **Salmon roe (Ikura)** - Fall | Briny, pops |

**Preparation:**
- Very sharp knife (yanagiba)
- Cut against grain
- Angle cut (30-45°)
- Slice immediately before serving

## Sushi Rice (Shari, 酢飯)

### The Foundation

**Perfect sushi rice is everything**

### Rice Selection

| Type | Characteristics |
|------|----------------|
| **Koshihikari** - Most popular, premium |
| **Sasanishiki** - Lighter, easier to handle |
| **Akitakomachi** - Northern variety, firm |
---

**Ratio:**
- 1 cup rice = 1.2 cups water (for sushi rice)

### Seasoning (Sushi Vinegar, 酢)

**Traditional ratio:**
- Rice vinegar: 200ml
- Sugar: 60g (3 tbsp)
- Salt: 10g (1 tsp)

**Alternative (store-bought):**
- Sushi vinegar (sushi-zu)
- Ready to use

### Cooking Process

**Steps:**
1. **Wash rice** - Until water runs clear (3-4 times)
2. **Soak rice** - 30 minutes
3. **Cook rice** - Rice cooker (1:1.2 ratio)
4. **Rest rice** - 10-15 minutes
5. **Season rice** - Add sushi vinegar while hot
6. **Cool rice** - Fan while mixing
7. **Use immediately** - Best at room temperature

**Mixing technique:**
- Use wooden tub (hangiri)
- Use cutting motion (cut, don't smash)
- Fan to cool
- Cover with damp cloth

## Quality Fish

### Freshness Indicators

**Visual:**
- Bright, clear eyes
- Red gills
- Firm flesh
- No off odors

**Sushi-grade fish:**
- Frozen to kill parasites (FDA requirement)
- Handled properly
- Fresh when frozen

### Buying Fish

**Where to buy:**
- Japanese markets
- Specialty fishmongers
- "Sushi-grade" labeled fish

**What to avoid:**
- Regular grocery store fish
- Previously frozen then thawed
- Fish sitting out at room temperature

## Garnishes and Condiments

### Wasabi (わさび)

**Japanese horseradish**

**Fresh:**
- Grate fresh wasabi root
- Delicate, complex flavor
- Use sparingly

**Paste:**
- Common substitute
- Stronger, simpler flavor
- Mix with soy sauce

### Gari (ガリ)

**Pickled ginger**

**Purpose:**
- Palate cleanser
- Anti-bacterial
- Digestive aid

### Soy Sauce (醤油, Shōyu)

**Dipping:**
- Light soy sauce preferred
- Don't drown nigiri
- Dip fish side, not rice

### Daikon Oroshi (大根おろし)

**Grated daikon radish**

**Use with:**
- Sashimi
- Grilled fish

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Rice too warm or cold | Serve at room temperature |
| Over-seasoned rice | Add less vinegar next time |
| Drowning nigiri in soy sauce | Light dip, fish side only |
| Using dull knife | Sharpen before use |
| Not using sushi-grade fish | Buy from reputable source |
| Making sushi rice too sticky | Rinse rice more thoroughly |

## Making Sushi at Home

### Maki Roll (Beginner)

**Steps:**
1. Place nori rough side up
2. Spread rice (leave 1-inch border)
3. Add fillings in center
4. Roll tight using makisu
5. Wet seal with water
6. Cut with wet knife

### Nigiri (Intermediate)

**Steps:**
1. Wet hands with water
2. Form rice ball (oval)
3. Add wasabi (optional)
4. Place topping
5. Press gently

### Sashimi (Advanced)

**Steps:**
1. Start with sushi-grade fish
2. Chill fish before cutting
3. Use sharp yanagiba knife
4. Cut against grain, at angle
5. Serve immediately

1. **Master sushi rice** - Perfect shari, essential foundation
2. **Make maki rolls** - California, cucumber, spicy tuna
3. **Try nigiri** - Start with tamago, ebi, maguro

**Remember:** Sushi is about simplicity and precision. Quality ingredients and proper technique are everything.
