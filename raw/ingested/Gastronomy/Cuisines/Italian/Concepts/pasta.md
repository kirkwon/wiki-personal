# Italian Pasta

## Overview

Pasta is the foundation of Italian cuisine - versatile, affordable, and beloved worldwide. From long strands to short shapes, fresh to dried, each pasta shape has its ideal sauce and regional tradition.

## Types of Pasta

### Fresh vs. Dried

| Type | Characteristics | Use |
|------|----------------|-----|
| **Fresh (pasta fresca)** | Soft, tender, cooks quickly | Special occasions, stuffed pasta |
| **Dried (pasta secca)** | Firm, al dente, shelf-stable | Everyday cooking |

### Long Pasta

| Pasta | Characteristics | Best Sauce |
|-------|----------------|------------|
| **Spaghetti** - Round, thin | Tomato, oil-based |
| **Linguine** - Flat, thin | Seafood, clam sauces |
| **Fettuccine** - Flat, wide | Creamy sauces |
| **Tagliatelle** - Flat, wider | Meat ragù |
| **Pappardelle** - Very wide | Game ragù |
| **Bucatini** - Thick, hollow | Amatriciana, carbonara |

### Short Pasta

| Pasta | Characteristics | Best Sauce |
|-------|----------------|------------|
| **Penne** - Angled cut, hollow | Chunky tomato |
| **Rigatoni** - Wide, ridged | Meat ragù, baked |
| **Ziti** - Smooth, straight | Baked ziti |
| **Fusilli** - Corkscrew | Pesto, cream sauces |
| **Orecchiette** - "Little ears" | Broccoli rabe, sausage |
| **Conchiglie** - Shells | Chunky sauces, baked |

### Stuffed Pasta

| Pasta | Filling | Region |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Ravioli** - Square, various | Cheese, meat, spinach | Nationwide |
| **Tortellini** - Ring-shaped | Meat, cheese | Emilia-Romagna |
| **Agnolotti** - Half-moon | Meat, herbs | Piedmont |
| **Cappelletti** - Hat-shaped | Meat, cheese | Emilia-Romagna |

## Cooking Pasta

### Al Dente (To the Tooth)

**The perfect doneness**

**Characteristics:**
- Firm to bite, not mushy
- Slight resistance in center
- Cooking time: 1-2 minutes less than package
- Pasta continues cooking off heat

### The Process

**Steps:**
1. **Large pot** - 4-6 quarts water per pound
2. **Add salt** - 1-2 tbsp (salts the pasta from within)
3. **Bring to boil** - Rapid boil
4. **Add pasta** - All at once
5. **Stir** - Prevent sticking
6. **Taste test** - 1-2 minutes before package time
7. **Reserve water** - Save 1/2 cup for sauce
8. **Drain** - Don't rinse (removes starch)

### Fresh Pasta Cooking Time

**Much faster than dried:**
- Thin pasta: 1-2 minutes
- Thick pasta: 2-3 minutes
- Stuffed pasta: 3-5 minutes

## Classic Pasta Dishes

**Carbonara**

- Pecorino Romano
- Black pepper
- **No cream**

- Black pepper

**Amatriciana**

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Overcooking pasta | Cook al dente, reserve cooking water |
| Not salting water | Salt water liberally (should taste like sea) |
| Rinsing pasta | Never rinse (unless for cold pasta salad) |
| Wrong sauce for shape | Match shape to sauce type |
| Not tossing in sauce | Finish cooking pasta in sauce |

## Making Fresh Pasta

**Ingredients:**
- Flour (all-purpose or semolina)
- Eggs (2 per cup flour)
- Pinch of salt

**Process:**
1. **Mound flour** on board
2. **Make well** in center
3. **Crack eggs** into well
4. **Mix** gradually incorporating flour
5. **Knead** 10 minutes until smooth
6. **Rest** 30 minutes
4. **Roll** thin using pasta machine or rolling pin
5. **Cut** into desired shape

## Practice

1. **Master carbonara** - Eggs, cheese, guanciale only
2. **Try cacio e pepe** - Master the cheese emulsion
3. **Make fresh pasta** - Start with tagliatelle
4. **Match shapes to sauces** - Experiment

**Remember:** Quality ingredients and proper technique are everything. Al dente is non-negotiable.
