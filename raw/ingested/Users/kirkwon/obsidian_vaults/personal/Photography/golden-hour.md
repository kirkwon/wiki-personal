## Overview

Golden hour is the period shortly after sunrise and before sunset when the sun is low in the sky, creating warm, soft, golden light. It's prized by photographers for its beautiful, flattering light.

## What is Golden Hour?

### Definition

**Golden hour** = The period when the sun is just above the horizon, typically the first hour after sunrise and the last hour before sunset.

**Characteristics:**
- **Warm, golden light**: Low sun angle creates warm color temperature
- **Soft, diffused light**: Light travels through more atmosphere, becomes softer
- **Long shadows**: Low angle creates dramatic shadows
- **Directional light**: Light comes from side (golden hour) or near horizon (blue hour)
- **Beautiful colors**: Orange, red, yellow tones

### Why Photographers Love Golden Hour

| Reason | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Flattering portraits** | Warm light is kind to skin tones |
| **Dramatic landscapes** | Long shadows, golden light |
| **Beautiful colors** | Warm tones, saturated colors |
| **Soft light** | No harsh shadows, gentle on subjects |
| **Directional light** | Creates depth and dimension |

## Golden Hour vs. Other Times

### Comparison of Lighting Conditions

| Time of Day | Light Characteristics | Best For |
|-------------|---------------------|-----------|
| **Golden hour** | Warm, soft, directional | Portraits, landscapes |
| **Blue hour** | Cool, blue, soft | Cityscapes, twilight shots |
| **Midday** | Harsh, direct, high contrast | Not ideal (use shade) |
| **Overcast** | Soft, diffused, no shadows | Portraits, flowers |
| **Moonlight** | Cool, soft, low light | Night photography |

## Timing

### Sunrise Golden Hour

**When:** First hour after sunrise

**Characteristics:**
- **Cool to warm**: Colors transition from cool blue to warm gold
- **Fresh morning**: Clean air, sometimes fog
- **Rising sun**: Sun moves up, changing angles

**Best for:**
- Landscapes (fresh morning light)
- Flowers (dew, morning light)
- Cityscapes (before people and traffic)
- Portraits (soft, flattering light)

### Sunset Golden Hour

**When:** Last hour before sunset

**Characteristics:**
- **Warm to orange to red**: Colors become more saturated, dramatic
- **Settling atmosphere**: Particles in air create rich colors
- **Lowering sun**: Sun moves down, changing angles

**Best for:**
- Portraits (warm, glowing skin)
- Landscapes (dramatic light, long shadows)
- Silhouettes (against sun)
- Beach photography (ocean reflections)

### Duration

**Golden hour varies by:**
- **Season**: Shorter in winter, longer in summer
- **Latitude**: Longer near equator, shorter near poles
- **Elevation**: Varies with local topography

**Typical duration:**
- **Summer**: 45-60 minutes
- **Winter**: 30-45 minutes
- **Near equator**: Up to 2 hours
- **Near poles**: Can be very short or non-existent

### Checking Weather

**Important considerations:**

| Weather | Effect on Golden Hour |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Clear** | Best golden hour, saturated colors |
| **Partly cloudy** | Good, clouds add drama |
| **Overcast** | No golden hour, soft diffused light (still good) |
| **Rain** | May or may not have golden hour, interesting reflections |

**Check forecast:**
- Weather apps
- Local weather stations
- Weather radar for approaching storms

### Scouting Locations

**Before golden hour:**

1. **Scout location**: Visit location during day
2. **Find compositions**: Plan your shots
3. **Check sun position**: Know where sun will rise/set
4. **Identify subjects**: What will you photograph?
5. **Check access**: How to get to location, parking

**Scouting tips:**
- Arrive early (30 minutes before golden hour)
- Bring headlamp/flashlight (for after sunset)
- Check sunset direction (using apps or compass)

## Equipment
---

### Camera Settings for Golden Hour

| Setting | Recommendation | Why |
|---------|----------------|-----|
| **Mode** | Manual or Aperture Priority | Creative control |
| **Aperture** | f/5.6 - f/8 for landscapes, wider for portraits | Depth of field |
| **Shutter** | Varies (may be slow, use tripod) | Correct exposure |
| **ISO** | Low (100-400) for landscapes, higher if handheld | Clean images |
| **White Balance** | Auto or Daylight preset | Adjust for desired warmth |
| **Lens** | Wide to normal | Capture environment + subject |

### Essential Accessories

| Accessory | Why |
|------------|-----|
| **Tripod** | Slow shutter speeds (landscapes, low light) |
| **Lens hood** | Reduce flare from low sun |
| **Polarizing filter** | Enhance colors, reduce reflections |
| **Graduated ND filter** | Balance bright sky and dark foreground |
| **Remote shutter release** | Prevent camera shake (slow shutter) |
| **Headlamp/flashlight** | See in dark, pack up after sunset |

## Subjects for Golden Hour

### Portraits

**Why golden hour for portraits:**
- Warm, flattering light
- Soft, no harsh shadows
- Natural catchlights in eyes
- Warm skin tones

**Techniques:**
- Place subject with sun to side (side-lighting)
- Face subject toward sun (front-lighting)
- Create silhouettes (backlight)
- Use reflector to fill shadows (if needed)

### Landscapes

**Why golden hour for landscapes:**
- Warm, golden light
- Long, dramatic shadows
- Saturated colors
- Atmospheric effects

**Techniques:**
- Include foreground element (depth)
- Look for long shadows
- Capture warm colors (golden hour glow)
- Use polarizing filter (enhance sky, reduce reflections)

### Cityscapes and Architecture

**Why golden hour for cityscapes:**
- Warm light on buildings
- Long shadows create dimension
- Beautiful golden light on windows
- People going about their day (street photography)

**Techniques:**
- Shoot from different angles
- Look for reflections (windows, water)
- Include people (street photography)
- Look for shadows and light patterns

### Beach and Water Photography

**Why golden hour for beach:**
- Warm light on water (golden reflections)
- Long shadows on sand
- Dramatic colors (orange, pink, red)
- People at beach (lifestyle, street)

**Techniques:**
- Look for reflections in water
- Include people (lifestyle)
- Capture sun (if possible, with caution)
- Long exposures (water motion, use tripod)

## Golden Hour Techniques

### Backlighting

**What it is:** Placing subject between camera and sun.

**Effect:**
- Rim light (hair, edges)
- Warm glow around subject
- Possible silhouette

**When to use:**
- Portraits (create warmth, glow)
- Landscapes (dramatic, atmospheric)
- Silhouettes (against sun)

### Side Lighting

**What it is:** Sun to side of subject.

**Effect:**
- Dramatic shadows
- Dimension and depth
- Textural details

**When to use:**
- Portraits (dimension, shadow on one side)
- Landscapes (shadows create depth)
- Architecture (emphasize texture)

### Front Lighting

**What it is:** Sun behind photographer, subject fully lit.

**Effect:**
- Flat, even lighting
- Saturated colors
- Minimal shadows

**When to use:**
- Landscapes (saturated colors)
- Flowers (no harsh shadows)
- Some portraits (even, flattering)

### Silhouettes

**What it is:** Subject is dark, background is bright (usually sky).

**Technique:**
1. **Expose for background**: Bright sky, not subject
2. **Subject becomes silhouette**: Dark against light
3. **Find recognizable shape**: Trees, people, buildings

**When to use:**
- Dramatic effect
- Shape recognition
- Abstract compositions

## Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Missed golden hour | Plan ahead, arrive early |
| Sun too high | Shoot at proper times (first/last hour) |
| Overexposed sky | Use graduated ND filter or bracket exposures |
| Camera shake | Use tripod, faster shutter, increase ISO |
| Flare in lens | Use lens hood, shade lens |
| Wrong white balance | Adjust for desired warmth (cooler/warmer) |

## Blue Hour

### What is Blue Hour?

**Blue hour** = The period when the sun is just below the horizon, the sky is deep blue.

**Characteristics:**
- **Cool, blue light**: Color temperature is blue
- **Soft, diffused**: No direct sun
- **City lights coming on**: Beautiful blue sky with warm city lights
- **Longer than golden hour**: Can last 30-60 minutes

**Best for:**
- Cityscapes (blue sky with warm lights)
- Twilight scenes
- Urban night photography
- Automotive light trails

**Timing:** 30 minutes after sunset, 30 minutes before sunrise
