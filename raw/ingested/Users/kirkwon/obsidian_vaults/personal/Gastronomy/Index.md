## Quick Reference

### Cuisine Comparison

|| Cuisine | Key Characteristics | Spice Level | Signature Technique |
||---------|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|
|| **Chinese** | Regional diversity, technique-driven | Variable (mild-very high) | Stir-fry, steaming, braising |
|| **Japanese** | Minimalist, ingredient-focused | Mild | Sushi, tempura, precision |
|| **Italian** | Regional, ingredient-focused | Low | Pasta, risotto, slow cooking |
|| **Mexican** | Complex, indigenous | Medium-very high | Nixtamalization, complex moles |
|| **Thai** | Balance of five flavors | High | Curry pastes, stir-fry |
|| **Indian** - Spice mastery, vegetarian | Variable (medium-high) | Tandoor, slow-cooking |
|| **French** | Technique, elegance | Low | Mother sauces, classical techniques |
|| **American** - Regional diversity, comfort food | Variable (mild-medium) | BBQ, frying, grilling |

### Cooking Techniques by Cuisine

| Technique | Cuisine | Application |
|-----------|---------|------------|
| **Stir-fry** | Chinese, Thai | High heat, quick cooking |
| **Steaming** | Chinese, Japanese | Gentle, healthy |
| **Braising** | Chinese, French, Indian | Long, slow cooking |
| **Grilling** | Japanese, Mexican, Thai | Direct heat, smoky |
| **Roasting** - French, Italian | Oven cooking |
| **Confit** | French | Slow cooking in fat |

## Tips for Success

1. **Start simple** - Master basics before complex dishes
2. **Quality ingredients** - They make all the difference
3. **Patience** - Rushing ruins technique-driven dishes
4. **Taste as you go** - Adjust seasoning throughout
5. **Practice** - Repetition builds muscle memory
