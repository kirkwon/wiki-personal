# Indian Cuisine

## Overview

Indian cuisine (भारतीय व्यंजन, Bhāratīya Vyañjan) is one of the world's oldest and most diverse culinary traditions, characterized by its masterful use of spices, regional diversity, and vegetarian richness. From the creamy curries of the North to the spicy seafood of the South, Indian food celebrates complexity, balance, and communal dining.

## Core Philosophy

### Ayurveda and Food

**Ancient wisdom:**

- **Food as medicine** - Balance body humors (doshas)
- **Seasonal eating** - Eat with nature's rhythms
- **Spice mastery** - Spices for health and flavor

### Flavor Balance

**Six flavors (Rasas):**

| Flavor | Spice/Ingredient | Purpose |
|--------|------------------|---------|
| **Sweet** - Sugar, jaggery | Satisfying |
| **Sour** - Tamarind, yogurt, lime | Digestive |
| **Salty** - Salt | Flavor base |
| **Bitter** - Fenugreek, bitter gourd | Blood purification |
| **Pungent** - Chilies, black pepper | Warming |
| **Astringent** - Lentils, turmeric | Cooling |

**Dish should contain multiple rasas for balance**

## Regional Cuisines

| Region | Style | Characteristics |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| **North** - Wheat-based, creamy, dairy-rich | Naan, paneer, tandoori |
| **South** - Rice-based, spicy, coconut | Dosa, sambar, rasam |
| **East** - Fish, mustard oil | Bengali fish, sweets |
| **West** - Vegetarian, Gujarati thali | Dhokla, thepla |
| **Coastal** - Seafood, coconut | Goan fish, Kerala seafood |

| Spice | Flavor | Use |
|-------|--------|-----|
| **Cumin (Jeera)** - Earthy, nutty | Base, tempering |

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Not toasting spices | Toast before grinding for more flavor |
| Using old spices | Buy whole, grind as needed |
| Overcooking paneer | Add last, cook gently |
| Not balancing flavors | Adjust salt, acid, sweetness |
