# American Burgers

## Overview

The hamburger is an American icon - a simple concept elevated to art form. From fast food to gourmet, burgers represent American comfort food at its finest. Juicy patty, toasted bun, toppings: the perfect burger balances texture, flavor, and construction.

## Philosophy

### The Perfect Burger

**"Bun, patty, toppings in harmony"**

A great burger needs:
- **Juicy patty** - Not dry, not overcooked
- **Fresh bun** - Toasted, supportive
- **Quality toppings** - Fresh, complementary
- **Proper construction** - Everything holds together
- **No need for fancy** - Simple is often best

## The Elements

### The Patty

**The heart of the burger**

**Ground beef:**
- **Chuck** (shoulder) - Best balance of fat (15-20%)
- **Sirloin** - Lean, less juicy
- **Blend** - Chuck and sirloin for balance

**Fat content:**
- 15-20% fat - Juicy, flavorful
- 10% fat - Lean, drier
- 30% fat - Too greasy

**Seasoning:**
- Salt (essential)
- Black pepper
- Optional: Worcestershire sauce, onion powder

**Cooking methods:**
| Method | Characteristics |
|--------|----------------|
| **Grill** - Smoky, charred |
| **Cast iron** - Crispy crust (best) |
| **Smash** - Thin, crispy, quick |
| **Thick** - Juicy, medium-rare center |

### The Bun

**Foundation**

**Types:**
| Bun | Characteristics |
|-----|-----------------|
| **Brioche** - Sweet, soft |
| **Potato** - Soft, slightly sweet |
| **Sesame** - Classic, nutty |
| **Pretzel** - Chewy, salty |
| **Brioche pretzel** - Soft, salty |

**Preparation:**
- Toast buttered bun
- Creates barrier against juices
- Adds crunch

### Cheese

**Melting quality matters:**

| Cheese | Melting Quality | Flavor |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| **American** - Melts perfectly | Mild |
| **Cheddar** - Good melt | Sharp |
| **Provolone** - Good melt | Mild |
| **Swiss** - Good melt | Nutty |
| **Blue** - Crumbly melt | Sharp |

**Placement:**
- Place on patty last 30 seconds
- Let melt slightly
- Don't overmelt (loss of cheese)

### Toppings

**Vegetables:**
- Lettuce (iceberg for crunch)
- Tomato (ripe, sliced)
- Onion (white, yellow, or red)
- Pickles (dill, sweet)
- Jalapeños (heat)

**Sauces:**
- Ketchup (classic)
- Mustard (yellow, Dijon, spicy)
- Mayonnaise (creamy)
- Special sauce (house-made)
- BBQ sauce (smoky)

**Additional:**
- Bacon (crispy)
- Avocado (creamy)
- Fried egg (breakfast burger)
- Mushrooms (sautéed)

## Building the Perfect Burger

### Stack Order (Bottom to Top)

1. **Bottom bun** (toasted, buttered)
2. **Sauce** (protects bun)
3. **Lettuce** (crunch)
4. **Tomato** (juiciness)
---
5. **Patty** (seasoned, cooked to doneness)
6. **Cheese** (melted on patty)
7. **Onion** (flavor)
8. **Pickles** (acid)
9. **Top bun** (toasted)

### Cooking the Patty

**Cast iron method (recommended):**

**Ingredients:**
- 6-8 oz ground beef (15-20% fat)
- Salt, pepper

**Process:**
1. Form patty (don't overwork meat)
2. Make small indent in center (prevents puffing)
3. Heat cast iron skillet until smoking
4. Add oil (high smoke point)
5. Place patty in pan
6. Don't flip too soon (wait 3-4 minutes)
7. Flip once
8. Cook to desired doneness:
   - Medium-rare: 130-135°F
   - Medium: 140-145°F
   - Medium-well: 150-155°F
   - Well: 160°F+
9. Rest 5 minutes
10. Assemble

**Why cast iron?**
- High heat retention
- Crispy crust (Maillard reaction)
- Even cooking

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Overworking meat | Form patties gently, don't compress |
| Overcooking patty | Check temperature, don't guess |
| Under-seasoning | Salt generously before cooking |
| Soggy bun | Toast bun, use sauce as barrier |
| Too many toppings | Keep construction manageable |
| Not resting patty | Rest 5 minutes, juices redistribute |
