# New England Seafood

## Overview

New England seafood cuisine celebrates the bounty of the Atlantic Ocean - clams, lobsters, cod, and scallops. Hearty, comforting, and rooted in maritime tradition, New England cooking is defined by clam chowder, lobster rolls, and fried seafood shacks.

## Philosophy

### Coastal Tradition

**"Fresh from the sea"**

New England seafood embodies:
- **Fresh catch** - What's in season, what's local
- **Hearty portions** - Fisherman's meals
- **Simple preparations** - Let seafood shine
- **Comfort** - Chowders, rolls, fried

### New England vs. Other Seafood

| Aspect | New England | Pacific Northwest | Gulf Coast |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|------------|
| **Clams** - Hard shell (quahog) | Razor clams, geoduck | Oysters |
| **Lobster** | Maine lobster | Dungeness crab | Gulf shrimp |
| **Chowder** - Creamy, white | Clear, tomato-based | Gumbo |

## Classic Dishes

### New England Clam Chowder

**The creamy classic**

**Note:** Not to be confused with Manhattan clam chowder (tomato-based)

**Ingredients:**
- Clams (quahogs) or canned clams
- Bacon (salt pork)
- Onions
- Potatoes (Yukon Gold)
- Milk or cream
- Thyme, bay leaf
- Salt, pepper, oyster crackers

**Preparation:**
1. Render bacon fat
2. Sauté onions in bacon fat
3. Add potatoes, cook 5 minutes
4. Add clams (with liquid if canned)
5. Add milk or cream
6. Simmer 20-30 minutes
7. Season to taste
8. Serve with oyster crackers

**Key:** Don't overcook clams (tough)

### Lobster Roll

**Two styles**

**Maine (Butter):**
- Lobster meat (chunks)
- Butter (melted, clarified)
- Toasted split-top bun
- Celery (optional)

**Connecticut (Mayo):**
- Lobster meat (chunks)
- Mayonnaise
- Lemon juice
- Celery
- Toasted split-top bun

**Preparation:**
1. Boil or steam lobster
2. Remove meat from shell
3. Chop into chunks
4. Mix with butter or mayonnaise
5. Stuff into toasted bun
6. Serve immediately

**Legend:** Lobster rolls originated in 1918 at Perry's in Milford, CT

### Fried Clams

**Seafood shack classic**

**Ingredients:**
- Clams (bellies, strips)
- Cornmeal or flour dredge
- Salt, pepper, paprika
- Oil for frying

**Preparation:**
1. Clean clams
2. Dredge in seasoned cornmeal/flour
3. Fry at 350°F (175°C)
4. 2-3 minutes until golden
5. Drain on paper towels
6. Serve with tartar sauce

### Fried Scallops

**Sweet, tender**

**Ingredients:**
- Sea scallops (or bay scallops)
- Seasoned flour or panko
- Lemon wedges
- Tartar sauce

**Preparation:**
1. Dry scallops
2. Dredge in seasoned coating
3. Fry 2-3 minutes per side
4. Don't overcook (tough)
5. Serve with lemon

### New England Boiled Dinner

**Hearty comfort**

**Components:**
- Corned beef brisket
- Cabbage (wedges)
- Potatoes (chunks)
- Carrots (chunks)
- Turnips (optional)
- Mustard, horseradish

**Preparation:**
1. Cover corned beef with water
2. Boil 2-3 hours
3. Add vegetables
4. Boil until tender (30-45 min)
5. Serve with mustard

### Fish and Chips

---
**British-influenced but New England**

**Components:**
- Cod or haddock
- Beer batter
- French fries
- Tartar sauce

**Batter:**
- Flour
- Baking powder
- Beer (light)
- Salt

**Frying:**
1. Dredge fish in flour
2. Dip in batter
3. Fry at 375°F (190°C)
4. 4-6 minutes until golden
5. Drain on paper towels

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Overcooking clams | Cook just until open |
| Overcooking scallops | 2-3 minutes max, don't overcook |
| Chowder too thick | Adjust milk/cream ratio |
| Undercooking lobster | Cook until bright red, meat opaque |
