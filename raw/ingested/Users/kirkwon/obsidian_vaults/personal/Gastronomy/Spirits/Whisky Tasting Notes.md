## Tasting Notes by Whisky

### Angel's Envy
**Type**: Kentucky Straight Bourbon (Port Wine Barrel Finish)
**Price**: $$50-60
**Tasting**: 
- **Nose**: Vanilla, caramel, subtle port wine sweetness
- **Palate**: Smooth, rich vanilla, dark fruit undertones
- **Finish**: Long, warming, port wine notes emerge
- **Verdict**: Premium bourbon with wine finish complexity

### Macallan 12
**Type**: Highland Single Malt Scotch
**Price**: $70-80
**Tasting**:
- **Nose**: Sherry oak, dried fruit, subtle spice
- **Palate**: Classic sherry malt, balanced sweetness
- **Finish**: Medium-long, elegant
- **Verdict**: Benchmark for aged Scotch

### Buffalo Trace
**Type**: Kentucky Straight Bourbon
**Price**: $26
**Tasting**:
- **Nose**: Vanilla, caramel, light oak
- **Palate**: Sweet, approachable, hint of spice
- **Finish**: Medium, pleasant warmth
- **Verdict**: Best value bourbon, perfect entry point

### Wild Turkey 101
**Type**: Kentucky Straight Bourbon
**Price**: $25
**Tasting**:
- **Nose**: Bold, spicy, high proof evident
- **Palate**: Intense vanilla, cinnamon, caramel
- **Finish**: Long, warming, assertive
- **Verdict**: High proof bargain, great for cocktails

### Johnnie Walker Black
**Type**: Blended Scotch
**Price**: $26
**Tasting**:
- **Nose**: Smoky, accessible, fruit
- **Palate**: Balanced, slightly peaty, approachable
- **Finish**: Medium, consistent
- **Verdict**: Reliable blended, great for newcomers to Scotch

### Knob Creek 9
**Type**: Kentucky Straight Bourbon
**Price**: $41
**Tasting**:
- **Nose**: Nutty, oak, vanilla
- **Palate**: Aged complexity, caramel, toasted nuts
- **Finish**: Long, satisfying
- **Verdict**: Aged bourbon value

### Blanton's
**Type**: Single Barrel Kentucky Bourbon
**Price**: $60-80
**Tasting**:
- **Nose**: Complex, caramel, dried fruit
- **Palate**: Rich, layered, sophisticated
- **Finish**: Exceptionally long, evolving
- **Verdict**: Collector's bourbon, worth the hunt

### Evan Williams 12/18
**Type**: Kentucky Bourbon (Age Statement)
**Price**: $12-18
**Tasting**:
- **Nose**: Simple, corn-forward
- **Palate**: Sweet, straightforward
- **Finish**: Short, uncomplicated
- **Verdict**: Budget sipper, good for mixing

### Jack Daniel's
**Type**: Tennessee Whiskey
**Price**: $16-26
**Tasting**:
- **Nose**: Charcoal, vanilla, corn
- **Palate**: Sweet, filtered, mellow
- **Finish**: Short, smooth
- **Verdict**: Gateway whiskey, widely available

### Woodford Reserve
**Type**: Kentucky Straight Bourbon
**Price**: $35-40
**Tasting**:
- **Nose**: Refined vanilla, oak, mint
- **Palate**: Balanced, complex, approachable
- **Finish**: Medium-long, elegant
- **Verdict**: Refined daily sipper
