## Overview

San Francisco is a vibrant, hilly city on the tip of the San Francisco Peninsula. Known for its iconic landmarks, diverse neighborhoods, and unique geography, it offers endless opportunities for exploration, dining, and culture.

## Geography

### The Hills

**SF is famous for its hills!**

**Notable hills:**
- **Russian Hill**: Lombard Street, expensive real estate
- **Nob Hill**: Historic hotels, Grace Cathedral
- **Pacific Heights**: Expensive, views
- **Telegraph Hill**: Coit Tower, gardens
- **Twin Peaks**: Highest point, 360° views
- **Bernal Heights**: Hilltop park, residential

**Tips for walking hills:**
- **Wear comfortable shoes**
- **Take breaks**: It's steeper than it looks
- **Use public transit** for the steepest hills
- **Cable cars**: Great way to experience hills

### The Water

**Water on three sides:**

- **Pacific Ocean**: Ocean Beach, western edge
- **San Francisco Bay**: Northern and eastern waterfront
- **Marin Headlands**: Visible across the bay (clear days)

### Bridges

**Iconic bridges:**

- **Golden Gate Bridge**: To Marin County (north)
- **Bay Bridge**: To Oakland (east)

## Climate

### The "Indian Summer"

**Surprising pattern:**
- **Winter**: Mild, 50-60°F, sunny days
- **Spring**: Variable, 55-65°F, occasional rain
- **Summer**: 55-65°F, FOGGY and windy
- **Fall**: 60-75°F, warmest, sunniest season

**Why?**
- **Summer fog**: Cold ocean creates fog
- **Fall**: Ocean warms, less fog, more sun
- **Winter storms**: Rain, but mild temperatures

### What to Wear

**Always bring:**
- **Layers**: T-shirt + sweater/light jacket
- **Windbreaker**: Windy, especially near water
- **Comfortable shoes**: Lots of walking, hills

**Seasonal adjustments:**
- **Winter**: Raincoat or umbrella
- **Summer**: Hoodie (it's cold in the fog!)
- **Fall**: Light jacket, maybe sunglasses
- **Spring**: Layers for variable weather

## Transportation

### Public Transit

**Muni (San Francisco Municipal Transportation):**

**Trains:**
- **J-Church**: Noe Valley to downtown
- **K-Ingleside**: To SF State, Balboa Park
- **L-Taraval**: Through Sunset
- **M-Ocean View**: Through Excelsior
- **N-Judah**: Through Sunset to downtown
- **T-Third**: Through SoMa to Mission Bay
- **S-Castro Shuttle**: In Castro

**Buses:**
- **Local buses**: Cover most neighborhoods
- **Express buses**: Faster, limited stops
- **Owl buses**: Night service (12am-5am)

**Cable Cars:**
- **Powell-Hyde**: Fisherman's Wharf to Nob Hill
- **Powell-Mason**: Fisherman's Wharf to North Beach
- **California**: Financial District to Chinatown

**Cost:**
- **Single ride**: $2.50 (exact change)
- **Day pass**: $5
- **Monthly pass**: $81 (Clipper card)

### Walking
---

**SF is a walkable city!**

**Tips:**
- **Allow time**: Hills take longer than maps suggest
- **Use apps**: Google Maps, Transit App for real-time info
- **Sidewalks**: Most streets have sidewalks
- **Crosswalks**: Be careful, some drivers don't stop

### Biking

**Bike-friendly city:**

**Bike share:**
- **Ford GoBike**: Stations throughout the city
- **Day pass**: ~$15 (includes 3-hour rides)
- **Annual membership**: ~$159

**Bike lanes:**
- **Protected lanes**: Growing network
- **Bike routes**: Painted lanes
- **Hills**: Challenging, plan routes

### Driving

**Driving in SF:**

**Parking:**
- **Street parking**: Expensive, time-limited
- **Garages**: Expensive, hourly
- **Resident permits**: For residents only

**Challenges:**
- **Hills**: Steep, requires skills
- **Traffic**: Congested
- **One-way streets**: Confusing
- **Tolls**: Bay Bridge ($6+)

**Tips:**
- **Use apps**: ParkWhiz, SpotHero for garages
- **Avoid driving**: Public transit, walking, biking better
- **Valet**: Available at some restaurants

### Ferries

**Bay crossings:**
- **SF Ferry**: To Sausalito, Tiburon, Oakland, Alameda
- **Golden Gate Ferry**: To Sausalito
- **Blue & Gold Fleet**: Tourist ferries to Alcatraz, Angel Island

## Practical Tips

### What to Expect

- **Hills**: Everything is uphill (or downhill)
- **Fog**: Summer fog is intense
- **Expensive**: One of the most expensive US cities
- **Public transit**: Good but slow
- **Walkable**: Many neighborhoods are walkable
- **Food scene**: Incredible variety
- **Tech influence**: Visible in neighborhoods, prices

### Budget Tips

- **Eat at taquerias**: Cheap, delicious burritos
- **Use public transit**: Cheaper than driving/parking
- **Free activities**: Parks, beaches, walking tours
- **Happy hour**: Drinks and food cheaper 4-7pm
- **Neighborhood restaurants**: Cheaper than tourist areas

### Safety

- **Car theft**: High, don't leave valuables in car
- **Homelessness**: Visible in many neighborhoods
- **Pickpockets**: In crowded tourist areas
- **Stay aware**: Especially at night
- **Emergency**: 911

### Local Etiquette

- **J-walking**: Common, but be careful
- **Cable cars**: Don't block the tracks
- **Restaurants**: Tipping 15-20% standard
- **Buses**: Board front, exit rear (unless bus full)
- **Bike lanes**: Respect bike lanes, don't block

## Day Trip Ideas

### Perfect SF Day (First Visit)

1. **Morning**: Coffee + croissant, walk Union Square
2. **Midday**: Cable car to Fisherman's Wharf
3. **Lunch**: Seafood at Fisherman's Wharf
4. **Afternoon**: Alcatraz tour (book ahead!)
5. **Evening**: Dinner in North Beach

### Local's Day

1. **Morning**: Blue Bottle coffee, walk neighborhood
2. **Midday**: Mission burrito at La Taqueria
3. **Afternoon**: Dolores Park (if sunny) or museum visit
4. **Evening**: Dinner in neighborhood, local bar

### Nature Day

1. **Morning**: Hike Land's End trails
2. **Midday**: Sutro Baths and Cliff House
3. **Lunch**: Ocean Beach cafe
4. **Afternoon**: Walk Golden Gate Park
5. **Evening**: Sunset at Ocean Beach (if clear)
