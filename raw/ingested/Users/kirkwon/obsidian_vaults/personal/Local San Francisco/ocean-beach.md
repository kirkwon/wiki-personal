## Overview

Ocean Beach is San Francisco's main beach, stretching from the Cliff House in the north to the San Francisco Zoo in the south. Known for its powerful waves, cold water, and iconic fog, it's a beloved destination for surfers, beachgoers, and storm watchers.

## Beach Sections

### Kelly's Cove (North End)

**Location:** Near the Cliff House and Sutro Baths

**Characteristics:**
- More protected from wind
- Smaller waves
- Popular with beginner surfers
- Good for beach walking

**Access:**
- Parking at Ocean Beach (near Great Highway)
- Walking from Sutro Baths and Land's End
- Closest to public transit

**Vibe:**
- More people
- Beginner surfers
- Beach volleyball area

### Noriega Street

**Location:** Center of beach

**Characteristics:**
- Consistent surf break
- Popular with intermediate surfers
- Good surf spot year-round

**Access:**
- Noriega Street parking lot
- Walking from Sunset District

**Vibe:**
- Surf community
- Morning surf sessions
- Quieter than north end

### Sloat Boulevard (South End)

**Location:** Near San Francisco Zoo

**Characteristics:**
- Larger waves
- More exposed to wind
- Stronger currents

**Access:**
- Sloat Blvd parking
- Walking from Outer Sunset

**Vibe:**
- More solitude
- Stronger conditions
- Less crowded

## Activities

### Surfing

**Ocean Beach is known for surfing!**

**Conditions:**
- **Waves**: 3-6 feet typical, can reach 10+ feet in winter
- **Water**: Cold (50-55°F), wetsuit required
- **Currents**: Strong, especially near shore
- **Fog**: Can create poor visibility

**Skill Levels:**
- **Beginners**: Kelly's Cove (north end)
- **Intermediate**: Noriega Street
- **Advanced**: Sloat Boulevard and beyond

**Gear Required:**
- Wetsuit (4/3mm minimum, 5/4mm in winter)
- Surfboard appropriate for conditions
- Booties (recommended)
- Hood and gloves (winter)

**Safety:**
- Always surf with a buddy
- Check conditions before going out
- Be aware of rip currents
- Know your limits
- Respect other surfers

### Beach Walking

**3.5 miles of beach to explore!**

**Best Times:**
- **Low tide**: More beach exposed
- **Sunset**: Beautiful views (when not too foggy)
- **Winter storm watching**: Dramatic waves

**What to See:**
- Golden Gate Park (from beach)
- Cliff House (north end)
- Sutro Baths ruins
- Marin Headlands (across the bay - rare clear days)

**Tips:**
- Wear layers (always cold near water)
- Bring water
- Check tide charts
- Watch for sneaker waves (stay back from edge)

### Bonfires

**Designated fire pits available!**

**Locations:**
- Between Fulton and Lincoln (north section)
- Various spots along the beach

**Rules:**
- Use only designated fire pits
- Wood available for purchase nearby
- Fires must be out before leaving
- Check fire restrictions (summer months)

**Best Times:**
- Summer evenings
- Weekends
- Clear nights (rare in summer!)

### Wildlife Watching

**Common sightings:**
- **Seabirds**: Gulls, pelicans, cormorants
- **Marine mammals**: Occasionally dolphins, seals
- **Shorebirds**: Sandpipers, plovers
- **Sea lions**: Occasionally near shore

**Best Spots:**
- Near rock formations (north end)
- Early morning
- During low tide

### Photography

**Great shots:**
- Storm waves (winter)
- Fog and mist (summer)
- Sunsets (fall - best visibility)
- Cliff House and Sutro Baths
- Long exposure shots of waves

## Weather & Conditions

### Temperature

| Season | Air Temp | Water Temp | Typical Conditions |
|--------|----------|------------|-------------------|
| Spring | 55-65°F | 52-55°F | Variable, windy |
| Summer | 55-60°F | 52-55°F | Foggy, windy |
| Fall | 60-70°F | 54-56°F | Best weather, sunnier |
| Winter | 50-60°F | 50-54°F | Storms, large waves |

### Fog

**"June Gloom":**
- Summer fog is intense
- Can last all day
- Visibility near zero
- Creates moody, atmospheric scenes

**Fall:**
- Less fog
- More sun
- Best beach weather

### Waves

**Seasonal Patterns:**
- **Winter**: Largest waves, storm swells, powerful
- **Spring**: Smaller waves, more variable
- **Summer**: Consistent surf, wind-driven
- **Fall**: Fall transition, good surfing conditions

## Safety

### Beach Safety

**Hazards:**
- **Rip currents**: Strong currents can pull you out to sea
- **Sneaker waves**: Large, unexpected waves (stay back from edge)
- **Cold water**: Hypothermia risk
- **Rocks and debris**: Hidden hazards
- **Strong winds**: Can knock you down

**Safety Tips:**
- Never turn your back on the ocean
- Stay back from the water's edge
- Swim only in designated areas (Ocean Beach isn't recommended for swimming)
- Wear layers (cold, windy)
- Check conditions before visiting
- Go with a friend

### Surfing Safety

**Risks:**
- Cold water hypothermia
- Strong currents
- Collisions with other surfers
- Impact with sea floor

**Precautions:**
- Always surf with a buddy
- Wear appropriate wetsuit
- Know your skill level
- Respect other surfers
- Check conditions
- Stay within your abilities

## Access & Parking

### Parking

**Options:**
- **Great Highway parking**: Along the beach (free)
- **Noriega Street lot**: Central location
- **Fulton Street**: Near north end
- **Sloat Boulevard**: Near south end

**Tips:**
- Parking fills up on nice days and weekends
- Summer weekends can be very crowded
- Fall weekdays: Easy parking
- Winter: Always space available

### Public Transit

**From Downtown:**
- **N-Judah**: To Great Highway (walk to beach)
- **28-19th Ave**: To Great Highway
- **5-Fulton**: To Great Highway

**Time:** 30-40 minutes from downtown

### Biking

**From City:**
- Bike lanes on Great Highway
- Flat terrain (easy biking)
- Wind from ocean can be challenging
- Bike parking available

## Best Times to Visit

### By Activity

| Activity | Best Time |
|----------|-----------|
| **Surfing** | Early morning (less wind), fall for best waves |
| **Beach walking** | Low tide, clear days |
| **Bonfires** | Summer evenings (bring warm clothes) |
| **Photography** | Winter storms, fall sunsets |
| **Wildlife** | Early morning |

### By Season

**Spring (March-May):**
- Variable weather
- Smaller waves
- Fewer crowds

**Summer (June-August):**
- Foggy and windy
- Consistent surf
- Most crowded (locals still go despite fog)
- Bonfires on clear evenings

**Fall (September-November):**
- BEST weather
- Sunnier, warmer
- Great for all activities
- Locals' favorite season

**Winter (December-February):**
- Storms and large waves
- Best for storm watching
- Dramatic photography
- Fewer crowds

## What to Bring

### Essentials
- **Layers**: Always bring a jacket
- **Windbreaker**: Wind can be strong
- **Water**: Stay hydrated
- **Sunscreen**: Sun reflects off sand and water

### For Surfing
- **Wetsuit**: 4/3mm minimum (5/4mm in winter)
- **Surfboard**: Appropriate for conditions
- **Booties**: Protect feet from rocks
- **Hood and gloves**: Winter surfing
- **Towel and change of clothes**: For after surf

### For Beach Walking
- **Comfortable shoes**: Sand can be soft
- **Jacket**: Always cold near water
- **Hat and sunglasses**: For sun (when visible)
- **Camera**: For photos (fog or sun)

### For Bonfires
- **Firewood**: Purchase nearby (don't bring from home)
- **Matches/lighter**
- **Blankets**: It gets cold!
- **Food and drinks**
- **Chairs**: Beach chairs or sand chairs

- Locals love it despite (or because of) the fog
