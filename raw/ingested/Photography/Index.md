## Photography in San Francisco

San Francisco offers incredible photography opportunities:

**Street Photography:**
- Mission District: Murals, Latino culture, Valencia Street
- Chinatown: Dense, colorful, activity
- North Beach: Italian restaurants, nightlife
- SoMa: Tech, urban grit
- Castro: Gay culture, nightlife

**Landscapes:**
- Ocean Beach: Beach, waves, sunsets
- Golden Gate Park: Gardens, lakes, trees
- Twin Peaks: 360° city views
- Golden Gate Bridge: Iconic views from multiple angles

**Golden Hour:**
- Ocean Beach: Sunset over ocean
- Golden Gate Bridge: Dramatic light
- Cityscapes: From Twin Peaks, Coit Tower
- Neighborhoods: Streets, architecture

See [[local-san-francisco]], [[sunset-district]], [[ocean-beach]] for detailed location guides.

## Equipment Quick Reference

### Camera Types

| Type | Best For |
---
|------|----------|
| **Mirrorless/DSLR** | Serious photography, interchangeable lenses |
| **Compact** | Street photography, portability |
| **Phone** | Always available, casual shooting |

### Lenses

| Focal Length | Best For |
|--------------|----------|
| **35mm** | Street photography (context + intimacy) |
| **50mm** | Portraits, general use |
| **24mm** | Landscapes, environmental portraits |
| **85mm** | Portraits, compression |

### Essential Accessories

- Tripod (for long exposures, stability)
- Lens hood (reduce flare)
- Extra batteries
- Memory cards
- Cleaning kit

## Practice Tips

### Daily Practice
- Carry camera everywhere (or use phone)
- Practice seeing light and composition
- Review and learn from your shots

### Weekly Practice
- Plan shoots around golden hour
- Experiment with different techniques
- Study great photographers' work

### Monthly Practice
- Complete a photo project (theme, location)
- Curate portfolio of best shots
- Get feedback from others

## Photography Philosophy

**Great photography = Vision + Technical Skills + Practice**

- **Vision**: Seeing the shot, composing creatively
- **Technical Skills**: Understanding exposure, settings, composition
- **Practice**: Shooting regularly, learning from mistakes, developing style

**Start with good captures, then enhance through post-processing.**

**The best camera is the one you have with you.**
