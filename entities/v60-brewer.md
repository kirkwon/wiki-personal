# Hario V60

The Hario V60 is a conical pour-over brewer with a single large hole at the bottom. It is known for fast draining and highlighting acidity and clarity in the cup.

## Characteristics
- Conical shape with single large hole
- Fast draining, requires technique
- Highlights acidity and clarity

## Technique
- Slow, controlled circular pours
- Bloom stage crucial
- Maintain water level above coffee bed
- Total time: 2:30-3:30 minutes

## Best For
Brewers who want to highlight nuanced acidity and complex flavor notes in single-origin specialty coffee.
