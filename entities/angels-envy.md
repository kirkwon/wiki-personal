# Angel's Envy

Kentucky Straight Bourbon with a port wine barrel finish. Rated as a premium bourbon with exceptional complexity.

## Key Facts
- **Type**: Kentucky Straight Bourbon (Port Wine Barrel Finish)
- **Price**: $50-60
- **Rating**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Proof**: 86.6

## Tasting Notes
- **Nose**: Vanilla, caramel, subtle port wine sweetness
- **Palate**: Smooth, rich vanilla, dark fruit undertones
- **Finish**: Long, warming, port wine notes emerge
- **Verdict**: Premium bourbon with wine finish complexity

## Comparison
High sweetness (★★★★) and complexity (★★★★★) with moderate spice (★★). Value rated at ★★★.
