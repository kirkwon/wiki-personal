# Leaders Eat Last - Simon Sinek

*Leaders Eat Last* is Simon Sinek's 2024 book on service-first leadership that explores the biology of leadership and trust. The central thesis is that organizations achieve better outcomes when leaders prioritize people over profit, creating cultures of trust, belonging, and shared purpose. The book draws on neurobiology to explain group dynamics, examining how effective leaders create environments of cooperation.

## Key Themes
- **Triple Bottom Line**: Balancing people, profit, and purpose
- **Belonging**: Creating a sense of belonging fosters trust and collaboration
- **Culture as Choice**: Leaders shape culture through deliberate prioritization of employees
- **Service Over Profit**: Putting employees first leads to better customer outcomes and profits
- **Physical Space**: Environment design impacts team dynamics and culture
- **Social Structure**: Communication patterns and decision-making build strong teams
- **Shared Purpose**: Clear purpose unites employees toward common goals

## Neurobiology of Leadership
- Leaders who sacrifice for their teams build deeper trust
- Organizational safety is tied to neurochemical responses (cortisol vs oxytocin)
- Intuitive sensing of group dynamics is a critical leadership skill

## Examples Used
- Southwest Airlines
- Costco Wholesale
- Starbucks
