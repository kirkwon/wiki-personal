# The Systems Bible - John Gall

*The Systems Bible* by John Gall is a foundational text on systems thinking, known for its wry and accessible exploration of how systems behave in ways that defy simple prediction. It provides the foundation for the "Theorem" mental model, emphasizing how complex systems evolve from simple ones that work and how systems tend to resist change. Gall emphasizes that systems are inherently more complex than the sum of their parts and that designers and managers frequently underestimate the emergent properties of complex systems.

## Key Ideas

- Systems exhibit behaviors that surprise their creators
- Complexity is an inherent property of systems, not merely a result of poor design
- Understanding system dynamics requires looking at patterns and relationships, not just components
- Complex systems evolve from simple ones that work
- Systems tend to resist change
