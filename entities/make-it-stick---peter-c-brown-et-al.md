# Make It Stick - Peter C. Brown et al.

*Make It Stick: The Science of Successful Learning* is a book by Peter C. Brown, Henry L. Roediger III, and Mark A. McDaniel that synthesizes research on how people learn effectively. The book emphasizes evidence-based strategies including retrieval practice, spacing, interleaving, and elaboration as alternatives to passive review and rereading.

The book is referenced as a cross-source perspective on the memorization mental model, providing scientific grounding for understanding memorization not as rote repetition but as an active process of pattern and relationship recognition.^[raw/articles/Memorization.md]

## Key Learning Strategies from the Book

- **Retrieval practice** — Actively recalling information strengthens memory more than passive review
- **Spacing** — Distributing practice over time improves long-term retention
- **Interleaving** — Mixing different topics or types of problems enhances learning
- **Elaboration** — Connecting new information to existing knowledge deepens understanding
