# The Signal and the Noise - Nate Silver

*The Signal and the Noise* is Nate Silver's book examining how we can better distinguish meaningful signals from random noise in predictions and data analysis. It covers forecasting in politics, sports, weather, earthquakes, and economics, emphasizing probabilistic thinking and the pitfalls of overconfidence and bias.
