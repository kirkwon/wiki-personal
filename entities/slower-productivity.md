# Slow Productivity

*Slow Productivity* by Cal Newport challenges the modern assumption that busyness equals productivity. The book advocates for a deliberate, sustainable pace—doing fewer things better rather than optimizing every hour for output.

## Key Concepts
- Reject "new work" mentality (constant input of tasks)
- Focus on a small number of mission-critical outputs
- Align work rhythms with deep, meaningful engagement

## Relevance
Enriches the Habits mental model by emphasizing sustainability and intentionality in habit formation.
