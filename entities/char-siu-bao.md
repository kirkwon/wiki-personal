# Char Siu Bao (叉烧包)

叉烧包（叉烧包，Chāshāobāo）是粤式蒸包，外白内馅。Char Siu Bao (叉烧包, Chāshāobāo) — BBQ Pork Buns — are puffy, filled buns with a white fluffy exterior and sweet, savory BBQ pork filling.

## Characteristics / 特征
- White, fluffy bun / 白色蓬松面皮
- Sweet, savory BBQ pork filling / 甜咸叉烧馅
- Slightly charred top / 顶部微焦
- Served steamed / 蒸制上桌

## Components / 组成
- **Bun / 面皮**: Flour, yeast, milk, sugar / 面粉、酵母、牛奶、糖
- **Filling / 馅料**: Char siu, hoisin, oyster sauce, seafood sauce / 叉烧、海鲜酱、蚝油

## Hosting Tip
Freeze raw char siu bao and steam when ready. Rest dough 30 minutes before shaping.
