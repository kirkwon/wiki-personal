# Resource: The Flavor Bible

*The Flavor Bible* by Karen Page and Andrew Dornenburg is a reference guide that maps flavor compatibilities and affinities between ingredients. It provides a systematic approach to understanding why certain foods pair well together and how to predict successful combinations.

Key features:
- Organized by ingredient with cross-referenced flavor pairings
- Emphasis on aromatic compatibility and taste contrast
- Practical framework for improvisation in the kitchen

This resource directly supports [[ingredient-pairings]] and [[flavor-theory]], providing the empirical backbone for understanding flavor combinations.
