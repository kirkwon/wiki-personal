# Atul Gawande

Atul Gawande is a surgeon, public health researcher, and author best known for his advocacy of checklists as a tool for improving reliability in complex professional environments and for exploring end-of-life care. His book *The Checklist Manifesto* argues that simple procedural tools can dramatically reduce errors and improve outcomes in fields like medicine, aviation, and construction. He is also referenced in the mental model of [[care]] as a cross-source perspective on navigating complex situations.

## Key Contributions
- **Checklists in medicine**: Demonstrated that the WHO surgical safety checklist reduces complications and mortality in surgical settings.
- **Cross-domain application**: Traced checklists from naval origins through aviation to modern medical practice, showing their universal utility.
- **Resource-limited settings**: Showed checklists' effectiveness in developing-world hospitals where resources are scarce but process discipline matters.
- **Personal life extension**: Argued checklists can be applied to family management and personal goal achievement.
- **End-of-life care**: Explored how medicine and the elderly interact, examining end-of-life care and the importance of care as a principle for navigating difficult decisions.
