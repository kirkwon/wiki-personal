# Har Gow (虾饺)

Har Gow (虾饺, Xiājiǎo) — Shrimp Dumplings — is considered the gold standard of dim sum. Known for its translucent, crystal skin with 8-12 pleated edges and plump shrimp filling. It is called the "gold standard" of dim sum (点心的"金标准").

## Characteristics / 特征

- Translucent, crystal skin / 透明水晶皮
- Plump shrimp filling / 饱满虾肉馅
- Classic crescent shape with pleats / 经典月牙形带褶
- Delicate, chewy texture / 细腻有嚼劲的口感
- 8-12 pleated edges / 8-12个褶的边缘

## Components / 组成

- **Wrapper / 皮**: Wheat starch, tapioca starch, boiling water / 面粉淀粉、木薯淀粉、沸水
- **Filling / 馅**: Shrimp, bamboo shoots, ginger, scallion / 虾、竹笋、姜、葱
- **Folding / 包法**: Classic crescent shape with pleats / 经典月牙形带褶

## Practice Tips / 练习要点

Mastering har gow is the first step in dim sum practice: perfect translucent skin and pleats require significant practice.

## Hosting Tip / 制作提示

Freeze raw har gow and steam from frozen. Do not oversteam — max 6-8 minutes.
