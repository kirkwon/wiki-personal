# Cornbread

Cornbread is a Southern staple baked in a cast iron skillet, made with cornmeal, flour, buttermilk, and eggs for a tender, slightly sweet crumb.

## Key Details
- **Base**: Cornmeal and flour
- **Leavening**: Baking powder, baking soda
- **Liquid**: Buttermilk, eggs
- **Fat**: Butter (for skillet)
- **Bake temperature**: 425°F (220°C)
- **Bake time**: 20-25 minutes

## Technique
1. Preheat cast iron skillet with butter
2. Mix dry ingredients
3. Mix wet ingredients
4. Combine (don't overmix)
5. Pour into hot skillet
6. Bake 20-25 minutes at 425°F
