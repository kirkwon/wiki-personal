# Smarter Faster Better - Charles Duhigg

Charles Duhigg's *Smarter Faster Better* explores how motivation, teams, and processes drive productivity and change. The book examines how information flow, psychological safety, and strategic choice enable both personal and organizational transformation.

## Key Ideas
- Motivation as the driver of self-direction
- How teams succeed through psychological safety and diverse perspectives
- The role of cognitive tunnels and strategic choice in decision-making
- Process redesign as a vehicle for organizational change

## Connection to Change

Duhigg's framework represents organizational change — the idea that change happens through redesigning how people work together, how information flows, and how teams make decisions.
