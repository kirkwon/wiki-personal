# Zero to One - Peter Thiel & Blake Masters

*Zero to One* is a book by Peter Thiel and Blake Masters that explores the nature of innovation and competition. The central thesis is that true innovation involves vertical progress—going from zero to one—rather than horizontal progress, which is merely copying what already works.

Key ideas include:
- Competition is for losers; monopolistic thinking drives genuine innovation
- Contrarian thinking is essential for creating breakthroughs
- Building the future requires asking questions that no one else is asking

The book serves as one of the foundational sources for understanding innovation as a mental model in the context of this wiki.
