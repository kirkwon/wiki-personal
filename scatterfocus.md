# Scatterfocus

Scatterfocus is the deliberate practice of letting the mind wander without directing it toward a specific task. Unlike distraction, which is reactive and pulls attention away, scatterfocus is an intentional mode where unfocused thinking generates creative insights, restores mental energy, and allows subconscious processing of complex problems.

## Key Properties
- Not the same as procrastination or boredom
- Mind actively processes information even without directed focus
- Drives novel connections and creative breakthroughs
- Prevents attention depletion and burnout from sustained [[hyperfocus]]
- Can be triggered by low-stimulation environments (walking, showering, staring out a window)

## Relationship to Creativity
Scatterfocus enables the "incubation" phase of creative work. After intensive [[hyperfocus]] on a problem, scatterfocus allows the subconscious mind to rearrange and synthesize information, often producing insights that conscious effort missed.

## When to Deploy
- After intensive focused work blocks
- When stuck on a problem despite effort
- During rest periods between demanding tasks
- As a regular practice for creative fields
