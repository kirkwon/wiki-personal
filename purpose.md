# Wiki Purpose

## Core Mission
To build and maintain a structured, interlinked personal knowledge base that facilitates lifelong learning and quick reference across my primary domains of interest. This wiki acts as an external brain, synthesizing information from diverse sources into actionable insights and a cohesive world model.

## Primary Domains
- **Cooking & Gastronomy:** Master techniques (wok, seafood, sauces), document recipes, and explore flavor theory and food science.
- **Photography:** Deepen understanding of composition, lighting, and post-processing (Lightroom/Photoshop) while documenting gear and neighborhood-specific guides.
- **Personal Finance:** Track investment strategies (factor investing, momentum), tax optimization (HSA, Roth, 1031 exchange), and retirement planning to ensure long-term financial health.
- **General Knowledge & Second Brain:** Collect mental models, cognitive biases, and productivity frameworks to improve decision-making and cognitive efficiency.

## Key Research Questions
1. How can complex culinary techniques be broken down into repeatable first principles?
2. What are the optimal tax and investment strategies for wealth preservation in the current economic climate?
3. How do various mental models interact to provide a more accurate prediction of complex systems?
4. What are the specific lighting and composition patterns that define high-quality street photography in San Francisco?

## Scope & Boundaries
- **In-Scope:** Technical guides, synthesis of multiple books/articles, personal experiments, and neighborhood-specific research.
- **Out-of-Scope:** Transient notes, non-actionable trivia, and topics unrelated to the core domains mentioned above.
