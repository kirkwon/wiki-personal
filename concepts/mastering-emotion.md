# Mastering Emotion

Mastering emotion refers to the practice of regulating emotional responses to maintain mental clarity, resilience, and focus when facing challenges. It is a central theme in Stoic philosophy and in Ryan Holiday's approach to overcoming obstacles.

## Stoic Approach
The Stoic tradition, as discussed in *The Obstacle Is the Way*, teaches that emotions are responses to our judgments about events — not to the events themselves. By examining and adjusting these judgments, we can maintain composure and clarity under pressure.

## Tension with Modern Perspectives
This concept sits in some tension with approaches that value emotional awareness and integration, such as those in [[emotion-and-intuition-in-decisions]] and [[dual-process-theory]]. The question of whether mastery means control or wise channeling remains open.
