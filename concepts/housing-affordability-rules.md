# Housing Affordability Rules

Guidelines for determining how much home you can afford, emphasizing that housing is typically the largest expense and overextension creates financial fragility.

## Rules of Thumb

- Maximum home price: 2-3x annual income
- Total housing cost: < 28-30% of gross income
- Include mortgage, taxes, insurance, and maintenance in calculations

## Example

- $100k income → max home price $200k-$300k
- Max monthly payment: ~$2,333 (28% of $100k/12)

## Hidden Costs

- Maintenance: 1-2% of home value annually
- Property taxes: varies widely by location
- Utilities: often higher than renting
- Repairs: ongoing and unpredictable

## Rent vs Buy

- **Buy if**: staying 7+ years, stable job, want control
- **Rent if**: might move, uncertain job, flexibility needed

## Caveats

In high-cost markets like San Francisco, the 2-3x income rule is impractical. Geographic market realities may require adjusting expectations or choosing to rent.
