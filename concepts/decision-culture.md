# Decision Culture

Decision culture refers to the organizational and personal environments that support effective decision-making processes. As discussed by Annie Duke in *How to Decide*, fostering a decision culture becomes increasingly vital as organizations grow larger, requiring leadership and structural changes to embed thoughtful decision practices.

Key aspects include:
- Developing shared frameworks for evaluating decisions based on process quality rather than just outcomes
- Creating environments where diverse perspectives are welcomed in team decisions
- Establishing norms around uncertainty acceptance and probabilistic thinking
- Building feedback loops that separate decision quality from outcome quality
