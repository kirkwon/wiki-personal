# Heat-Based Techniques

Heat-based techniques are cooking methods that rely on the application of heat to transform ingredients. These are foundational to virtually all forms of cooking and include methods ranging from high-heat searing to gentle steaming. Mastery of heat-based techniques allows for control over flavor development, texture, and moisture retention in finished dishes.

## Overview
Heat-based techniques are categorized by how heat is applied and controlled:
- **Direct dry heat**: Searing, grilling, roasting
- **Indirect moist heat**: Steaming, braising, poaching
- **High-heat rapid cooking**: Wok cooking, stir-frying, flash frying

## Key Techniques
- [[Searing]] - Uses high heat to trigger the [[Maillard Reaction]], developing deep flavor on protein surfaces
- [[Steaming]] - Applies gentle, moist heat to preserve nutrients and moisture
- [[Braising]] - Combines moist and dry heat to tenderize tough cuts over extended cooking times
- [[Wok Hei]] - High-heat stir-fry technique that imparts a characteristic smoky flavor
- [[Wok Cooking]] - Wok-based rapid cooking methods
