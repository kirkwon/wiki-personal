# Inbox Resistance

Inbox Resistance refers to techniques for managing email overload and establishing boundaries with digital communication to reduce stress and increase productivity. Timothy Ferriss devotes a chapter to this topic, advocating for proactive control over inboxes rather than reactive responding.

The concept addresses a common friction point in modern work life: the tendency to let email dictate priorities. By setting communication boundaries and outsourcing or batching email tasks, individuals can reclaim focused time for higher-value activities. This connects to broader themes of [[systematic-decision-making]] and [[outsourcing-your-life]].
