# Yes/No Rule

If the decision isn't a "hell yes," it's a no. Prevents commitment creep and protects your time for what matters. Derek Sivers' version: "If it's not a 'fuck yes,' it's a no."

## Application

When faced with a decision (invitation, project, commitment), ask yourself if you feel enthusiastic and energized about it. If it's not a clear "yes!" with excitement, treat it as a no. This protects your time and energy for what truly matters.

## Personal Reflection

I used to say yes to things out of guilt or fear of missing out. This rule has helped me be more selective and feel less overwhelmed by commitments.
