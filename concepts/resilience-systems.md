# Resilience (Systems)

Resilience is the ability of a system to absorb disturbance and maintain its essential function. Meadows identifies diversity, feedback loops, and the capacity for self-organization as key sources of resilience in complex systems.

A resilient system can withstand shocks without collapsing into a degraded state, and can reorganize when conditions change. This contrasts with efficiency-focused designs that may be brittle — optimized for one condition but vulnerable to disruption.

Resilience connects to [[carrying-capacity]] and [[the-limits-to-growth]], as resource constraints and ecological limits directly affect a system's ability to maintain function under stress.
