# Knowledge Ecosystem Ops

> Operate the personal knowledge ecosystem: GBrain graph management, unified multi-system search, and academic paper ingestion pipelines. Covers orphan detection/remediation, parallel search across GBrain/NotebookLM/books/videos, and end-to-end arXiv→NotebookLM multi-modal artifact generation.

## Overview

- **Shared Infrastructure** — All three workflows depend on these tools and data paths:
- **Section A: GBrain Orphan Remediation** — Diagnose and resolve orphan pages (pages with no incoming links) in GBrain knowledge graphs. Covers auto-linking strategies, metadata extraction, category hub creation, and pipeline patching to prevent future orphans.
- **Section B: Unified Knowledge Search** — Deploy and operate the `knowledge-connect` CLI — a unified search interface that queries across GBrain, NotebookLM, unified_books, and unified_videos simultaneously with RRF fusion ranking and TTL caching.
