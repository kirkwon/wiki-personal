# Options

The specific alternatives available within a decision context. While options are necessary for choice, an overabundance of options creates psychological costs.

## Key Idea
Options are the building blocks of decisions, but too many options lead to [[choice-overload]] and [[decision-paralysis]].^[The Paradox of Choice - Barry Schwartz.md]

## Practical Approach
- Set deliberate limits on how many options you evaluate.
- Filter options through values before deep analysis.
- Recognize when the cost of evaluating one more option exceeds its potential benefit.
