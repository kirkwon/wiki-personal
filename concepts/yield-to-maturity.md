# Yield to Maturity (YTM)

The total return anticipated on a bond if held to maturity, accounting for coupon payments and any capital gain or loss.

## Usage

Tracked in the [[bond-investment-template]] alongside coupon rate, current yield, duration, and credit rating. Provides a comprehensive yield measure that differs from current yield by incorporating the price paid relative to face value.
