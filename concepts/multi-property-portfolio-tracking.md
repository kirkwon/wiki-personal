# Multi-Property Portfolio Tracking

Aggregated performance metrics across a portfolio of investment properties, enabling investors to assess overall portfolio health and make rebalancing decisions.

## Portfolio Summary
- Total properties, total investment, total market value
- Total equity, average LTV
- Total monthly cash flow, annualized cash flow
- Portfolio cap rate

## Individual Property Performance Table
Each property tracked for purchase price, current value, monthly rent, monthly expenses, net cash flow, cash on cash return, and cap rate. Portfolio totals roll up across all properties.

## Portfolio-Level Analysis
- Total monthly income and expenses
- Total monthly and annual cash flow
- Portfolio value and equity
- Portfolio LTV, cash on cash, cap rate, debt coverage ratio

## Connection to Sensitivity Analysis
The multi-property template integrates what-if scenarios at the portfolio level, testing how changes in purchase price, interest rates, rent levels, and expenses affect aggregated returns. This connects to [[sensitivity-analysis-investing]] and broader [[scenario-planning]] frameworks.
