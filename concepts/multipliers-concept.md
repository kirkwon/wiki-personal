# Multipliers

Multipliers is a leadership concept developed by Greg McKeown that identifies two types of leaders: those who multiply the intelligence and capability of their teams, and those who inadvertently diminish it.

## Key Distinctions
- **Multipliers**: Leaders who draw out the best thinking from their people. They create environments where individuals feel safe to take intellectual risks, share ideas, and contribute their full capability.
- **Diminishers**: Leaders who, often unintentionally, suppress talent by creating fear, hoarding authority, or sending signals that their team members' contributions aren't valued.

## Connection to Mindset
McKeown's framework extends the concept of mindset into organizational contexts. A leader's assumptions about their team's capabilities—essentially their leadership mindset—directly shape whether they multiply or diminish the intelligence around them.
