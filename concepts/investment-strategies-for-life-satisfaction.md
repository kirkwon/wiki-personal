# Investment Strategies for Life Satisfaction

Galloway's financial chapter advocates for long-term investments aligned with personal values over short-term gains. The approach mirrors sound personal finance principles: investing consistently, avoiding impulsive spending, and ensuring financial decisions reflect deeper life priorities. The chapter connects financial discipline to overall wellbeing, arguing that money is a means to support purpose rather than an end in itself.

This concept extends [[personal-finance]] by explicitly linking financial strategy to life satisfaction. It parallels [[real-estate-investment]] and [[retirement-planning]] through the emphasis on long-term positioning, and connects to [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]] by valuing the process of aligned decision-making over immediate returns.
