# Optionality Valuer

> Use when valuing flexibility and future decision rights in decisions with significant uncertainty — investments that create options for future actions (learning, networking, asset building). Recognizes the intrinsic worth of the ability to adapt or pivot later.

## Overview

- **Purpose** — Explicitly value flexibility and future decision rights in opportunities. Recognizes that the ability to adapt, pivot, or choose later has intrinsic worth beyond immediate outcomes, especially in uncertain environments.
- **When to Apply** — - Decisions with significant uncertainty about future conditions - Investments that create options for future actions (learning, networking, asset building) - When preserving ability to respond to unknown future opportunities is valuable - Early-stage decisions where path dependence creates valuable flexibility - Comparing options where one preserves more future choice than others
- **Core Principles** — - **Optionality Has Value**: The right to choose later is valuable, especially when uncertain - **Asymmetric Value**: Options gain more from favorable outcomes than they lose from unfavorable ones - **Volatility Benefits**: Optionality increases in value with uncertainty and change - **Irreversibility Cost**: Losing flexibility has real costs, even if not immediately apparent - **Strategic Patience**: Sometimes the best action is preserving the ability to actiability,current value based on the underlying asset. -tailed by stochastic of** or ( for the    or  the  as  u is time  or) of  n   asse
