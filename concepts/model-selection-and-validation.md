# Model selection and validation

The process of choosing appropriate predictive models and testing their performance. Silver emphasizes that the choice of model shapes what can be predicted and understood.

## Key Ideas

- Different models are suited to different types of problems
- Validate models against historical data through backtesting
- Compare simple vs complex models to avoid overfitting
- Test predictions against naive baselines
- Use Monte Carlo simulations to model uncertainty
