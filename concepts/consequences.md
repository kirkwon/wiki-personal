# Consequences

Map the second- and third-order effects of a decision. "And then what?" asked 3 times. Prevents solving one problem while creating a bigger one downstream.

## Application

For any important decision, ask "What happens if I do this?" Then ask "And then what?" at least three times to map out both immediate and downstream consequences. This helps identify unintended negative effects before they occur.

## Personal Reflection

I've used this to avoid quick fixes that created maintenance headaches later, like choosing a convenient but inflexible technology that became costly to replace.
