# Infrastructure Investments

Infrastructure investments target essential public services — toll roads, utilities, ports, and communication networks. They offer stable cash flows from monopolies or oligopolies, inflation-protected regulated rates, long-term contracts, and non-cyclical revenue, but face political/regulatory risk, long investment horizons, and capital intensity.

## Types
- Toll roads: Bridge/road tolls
- Utilities: Pipelines, power grids
- Ports: Shipping terminals
- Communication: Cell towers, fiber optics

## Key Risks
- Political and regulatory risk
- Long investment horizon
- Capital intensive
- Limited growth potential
