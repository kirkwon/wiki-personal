# Decision Timing

Decision timing is the critical factor in knowing when to quit. Duke emphasizes understanding market trends, personal energy levels, and external factors that may signal a strategic retreat. The right decision made at the wrong time can be as harmful as the wrong decision.

This concept connects to [[scenario-planning]], which involves anticipating how situations may evolve. It also relates to [[real-options-analysis]], which treats decisions as options that can be exercised, deferred, or abandoned based on changing conditions.

The [[the-power-of-timing]] chapter in Duke's book frames timing as a learnable skill — not just intuition but a factor that can be analyzed and improved. See [[quit-the-power-of-knowing-when-to-walk-away---annie-duke]].
