# Factor Crowding & Rotation

Factor crowding occurs when many investors pile into the same factor, potentially driving up valuations and causing underperformance. Factor rotation refers to the tendency for different factors to outperform in different market regimes.

## Signs of Factor Crowding

- High valuations for factor stocks
- Media hype around a particular factor
- Factor performance divergence from fundamentals

## Regime-Dependent Factor Performance

| Market Regime | Outperforming Factors |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Bull market (growth) | Momentum, Quality |
| Bear market | Value, Low Volatility |
| Recovery | Small Cap, Momentum |
| High inflation | Value, Small Cap |

## Diversification Guidance

- Don't over-concentrate in one factor
- Maintain broad market exposure (core + factors)
- Monitor factor correlations to avoid highly correlated factors
