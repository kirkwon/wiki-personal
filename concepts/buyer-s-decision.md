# Buyer's Decision

Map the buying process: need recognition → information search → evaluation → purchase → post-purchase feelings. Apply to any "selling" you do — of ideas, proposals, or yourself.

## Application

When preparing to sell an idea, proposal, or yourself, walk through the buyer's journey: 1) Need recognition: help them see they have a problem. 2) Information search: provide the information they need. 3) Evaluation: make it easy to compare options. 4) Purchase: reduce friction to act. 5) Post-purchase: follow up to ensure satisfaction and address concerns.

## Personal Reflection

I've applied this when proposing new projects at work, focusing on helping stakeholders recognize the need, providing clear information, making evaluation straightforward, and ensuring smooth implementation.
