# Johari Window

A four-pane grid mapping what you know about yourself vs. what others know about you: Open (known to both), Blind (others see it, you don't), Hidden (you know, others don't), Unknown (neither). Use it through feedback and self-disclosure to expand the Open pane.

## Application

Use feedback and self-disclosure to expand the Open pane and reduce Blind/Hidden areas.

## Personal Reflection

My blind spot per past feedback: I optimize for correctness at the expense of warmth, and read directness as efficiency while others read it as coldness. Expanding the Open pane means asking for feedback explicitly instead of waiting for it.
