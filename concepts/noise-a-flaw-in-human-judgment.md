# Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment

"Noise: A Flaw in Human Judgment" is Daniel Kahneman's follow-up to "Thinking, Fast and Slow," co-authored with Olivier Sibony and Cass R. Sunstein. It identifies "noise"—unwanted variability in judgments—as a pervasive and often overlooked problem in human decision-making.

## Key Concept: Noise vs. Bias

Kahneman distinguishes noise from bias:
- **Bias** is a systematic, directional error (e.g., consistently pessimistic forecasts)
- **Noise** is random variability in judgment (e.g., inconsistent risk assessments across evaluators)

Noise is significant because it means that even competent professionals make inconsistent decisions when evaluating similar situations. This variability can be reduced through structured decision processes.
