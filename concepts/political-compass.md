# Political Compass

A 2D political spectrum (economic left-right × social authoritarian-libertarian) repurposed as a self-assessment tool to locate your worldview. Use it to understand your biases and how you relate to others' perspectives.

## Application

Take the Political Compass test to see where you fall on economic and social axes, then reflect on how this shapes your worldview and interactions.

## Personal Reflection

Knowing where I sit (economically market-leaning, socially libertarian, high epistemic autonomy) helps me notice which arguments I'll under-weight by default — usually ones favoring coordination or constraint. Deliberate steelmanning is the countermeasure.
