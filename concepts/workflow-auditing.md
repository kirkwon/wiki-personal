# Workflow auditing

Workflow auditing is the practice of systematically reviewing how time, attention, and effort are spent in order to identify efficiency losses and optimize output. It involves tracking current patterns, eliminating low-value activities, focusing on high-impact tasks, and iterating on approaches based on measured results.

This concept is central to the "Vulnerability-based trust assessment" skill card, though the name is misleading — the actual content describes generic productivity optimization rather than trust-related assessment.

## Key practices
- Identify where efficiency losses occur
- Track how time and attention are currently spent
- Note patterns that lead to wasted effort
- Focus on high-impact activities
- Eliminate or delegate low-value tasks
- Measure impact of changes and adjust
- Standardize effective approaches for reuse
