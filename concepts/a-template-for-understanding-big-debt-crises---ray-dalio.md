# A Template for Understanding Big Debt Crises - Ray Dalio

Ray Dalio's framework for understanding how big debt crises unfold and how they are managed. This template describes the debt cycle, credit allocation, and deleveraging phases that drive economic behavior.^[raw/articles/Economies.md]

## Key Concepts

- **Debt cycles** — The recurring pattern of borrowing, spending, and deleveraging that drives economic expansions and contractions
- **Credit allocation** — How credit is distributed through the economy and the impact of credit expansion vs contraction
- **Deleveraging** — The painful but necessary process of reducing debt levels, which can involve austerity, debt restructuring, or money printing
- **Template framework** — A repeatable pattern that has characterized major debt crises throughout history
