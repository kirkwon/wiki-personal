# The Many Model Thinker

The central thesis of Scott E. Page's *The Model Thinker*: using multiple models in concert provides better understanding of complex phenomena than relying on any single framework. Each model has blind spots, and diversity of perspective reveals what any individual model misses.

## Key Ideas

- **Complementarity**: Different models capture different aspects of reality. Linear models reveal proportional relationships; power law models reveal concentration; network models reveal connection structure.
- **Reduced blind spots**: No single model captures all relevant dynamics. Multiple models triangulate on truth.
- **Practical over theoretical**: Page emphasizes application, validation, and creative model building over mathematical elegance.
- **Model diversity as skill**: Building a toolkit of models you can apply across domains is a core competency.

## Connection to Existing Frameworks

This approach mediates between [[thinking-fast-and-slow---daniel-kahneman]]'s emphasis on cognitive limitations and [[naturalistic-decision-making]]'s reliance on pattern recognition. It also complements [[superforecasting-methodology]]'s emphasis on aggregating diverse perspectives.
