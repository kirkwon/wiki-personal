# 25x Expenses Rule

The 25x expenses rule is a core retirement planning heuristic: your portfolio should be 25 times your annual retirement expenses. This derives from the 4% withdrawal rule — if you withdraw 4% per year, you need 25x your annual income to sustain the portfolio.

**Example**:
- Annual expenses: $60k
- Portfolio target: $60k × 25 = $1.5M

The rule assumes Social Security income reduces the portfolio draw, so the target should be adjusted downward by the present value of Social Security benefits.

A more precise method:
- Annual retirement income needed = Current annual expenses × (1 - Savings rate in retirement)
- Portfolio needed = Annual income ÷ Withdrawal rate

This concept is central to [[retirement-planning]] and works alongside [[social-security-breakeven-analysis]] to determine total income needs.
