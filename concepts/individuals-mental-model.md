# Individuals Mental Model

The **Individuals** mental model is a conceptual tool that helps understand and navigate complex situations by providing a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Individuals.md]

## Definition
Individuals functions as a framework for recognizing how individual actors — people, decision-makers, contributors — generate observable patterns across domains. It is not about studying individuals in isolation but about using the lens of individual behavior and agency to decode broader systemic patterns.

## Cross-Domain Applicability
This model is referenced across six distinct sources spanning very different fields:

- **Richard H. Thaler** — [[Misbehaving - Richard H Thaler]]: Behavioral economics shows how individual cognitive biases create market-level patterns.
- **Cal Newport** — [[Deep Work - Cal Newport]]: Individual focus and deliberate practice drive exceptional productivity outcomes.
- **Ray Dalio** — [[Principles for Dealing with the Changing World Order - Ray Dalio]]: Individual decision-making patterns aggregate into macroeconomic cycles and geopolitical shifts.
- **Greg McKeown** — [[Multipliers - Greg McKeown]] and [[Effortless - Greg McKeown]]: Individual leaders either multiply or diminish the effectiveness of their teams through how they frame problems and allocate effort.
- **Peter M. Senge** — [[Presence_ Human Purpose and the Field of the Future - Peter M Senge]]: Individual awareness and presence shape systemic outcomes in organizations and communities.

## Key Insight
The model's strength is its cross-domain recurrence. Whether in behavioral economics, productivity science, macroeconomics, leadership theory, or systems thinking, the pattern of looking at how individuals act and decide to understand larger dynamics is consistently valuable.^[raw/articles/Individuals.md]
