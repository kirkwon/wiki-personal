# Whitmore

Sir John Whitmore's coaching model (GROW): Goal → Reality → Options → Will. A structured self-coaching dialogue. Use it to talk yourself through any challenge.

## Application

When facing a challenge, walk through the GROW stages: 1) Goal: What do you want to achieve? 2) Reality: What is the current situation? 3) Options: What could you do? 4) Will: What will you commit to doing? Use this structure for self-coaching or coaching others.

## Personal Reflection

I often jump straight to options without clearly defining the goal or assessing reality. Using GROW has helped me make more thoughtful decisions by ensuring I understand what I'm trying to achieve and what resources I actually have available.
