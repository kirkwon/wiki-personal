# Dollar-Cost Averaging

Investing the same amount at regular intervals regardless of price, which eliminates market timing risk and removes emotion from investing.

## How It Works

- Invest same amount every month (e.g., $500/month to Roth IRA)
- Buy more shares when prices are low
- Buy fewer shares when prices are high
- Over time, the average cost per share evens out

## Why It Matters

- Eliminates market timing risk
- Removes emotion from investing decisions
- Automatic monthly contributions make this happen by default
- "Time in market > timing market"
