# Hard Choice

When two options are roughly equal in value, the rational framework breaks down. Ruth Chang's insight: in hard choices, you are the author of your own values — the choice itself creates who you become.

## Application

When facing a hard choice, recognize that you get to define your values through the decision, rather than trying to find a "right" answer.

## Personal Reflection

Most of my real decisions aren't analysis-starved, they're commitment-starved — two options genuinely close. Chang's framing helps: stop looking for the discriminating metric and accept that choosing IS the value-creation step.
