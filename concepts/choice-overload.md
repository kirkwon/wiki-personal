# Choice Overload

More options = worse decisions (the paradox of choice). When faced with >7 options, people either freeze or choose poorly. Constrain your options deliberately before deciding.

## Application

Limit your choices intentionally. When faced with many options, pre-select a manageable subset (e.g., top 3) based on key criteria, then decide from that smaller set. This reduces decision fatigue and improves quality.

## Personal Reflection

I've noticed that when researching tools or services, I often get stuck comparing dozens of options. Applying this principle helps me set a deadline and narrow the field quickly.
