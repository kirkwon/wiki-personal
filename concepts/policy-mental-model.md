# Policy as a Mental Model

Policy is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition

Policy serves as a universal mental model applicable across domains. It functions as a principle for making sense of patterns and relationships in complex situations, acting as a mental shortcut rather than a rigid rule.

## Dalio's Perspective

In Ray Dalio's framework from [[How the Economic Machine Works - Ray Dalio]], policy operates at the level of systemic rules and incentive structures rather than individual decisions. Monetary and fiscal policy shape economic outcomes at a macro level, influencing the behavior of markets, governments, and institutions.^[raw/articles/Policy.md]

## Relationship to Other Mental Models

Policy connects to several existing mental models in the wiki:

- [[settings]] — policy establishes the contextual rules within which decisions occur
- [[environment]] — policy shapes the environment in which systems operate
