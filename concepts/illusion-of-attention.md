# Illusion of Attention

The tendency to believe we notice everything when we actually miss far more than we realize. Despite feeling fully attentive, our attention is highly selective and we miss significant information.

## Key Points
- We overestimate the completeness of our awareness
- Connects to [[inattention-to-results]] — failing to notice important outcomes
- Connects to [[tunnel-vision]] — focus blinding to alternatives
- Connects to [[salience-effect]] — focusing on what's noticeable, not important

## Practical Example
Missing a gorilla walking through a basketball game while counting passes — the classic selective attention experiment.

## Guardrail
Build in systematic checks and second opinions. Don't rely solely on your sense that you've noticed everything.
