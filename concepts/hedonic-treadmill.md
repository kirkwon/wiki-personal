# Hedonic Treadmill

The tendency to adapt to changes in circumstances and return to a baseline level of happiness. Positive events (promotion, new car, lottery win) provide temporary boosts but we quickly adjust and return to our previous emotional state.

## Key Points
- Happiness is less determined by events than by our baseline adaptation level
- Connects to [[relative-deprivation]] — satisfaction depends on comparisons
- Connects to [[expectation]] — expectations shape experiences
- Connects to [[lasting-effect]] — we overestimate emotional impact of future events

## Practical Implication
Since we adapt to both gains and losses, strategies for sustainable well-being should focus on habits, relationships, and meaning rather than chasing discrete achievements.

## Guardrail
When evaluating potential life changes, account for adaptation. The expected happiness boost will likely be shorter-lived than predicted.
