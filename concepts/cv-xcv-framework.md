# CV = XCV Framework

CV = XCV is Scott Galloway's central analogy in *The Algebra of Happiness*, treating life decisions as a competitive strategy problem. Just as a company's choices (CV) produce market outcomes (XCV), an individual's life choices—career, relationships, investments, identity—produce corresponding life outcomes. The framework encourages treating personal decisions with the same rigor and long-term orientation applied to business strategy.

This concept connects to [[strategic-vs-reactive-thinking]] by emphasizing proactive, deliberate choice-making rather than reactive responses. It also relates to [[decision-quality-vs-outcome-quality]] by separating the quality of the decision process from the eventual result, much as [[real-options-analysis]] treats decisions as flexible options rather than irreversible commitments.
