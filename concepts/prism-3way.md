# Prism 3way

> Three orthogonal analytical operations (WHERE/WHEN/WHY) + cross-operation synthesis. Each operation attacks the problem from a fundamentally different angle. The disagreements between the three ARE the valuable output. Works on any domain — code, business, strategy, design, text.

## Overview

- **When to Use** — Use when one angle is not enough and the artifact is not necessarily code — a business plan, a strategy doc, a research note, an architecture as well as a source file. Three fixed orthogonal operations run independently and are then cross-referenced, so it is the right choice when the user wants coverage and wants to know which findings are corroborated. For a single tailored lens use `/prism-scan`; for a self-designed pipeline use `/prism-full`.
- **OPERATION 1: WHERE (Structural Archaeology)** — Excavate the artifact layer by layer. Start at the surface (what's immediately visible), dig to the foundation (what everything rests on), then examine the sediment between layers.
- **OPERATION 2: WHEN (Temporal Simulation)** — Run the artifact forward through 3-5 concrete cycles of change (maintenance, growth, evolution, external pressure). For each cycle: - What breaks? - What calcifies into permanent behavior that nobody questions? - What knowledge is lost?

## Further detail

### OPERATION 3: WHY (Structural Impossibility)

Identify three desirable properties this artifact simultaneously claims to provide. Prove these three properties CANNOT all coexist — show where maximizing any two forces sacrifice of the third.
