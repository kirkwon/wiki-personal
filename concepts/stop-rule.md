# Stop Rule

Pre-commit to an exit condition: "If X happens, I stop." Prevents sunk-cost escalation. Used in gambling (set a loss limit), investing (stop-loss), and relationships.

## Application

Before starting a project or commitment, define clear conditions under which you will walk away (e.g., budget exceeded, timeline doubled, loss of interest). Honor those conditions without exception.

## Personal Reflection

I've used this to avoid throwing good money after bad in investments and to end unproductive collaborations before they drained too much energy.
