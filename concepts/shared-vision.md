# Shared Vision

Shared vision is one of Senge's five disciplines, covering methods to create common understanding among stakeholders about desired futures through dialogue and collaborative visioning exercises.

## Core Ideas

- Common understanding among stakeholders about desired futures
- Created through dialogue and collaborative visioning exercises
- Distinguishes shared vision from mandated vision or individual aspirations alone
- Requires open, honest communication to develop authentically

## Key Practices

- Collaborative visioning exercises that engage diverse perspectives
- Dialogue that surfaces individual aspirations and connects them to collective goals
- Building alignment without requiring uniformity of thought
