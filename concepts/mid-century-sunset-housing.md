# Mid-Century Sunset Housing

The Sunset District is characterized by mid-century modern tract homes built between the 1940s and 1960s. These single-family homes are the dominant housing type in the neighborhood and feature distinctive architectural elements including bay windows, hardwood floors, and yards (front and/or back).

## Key Features

- **Time period**: 1940s-1960s construction
- **Style**: Mid-century modern tract homes
- **Layout**: Single-family homes predominate; some row houses and multi-family buildings
- **Bay windows, hardwood floors, yards**: Typical features
- **Garages and driveways**: Common, representing a major advantage rare elsewhere in San Francisco

## Market Overview (2024)

- Median home price: ~$1.3-1.5 million
- 2BR rent: ~$2,800-3,200
- More affordable than Mission, Noe Valley, and Pacific Heights

The combination of driveways and garages, along with the relatively flat terrain, makes the Sunset particularly attractive for families and those who value parking, which is scarce in most San Francisco neighborhoods.
