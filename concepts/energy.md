# Energy

Map your activities on a grid: which ones give you energy vs. drain you. Helps optimize your schedule by front-loading energy-giving tasks and minimizing drainers.

## Application

Track activities for a week, note energy gained/lost, then schedule more energizing tasks and reduce drainers.

## Personal Reflection

Deep analytical build sessions give energy; context-switching across many chat threads drains it. Scheduling consequence: batch similar tasks and protect morning blocks for the highest-leverage work instead of fragmenting them with reactive replies.
