# Japanese Brewing (日本冲泡法)

Japanese brewing methods emphasize precision and mindfulness, with specific parameters for different tea types. Temperature and steeping time vary significantly by tea variety.

## Sencha
- Temperature: 175-185°F (79-85°C)
- Amount: 2 tsp per 6 oz water
- Time: 1-2 minutes
- Technique: Pour gently, avoid agitation

## Gyokuro
- Temperature: 140-160°F (60-71°C)
- Amount: 2-3 tsp per 6 oz water
- Time: 2-3 minutes
- Technique: Lower temperature, longer steep

## Matcha
- Temperature: 175°F (80°C)
- Amount: 2 tsp matcha per 6 oz water
- Technique: Whisk vigorously to froth
