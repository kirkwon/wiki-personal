# Cognitive Dissonance

Festinger's theory: when your beliefs contradict your actions, you feel mental discomfort and rationalize to resolve it. Use it to catch yourself justifying decisions that don't align with your values.

## Application

Notice when you rationalize actions that conflict with your beliefs, and use the discomfort as a signal to realign.

## Personal Reflection

I notice this most in trading: holding losers while narrating why the thesis is intact. The discomfort is data — when I catch myself building an elaborate justification, that's the signal to re-underwrite the position from scratch rather than defend it.
