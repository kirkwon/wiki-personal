# Asymmetry Hunter

> Use when evaluating opportunities with uncertain outcomes for asymmetric payoffs: limited downside, potentially unlimited upside. Targets option-like situations where you have the right but not the obligation to benefit.

## Overview

- **Purpose** — Seek opportunities with asymmetric payoffs: limited downside, potentially unlimited upside. Focuses on finding "option-like" situations where you have the right but not the obligation to benefit from favorable outcomes.
- **When to Apply** — - Evaluating opportunities with uncertain outcomes - Considering investments of time, money, or reputation - Assessing learning opportunities or skill acquisition - When you want to limit potential losses while preserving upside potential - Early-stage decision making where outcomes are highly uncertain
- **Core Principles** — - **Asymmetric Payoff**: Downside limited, upside potentially unbounded - **Optionality**: Having the right, not obligation, to take future actions - **Convexity**: Benefiting from volatility and uncertainty (rather than being harmed by it) - **Contrarian Opportunities**: Finding asymmetry where others see only risk - **Non-Linearity**: Small inputs can lead to disproportionately large outputs

## Further detail

### Output Structure

1. **Downside Analysis**: Maximum possible loss, probability of loss, impact assessment 2. **Upside Analysis**: Potential gains, probability distribution, scalability 3. **Asymmetry Ratio**: Upside potential / Downside risk (target > 2:1) 4. **Option Value**: Value of flexibility and future decision rights 5. **Convexity Assessment**: How the situation benefits from volatility/uncertainty 6. **Recommendation**: Clear go/no-go with position sizing guidance

### Verification

- [ ] Downside is clearly defined and bounded (not "infinite loss") - [ ] Upside potential exceeds downside by meaningful margin - [ ] Asymmetry ratio calculated and evaluated - [ ] Option value and convexity considered - [ ] Personal context and constraints factored in - [ ] Clear recommendation with sizing guidance

### Example Application

**Opportunity**: Spend 200 hours learning advanced machine learning over 3 months
