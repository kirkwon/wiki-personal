# Options Trading Guide

Comprehensive framework for understanding and implementing options trading strategies within a personal finance context.

## Scope

This guide covers LEAPS, straddles, butterflies, time decay management, and practical applications for investors including income generation, risk management, portfolio enhancement, and strategic wealth building.

## Quick Reference

**Purpose**: Options trading strategies for wealth building  
**Scope**: LEAPS, straddles, butterflies, and time decay management  
**Risk Level**: Intermediate to advanced  
**Time Horizon**: Short-term to multi-year strategies

## Key Strategies

- **LEAPS**: Long-term exposure with minimal theta decay
- **Straddles**: Volatility capture with rapid price movement requirements
- **Butterflies**: Defined risk with theta advantages for range-bound markets
- **Covered Calls**: Income generation on existing holdings
- **Credit Spreads**: Limited risk income generation
- **Protective Puts**: Downside protection
- **Collars**: Portfolio protection with income
- **Iron Condors**: Range-bound market income

## Position Sizing

- Long options: 1–5% of portfolio per position
- Short options: 5–15% of portfolio per position
- Single position limit: 5% of total portfolio

## Implementation

Start with conservative strategies (covered calls, ITM options) and gradually incorporate more complex strategies as experience grows. Always prioritize risk management and position sizing appropriate to financial situation and risk tolerance.^[raw/articles/Options Trading Guide.md]
