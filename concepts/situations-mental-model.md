# Situations Mental Model

Situations is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

## Definition
The Situations mental model functions as a universal framework for recognizing underlying patterns and relationships in complex environments. Rather than treating each situation as unique, it encourages identifying structural similarities and recurring dynamics that shape outcomes.

## Applicability
- **Universal**: Can be applied across any domain where complexity and ambiguity are present.
- **Domains**: General, with particular relevance to persuasion, decision-making, and social dynamics.

## Source
This model is attributed to Robert B. Cialdini's *Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion*.^[Situations.md]
