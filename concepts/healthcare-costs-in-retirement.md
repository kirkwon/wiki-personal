# Healthcare Costs in Retirement

Healthcare is typically the largest and most unpredictable expense in retirement and requires dedicated planning beyond Medicare. A couple retiring at age 65 can expect to spend hundreds of thousands on healthcare throughout retirement.

## Medicare Overview
- **Age 65**: Eligible for Medicare Part A (hospital)
- **Part B**: Medical insurance (premium + deductible)
- **Part D**: Prescription drugs (premium)

### What Medicare Doesn't Cover
- Long-term care (nursing home)
- Most dental, vision, hearing
- Prescription drugs fully
- Deductibles and co-pays

## Fidelity Estimate (2024)
- **Average 65-year-old couple**: $315,000 (lifetime healthcare costs)
- **Includes**: Medicare premiums, copays, deductibles, out-of-pocket
- **Excludes**: Long-term care, dental, vision, hearing (unless specified)

## Breakdown of Healthcare Costs
- **Medicare Premiums**: $140,000 (Part B + Part D, no IRMAA)
- **Out-of-Pocket**: $175,000 (copays, deductibles, uncovered services)
- **Total Medicare-related**: $315,000
- **Long-Term Care**: Variable (not included above)
- **Dental/Vision/Hearing**: Variable

## Total Estimated Cost (Couple, Age 65-85)
Using a simplified estimation worksheet:
- Medicare premiums (20 years): ~$203,860
- Out-of-pocket (20 years): ~$106,000
- Expected LTC (70% probability × $216,000 avg): ~$151,200
- Dental/vision/hearing (20 years): ~$60,000
- **Base total**: ~$521,060
- **Adjusted for 5% healthcare inflation over 20 years**: ~$1.4M

> Note: This is a simplified example. Actual costs vary widely based on health, location, lifestyle, and luck.

## Strategies
- **Maximize HSA contributions** — triple tax advantage for medical
- **Stay healthy** — preventive care, exercise, nutrition
- **Shop for care** — compare prices, use telemedicine, generics
- **Consider geographic factors** — some states have lower costs; retire overseas for lower costs
- **Plan for surprises** — emergency healthcare fund, annual reviews
- **Long-term care insurance**
- **Keep working for employer coverage**
