# Gift

Identify what comes so naturally to you that you don't even think of it as a skill. That's your gift. Others struggle with it; you do it effortlessly. Build your life around it.

## Application

Reflect on activities that you find easy and enjoyable while others find them difficult or tedious. These are likely your gifts. Seek roles and projects that allow you to use these gifts daily.

## Personal Reflection

I've realized that explaining complex technical concepts in simple terms comes naturally to me, while many colleagues struggle with it. This has guided me toward roles in teaching, documentation, and communication.
