# Role of Emotions in Decision-Making

The role of emotions in decision-making examines how emotional states shape choices, often leading to irrational outcomes that contradict purely logical economic analysis.

## Core Idea

Ariely examines how emotions play a significant role in shaping human decision-making, often leading to irrational choices that contradict economic predictions based solely on logic.^[raw/articles/the-upside-of-irrationality---dan-ariely.md]

## Key Insights

- Emotions override logic in many decision contexts
- Emotional states bias risk assessment and valuation
- Decisions made under emotional influence are predictable in their irrationality
- Understanding emotional patterns can improve decision quality

## Connection to Existing Concepts

This concept extends [[emotion-and-intuition-in-decisions]] and [[dual-process-theory]] by providing empirical behavioral economics evidence for the dominance of emotional processing in everyday choices.
