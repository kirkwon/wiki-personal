# Brief mental model

Brief is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.

The model is described as universally applicable across domains, with a confidence level of 0.7. It is referenced in Scott Galloway's *Adrift* as a mental model for processing the world.

This concept overlaps with several existing mental models in the wiki, including [[overview]], [[thinking-mental-model]], and [[solutions-mental-model]], all of which serve as navigational tools for pattern and relationship recognition in complex situations. Further clarification of how Brief differs from these existing frameworks would strengthen its distinction.
