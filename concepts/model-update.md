# Model Update

> Use when updating financial models with new data — quarterly earnings, guidance changes, or material macro events (interest rates, inflation, GDP) — to keep investment analysis accurate. Structured process for incorporating material events into coverage models.

## Overview

- **Purpose** — Update financial models with new data (earnings, macro, industry) to maintain accuracy and relevance for investment analysis. Follows a structured process to incorporate quarterly earnings, guidance changes, and other material events.
- **When to Apply** — - After quarterly earnings releases for companies in your coverage universe - When significant macroeconomic data changes (interest rates, inflation, GDP) - Following material company events (M&A, leadership changes, regulatory decisions) - When industry dynamics shift meaningfully (commodity prices, regulatory changes) - As part of regular model maintenance cycle (monthly/quarterly)
- **Core Principles** — - **Materiality Focus**: Prioritize updates that meaningfully impact valuation or investment thesis - **Consistency**: Maintain modeling methodology and assumptions unless fundamentals change - **Transparency**: Clearly document what changed and why - **Version Control**: Track model iterations to enable rollback and comparison - **Assumption Review**: Use new data to challenge and validate existing assumptions

## Further detail

### Output Structure

1. **Change Log**: Document what data triggered the update 2. **Updated Inputs**: Revised financials, assumptions, and drivers 3. **Impact Analysis**: How changes affect key outputs (valuation, ratings, price targets) 4. **Assessment**: Whether investment thesis remains intact or requires revision 5. **Next Steps**: Actions required based on updated analysis

### Verification

- [ ] All material financial statement changes incorporated - [ ] Key assumptions reviewed and justified with new data - [ ] Valuation outputs updated consistently across all methods - [ ] Impact on investment thesis clearly articulated - [ ] Limitations and areas of uncertainty disclosed - [ ] Version control updated with change summary and date

### Example Application

**Trigger**: Company XYZ reports Q2 earnings with: - Revenue: $1.2B vs. $1.15B estimate (+4.3%) - Gross margin: 42.1% vs. 40.5% estimate (+160 bps) - Management raises full-year revenue guidance 3% to $4.8-5.0B
