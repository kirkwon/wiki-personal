# Unconscious Thinking

Dijksterhuis' research: for complex decisions, gather info, then distract yourself, and let your unconscious decide. Often produces better results than overthinking. "Sleep on it" has science behind it.

## Application

For a complex decision, gather all relevant information, then engage in a distracting activity (like walking or a hobby) for a period of time, and return to the decision later. Trust that your unconscious has been working on it.

## Personal Reflection

I've found that after sleeping on a difficult technical design problem, I often wake up with a clearer solution than if I had stayed up late overanalyzing it.
