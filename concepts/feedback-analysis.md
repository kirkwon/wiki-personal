# Feedback Analysis

Peter Drucker's method: before making a decision, write down what you expect to happen. Later, compare results to expectations. Over time, reveals your patterns of over/under-confidence.

## Application

Before making an important decision, write down what you expect will happen and why. After the outcome is known, compare actual results to your expectations. Track these over time to identify whether you tend to be overconfident (results worse than expected) or underconfident (results better than expected) in different areas.

## Personal Reflection

I've discovered through this practice that I'm consistently overconfident about timelines and underconfident about my ability to handle complex technical challenges. This has helped me adjust my planning and seek help earlier.
