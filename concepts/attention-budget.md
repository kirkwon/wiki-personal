# Attention Budget

The attention budget is the concept of treating attention as a finite, precious resource that must be managed carefully throughout the day. Chris Bailey frames this in *Hyperfocus* as a way to prioritize how attention is spent.

## Core Idea
Just as money is limited, attention is limited. Every task, distraction, or decision consumes a portion of this budget. Managing it effectively means allocating attention to high-value tasks and protecting it from unnecessary drains.

## Application
- Identify peak attention hours for important work
- Avoid low-value tasks during high-energy periods
- Minimize attention residue through single-tasking
- Plan breaks and unfocused time to replenish the budget
- Use [[scatterfocus]] intentionally to restore attention

## Connection to Other Frameworks
The attention budget relates to [[quadrant-2-focus]] by suggesting that important but not urgent tasks should be prioritized when attention is high. It also connects to [[time-management-matrix]] by encouraging deliberate planning of focus periods.
