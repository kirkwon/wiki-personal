# AI Safety Methods

AI safety methods are practical approaches for improving the safety and alignment of artificial intelligence systems. According to [[The Alignment Problem - Brian Christian  Tom Griffiths]], several methods have shown promise:

- **Iterative design**: Continuously refining AI systems based on feedback and observed failures rather than attempting a perfect design upfront.
- **Testing with external agents**: Using independent evaluators or adversarial testers to identify failures and edge cases that designers might miss.
- **Transparency**: Making AI systems' reasoning and decision processes observable and auditable, enabling human oversight.
- **Collaborative research**: Sharing safety findings and coordinating efforts across organizations to avoid duplication and accelerate progress.

These methods address different aspects of the alignment challenge—from technical verification to institutional coordination—and represent the practical frontier of AI safety work.
