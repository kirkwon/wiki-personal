# Robber's Hand

A prioritization game: if a robber held a gun to your head and said "pick one thing to do today," what would it be? Forces radical prioritization. Do that thing first.

## Application

Each morning, ask yourself: "If I could only accomplish one thing today, what would it be?" Do that task first before anything else. This ensures your most important priority gets your best energy.

## Personal Reflection

This has helped me cut through procrastination on important but unpleasant tasks by forcing me to confront what truly matters versus what feels urgent or easy.
