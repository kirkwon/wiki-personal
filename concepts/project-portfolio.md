# Project Portfolio

Categorize your active projects by risk level (safe bet, long shot, moonshot). Maintain a balanced portfolio — not all safe bets, not all moonshots. Aim for a barbell strategy.

## Application

List all your current projects or major goals. Classify each as Safe Bet (low risk, predictable reward), Long Shot (high risk, high potential reward), or Moonshot (very high risk, transformative potential). Aim for a barbell strategy: allocate most resources to Safe Bets and Moonshots, with few Long Shots.

## Personal Reflection

I tend to over-index on Moonshots and neglect Safe Bets that provide steady progress. This model helps me balance my portfolio for both stability and breakthrough potential.
