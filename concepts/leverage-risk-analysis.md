# Leverage Risk Analysis

Assessment of financial risk arising from the use of debt in investment properties, evaluating metrics like Loan-to-Value (LTV) and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR).

## Leverage Metrics

- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Higher LTV = Higher risk
- **Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)**: <1.2 = High risk
- **Interest Rate Risk**: Variable rate exposure
- **Refinancing Risk**: Loan maturity and refinancing ability

## Risk Thresholds

- **Low Risk**: LTV < 60%, DSCR > 1.5, Fixed rate
- **Medium Risk**: LTV 60-75%, DSCR 1.2-1.5, Mix of rates
- **High Risk**: LTV > 75%, DSCR < 1.2, Variable rate

Leverage risk analysis is central to [[risk-assessment-framework]] and connects to [[cash-flow-stress-testing]] for evaluating financial resilience.
