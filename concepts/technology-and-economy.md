# Technology and the Economy

Technology plays a transformative role in economic growth, productivity, labor markets, and financial innovation. In Dalio's framework, technological change is one of the forces driving the economic machine forward.

Key impacts include:
- Increases in productivity and output
- Changes in labor market dynamics
- Financial innovation and new asset classes
- Potential challenges such as displacement and inequality

Understanding technology's role helps anticipate shifts in economic structure and investment opportunities.
