# Bayesian Rogue Explore

> Use when facing open-ended problems with no clear single answer: generate 3-5 varied approaches following the Id-forward Explorer principle — 1 Safe path, 1 High-Leverage move, 1 Gittins Wildcard (high uncertainty / high reward).

## Overview

- **Purpose** — Generate 3-5 varied approaches to open problems following the Id-forward Explorer principle with Lucky Bias. Outputs: 1 Safe path, 1 High-Leverage move, 1 Gittins Wildcard (high uncertainty/high reward).
- **When to Apply** — - Open-ended decisions with no clear single answer - Early-stage idea generation and brainstorming - Situations requiring creative exploration before commitment - When you need to overcome analysis paralysis - Strategic planning sessions requiring multiple pathways
- **Core Principles** — - **Id-forward Explorer**: Driven by curiosity and novelty, prioritize high-entropy ideas - **Lucky Bias**: Assume the most efficient path is discoverable through superior leverage - **Gittins-style Exploration**: Balance exploration vs exploitation with uncertainty premium - **Leverage Focus**: Seek asymmetric upside with minimal downside

## Further detail

### Output Structure

1. **Safe Path** (Conservative): Low-risk, proven approach with predictable outcomes 2. **High-Leverage Move** (Aggressive): High upside potential with bounded downside 3. **Gittins Wildcard** (Speculative): High uncertainty, potentially transformative outcome

### Verification

- [ ] All three core options presented (Safe, High-Leverage, Wildcard) - [ ] Each option includes brief rationale and expected outcome profile - [ ] Clear distinction between risk/return profiles of each option - [ ] No premature convergence - waiting for user selection - [ ] Options reflect genuine exploration, not just variants of same idea

### Example Application

**Problem**: "How should I improve my productivity system?"
