# Dry Rubs

Dry rubs are spice mixtures applied directly to meat or protein surfaces without a liquid carrier. They are used in roasting, grilling, and smoking to build flavor crusts.

## How They Work

- No liquid — just spices and seasonings applied to the surface
- Create a flavorful crust during cooking
- Can be applied hours or even days before cooking

## Example

Five-spice + salt + sugar — suitable for roasting and grilling applications.

## Combination Technique

Dry rubs can be combined with wet brining: brine first (12–24 hours), then apply dry rub before cooking, for the best of both worlds.
