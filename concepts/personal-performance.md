# Personal Performance

A framework for measuring your own output: what are the 3–5 metrics that actually matter for your role? Define them yourself rather than accepting externally imposed KPIs.

## Application

Identify 3-5 key output metrics for your role that truly reflect your performance and track them regularly.

## Personal Reflection

Externally imposed metrics (tasks closed, messages answered) reward responsiveness over leverage. My self-defined metrics should be things like: decisions logged with follow-through, cron/automation uptime, and portfolio Sharpe vs. plan.
