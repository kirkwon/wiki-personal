# Superforecasting Methodology

Superforecasting is a structured approach to making accurate probabilistic predictions, based on research identifying individuals who consistently outperform average forecasters. The methodology emphasizes that forecasting skill is learnable through specific techniques rather than innate talent alone.

## Core Techniques

- **Probabilistic thinking** — Expressing predictions as likelihoods (percentages) rather than certainties, which forces more nuanced reasoning.
- **Multiple hypotheses** — Considering several alternative explanations for any event simultaneously, rather than anchoring on a single narrative.
- **Avoiding confirmation bias** — Actively seeking disconfirming evidence and steelmanning opposing views before committing to a probability estimate.
- **Bayesian updating** — Continuously revising probability estimates as new information arrives, rather than treating initial judgments as fixed.
- **Seeking diverse perspectives** — Consulting a wide range of experts and viewpoints to counteract individual blind spots.
- **Base rate sensitivity** — Paying attention to historical frequencies and statistical baselines before evaluating specific cases.
- **Fermi estimation** — Breaking complex questions into tractable sub-questions to arrive at rough order-of-magnitude estimates.
- **Disaggregation** — Decomposing broad questions into more specific, measurable sub-questions that are easier to forecast.

## Calibration Training

Superforecasters invest significant effort in calibrating their judgment — aligning their stated confidence levels with actual accuracy over time. This is closely related to [[decision-tracking]], where recording and reviewing past forecasts helps identify systematic errors.

## Relationship to Other Frameworks

The superforecasting methodology shares significant overlap with [[thinking-in-bets---annie-duke]], which similarly treats decisions as probabilistic bets. Both approaches emphasize separating decision quality from outcome quality. The methodology also connects to [[bayesian-thinking]] through its emphasis on probabilistic updating, and to [[confirmation-bias]] avoidance as a core discipline.

## Key Distinction from Intuition-Based Approaches

Unlike [[recognition-primed-decision-model]] or [[naturalistic-decision-making]], which rely on pattern recognition and expertise, superforecasting prioritizes explicit probabilistic reasoning and systematic error correction. This makes it particularly valuable in domains where historical patterns are weak or changing.
