# Relative Deprivation

The tendency for satisfaction to depend on comparisons with others rather than absolute outcomes. We feel deprived not because we lack something, but because others have more.

## Key Points
- Connects to [[hedonic-treadmill]] — adaptation to circumstances
- Connects to [[satisfaction-under-constraints]] — satisfaction under limitations
- Connects to [[envy]] — corrosive comparison effects
- Absolute wealth matters less than relative position

## Guardrail
When evaluating your situation, try to assess absolute outcomes separately from relative comparisons. Journaling about specific grateful moments can counteract relative deprivation.
