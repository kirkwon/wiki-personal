# Differential Equation Models

Models that describe continuous change over time. The rate of change of a variable is a function of its current state and other variables.

## Types

- **Ordinary differential equations (ODEs)**: Single variable changing over time
- **Partial differential equations (PDEs)**: Multiple variables changing across space and time
- **Stochastic differential equations**: Continuous dynamics with random shocks

## Applications

- Population growth and decay
- Epidemic spread
- Chemical reactions
- Climate modeling
