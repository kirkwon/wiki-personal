# Crossroads

A visualization of life decisions as a branching tree. Map your current decision point, identify which paths are reversible vs. one-way doors, and make reversible decisions quickly.

## Application

Use for major life choices: distinguish reversible experiments from irreversible commitments; act fast on reversible paths.

## Personal Reflection

My bias is treating almost everything as reversible (talk-over-action), which is mostly right for technical work but wrong for anything that leaks — public posts, money committed, trust spent. I should apply a quick one-way-door test before moving fast.
