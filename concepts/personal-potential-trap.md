# Personal Potential Trap

The danger of focusing on what you *could* do rather than what you *actually* do. Potential unrealized is just fantasy. Use it to audit: are you shipping or just planning?

## Application

Regularly review your plans vs. actual outputs; shift focus from potential to realized progress.

## Personal Reflection

Direct hit: I generate more plans than shipped artifacts. The audit question that matters is 'what exists now that didn't last week?' — if the answer is a better plan, that's a warning, not progress.
