# Kansas City BBQ

Kansas City BBQ is known as the "Sauce Capital" of American barbecue, defined by sweet, tomato-based sauce applied generously to both beef and pork dishes.

## Philosophy

- Sweet, tomato-based sauce (molasses, brown sugar, vinegar tang)
- Both beef and pork featured
- Burnt ends as signature specialty
- Hickory wood for smoking

## Burnt Ends

Burnt ends are a Kansas City specialty: the fatty point of the brisket is cubed, resmoked with sauce, and caramelized until crispy. They represent the most iconic KC contribution to American BBQ.

## Signature Dishes

- **Burnt ends** — Cubed brisket point, resmoked with sauce
- **Pork ribs** — Sweet, saucy
- **Pulled pork** — Tomato-based sauce

## The Sauce

- Tomato base
- Sweet (molasses, brown sugar)
- Tangy (vinegar)
- Thick, glossy consistency
