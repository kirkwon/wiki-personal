# SWOT Analysis

Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats. A structured audit of your position. Strengths/Weaknesses are internal; Opportunities/Threats are external. Use it for personal strategy or project planning.

## Application

List your internal strengths (skills, resources) and weaknesses (limitations, blind spots). Then scan external opportunities (trends, openings) and threats (competition, risks). Use insights to leverage strengths, fix weaknesses, pursue opportunities, and mitigate threats.

## Personal Reflection

I often focus too much on fixing weaknesses instead of doubling down on strengths. SWOT reminds me to balance improvement with leveraging what I'm already good at.
