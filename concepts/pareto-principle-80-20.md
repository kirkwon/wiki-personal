# Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule)

**Also known as:** 80/20 Rule, Pareto Principle, Law of the Vital Few

The observation that roughly 80% of effects come from 20% of causes. Named after economist Vilfredo Pareto who observed that 80% of Italy's land was owned by 20% of the population.

## Core Idea

In any system, a small fraction of inputs produces the majority of outputs. Identifying and focusing on that fraction is the highest-leverage activity available.

## Applications in Strategy

- **Effort:** 20% of your effort produces 80% of results. Find that 20%.
- **Features:** 20% of features serve 80% of use cases. Build those first.
- **Risk:** 20% of risks account for 80% of potential losses. Mitigate those.
- **Code:** 20% of code causes 80% of bugs. Test that 20%.
- **Decisions:** 20% of decisions drive 80% of outcomes. Get those right.

## Connection to Strategic Framework

The Pareto Principle is the engine of **Step 6 (Leverage)** in the Strategic Filter. Before any execution, identify the 20% that gives 80% — then lead with it. If you can't find that leverage point, reconsider whether to proceed.

## Counterpoint

The exact ratio varies. Sometimes it's 90/10, sometimes 70/30. The principle isn't about the specific number — it's about **concentration of impact**. Always measure the actual distribution in your domain.
