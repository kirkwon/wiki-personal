# Debt Coverage Ratio

A lender risk metric measuring the property's ability to cover debt obligations from operating income.

## Formula

**Debt Coverage Ratio = NOI / Annual Debt Service**

## Interpretation

- DCR > 1.0: Property generates enough income to cover debt payments
- DCR < 1.0: Property does not cover debt from operations alone
- Typical lender requirement: DCR ≥ 1.20

## Usage

Tracked in the [[single-property-analysis-template]] and [[multi-property-portfolio-template]] alongside other metrics like [[net-operating-income]] and [[cap-rate]].
