# Blind Spot Bias

The inability to recognize our own cognitive biases while easily seeing them in others. This is perhaps the most fundamental bias because it undermines our ability to debias ourselves.

## Key Points
- We are blind to our own biases but perceptive of others'
- Connects to [[self-serving-bias]] — attributing success to self
- Connects to [[overconfidence-and-certainty]] — overestimating our judgment
- Makes [[bias-checklist]] and [[devil-s-advocate-perspective]] essential tools

## Practical Implication
Since we cannot see our own blind spots, we need external checks: decision journals, diverse perspectives, and structured debiasing tools.

## Guardrail
Regularly use [[pre-mortem-analysis]] and [[consider-the-opposite-exercise]] to surface biases you cannot see yourself.
