# 鮭（Sake/Salmon）

養殖または野生。通年。油っこく、脂が多い。

Salmon is a versatile fatty fish featured prominently in the gastronomy portal, particularly in premium preparations like [[salmon-collars]] and [[recipe:glazed-salmon-collars]]. Its rich fat content makes it well-suited to high-heat cooking methods and glazing techniques that develop deep flavor through [[maillard-reaction]].

## Key characteristics
- **Fat content** — High fat content provides richness and moisture retention
- **Cuts** — Fillets, collars, belly, and tail sections offer different textures and flavors
- **Cooking methods** — Seared, grilled, roasted, and stir-fried
- **Pairings** — Complements soy-based glazes, citrus, dill, and Asian aromatics like ginger and garlic

Proper [[resting-meat]] after cooking is important for salmon to maximize juiciness.
