# Bets

Bets is a universal conceptual tool for navigating complex situations by framing circumstances as bets. It provides a mental shortcut for making sense of patterns and relationships in uncertain environments.

## Definition

At its core, framing situations as "bets" encourages probabilistic thinking—recognizing that outcomes are uncertain and that the quality of a decision should be evaluated separately from the quality of its outcome. This mental model helps identify patterns and relationships by treating each situation as a bet with a range of possible outcomes rather than a binary win or loss.

## How It Works

- Treat every decision as a bet with probabilities attached.
- Separate the quality of the decision from the quality of the outcome.
- Use probabilistic language to describe beliefs and uncertainties.
- Recognize that repeated good bets can produce bad outcomes and vice versa.
