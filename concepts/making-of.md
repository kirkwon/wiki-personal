# Making-of

Document your own process — track how you make decisions, not just the outcomes. Creates a personal playbook you can refine over time. Like a "post-mortem" but for everyday choices.

## Application

Keep a decision journal where you note the context, options, reasoning, and outcome for each decision, then review periodically.

## Personal Reflection

This is basically the decision-journal habit I keep intending but not doing. Concrete commitment: log consequential decisions (context, options, expected outcome) so quarterly reviews can score my calibration instead of my memory of it.
