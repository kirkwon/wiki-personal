# Golden Ratio

The golden ratio is a mathematical ratio of approximately 1:1.618 found throughout nature and art. In photography, it serves as a more complex alternative to the rule of thirds for placing subjects and elements.

## How to Apply

- **Spiral (Fibonacci)**: Place the subject on the curve of the golden spiral
- **Golden triangle**: Position the subject at the intersection of diagonal lines
- **Golden ratio grid**: Similar to the rule of thirds but with different proportions

## When to Use

- Portraits: Place the eye on the golden ratio point
- Landscapes: Align the horizon at the golden ratio line
- Abstract: Create mathematical harmony in the composition

The golden ratio is mathematically more complex than the rule of thirds but produces a similar effect in practice. See [[rule-of-thirds]] and [[composition-techniques]] for related techniques.
