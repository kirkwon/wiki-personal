# Digital Minimalism - Cal Newport

Digital minimalism is Cal Newport's framework for intentional technology use, emphasizing that the value of any tool lies in how deliberately it is applied rather than in passive consumption.^[raw/articles/Technology.md]

## Core Ideas
- Technology should serve defined purposes, not dictate attention.
- Intentional engagement produces better outcomes than default use.
- Stripping away unnecessary digital tools reveals what truly matters.

## Connection to Mental Models
This concept extends the wiki's [[minimalism]] mental model into the technology domain, connecting to [[attention-budget]] (treating attention as finite), [[scatterfocus]] (unfocused wandering), and [[hyperfocus]] (deep concentration). Digital minimalism is the practice of choosing which mode to prioritize.
