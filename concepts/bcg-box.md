# BCG Box

Boston Consulting Group's portfolio matrix: Stars (high growth, high share), Cash Cows (low growth, high share), Question Marks (high growth, low share), Dogs (low/low). Adapt for personal projects: which activities are stars vs. dogs?

## Application

Review your personal projects or goals monthly. Classify each as Star (high growth, high investment), Cash Cow (low growth, high return), Question Mark (high growth, low return), or Dog (low growth, low return). Invest more in Stars, maintain Cash Cows, evaluate Question Marks for potential or divestment, and eliminate Dogs.

## Personal Reflection

I tend to hold onto Question Marks too long hoping they'll become Stars. The BCG Box forces me to be honest about which projects are truly promising versus just interesting distractions.
