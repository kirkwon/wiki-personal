# Think Win-Win

Think Win-Win is a cooperative mindset where all parties in an interaction or agreement can benefit. Covey contrasts this with zero-sum thinking, where one party's gain requires another's loss.

## Key Ideas

- **Abundance mentality**: Believing there is enough to go around enables creative solutions where everyone gains.
- **Mutual benefit**: Win-Win is not compromise—it seeks solutions that are better for all parties than any individual could achieve alone.
- **Maturity and strength**: Covey frames Win-Win as requiring both courage and consideration, balancing assertiveness with empathy.

## Relation to Other Concepts

This concept relates to [[synergize]], where collective effort produces outcomes beyond individual contributions. It also connects to [[skin-in-the-game]], which emphasizes accountability for outcomes, and to [[decision-making-frameworks]] where the framing of outcomes shapes the decision itself.

## Tensions

The Win-Win ideal may conflict with situations where genuine trade-offs exist. Not every negotiation allows mutual gain, and recognizing this is itself a form of strategic clarity.
