# Commodities Investing

Commodities investing involves allocating capital to physical goods or commodity-related financial instruments for inflation protection and portfolio diversification.

## Types

**Physical Commodities**:
- Precious metals (gold, silver, platinum)
- Energy (oil, natural gas)
- Agriculture (corn, wheat, cattle)
- Base metals (copper, aluminum)

**Commodity ETFs**:
- [[GLD]] (Gold ETF)
- [[USO]] (Oil ETF)
- [[DBA]] (Agriculture ETF)

**Futures**:
- Direct commodity exposure with leverage
- Professional tool with high risk

## Pros and Cons

**Pros**: Real asset inflation hedge, tangible ownership, crisis protection (gold in crashes)

**Cons**: No cash flow or dividend, storage costs, no earnings growth, volatile prices
