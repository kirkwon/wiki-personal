# Circle of Competence

**Also known as:** Circle of Confidence, Stay Within Your Circle

A mental model popularized by Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger: know the boundaries of your expertise and operate only within them.

## Core Idea

Every person and system has a "circle of competence" — the set of problems they understand deeply enough to make reliable decisions about. The size of the circle matters less than knowing its boundaries. Operating outside your circle without knowing you're outside is the single most dangerous strategic error.

## The Two Rules

1. **Know your circle:** Clearly define what you know and don't know. Write it down.
2. **Stay inside it:** Make decisions only within your circle of competence. When outside, learn, delegate, or skip.

## How to Determine Your Circle

- **Can you explain it to someone with no background?** If not, you're outside.
- **Have you made 5+ good decisions in this area?** If not, you haven't earned competence.
- **Do you know when you'd be wrong?** If you can't describe failure modes, you don't understand it.

## Connection to Strategic Framework

Circle of Competence is a pre-filter before **Step 1 (WHY)** . Before assessing any opportunity:
- **Is this inside my circle?** → Proceed to full strategic filter
- **Is this near my circle?** → Allocate time to learn first (spike)
- **Am I outside my circle?** → Delegate or skip
