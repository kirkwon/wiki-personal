# Mindfulness Meditation

Mindfulness meditation is a practice of deliberately focusing attention on the present moment, observing thoughts and sensations without judgment, and cultivating a state of calm awareness. Ryan Holiday identifies it as a key method for silencing the mind and achieving the inner quietude central to his philosophy of stillness.

## Role in Stillness
- Quiets mental chatter and reactive thought patterns
- Creates space between stimulus and response
- Enhances clarity for decision-making
- Reduces susceptibility to external and internal triggers

## Practical Application
Regular practice of sitting meditation, breath awareness, and body scanning supports the capacity for stillness that Holiday argues is essential for mastery, relationships, and personal growth.
