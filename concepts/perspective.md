# Perspective

Perspective in photography refers to the camera position and angle used to capture a scene. Changing perspective is one of the most effective ways to create interest and alter the feeling of an image.

## Camera Positions

| Position | Effect | Example |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Eye level | Natural, documentary | Street photography |
| Low angle | Powerful, grand | Architecture, portraits |
| High angle | Diminished, overview | Landscapes, crowds |
| Bird's eye | Abstract, graphic | Cityscapes, patterns |
| Worm's eye | Dramatic, towering | Buildings, trees |

See [[composition-techniques]] for the broader context of composition techniques.
