# So Good They Can't Ignore You

Cal Newport's book argues that passionate careers are built, not found, through the accumulation of career capital — rare, valuable skills developed through deliberate practice and strategic effort.

## Key Concepts

- **Career capital**: Skills and traits that are rare and valuable, which can be traded for control and purpose
- **Deliberate practice**: Focused, structured effort that pushes beyond current ability
- **Mission**: A meaningful direction that emerges after developing sufficient career capital
- **Control**: The ability to choose what you work on, earned through accumulated value

## Connection to Mastery

This framework treats mastery as the product of sustained, strategic skill development — aligning with mastery as a mental model for navigating career complexity.
