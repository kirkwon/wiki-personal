# Probability Distributions and Risk Management

Probability distributions are tools for modeling uncertain events. Understanding different types of distributions helps individuals manage risks by making decisions with skin in the game. Taleb emphasizes that conventional models often underestimate tail risks—low-probability, high-consequence events—leading to fragile systems when decision-makers lack accountability.

This connects to [[black-swan-events]] and the broader theme that skin in the game forces decision-makers to confront the true nature of uncertainty rather than relying on misleading statistical models.

## Key Points

- Probability distributions model uncertain events for risk management
- Understanding distributions helps make better decisions with accountability
- Conventional models underestimate tail risks
- Skin in the game forces confrontation with true uncertainty
- Connects to [[black-swan-events]] and [[decision-making-under-uncertainty]]
