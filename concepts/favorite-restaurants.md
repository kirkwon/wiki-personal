## Discovery Process

### Finding New Spots
- **Local blogs** - Eater SF, food writers
- **Social media** - Instagram food accounts, Yelp reviews
- **Friend recommendations** - Most reliable source
- **Walking neighborhoods** - Stumble upon hidden gems
- **Chef/restaurant group followings** - Track favorite chefs

### Evaluation Checklist
When trying a new restaurant:
- [ ] **Food quality** - Flavor, preparation, freshness
- [ ] **Service** - Attentiveness, knowledge, timing
- [ ] **Atmosphere** - Vibe matches expectation
- [ ] **Value** - Worth the price?
- [ ] **Consistency** - Would I return?
- [ ] **Uniqueness** - What makes it special?

### Keeping Notes
After each visit:
- **What I ordered** - Specific dishes
- **What I loved** - Why I'd return
- **What to skip** - What didn't work
- **Improvements needed** - What could be better
- **Rating** - 1-5 stars, would I recommend?
