# Unimaginable

A thought experiment: what would you do if you knew you could not fail? Reveals your true desires by removing the fear filter. Use it to separate "I don't want it" from "I'm afraid to try."

## Application

Ask yourself what you would do if failure were impossible, then compare with what you're actually doing to see where fear is holding you back.

## Personal Reflection

The no-failure answer surfaces ambition I filter out too early for being impractical. Worth running quarterly: compare the fearless list against the actual roadmap and ask whether fear or genuine disinterest caused each gap.
