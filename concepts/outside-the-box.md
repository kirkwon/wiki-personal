# Outside the Box

Classic lateral thinking. The "box" = your current assumptions. List them, then negate each one and explore what becomes possible. De Bono's "po" technique.

## Application

Write down your assumptions about a problem or situation. For each assumption, ask "What if the opposite were true?" or "What if this weren't necessary?" Explore the resulting ideas without judgment.

## Personal Reflection

I've used this to challenge assumptions like "I need to be an expert to start" or "This must follow the standard process," leading to simpler, faster solutions.
