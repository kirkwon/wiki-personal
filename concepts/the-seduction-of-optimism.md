# The Seduction of Optimism

Overconfidence leads to taking too much risk. Believing you can beat the market, underestimating potential downsides, and overestimating control over outcomes are all forms of optimism bias that erode wealth.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## Manifestations

- Believing you can time the market.
- Underestimating risks.
- Ignoring professional advice.
- Overestimating the probability of favorable outcomes.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]

## Countermeasures

- Plan for things to take longer than expected.
- Build in margin of safety.
- Use conservative assumptions.
- Practice tail event thinking — assume rare events will happen.^[raw/articles/The Psychology of Money.md]
