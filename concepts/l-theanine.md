# L-theanine (L-茶氨酸)

L-theanine is an amino acid found naturally in tea, particularly green tea. It promotes relaxation and improved focus without causing drowsiness, and counterbalances the stimulating effects of caffeine.

## Effects
- Promotes calm, focused attention
- Reduces stress without sedation
- Works synergistically with caffeine for alert relaxation
- Increases alpha brain wave activity

## Sources
- Highest concentrations in shade-grown teas (Gyokuro, Matcha)
- Present in all tea types but especially green tea
