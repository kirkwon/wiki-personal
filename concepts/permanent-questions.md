# Permanent Questions

> Manage and extend the permanent research questions framework at ~/wiki-personal/questions/ — Q01 through Q0N drive methodology development. Covers canonical location, naming, Q-file structure, the weekly vault-scan maintenance cycle, and when to create new Q-files.

## Overview

- **Overview** — The permanent questions framework lives at `~/wiki-personal/questions/` and drives methodology development through a six-month rhythm: weekly vault scans, monthly cross-question synthesis, and quarterly full synthesis. Each question accumulates knowledge, not just conclusions.
- **Pre-Flight: Verify Canonical Location** — Before reading or writing Q-files, ALWAYS check that the canonical location exists:
- **Canonical Location** — **Important:** A backup at `brain/wiki-personal.backup-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS/questions/` may contain older Q-files. The live canonical location is `wiki-personal/questions/` — always read/write there, not in the `brain/` subdirectory.

## Further detail

### File Naming

Format: `Q##-<short-descriptive-name>.md`

### When to Create a New Q-File

Trigger: starting a new research thread that doesn't fit existing Q01-Q06, with enough depth to accumulate knowledge over time.

### Standard Q-File Structure

Every Q-file MUST have: (1) confidence/evidence frontmatter, (2) a Current State of Knowledge section, and (3) a `## Evidence Log` section where the weekly scan appends dated evidence blocks.
