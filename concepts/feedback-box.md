# Feedback Box

A structured approach to receiving feedback: separate facts from interpretation, ask for specific examples, and decide what to act on vs. what to discard. Not all feedback deserves a response.

## Application

When receiving feedback, first listen for the factual content (what happened). Separate this from interpretations or judgments. Ask for specific examples to clarify vague feedback. Then decide which parts are actionable and worth integrating, and which to discard as not relevant or not within your control.

## Personal Reflection

I used to take all feedback personally and try to act on everything. This model has helped me be more discerning about feedback, focusing on what's truly useful and letting go of the rest.
