# Ocean Beach Surf Skill Progression

Ocean Beach offers a natural progression in surf difficulty across its three sections, allowing surfers to match skill level to conditions.

- **Beginners**: Kelly's Cove (north end) — more protected, smaller waves
- **Intermediate**: Noriega Street (center) — consistent surf break, year-round conditions
- **Advanced**: Sloat Boulevard (south end) — larger waves, more exposed, stronger currents

This progression makes Ocean Beach suitable for surfers of all levels. Always surf with a buddy, wear appropriate wetsuit (4/3mm minimum), and check conditions via Surfline before heading out.
