# Personal Finance

The comprehensive management of financial resources including budgeting, saving, investing, debt management, and retirement planning. This personal finance system encompasses comprehensive wealth management strategies including investment principles, risk management, tax optimization, and retirement planning. The content is structured to provide both conceptual understanding and practical implementation frameworks.
