# Gap-in-the-Market

Spot the unmet need: what do people complain about but nobody has solved? Apply to yourself too — what's your personal "gap" that nobody else fills?

## Application

Listen for complaints and frustrations in your daily life, work, or hobbies. Identify recurring problems that people mention but nobody has addressed. For personal gaps, consider skills or services you could offer that others lack.

## Personal Reflection

I've identified a gap in providing clear, actionable summaries of complex technical topics for non-experts, which aligns with my strength in simplifying concepts.
