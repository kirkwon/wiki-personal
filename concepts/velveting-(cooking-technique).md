# Velveting (Cooking Technique)

Velveting is a Chinese restaurant-style technique that creates a silky, tender texture in stir-fried proteins. It involves coating the protein in a mixture of soy sauce, cornstarch, and optionally egg white before cooking.

## How It Works

- Cornstarch dissolved in the marinade coats the protein surface
- During cooking, the starch gelatinizes, creating a protective layer
- The result is a silky, restaurant-quality texture

## Velveting Marinade

- Soy sauce
- Cornstarch
- Egg white (optional)

Use for Chinese restaurant-style stir-fry, beef, chicken.

## Connection to Marinades

Velveting is a specialized application of the marinade framework, where cornstarch serves as the fifth component specific to Chinese technique. The Chinese basic marinade template (soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch, sesame oil, white pepper, ginger, garlic) incorporates velveting principles.
