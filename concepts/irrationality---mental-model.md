# Irrationality as a Mental Model

Irrationality is a universal mental model that functions as a conceptual tool for navigating complex situations. Rather than viewing irrationality purely as a flaw or error in judgment, this model frames it as a framework for understanding patterns and relationships in human behavior and decision-making.

## Key Idea

Irrationality provides a mental shortcut for making sense of the world. When understood systematically, irrational patterns become predictable and can even be leveraged—what Dan Ariely calls "the upside of irrationality." This perspective extends beyond Kahneman's focus on System 1 and System 2 errors to emphasize that irrationality is a structured, exploitable feature of cognition.
