# Italian Cuisine

## Overview

Italian cuisine (Cucina italiana) is celebrated for its regional diversity, high-quality ingredients, and simple preparations that let ingredients shine. From the fresh seafood of Sicily to the rich dairy of the Alps, Italy's cuisine reflects its geography, history, and deep cultural connection to food.

## Core Philosophy

### La Cucina Povera (The Cuisine of the Poor)

**"Make the most of what you have"**

Italian cooking is rooted in:
- **Seasonal ingredients** - What's in season, what's local
- **Minimal manipulation** - Ingredients speak for themselves
- **Waste nothing** - Use every part of ingredient
- **Quality over quantity** - Few ingredients, best quality

### Regional Diversity

**Italy = 20 regions, 20 cuisines**

| Region | Style | Characteristics |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| **Emilia-Romagna** - Rich, dairy, pork | Prosciutto, parmesan, ragù |
| **Tuscany** - Rustic, meat-heavy | Bistecca, beans, bread |
| **Sicily** - Mediterranean, seafood | Arancini, pasta alla norma |
| **Naples** - Pizza, pasta | Pizza Margherita, pasta dishes |
| **Rome** - Offal, simple | Carbonara, cacio e pepe |
| **Veneto** - Rice, seafood | Risotto, radicchio |

## Eating Traditions

### Italian Meal Structure

1. **Antipasti** - Appetizers (cold cuts, olives, cheese)
2. **Primi** - First course (pasta, risotto, soup)
3. **Secondi** - Second course (meat, fish)
4. **Contorni** - Side dishes (vegetables)
5. **Dolci** - Dessert
6. **Caffè** - Coffee (espresso)
7. **Digestivo** - Liqueur (limoncello, grappa)

### Wine Pairing

**Simple rules:**
- Red wine with meat
- White wine with fish
- Regional wine with regional food

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | How to Fix |
|---------|------------|
| Overcooking pasta | Cook al dente, save water for sauce |
| Using cheap olive oil | Extra virgin for finishing |
| Adding cream to carbonara | Authentic uses eggs, cheese only |
| Not letting risotto rest | Rest 5 minutes before serving |

## Practice

1. **Master carbonara** - Eggs, cheese, guanciale only
2. **Make risotto** - Slow-stirred, creamy
3. **Try authentic pizza** - Neapolitan style
4. **Host Italian dinner** - Antipasti, primi, secondi

**Remember:** Italian cooking is about quality ingredients, simple preparations, and regional pride. Less is more.
