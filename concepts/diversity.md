# Diversity

Diversity is a conceptual tool or principle that helps understand and navigate complex situations. It provides a mental shortcut or framework for making sense of patterns and relationships in the world.^[raw/articles/Diversity.md]

As a mental model, diversity emphasizes the value of varied perspectives, information sources, and viewpoints when trying to make sense of complex systems. This aligns with James Surowiecki's findings in [[The Wisdom of Crowds]] that diversity of opinion is one of four essential conditions for collective intelligence to outperform expert judgment.^[raw/articles/Diversity.md]

## Key Connections

- [[the-wisdom-of-crowds]] — Surowiecki identifies diversity of opinion as a prerequisite for crowd wisdom.
- [[superforecasting]] — Diverse perspectives and intellectual humility are central to making accurate predictions.
