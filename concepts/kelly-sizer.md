# Kelly Sizer

> Determine optimal resource allocation (time, money, effort) for opportunities with positive expected value using Kelly criterion principles. Includes the Kelly-Sharpe-Alpha factor chain identity and diagnostics (DSR, Kelly divergence, Sortino/Sharpe spread).

## Overview

- **Purpose** — Determine optimal resource allocation (time, money, effort) for opportunities where you have an edge, using Kelly criterion principles adapted for life decisions. Maximizes long-term growth while managing risk of ruin.
- **When to Apply** — - You've identified an opportunity with positive expected value (edge) - You can estimate probability of success and potential payoff - Deciding how much to invest/commit (not whether to do it) - Repeated or scalable opportunities where sizing matters - When you want to balance aggressive growth with survival
- **Core Principles** — - **Kelly Formula**: f* = (bp - q) / b where b = net odds, p = win probability, q = loss probability - **Growth Optimization**: Maximizes logarithm of wealth over time - **Risk of Ruin**: Full Kelly can be volatile; fractional Kelly (½Kelly, ⅓Kelly) reduces volatility - **Edge Estimation**: Critical to accurately assess your advantage - **Survival First**: Avoiding ruin is priority #1; growth comes second

## Further detail

### Output Structure

1. **Edge Assessment**: Your estimated advantage (win probability × payoff) 2. **Kelly Fraction**: Optimal % of resources to allocate 3. **Fractional Kelly Options**: Conservative allocations (½, ⅓ Kelly) 4. **Risk Metrics**: Expected growth, volatility, drawdown potential 5. **Implementation Plan**: How to allocate resources over time 6. **Monitoring Triggers**: When to re-evaluate size or exit

### Verification

- [ ] Clear definition of win/loss outcomes and their values - [ ] Reasonable, conservative estimates for p and b - [ ] Kelly fraction calculated correctly (not negative) - [ ] Fractional Kelly applied for risk management - [ ] Risk metrics considered (drawdown, volatility) - [ ] Practical implementation plan with review schedule - [ ] Recognition that models are approximations

**Opportunity**: Spend 5 hours/week developing a niche SaaS tool
