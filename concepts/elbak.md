# Elbak

A self-reflection exercise: write down what you're good at, what you love doing, and what the world needs. The intersection is your "sweet spot." Similar to ikigai but simplified.

## Application

List your strengths, passions, and ways you can contribute; find the overlap for your sweet spot.

## Personal Reflection

The honest intersection for me: systems thinking + quantitative rigor + building agent infrastructure. The recurring failure mode is drifting toward what's merely interesting; this model is a reminder to check new projects against the sweet spot, not just curiosity.
