# Flow

Csikszentmihalyi's concept of optimal experience — the state where challenge perfectly matches skill. You enter "flow" when a task is hard enough to engage you but not so hard it causes anxiety. Use it to identify which activities put you in the zone and structure your work around them.

## Application

Identify activities that put you in flow and structure work around them.

## Personal Reflection

Flow shows up for me in long coding/reasoning sessions with a hard problem — rarely in meetings or email. The implication is to structure days around one deep block and treat interruptions as flow-killers worth charging for, not free.
