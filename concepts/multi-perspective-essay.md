### Pitfalls

- **Don't make it a list.** The essay should read as a coherent argument, not a sequence of philosopher summaries. The synthesis section is where the magic happens. - **Don't use formulaic closings.** "The X lesson:" and "The practical implication:" are AI tells. End sections with direct statements. - **Don't lose the voice.** Philosophical essays can become dry and academic.
