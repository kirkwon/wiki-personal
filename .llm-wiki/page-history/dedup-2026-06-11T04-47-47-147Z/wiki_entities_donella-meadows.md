# Donella H. Meadows

Environmental scientist, author, and systems thinker best known for her work on system dynamics and the limits of growth in complex systems. She co‑authored *Limits to Growth* (1972), which used computer modeling to explore the interactions between population growth, resource depletion, and environmental impact, and later *Thinking in Systems: A Primer*, which introduces feedback loops as a core tool for understanding complex situations.

## Key Contributions
- Co‑authored *Limits to Growth* (1972) and its 30‑year update  
- Co‑authored *Thinking in Systems: A Primer* – introduces feedback loops, reinforcing and balancing dynamics as tools for navigating complexity  
- Pioneered the field of systems thinking and system dynamics  
- Developed concepts around leverage points in complex systems  
- Provided a rigorous framework for understanding feedback loops, stocks and flows, and leverage points in complex systems  

## Relevance to Mental Models
- Meadows’ work on loops is central to the mental‑model framework in this wiki. Her concept of feedback loops provides a universal tool for recognizing patterns and relationships in complex systems, applicable across domains from personal decision‑making to organizational strategy.  
- The “Overview” mental model draws from her emphasis on pattern and relationship recognition.  
- Her work on systems thinking is one of the two canonical sources referenced for the [[system-mental-model]].
