# Hyperfocus - Chris Bailey

Chris Bailey's 2024 book *Hyperfocus* presents a comprehensive framework for managing attention through two complementary modes: hyperfocus and scatterfocus. The book argues that mastering both modes—and understanding the costs of task-switching—is essential for productivity, creativity, and well-being.

## Core Framework
The book introduces [[hyperfocus-and-scatterfocus]] as two distinct but complementary ways the brain processes information. [[hyperfocus]] is intense, sustained concentration on a single task, while [[scatterfocus]] is deliberate mind-wandering that drives creativity and restoration.

## Key Findings
- Task-switching creates [[attention-residue]] that measurably reduces performance on subsequent tasks
- [[single-tasking-vs-multitasking]] is more effective than multitasking for both quality and speed
- Peak attention hours vary individually and should be mapped and protected
- Unfocused time (scatterfocus) is not wasted—it drives insight and prevents burnout
- Attention is a finite budget requiring deliberate allocation
- Strategic use of distraction (rather than total elimination) can be more sustainable than digital abstinence
- Mindfulness practice strengthens attention and reduces mind-wandering

## Structure
The book is organized into five parts:
1. How We Pay Attention (modes, residue, building attention muscle)
2. How to Hack Your Attention (peak hours, mindfulness, environment, single-tasking)
3. How to Use Hyperfocus (planning, energy, breaks, habits)
4. How to Use Scatterfocus (creativity, restoration, when to use each mode)
5. The Mindful Productivity (attention budget, digital minimalism, intentional life)
