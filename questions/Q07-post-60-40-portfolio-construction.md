# Q07: How do you construct a portfolio when bonds no longer diversify equities?

## Context

The 40-year bond bull market (1982-2022) made 60/40 work: bonds reliably rallied when equities sold off. Post-2022, with inflation sticky and rates near structural floors, bonds and equities have become positively correlated. Duration is now a risk factor, not a hedge.

## The Question

If bonds/debt are too correlated to equities to serve as counterweights, what replaces them?

## Candidate Answers (accumulating)

1. **Sector rotation** — Defensive/value sector rotation during equity drawdowns (utilities, healthcare, staples) as a substitute for duration
2. **Commodities** — Real assets that benefit from the same inflation that breaks financial assets
3. **Cash** — Tactical dry powder for optionality at dislocation points; not a permanent allocation (currency debasement risk)
4. **Options overlay** — Tail-risk hedging via put spreads or VIX call spreads

## Sub-Questions

- What correlation regime are we actually in? Is the bond-equity correlation break permanent or cyclical?
- Which commodity exposures provide the best equity drawdown protection? (gold vs. broad vs. energy)
- How do you size sector rotation signals without whipsaw?
- What's the optimal cash allocation when real rates are negative?
